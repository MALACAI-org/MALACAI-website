date
 
n-ct.
 
1.
nateil ne aldew wey bulan te tuig
Eng. aldew wey bulan
a particular day in a month in a year (e.g., June 22nd, 2004).
What date is your wedding?
Nekey ne aldew ka kasal nu?
 
n-ct.
 
2.
himu te kanakan wey mengebay ne pegpabehayey te timpu eyew egpeggeyinaweey
a social activity in which a male and female spend time together because they want to have a relationship.
 
vi.
 
3.
egkaraan
Eng. egkaraan
If something dates, it begins to look old-fashioned, because it comes from a particular time.
That style is very fashionable, but it will date quickly.
Sika ne pegkeyimu amana ne mabantug, piru mahaan sika egkaraan.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0676-0700
