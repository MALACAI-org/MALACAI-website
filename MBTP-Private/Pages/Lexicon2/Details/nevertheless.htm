nevertheless
/ne-ver-thə-less/
 
adv.
piru minsan pad
Eng. piru minsan pad
You use nevertheless to say that one fact is not changed or affected by another fact.
He felt very tired. Nevertheless, he went to work.
Nabeley-beley sikandin. Piru minsan pad due, miggendiyè ded sikandin te trabahu rin.
Sem.Dom.: adverbs; AWL6
