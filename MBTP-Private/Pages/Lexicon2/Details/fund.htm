fund
/fand/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
seleppì ne tabang eyew te nateil ne tumung
Eng. seleppì ne tabang; pundu
pundu
a supply of money to use for a particular purpose.
The rich man created a fund to help poor students go to college.
Ka meyaman ne etew migbehey te pundu para te pubri ne istudyanti ne egkulihiyu.
 
vt.
 
2.
egtabang te seleppì
Eng. egtabang te seleppì
If you fund something, you provide the money for it.
His uncle is funding his college study.
Ka anggam din migbehey te seleppì para te peg-iskuyla rin te kulihiyu.
Sem.Dom.: AWL3
 
 
funds
/fandz/
 
n-pl.
seleppì
Eng. seleppì
money
We don't have the funds to buy a motorbike.
Warè seleppì ta ne igbeli te mutur.
Sem.Dom.: plural nouns
