forget
/for-get/
 
vt.
 
1.
egkalingawan
Eng. egkalingawan
If you forget something that you knew before, it is not in your mind any more. If you forget something, you leave it somewhere by mistake.
Sorry, I have forgotten your name.
Oh, I forgot my lunch! I left it at home.
 
vt.
 
2.
egkalingew
Eng. egkalingew
If you forget to do something, you don't do it because it is not in your mind to do it.
She forgot to close the window.
Irreg.  forgot, forgotten.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0801-0825
 
 
forgetful
/for-get-ful/
 
adj.
lilingawen
Eng. lilingawen
a forgetful person often forgets.
