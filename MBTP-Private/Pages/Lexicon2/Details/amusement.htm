amusement
/a-myooz-mənt/
 
n-uc.
 
1.
pegkatuen; pegpakangisi; pegkabayat
Eng. pegkatuen; pegpakangisi; pegkabayat
a feeling that something is funny.
He couldn't hide his amusement when he read his son's story.
Kenè din igkeeles [igke͡eles] ka pegkatuen te nabasa rin e ka guhuren te me anak din ne lukes.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Eng. kalingawan
kalingawan
something (e.g., a roller-coaster) which people ride, use or do to have fun.
Cities usually have more amusements than country areas.
Masalig ka kaliwangan diyè te siyudad du te diyè te kenè pad ne siyudad ne inged.
Sem.Dom.: GSL1551-1575 - related
