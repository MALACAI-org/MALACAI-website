burn
/bern/
 
vi.
 
1.
egkalegleg
Eng. egkalegleg
If a fire burns, there is a fire.
There is a fire burning in the fireplace.
 
vi.
 
2.
egkatutung te hapuy
Eng. egkatutung
If something burns, it has fire on it.
The wood in the fireplace is burning.
 
vt.
 
3.
egsilab; egtutung
Eng. egsilab; egtutung
If you burn something, you destroy it with fire.
We burn our rubbish in the afternoons.
 
vt.
 
4.
egkatutung te kimikal wey ke meinit; egkaluwasan
Eng. egkatutung; egkaluwasan
If you burn part of your body, you hurt it with a chemical or something hot, such as fire.
I burned my fingers on the hot pot.
The petrol burned his skin.
 
n-ct.
 
5.
natutung
Eng. natutung
a painful part of your body where a chemical or something hot has hurt it.
She had bad burns on her leg.
 
vt.
 
6.
egpakahamit eyew due te kanekal
Eng. egpakahamit
If a machine burns something such as diesel, it uses that thing to make power or energy.
The generator burns a lot of diesel.
Ka diniritur nakahamit te dakel ne kurudu.
 
vt.
 
7.
eggalin egtahù te me kanengnengan puun te kumpyutir pendiyè te CD
Eng. egpaburn
egpaburn
If you burn information onto a compact disk, you save the information on the disk using a computer.
She burned the files onto a disk.
Irreg.  burned [bernd]/fb:burnt , burned/fb:burnt.
