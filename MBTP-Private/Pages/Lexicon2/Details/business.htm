business
/biz-nəss/
 
n-uc.
 
1.
himu ne due kalabetan te pegbelegyè wey pegbebeli
Eng. pegbelegyè; pegbebeli
work which is connected to buying and selling things and services.
He works in business, so he travels to visit different companies.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
kumpanya eyew te pegpameligyè
Eng. kumpanya
a company which sells things or services.
She has a cleaning business.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
nateil ne hitabù wey sitwasyun
Eng. hitabù; sitwasyun
a particular event or situation.
The crime was a terrible business.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0251-0275
