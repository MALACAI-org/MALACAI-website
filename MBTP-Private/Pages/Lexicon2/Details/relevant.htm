relevant
/rel-ə-vənt/
 
adj.
mabulig wey keilangan
Eng. mabulig; keilangan; impurtanti
impurtanti
If something is relevant to someone or something, it is useful or important for them.
That radio program is relevant to both older and younger people.
Ka sika ne prugrama te radyu, amana ne mabulig diyè te me buyag wey me kabataan.
Sem.Dom.: AWL2
 
 
irrelevant
/i-rel-ə-vənt/
 
adj.
not relevant.
 
 
relevance
/rel-ə-vənss/
 
n-uc.
pegpakabulig wey pegkakeilangan
Eng. pegpakabulig wey pegkakeilangan
the state of being relevant.
What is the relevance of your question to our topic today?
Nekey ka bulig te insè nu kuntee te guhuren ta?
Status: ready
