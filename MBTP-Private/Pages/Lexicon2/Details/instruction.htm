instruction
/in-strak-shən/
 
n-uc.
 
1.
pegpanulù
Eng. pegpanulù
the action of teaching somebody.
Our teacher gave us instruction in mathematics.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
pegsuhù te peggimu
Eng. pegsuhù te peggimu
something which someone tells you to do.
He is a good child who follows his parents' instructions.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
panulù ne lalag
Eng. panulù ne lalag
words which which tell you how to do something.
I bought a cellphone, and it had a small book of instructions to teach me how to use it.
Migbebeli a te silpun, wey due deisek ne libru ne egpanulù ke egmenuwen te eggamit seini.
Sem.Dom.: AWL6
