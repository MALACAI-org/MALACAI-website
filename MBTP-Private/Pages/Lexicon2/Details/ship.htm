ship
 
n-ct.
 
1.
unturanan (ne eggampung wey diyè egbayè te dahat); barku
Eng. unturanan; barku
a very large boat which travels on the sea, and carries people or things.
 
vt.
 
2.
egpapeendiyè [egpape͡endiyè]; egpeuyan; egparulung
Eng. egpapeendiyè; egpeuyan; egparulung
If you ship something to a place, you send it there by some kind of transport, such as ship or truck.
