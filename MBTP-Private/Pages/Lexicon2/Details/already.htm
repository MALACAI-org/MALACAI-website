already
/all-red-ee/
 
adv.
e (meyitenged te neimpus wey ke neyitabù)
Eng. e
If something has already happened, it happened before now. Sometimes you are surprised that it happened so early.
It's only 2pm and you've already finished your work!
Alas dus pad seini wey nekeimpus kad e te himu nu.
Sorry, he's not here, because he's already gone to his farm.
Sem.Dom.: adverbs; GSL0401-0425
