program
/proh-græm/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
nasinsinundul ne me himuwen
Eng. nasinsinundul ne me himuwen; prugrama
prugrama
a series of planned events.
What is the program for the fiesta?
Nekey ka me himuwen te pista?
 
n-ct.
 
2.
pepil ne nasulatan te nateil e ne me himuwenen
a piece of paper which shows a series of planned events.
We printed a program for the fiesta, to show all the activities.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
igmakita diyè te tibi wey egkarineg diyè te radyu
a show on television or the radio.
That's my favorite TV program.
 
vt.
 
4.
egpeyimu te nateil ne pamaahi (meyitenged te kumpyutir wey ke mekina rè.
If you program a machine or computer, you cause it to work in a particular way.
They programmed the electric bell to ring at 4pm every day.
 
n-ct.
 
5.
punduk te me panulù para te kumpyutir
Eng. punduk te me panulù para te kumpyutir
a group of instructions for a computer.
I had to write a computer program for my computer class.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0176-0200
