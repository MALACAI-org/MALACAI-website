affect
/a-fekt/
 
vt.
 
1.
egpakabehey te meupiya wey ke mareet ne kapabulusan; egpakaapiktu
Eng. egpakabehey te kapabulusan; egpakaapiktu
If something affects another thing, it produces some result or change in that other thing.
Tiredness affects your work.
Egpakaapiktu te himu nu ka pegkabeley.
 
vt.
 
2.
egpakasenggil te geyinawa; egkaapiktuwan
Eng. egpakasenggil te geyinawa; egkaapiktuwan
If something affects you, it causes you to feel an emotion, especially sadness.
The sad movie affected me.
Nakaapiktu keddì ka salida.
 
vt.
 
3.
eglilibat (te kenè ne malehet ne eggeramen)
eglilibat
If someone affects a particular feeling, they pretend to have that feeling.
She affected interest, but she really didn't care.
Eglilibat sikandin te pegkabagget, piru migbalahad din seini.
Sem.Dom.: AWL2
