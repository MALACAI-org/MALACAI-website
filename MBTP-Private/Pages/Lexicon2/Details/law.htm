law
 
n-uc, n-ct.
 
1.
kukuman; balaud
Eng. kukuman; balaud
a rule or group of rules which the people in a society must obey.
It is against the law to steal.
Ka egpanakew supak te balaud.
See legal
 
n-ct.
 
2.
puhawang meyitenged ke egmemenu ka kalibutan ne eggimu
a basic idea about how the world works (e.g., the law of gravity).
 
n-ct.
 
3.
ka hun-a ne lalimma ne libru diyè te Tapey ne Kasabutan te Bibliya (Hinisis, Iksudu, Libitiku, Numiru wey Diyutirunumiyu)
the first five books of the Old Testament in the Bible (Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers and Deuteronomy).
Sem.Dom.: GSL0276-0300; keyterms
