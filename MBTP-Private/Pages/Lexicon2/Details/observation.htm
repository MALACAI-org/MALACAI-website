observation
/ob-zer-vay-shən/
 
n-uc, n-ct.
 
1.
1 peg-ipat; 2 pegkakita; 3 pegkahi; 4 pegpalintutuu
Eng. peg-ipat; pegpakita; pegkahi; pegpalintutuu
the action of observing.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
egkakita
Eng. egkakita
something that you see
Watch the children for a while, and write down your observations.
Tengteng ka te me batè taheed, wey ipansulat nu ka egkakita nu.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
pangguhuren; panugtulen
Eng. pangguhuren; panugtulen
something that you say about a thing you have noticed (make an observation).
He made some very interesting observations about the movie.
Miggimu sikandin te amana ne makabebagget ne pangguhuren meyitenged te salida.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0476-0500 - related
