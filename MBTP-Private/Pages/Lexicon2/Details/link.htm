link
/lingk/
 
vt.
 
1.
egpegsumpul
Eng. egpegsumpul
If something links two things, it connects them in some way.
This road links Davao and Cagayan.
Seini ne kelesadda ka nekegsumpul te Dabaw wey Kagayan.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
egsumpul
Eng. egsumpul
something which connects things together
That road is the link between Davao and Calinan.
 
vt.
 
3.
egsuman-suman ne due kalabetan te tagsè sabeka kandan
If you link two things in your mind, you believe that there is some relationship between them.
Many people link rain with sickness.
Masalig ne etew ne impalintutuu ne ka uran ka aliddanan te me dalu.
 
n-ct.
 
4.
ka sabeka ne butang iyan aliddanan te sabeka ne butang
If there is a link between two things, one thing causes the other.
There is a strong link between smoking and lung cancer.
Due makeseg ne kalabetan te pegpanihupan wey te dalu te bahè.
 
n-ct.
 
5.
sabeka ne sumpul te kedina
Eng. sumpul te kedina
one of the pieces of a chain.
That chain has 70 links.
Ka kedina due pitun pulù ne sumpul.
