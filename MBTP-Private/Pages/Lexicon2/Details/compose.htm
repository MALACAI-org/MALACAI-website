compose
 
v-passive.
 
1.
neyimu puun te
Eng. neyimu
If something is composed of things, it is made of those things.
This raft is composed of wood and rope.
Ka seini ne gakit su neyimu puun te kayu wey lubid.
 
vt.
 
2.
egpangimu te minsan nekey iling te ulahingen
Eng. egpangimu
If you compose something such as a song or a piece of music, you create it.
My father composes new songs and sings them to us.
Ka amey ku migpangimu te iyam ne me ulahingen wey eg-ulahingen seini kayi te kanta.
 
vt.
 
3.
egliwangen nu ka kauhalingen nu ne egkaawè ka mareet ne suman-suman nu
If you are upset, and you compose yourself, you cause yourself to become calm.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0976-1000
