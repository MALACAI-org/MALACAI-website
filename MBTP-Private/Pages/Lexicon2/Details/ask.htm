ask
/ahsk/
 
vt.
 
1.
eg-insè
Eng. eg-insè
If you ask someone something, you say a question to that person.
He asked me why I wanted to go.
 
vt.
 
2.
egpeyimu; egpabulig; eggangyù
Eng. egpeyimu; egpabulig; eggangyù
If you ask someone to do something, you politely say that you want them to do it.
She asked me to help her in the garden.
 
vt.
 
3.
egbuyù
Eng. egbuyù
If you ask someone for something, you politely say that you want them to give you something.
He asked me for some money.
