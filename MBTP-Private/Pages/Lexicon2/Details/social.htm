social
/soh-shəl/
 
adj.
 
1.
due kalabetan te pegbehey te uras eyew te peg-alukuy wey te pegpalallalahey te duma ne me etew.
Something social is related to people spending time together and talking.
There were many social times during the holidays, because people were free.
That child doesn't have very good social skills, as he always fights with other children.
 
adj.
 
2.
keilangan ne eg-ugpè wey ke egbehey te panahun te peg-alukuy te duma (meyitenged te etew wey mananap).
A social person or animal needs to live or spend time with others.
Bees are social animals who live in large communities.
 
adj.
 
3.
due kalabetan te kumunidad te me keet-etawan
Eng. kumunidad, due kalabetan te
Something social is related to communities of people (e.g., social problems, social structure).
Drugs are a big social problem in some communities.
