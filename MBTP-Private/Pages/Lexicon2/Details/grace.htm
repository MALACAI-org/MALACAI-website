grace
/grayss/
 
n-uc.
 
1.
keupiya wey keyid-u; grasya
Eng. keupiya wey keyid-u; grasya
goodness and kindness.
Even though I had said some very bad things to her, she shared her lunch with me. That's grace!
Minsan pad miglalag a kandin te maddeet, intuhen e red nikandin te paniudtu rin. Keyid-u sika!
 
n-uc.
 
2.
mateles ne egtengtengan wey mateles ne egweil
If someone or something has grace, they are beautiful to look at, and beautiful when they move.
She dances with grace.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
malibutut ne tubtubaren te kenè pad egkeen te pegpasalamat te Manama te pegkeen.
a short prayer before a meal thanking God for the food.
Sem.Dom.: keyterms; GSL1376-1400
