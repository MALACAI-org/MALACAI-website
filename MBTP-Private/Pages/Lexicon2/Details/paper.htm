paper
/pay-per/
 
n-uc.
 
1.
pepil
Eng. pepil
the flexible, flat material made from processed wood. You often write on it. It is counted as pieces or sheets (e.g., two sheets of paper).
 
n-ct.
 
2.
nasulatan te me guhuren ne pepil
Eng. nasulatan te me guhuren ne pepil
a newspaper.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
sinulat ne egnengnengen
Eng. sinulat ne egnengnengen
a long piece of writing about something that you have studied.
I wrote a paper about mosquitoes for my science class.
 
n-ct.
 
4.
sinulat ne me insè eyew te peggeram
Eng. sinulat ne me insè eyew te peggeram
an exam.
Please leave your exam papers on the table when you have finished.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0526-0550
 
 
papers
 
n-pl.
me keilangan ne pepil ne natahuan te kanengnengan
Your papers are pieces of paper with important personal information on them (e.g., your birth certificate).
Keep your papers in a safe place.
