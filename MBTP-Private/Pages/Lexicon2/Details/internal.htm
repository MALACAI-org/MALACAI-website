internal
/in-ter-nəl/
 
adj.
 
1.
diyè te diralem te etew wey ke minsan nekey
Eng. diyè te diralem
Something internal relates to the inside of something (e.g., the body, a machine).
He received internal injuries in the accident: his stomach and lungs were damaged.
 
adj.
 
2.
diyè te diralem te suman-suman wey eggeramen
Eng. diyè te diralem
Something internal relates to your mind or feelings.
On the outside she looked happy, but internally she was very unhappy.
Egtengtengan nu sikandin ne mahale-galè, piru ka diralem din warad iya kahalaan.
She had a strong internal struggle as she tried to decide what to do.
 
adj.
 
3.
egkeyitabù seled te nasud wey ke punduk.
Something internal relates to the things that happen inside a country or group.
There was a lot of internal fighting in the family.
Sem.Dom.: AWL4
