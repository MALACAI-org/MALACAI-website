apart
/a-part/
 
adj, adv.
nekegdiyù; nekegtalipag
Eng. nekegdiyù; nekegtalipag
If two things are apart, they are not close together.
The two houses are about ten meters apart.
Ka daruwa ne baley ney nekegdiyù te daruwa ne mitrus.
 
 
apart from
warè labet
Eng. warè labet
You use apart from to say that you are not including something.
I sold all my animals apart from my carabao.
Migbelegyè ku langun ka me mananap ku warè labet te kalabew.
I have nothing in my bag apart from a water bottle.
