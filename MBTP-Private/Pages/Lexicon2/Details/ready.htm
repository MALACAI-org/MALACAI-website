ready
/red-ee/
 
adj.
 
1.
nataggel
Eng. nataggel
If someone is ready, they can do something right now. If something is ready, it can be used now.
He's not ready yet, so we need to wait.
Warè pad nataggel sikandin, sika ka keilangan ne egtaharan te pad.
 
adj.
 
2.
nakataggel
Eng. nakataggel
If you are ready to do something, you are willing and happy to do it.
He is very ready to help us if we ask.
Layun nakataggel sikandin ne egbulig ke egpangananey ki kandin.
 
vt.
 
3.
egtaggel
Eng. egtaggel
If you ready something, you cause it to be ready.
She readied our bags for our trip.
Migtaggel din ka me kemuyut ney eyew te peggipanew ney.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0526-0550
 
 
readiness
/red-ee-nəss/
 
n-uc.
1 pegkataggel; 2 pegpakataggel
Eng. pegkataggel; pegpakataggel
the state of being ready.
Thank you for your readiness to help.
