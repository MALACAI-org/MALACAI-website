seek
 
vt.
 
1.
egpamitew
Eng. egpamitew
If you seek something, you try to find and get it.
He went to the city to seek work.
Miggendiyè sikandin te siyudad eyew te pegpamitew te trabahu.
We are seeking a solution to our problem.
 
vt.
 
2.
eggeraman eggimuwa
Eng. eggeraman eggimuwa
If you seek to do something, you try to do it.
I'm seeking to finish this project tonight.
Eggeraman ku te eg-impus seini ne himuwen kunteen karusileman.
Irreg.  sought, sought.
