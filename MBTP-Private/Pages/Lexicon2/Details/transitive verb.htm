transitive verb
 
n-ct.
weil ne due elin eglalahen wey egpakarawat te lalag. Due etew wey ke butang eggimu te weil, wey due degma etew wey ke butang ne egpakarawat te weil.
a verb which has both a subject and an object. There is a person or thing which does the action, and also a person or thing which receives the action.
He kicked the ball ['He' is the subject, and 'the ball' is the object].
Insipè din ka bula ['din' ka eglalahen, ne 'ka bula' ka butang ne egdawat te weil].
