series
/seer-reez/
 
n-ct.
pegsinsinundul
Eng. pegsinsinundul
a group of things which are one after the other.
She tried a series of medicines before she found one which helped.
Miggeraman din pegsinsinundula te eg-inum ka bawì ne wey rin pad nataheyi ne duen bes sabeka ne klasi ne egpakabawì iya.
