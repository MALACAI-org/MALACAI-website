bank2
/bængk/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
tahuanan wey angayanan te seleppì; bangku
Eng. bangku
bangku
an organization which looks after money. People can keep money there or borrow money.
I have an account with Metrobank, and I put in money every month.
 
vt.
 
2.
egtahù te seleppì
Eng. egtahù te seleppì
If you bank money or a cheque, you take it to a bank to put it in a bank account.
He tries to bank part of his pay every month.
