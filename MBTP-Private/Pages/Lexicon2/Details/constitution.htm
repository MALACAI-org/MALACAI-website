constitution
/kon-sti-too-shən/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
kasuluhan ne baad te minsan nekey
Eng. kasuluhan ne baad
The constitution of something is the parts which it is made of.
What is the constitution of this area? How many different tribes are there?
Nekey ka kasuluhan kayi ne inged? Kamenu kasalig ka tribu kayi?
 
n-ct.
 
2.
me kukuman
Eng. kukuman, me
The constitution of a country or organization is the set of rules which it follows.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
kanekalan te lawa
Eng. kanekalan
Your constitution is the health of your body.
I don't get sick often, because I have a strong constitution.
Kena a egderalu layun, su due kanekalan te peglelawa ku.
Sem.Dom.: AWL1
