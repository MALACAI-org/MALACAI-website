place
 
n-ct.
 
1.
nateil ne lugar
Eng. lugar
a particular point somewhere.
I have pictures in a few places around the house.
Due me litratu ku te nenateil ne lugar kayi te baley.
 
vt.
 
2.
egtahù; egsabuk
Eng. egtahù; egsabuk
If you place something somewhere, you put it there carefully.
She placed the flowers in a vase.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
nateil ne ugpaanan
Eng. ugpaanan
a seat or a space where someone can sit or stand.
He sat in his usual place at the table.
 
n-ct.
 
4.
nakuwa (meyitenged te pegdeeg)
Eng. nakuwa
In a competition, your place is your rank (e.g., first, second).
He achieved second place in his class.
Nakuwa rin ka ikaruwa ne baad te pegkamateu diyè te klasi ran.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0101-0125
