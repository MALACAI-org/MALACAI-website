man
/mæhn/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
lukes (buyag-buyag e)
Eng. lukes
a male adult human
My brother is only fourteen; he is not a man yet.
Pl. men
 
n-uc.
 
2.
me etew
Eng. etew, me
the human species, including both males and females. Also called mankind.
Man is very different from the animals.
