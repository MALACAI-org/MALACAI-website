conflict
/kon-flikt (n); kon-flikt (v)/
 
n-uc, n-ct.
 
1.
ehetanan wey ke apulanan
Eng. ehetanan; apulanan
fighting or arguing between people or groups.
There was a conflict between two men at the market yesterday.
Due apulanan te daruwa ne lukes gabii diyè te palingki.
The committee doesn't work well because there is a lot of conflict between committee members.
Life is very hard in regions where there is military conflict.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
kaleinan te daruwa ne butang ne egpekeyimu kandan ne kenè egpekeg-eleg ka matagsabeka
Eng. kaleinan
an important difference between two things which causes them to not fit together.
There is a conflict between the information in the two books, so I don't know which one to follow.
Due kaleinan te me kanengnengan te daruwa ne libru, sika ka wara a nakataha ke hendei ka egpalintutuuwen ku.
 
v-recip.
 
3.
egpekeglein
Eng. egpekeglein
If something conflicts with another thing, they don't fit together well.
They seldom see each other because their work schedules conflict.
Marelag dè egpekegkita sikandan su nekeglein ka timpu te trabahu ran.
Sem.Dom.: AWL5
