peak
/peek/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
puntul; iddus
Eng. puntul; iddus
the pointed top of a mountain.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
katammanan; pupul
Eng. katammanan; pupul
The peak of something is the time or place when it is highest or best.
 
vi.
 
3.
egpatamanen; egtamanen
Eng. egpatamanen; egtamanen
If someone peaks, they reach their best level of ability. If something (e.g. a company's monthly profit) peaks, it reaches its highest level.
 
adj.
 
4.
keepusan
Eng. keepusan
Peak time is the busiest time (e.g., peak season for tourism, peak hour for traffic).
Sem.Dom.: extra
