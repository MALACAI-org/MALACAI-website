convene
/kon-veen/
 
vi.
 
1.
egpatemtemuwey eyew te pegpalallalahey
Eng. egpatemtemuwey
If a group of people convene, they meet together for a formal meeting.
The committee will convene next Monday.
Ka me nabehayan te himuwen egmatemtemuwey te seup ne Lunis.
 
vt.
 
2.
egpatawag te kalibulungan
Eng. egpatawag te kalibulungan
If you convene a meeting, you call people to meet together for it.
The leader convened a meeting of the committee.
Ka ahalen migpeumew te kalibulungan para te me nabehayan te me himuwenen.
