nor
 
conj.
kenè...wey minsan; minsan...wey ke...kenè
Eng. kenè...wey minsan; minsan...wey ke...kenè
You use nor to talk about two things, to mean 'not one and not the other'. You sometimes use it together with neither.
Neither Coke nor Pepsi is very healthy.
Minsan Kuk wey ke Pipsi kenen meupiya.
I didn't forget and nor did my sister.
Wara a nalingew wey minsan ka atebey ku.
