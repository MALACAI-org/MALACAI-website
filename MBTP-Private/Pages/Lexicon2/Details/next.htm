next
 
adj, pron, adv.
 
1.
seup; egsinundul
Eng. seup; egsinundul
The next thing comes after another thing.
She missed the bus so she needed to wait for the next one.
Namewuri sikandin te terak sika ka keilangan sikandin ne egtatahad te seup ne terak.
He came first in the race, and I came next.
 
adj.
 
2.
sinundul; kilid; hulid; dulug
Eng. sinundul; kilid; hulid; dulug
If you choose one thing in a row of things, the next thing is the one beside it.
She lives in the next house.
Mig-ugpè sikandin te sinundul ne baley.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0276-0300
