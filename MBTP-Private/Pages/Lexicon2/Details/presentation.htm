presentation
/prez-ən-tay-shən/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
pegbehey
Eng. pegbehey
the action of giving something to someone.
 
n-uc.
 
2.
pegpeila-ila
Eng. pegpeila-ila
the action of introducing someone or something to a group.
 
n-uc.
 
3.
pegpakita; pegpapitew
Eng. pegpakita; pegpapitew
the action of showing something.
 
n-ct.
 
4.
himuwen (meyitenged te pegbehey te minsan nekey)
Eng. himuwen; sirimunyas
sirimuniyas
a formal ceremony in which someone receives something.
We attended the presentation for the high school graduates.
 
n-ct.
 
5.
pegpapitew (meyitenged te ulahingen, sayew wey duma pad)
Eng. pegpapitew
an event such as such as a concert, where people perform.
All the parents are invited to the dancing presentation at the school.
 
n-uc.
 
6.
ke nekey ka egkeilingan (meupiya naa wey ke mareet)
Eng. ke nekey ka egkeilingan
The presentation of a person or thing is what they look like.
Your project should not just have good research; attractive presentation is also important.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0151-0175
