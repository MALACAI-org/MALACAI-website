astonish
/a-ston-ish/
 
vt.
egpakapeinu-inu
Eng. egpakapeinu-inu
If something astonishes you, it causes you to feel very surprised.
Their ability to carry very heavy sacks astonishes me.
Egpakapeinu-inu ka katuenan din ne egpekeeleg te mabehat ne saku.
 
 
astonished
/a-ston-isht/
 
adj.
neinu-inu
Eng. neinu-inu
If someone is astonished, they feel very surprised.
I was astonished to hear that the big tree had fallen down in the storm.
Sem.Dom.: GSL1801-1825
 
 
astonishment
 
n-uc.
pegkeinu-inu
Eng. pegkeinu-inu
a feeling of being very surprised.
