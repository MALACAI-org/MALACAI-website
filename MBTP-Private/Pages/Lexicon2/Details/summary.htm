summary
/sam-ə-ree/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
minalepet ne lalahen wey ke sinulat
Eng. minalepet ne lalahen wey ke sinulat
a short piece of speaking or writing, which gives the main ideas from what was said or written before it.
He gave a summary of the things that they had decided in the meeting.
Migbehey sikandin te minalepet ne me puhawang te miglihad ne kalibulungan.
Don't copy the whole piece of writing. Just make a summary of it so that you don't forget the main points.
The last paragraph of your writing should be a summary of the main part of your essay.
 
adj.
 
2.
malabundus wey warad labet te duma
Eng. malabundus wey warad labet te duma
A summary action is done immediately, and doesn't include all the things which should be done with it.
The mechanic did a summary check of the car, but didn't check it carefully.
