alarm
/a-larm/
 
n-uc.
 
1.
sahuhunè ne eggeramen ne liyas tenged su dilikadu
a sudden feeling of fear because of danger.
 
vt.
 
2.
egpataha; egpangilam; egpakahuhud
Eng. egpataha; egpangilam; egpakahuhud
If something alarms you, it makes you feel suddenly very afraid or worried.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
dahing te pegpanengneng te due mareet
Eng. dahing te pegpanengneng te due mareet
a sound which tells people that there is danger.
I woke up late because I didn't hear the alarm this morning.
Namewuri a ne miggimata su warè ku narineg ka dahing te pegpanengneng te seini ne maselem.
 
n-ct.
 
4.
parahingen eyew te pegpanengneng te due dilikadu iling te egkasilaban wey ke duma pad
a thing which produces a noise to tell people that there is danger, or to wake someone up.
She was smoking inside the building, and it caused the fire alarm to sound.
Sem.Dom.: extra
Status: nc3
