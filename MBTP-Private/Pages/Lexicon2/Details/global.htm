global
/gloh-bəl/
 
adj.
 
1.
due kalabetan te langun ne inged.
Eng. langun ne inged, due kalabetan te; pangkinatibuk-an
pangkinatibuk-an
You use global to talk about something related to the whole world (e.g., a global financial problem).
That is a global problem, not just a problem in our country.
Pangkinatibuk-an sika ne prublima, kenè ne kayi rè te nasud ta.
 
adj.
 
2.
langun te minsan nekey wey ke labet ka langun ne baad.
Eng. langun
You use global to talk about the whole of something, with all the parts included.
He has a global understanding of the system, because he has studied all the different parts of it.
Sem.Dom.: AWL7
