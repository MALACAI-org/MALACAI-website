terms
 
n-pl.
 
1.
klasiyan te rilasyun
Eng. klasiyan te rilasyun
If two people or groups are on particular terms, that is the kind of relationship that they have (e.g. on friendly terms, on bad terms).
I am on first-name terms with my boss, because we were classmates before.
 
n-pl.
 
2.
nenakatahù ne kasabutan
Eng. nenakatahù ne kasabutan
When people or groups have an agreement, the terms are the things that each one has to do or accept.
According to the terms of his contract, he cannot work more than 40 hours per week.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0426-0450 - related; plural nouns
