follow
/foll-oh/
 
vt.
 
1.
egsinundul
Eng. egsinundul
If you follow someone, you move after them, going the same way they they are going.
Why are you following me?
 
vt.
 
2.
eg-ikul
Eng. eg-ikul
If you follow a path or line, you move along that path or line.
Follow the highway until you get to Valencia.
 
vi, vt.
 
3.
egpakasinundul
Eng. egpakasinundul
If something follows another thing, it comes after that other thing.
An afternoon storm sometimes follows a hot morning.
 
vt.
 
4.
eg-ikul te panulù; egtuman
Eng. eg-ikul; egtuman
If you follow instructions, you do what the instructions say to do.
If you take medicine, you should follow the doctor's instructions carefully.
 
vi, vt.
 
5.
egpakanengneng; egpakasabut
Eng. egpakanengneng; egpakasabut
If you follow something that someone says, you understand it.
Sorry, I don't follow. Could you please explain it to me again?
Usage level: informal
Sem.Dom.: GSL0151-0175
Status: ready
