distinct
/də-stinkt/
 
adj.
 
1.
nekegsuwey; nekeglein
Eng. nekegsuwey; nekeglein
Something distinct is different and separate from other things.
There are two distinct yokes in this egg.
Due daruwa ne natù ne nekegsuwey te seini ne atelug.
 
adj.
 
2.
mating-ew; egkamelmelehi; mahaan egkakita, egkarineg, wey egkataheyi
Eng. mating-ew; egkamelmelehi; egkakita, mahaan; egkarineg, mahaan; egkataheyi, mahaan
Something distinct is clear and easy to see, hear or know.
He saw the distinct shadow of a snake on the fence.
Nakakita sikandin te mating-ew ne alung te uled diyè te alad.
Sem.Dom.: AWL2
 
 
indistinct
 
adj.
not distinct (sense 2).
