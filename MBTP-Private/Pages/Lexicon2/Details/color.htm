color
/kal-er/
 
n-ct, n-uc.
 
1.
batek
Eng. batek
What's your favorite color?
 
vt.
 
2.
egbatek
Eng. egbatek
If you color something such as a picture, you put color on it.
The children used crayons to color the pictures.
She colors her hair with dye.
 
vt.
 
3.
Ke due egpakapabatek te suman-suman nu meyitenged te minsan nekey, egpakapanulud sika keykew te pegsuman-suman ke nekey-a sika.
If something colors your thinking about something, it causes you to think in a particular way about it.
His bad experience at the school colored his opinion of all children.
Ka mareet ne nabaybayaan din diyè te iskuyalaan [iskuyla͡an] ka nakabehey te nateil ne puhawang din diyè te langun ne me batè.
