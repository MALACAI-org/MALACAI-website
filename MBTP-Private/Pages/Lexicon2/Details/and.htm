and
/æhnd/
 
conj.
 
1.
wey
Eng. wey
You use and to say that there is another thing
I bought rice, salt and fish.
 
conj.
 
2.
pegkeimpus
Eng. pegkeimpus
You use and if one thing happens after another thing, or if one thing causes another thing.
They said goodbye and went home.
Migakahi sikandan te kayi key e, pegkeimpus mig-ulì e te baley.
She saw the snake and screamed.
Sem.Dom.: conjunctions; GSL0001-0025
