factor
/fæk-tor/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
aliddanan
Eng. aliddanan
something which helps to produce a result.
The good weather was a factor in our wonderful day at the beach.
Ka meupiya ne aldew ka aliddanan ne mateles ka aldew diyè te ilis te dahat.
One factor in the team's success was their excellent goal defence.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
numiru ne igpakatayimis wey igpakadibayid
a number by which you multiply or divide another number.
Our sales increased by a factor of 5, so that we sold 100 units per month instead of 20 units.
Sem.Dom.: AWL1
 
 
factor in
 
v-phrasal.
eglabet
Eng. eglabet
If you factor something in, you include it when you decide something, or work out an amount.
When you calculate the total cost, you need to factor in the cost of food.
Ke egseel ka te langun ne bali, keilangan ne eglabeten ka bali te pegkeen.
