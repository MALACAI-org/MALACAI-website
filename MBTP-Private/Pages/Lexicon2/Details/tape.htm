tape
 
n-uc.
 
1.
manipis ne silupin ne egdekket ka limang; parekket
Eng. silupin ne egdekket; parekket
a thin strip of plastic which is sticky on one side (e.g., scotch tape).
 
vt.
 
2.
egtahuan te parekket
Eng. egtahuan te parekket
If you tape something, you put tape on it to stick it somewhere.
He taped the box so that it would not open.
Migtahuan din te parekket ka kaban eyew kenè egkalukatan.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
tip
Eng. tip
a plastic case which has a roll of thin magnetic tape inside it, and which is used for recording something such as sounds or pictures (e.g., cassette tape, video tape).
People listen to music on tapes or CDs.
 
vt.
 
4.
egtahù te ulahingen wey ke salida diyè te tip
If you tape something, you record the sounds or pictures onto a video or cassette tape.
He taped a TV program so that he could watch it again.
Sem.Dom.: AWL6
