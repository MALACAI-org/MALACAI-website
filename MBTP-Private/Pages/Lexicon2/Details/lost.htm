lost
 
vt.
 
1.
past and past participle of lose
 
adj.
 
2.
natarin; nalaag
Eng. natarin; nalaag
If something is lost, you don't have it any more, and can't get it back.
 
adj.
 
3.
nalingawan
Eng. nalingawan
If something is lost, you don't know where it is.
 
adj.
 
4.
nalaag te bayaan wey ke egpabulusan
Eng. nalaag te bayaan
If someone is lost, they don't know where they are, and don't know where to go.
