atmosphere
/æt-mə-sfeer/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
hawhawanan; kawkawangan
Eng. hawhawanan; kawkawangan
The atmosphere of a planet is the mixture of gases and air around it.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
kalamag te nateil ne lugar
Eng. kalamag te nateil ne lugar
the air in a particular place.
The atmosphere of that city is very dirty and smoky.
Ka kalamag te siyudad amana ne malindit wey ebelen.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
geyinawa ne eggeramen te nateil ne punduk wey ke lugar
The atmosphere in a particular group or place is the general feeling that is there.
There was an atmosphere of excitement in the group while they waited for the game to start.
Due geyinawa te pegkabagget te punduk te pegtetahad te pegbunsud te galew.
Sem.Dom.: extra
