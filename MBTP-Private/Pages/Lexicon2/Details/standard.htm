standard
/stæhn-derd/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
seked te keupiya te minsan nekey
Eng. seked te keupiya
a level of quality; how good something is.
The standard of his work is very high.
Ka seked te trabahu rin amana rè ne titikangi.
Since he got a job, his family's standard of living has improved.
Ligkat te nakatrabahu sikandin, ka keugpè te pamilya rin migmeupiye e.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
minsan nekey ne egpakita ke nekey ka keilangan ne eggimuwen wey egkahamit eyew te pegtengteng te keupiya te trabahu nu.
something which shows you what you need to do, and which is used to check the quality of your work.
This document is the standard that you need to follow when you write your project. It will tell you what you need to include, and we will use it when we check your work.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
pegtuu meyitenged te eleg wey kenè
Eng. pegtuu meyitenged te eleg wey kenè
an idea about what is right and wrong.
He has very high moral standards, so I trust him not to steal from me.
 
adj.
 
4.
malasi; layun
Eng. malasi; layun
If something is standard, it is usual and normal.
That is the standard price for rice at the moment.
Sika ka malasi ne bali te behas te kuntee ne panahun.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0576-0600
