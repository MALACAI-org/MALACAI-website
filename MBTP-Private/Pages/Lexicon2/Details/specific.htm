specific
/spə-si-fik/
 
adj.
 
1.
egkamelmelehi
Eng. egkamelmelehi
If words are specific, they talk about something very clearly and exactly.
My father gave me very specific instructions about how to fix it.
Ka amey ku migbehey te amana egkamelmelehan ne panulù ke egmenuwen te eg-upiya seini.
 
adj.
 
2.
nateil ne minsan nekey
Eng. nateil
If you talk about a specific thing, you are talking about that thing, and not other things.
On which specific day do you want to go there?
Nekey ka nateil ne aldew ne egkeupian ka ne eggendiyè.
Sem.Dom.: AWL1
