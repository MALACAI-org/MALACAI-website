index
/in-deks/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
listaan [lista͡an] te me lalag diyè te peka te libru ne egpataha ke hendei egpamitawa ka lalag wey ka uluwan.
a list at the back of a book which tells you where you can find words and topics in the book.
I couldn't remember which part of the book talked about Bonifacio, so I checked the index.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
listaan [lista͡an] te me butang iling te unag wey ke bayad ne egpakita ke pilaa ka nabalbalawan te peglihad te me timpu.
a list of things such as prices or wages which shows how much they change over time.
The Consumer Price Index shows the average prices of things.
Sem.Dom.: AWL6
