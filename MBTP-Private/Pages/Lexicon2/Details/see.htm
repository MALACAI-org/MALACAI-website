see
 
vt.
 
1.
egpakakita
Eng. egpakakita
If you see something, your eyes get a picture of it.
I saw the cat as it ran past.
Nakakita a te iiyali ne miyew ne migpallahuy.
See sight
 
vt.
 
2.
egpakanengneng, egpakasabut (meyitenged te pegpakataha)
Eng. egpakanengneng; egpakasabut
If you see, you understand.
Do you see what I mean?
Nakanengneng ka ke nekey ka igpasabut ku?
 
vt.
 
3.
egleuy
Eng. egleuy
If you see someone (e.g., a doctor), you visit them.
The midwife told me to see a doctor.
Egpaleuyen a te manguyamu diyè te duktur.
 
vt.
 
4.
egpaniguru
Eng. egpaniguru
If you see that something happens, you do something to make sure that it happens.
My mothers always sees that I don't forget my umbrella.
Egpaniguru ka iney ku ne kenè ku egkalingawan ka payung ku.
 
vt.
 
5.
egtengtengan pad; egpatpateen pad (meyitenged te pegbehey te puhawang)
Eng. egtengtengan pad; egpatpateen pad
If you say that you will see, you mean that you will decide later.
I'll see if I can visit you tomorrow; it depends on my work.
Egtengtengan ku pad ke egpakaleuy a kuna kaaselem; iyan ku egkataharan ka himu ku.
Irreg.  saw, seen.
