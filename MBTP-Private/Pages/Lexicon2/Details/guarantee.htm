guarantee
/gæh-rən-tee/
 
vt.
 
1.
egsaad; egpasalig
Eng. egsaad; egpasalig
If you guarantee that something will happen, you promise that it will certainly happen.
I guarantee that you will enjoy your time there.
Egsaad a te egkahalè ka te peg-uugpè nu diyè.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
egpakabehey te pegpalintutuu
Eng. egpakabehey te pegpalintutuu
If something is a guarantee of another thing, it causes you to be sure about the other thing.
Rain is a guarantee that it will be muddy.
Ka uran ka egpakabehey te pegpalintutuu ta ne due basak.
 
vt.
 
3.
egpasalig te pegbulig ke due prublima (meyitenged te kumpanya); eggerentiya
Eng. egpasalig; eggerentiya
If a company guarantees something that they make, they promise that they will help you if there are problems with it (e.g., they promise to fix it, or give you a new one).
 
n-ct.
 
4.
sinulat ne puun te kumpanya ne egkahi te egbulig ke due igkasahukul wey ke prublima te butang ne imbeli nu puun te kumpanya; gerentiya
Eng. gerentiya
a piece of writing from a company saying that they will help you if you have any problems with something that you buy from them.
This cellphone has a 6 month guarantee.
Sem.Dom.: AWL7
