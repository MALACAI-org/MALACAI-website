Genealogy

631

Salamat te geyinawa nu

Rosalyn U. Lacubay

Aldew aldewk geyinawa nu keddi
Wareg keyimu ku ke wa ka Hisus
Keg kapeled e ma, egtinaween a
Weyg pasasindeg an keykew.

Salamat te geyinawa nu
Salamat de te keyid-u nu keddi
Minsan kena an eleg Hisus
Inggeyinawaan an keykew.
