Genealogy

620

Layahi key, Hisus

G

RUL

1. E Hisus, iling ka te sulu
Neg layag te langun ne inged
Warad e delem kayit kanami
Impapitew nuk kamalehetan
Layahi key, layahi key.

Layag ka Hisus te langun keet-etawan
Behayi key te hapuyt pusung
Timuli nu degmak pegpalintutuu ney
Eyew eglayag key iling keykew.

2. E Hisus, parani key keykew
Kayi key e te keykewn tangkaan
Pasaylua key te me sale ney
Awaa nuk isip neyn mareet
Layahi key, layahi key.

3. E Hisus, layahi keyn keykew
Eyewg pekeiling key e keykew
Weyg kakita ka layag te langun
Eyew ka langun egsaye keykew
Layahi key, layahi key.
