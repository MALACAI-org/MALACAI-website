Genealogy

108

Keupianan te pegsalig ku

Blessed assurance

D

9/8

RUL

1. Keupianant pegsalig ku
Si Hisus iyak Manluluwas ku
Keupianant gimukud ku
Ne naluwas te langesa rin.

Seini ka ulahingen ku
Dayanen tak Manluluwas ku
Seini ka ulahingen ku
Dayanen tak Manluluwas ku

2. Igkahale ku ka pegsalig
Kakita ku ka katelesan din
Ne nahuhud puun te langit
Ne geyinawa te Manama.

3. Pekeyimmeley ka migtuu
Wey due kahale dumak Hisus
Te keddiey ne me peg-ampu
Hipenuen at geyinawa rin.
