Genealogy

244

Hisus, egsaad a

O Jesus, I have promised

G

4/4

RGN

1. Hisus egpanaad a te peg-ikul keykew
Parani ka keddiey alukuy kus keykew
Ware igkaaldek ku keg gen-gen ka keddi
Kena ad egkalaag keg duma ka keddi.

2. Ipaheram nu keddi ne marani ke re
Masulug kag balabag neg lit-ag keddiey
Layun de egparani kag kuntere keddi
Hisus iparani a paandilungi a.

3. Migsaad ka te langun iya ne anak nu
Egduma ka kandan diye te ugpaan nu
Hisus egpanaad a neg pamakayen ka
Behayi at kanekal te peg-ikul keykew.
