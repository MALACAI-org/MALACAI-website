Genealogy

56

Langesa rin ne mahal

There is power in the blood

Bb (A)

4/4

LTA

1. Egkeupian ka neg kaluwas
Te mahal ne langesan Hisus
Parani ka ki Hisus kuntee
Wey salihi nu re sikandin.

Kein-inuwan ka geem
Te langesan Hisus
Kein-inuwan ka geem
Te mahal ne langesa rin.

2. Nabehayan ke naat kanekal
Te mahal ne langesan Hisus
Parani ka kunteet Kalbaryu
Wey salihi nu res kandin.

3. Keupian ka neg kalinisan
Te mahal ne langesan Hisus
Ka malindit eglinisan din e
Wey salihi nu res kandin.

4. Egkeupian ka neg parani
Te mahal ne langesan Hisus
Kuntee dayana nud sikandin
Wey salihi nu res kandin.
