Genealogy

67

Salig ki re

Only trust him

G

4/4

LTA

1. Parani kewk nabehatan
Tenged de te sale
Behayan ki te himeley
Salig ki re kandin.

Salig ki re, salig ki re
Salig kik Hisus
Egluwas sikandin keykew
Egluwas kanta.

2. Imbeheyk langesan Hisus
Imbehey e
Parani kew re ki Hisus
Neg maawang ka.

3. Si Hisus de iyan dalan
Peendiyet amey ta
Salig ka kandin kuntee
Egbulihan ka.

4. Imbeheyn Hisus lawa rin
Te pegpeyimatey
Langun neg palintutuu
Egkaluwas iya.
