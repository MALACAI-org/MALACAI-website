Genealogy

162

Egluwas si Hisus

Jesus saves

G

3/4

LTA

1. Narineg tak guhuren, neg luwas si Hisus
Igpataha te langun, neg luwas si Hisus
Uyanen tak guhuren diye te langun inged
Tumanen tak suhu din, egluwas si Hisus.

2. Igguhud tak guhuren, neg luwas si Hisus
Egluwas si Hisus te me makasesale
Ka tane wey ka dahat egdengan te pegsaye
Wey eghale-gale ki, sug luwas si Hisus.

3. Migpakamateys kandin, ka egluwas kanta
Piru neuyag sikandin, ka egluwas kanta
Ke due igkasasew ta, kene ki egkalaggew
Sabeka kit pegsaye, egluwas si Hisus.

4. Narineg ta te langun, egluwas si Hisus
Diye te langun inged, egluwas si Hisus
Ka kaluwasan ta iya ki Hisus deg kaangken
Ulahing kit pegsaye, sug luwas si Hisus.
