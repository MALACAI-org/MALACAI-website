Genealogy

134

Egkasasew rin

Does Jesus Care

Db (C)

6/8

LTA

1. Ke masakit ka geyinawa nu
Puun te me kalised
Wey masalig ka igkengasasew
Egkasasew bu red mules Hisus?

Uya iya, kasasew red
Wey egkeyid-us kandin
Ke egkalaggew ki wey egkasasew
Sengeg kasasew rin.

2. Keg pakabaye kit marusilem
Ne malised ka dalan
Wey nakasabsabeka pad man-e
Egkasasew bu red mules Hisus?

3. Ke due timpu ne egginguma
Neg keepes kit langun
Su nekeyimu ki te mareet
Egkasasew bu red mules Hisus?

4. Ne emun ke egmandaluwan ki
Ne subla ne masumpit
Weyg patayan kit pinalangge ta
Egkasasew bu red mules Hisus?
