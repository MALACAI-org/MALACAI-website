The letter by Paul to

The (ones) from_Colosse

Introduction

This letter by Paul (MS Pablu) he sent to the believers in Colosse (MS Kulusas). Colosse, was a (LIT one) town in Asya Minur located there east of Ipisu. Paul had_ not yet _been_able_to_visit them but he was very concerned and he sent helpers (to them) from Ipisu.

Paul heard that there are false teachers who are teaching the believers in Christ in Colosse. They taught that if a person wants to be_saved and wants his knowing God (MS Manama) to be deep, his/her believing/faith is not enough it_is_said. For they also say that he/she needs to still/also obey other restrictions and beliefs. And it's also necessary that he/she worships angels.

Therefore Paul wrote to the believers in Colosse and informed them of the truth about Jesus Christ (MS Hisu Kristu). He told them that Jesus (MS Hisus) indeed is the (one who) made all that we(incl) see, therefore he is greater than the angels, and of yet others that can't be seen. The Godlikeness of his Father can also be seen in him, and there's_no other source of life for believers other than him. So then, it's_necessary that believers, don't follow religions and the customs/behavior of people, but rather, it's_necessary that Christ only is followed.

The true life in Christ
