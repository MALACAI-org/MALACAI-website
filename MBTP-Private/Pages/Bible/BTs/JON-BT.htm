The story about

Jonah

Introduction

This document (LIT reading), tells about a prophet called Jonah (MS Hunas). He lived there in Israil (MS Israil) in the north (LIT on the left side) of Israil, about 250 years after Dabid had_finished reigning. This document, is not like other stories concerning prophesying, because it explains about the fate of one prophet who did not obey God's (MS Manama) command to him to go_there to the city of Ninibi. He didn't obey what he was commanded because he knew that God wouldn't continue to punish that city if the people turn_from their sins. But due to the wonders that he experienced, he was persuaded to go to Ninibi and (he) told what_ God _had_caused_ him _to_tell. Finally, he got_upset and hurt_feelings because what_ he _said to the people that their place would_be_punished by God wasn't fulfilled.

(It is) also told in this document (LIT reading) that everything that God created obeys/believes him like water, wild_animal, air/wind and others. But above all, (it is) also told here that God is merciful and his love is great, for even the enemy of his people, he forgave (them) and he saved (them) at their turning_from their sins.
