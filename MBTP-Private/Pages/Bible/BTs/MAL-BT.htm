This was_written about five hundred (500) years before Jesus Christ was_born. At that time, the rebuilding of the temple of Hirusalim was completed. The main (LIT big) purpose of the prophet is to encourage the sacrificers and to make_known to the people to obey what they have agreed with the Lord God.
