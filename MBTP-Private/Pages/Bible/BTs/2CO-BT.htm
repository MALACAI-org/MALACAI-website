Introduction

The second letter by Paul (MS Pablu) to The (ones) from_Kurintu, he wrote from Masidunya. He wrote this at a time when his relationship with the believers in Kurintu wasn't very good.

Here in the first part of this document, Paul told about his relationship to the believers there in Kurintu. He told his concern about the believers shaming and disobeying him. And he also told of his joy that that anger of his, (it) bore_fruit in the believers turning_from their sin(s) and restoring_[reciprocally] their good relationship (LIT breath). And he requested the believers to continue to collect their gift as a help for the poor believers in Hirusalim (MS Hirusalim).

In the final four chapters, Paul defended his apostleship to those who claimed that only they were the true apostle(s). They were those who accused Paul of not (hearsay) being a true apostle.
