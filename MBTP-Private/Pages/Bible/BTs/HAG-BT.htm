Haggai

Introduction

This document, gathers words/sayings from the Lord God (MS Manama) which was caused_to_tell by prophet Hagiyu. This happened in the year 520 before Kristu was_born. At that time, the people of God that had_been_captured(pl) into Babilunya returned_home(pl) to Hirusalim. But even_though it had_been_long since they returned_home to Hirusalim, they hadn't yet redone building the temple. Therefore, all the contents of this document, encouraged them to really redo building the temple. And the Lord God promised that he would prosper and give good living to those people who changed (their) customs and obeyed him.

The command of God for redoing the temple

Sirubabil (was) the child of Salatiil, and Huswi (was) the child also of Hihusadak.

The obeying of the command of God

12 And Sirubabil and Prominent Sacrificer Huswi, along_with all who returned from being_enslaved in Babilunya, obeyed the word of the Lord their God. They feared the Lord God, so_that they obeyed that_was_spoken by the Hagiyu, the prophet commanded by the Lord. 13 And Hagiyu storied the word of the Lord God to the people going, “I promise that I will_accompany you(pl).” 14 And the Lord God caused_ Sirubabil the governor of Huda _to_be_encouraged to make a/the temple, and Huswi the Prominent Sacrificer, and all the people. And they started making a temple for their Very Strong/Powerful Lord, their God 15 on the twenty-fourth (24) day of the sixth month. It was in the second year of the kinging by Daryu.

The questioning of the sacrificers by Hagiyu

10 On the twenty fourth (24) day of the ninth month of the second year of the kinging by Daryu, the Lord God spoke again with_the prophet Hagiyu that went_like_this, 11 “Ask(sg) the sacrificers what_ they _have_to_say about this: 12 If there_was a person who took meat that was_to_be_sacrificed and wrapped in clothes, would the bread, the main_course, the wine, the cooking_oil and the other food become_holy that touched it?”

And the sacrificers answered, “No.”

13 [xr]And prophet Hagiyu asked again, “If there_is a person who has_become_unclean because (he/she) touched a corpse, and also touches something_to_be_sacrificed, does that something_sacrificed he had touched also become_unclean?”

And the sacrificers answered, “Yes, it also becomes_unclean.”

14 And Hagiyu said, “Therefore the Lord God said that the people of Israil are also like this. They are unclean in front of him because of what they did,[fn] and so all their sacrifices have_become_unclean there in front of the Lord.

15 “Therefore consider(pl) what_happened to_you(pl) before you(pl) began the redoing of the temple of the Lord God. 16 At your(pl) going to the field, hoping that you(pl) will_be_able_to_get four sacks of wheat, but you(pl) get just two sacks.
