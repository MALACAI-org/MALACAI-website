Ezra

Introduction

The document of/by Isra, (is) like an addition to The documents of Krunikas. It's told here about the returning_home of some of the Hudiyus from being_captured there in Babilunya, and the returning of the living and worshiping there in Hirusalim. This is divided into three: 1/ The sending_ of Siru the king of Pirsya _to_return_home of those first group of Hudiyus from being_taken_captives that are already in Babilunya. 2/ The redoing of building of the temple and the dedication of it, and the returning of the worshiping of God (MS Manama) in Hirusalim. 3/ The returning_home again to Hirusalim of those different group of Hudiyus that were led by Isra, one skilled in the Law of God. He was_able_to_help the Israilitas in returning to their beliefs and customs, so_that the worshiping of the true God wouldn't be_forsaken by_them.
