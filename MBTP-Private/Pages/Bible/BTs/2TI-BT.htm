Introduction

The second letter by Paul (MS Pablu) to Timutiyu (MS Timutiyu), (it) was_written in Ruma at the time he was imprisoned as a result of his preaching about Hisu Kristu. Paul requested Timutiyu to go_there to him before he was_killed (LIT when he had_ not yet _been_killed). And Timutiyu, he was still in Ipisu teaching the believers.

In this letter, Paul advised Timutiyu to be strong/firm so_that he would_be_able_to_suffer/endure and to preach and do what God (MS Manama) caused_ him _to_do even in the midst of suffering/enduring and opposition from other people. He also warned Timutiyu about the bad result (LIT happening) of “foolish and meaningless (LIT without use) arguments” because those will_ruin the people who listen.

Above all, Paul reminded Timutiyu about his life so_that the faith of Paul, his humility, his love, his continuing, and his suffering/enduring in the midst of his persecutions would_become an example for Timutiyu.

He removed the power of death and by_means of the Good Report, he revealed the life that is without end.

11 [xr]I was_chosen by God as an apostle in_order to preach and teach this Good Report. 12 That is the reason that I suffer today. But I am_ not _ashamed, because I know whom I believe_in and I am certain that he has power to guard what he entrusted to me[fn] until the day of his returning. 13 The true word(s) that you(sg) learned from me, teach(sg) like my teaching. 15 You(sg) know that I was_abandoned by all the believers from the province of Asya, including Pigilu and Hirmuginis. 16 And concerning Unisipuru, the Lord will_have mercy on him along_with his family because he encouraged me many_times, and he was also not ashamed of me the imprisoned (one). 17 But rather, he immediately searched for me upon his arrival here in Ruma until he found me. 18 The Lord will_be_merciful to Unisipuru on the day of his returning. You(sg) know that his assistance to me was very great indeed when I was still there in Ipisu.

The good soldier of Kristu Hisus

3 Suffer/Endure(sg) difficulties along_with us(excl) as a good soldier of Kristu Hisus. 4 A soldier who is doing his duty, he can no_longer be preoccupied with his former work because he wants to please his master/commander. 5 And that (one) (who is) included in a race, (he is) not able_to_get his prize if he doesn't obey the law(s)/rule(s) of the race. 6 And the person who is hard_working (NOTE implied agricultural context), (it's) necessary that he be_able_to_be_first to eat_the_first_fruits of his planting. 7 Consider(sg) what_ I _say to you(sg), because the Lord will_help you(sg) so_that you(sg) will_understand all of it.

8 Don't(sg) forget that Hisu Kristu the descendant of King Dabid, (he) came_alive from death. That is the Good Report that I preach. 9 And as a result of my preaching that Good Report, I was persecuted and bound like a doer of evil_things. But the word of God, (it's) not bound. 10 Therefore I will_suffer/endure all difficulties so_that the people whom God chose by_means of Kristu Hisus will_be_saved and will_be_included in his honor without end. 11 The saying is true that goes_like_this:

The good servant of God

14 Cause(sg)_ the believers _to_think_about that, and warn(sg) them in the presence of God to quiet/stop arguing about the meaning of words, because it has no purpose and it will_ just _ruin the ones_who_listen to them. 15 Strive(sg) as hard as you(sg) can in obeying God, so_that he will_be_happy with you(sg). Teach(sg) the truth carefully/completely so_that there_is_nothing to be_ashamed of in your(sg) work. 16 Distance(sg) (yourself) from speech that is without purpose that opposes God because those who do like that, (they) will_distance (themselves) from God. 17 That speech, (it is) like a skin_sore that eats_away. It is comparable to that teaching of Himiniyu and Pilitu. 18 They have already strayed from the truth. They teach that the resurrection of corpses has already passed (hearsay), and they also ruin the faith of some people. 19 [xr]But the truth that is from God, (it) is like a foundation, (it) does not change, and on it is written like this: “The Lord knows his people,” and “Those who acknowledge that they are under_the_rule of the Lord God, (it's) necessary that (they) turn_away from whatever is evil.”

20 There in a beautiful house, there are utensils (LIT things_used) that are made from gold and from silver. And there are also things_made from wood and earth. The expensive_things will_ only _be_used if there_is an important occasion (LIT use), but if none, what is used is just the cheap_things. 21 The person who distances (himself) from evil, (he) is like an expensive utensil that is used by God because he is holy, and will_be_used by his Master God because he is prepared to do good. 22 Distance(sg) (yourself) from the evil desires of young_men. And strive(sg) for righteousness, faith, love, and peace/well-being, along_with all of those who cry_for_help to the Lord with a clean heart. 23 Also distance(sg) (yourself) from foolishness and useless argument, because you(sg) know that it results in conflict(s). 24 And the servant of the Lord, (it's) necessary that (he) doesn't antagonize_others, but rather, he is good to all people. (It's) also necessary that he is skillful in teaching, is patient, 25 and his teaching to people who oppose him is not bossy. And perhaps God will_permit them to turn_away from their sin and be_able_to_understand the truth. 26 And from that, they will_be_aroused and be_able_to_escape of being enticed by the master of the evil_spirits, who captured them in_order to do his desire.

Their thinking is badly_ruined and their faith is not true. 9 But they will_ not _be_able_to_continue for long, because everyone will_realize their foolishness, like what also happened to Hanis and Hambris.

The last instructions of Pablu to Timutiyu

10 But you(sg) on_the_contrary Timutiyu, you(sg) know all that I have taught, my customs, my purpose here on earth (LIT on the top of the ground), my faith, my patience, my love, and my suffering/endurance. 11 [xra][xrb][xrc]You(sg) also know that I was persecuted and I was caused_hardship by people, and what happened to me in Antiyukiya, Ikuniyu, and Listra, but I was_helped/rescued by the Lord from all the persecutions that I suffered/endured. 12 What is true, all who want to follow/imitate God in their customs/behavior as a result of their unity with Kristu Hisus will_ indeed _be_persecuted. 13 But wicked people and deceivers, their wickedness will yet increase. They will_continue to deceive and they will_ also _be_deceived. 14 But you(sg) on_the_contrary, continue to firmly believe what_was_taught to you(sg), because you(sg) know those who taught this to you(sg). 15 From when you(sg) were still a child, you(sg) were taught the holy Writings which are able_to_give knowledge/ability which results in salvation by_means of faith in Kristu Hisus. 16 All the Writings, (it is) indeed from God, and it is very useful for teaching the truth, for scolding/warning, for correcting (LIT straightening), and for teaching how to live righteously, 17 so_that the people of God will_be_prepared to do whatever good_thing.

41-2 Here in the presence of God and Kristu Hisus, I command you(sg) to preach the word of God. Diligently(sg) preach at whatever time, (whether) there are ones_who_will_listen or not. For Hisus will_arrive again in_the_future in_order to rule_as_king and to judge all people, living (LIT strong) or already dead. 6 For as_for me, the time has already arrived for my blood to be poured_out like a sacrifice, and (it is) now time for me to depart (LIT disappear) from the earth (LIT top of the ground). 7 I am_like a runner and I have indeed done as much as I could do, and I have finished my race.
