Hosea

Introduction

This document was about the prediction of prophet Husiyas the child of Biiri long_ago at about eight hundred (800) years before Hisus was_born. Prophet Husiyas and the prophet Amus were prophesying to the people about the same time. But Husiyas continued prophesying for (more) following years. At that time, the place of Israil is divided into two and Husiyas preached there at the upper part which he sometimes called Israil, and sometimes he called Ipraim. Ipraim was the name of an important tribe in the upper part. And Samaria was the name of the capital (LIT mother) city. And the small part of the place Israil, he called_it Huda. He preached before Samarya was_destroyed when their enemies invaded (it).

In this document, Husiyas showed the disobedience of the people of the Lord God (MS Manama) and their leaving of him. They were_like his disobedient spouse Gumir and left him. And Husiyas was very sad at the disobedience of the people and (who) worshiped false_gods, and no_longer trusted the Lord God. But at the end, what_persisted is the love of the Lord God to his people and they just returned again to him. His love to them is big, and he made_it_known by these very compassionate words, “How could I desert you(sg), oh Ipraim? I will_ not _abandon you(sg) to be_defeated, oh Israil. I won't destroy you(sg) like Adma and Sibuwim. My heart doesn't allow (things) like that, because my love for you(sg) is big.” (11:8).
