Habakkuk

Introduction

In this document, we(incl) read Prophet Habakuk's conversation with Lord God (MS Manama). Habakuk resided in Huda and probably he was writing this document a_little_more than six hundred (600) years before Christ was born. Habakuk asked God why his fellow (ones) from_Israil who oppressed good people were not punished. God answered that he would send the soldiers from_Babylunya in_order for punishing them. At that time, the soldiers of the nation of Babilunya were greatly feared because they were very fierce and they defeated the nations of Asiriya and Ihiptu. Habakkuk did not understand why the Lord God did like that. And he asked God going, “Why did you(sg) permit the people to do evil? And why are you(sg) silent when they will_destroy the people who are more righteous than them?” (1:13). And God answered that he would help at the correct time. And the righteous (ones), will_live on_the_contrary due to their believing.” (2:4)

The other parts of this document (are) the predicting of the coming destruction of those evil (ones). And even Habakuk is scared that God now punishes Huda, but he still believes that he will_ still _save his people and give them strength/perseverance to endure the difficulty that they will_experience. This document finishes by a song praising the Lord who is powerful.
