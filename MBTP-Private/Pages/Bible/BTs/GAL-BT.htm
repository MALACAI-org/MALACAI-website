Introduction

The question was if the people indeed needed to follow the Law of Moses (MS Muwisis) to become true believers. Paul (MS Pablu) argued that it is not required because faith is the true source of life in Christ (MS Christ), and by means of that people (are) worthy in front of God (MS Manama).
