The first letter by Paul to

Timothy

Introduction

Timothy, (MS Timutiyu) is a Christian from Asia. His mother was a Hudiyu and his father a Greek (MS Grigu). Since Paul saw that Timothy's believing was true, he made him his companion and his apprentice in preaching and telling the Good Report (Acts 16:1-3). Ephesus (MS Ipisu) was one town that they went to. And there Paul left_behind Timothy to lead the believers. When Timothy had been at Ephesus a reasonably long_time, he was_written to by Paul. Timothy's age was probably about thirty (30) at that time.

There are three purposes of this letter by Paul to Timothy:

1/ The first purpose of Paul, (was that) he warned about not correct teaching in the group of believers. This teaching, (it) was mixed with thinking of the Hudiyus and thinking of the non Hudiyus. For according to them this world where we(incl) live is bad. And a person can only (hearsay) be_saved if he/she has skills/knowledge and doesn't eat some kinds of food and also doesn't marry. Paul taught that this isn't correct.

2/ This letter also teaches about managing a group of believers and (about) worshiping. Written here is the proper behavior for a leader of believers, a helper of believers and other believers who are widows and slaves.

3/ Finally, he advised Timothy how to become a good servant of Jesus Christ, and about his responsibility(s) to the Christians.

11 I Paul am sending this letter. I (am) an apostle of Christ Jesus as_a_result of the command of God (MS Manama) who is our(incl) Savior and of Christ Jesus whom we(incl) will_be_hope_in. 2 [xr]I greet you(sg) Timutiyu whom I consider my true offspring in believing. (May) the goodness, mercy and peace/well-being from God who is our(incl) Father and from Kristu Hisus who is our(incl) Lord, be received by you(pl).

Causing_ the false teaching _to_stop

3 Continue(sg) to live there in Ipisu, like I formerly requested you(sg) when I was_going to Masidunya, so_that you(sg) will_be_able_to_reprimand the people who teach what is not true. 4 Also tell(sg) them to not pay_attention to the folk_tales and genealogies about elders. This only causes_quarrels, and is not able_to_help with fulfilling the plan of God which is only understood by means of our(incl) faith. 5 The purpose of this command of mine is for them to love_each_other from their clean hearts, their clean consciences, and their true faith. 6 There are people who have strayed from those, and instead debate/argue about useless argumentation. 7 They want hopefully to become teachers of the Law of God, but they don't understand whatever they have said, or what they have rigorously taught.

8 We(incl) realize that the Law of Moses is good if the using of it is correct. 9 We(incl) also realize that God did not make the Law for people who are righteous, but rather, for those who disobey, those who are contentious, those people who don't follow God, those who are sinners, those who have_no religion/practice(s)/belief(s), those who disbelieve God, and those who kill even their father and mother. 10 Also included are those who are sexually_immoral (LIT meddle without being_married), those males who court (others) just like them, those who capture(pl) people, those who (are) liars, those who testify lies, and all those who don't obey the true teaching. 11 And that true teaching, (it) is from the wonderful Good Report that was entrusted to me by God[fn] who is rightly praised.

The thanksgiving of Paul for the mercy of God

12 I thank Christ Jesus our(incl) Lord who gave me strength for this work of mine. For I was recognized as trustworthy by him, and I was_chosen by him to become his servant. 13 [xr]And even_though I blasphemed, I persecuted, and I ridiculed those who believe in him, yet I was_given_mercy by him since I did not yet believe/obey him formerly and I did not understand that my deeds were wicked. 14 The mercy of our(incl) Lord to me is very great, for he gave me faith and love that are ours(incl) by_means of our(incl) oneness with Christ Jesus. 15 The saying is really true and (it is) worthy to be accepted and believed: Christ Jesus came to earth (LIT top of the ground) in_order for saving sinners—and I am truly the worst sinner. 16 But this is the source/reason that I was_given_mercy by God so_that Christ Jesus would_be_able_to_show that he is indeed not easily_frustrated with me the worst sinner. He did that so_that I would_become an example to those who will_believe/obey and trust in him and (who) will_receive life without end. 17 Now then, all honor and splendor without ending are indeed the king's who has no ending, no death, can't be seen, and (is) the one_and_only God. Really true.

18 My child Timutiyu, this is what_ I _instruct you(sg) according to what_was_foretold previously by prophet(s) concerning you(sg), for if you(sg) follow this, you(sg) will_be_able_to_refute as much as you(sg) can[fn] those people who argue about the truth. 19 So then, tighten(sg) your(sg) faith/believing, and take_care(sg) that your(sg) conscience is clean. There are people who ignore their consciences, and so they harm/ruin their faith, 20 like Himiniyu and Alihandru. I have entrusted them to the power of Satan (MS Meibulan) so_that they will_learn to no_longer blaspheme God.

Concerning worship

21 Therefore, first of all, I request you(pl) that you(pl) pray, ask(pl) God for the needs of all people, and thank(pl) him. 2 Also pray(pl) for Kings and all who have responsibility/authority so_that (it) will_be_peaceful and there will_be_no conflicts in our(incl) living_situation and so_that we(incl) will_be_able_to_imitate/follow God in correct behavior. 3 That praying is good and is able_to_please God our(incl) Savior, 4 because he wants all people to be_saved and able_to_understand the truth. 5 For God is just one, and the mediator/peacemaker between God and people is also only one, and that person, (is) Christ Jesus. 6 He gave his life in_order to redeem all people from their sin(s). That is what proved at the right time that God desires to save all people. 7 [xr]And so I was_chosen to be an apostle and teacher to the non Hudiyus in_order to preach about faith and truth. These words of mine are really true, not a lie.

8 Therefore, when you(pl) worship, I want the males to pray, but (it is) necessary that they have_no sin at their lifting of their hands, and with no anger in their hearts and no quarreling.

9 [xr]And the females also, I want their beautifying_of_themselves to be proper and not excessive and not to show_off their expensive clothes, expensive necklesses, and decorations in their hair. 10 For women who claim that they serve God, (they) become beautiful by_means of their good work(s). 11 It is also necessary that they know_how_to be quiet in the gathering and submissive to those who teach them. 12 I do not permit a female to teach or to rule_over a male. She needs to just be quiet. 13 [xra][xrb]For Adan was the first (one) made by God, and afterwards Iba, 14 [xr]and (it) was not Adan who was deceived, but rather, the female and she disobeyed the command of God. 15 But females, (they) will_be_saved by_means of birthing if they continue in their faith, their love, their holiness, and (if) they are not haughty.

The character of a leader of believers

31 This saying that goes_like_this is really true: if there_is a male who wants to become a leader of the believers, the work he desires is good. 2 [xr]And so, a leader of the believers, (it is) necessary that there_is_nothing to scold/blame him for. (It's) necessary that he has only one spouse, he is able_to_control_himself, his thoughts are sensible (LIT straight), his reputation is good, he is friendly/welcoming, and is skillful in teaching. 3 (It's) necessary that he not be a drunkard, not one_who_beats (others), not contentious, but rather, he is patient/submissive. (It's) necessary that he doesn't covet money. 4 It's_necessary that his managing of his family is good so_that he is obeyed/believed and always respected by his offspring. 5 Because if there_is a person who is not skillful in managing his family, how will he care_for the believers in God? 6 (It's) necessary that he has not recently believed, for perhaps he will_ just _take_pride_in his big responsibility, and afterwards, he will_be_punished like the punishing of the master of the evil_spirits. 7 (It's) also necessary that his reputation is good in the minds of people who don't believe so_that no one will_be_able_to_blame/scold him and (he) will_ not _be_enticed by the master of the evil_spirits.

The character of a helper of the believers

If there_is really nothing in them to be scolded, (it's) possible for them to become helpers of the believers. 11 And also just like that are their spouses. (It's) necessary that they are also respected, not backstabbers, able_to_control their self/personhood, and trustworthy with absolutely everything. 12 And added to that, the helper to the believers, (it's) necessary that he has only one spouse, and that his managing of all the members of his family is good (children is incl in family). 13 And those helper(s) of believer(s) who do well, (they) will_ really _be_respected and they will_ also _have_no fear in speaking about their faith in Christ Jesus.

The good servant of Christ Jesus

Advice concerning widows, leaders of believers, and slaves

The teaching which is not true and true wealth
