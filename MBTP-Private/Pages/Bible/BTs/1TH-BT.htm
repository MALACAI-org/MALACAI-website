The first letter by Paul to

The (ones) from_Thessalonica

Introduction

Thessalonica (MS Tisalunika) is the mother city of the province of Macedonia (MS Masidunya) which is under_the_jurisdiction of Ruma (MS Ruma). Paul (MS Pablu) taught the word of God (MS Manama) there in Thessalonica after he left Pilipus (Acts 17:1-4). Before long, there were protests from the Hudiyus upset at the preaching of Paul to the non Hudiyus (Acts 17:5-9). From that, Paul was persuaded/forced to leave Thessalonica and go to Berea (MS Birya) (Acts 17:10-13). Before long, he went to Athens (MS Atinas) (Acts 17:14-15). Then, continued to Kurintu (Acts 18:1-17). When he arrived there, he received the story of Timothy (MS Timutiyu) his work companion. Timothy told him about the situation of the group of believers at Thessalonica.

This First letter by Paul to the Thessalonicans, he wrote to encourage and strengthen the believers there. He gave_thanks for the story about their believing and their love. He also reminded them about his living that he showed them when he was still there with them. He also answered the questions from the believers about the returning by Kristu, and whether the believers who die before Kristu returns will_receive life without ending. Paul used this letter to tell them that they need to continue to work every day while they wait for the returning by Kristu.
