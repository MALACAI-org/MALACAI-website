Introduction

The Letter of Pablu to The (ones) from_Ruma he sent to the Hudiyus and non Hudiyus who believe and trust Hisu Kristu. This document (LIT reading), (it) was_written with the complete revelation (LIT causing_to_understand) of Pablu concerning his message.
