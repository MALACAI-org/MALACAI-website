The story about

Rut

Introduction

This document (LIT reading), tells about what happened long_ago when the judges still ruled the place of Israil. At that time, severe famine came that place, that's why Ilimilik and his family moved to the place Muwab. Then, Ilimilik died and his two male children got married there, and the woman who had_become_the_wife of his child was_named Rut. When Rut's husband died, she showed her good habits and love to her mother-in-law named Nawumi. When God (MS Manama) blessed again his people, Nawumi returned_home to Israil and Rut accompanied her. And Rut has_been_married_widow by a relative of her first spouse, and from that, she became an ancestor of Dabid, the famous king of Israil. After_a_long_time, Hisu Kristu was also born that was a descendant of Rut as well.

Here in this document (LIT reading) Judges we(incl) will_know that bad_things were experienced by the Hudiyus because they distanced/turned from God. And here in the Story concerning Rut (it/this) shows us(incl) that a (LIT one) person who wasn't a Hudiyu was blessed because he/she approached the God of Israil.

The transfer of Ilimilik and_company to Muwab

11 In the time that the judges still ruled the place of Israil, there_was a severe famine came in that place. And there_was a person from_Bitlihim under_the_jurisdiction of Huda who moved to a place of Muwab, with his spouse and two male children in_order to live there temporarily. 2 The person was_named Ilimilik and his spouse's name, Nawumi. And their two male children were_named Malun and Kiliyun. They were descendants of Iprata from_Bitlihim that was under_the_jurisdiction of Huda. They went to Muwab and lived there. 3 And then, Ilimilik died, so then Nawumi became widow and her two male children were left with her. 4 The siblings/cousins, married women from_Muwab. One woman was_named Urpa and the other named Ruth. When they had lived there about ten years, 5 Malun and Kiliyun also died, so then just Nawumi was the only one_left in the family.

The accompanying of Rut to Nawumi returning_home to Bitlihim

6 Then, Nawumi found_out that the Lord God had blessed his people by_means of giving them a good harvest. At that, she prepared along_with her daughters-in-law to leave Muwab. 7 Then the traveled going to Huda. While they were_walking, 8 Nawumi said to her daughters-in-law, “Go_back(pl) and go_home to your(pl) mothers. And like your(pl) goodness to me and to your(pl) deceased husbands, (may it) will_be_like also the goodness that will_be_shown to you(pl) by the Lord God. 9 And may the Lord God allow you(pl) to marry again so_that you(pl) will_have a new_home (LIT moved into their spouse's house).”

And then, Nawumi kissed them. But they wept,10 and said to her, “We(excl) won't return_home to ours(excl) because we(excl) will_accompany you(sg) to your(sg) relatives.”

11 But Nawumi said, “Daughters-in-law, it is good for you(pl) to return_home. Why do you(pl) still accompany me when I can't have children for me to marry you(pl) with? 12 Go_home because I (am) old and I won't marry again. Even if I still have hope, and even if I can_be_able_to_marry again tonight and have_ male _children, 13 would you(pl) be_able_to_wait until they grew? Would you(pl) not marry because of them? Daughters-in-law, that is not possible, because God has turned_his_back on me, and my experience is severe than yours(pl).”

14 And they wept even_more. And there, Urpa kissed her parent-in-law goodbye. But Ruth, on_the_contrary didn't separate from her parent-in-law. 15 And Nawumi said to Rut, “Now, your(sg) sister-in-law has returned_home to her relatives and to her god, so follow_after her.”

16 Rut answered, “Don't persuade me to leave and to separate from you(sg). Because wherever you(sg) go, I will_ also _go_there, and wherever you(sg) live, I will_ also _live there. And your(sg) relatives will_ also _become my relatives, and your(sg) God will_ also _become my God. 17 Wherever you(sg) die, I will_ also _die there and be buried. May the Lord God punish me severely if I will_separate from you(sg) in whatever reasons. Just death can separate us(incl).”

18 When Nawumi saw that Rut would indeed accompany her, she just kept_quiet.

19 So then, they continued traveling until they arrived in Bitlihim. All the people were stirred at their arrival, and the women said, “Is this really Nawumi?”

20 And Nawumi said to them, “Don't call me Nawumi anymore, rather, just Mara[fn] because what the Powerful God caused_me_to_experience was very difficult. 21 When I left here, I still had everything, but I was_sent_home by the Lord God here poor. So why do you(pl) still call me Nawumi when I've been_punished and caused_to_suffer by the Powerful Lord God?”

22 And that was Nawumi's leaving_from Muwab. She returned_home to Bitlihim with her daughter-in-law Rut from_Muwab, and it was the time of harvesting of barley (Cebuano loan) when they arrived in Bitlihim.

The gleaning of Rut at Buas' field

21 And there, there_was a person named Buas who was a relative of Nawumi's husband long_ago Ilimilik. He was rich and very respected person. 2 [xr]One day, Rut said to Nawumi, “Allow me to go to the field to glean the heads left_behind by the people that allow me to glean.”

And Nawumi answered, “Yes, go daughter-in-law.”

3 Therefore Rut went to the field and followed the people_harvesting barley to glean. It happened that she was gleaning at the field of Buas the relative of Ilimilik.

4 Before long, Buas arrived from Bitlihim and said to the people_harvesting, “The Lord God be with you(pl).”

And the people_harvesting answered, “The Lord God bless you(sg) also.”

5 And Buas asked his servant the leader of the people_harvesting, “Whose child is that woman?”

6 The leader of the people_harvesting answered, “She is the woman from_Muwab that accompanied Nawumi on her returning_home here from Muwab. 7 She implored me that she will_follow the people_harvesting to glean the heads left_behind. She was early this morning, and only just now has stopped to briefly rest in the small_shelter.”

8 And then, Buas said to Rut, “Hey young_woman, listen(sg). Don't glean in other fields. Just glean here with my female servants. 9 Watch where my male servants are harvesting, and follow with the female. I have instructed the males not to touch/harm you(sg). And if you(sg) are thirsty, just drink the water in the water_containers that they have filled.”

10 At that, Rut bowed_to_the_ground and said to Buas, “Why are you(sg) good to me and you(sg) pay_attention to me when I am just a foreigner?”

11 Buas answered, “I've been_told concerning all you(sg) did of your(sg) parent-in-law from the death of your(sg) spouse. You(sg) left your(sg) father and mother and your(sg) birth_place, and live_among the people that you(sg) don't know yet. 12 May the Lord God reward you(sg) for what you(sg) have done. And may you(sg) receive a big/great reward from the Lord God of Israil in whom you(sg) have sought_protection.”

13 And Rut answered, “You(sg) are very good to me, master. You(sg) comforted me and talked to me nicely similar to your(sg) servant even though I am not one of your(sg) servants.”

14 When they ate, Buas said to Rut, “Come here and get bread and dip it in the vinegar.” So then, Rut sat with the harvesters, and Buas gave her roasted barley and she ate. She was very full and she still had some leftovers. 15 When Rut had left to glean again, Buas urged his male servants, “Just ignore her gleaning even near the tied_up_bundles, and don't embarrass her. 16 And pull(pl) out some of the heads from the bundles and drop(pl) them for her to pick up, and don't tell_ her _off.”

17 So then, Rut gleaned in the field until dusk. Then, she trod_out her gleanings, and what she had collected was one large_can (about fifteen kilograms or one-third sack). 18 And she carried it home to the city and showed it to her parent-in-law. She also took her leftovers of food and gave it to her. 19 And Nawumi asked her, “Where did you(sg) glean today? Whose field did you(sg) glean in? God bless the person who paid_attention to you(sg)!”

And there, Rut told Nawumi concerning those_things and said, “I gleaned in the field of a person named Buas.”

20 [xr]And Nawumi said to Rut, “The Lord God who always good to the living and the dead bless him.” And Nawumi also said, “That person is a close relative to us(incl), and he is one of those who have responsibility to look after us(incl).”

21 And Rut said, “I was_told by him to go_along_with his servants to glean until the end of his harvest.”

22 And Nawumi said to Rut, “It's really better daughter-in-law if you(sg) go_along_with his female servants, because perhaps you(sg) are in danger if you(sg) glean in the field(s) of other people.”

23 Therefore Rut went_along_with the female servants of Buas to glean until the end of the harvest of barley and wheat. And she continued to live_with her parent-in-law.

The requesting of Buas by Rut

31 One day, Nawumi said to Rut, “Daughter-in-law, I will_look for you(sg) a husband so_that your(sg) living will_be_better. 2 Do you(sg) remember our(incl) relative Buas whose female servants became_ your(sg) _companion? Now listen(sg). Later at night, he will_cause_to_tread the barley. 3 So bathe, put_on_perfume, and put_on your(sg) nice dress. And go to (where) the ones_are_treading, but don't show yourself to him until he finishes eating and drinking. 4 And when he go to sleep, watch/remember where he lies_down. And_then, approach (him) and lift the blanket at his feet and lie there. And there, he will_tell you(sg) what to do(sg).”

5 And Rut answered, “Yes, I will_do all you(sg) have said.”

6 So then, Rut went there to the trampling_place and did all that her parent-in-law had said to her. 7 And when Buas had_finished eating and drinking, he felt good. Then he lay_down near the gathered barley to sleep. And there, Rut slowly went_there and lifted the blanket at the feet of Buas and she lay_down. 8 In the middle of the night, Buas shook and he was awaken. When he turned_over, he was surprised that there_was a woman lying at his feet. 9 And Buas asked, “Who are you(sg)?”

He/She answered, “I am Rut your(sg) servant. You(sg) (are) my close relative, so you(sg) have responsibility to look after me. Therefore marry_(widow) me.[fn]”

10 And Buas answered, “The Lord God bless you(sg), young_woman. Your(sg) loyalty to your(sg) family that you(sg) have shown today/now, is even better of the goodness that you(sg) have shown before to your(sg) mother-in-law. 14 So then, Rut lay_down at the feet of Buas until dawn. But she got_up before dawn so_that (she) wouldn't be recognized by the other people, because Buas wanted that nobody would know that she went to him. 15 And Buas said to her, “Bring_here(sg) the cloth that you(sg) wrapped around and spread(sg)_ it _out.” Rut obeyed it and Buas poured in about six cans (about twelve kilograms) of barley. Buas lifted_it_onto her, and she returned_home to the city. 16 When she arrived at her parent-in-law, she asked her, “What was your(sg) result daughter-in-law?”

So Rut told Nawumi everything that Buas did to her.17 And Rut again said, “He gave me this six cans of barley because he didn't want me to have nothing to bring_home to you(sg).”

18 Nawumi said to her, “Just wait daughter-in-law until you(sg) know what is the result of this, because Buas won't rest if he can't resolve this today.”

The marrying_(widow) of Buas to Rut

41 At that time, Buas went to the doorway of the town and sat there. And there, the close relative of Ilimilik that Buas had mentioned passed_by and he said (to him), “Brother/friend, come here, and sit_down here.” And the person approached and sat_down. 2 And Buas gathered ten leaders (MS datu's) of the town and caused_them_to_sit_down also there. 3 And there, Buas said to his relative, “Now that Nawumi has returned from Muwab, she wants to sell the land of our(incl) relative Ilimilik. 4 That's why I thought to tell you(sg) about this. And if you(sg) want, buy it here in front of our(incl) brothers sitting here, and of these leaders. But if you(sg) don't want, just let me know. For you(sg) (are) the one having a big authority to buy it, and I can only follow you(sg).”

The person answered, “Yes, I'll buy (it).”

5 And Buas said again, “Now, if what day you(sg) buy that land of Nawumi, you(sg) also need to marry_(widow) Rut from_Muwab, so_that the descendant(s) of our(incl) dead relative still will_become_the_owner of the land.”

6 The person answered, “If it's like that, I won't buy that land, because maybe the wealth my children will_receive will_be_reduced. You(sg) buy (it), because it's_ not _possible to me.”

7 [xr]What they did before in Israil to verify/confirm if they bought something or traded their wealth was like this: the one removes his/her sandal and gives it to the (one) buying. That was the custom in Israil to verify/confirm the settlement/negotiation.

8 Therefore when the person said to Buas, “You(sg) buy the land,” he took off his sandal and gave it to Buas. 9 At that, Buas said to the leaders and to all the people listening, “Today, you(pl) are all the witnesses that I have bought from Nawumi all the properties/wealth of Ilimilik and his children Kiliyun and Malun. 10 [xr]And here is more, I will_marry_widow Rut from_Muwab, the widow of Malun, so_that Malun's name will_be_able_to_accompany his wealth. And so_that his family also will_grow and won't be forgotten by their relatives, and by the people in this place. Now_then, you(pl) are all the witnesses of this today.”

11 [xr]And the leaders and all the people there said, “Yes, we(excl) (are) the witnesses. The Lord God bless the woman that you(sg) are marrying_widow and be_like Rakil and Liya that had many children that became the descendant of Israil. May you(sg) become wealthy here in this place of Iprata and famous here in Bitlihim.[fn] 12 [xr]May your(sg) descendant be blessed by the Lord God that he will_give to you(sg) by_means of that woman, like the descendants of Piris the child of Huda and Tamar.”

The descendants of Buas

13 After that, Buas took Rut and marry_widow (her). And they slept/cuddled and the Lord God blessed Rut and became_pregnant. Then, she gave_birth to a male. 14 The women said to Nawumi, “Let us(incl) praise the Lord God because you(sg) have_been_given a grandchild today to look after you(sg). The child will_become famous here in Israil. 15 He will_be_able_to_restore your(sg) breath and look after you(sg) when you(sg) become_old, because he is the child of your(sg) daughter-in-law who loves you(sg) very_much. And even you(sg) may_be have seven male children, the love of your(sg) daughter-in-law for you(sg) can't be compared_with.” 16 And Nawumi took the child, and she cuddled (him). And she looked_after the child.

17 The neighbor women said, “This child was_born for Nawumi, and we(incl) will_name him Ubid.”

Then, Ubid have married and became the father of Hisi, and Hisi was the father of Dabid.18 These (are) the descendants of Piris: Piris is the father of Hisrun, 19 Hisrun is the father of Ram, Ram is the father of Aminadab, 20 Aminadab is the father of Nasun, Nasun is the father of Salmun, 21 Salmun is the father of Buas, Buas is the father of Ubid, 22 Ubid is the father of Hisi, and Hisi is the father of Dabid.
