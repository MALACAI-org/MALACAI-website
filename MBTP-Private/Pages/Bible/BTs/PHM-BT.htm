The Letter by Paul to

Philemon

Introduction

Pilimun, (was) a recognized Christian and he was a member of the group of believers in the city of Kulusas. He (was) the owner of a slave named Unisimu. Unisimu fled from his master and the time arrived when he met Apostle Paul in prison. Now there, Unisimu became_a_Christian because of Paul. Now this Letter by Paul, to Pilimun is a request that the good love that Pilimun and Unisimu his slave had for each_other_will_return. For Paul is returning Unisimu to Pilimun, and his desire is that Pilimun will_receive his slave not only because he forgave him as a slave, but rather, because he is also a sibling of his in believing.
