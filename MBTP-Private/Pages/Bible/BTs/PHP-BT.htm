The letter by Paul to

The (ones) from_Philippi

Introduction

This letter by Paul (MS Pablu), he sent to the believers in Philippi (MS Pilipus) under_the_jurisdiction of the province of Masidunya (Acts 16:6-40). That group of believers was the first which he established (LIT built) in the nation of Roma under_the_jurisdiction of Iyurupa. He wrote this while he was imprisoned from his preaching the Good Report concerning Jesus Christ (MS Hisu Kristu).

The first purpose of Paul in writing this document (LIT reading), was because he wanted to thank the believers at Philippi due to the gift which they sent to him in_order_to_help him at that time. His second purpose in writing this, is that they would_be_encouraged and their faith strengthened even through the difficulties that came to them and even also to him. They were advised by Paul that they need to be humble like the humility of Jesus when he was still here in the world. He reminded them again that it's just due to the goodness and mercy of God (MS Manama) and their faith in Christ (MS Kristu) that they are_saved. They don't need to continue to trust in the Law of Muwisis and in the traditions of the people. He also wrote about joy and well-being that God will_give to those united in Christ.

11 [xr]I Paul am sending this letter, along_with Timothy; we(excl) are slaves of Christ Jesus. We(excl) greet all the people of God (MS Manama) that are_united in Christ Jesus (MS Kristu Hisus) from_Philippi (MS Pilipus) along_with the leaders of the believers and their helpers.

2 (May) the mercy and peace/well-being from God our(incl) Father and the Lord Hisu Kristu, be received by you(pl).

The thanking and praying of Paul

3 I thank God whom I serve every (time) I think of you(pl). 4 Each (time) I pray for you(pl) all, I pray with joy 5 due to your(pl) helping me to tell the Good Report from the beginning of your(pl) believing, until now. 6 I know indeed that God who started doing good in you(pl) will_continue his work until it is finished on the day of Jesus Christ's returning. 7 It's indeed correct/fitting that I should feel like this concerning you(pl) all, yes because I have a big love for you(pl). For you(pl) participated with me in the help of God in my imprisonment, and even in my defense and my witnessing about the Good Report. 8 God knows that I greatly miss you(pl) all because I love you(pl) like Christ Jesus loves you(pl) also.

9 I pray that your(pl) loving_each_other will_be_strengthened more and that your(pl) knowledge/skills and your(pl) recognizing what is true will yet increase, 10 so_that you(pl) will_know what is very good, so_that you(pl) will_become clean and have nothing to_be_scolded_for until the returning of Christ. 11 And then, your(pl) customs and all that you(pl) do by_means of the power of Jesus Christ will_ really _become_righteous,[fn] so_that God will_be_praised and honored.

The happiness of Paul that Jesus is preached

12 Siblings, I want you(pl) to know, that what_happened to me greatly helped the spreading of the Good Report. 13 [xr]And from that, all those soldiers who are guards at the palace and all the people know that I was imprisoned for my serving Christ. 14 Due to my imprisonment, the trust of our(incl) siblings in the Lord has been_strengthened, and they have_no inhibition and no fear to tell the word of God.

15 It's indeed true that there are other(s) who preach about Christ just from the envy of me, and who just want to contradict. 18 But never_mind! I am still happy if Christ is preached for whatever purpose, bad or not. But I don't know which to choose. 23 I am unable_to_decide (LIT squeezed) which of the two I will_choose. I would like to leave_behind this life and accompany Christ, because that indeed is good, 24 but because of you(pl), it's really necessary that I still live. 25 And since I am sure of that, I know that I'll indeed still live so_that your(pl) believing can be confirmed/strengthened and your(pl) joy in the truth can be increased. 26 And so_that when I will_be_able_to_return to you(pl), Christ Jesus will_be_praised even_more by you(pl) due to me.

27 And whatever happens, it's_necessary that your(pl) living, (it) matches the teaching of the Good Report about Christ. And so_that whether I am_able_to_go_there or not, or if I am just able_to_hear about you(pl), I will_know that your(pl) uniting is strengthened, and your(pl) thoughts are one in striving for the faith that was taught in the Good Report. 28 And don't be_afraid of those who oppose/go_against you(pl), because your(pl) boldness, (it) is a (LIT one) sign to them that they will_be_destroyed by God, and you(pl) on_the_contrary will_be_saved. Shine(pl) here in the world like stars

Timitiyu and Ipapruditu
