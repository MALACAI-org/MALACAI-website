The third letter of

John

Introduction

This is the third letter of Apostle John (MS Huwan) who was one of the disciples of Jesus (MS Hisus). He sent this to one of his friends who believes named Gayu. John commended Gayu for his looking_after other believers who go_around storying the word of God (MS Manama). He also warned Gayu about a person named Diyutripis who had bad custom(s).

11 [xr]It (is) me John the elder of the believers the (one) sending this letter. I greet you(sg) Gayu as a sibling in the truth whom I love.

2 My beloved sibling, I pray that your(sg) living_situation and your(sg) health are good like the goodness of your(sg) soul. 3 Some of our(incl) siblings arrived here, and I was_ very _happy to be storied by them about your(sg) obedience to the truth, and how is your(sg) living there in the truth. 4 What really pleases me is when I hear that my children in believing obeyed the truth.

The commendation of Gayu

5 My beloved sibling, you(sg) are trustworthy because you(sg) cared for our(incl) siblings even_though you(sg) didn't know them. 6 They storied here in front of the believers about your(sg) love for them. And if/when they return there, it would_be good if your(sg) helping them would_please God by/through your(sg) giving what they need at their departure. 7 For they went in_order to teach about Christ and they didn't accept anything from people who don't believe in God. 8 Therefore, (it's) necessary that we(incl) help people like that, so_that we(incl) will_become companions in the work concerning the truth.

Diyutripis and Dimitriyu

9 I sent a letter to the believers there, but since Diyutripis didn't want to_be_superceded_in_status(_by_others), we(excl) were ignored by him. 10 Therefore if I go_there, I will_story about what he did which was slandering and mocking us(excl). And not only that, he also doesn't welcome our(incl) siblings, he also hinders and drives_off from the group of believers those who wanted to welcome them.

11 My beloved sibling, don't you(sg) imitate evil, but rather, that which is good. Whoever does good, he is God's. Whoever does evil, (he) doesn't know God.

12 Dimitriyu is commended by all and it is seen by his work/actions that he follows the truth. Also even us(excl), (we) are able_to_commend him and you(sg) also know that what we(excl) say is really true.

The goodbye

13 I would have_ much yet _to_tell you(sg), but I don't want to write more of this. 14 I would like that we(incl) will_be_able_to_see_one_another soon, and to talk_to_one_another.

15 God will_give you(sg) peace. Your(sg) friends here greet you(sg), and I also send_regards to all of our(incl) friends there.
