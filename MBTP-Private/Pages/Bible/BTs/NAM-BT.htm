Introduction

This document, (it) was_written with the stories concerning the destruction of Ninibi, the capital (LIT mother) city of Asiya that was an enemy of Israil long_ago. This was written between 663 and 612 years before Kristu was_born. Prophet Nahum was_happy about the demolition of Ninibi, because that's the sign that the Lord God (MS Manama) truly punishes the sinners. Even the people, believed also_that God punished Ninibi because the inhabitants in that city were fierce and proud.
