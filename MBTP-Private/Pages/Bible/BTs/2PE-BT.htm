Pidru

Introduction

The second letter of Pidru, he sent to the believers there in various cities of Asya Minur (1 Pidru 1:1, 2 Pidru 3:1). The reason for his writing this, (it) is to oppose the work/deed(s) of those (who) teach (what's) not correct, and to oppose the dirty actions of the people that they got from that teaching. Therefore he urged them not to believe that teaching because what_ (they_should) _believe is just the teaching about Christ. It's also necessary that there's something_ they _add to their believing: good behavior/custom(s), skill(s)/knowledge, controlling self, not becoming_tired of waiting, obeying God (MS Manama), and loving other(s).

Those people taught that Christ won't return since it's already been_quite_a_while, and he hasn't returned. But that is answered by Pidru that Christ will_ indeed _return, but God doesn't want any one to be_punished, because his desire is that the people turn_from their sin(s).
