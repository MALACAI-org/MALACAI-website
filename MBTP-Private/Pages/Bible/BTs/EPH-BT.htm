The letter by Paul to

The (ones) from_Ipisu

Introduction

This Letter by Paul (MS Pablu) to the (ones) from_Ipisu, he wrote at a time that he was in prison. And it wasn't just the group of believers there in Ipisu that this letter was_sent to, but rather, even to other groups of believers also.

The big purpose of Paul here is about the plan of God (MS Manama) that “he will_unite everything that he made there in sky/heaven and here on the earth/ground and he will_cause_(them)_to_be_ruled by Christ” (1:10).

There in the first part of the letter, is written about unity. Paul carefully explained about the way of God the Father in choosing his people, in forgiving and in saving them from their sins by_means of Jesus Christ his child.

There in the second part of the letter, Paul requests of those readers of this letter, that it_is_necessary that their unity in Christ be seen by_means of their unity with other(s) also.

Paul used many examples/parables about the unity of the people of God in Christ: the believers, they (are) the body of Christ, and he is the head; like a house, the foundation stone, it is really Christ; and they are also like a wife (LIT female spouse), and Christ is their husband/spouse.

We(incl) will_ also _understand here about the correct behavior of married_couple(s), of children_and_parents, and of slaves and masters. And at the end of this letter, we(incl) will_ also _understand about what God gave to the believers that they will_use in_order to resist Satan's (MS Meibulan's) deception of them.
