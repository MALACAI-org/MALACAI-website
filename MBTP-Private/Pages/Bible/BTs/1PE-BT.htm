The first letter of

Pidru

Introduction

The first letter of Pidru, he wrote for the believers that lived scattered there around Asya Minur. The purpose of Pidru in writing this, (was) the encouraging of the believers that they need to be_strong in_the_midst of the great persecution of them due to their believing/faith. Pidru reminded them about their hope, about the death of Jesus Christ, about his resurrection and about his returning. Because Pidru thought that if they just always think about their hope in heaven/sky, they will_ indeed _be_able_to_endure the difficulties here on top of the earth. He also explained to them that if their believing/faith is strengthened (LIT tightened) by_means of their suffering of difficulties and hardships, they will_receive the gift at “The time that Jesus Christ appears.” (1:7,13)

He also encouraged them that they need to take_care_of their behavior as people of Christ, and they also need to be_obedient to those that have authority to command them.

Greetings \ior 5:12‑14\ior*

11 I Pidru (am) the (one) sending this letter, I (am) an apostle of Jesus Christ (MS Hisu Kristu).

I greet you(pl) who are chosen by God (MS Manama), who are just foreigners in Puntu, Galasya, Kapadusya, Asya, and Bitinya.2 You(pl) were_chosen by God the Father according to his prior plan, and you(pl) were_made_holy by his Spirit so_that you(pl) will_be_able_to_believe/obey Jesus Christ, and you(pl) will_be_cleaned by his blood.

(May) the mercy and peace/well-being from God, be received by you(pl) in great_excess.

The hoping_for life without ending

3 (May) God and the Father of our(incl) Lord Jesus Christ be_praised! Since his mercy is great, we(incl) were_given new life by him by_means of the raising_to_life of Jesus Christ. Therefore we(incl) firmly hope that we(incl) will_ also _be_raised_to_life in_the_future. 4 And (what) God has prepared for you(pl) in heaven/sky, can't rot, can't be dirtied, and can't be removed. 5 Due to your(pl) trusting in God, you(pl) are looked_after by him by_means of his power, until you(pl) receive the salvation that (he) will_reveal on the final day.

6 And from that, be very happy(pl) even if you(pl) need to suffer for just a short time due to various testings. 7 The purpose of those difficulties, (is) knowing if your(pl) believing/faith is truly real. Because even gold which just passes_by, is tested by_means of melting it, so_that it_can_be_know if it's truly real. And your(pl) believing/faith is greater than gold. And if/when it can be proven that your(pl) believing/faith is true, you(pl) will_be_praised, respected, and honored until the time that Jesus Christ is revealed. 8 You(pl) loved him even though you(pl) haven't seen him. And even though you(pl) can't see him now, but you(pl) believe/obey and trust him. And your(pl) joy can't be measured or compared 9 since you(pl) received the gift of your(pl) believing/faith which is salvation.

10 The prophets who prophesy about the goodness and mercy of God that will_be_given to you(pl), investigated and carefully verified about that salvation. 11 And by_means of the Spirit of God that was in them, they prophesied about the suffering of the Christ[fn] and his great honor when it was_finished. They greatly verified when and by what means that would_happen. 12 God revealed to them that (what) they were speaking (about), wouldn't happen to them because on_the_contrary it (will happen) here to you(pl) now/today. And you(pl) heard this now from the people that preached the Good Report by_means of the help of the Spirit of God that he sent from heaven/sky. And even the angels, really want to understand this.

The holy behavior of believers

13 Therefore prepare(pl) your(pl) thoughts[fn] in_order to do the desire of God, and you(pl) need to control your(pl) behavior. Settle your(pl) hope about the mercy that will_be_given to you(pl) at the time that Jesus Christ is revealed. 14 Since you(pl) are obedient children of God, you(pl) need to not fulfill the evil desire of your(pl) body(s), like (what) you(pl) did before when you(pl) didn't yet know the truth. 15 And because the (one who) choose you(pl) is holy, it's_necessary that all of your(pl) actions (are) holy 16 [xr]because it's written in the Writings, “You(pl) need to be holy because I (am) holy.”

17 The God who you(pl) call_father if/when you(pl) pray, is the (one who) will_judge all people without bias according to their actions. Therefore, respect him while you(pl) still live here on top of the earth. 18 You(pl) know what God did to redeem you(pl) from your(pl) worthless behavior that you(pl) got from your(pl) ancestors. He didn't use those temporary riches like gold or silver, 19 but rather, he used the expensive blood of Christ. He was like a lamb without defect or stain. 20 Before the world was_made, he was already chosen as Savior. But now at the final days he is revealed for you(pl). 21 And by_means of him, you(pl) believed God who raised him from the dead and who gave him great honor. Therefore God (is the one) that your(pl) believing/faith and your(pl) hope has settled on.

22 Now, because your(pl) thought(s) have_been_cleaned by_means of your(pl) obeying the truth, your(pl) love of your(pl) believer brothers/siblings has become_true. And now/today, each one of you(pl) need to really love_each_other from your(pl) heart(s). 23 Because you(pl) were_born again (LIT redone), that new life of yours(pl), isn't from a person/people that quickly die, but rather, that's from the without ending and effective word of God. 24 [xr]Because it's written in the Writings,

“All people, are_like weeds,

and all of their splendor, is like the flower(s) of the weeds.

And the weed(s) wither and their flowers fall_off,

25 but the word of the Lord, continues without ending.”

That word, is the Good Report that was preached to you(pl).

21 Therefore, turn_ your(pl) _back_on all evil activities like cheating, hypocrisy, jealousy, and backbiting your(pl) companion(s). 2 You(pl) always need to drink pure milk that is the Word of God, like a new-born (LIT red) baby that always wants milk. Because if you(pl) do that, your(pl) believing/faith will_grown until you(pl) are_saved, 3 [xr]because you(pl) have tried/tasted the goodness of the Lord.

The secure stone and the believers

4 Approach(pl) the Lord, the secure stone that was rejected by the people, but [surprisingly] chosen by God and considered by him as precious. 5 And you(pl) also, you(pl) are_like secure stone(s) that was used by a builder for a spiritual temple. You(pl) became holy sacrificers to/of God, and by_means of Jesus Christ, you(pl) can_give to God a spiritual offer/sacrifice that he will_accept. 6 [xr]Because it's written in the Writings,

“Look(pl)! I have_chosen the precious stone,

I have put it in Hirusalim[fn] so_that (it/he) will_become a foundation_stone;

and those who trust (in) him, won't be_ashamed.”

7 [xr]Therefore, there with you(pl) (who) believe, he/it is very precious. But concerning those who don't believe, it's written in the Writings,

“The stone that was rejected by the builders

despite_that it became an important foundation_stone.”

8 [xr]And there's also in the Writings that which says,

“This is the stone that makes_ the people _stumble,

and the (one that) makes_ them _fall_down.”

They will_stumble because they didn't believe/obey the word of God. And that's really what God planned for them.

9 [xra][xrb][xrc][xrd]But you(pl) (are) the chosen people, the sacrificers to/of the king, the holy nation, and the people who are God's. You(pl) were_chosen by him in_order_to tell about this glorious activities. He called you(pl) from darkness unto his amazing brightness. 10 [xr]Before, you(pl) weren't people of God, but now/today, you(pl) (are) his people. You(pl) weren't pitied by God before, but now/today, you(pl) are pitied by him.

The behavior of believers

11 Siblings/friends, because you(pl) just live temporarily and are just foreigners here on top of the earth, I urge you(pl) to distance yourselves from the evil desires of your(pl) bodies that always conflict with your(pl) souls. 12 Make_good your(pl) behavior in the midst of people that don't believe (in) God, because even if you(pl) are accused by them of [reportedly] doing evil, they will_see your(pl) good work and they will_praise God in the day of his_coming.

13 Due to the Lord, it's_necessary that you(pl) comply_with all people that have authority over you(pl), like the Emperor because he is the chief of leaders, 14 and to governors. The governors are commanded by the Emperor to punish those who do evil, and to praise those who do good. 15 Because what God wants is that by_means of your(pl) doing good, those foolish people have nothing to say (against) you(pl). 16 Live(pl) as people who have freedom, but don't use that to cover evil work(s), but rather, live(pl) as slaves of God. 17 Respect(pl) all people and love(pl) your(pl) siblings/friends in believing. Fear(pl) God and respect the Emperor.

Advice to slave(s)

18 You(pl) slaves, you(pl) need to obey your(pl) masters along_with respect for them. And don't just do like that to good and sympathetic masters, but rather, even also fierce (ones). 19 God will_be_pleased if due to your(pl) thinking of him, you(pl) endure the persecutions that have_no source/reason. 20 Don't(pl) (allow yourselves to) be_praised if you(pl) are beaten for your(pl) evil actions. But if your(pl) actions are good and you(pl) suffer/endure due to that, God will_be_happy with you(pl). 21 You(pl) were_chosen by God in_order_to suffer/endure, because Jesus was also persecuted for you(pl) and he gave you(pl) an example in_order_to be imitated_by you(pl). 22 [xr]He didn't sin and no lie could be heard from him. 23 [xr]At the time that he was insulted/oppressed, he didn't revenge insult/oppress. At the time that he was persecuted, he didn't threaten, but rather, he trusted God, the righteous judge. 24 [xr]Christ himself took our(incl) sins by_means of the nailing of his body to a cross so_that we(incl) wouldn't (need to) sin, but rather, our(incl) living would_become_righteous. And by_means of his wounds, you(pl) were healed. 25 Because you(pl) before, you(pl) were_like sheep that are lost, but now, you(pl) have returned to the Guard and Caretaker of your(pl) souls.

Advice to couples

31 [xr]In that way, you(pl) married women, you(pl) need to comply_with your(pl) spouses, so_that if there are some of them who don't believe the word of God, maybe they will_ just _believe if they see that your(pl) behavior is good. And you(pl) won't need to say more to them 2 because they will_see that your(pl) lifestyle is good and your(pl) respecting of God. 3 [xr]Don't(pl) be concerned about the beautifying of your(pl) body(s), like the arranging of hair, dressing in expensive ornaments of the body, and dressing in good clothes, 4 but rather, just beautify(pl) your(pl) heart(s). Because the splendor of a person that can't be changed and that's very precious to God, that's the humble and quiet (attitude). 5 Like that was the beautifying long_ago by the holy women that hoped God. They complied_with their spouses, 6 [xr]like Sarah who believed/obeyed her spouse Abraham and called him “master”. And you(pl) now are her children if you(pl) do good and are_ not _afraid of anything.

7 [xr]And just like that you(pl) married men, you(pl) need to_respect your(pl) spouses. Look_after them while you(pl) live_together, because they are weaker than you(pl), and you(pl) are just the same in front of God because they will_ also _receive the mercy of God which is life without ending. And do that so_that nothing can hinder your(pl) prayers.

Suffering for doing good

8 Finally, you(pl) all, you(pl) need to unite your(pl) thought(s) and show_pity(pl) on other(s). And you(pl) love_each_other as siblings, be_good_to_each_other(pl), and be_humble(pl). 9 Don't(pl) repay evil to those who do evil to you(pl), and don't(pl) repay by cursing those who curse you(pl), but rather, repay(pl) by_means of blessing them because you(pl) were_chosen by God in_order_to receive blessing. 10 [xr]Because it's written in the Writings,

“If there_is a person who wants to be happy

and wants his/her living to be good,

he/she needs to not speak evil and lie.

11 He/She needs to turn_on evil and do good;

he/she needs to strive that there be well-being/peace (between him/her and) his/her companions/others.

12 Because God guards the righteous

and he listens to their prayers,

but he opposes those who do evil.”

13 Now_then, who will_ruin you(pl) if you(pl) are eager to do good? 14 [xr][xr]But even if you(pl) suffer due to your(pl) doing good, you(pl) will_be_blessed. Therefore don't(pl) fear what_they_fear and don't be_troubled, 15 but rather, really respect Christ as your(pl) Lord. And prepare(pl) always a defence for those who question you(pl) about the hope of believers. 16 But answer(pl) (them) without anger and with respecting. It's_necessary that your(pl) conscience(s) are always clean, so_that if (some) speaks bad (to/about) you(pl), they will_be_ashamed of their evil speech if they see that your(pl) custom is good as believers in Christ. 17 If it's the will of God, it's better to suffer for the doing of good, than for doing of evil. 18 Because Christ also suffered[fn] just once for the sin(s) of people. He hadn't [in contrast] sinned, but he died for sinner(s) in_order_to take us(incl) to God. He suffered the death of his body, but his spirit rose/lived.[fn] 19 His spirit went and preached to the imprisoned spirits. 20 [xr]It was those spirits who did not believe/obey long_ago while Nuwi was still making the ark, and God didn't get_tired_of waiting for them. And just a few were in the ark and saved from/by water because there_was just eight people indeed. 21 And that water, can be compared to the baptism that saved you(pl) now by_means of the power of the raising of Jesus Christ. Baptism, is not for the removing of dirt from the body, but rather, (for) the requesting God to clean our(incl) conscience. 22 And now/today, Jesus Christ is there in heaven/sky, there on the right (side)[fn] of God. And he is the (one who) commands all the angels, (those) that have authority and (the) powers that can't be seen.

The new living in God

41 Therefore, because Christ suffered when he was persecuted, you(pl) also need, to prepare to suffer. Because whoever suffers persecution, won't continue sinning. 2 And from now until he/she dies, he/she won't follow his/her evil desire, but rather, will_follow the will of God. 3 Because it was already more_than_enough what you(pl) did in the past time, that was like what those who don't believe in God wanted to do. Because before, you(pl) did ashaming (things), the evil desire(s) of your(pl) body(s), got drunk, greatly over indulged and drunk at feast(s), and you(pl) weren't ashamed of worshiping false_gods. 4 And now, they are amazed that you(pl) don't join them in doing very ashaming (things), and you(pl) are ridiculed by them. 5 But they will_ just _answer for their actions to God who is prepared to judge the dead(pl) and the still living/strong(pl). 6 That's the reason that the Good Report is preached even to (those) dead now, so_that even if their bodies are judged by_means of death like all people, their spirit(s) will_live like God.

7 The end of the world is close, therefore control yourselves and guard(pl) your(pl) thinking so_that you(pl) can_pray. 8 [xr]Greater than all, really love_each_other(pl) each one strongly, because if/when you(pl) do that, you(pl) will_ just quickly/easily _be_able_to_forgive others.[fn] 9 Be hospitable_to_each_other(pl) each one without complaining. 10 Because each one has received knowledge/wisdom/skill, he/she needs to use that for the welfare of other(s) like a good (person)_entrusted_with various knowledge/wisdom/skill from/of God. 11 Whoever preaches, he/she needs to preach as if it's God who is speaking through him. Whoever is (always) helpful, he/she needs to do that by_means of the strength that God gives to him/her, so_that God will_be_praised by all that you(pl) do by_means of Jesus Christ. All honor and power, are really just his without ending. True indeed.

The suffering of believer(s)

12 Beloved(pl), don't be surprised due to great persecution (which seem to be) testing you(pl). Don't think this is an amazing thing that happened to you(pl), 13 but rather, be happy(pl) because you(pl) can_experience suffering like the suffering of Christ, so_that you(pl) will_ indeed _be very happy at the time that his splendor is revealed. 14 You(pl) are/will_be_blessed if/when you(pl) are mocked due to your(pl) believing/faith (in) Christ, because that's the sign that the strong/powerful Spirit is living in you(pl), the Spirit of God. 15 But don't permit(pl) that one of you(pl) suffers because he/she is a killer/murderer, a thief, does evil, or interferes with the work/actions of other(s). 16 But if one person sufferes because he/she is a Christian, it's_necessary that he/she not be_ashamed, but rather, he/she (should) praise God because he/she is considered under_the_jursidiction of Christ.

17 Because the time has come God will_judge, and this will_begin with his people. And if we(incl) (are) the first judged, what maybe will_come to those who didn't believe the Good Report of/about God? 18 [xr]And it's written in the Writings,

“If it's difficult for a righteous person to be_saved,

how much more (for) those who don't follow/imitate God and the sinner(s)!”

19 Therefore, those who suffer according to the will of God, need to continue to do good and to entrust their life(s) to the faithful Creator of them which is God.

Advice to believers

51 So then, I urge you(pl) elders of believers there, that are also just like me also an elder. I myself, I saw the suffering of Christ and I will_be_given_a_portion of his honor when he is revealed in_the_future. 2 [xr]What I urge you(pl), (is) to look_after(pl) the people that have_been_entrusted to you(pl) by God because they are_like his sheep. But look_after them not because you(pl) are just persuaded, but rather, because that's your(pl) desire according to the will of God for you(pl). Do(pl) that not because you(pl) will_be_paid excessively, but rather, because you(pl) really want to_help them. 3 Don't(pl) fiercely command them who have_been_entrusted to you(pl), but rather, make your(pl) lifestyle(s) an example for them. 4 And if/when the Leader of the Caretaker of sheep who is Jesus Christ appears, you(pl) will_receive a very splendid reward that will_ not _be_removed.

5 [xr]You(pl) teenage_boys also, you(pl) need to comply with your(pl) elders. And you(pl) all, you(pl) need to be humble/low to each one, because it's written in the Writings, “The proud are enemy(s) of God, but he help on the humble.” 6 [xr]Therefore be humble/low(pl) towards the very strong/powerful God so_that you(pl) are lifted_up by him in the day that he has chosen. 7 Give(pl) to him (what) you(pl) are sad/worried_about because he is concerned about you(pl).

8 Watch_out(pl) and don't(pl) be carefree! Because your(pl) enemy the master of evil_spirits, roams_round_and_round like a lion growling looking for (what) he/she will_chomp_on. 9 Resist(pl) him and tighten(pl) your(pl) believing/faith, because you(pl) know that your(pl) siblings in believing here on top of the earth, are/will_ also _go_through suffering just like that. 10 God who is the source of all blessing chose you(pl) so_that you(pl) will_be_able_to_receive without end his honor as a result of our(incl) oneness with Christ. When your(pl) just short suffering has finished, you(pl) will_be_complete in God, he will_tighten your(pl) believing/faith, you(pl) will_be_strengthened by him so_that your(pl) believing/faith will_ not _be_moved. 11 The power without ending is just his. True indeed.

Final greetings
