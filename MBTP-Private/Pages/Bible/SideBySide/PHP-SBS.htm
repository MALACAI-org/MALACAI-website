Ka sulat ni Pablu te

The letter by Paul to

Matig-Pilipus

The (ones) from_Philippi

Igpewun-a

Introduction

Ka seini se sulat ni Pablu, impeuyan din diye te migmalintutuu diye te Pilipus ne sakup te prubinsya te Masidunya (Me Himu 16:6-40). Sika ne punduk te migmalintutuu ka an-anayan ne impes-ek ni Pablu diye te nasud te Ruma ne sakup te Iyurupa. Insulat din seini taheed te naprisu sikandin puun te pegwali rin te Meupiya ne Panugtulen meyitenged ki Hisu Kristu.

This letter by Paul (MS Pablu), he sent to the believers in Philippi (MS Pilipus) under_the_jurisdiction of the province of Masidunya (Acts 16:6-40). That group of believers was the first which he established (LIT built) in the nation of Roma under_the_jurisdiction of Iyurupa. He wrote this while he was imprisoned from his preaching the Good Report concerning Jesus Christ (MS Hisu Kristu).

Ka an-anayan ne tuyu ni Pablu te migsulat te seini ne baseen, su egkeupian sikandin ne egpasalamat te migmalintutuu diye te Pilipus tenged te dasag ne impeuyan dan kandin eyew te pegbulig kandin te seeye ne timpu. Ka igkarangeb ne tuyu din te migsulat kayi, su eyew egkabagget sikandan wey egkalig-en ka pegpalintutuu ran minsan te kalised ne migginguma kandan wey minsan degma diye te kandin. Migtambahan sikandan ni Pablu ne keilangan ne egpariralem sikandan iling te pegpariralem ni Hisus te kayi pad sikandin te kalibutan. Impasuman-suman din man-e sikandan ne tenged de te keupiya wey keyid-u te Manama wey te pegpalintutuu ran ki Kristu ne naluwas sikandan. Ne kenad ne keilangan ne egsalig pad sikandan te Balaud ni Muwisis wey te tuluuwen te me etew. Migsulat man-e sikandin meyitenged te kahale wey keupianan ne igbehey te Manama te seeye se nasabeka diye ki Kristu.

The first purpose of Paul in writing this document (LIT reading), was because he wanted to thank the believers at Philippi due to the gift which they sent to him in_order_to_help him at that time. His second purpose in writing this, is that they would_be_encouraged and their faith strengthened even through the difficulties that came to them and even also to him. They were advised by Paul that they need to be humble like the humility of Jesus when he was still here in the world. He reminded them again that it's just due to the goodness and mercy of God (MS Manama) and their faith in Christ (MS Kristu) that they are_saved. They don't need to continue to trust in the Law of Muwisis and in the traditions of the people. He also wrote about joy and well-being that God will_give to those united in Christ.

Ka pegpariralem ni Kristu

The humility of Christ
