Igpewun-a

Introduction

Te timpu ne imbunsuran e te pegwali ka Meupiya ne Panugtulen meyitenged ki Hisus, wey te indawat e te kene ne me Hudiyu, duen e migginguma ne inse. Iyan inse ne keilangan kun naa ne eg-ikul iya te Balaud ni Muwisis ka etew eyew egkeyimu sikandin ne malehet ne migpalintutuu. Mig-apul si Pablu te kene ne keilangan sika su ka pegpalintutuu re ka malehet ne egpuunan te umul due ki Kristu, ne pinaahi rue egkeyimu ne eleg diye te tangkaan te Manama ka etew. Piru diye te me punduk te migmalintutuu te Galasya, ka sabeka ne prubinsya te Ruma diye te Asya Minur, due me etew ne migsupak ki Pablu wey migpalintutuu ne ka etew keilangan ne egtuman iya te Balaud ni Muwisis eyew egkeyimu ne eleg diye te tangkaan te Manama.

At the time that the Good Report about Hisus began to be preached, and to be received by the non Hudiyus, a question arose. The question was if the people indeed needed to follow the Law of Moses (MS Muwisis) to become true believers. Paul (MS Pablu) argued that it is not required because faith is the true source of life in Christ (MS Christ), and by means of that people (are) worthy in front of God (MS Manama). But there in the groups of believers in Galatia (MS Galaysa), a province of Ruma (MS Ruma) there in Asya Minur, there were people who disagreed with Paul and believed that the people needed to indeed obey the Law of Moses in_order to become worthy in front of God.

Ka seini ne Sulat ni Pablu te Matig-Galasya, insulat din eyew egpakalibed diye te malehet ne pegpalintutuu seeye se me etew ne neuyan te kene ne malehet ne pegpanulu. Imbunsuran din seini pinaahi te pegpangguhud din ke nekey ka egpuunan ne neyimu sikandin ne apustulis. Migpamalehet sikandin ne kene ne diye egpuun te geem te me etew ka pegkaapustulis din su diye iya te Manama wey labew te langun, diye sikandin ipeuyan te Manama te kene ne me Hudiyu. Ne dutu, migpakannekal sikandin ka egpakig-apul ne ware lein ne paahi ne egkeyimu ne eleg diye te tangkaan te Manama ka me etew, gawas te pegpalintutuu re iya.
