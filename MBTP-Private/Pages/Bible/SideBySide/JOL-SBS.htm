The document of/by

HUWIL

Igpewun-a

Introduction

Ka seini ne baseen, nasulat te me hep-at ne gatus (400) ne leg-un wey ke me lalimma ne gatus (500) ne leg-un naa te ware pad neetew si Kristu, te timpu ne iyan migmandu te duma ne me nasud ka me Hari te Pirsya. Ka migpangguhud te seini ne guhuren iyan si prupita Huwil ne anak ni Pituwil.

This document, was_written about four hundred (400) years or about five hundred (500) years before Kristu was_born, at the time when the kings of Pirsya were_ still _ruling the other nations. The (one who) told this story was the prophet Huwil the child of Pituwil.

Nasulat ka seini ne baseen tenged su impakita te Manama ki Huwil ka meyitenged te pegpaney-paney te peggukum wey peglegpad te Manama te me nasud. Te pegkeyitabu kayi, nakita rin ka sabeka ne palinneu te pegginguma te aldew te Magbebaye ne Manama, ka timpu ne iyan peglegpad te Manama te seeye se migsupak te igkeupii rin ne meupiya. Impanengneng te prupita ka pegpangumew te Magbebaye ne Manama te keet-etawan eyew eg-inniyug sikandan te me sale dan. Impanengneng din man-e ka saad te Magbebaye ne Manama ne igpalibed din ka meupiya ne peg-ugpe te keet-etawan din wey egpanalanginan din sikandan. Nasulat man-e kayi te seini ne baseen ka saad te Magbebaye ne Manama ne egpeuyan sikandin te Panisingan din diye te langun ne keet-etawan din.

This document was_written because God (MS Manama) showed Joel concerning the warning of judgment and punishing of nations by God. When this was_happening, he saw one sign of the arrival of the day of the Lord God, the time of God's judging to those who disobeyed the good_thing that he likes/desires. The prophet made_known the calling of the Lord God of his people so_that they will_turn_back from their sins. He made_known also the promise of the Lord God that he will_restore the good living_condition of his people and he will_bless them. Also in this document is written the promise of the Lord God that he will_send his Spirit to all his people.
