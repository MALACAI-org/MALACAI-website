Ulahingen

Song

ni Sulumun

of Solomon

Igpewun-a

Introduction

Ka Ulahingen ne baseen, nasulat ka pegpaalihey, pegpaheyinaweey, wey pegpaasaweey. Nasulat man-e te seini ne baseen ka pegkahale te lukes wey te malitan tenged te pegpaheyinaweey ran. Ka me etew te seini ne baseen iyan ka lukes, ka malitan, me mengebay te Hirusalim, wey ka me kanakan.

The Song document, writes_about having_fiancèes, loving_each_other, and marrying. This document also writes_about the joy of a male and of a female due to their loving_each_other. The people in this document (are) the male, the woman, maidens of Hirusalim, and young_men.

Ka Ulahingen ne baseen, impanunggiling layun te me Hudiyu te geyinawa te Manama wey te keet-etawan din, wey para te migmalintutuu ki Hisus, impanunggiling seini te rilasyun ni Hisus wey te simbaan.

The Song document, always illustrates to Hudiyus about the love (between) God (MS Manama) and his people, and for the believers of Hisus, it illustrates the relationship (between) Hisus and the church.
