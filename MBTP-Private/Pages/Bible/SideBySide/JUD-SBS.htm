Ka sulat ni

The letter of

Hudas

Jude

Igpewun-a

Introduction

Ka seini ne sulat, insulat ni Hudas ne hari ni Santiyagu. (Piru kene ne sikandin ka Hudas ne mig-akal ki Hisus.) Ne igpeendiye din naa seini se sulat te me Kristuwanen ne ware ta nanengnengi ke hendei nangugpe. Kayi te sulat, impanpanayan din ka me Kristuwanen meyitenged te seeye se me talagpanulu te kene ne malehet ne egmapitew-pitew re. Migpanulu sikandan ne egkaayun kun ded ne egpabulus eggimu te sale ka migmalintutuu su egpasayluwen kun ded sikandan te hiimenawen ne Manama. Sika ka egpuunan ne insulat seini ni Hudas su eyew egpakabantey ka me Kristuwanen te sika ne me etew, wey eyew egpakaayad-ayad e sikandan eg-ikul te malehet ne pegpanulu ne egpuun te Manama. Imbagget din degma ka me talagbasa te keilangan ne egpakannekal egpabulus te pegpalintutuu ran te Manama duma te peg-inniyug te mareet ne me himu.

This letter, (it) was_written by Jude (MS Hudas) the younger_sibling of Santiyagu. (But he's not the Hudas that betrayed Jesus.) And he sent it to Christians whom we(incl) don't know where they lived(pl). Here in the letter, he warns the Christians about those teachers of what is not true and are just hypocrites. They teach that it's_possible [reportedly] for believers to continue sinning because they will_ just _be_forgiven [reportedly] by the compassionate God (MS Manama). That is the reason that this was_written by Jude so_that the Christians can_watch_out for those people, and so_that they can_be_careful to follow/copy the correct teaching that is from God. He also encouraged the readers that (they) need to strive to continue in their believing God along_with turning_from evil acts.

11 [xr]Sikeddiey si Hudas ka migpeuyan te seini ne sulat. Uripen a ni Hisu Kristu wey hari a ni Santiyagu.

11 [xr]I Jude (am) the (one) sending this letter. I (am) a slave of Jesus Christ and a younger_sibling of Santiyagu.

Egpangemusta a te seeye se in-alam te Manama ne Amey, ne miggeyinawaan wey intanggu rin taman te peglibed ni Hisu Kristu.

I greet those (who) are chosen by God the Father, and loved and looked_after by him until the return of Jesus Christ.

2 Ka Manama ka egbehey kaniyu te timul pad ne keyid-u, keupianan wey geyinawa.

2 (May) God give you(pl) still more mercy, peace and love.

Ka himu wey legpad te me talagpanulu te kene ne malehet

The deed(s) and punishment of the teachers of what is not true

3 Pinalangge ku ne me suled, minsan amana ad iya egkabagget ne egsulat kaniyu meyitenged te kaluwasan ne insaamulan ta, piru neisip ku ne keilangan ne iyan ku igsulat ka pegbanasal kaniyu te keilangan ne pakannekali niyu te eggen-gen ka kamalehetan ne kasabeka re iya ibehey te Manama diye te keet-etawan din. 4 Iyan egpuunan ne inlalag ku sika, tenged su due me etew ne miggeles-heles de migpasewug-sewug kaniyu. Ka sika ne me etew, tapey e nasulat ka meyitenged te peglegpad kandan. Kene sikandan eg-ikul te Manama wey impasuwey ran ka pegpanulu meyitenged te keupiya wey keyid-u te Manama ta su egkaayun kun ded ne eggimu ki te mareet wey malindit ne igkeupii te lawa ta. Te iling due ne paahi, mig-inniyuhan dan ka sabsabeka ne Ahalen ta wey Magbebaye ne si Hisu Kristu.

3 My beloved friends/siblings, even though I was really looking_forward to writing to you(pl) about our(incl) common salvation, but I thought that I need to write to you(pl) in_order_for encouraging that you(pl) strive to hold the truth that God gave just once to his people. 4 The reason that I said that, (is) because there are (those who) secretly mixed_among you(pl). Those people, it was previously written about their punishment. They doesn't follow/imitate God and they altered the teaching about the goodness and mercy of our(incl) God because it's_possible [reportedly] to do the bad and dirty desire(s) of our(incl) body(s). In that way, they turn_ their _backs_on our(incl) one_and_only Master and Lord Jesus Christ.

5 [xra][xrb]Minsan ke nakanengneng kew e te langun ne iglalag ku, egkeupian e pad iya man-e ne igpasuman-suman kaniyu ka pegluwas te Magbebaye te keet-etawan te Israil puun te Ihiptu. Ne nataman, inggule din e seeye se ware migpalintutuu. 6 Kema, sumsumana niyu ka me panalihan ne nanleke te katungdanan dan wey nasi nangawe diye te ugpaan dan. Imbaku sikandan te Manama te keddina ne kene egkabigtus[fn] wey imprisu diye te durusilemi ne bitu eyew igtahahe te dakel ne aldew te peggukum. 7 [xr]Kene niyu degma kalingawi ka siyudad te Suduma wey Gumura wey ka nakalingut ne me lungsud dutu. Me agkayen ka me etew diye wey me bantut, sikan naa ka insilaban sikandan te hapuy ne kene egkeebukan, isip paney-paney te langun ne etew ne eglegparan iya ka makasesale.

5 [xra][xrb]Even_though you(pl) know everything that I will_say, I still really want again to remind_ you(pl) _about the salvation of the Lord of the people of Israil from Ihiptu. And after_that, he exterminated those that didn't believe. 6 Look/Listen_then, remember(pl) the angels that released(pl) their responsibilities and instead left(pl) their living_place. God bound them with chain(s) that can't be broken[fn] and imprisoned them in a dark pit prepared for the great day of judging. 7 [xr]Don't forget(pl) the cities of Suduma and Gumura and the surrounding towns there. The people there had an immoral_spirit and (were) homosexuals, so then they were burned_up by fire that doesn't go_out, considered as a warning to all the people that sinners indeed will_be_punished.

8 Iling naan ded ka me etew ne migsewug kaniyu, due me taheinep dan ne iyan egpuunan ne miglinditan nikandan ka me lawa ran tenged te pegparuma-ruma ran dutu, wey mig-ang-anggaan dan ka katenged te Manama wey imbaley-baley ran ka maddesen diye te langit. 9 [xra][xrb][xrc]Piru minsan si Mikail, ka pangulu te me panalihan te Manama, ware ma miggimu te iling due te pegpakig-ehet din te ahalen te me busew. Ware sikandin nakabayung te ahalen te me busew pinaahi te peglalag din te mareet te nekeg-apul sikandan meyitenged te minatey, ka lawa ni Muwisis. Su migkahi re si Mikail, “Egsaparan ka te Manama!” 10 Piru imbaley-baley mule te seini ne me etew ka langun ne ware dan nasabut. Egpekeiling sikandan te me mahintalunan ne egpangawang de due eggimu ne kene egsuman-suman pa, sikan naa ka egsahap iya sikandan. 11 [xra][xrb][xrc]Mekeyid-u-hid-u sikandan! Su mig-iling sikandan te mareet ne inggimu ni Kain, wey mig-ilingan dan si Balaam ne naalamlam te seleppi, sikan naa ka nekeyimu e te mareet. Ne egkahule sikandan tenged te pegsukul ran te Manama iling ki Kura. 12 Ka sika ne me etew, egpakamalindit kaniyu ke egpakiglagpu ne egkeen kaniyu te pegsahakeen niyu te Iglabung te Magbebaye. Egpakamalindit sikandan tenged su iyan dan de egsumsumanen ka pegkeetew ran wey ware keyilew ran. Egpekeiling sikandan te me gapun ne ware mig-uyan te uran, ne inniyup te kalamag. Ne iling man-e sikandan te kayu ne ware migbebehas minsan te tigpamehas e, ne impamarut sikandan wey nahangu-hangu en iya. 13 Iling sikandan te makeseg ne bunlud te dahat ne egkakita ka mekeyilew-hilew ne himu ran iling te buke. Iling man-e sikandan te nakatulimpas ne me bituen peendiye te inged ne durusilemi ne intahahe te Manama ne egpeugpaan kandan te ware egtamanan.

8 It's like that with the people that mixed_among you(pl), they had dreams that (are) the source of them defiling (LIT dirtying) their bodies since they followed_ them _along, and they reject the authority of God and the blaspheme the strong (ones) in heaven/sky. 9 [xra][xrb][xrc]But even Mikail, the leader of the angels of God, didn't do like that when he disagreed with the master of evil_spirits. He didn't accuse the master of evil_spirits by_means of his speaking evil when they argued about the corpse, the body of Muwisis. Because Mikail just said, “(May) God punish you(sg)!” 10 But on_the_contrary these people blasphemed all that they didn't understand. They are_like wild animals that just act instinctively without thinking, so_then they are in danger_of_destruction. 11 [xra][xrb][xrc]They are to be pitied! They imitated the evil that Kain did, and they copied Balaam who was greedy for money, so_that's why he did evil. And they will_be_exterminated due to their resistance to God like Kura. 12 Those people, if (they) join_with you(pl) in eating your(pl) feast of the Supper of the Lord defile it (lit make it dirty). They defile it (LIT make it dirty) because they just think about themselves and have_no shame. They are_like clouds that don't bring rain, that are blown by the wind. They are again like trees that don't bear_fruit even in fruiting_season, and they are uprooted and wither_up. 13 They are_like strong waves of the sea whose embarrassing acts can be seen like foam. They are also like veering stars going to the very_dark place prepared by God as a living_place for them without ending.

14 [xr]Ne si Inuk, ka igkeen-em ne kabuhalan ni Adan, ka migtagne meyitenged kandan. Migkahi si Inuk, “Pammineg kew! Eggendini ka Magbebaye duma te pila ne luyuran[fn] te matulus ne panalihan din 15 eyew te peggukum te langun. Egpataheen din ka langun ne kene eg-ikul kandin ne makasesale sikandan tenged te langun ne dereeti ne hinimuwan dan, wey tenged te mangune-kune ne peglemet dan kandin.” 16 Ka sika ne me etew, me sagsahukulen, wey ware igkabennali. Intuman dan ka mareet ne igkeupii ran, wey migpabekal te me pakpak dan.[fn] Egguluan dan ka duma eyew eggimuwen ne teytey te mareet ne igkeupii ran.

14 [xr]And Inuk, the sixth descendant of Adan, (is) the (one who) prophesied about them. Inuk said, “Listen(pl)! The Lord is_coming_here along_with some batallions[fn] of his holy angels 15 in_order to judge everyone. He will_make_known to all who don't follow/imitate him that they are sinners as a result of all their wicked deeds, and due to their terrible mocking/insulting of him.” 16 Those people, (they) are complainers, and not contented. They follow (LIT obey) their own bad desires, and flare their wings.[fn] They flatter others so_that (they) become a bridge to their evil desire(s).

Ka me pegpaney-paney wey pegpanulu

Warnings and teaching

17 Piru me pinalangge ku ne me suled, keilangan ne egsumsumanen niyu ka intagne te me apustulis te Magbebaye ta ne si Hisu Kristu. 18 [xr]Migkahiyan kew nikandan, “Te katammanan ne timpu, due me etew ne me lemeten, wey egtumanen dan ka mareet ne igkeupii ran ne igkeepes te Manama.” 19 Sikandan ka nakapabaad-baad kaniyu. Iyan dan imparapit ka igkeupii te lawa ran wey ware diye te kandan ka Panisingan te Manama. 20 Piru sikaniyu pinalangge ku ne me suled, keilangan ne ipatulin[fn] niyu ka pegpalintutuu niyu te Manama, wey ampu kew sumale te igpanulu te Panisingan te Manama. 21 Palunggehaat kew due te geyinawa te Manama taheed te egtetahad kew te keyid-u te Magbebaye ta ne si Hisu Kristu ne egbehey te umul ne ware egtamanan.

17 But beloved friends/siblings, you(pl) need to think_of/remember what was prophesied by the apostles of our(incl) Lord Jesus Christ. 18 [xr]They said to you(pl), “At the final time, there will_be_people who are mockers, and they will_follow (LIT obey) their own evil desire(s) that God dislikes.” 19 They (are) the (ones) causing_ you(pl) _to_be_divided. They side with the desire of their body(s) and the Spirit of God is not with them. 20 But you(pl) my beloved friends/siblings, you(pl) need to cause_to_grow[fn] your(pl) faith/believing in God, and pray(pl) according to (what) the Spirit of God will_teach. 21 Remain(pl) in the love of God while you(pl) wait for the mercy of our(incl) Lord Jesus Christ who will_give life without end.

22 Ne ikeyid-u niyu seeye se migduwa-ruwa te pegpalintutuu ran, 23 wey luwasa niyu ka duma pinaahi te pegsalukagmit kandan puun te hapuy. Ikeyid-u niyu degma ka duma ne makasesale, piru bantey kew wey pakaddiyu kew minsan te me kumbale dan ne nabiluwan te maddeet ne igkeupii ran.

22 And show_pity to those who doubt their faith, 23 and save(pl) others by_means of snatching them from the fire. Also pity other sinners, but guard and keep_distant(pl) even from their clothes dirtied by their evil(pl) desires.

Ka peg-ampu te pegsaye te Manama

Prayer and praise to God

24 Na, egdayanen ka Manama ne egpekeyimu ne egbantey kaniyu eyew kene kew egkasaligkat te sale. Sikandin keureme ka eg-uyan kaniyu ne ware igkarew-ey wey egkahale-gale diye te tetelesi ne tangkaan din. 25 Egsayeen ka migsabsabeka ne Manama ne Manluluwas ta pinaahi ki Hisu Kristu ne Magbebaye ta. Ka katelesan, ka dakel ne kabantug, ka karesen, wey ka katenged, kandin de iya puun pad dengan wey te ware egtamanan. Malehet iya.

24 Now_then, (may) God be_praised who is able to watch/guard you(pl) so_that you(pl) don't stumble over sin. In_the_future he will_take you(pl) without anything_to_be_scolded_for and very_happy into his splendid presence. 25 (May) the one God our(incl) Savior be_praised by_means of Jesus Christ our(incl) Lord. The splendor, the great prestige, the strength/power, and the authority, were just his from long_ago/before and without ending. True indeed.
