Introduction

Ka ikarangeb ne sulat ni Pidru, igpeendiye din te migmalintutuu diye te nalein-lein ne lungsud te Asya Minur (1 Pidru 1:1, 2 Pidru 3:1). Ka tuyu din te migsulat kayi, iyan sika se pegsupak te himu te seeye se migpanulu te kene ne malehet, wey pegsupak te malindit ne eggimuwen te me etew ne naangey ran puun te sika ne pegpanulu. Purisu imbanasalan din sikandan ne kene egpalintutuu te sika ne pegpanulu su iyan de egpalintutuuwen ka pegpanulu meyitenged ki Kristu. Keilangan degma ne due igtimul ran te pegpalintutuu ran: meupiya ne pegbebatasan, katuenan, egtigkel te pegkeetew, kene egkapelaan ne egtetahad, egtuman te Manama, wey eggeyinawa te duma.

The second letter of Pidru, he sent to the believers there in various cities of Asya Minur (1 Pidru 1:1, 2 Pidru 3:1). The reason for his writing this, (it) is to oppose the work/deed(s) of those (who) teach (what's) not correct, and to oppose the dirty actions of the people that they got from that teaching. Therefore he urged them not to believe that teaching because what_ (they_should) _believe is just the teaching about Christ. It's also necessary that there's something_ they _add to their believing: good behavior/custom(s), skill(s)/knowledge, controlling self, not becoming_tired of waiting, obeying God (MS Manama), and loving other(s).

Migpanulu ka seeye ne me etew te kene kun e eglibed si Kristu tenged su nalugluhayan e, ware kun de due miglibed sikandin. Piru intabak sika ni Pidru te eglibed ded iya si Kristu, piru kene egkeupian ka Manama ne egkaparusaan ka minsan hentew, su ka igkeupii rin ne eg-inniyug ka me etew te sale dan.

Those people taught that Christ won't return since it's already been_quite_a_while, and he hasn't returned. But that is answered by Pidru that Christ will_ indeed _return, but God doesn't want any one to be_punished, because his desire is that the people turn_from their sin(s).
