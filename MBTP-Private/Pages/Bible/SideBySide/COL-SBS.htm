Igpewun-a

Introduction

Ka seini ne sulat ni Pablu igpeendiye din te migmalintutuu diye te Kulusas. Ka Kulusas, sabeka ne lungsud diye te Asya Minur ne diye dapit te igsile te Ipisu. Ware pad nakapanumbaley diye te kandan si Pablu piru amana rin e igkasasew wey impeuyanan din te me talagbulig ne egpuun te Ipisu.

This letter by Paul (MS Pablu) he sent to the believers in Colosse (MS Kulusas). Colosse, was a (LIT one) town in Asya Minur located there east of Ipisu. Paul had_ not yet _been_able_to_visit them but he was very concerned and he sent helpers (to them) from Ipisu.

Nakarineg si Pablu ne due me talagpanulu ne kene ne malehet ne egpanulu te migmalintutuu diye te Kulusas. Migpanulu sikandan te emun ke due etew ne egkeupian ne egkaluwas wey egkeupian ne egmaralem pad ka peg-ila rin te Manama, kene kun ne eleg ka pegpalintutuu rin ki Kristu. Su kahiyen pa nikandan ne keilangan ne egtuman pad sikandin te duma ne ingkene ne eggimuwen wey me tulumanen. Ne keilangan man-e ne egsimba sikandin te me panalihan.

Purisu insulatan ni Pablu ka migmalintutuu diye te Kulusas wey impanengneng din sikandan te kamalehetan meyitenged ki Hisu Kristu. Migkahiyan din sikandan te si Hisus iya ka miggimu te langun ne egkakita ta, purisu labew pad iya sikandin te me panalihan, wey te duma pad ne kene egkakita. Egkakita degma diye te kandin ka pegka-Manama te Amey rin, wey warad e lein ne egpuunan te umul te migmalintutuu gawas kandin. Sikan naa, keilangan ne ka migmalintutuu, kene eg-ikul te me tulumanen wey pegbebatasan te me etew, ke kene, keilangan ne si Kristu re iya ka eg-ikulen.

Therefore Paul wrote to the believers in Colosse and informed them of the truth about Jesus Christ (MS Hisu Kristu). He told them that Jesus (MS Hisus) indeed is the (one who) made all that we(incl) see, therefore he is greater than the angels, and of yet others that can't be seen.
