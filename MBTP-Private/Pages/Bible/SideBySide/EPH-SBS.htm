Introduction

Ka seini ne Sulat ni Pablu te Matig-Ipisu, insulat din te timpu ne diye sikandin te prisuwan. Ne kene ne ka punduk te migmalintutuu re diye te Ipisu ka egpeuyanan te seini ne sulat, ke kene, minsan degma ka duma ne me punduk te migmalintutuu.

Iyan dakel ne tuyu ni Pablu kayi ka meyitenged te planu te Manama ne “egsabekeen din ka langun ne inggimu rin diye te langit wey kayi te tane wey igpasakup din ki Kristu” (1:10).

The big purpose of Paul here is about the plan of God (MS Manama) that “he will_unite everything that he made there in sky/heaven and here on the earth/ground and he will_cause_(them)_to_be_ruled by Christ” (1:10).

Diye te an-anayan ne baad te sulat, nasulat e ka meyitenged te pegsabeka. In-ay-ayaran ni Pablu te pegpasabut ka meyitenged te pamaahi te Manama ne Amey te peg-alam te keet-etawan din, te pegpasaylu wey te pegluwas kandan puun te me sale dan pinaahi ki Hisu Kristu ne anak din.

There in the first part of the letter, is written about unity. Paul carefully explained about the way of God the Father in choosing his people, in forgiving and in saving them from their sins by_means of Jesus Christ his child.

Diye te igkarangeb ne baad te sulat, mighangyu si Pablu te seeye se talagbasa te seini ne sulat, ne keilangan ne egkakita ka pegsabeka ran diye ki Kristu pinaahi te pegsabeka ran degma diye te duma.

Masalig ka inggamit ni Pablu ne panunggilingan meyitenged te pegsabeka te keet-etawan te Manama ki Kristu: ka migmalintutuu, iyan sika ka lawa ni Kristu, ne sikandin ka ulu; egpekeiling te sabeka ne baley, ne ka batu ne pabunsuran, iyan iya si Kristu; ne egpekeiling degma sikandan te asawa ne malitan, ne si Kristu ka asawa ran.

Paul used many examples/parables about the unity of the people of God in Christ: the believers, they (are) the body of Christ, and he is the head; like a house, the foundation stone, it is really Christ; and they are also like a wife (LIT female spouse), and Christ is their husband/spouse.

Egkanengnengan ta degma kayi ka meyitenged te eleg ne pegbebatasan te alunggun, te me taltalaanak, wey te me uripen wey me ahalen. Ne diye te katamanan te seini ne sulat, egkanengnengan ta man-e ka meyitenged te imbehey te Manama diye te migmalintutuu ne egkahamit dan eyew te pegsukul te limbung ni Meibulan kandan.
