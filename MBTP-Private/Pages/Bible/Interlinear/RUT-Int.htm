Introduction

Ka seini ne baseen, migpangguhud meyitenged te neyitabu dengan te me talewukum pad ka migmandu te inged ne Israil. Te seeye ne timpu, miggingumaan te masumpit ne bitil ka seeye ne inged, sikan naa ka miggalin ka taltalaanak ne ensi Ilimilik diye te inged ne Muwab. Nataman, migpatey e si Ilimilik wey diyad nenakaasawa ka daruwa ne anak din ne me lukes, wey egngaranan ki Rut ka sabeka ne malitan ne naasawa te anak din. Te pegpatey te asawa ni Rut, impapitew rin ka meupiya ne batasan wey geyinawa rin diye te anuhang din ne malitan ne si Nawumi. Te impanalanginan e man-e te Manama ka keet-etawan din, mig-uli si Nawumi diye te Israil wey migduma si Rut. Ne napamalu si Rut te karumaan ded te an-anayan ne asawa rin, ne puun due, neyimu sikandin ne kaapuan ni Dabid, ka mabantug ne Hari te Israil. Te naluhey-luhey, neetew e degma si Hisu Kristu ne kabuhalan ded ni Rut.

This document (LIT reading), tells about what happened long_ago when the judges still ruled the place of Israil. At that time, severe famine came that place, that's why Ilimilik and his family moved to the place Muwab. Then, Ilimilik died and his two male children got married there, and the woman who had_become_the_wife of his child was_named Rut. When Rut's husband died, she showed her good habits and love to her mother-in-law named Nawumi. When God (MS Manama) blessed again his people, Nawumi returned_home to Israil and Rut accompanied her. And Rut has_been_married_widow by a relative of her first spouse, and from that, she became an ancestor of Dabid, the famous king of Israil. After_a_long_time, Hisu Kristu was also born that was a descendant of Rut as well.

Kayi te seini ne baseen Me Talewukum egkanengnengan ta ne mareet ka neula-ula te me Hudiyu puun su mig-inniyug sikandan te Manama. Ne kayi te Guhuren meyitenged ki Rut impakita kanta ne impanalanginan ka sabeka ne etew ne kene ne Hudiyu puun su migparani sikandin te Manama te Israil.

Here in this document (LIT reading) Judges we(incl) will_know that bad_things were experienced by the Hudiyus because they distanced/turned from God. And here in the Story concerning Rut (it/this) shows us(incl) that a (LIT one) person who wasn't a Hudiyu was blessed because he/she approached the God of Israil.

Ka peggalin enni Ilimilik diye te Muwab

The transfer of Ilimilik and_company to Muwab

11 Te timpu ne iyan pad migmandu te inged ne Israil ka me talewukum, due masumpit ne bitil ne migginguma te seeye ne inged. Ne due etew ne matig-Bitlihim ne sakup te Huda ne miggalin diye te inged ne Muwab, duma te asawa rin wey te daruwa ne anak dan ne me lukes eyew diye pad egtagtaheed eg-ugpe. 2 Egngaranan ki Ilimilik ka seeye ne etew wey ka ngaran te asawa rin, si Nawumi. Ne ka daruwa ne anak dan ne me lukes egngaranan ki Malun wey ki Kiliyun. Me kabuhalan sikandan ni Iprata ne matig-Bitlihim ne sakup te Huda. Ne miggendiye sikandan te Muwab wey diyad mig-ugpe. 3 Nataman, migpatey e si Ilimilik, sikan naa ka nabalu e si Nawumi wey iyan din naan de duma ka daruwa ne anak din ne me lukes. 4 Ka tahasuled, nangasawa te me malitan ne matig-Muwab. Ka sabeka ne malitan egngaranan ki Urpa wey ka dangeb egngaranan ki Rut. Te me sapulu e ne leg-un sikandan ka mig-ugpe diye, 5 migpatey e degma ensi Malun wey Kiliyun, sikan naa ka si Nawumi naan de ka nasame te taltalaanak.

11 In the time that the judges still ruled the place of Israil, there_was a severe famine came in that place. And there_was a person from_Bitlihim under_the_jurisdiction of Huda who moved to a place of Muwab, with his spouse and two male children in_order to live there temporarily. 2 The person was_named Ilimilik and his spouse's name, Nawumi. And their two male children were_named Malun and Kiliyun. They were descendants of Iprata from_Bitlihim that was under_the_jurisdiction of Huda. They went to Muwab and lived there. 3 And then, Ilimilik died, so then Nawumi became widow and her two male children were left with her. 4 The siblings/cousins, married women from_Muwab. One woman was_named Urpa and the other named Ruth. When they had lived there about ten years, 5 Malun and Kiliyun also died, so then just Nawumi was the only one_left in the family.

Ka pegduma ni Rut ki Nawumi te mig-uli diye te Bitlihim

The accompanying of Rut to Nawumi returning_home to Bitlihim

Seeye naa, migtaggel e sikandin duma te me ambey rin eyew eg-awe e diye te Muwab. 7 Ne nanligkat e sikandan ka egpeendiye te Huda. Ne iling te keupiya niyu kayi te keddiey wey diye te me asawa niyu ne nammatey e, iling ded degma ka keupiya ne igpapitew te Magbebaye ne Manama due te kaniyu. 9 Ne igtuhut perem te Magbebaye ne Manama ne egpakaasawa kew pad man-e eyew egkatalipun kew e.”

6 Then, Nawumi found_out that the Lord God had blessed his people by_means of giving them a good harvest. At that, she prepared along_with her daughters-in-law to leave Muwab. 7 Then the traveled going to Huda. While they were_walking, 8 Nawumi said to her daughters-in-law, “Go_back(pl) and go_home to your(pl) mothers. And like your(pl) goodness to me and to your(pl) deceased husbands, (may it) will_be_like also the goodness that will_be_shown to you(pl) by the Lord God. 9 And may the Lord God allow you(pl) to marry again so_that you(pl) will_have a new_home (LIT moved into their spouse's house).”

Minsan ke duen pad egkapallateng ku, wey minsan pad ke egpakaasawa a kuntee te marusilem wey eg-anak te me lukes, 13 egpakatetahad kew naan pad ne egtulin sikandan? Kene kew naan pad eg-asawa tenged kandan? Me ambey, kene iya sika egkaayun, su mig-inniyuhan ad te Manama, wey masumpit pad ka neula-ula ku du te kaniyu.”

11 But Nawumi said, “Daughters-in-law, it is good for you(pl) to return_home. Why do you(pl) still accompany me when I can't have children for me to marry you(pl) with? 12 Go_home because I (am) old and I won't marry again. 14 Ne nanasnasian de iya sikandan migpandalawit. Ne dutu, miggarek e si Urpa te anuhang din ka egpanaha-taha. Piru si Rut, ware mule migpitas te anuhang din. 15 Ne migkahiyan ni Nawumi si Rut, “Na, mig-uli man-e ka iras nu diye te me karumaan din wey diye te manama rin, ne sinundul kad e kandin.”

14 And they wept even_more. And there, Urpa kissed her parent-in-law goodbye. But Ruth, on_the_contrary didn't separate from her parent-in-law. 15 And Nawumi said to Rut, “Now, your(sg) sister-in-law has returned_home to her relatives and to her god, so follow_after her.”

16 Ne migtabak si Rut, “Kena a pehesa ne eg-engked wey egpitas keykew. Su minsan hendei ka eggendiye, diya a degma eggendiye, wey minsan hendei ka eg-ugpe, diya a degma eg-ugpe. Ne ka karumaan nu egkeyimu e degma ne karumaan ku, wey ka Manama nu egkeyimu e degma ne Manama ku. 17 Ne minsan hendei ka egpatey, diya a degma egpatey wey iglebeng. Eglegparan a perem te susumpiti te Magbebaye ne Manama ke egpitas a keykew te minsan nekey ne puunan. Kamatayen de ka egpekegpitas kanta.”

16 Rut answered, “Don't persuade me to leave and to separate from you(sg). Because wherever you(sg) go, I will_ also _go_there, and wherever you(sg) live, I will_ also _live there. And your(sg) relatives will_ also _become my relatives, and your(sg) God will_ also _become my God. 17 Wherever you(sg) die, I will_ also _die there and be buried. 19 Seeye naa, migpabulus e sikandan ka miggipanew taman te nekeuma e sikandan diye te Bitlihim. Newukag ka langun ne etew te pegginguma ran, wey nakakahi ka me malitan, “Si Nawumi iya seini?”

19 So then, they continued traveling until they arrived in Bitlihim. All the people were stirred at their arrival, and the women said, “Is this really Nawumi?”

20 Ne migkahiyan sikandan ni Nawumi, “Kena ad nikaniyu ngarani ki Nawumi, ke kene, si Mara[fn] naan de su amana ne masumpit ka impeula-ula keddiey te Maresen ne Manama. 21 Te pegligkat ku kayi, duen pad keddi ka langun, piru impeuli a te Magbebaye ne Manama kayi ne egkaayu-ayu e. Ne mania te egngaranan e pad nikaniyu ki Nawumi te inlegparan ad wey impaantus ad te Maresen ne Magbebaye ne Manama?”

20 And Nawumi said to them, “Don't call me Nawumi anymore, rather, just Mara[fn] because what the Powerful God caused_me_to_experience was very difficult. 21 When I left here, I still had everything, but I was_sent_home by the Lord God here poor. So why do you(pl) still call me Nawumi when I've been_punished and caused_to_suffer by the Powerful Lord God?”

22 Ne seeye ka peg-awe ni Nawumi diye te Muwab. Mig-uli sikandin diye te Bitlihim duma te ambey rin ne si Rut ne matig-Muwab, wey sikan pad iya eggaani te sibada te pegginguma ran diye te Bitlihim.

22 And that was Nawumi's leaving_from Muwab. She returned_home to Bitlihim with her daughter-in-law Rut from_Muwab, and it was the time of harvesting of barley (Cebuano loan) when they arrived in Bitlihim.

Ka pegpakilalew ni Rut diye te kamet ni Buas

The gleaning of Rut at Buas' field

21 Ne dutu, due etew ne egngaranan ki Buas ne karumaan te asawa ni Nawumi dengan ne si Ilimilik. Meyaman sikandin wey amana ne talahuren ne etew. 2 [xr]Sabeka ne aldew, migkahiyan ni Rut si Nawumi, “Tuhuti a nikeykew ne eggendiye te kamet eyew egpakilalew te egkeengkeran ne me tagdey te me etew ne eg-uyun ne egpakilalawan ku.”

Ganna re sikandin te maselem, wey kunteen pad iya migsanggel eyew egdagdahew eggimeley due te leeb.”

8 Ne nataman, migkahiyan e ni Buas si Rut, “Kema beyi, pammineg ka. Kene kad e pakilalew diye te duma ne kamet. Kayi ke re pakilalew duma te me suluhuanen ku ne me malitan. 9 Pitawa nu ke hendei eggaani ka suluhuanen ku ne me lukes, wey sinundul ka te me malitan. Impanpanayan kud te egkahi ka me lukes ne kene ka eggilabetan. Ne emun ke egkammaraan ka, inum ke re te weyig ne insakug dan.”

Ka peghangyu ni Rut ki Buas

The requesting of Buas by Rut

31 Sabeka ne aldew, migkahiyan ni Nawumi si Rut, “Ambey, egpammitawan ka te asawa eyew egmeupiya pad ka peg-ugpe nu. 2 Nakasuman-suman ke red te karumaan ta ne si Buas ne naruma nu se me suluhuanen ne me malitan? Ne hendiye ka te egdiriek, piru kene ka pakita kandin taman te egpekeimpus e sikandin ka egkeen wey eg-inum. 4 Ne emun ke eglipereng e sikandin, tantanuri nu ke hendei sikandin eggibat. Egkataman, dugpua nu wey wakisa nu ka ulesen diye te kasikuwayan din wey diye ka hibat. Ne dutu, eglalahan ka nikandin ke nekey ka eggimuwen nu.”

Now listen(sg). Later at night, he will_cause_to_tread the barley. 3 So bathe, put_on_perfume, and put_on your(sg) nice dress. And_then, approach (him) and lift the blanket at his feet and lie there. And there, he will_tell you(sg) what to do(sg).”

5 Ne migtabak si Rut, “Uya, eggimuwen ku ka langun ne ingkahi nu.”

6 Seeye naa, miggendiyad e si Rut te diekanan wey inggimu rin ka langun ne ingkahi te anuhang din. 7 Ne hengkayi se nekeimpus e si Buas ka migkeen wey mig-inum, meupiye e ka peggeram-geram din. Ne miggibat e sikandin diye te lenged te impanlimud ne me sibada su eglipereng e. Ne dutu, migpahanad-ganad migdugpu si Rut wey inwakis din e ka ulesen diye te kasikuwayan ni Buas wey miggibat e sikandin. 8 Te egliware te marusilem, ingkemed si Buas wey nekeyimata sikandin. Te pegbalikid din, iyan din igkateu-teu su due malitan ne miggibat diye te kasikuwayan din. 9 Ne mig-inse si Buas, “Hentew kaa?”

6 So then, Rut went there to the trampling_place and did all that her parent-in-law had said to her. 7 And when Buas had_finished eating and drinking, he felt good. Then he lay_down near the gathered barley to sleep. And there, Rut slowly went_there and lifted the blanket at the feet of Buas and she lay_down. 8 In the middle of the night, Buas shook and he was awaken. When he turned_over, he was surprised that there_was a woman lying at his feet. 9 And Buas asked, “Who are you(sg)?”

Ne migtabak sikandin, “Sikeddiey si Rut ne suluhuanen nu. Amana ku pad iya sikeykew ne karumaan, sikan naa ka due katenged nu ne egtanggu keddiey. Purisu pamaluwa ad nikeykew.[fn]”

He/She answered, “I am Rut your(sg) servant. You(sg) (are) my close relative, so you(sg) have responsibility to look after me. Therefore marry_(widow) me.[fn]”

10 Ne migtabak si Buas, “Egpanalanginan ka te Magbebaye ne Manama, beyi. Seini se impakita nu kuntee ne peg-unung te pamilya nu, subla pad te keupiya ne impakita nu dengan te anuhang nu. Su ware ka mig-awes te kanakan pad, meyaman wey ke egkaayu-ayu naa. 11 Sikan naa beyi, kene ka kaaldek. Eggimuwen ku ka langun ne egbuyuen nu, su nakataha ka langun ne duma ku ne nangugpe kayi te meupiya ka ne malitan. 12 [xr]Ne malehet iya ne amane e pad ne karumaan te migpatey e ne asawa nu, sikan naa ka due katenged ku te pegtanggu keykew. Piru duen pad lukes ne karumaan nu ne amana pad ne marani keykew. 13 Ne kayi kad iya hirehe, ne kaaselem te maselem, egpitawen ta ke egdawaten te seeye ne lukes ka katenged te pegpamalu keykew. Ne emun ke egkeupian sikandin, ne meupiya, piru ke kene, egpahunlibet a diye te Manama ne sikeddiey ka egdawat te katenged te pegpamalu keykew. Ne lipereng kad.”

10 And Buas answered, “The Lord God bless you(sg), young_woman. Your(sg) loyalty to your(sg) family that you(sg) have shown today/now, is even better of the goodness that you(sg) have shown before to your(sg) mother-in-law. For you(sg) didn't desire for a single_man, rich or poor. 11 So then young_woman, don't be_afraid. I will_do everything that you(sg) asked for, because all my companion living here know that you(sg) (are) a good woman. 12 [xr]It is indeed true that I (am) a close relative of your(sg) husband who died, thus I have a responsibility to look after you(sg). But there_is still a man who is your(sg) relative that is even closer to you(sg). 13 Now just stay_overnight here, and tomorrow morning, we(incl) will_see if that man will_accept the responsibility to marry_(widow) you(sg). And if he wants to, good, but if not, I swear to God that I will_accept the responsibility to marry_(widow) you(sg). Now go to sleep.”

14 Seeye naa, miggibat e si Rut diye te kasikuwayan ni Buas taman te napawe. Piru mig-enew sikandin te egpamalat-palat pad su eyew kene sikandin egkeilaan te duma ne me etew, su igkeupii ni Buas ne ware egpakataha ne miggendiye sikandin te kandin. 15 Ne migkahiyan sikandin ni Buas, “Ihendini nu ka ingkukuyumbu nu ne hinabel wey belata nu.” Intuman seeye ni Rut wey in-ug-uhan e ni Buas te me hen-em ne gantang ne sibada. Miggenatan pad sikandin ni Buas, wey mig-uli e sikandin diye te siyudad. 16 Te pegginguma rin diye te anuhang din, mig-insaan e sikandin, “Nekey naan e ka natamanan nu ambey?”

14 So then, Rut lay_down at the feet of Buas until dawn. But she got_up before dawn so_that (she) wouldn't be recognized by the other people, because Buas wanted that nobody would know that she went to him. 15 And Buas said to her, “Bring_here(sg) the cloth that you(sg) wrapped around and spread(sg)_ it _out.” Rut obeyed it and Buas poured in about six cans (about twelve kilograms) of barley. Buas lifted_it_onto her, and she returned_home to the city. 16 When she arrived at her parent-in-law, she asked her, “What was your(sg) result daughter-in-law?”

Ne migpangguhuran e ni Rut si Nawumi te langun ne inggimu ni Buas kandin.17 Ne migkahi pad man-e si Rut, “Imbehey rin keddi seini se hen-em ne gantang ne sibada su kene sikandin egkeupian ne ware eg-uyanen ku te eg-uli kayi te keykew.”

So Rut told Nawumi everything that Buas did to her.17 And Rut again said, “He gave me this six cans of barley because he didn't want me to have nothing to bring_home to you(sg).”

18 Ne migkahi si Nawumi, “Tetahad ke re ambey taman te egkatahaan nu ke nekey ka egkeyingumaan kayi, su kene eggimeley si Buas ke kene din seini egkewusey kuntee ne aldew.”

18 Nawumi said to her, “Just wait daughter-in-law until you(sg) know what is the result of this, because Buas won't rest if he can't resolve this today.”

Ka pegpamalu ni Buas ki Rut

The marrying_(widow) of Buas to Rut

41 Te seeye ne timpu, miggendiye si Buas te gumawan te lungsud wey diye migpinpinnuu. Ne dutu, miglihad seeye se amana pad iya ne karumaan ni Ilimilik ne egkahiyen ni Buas wey migkahiyan din e, “Suled, hengkayi ke pa, wey pinnuu ke pa kayi.” Ne migdugpu ka etew wey migpinnuu. 2 Ne miglibulung si Buas te sapulu ne igdatu te lungsud wey impapinnuu rin degma diye. 3 Ne dutu, migkahiyan e ni Buas ka karumaan din, “Kuntee te miglibed e si Nawumi puun te Muwab, igkeupii rin ne igbelegye e ka tane te karumaan ta ne si Ilimilik. 4 Sikan naa ka nakasuman-suman a ne egguhuran ka meyitenged kayi. Ne emun ke egkeupian ka, beliya nu seeye kayi te tangkaan te me etew ne naminnuu, wey te seini se me datu. Piru ke kene ka egkeupian, patahee e re nikeykew. Su sikeykew ka due dakel se katenged ne egbeli kayi, wey egpakasinundul e re keykew.”

41 At that time, Buas went to the doorway of the town and sat there. And there, the close relative of Ilimilik that Buas had mentioned passed_by and he said (to him), “Brother/friend, come here, and sit_down here.” And the person approached and sat_down. 2 And Buas gathered ten leaders (MS datu's) of the town and caused_them_to_sit_down also there. 3 And there, Buas said to his relative, “Now that Nawumi has returned from Muwab, she wants to sell the land of our(incl) relative Ilimilik. 4 That's why I thought to tell you(sg) about this. And if you(sg) want, buy it here in front of our(incl) brothers sitting here, and of these leaders. But if you(sg) don't want, just let me know. For you(sg) (are) the one having a big authority to buy it, and I can only follow you(sg).”

Ne migtabak ka etew, “Uya, egbeliyen ku.”

The person answered, “Yes, I'll buy (it).”

5 Ne migkahi e man-e si Buas, “Na, ke nekey ne aldew ne egbeliyen nud ka tane diye ki Nawumi, keilangan degma ne egpamaluwen nu si Rut ne matig-Muwab, su eyew iyan ded egpakakamuney te tane ka kabuhalan te impatey e ne karumaan ta.”

5 And Buas said again, “Now, if what day you(sg) buy that land of Nawumi, you(sg) also need to marry_(widow) Rut from_Muwab, so_that the descendant(s) of our(incl) dead relative still will_become_the_owner of the land.”

6 Ne migtabak ka etew, “Ke iling ma rue, kena a egbeli te seeye ne tane, su kema ke egkasalinan ka egkarawat ne karatuan te me anak ku. Iyan kad beli, su kene egkaayun kayi te keddi.”

6 The person answered, “If it's like that, I won't buy that land, because maybe the wealth my children will_receive will_be_reduced. You(sg) buy (it), because it's_ not _possible to me.”

7 [xr]Ne iling kayi ka eggimuwen dan dengan diye te Israil eyew pegpamalehet ke egbebelegye wey ke egsesaliyu te me karatuan: egluungen te sabeka ka sandal din wey igbehey rin diye te egbebeli. Sika ka nabatasan diye te Israil te pegpamalehet te husayan.

7 [xr]What they did before in Israil to verify/confirm if they bought something or traded their wealth was like this: the one removes his/her sandal and gives it to the (one) buying. That was the custom in Israil to verify/confirm the settlement/negotiation.

8 Purisu te pegkahi te etew diye te ki Buas, “Sikeykew e beli te tane,” inluung din e ka sandal din wey imbehey e ki Buas. 9 Seeye de iya, migkahiyan e ni Buas ka me igdatu wey ka langun ne etew ne migpammineg, “Kuntee ne aldew, sikaniyu langun ka talagpamalehet te egbeliyen kud diye te ki Nawumi ka langun ne karatuan enni Ilimilik wey te me anak din ne ensi Kiliyun wey Malun. 10 [xr]Ne sein pad man-e, egpamaluwen kud e si Rut ne matig-Muwab, ka balu ni Malun, su eyew egpakaruma red ka ngaran ni Malun diye te karatuan din. Ne eyew man-e egkabuhal ded ka pamilya rin wey kene egkalingawan te me karumaan dan, wey te me etew te seini ne inged. Na, sikaniyu naa ka talagpamalehet kayi kuntee ne aldew.”

8 Therefore when the person said to Buas, “You(sg) buy the land,” he took off his sandal and gave it to Buas. 9 At that, Buas said to the leaders and to all the people listening, “Today, you(pl) are all the witnesses that I have bought from Nawumi all the properties/wealth of Ilimilik and his children Kiliyun and Malun. 10 [xr]And here is more, I will_marry_widow Rut from_Muwab, the widow of Malun, so_that Malun's name will_be_able_to_accompany his wealth. And so_that his family also will_grow and won't be forgotten by their relatives, and by the people in this place. Now_then, you(pl) are all the witnesses of this today.”

11 [xr]Ne migkahi ka me igdatu wey ka langun ne etew diye, “Uya, sikanami iya ka me talagpamalehet. Egpanalanginan te Magbebaye ne Manama ka malitan ne egpamaluwen nu wey igpeiling engki Rakil wey Liya ne nakaanak te masalig wey neyimu ne kabuhalan ni Israil. Egmeyaman ka kayi te inged ne Iprata wey egmabantug ka kayi te Bitlihim.[fn] 12 [xr]Ne egpanalanginan te Magbebaye ne Manama ka kabuhalan ne igbehey rin keykew pinaahi te sika ne malitan, ne egpekeiling te kabuhalan ni Piris ne anak ni Huda ki Tamar.”

11 [xr]And the leaders and all the people there said, “Yes, we(excl) (are) the witnesses. The Lord God bless the woman that you(sg) are marrying_widow and be_like Rakil and Liya that had many children that became the descendant of Israil. May you(sg) become wealthy here in this place of Iprata and famous here in Bitlihim.[fn] 12 [xr]May your(sg) descendant be blessed by the Lord God that he will_give to you(sg) by_means of that woman, like the descendants of Piris the child of Huda and Tamar.”

Ka kabuhalan ni Buas

The descendants of Buas

13 Pegkapenga dutu, indume e ni Buas si Rut wey impamalu rin e. Ne migpewulirey e sikandan wey impanalanginan te Magbebaye ne Manama si Rut wey naberes e. Nataman, mig-anak e sikandin te lukes. 14 Ne migkahiyan te me malitan si Nawumi, “Egsayeen ta ka Magbebaye ne Manama su imbehayan ka nikandin kuntee te apu nu ne egtanggu keykew. Egkeyimu ne mabantug ka bate kayi te Israil. 15 Egpakapeuli sikandin te geyinawa nu wey iyan egtanggu keykew ke egkabuyag kad e, su anak sikandin te ambey nu ne amana miggeyinawa keykew. Ne minsan bu pad ke due anak nu ne pitu ne lukes, kene iya egkeilingan ka geyinawa te ambey nu keykew.” 16 Ne ingkuwa ni Nawumi ka bate, wey ingkepkepan din. Ne sikandin naa ka talagtangguwan te bate.

13 After that, Buas took Rut and marry_widow (her). And they slept/cuddled and the Lord God blessed Rut and became_pregnant. Then, she gave_birth to a male. 14 The women said to Nawumi, “Let us(incl) praise the Lord God because you(sg) have_been_given a grandchild today to look after you(sg). The child will_become famous here in Israil. 15 He will_be_able_to_restore your(sg) breath and look after you(sg) when you(sg) become_old, because he is the child of your(sg) daughter-in-law who loves you(sg) very_much. And even you(sg) may_be have seven male children, the love of your(sg) daughter-in-law for you(sg) can't be compared_with.” 16 And Nawumi took the child, and she cuddled (him). And she looked_after the child.

17 Ne migkahi ka sumbaley ran ne me malitan, “Neetew para ki Nawumi ka seini ne bate, wey egngaranan ta sikandin ki Ubid.”

17 The neighbor women said, “This child was_born for Nawumi, and we(incl) will_name him Ubid.”

Nataman, nakaasawe e si Ubid wey sikandin ka amey ni Hisi, ne si Hisi iyan amey ni Dabid.18 Seini ka me kabuhalan ni Piris: si Piris ka amey ni Hisrun, 19 si Hisrun ka amey ni Ram, si Ram ka amey ni Aminadab, 20 si Aminadab ka amey ni Nasun, si Nasun ka amey ni Salmun, 21 si Salmun ka amey ni Buas, si Buas ka amey ni Ubid, 22 si Ubid ka amey ni Hisi, wey si Hisi ka amey ni Dabid.

Then, Ubid have married and became the father of Hisi, and Hisi was the father of Dabid.18 These (are) the descendants of Piris: Piris is the father of Hisrun, 19 Hisrun is the father of Ram, Ram is the father of Aminadab, 20 Aminadab is the father of Nasun, Nasun is the father of Salmun, 21 Salmun is the father of Buas, Buas is the father of Ubid, 22 Ubid is the father of Hisi, and Hisi is the father of Dabid.
