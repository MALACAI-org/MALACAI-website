Ka an-anayan ne sulat ni Pablu ki

The first letter by Paul to

Timutiyu

Timothy

Igpewun-a

Introduction

Si Timutiyu, sabeka ne Kristuwanen ne diye egpuun te Asya. Hudiyu ka iney rin ne Grigu mule ka amey rin. Tenged su nakita ni Pablu ne malehet ka pegpalintutuu ni Timutiyu, inggimu rin sikandin ne duma rin wey bate-bate din te pegwali wey te pegpangguhud te Meupiya ne Panugtulen (Me Himu 16:1-3). Ne ka Ipisu iya ka sabeka ne lungsud ne miggendiyaan dan. Ne diye ihaat ni Pablu si Timutiyu eyew iyan eg-uyan te migmalintutuu. Te bayew e naluhey si Timutiyu diye te Ipisu, migsulatan e sikandin ni Pablu. Me tatelu ne pulu (30) buwa ka idad ni Timutiyu te seeye ne timpu.

Timothy, (MS Timutiyu) is a Christian from Asia. His mother was a Hudiyu and his father a Greek (MS Grigu). Since Paul saw that Timothy's believing was true, he made him his companion and his apprentice in preaching and telling the Good Report (Acts 16:1-3). Ephesus (MS Ipisu) was one town that they went to. And there Paul left_behind Timothy to lead the believers. When Timothy had been at Ephesus a reasonably long_time, he was_written to by Paul. Timothy's age was probably about thirty (30) at that time.

Due tatelu ne tuyu te seini ne sulat ni Pablu ki Timutiyu:

There are three purposes of this letter by Paul to Timothy:

1/ Ka an-anayan ne tuyu ni Pablu, migpaney-paney sikandin meyitenged te kene ne malehet ne pegpanulu diye te punduk te migmalintutuu. Ka seini ne pegpanulu, nenekegsewug e te me suman-suman te me Hudiyu wey te kene ne me Hudiyu. Su sumale kandan mareet ka seini se kalibutan ne mig-ugpaan ta. Ne wey kun de egkaluwas ka etew ke due katuenan din wey kene egpangeen te duma ne klasi te egkeenen wey kene degma eg-asawa. Migpanulu si Pablu ne kene ne malehet sika.

1/ The first purpose of Paul, (was that) he warned about not correct teaching in the group of believers. This teaching, (it) was mixed with thinking of the Hudiyus and thinking of the non Hudiyus. For according to them this world where we(incl) live is bad. And a person can only (hearsay) be_saved if he/she has skills/knowledge and doesn't eat some kinds of food and also doesn't marry. Paul taught that this isn't correct.

2/ Migpanulu man-e ka seini ne sulat meyitenged te peg-uyan te punduk te migmalintutuu wey te pegsimba. Insulat kayi ka eleg ne egbatasanen te sabeka ne pangulu te migmalintutuu, te talagbulig te migmalintutuu wey te duma ne migmalintutuu ne me balu wey me uripen.

2/ This letter also teaches about managing a group of believers and (about) worshiping. Written here is the proper behavior for a leader of believers, a helper of believers and other believers who are widows and slaves.

3/ Te katammanan, migtambahan din si Timutiyu ke egmenuwen ne egkeyimu ne meupiya ne suluhuanen ni Hisu Kristu, wey meyitenged te katungdanan din diye te me Kristuwanen.

3/ Finally, he advised Timothy how to become a good servant of Jesus Christ, and about his responsibility(s) to the Christians.
