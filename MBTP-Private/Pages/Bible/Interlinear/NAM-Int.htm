Introduction

Ka seini ne baseen, insulatan te guhuren meyitenged te karereetan te Ninibi, ka iney ne siyudad te Asirya ne kuntere dengan te Israil. Nasulat seini te me elat-elat te 663 wey 612 ne leg-un te ware pad neetew ka Kristu. Nahale si prupita Nahum te nareetan e ka Ninibi, su sika ka palinneu ne eglegparan iya te Magbebaye ne Manama ka me makasesale. Minsan ka me etew, migpalintutuu degma ne inlegparan te Manama ka Ninibi su mabbulut ma wey me hambuhen ka me mahinged te sika ne siyudad.

This document, (it) was_written with the stories concerning the destruction of Ninibi, the capital (LIT mother) city of Asiya that was an enemy of Israil long_ago. This was written between 663 and 612 years before Kristu was_born. Prophet Nahum was_happy about the demolition of Ninibi, because that's the sign that the Lord God (MS Manama) truly punishes the sinners. Even the people, believed also_that God punished Ninibi because the inhabitants in that city were fierce and proud.
