Ka sulat ni

The letter of

Santiyagu

James

Igpewun-a

Introduction

Ka seini ne Sulat ni Santiyagu, iyan seini ka nalimud ne me tambag ni Santiyagu te me etew ne migpalintutuu te Manama. Insulat din seini diye te langun ne keet-etawan ne nekegsuwey-suwey kayi te ampew te tane. Miggamit si Santiyagu te me panunggilingan eyew te pegpasabut te me etew wey te pegtambag, eyew egmeupiya ka batasan dan wey ka himu ran te tagse aldew. Migpanulu man-e sikandin te nakalkalasi ne pegpanulu iling te: pegpalintutuu, peg-eleg-eleg, peg-antus, pegtahud, meupiya ne pegbebatasan, meupiya ne peglalag-lalag, malehet ne katuenan ne egpuun te Manama, wey peg-ampu.

This Letter of James (MS Santiyagu), this is the collected advice(s) of James to the people who believe in God (MS Manama). He wrote this to all the people scattered across the world (LIT on top of the soil). James used examples/parables to explain to the people and to advise, so_that their customs would_become_good and their work(s) every day. He also taught various teachings like: believing/faith, testing, persevering, respecting, good customs, good speaking, the true wisdom that comes from God, and praying.

Migkahi sikandin te kulang ka kanta ne pegpalintutuu ke kene egdumaan te himu.

He said that our(incl) believing/faith is lacking if not accompanied by work(s).

Ka malehet ne ngaran te etew ne migsulat kayi si Hakubu, piru si Santiyagu ka igngengaran te me Kastila.

The true name of the person who wrote this was Hakubu, but James (MS Santiyagu) is what the Spanish (MS Kastila) name (him).

Ka pegpalintutuu wey ka katuenan

Believing/Faith and wisdom/knowledge

Su ka egduwa-ruwa egpekeiling te bunlud ne egkeuyan-uyan te kalamag minsan hendei eggiyupa. 7-8 Ka etew ne egduwa-ruwa, kene egpakapallateng ne due egkarawat puun te Manama, su ware kamalehetan te langun ne eggimuwen din.

Ka peg-eleg-eleg wey ka pegtintal

Testing and temptation

Peg-ilingen te egtahud ka etew

Hentew bes ka eg-ayu-ayu kaniyu wey egsusumbung kaniyu diye te me huwis? Sika iya se me meyaman. 7 Sikandan ka egbaley-baley te meupiya ne ngaran ni Hisu Kristu ne insabekaan ta.

Ka pegpalintutuu wey ka meupiya ne himu

Believing/Faith and good works

Ka dile

The tongue

Me suled ku, kene perem ne iling due. 11 Kene egkaayun ne sabeka re ne sebseb ka egpuunan te meupiya ne weyig wey te mareet degma ne weyig. 12 Me suled ku, ka kayu ne iggira, kene egpakabebehas te ulibu, ne ka paras, kene egpakabebehas te iggira.
