Ka igkarangeb ne sulat ni

The second letter of

Huwan

John

Igpewun-a

Introduction

Seini ka igkarangeb ne sulat ni Apustul Huwan ne sabeka te me hibateen ni Hisus. Impeendiye din ka seini ne sulat te malitan ne in-alam te Manama wey diye degma te me anak din. Ne kema ke punduk buwa te migmalintutuu ka igpasabut din kayi. Seini se malepet ne sulat, mighangyu te keilangan ne eg-ikul te kamalehetan wey egpaheyinaweey ka tagse sabeka, wey migpaney-paney degma seini meyitenged te me etew ne migpanulu ne ware kun migpekeetew si Hisu Kristu.

This is the second letter of Apostle John (MS Huwan) who was one of the disciples of Hisus. He sent this letter to a woman whom was_chosen by God (MS Manama) and also there to her children. Perhaps a group of believers might be what he means here. This short letter, (it) pleads that it's_necessary that each one follow the truth and love_one_another, and it also warns about the people who teach that Hisu Christ did not become a person.

11 Sikeddiey si Huwan ka igbuyag te migmalintutuu ka migpeuyan te seini ne sulat. Egpangemusta a te malitan[fn] ne in-alam te Manama wey diye degma te me anak din. Miggeyinawaan ku sikaniyu langun due te kamalehetan, ne kene ne sikeddiey re, ke kene, ka langun degma ne nakataha te kamalehetan, 2 tenged su kayid e te pusung ta ka kamalehetan wey kenad e egkaawe te minsan ken-u.

11 It is me John the elder of the believers the (one) sending this letter. I greet the woman[fn] that is chosen by God and there also to her children. I love you(pl) all in the truth, and not just I, but rather, also all (those) who understand the truth, 2 because the truth is here in our(incl) heart(s) and (will) no_longer/not_from_now_on be_removed whenever.

3 Ka keupiya, keyid-u, wey keupianan ne egpuun te Manama ne Amey wey te Anak din ne si Hisu Kristu, egkaangken niyu. Igbehey sika te Manama te seeye se egdawat te kamalehetan wey te geyinawa.

3 (May) the goodness, mercy, and peace/well-being from God the Father and his Child Hisu Kristu, be received by you(pl). That was_given by God to those who receive truth and love.

Ka kamalehetan wey ka geyinawa

Truth and love

4 Amana a nahale te pegkanengnengi ku ne due me anak nu ne mig-ikul te kamalehetan, sumale te insuhu kanta te Amey. 5 [xr]Ne kuntee, eghangyuen ku sikeykew atebey, ne paheyinaweey ki ka tagse sabeka. Kene ne iyam sika ne suhu, ke kene, tapey e sika puun pad te bunsuranan. 6 Egkakita ka geyinawa ne egkahiyen ku pinaahi te pegtuman ta te me suhu din. Tapey niyud e ne narineg ka sika ne suhu puun pad te bunsuranan, ne keilangan ne egbatasanen niyu ka peggeyinawa.

4 I am very happy at my realization that you(sg) have children who imitate the truth, according to what the Father commanded us(incl). 5 [xr]Now today, I plead with you(sg) sister, that each one of us(incl) love_one_another. That is not a new command, but rather, that is beforehand from the beginning. 6 The love that I speak of can_be_seen through our(incl) obeying his commands. That command you(pl) heard of beforehand from the beginning, that (it's) necessary that you(pl) make_ loving _habitual.

7 Masulug ka nanginguma kayi te kalibutan ne eg-akal te me etew. Kene egpalintutuu sikandan ne migpekeetew si Hisu Kristu. Ka me etew ne iling due, talag-akal wey kuntere ni Kristu! 8 Bantey kew ne kene egkalaag ka ingkalasey ta su eyew kene egkasalinan ka dasag ne egkarawat niyu.

7 Many who trick people have arrived here in the world. They don't believe that Jesus Christ became_a_person. People who are_like that, are trickers and enemies of Christ! 8 Guard(pl) that what we(incl) struggled_for is not lost so_that the reward that you(pl) will_receive will_ not _be_lessened.

9 Ka minsan hentew ne ware mig-ikul te impanulu ni Kristu piru nasi migtimul kayi, ware diye te kandin ka Manama. Piru ka minsan hentew ne mig-ikul te impanulu ni Kristu, due te kandin ka Amey wey ka Anak degma. 10 Ke due eggendue te kaniyu ne kene egpanulu te impanulu ni Kristu, kene niyu sikandin palasura diye te baley niyu, wey kene niyu banasali. 11 Su seeye se egbanasal kandin, egpakaruma te mareet ne himu rin.

9 Whoever doesn't follow what Christ taught but instead adds to this, God is not there with him. But whoever imitates what Christ taught, the Father and also the Child are there with him. 10 If there_is someone_who_goes to you(pl) who doesn't teach what Christ taught, don't you(pl) admit (LIT cause_to_enter) him into your(pl) house, and don't you(pl) encourage him. 11 For those who encourage him, (they) accompany his wicked work.

Ka pegpanaha-taha

The farewell_instructions

12 Masulug pad perem ka iglalag ku kaniyu, piru kena a egkeupian ne igsulat ku pad seini. Igkeupii ku ne egpakapanumbaley e pad kaniyu wey egpakiglalag iya kaniyu su eyew amana ki egkahale.

12 I would have much more to say to you(pl), but I don't want to continue to write this. I want to be_able_to_visit you(pl) and to talk_face_to_face with you(pl) so_that we(incl) will_be_very happy.

13 Egpangemusta degma keykew ka me anak te suled nu[fn] ne in-alam degma te Manama.

13 The children of your(sg) sibling[fn] who are also chosen by God also greet you(sg).
