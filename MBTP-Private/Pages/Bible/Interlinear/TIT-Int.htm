Ka sulat ni Pablu ki

The letter by Paul to

Titu

Titus

Igpewun-a

Introduction

Si Titu, kene sikandin ne Hudiyu wey kene egpalintutuu dengan te Manama. Piru hengkayi te mewuri e ne timpu na-Kristuwanen e sikandin. Ne puun dutu, neyimu sikandin ne talagbulig ni Pablu te me himu rin. Ka seini ne sulat, impeuyan ni Pablu diye te kandin te inggaat din diye te Krita eyew eg-uyan te punduk te migmalintutuu. (Ka Krita, pulu te dahat ne sakup te Grisya.)

Titu, (he) was not a Hudiyu and formerly didn't believe in God (MS Manama). But then at a later time he became_a_Christian. And from that, he became a helper of Paul in his work. This letter, (it was) sent by Paul to him when he remained in Krita in_order to administrate the group of believers. (Krita, (it is) an island in the sea under_the_jurisdiction of Grisya.)

Ne due tatelu ne impanengneng te seini ne sulat: Ka an-anayan, impasuman-suman si Titu ke nekey ka eleg ne egbatasanen te me pangulu te migmalintutuu. Keilangan ne egpakataha sikandin kayi tenged su masulug ka mareet ne batasan te matig-Krita wey due masulug ne talagpanulu ne kene ne malehet ne egpasuwey te migmalintutuu.

Now there were three (things) that were conveyed (LIT caused_to_be_known) in this letter: First, Titu was caused_to_understand the correct behavior of leaders of the believers. (It is) necessary that he is able_to_understand this because the bad customs of the (ones) from_Krita were many and there are many false teachers who will_misguide the believers.

Ka igkarangeb, intambahan si Titu ke egmenuwen din te pegpanulu ka nalein-lein ne me etew diye te punduk te migmalintutuu, lagkes seeye se me lukes-lukes wey me uripen, wey seeye se bayew e me buyag ne me lukes wey me malitan. (Ne iyan egpanulu te me mengebay ka buyag e ne me malitan.)

Secondly, Titu was encouraged how he should teach the various people in the group of believers, including those young_males and slaves, and those adult (LIT almost old) males and females. (The older females (are) the ones_to_teach the unmarried_females.)

Te katammanan, migtambahan ni Pablu si Titu meyitenged te Kristuwanen ne peg-ugpe, wey pegpanalliya te peg-ehet te duma, pegpaapuley, wey pegbaad-baad diye te punduk te migmalintutuu. Impasuman-suman man-e ni Pablu si Titu ne egpakatuman ki te seeye se insuhu kanta tenged te kaluwasan ne imbehey kanta ni Hisu Kristu.

Lastly, Paul encouraged Titu concerning Christian living, and avoiding arguing with others, disagreements, and divisions in the group of believers. Paul also reminded Titu that we(incl) can obey those what was commanded them due to the salvation given them by Hisu Kristu.

Egkeupian si Pablu ne egkanengnengan kayi te seini ne sulat ne amana ne impurtanti diye te migmalintutuu ka peggimu te meupiya (1:6; 2:7,14; 3:1,8-9,14) piru kene sika egpakaluwas (3:5).

Paul wants it to be understood in this letter that doing good is very important of believers (1:6; 2:7,14; 3:1,8-9,14) but that is not what is able_to_save (3:5).

11 Sikeddiey si Pablu ka migpeuyan te seini ne sulat. Uripen a te Manama wey apustulis a ni Hisu Kristu.

11 I Paul am sending this letter. I (am) a slave of God (MS Manama) and I (am) an apostle of Hisu Christ.

Insuhu a te Manama eyew te peglig-en te pegpalintutuu te keet-etawan ne in-alam din wey eyew egpakanengneng sikandan te kamalehetan ne egpakapanulu te peg-ugpe ne egpekeikul te Manama.2 Ne puun te pegpalintutuu ran wey pegkanengnengi ran te kamalehetan, egpallateng sikandan ne egkeuyag te ware egtamanan tenged su sika ka insaad te Manama te ware pad himuwa ka kalibutan, ne kene iya eg-uubat sikandin. 3 Ne kuntee te eleg e ka timpu, impataha rin e seini se Meupiya ne Panugtulen. Ne seini se Meupiya ne Panugtulen ka insalig keddiey, ne seini naan iya ka igwali ku sumale te suhu keddiey te Manama ne Manluluwas ta.

I was commanded/sent by God for the strengthening of the faith/believing of the people whom he has chosen and so_that they will_understand the truth that is able_to_teach a lifestyle that follows/imitates God.2 From their faith/believing and their knowing of the truth, they expect to live without end since that is what God promised when the world had_ not yet _been_created, and he truly does not lie. 3 And now at the correct time, he has made_known this Good Report. And this Good Report is what_ I _have_been_entrusted_with, and this is indeed what_ I _preach according to the command of God our(incl) Savior.

4 [xr]Impeuyan ku seini diye te keykew Titu, isip malehet ne anak ku te pegpalintutuu ta.

4 [xr]I am sending this to you(sg) Titu, considered_as my true child in our(incl) faith.

Ka keyid-u wey keupianan ne egpuun te Manama ne Amey wey te Manluluwas ta ne si Kristu Hisus, egkaangken niyu.

(May) the goodness and mercy from God the Father and our(incl) Savior Kristu Hisus, be received by you(pl).

Ka eleg ne pegbebatasan te me upisyal diye te punduk te migmalintutuu

The correct behavior of officials in the group of believers

5 Seini ka egpuunan ne inggaat ku sikeykew diye te Krita eyew egkeupianan nu ka ware te pad napenga, wey eyew egpakaalam ka te me igbuyag te migmalintutuu diye te tagse lungsud sumale te ingkahi ku keykew. 6 [xr]Keilangan ne ka igbuyag, ware igkarew-ey kandin, wey sabeka re ka asawa. Ne keilangan degma ne egpalintutuu te Manama ka me anak din, kene ne iyan dan de egpammitawen ka kahalaan kayi te ampew te tane, wey kene sikandan eg-atu te buyag kandan. 7 Ne puun su ka pangulu ka insalihan te himu te Manama, keilangan ne ware igkarew-ey kandin: keilangan ne kene sikandin ne hambuhen, kene ne mahaan egkabelu, kene ne talewubug, kene ne taleusig, wey kene egpaneleppi te mareet ne paahi. 8 Ke kene, keilangan ne maal-alumama sikandin, eggeyinawaan din ka meupiya, maheteng ka suman-suman din, matareng sikandin, matulus, wey egpakatigkel te pegkeetew rin. 9 Keilangan ne kene egsuwey sikandin te malehet ne pegpanulu su eyew egpakabagget sikandin te duma pinaahi te sika ne pegpanulu wey egpakasapad sikandin te seyyup te seeye se eg-apul.

5 This is the reason that I left you(sg) behind there in Krita so_that you(sg) could improve the (things) we(incl) had_ not yet _finished, and so_that you(sg) would_be_able_to_choose elders of the believers in every village according to what_ I _told you(sg). 6 [xr](It is) necessary that an elder, there_is_nothing to reprimand him, and (he) has only one spouse. And (it is) also necessary that his children believe/obey God, what they search for is not just pleasure here on the earth (LIT top of the ground), and they don't disrespect their elders. 7 Now since the leader is the (one who) is entrusted_with the work of God, (it is) necessary that there_is_nothing to reprimand him for: (it is) necessary that he is not proud, not quick to anger, not a drunkard, not argumentative/bullying, and does not make_money in an evil way. 8 But rather, (it is) necessary that he is friendly/welcoming, he loves what is good, his thinking is straight, he is righteous, holy, and able_to_control his self. 9 (It is) necessary that he doesn't stray from true teaching so_that he is able_to_encourage others by_means of that teaching and he is able_to_scold the errors of those who resist.

10 Keilangan ne eggimuwen sika su due masulug ne etew ne masinupaken, ne eglalag te ware karuan wey egpanulu te kene ne malehet, labi e seeye se me Hudiyu[fn] ne eglalag te keilangan ne egpatuli ka langun ne egpalintutuu. 11 Keilangan ne egbalabahan sikandan, su egkasamuk dan de ka me pamilya pinaahi te pegpanulu dan te kene perem ne eleg ne igpanulu. Inggimu ran de sika eyew egpakaseleppi. 12 Due sabeka ne talagpanagne dan diye te Krita ne migkahi, “Me ubaten ka matig-Krita, me mabbulut iling te mahintalunan ne me mananap, wey me pehulen sikandan wey me balbalen.” 13 Malehet iya ka ingkahi rin. Purisu kene nu sikandan ul-ulungi te egsapad eyew egmaheteng ka peg-ikul ran te kamalehetan, 14 wey eyew kenad e sikandan egpammineg te me gungutanen te me Hudiyu wey te me suhu te me etew ne kenad e egdawat te kamalehetan. 15 Su ka etew ne nalinisan e, eg-isipen din ne malinis ka langun. Piru ka etew ne mareet wey kene egpalintutuu te Manama, ware eg-isipen din ne malinis, tenged su nareetan e ka suman-suman din wey ka kunsinsya rin. 16 Mig-angken sikandan ne nekeila te Manama, piru kene egkakita sika diye te me himu ran. Igkengeepes sikandan, masinupaken, wey kene egpekeyimu te minsan nekey ne meupiya.

10 (It is) necessary to do that because there are many people who are rebellious, who speak without purpose and teach what isn't true, especially those Hudiyus[fn] who say that all who believe need to be circumcised. 11 (It is) necessary that they are stopped, for they will_ just _make_trouble for families by_means of their teaching which should not be suitable/correct to teach. They just do that in_order to make money. 12 They have a soothsayerer in Krita who said, “The (ones) from_Krita (are) liars, fierce/cruel like wild animals, and they are lazy and gluttons.” 13 What he said is really true. Therefore don't you(sg) hesitate to warn them so_that their following/imitating the truth will_be_straight, 14 and so_that they will_ no_longer _listen to the Hudiyu folk_tales or to the commands of people who will_ no_longer _receive the truth. 15 For the person who has been_cleaned, he considers everything clean. But the person who is wicked and doesn't believe in God, there_is_nothing he considers clean, because his thinking and his conscience are ruined. 16 They claim that (they) know God, but that is not seen in their works. They are disliked_by_many, rebellious, and not able_to_do anything good.

Ka eleg ne pegbebatasan te migmalintutuu

The correct behavior for believers

21 Piru sikeykew Titu, keilangan ne iyan nu ipanulu ka eleg ne igpanulu. 2 Panulua nu ka buyag-buyag e ne me lukes te keilangan ne egpakatigkel sikandan te pegkeetew ran, talahuren sikandan, maheteng ka suman-suman dan, manekal ka pegpalintutuu ran, dakel ka geyinawa ran diye te duma, wey andam sikandan ne eg-antus. 3 Te iling ded ne paahi, panulua nu degma ka buyag-buyag e ne me malitan te keilangan ne eleg ka pegbebatasan dan diye te tangkaan te Manama, kene eg-iran-iran te me duma ran, wey kene ne talewubug. Keilangan ne egpanulu sikandan ke nekey ka meupiya 4 diye te me bate-bate pad ne me malitan eyew egkabasbas sikandan ke egmenuwen dan te eggeyinawa ka me asawa ran wey me anak dan. 5 Keilangan degma ne egpanuluen dan ka me bate-bate pad ne me malitan ne egmahetengen se suman-suman dan wey kene egkasewuhan te mareet, eg-atiman sikandan te ugpaan dan, egmeupiya ka batasan dan, wey egparuma-ruma sikandan te me asawa ran, eyew kene egkalemetan ka lalag te Manama.

21 But you(sg) Titu, (it's) necessary that what you(sg) teach is correct teaching. 2 Teach the older males that (it's) necessary that they can control themselves, they are respected, their thoughts are proper, their faith is strong, their love for others is big, and they are prepared to suffer/endure. 3 In this same way, also teach the older females that (it's) necessary that their behavior is suitable/correct in front of God, they don't gossip about their companions, and aren't drunkards. (It's) necessary that they teach what is good 4 to the younger women so_that they will_be_trained how they can love their spouses and their children. 5 (It is) also necessary that they teach the younger women to straighten their thoughts and not have bad/evil mixed_in, for them to care for their living_place, to improve their customs, and for them to comply_with their spouses, so the word of God will_ not _be_mocked.

6 Te iling ded due ne paahi, baggeta nu degma ka me lukes-lukes ne egtigkel te pegkeetew ran. 7 Keilangan ne sikeykew ka egkeyimu ne panunggilingan pinaahi te peggimu nu te meupiya. Ipakita nu pinaahi te pegpanulu nu ne matareng ka wey kene ne keungelan de. 8 Keilangan ne malehet ka iglalag nu wey ware igkarew-ey, eyew egkeyilawan seeye se egkuntere kanta tenged su ware igkalalag dan ne mareet meyitenged kanta.

6 In just this same way, also encourage(sg) the young men to control their selves/personhood. 7 (It's) necessary that you(sg emph) become an example by means of your(sg) doing good. Show by_means of your(sg) teaching that you(sg) are righteous and not just foolish. 8 (It's) necessary that what you(sg) say is true and there_is_nothing to reprimand, so_that those who oppose us(incl) will_be_shamed since there_is_nothing bad for them to say about us(incl).

9 Panulua nu degma ka me uripen te keilangan ne egparuma-ruma sikandan te me ahalen dan te langun-langun de iya eyew te pegpahale kandan, wey kene egtabak-tabak kandan. 10 Keilangan ne kene dan egpanakawan ka me ahalen dan, ke kene, igpapitew ran ne meupiya sikandan wey egkasalihan, eyew pinaahi te langun ne eggimuwen dan, egpakakahi ka me etew te meupiya iya ka impanulu ta kandan meyitenged te Manama ne Manluluwas ta.

9 Also teach slaves that (it's) necessary that they comply_with their masters in absolutely everything in_order to please them, and don't talk_back to them. 10 (It's) necessary that they don't steal from their masters, but rather, they show that they are good and trustworthy, so_that by_means of everything they do, people will_be_able_to_say that what we(incl) taught them about God our(incl) Savior is very good.

11 Eggimuwen ta seeye su impakite e te Manama ka keupiya wey keyid-u rin eyew te pegluwas te langun ne etew. 12 Ne pinaahi te sika se keupiya wey keyid-u rin, egkapanulu ki ne eg-engked te batasan ta ne kene egpekeikul te Manama wey te mareet ne igkeupii ta kayi te ampew te tane. Te seini ne timpu, keilangan ne maheteng ka suman-suman ta, matareng ki, wey eg-ikul ki te Manama, 13 taheed te egtetahad ki te impallateng ta ne iyan sika se peglepew te katelesan te maresen ne Manama ne Manluluwas ta ne si Hisu Kristu. 14 [xra][xrb]Imbehey rin ka umul rin eyew te peglekat kanta puun te langun ne sale ta, wey eyew egkeyimu ki ne kandin mismu ne keet-etawan ne malinis wey maali-ali ne eggimu te meupiya.

11 We(incl) do those (things) because God already revealed his goodness and mercy in_order to save all people. 12 Now by_means of that goodness and mercy of his, we(incl) learn to abandon our(incl) customs which do not follow/imitate God and we(incl) are able_to_abandon our(incl) bad desires here in the world (LIT top of the ground). At this time, (it's) necessary that our(incl) thoughts are proper, we(incl) are righteous, and we(incl) follow/imitate God, 13 while we(incl) await what we(incl) hope for which is that appearance of the splendor of the powerful God who is our(incl) Savior Hisu Kristu. 14 [xra][xrb]He gave his life in_order to redeem us(incl) from all our(incl) sins, and so_that we(incl) will_become his own people who are clean and diligent to do good.

15 Ipanulu nu naa seini langun, baggeta nu ka migmalintutuu, wey sapari nu sikandan su due katenged nu te peggimu te iling due. Ne kene nu ituhut ne egbalaharen dan ka pegpanulu nu.

15 Teach(sg) all of this, encourage(sg) the believers, and warn them because you(sg) have authority to do like that. And don't(sg) permit them to ignore your(sg) teaching.

Ka meupiya ne pegbebatasan

Good behavior

31 Ipasuman-suman nu man-e te migmalintutuu te keilangan ne egparuma-ruma sikandan wey egtuman te balaud te gubirnu wey te me upisyal. Keilangan ne layun sikandan andam ne eggimu te meupiya, 2 kene eg-iran-iran, kene ne apulen, ke kene, eg-ay-ayaran dan ka langun ne etew wey igpapitew ran ne kene ne mabulut sikandan. 3 Su sikanta degma dengan, egkengeungel ki, masinupaken ki, neuyan ki te duma wey neuripen ki te langun ne klasi te kahalaan ne kene ne meupiya ne igkeupii mule te lawa ta. Iyan te re impulungan ka mig-usig te duma wey neimma kandan. Indumutan ki te me etew wey indumutan ta degma sikandan. 4 Piru hengkayi te impapitew e te Manama ne Manluluwas ta ka keupiya rin wey ka geyinawa rin, 5 inluwas kid e nikandin, ne kene ne tenged te himu ta ne matareng, ke kene, tenged te keyid-u rin kanta. Inlu-luan ki nikandin te me sale ta wey imbehayan ki te iyam ne umul pinaahi te Panisingan te Manama. 6 Imbehey te Manama kayi te kanta ka Panisingan din te ware pegkannuhun pinaahi ki Hisu Kristu ne Manluluwas ta, 7 eyew eg-isipen ki te Manama ne matareng pinaahi te keupiya wey keyid-u rin wey egkaangken ta ka umul ne ware egtamanan ne impallateng ta. 8 Malehet iya ka impangguhud ku kaniyu. Igkeupii ku naa ne ipehet-pehet nu ipanulu sika diye te kandan, eyew egpakannekal ne eggimu te meupiya ka langun ne migpalintutuu te Manama. Meupiya seeye wey egpakabulig te langun ne etew.

31 Also remind the believers that (it's) necessary that they comply_with and obey the laws of government and of the officials. (It's) necessary that they are always prepared to do good, 2 don't backbite (others), are not contentious/argumentative, but rather, they are careful with all people and they show that they are not bold/aggressive. 3 For we(incl) also long_ago, we(incl) were foolish, we(incl) were rebellious, we(incl) were negatively_influenced (LIT carried_along) by others and we(incl) were enslaved by all kinds of pleasures that aren't good but which our(incl) bodies desired. Our(incl) only activity was to oppose/bully others and to envy them. We(incl) were hated by people and we(incl) hated them too. 4 But when God our(incl) Savior had revealed his goodness and his love, 5 we(incl) were_saved by him, but not due_to our(incl) work that is righteous, but rather, due_to his mercy for us(incl). We(incl) were washed by him from our(incl) sins and we(incl) were_given a new life by_means of the Spirit of God. 6 God gave us(incl) his Spirit without stinginess by_means of Hisu Christ our(incl) Savior, 7 so_that we(incl) will_be_considered by God to be righteous by_means of his goodness and mercy and we(incl) will_receive the life without end for which we(incl) hoped. 8 What I storied/told to you(pl) is really true. I want you(sg) to teach that emphatically to them, so_that all who believe God will_strive to do good. That is good and is able_to_help all people.

9 Piru pakaddiyu ka te ware karuan ne apulanan, ka peg-iitul te me kabuybuyahan niyu, ka egpakig-ehet wey egpakig-apul meyitenged te Balaud su ware karuan due wey ware egkaangey nu rue. 10 Ke due duma nu ne iyan nakapasuwey-suwey te punduk te migmalintutuu, sapari nu sikandin te me kararuwa, ne emun ke kene egpammineg, pakaddiyui nud e sikandin. 11 Nakanengneng ka te ka etew ne iling due, nakapariyu e te kamalehetan wey makasesale e. Ne ka himu rin mismu ka nakarereet kandin.

9 But distance (yourself) from useless argumentation, genealogies of your(pl) elders, fighting and arguing about the Law for there_is_no value in that and nothing there for you(sg) to gain. 10 If you(sg) have a companion who causes_division in the group of believers, warn him once or twice, and if (he) doesn't listen, distance (yourself) from him. 11 You(sg) know that a person like that, (he) has already distanced (himself) from the truth and is a sinner. And his own acts ruined him.

Ka pegpanaha-taha

The goodbye

12 [xr]Ke igpeenduen kud e te keykew si Artimas wey ke si Tikiku naa, apas ke naa temu keddi diye te Nikupulis su nakaplanu ad ne diye e pad eg-ugpe te timpu te maagsil. 13 [xr]Pakannekali nu degma te egbulig si Sinas ne ebugadu wey si Apulus eyew egpakaligkat e sikandan ne ware kulang. 14 Keilangan ne eggimu te meupiya ka keet-etawan ta eyew egpakabulig sikandan te mabehat ne prublima te duma, wey eyew egbebehas sikandan.

12 [xr]If/when I send Artimas or Tikiku there to_you(sg), hurry(sg) to meet me in Nikupulis because I planned that I will_live there at the time of cold (weather). 13 [xr]Also strive(sg) to_help the attorney Sinas and Apulus so_that they will_be_able_to_depart without lacking_anything. 14 Our(incl) people need to do good so_that they will_be_able_to_help with the big problems of others, and so_that they will_be_productive (LIT bear_fruit).

15 Egpangemusta keykew ka langun ne me duma ku kayi. Ipangemusta key re degma te langun ne miggeyinawa kanami ne duma ta te mig-ikul te kamalehetan.

15 All of my companions here send_regards to you(sg). We(excl) also send_regards to all who love us(excl) who are our(incl) companions in following/imitating the truth.
