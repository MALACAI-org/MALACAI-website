Introduction

Ka igkarangeb ne sulat ni Pablu ki Timutiyu, diye din sulata te Ruma te timpu ne naprisu sikandin tenged te pegwali rin meyitenged ki Hisu Kristu. Inhangyu ni Pablu si Timutiyu ne egpeendiyeen te kandin te kene pad sikandin eggimatayan. Ne si Timutiyu, diye pad sikandin te Ipisu ka egpanulu te migmalintutuu.

The second letter by Paul (MS Pablu) to Timutiyu (MS Timutiyu), (it) was_written in Ruma at the time he was imprisoned as a result of his preaching about Hisu Kristu. Paul requested Timutiyu to go_there to him before he was_killed (LIT when he had_ not yet _been_killed). And Timutiyu, he was still in Ipisu teaching the believers.

Kayi te seini ne sulat, intambahan ni Pablu si Timutiyu ne eglig-en eyew egpakaantus sikandin ne egwali wey eggimu te impeyimu te Manama kandin minsan taliware te me peg-antus wey me pegsupak te duma ne etew. Impanpanayan din man-e si Timutiyu meyitenged te mareet ne egkeyingumaan te “keungelan wey te ware karuan ne apulanan” su egpakareet seini te me etew ne egpammineg.

In this letter, Paul advised Timutiyu to be strong/firm so_that he would_be_able_to_suffer/endure and to preach and do what God (MS Manama) caused_ him _to_do even in the midst of suffering/enduring and opposition from other people. He also warned Timutiyu about the bad result (LIT happening) of “foolish and meaningless (LIT without use) arguments” because those will_ruin the people who listen.

Labew te langun, impasuman-suman ni Pablu si Timutiyu meyitenged te kandin ne umul su eyew egkeyimu ne panunggilingan ni Timutiyu ka pegpalintutuu ni Pablu, ka pegpariralem din, ka peggeyinawa rin, ka pegpabulus din, wey ka peg-antus din taliware te me pegbayad-bayad kandin.

Above all, Paul reminded Timutiyu about his life so_that the faith of Paul, his humility, his love, his continuing, and his suffering/enduring in the midst of his persecutions would_become an example for Timutiyu.
