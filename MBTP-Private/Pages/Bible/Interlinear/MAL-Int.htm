Malakyas

Malachi

Igpewun-a

Introduction

Seini ne baseen, nasulatan te me lalag te Magbebaye ne Manama ne impahuhud ki prupita Malakyas. Nasulat seini te me lalimma ne gatus (500) ne leg-un te ware pad neetew si Kristu Hisus. Te seeye ne timpu, neimpusan e ka pegbalew-balew te timplu diye te Hirusalim. Ka dakel ne tuyu te prupita iyan ka peg-inniyat te me talagpanubad wey pegpataha te keet-etawan ne igpatuman ka nasabutan dan e diye te Magbebaye ne Manama. Klaru amana ne kenad e ne matinumanen sikandan te pegpamakey ran te Manama wey nakapariyu e te kamalehetan. Ka me talagpanubad wey ka keet-etawan, miglilimbung diye te Manama tenged su ware sikandan migbehey te eleg ne igpanubad wey ware sikandan mig-ikul sumale te igkeupii te Magbebaye ne Manama. Piru eggendini ka Manama eyew te peggukum wey peglinis te keet-etawan din, wey egpeuyan sikandin te talagpangguhud ne eggun-a kandin eyew eg-andam te dalan wey egpamalehet te kasabutan din.

This document (LIT reading), that's_where_ the words of the Lord God (MS Manama) _are_written which were caused_to_tell by prophet Malakyas. This was_written about five hundred (500) years before Jesus Christ was_born. At that time, the rebuilding of the temple of Hirusalim was completed. The main (LIT big) purpose of the prophet is to encourage the sacrificers and to make_known to the people to obey what they have agreed with the Lord God.
