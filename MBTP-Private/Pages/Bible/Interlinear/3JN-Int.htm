Igpewun-a

Introduction

Seini ka igkatelu ne sulat ni Apustul Huwan ne sabeka te me hibateen ni Hisus. Igpeendiye din seini te sabeka ne alukuy rin ne migpalintutuu ne migngaranan ki Gayu. Indayan ni Huwan si Gayu tenged te pegtanggu rin te duma ne migmalintutuu ne miggiphipanew ka egpangguhud te lalag te Manama. Impanpanayan din degma si Gayu meyitenged te sabeka ne etew ne egngaranan ki Diyutripis ne mareet se batasan.

This is the third letter of Apostle John (MS Huwan) who was one of the disciples of Jesus (MS Hisus). He sent this to one of his friends who believes named Gayu. John commended Gayu for his looking_after other believers who go_around storying the word of God (MS Manama). He also warned Gayu about a person named Diyutripis who had bad custom(s).

11 [xr]Sikeddiey si Huwan ka igbuyag te migmalintutuu ka migpeuyan te seini ne sulat. Egpangemusta a keykew Gayu ne miggeyinawaan ku ne suled due te kamalehetan.

11 [xr]It (is) me John the elder of the believers the (one) sending this letter. I greet you(sg) Gayu as a sibling in the truth whom I love.

2 Miggeyinawaan ku ne suled, mig-ampu a ne meupiya ka peg-ugpe nu wey ka peglelawa nu iling keupiya te gimukud nu. 3 Due nanginguma kayi ne me suled ta, ne amana a nahale te napangguhuran a nikandan meyitenged te pegtuman nu te kamalehetan, wey ke immenu nu te peg-ugpe due te kamalehetan. 4 Iyan de amana egpakapahale keddiey ke egpakarineg a ne migtuman te kamalehetan ka me anak ku te pegpalintutuu.

2 My beloved sibling, I pray that your(sg) living_situation and your(sg) health are good like the goodness of your(sg) soul. 3 Some of our(incl) siblings arrived here, and I was_ very _happy to be storied by them about your(sg) obedience to the truth, and how is your(sg) living there in the truth. 4 What really pleases me is when I hear that my children in believing obeyed the truth.

Ka pegdayan ki Gayu

The commendation of Gayu

5 Miggeyinawaan ku ne suled, egkasalihan ka su intanggu nu ka me suled ta minsan ware ka nekeila kandan. 6 Migpangguhud sikandan kayi te tangkaan te migmalintutuu meyitenged te peggeyinawa nu kandan. Ne emun ke eglibed sikandan due, meupiya perem ke egpakapahale te Manama ka pegbulig nu kandan pinaahi te pegbehey nu te keilangan dan te pegligkat dan. 7 Su nangipanew sikandan eyew te pegpanulu meyitenged ki Kristu wey ware sikandan migdawat te minsan nekey ne diye egpuun te me etew ne ware migpalintutuu te Manama. 8 Purisu, keilangan ne egbulihan ta ka iling due ne me etew, su eyew egkeyimu ki ne talagduma te himu meyitenged te kamalehetan.

5 My beloved sibling, you(sg) are trustworthy because you(sg) cared for our(incl) siblings even_though you(sg) didn't know them. 6 They storied here in front of the believers about your(sg) love for them. And if/when they return there, it would_be good if your(sg) helping them would_please God by/through your(sg) giving what they need at their departure. 7 For they went in_order to teach about Christ and they didn't accept anything from people who don't believe in God. 8 Therefore, (it's) necessary that we(incl) help people like that, so_that we(incl) will_become companions in the work concerning the truth.

Si Diyutripis wey si Dimitriyu

Diyutripis and Dimitriyu

9 Migpeuyanan kud te sulat ka migmalintutuu due, piru tenged su kene egkeupian si Diyutripis ne egkalepengan, imbalahad key nikandin. 10 Purisu ke eggenduen a, igpangguhud ku ke nekey ka inggimu rin ne pegsesalupet wey peg-iran-iran kanami. Ne kene ne sikan de, kene din man-e egsagsahipeen ka me suled ta, egbalabahan din pad man-e wey egdeldelen diye te punduk te migmalintutuu seeye se egkeupian ne egsagsahipe kandan.

9 I sent a letter to the believers there, but since Diyutripis didn't want to_be_superceded_in_status(_by_others), we(excl) were ignored by him. 10 Therefore if I go_there, I will_story about what he did which was slandering and mocking us(excl). And not only that, he also doesn't welcome our(incl) siblings, he also hinders and drives_off from the group of believers those who wanted to welcome them.

11 Miggeyinawaan ku ne suled, kene nu ilingi ka mareet, ke kene, sikan de se meupiya. Ka minsan hentew ne eggimu te meupiya, kandin te Manama. Ka minsan hentew ne eggimu te mareet, ware nekeila te Manama.

11 My beloved sibling, don't you(sg) imitate evil, but rather, that which is good. Whoever does good, he is God's. Whoever does evil, (he) doesn't know God.

12 Narayan si Dimitriyu te langun wey egkakita diye te himu rin ne mig-ikul sikandin te kamalehetan. Minsan degma sikanami, nakarayan kandin wey nakataha ka degma ne malehet iya ka impangguhud ney.

12 Dimitriyu is commended by all and it is seen by his work/actions that he follows the truth. Also even us(excl), (we) are able_to_commend him and you(sg) also know that what we(excl) say is really true.

Ka pegpanaha-taha

The goodbye

13 Masulug pad perem ka iglalag ku keykew, piru kena a egkeupian ne igsulat ku pad seini. 14 Egkeupian a perem ne egpekegkita ki pad te mahaan, wey egpekeglalag pad.

13 I would have_ much yet _to_tell you(sg), but I don't want to write more of this. 14 I would like that we(incl) will_be_able_to_see_one_another soon, and to talk_to_one_another.

15 Ka Manama ka egbehey keykew te keupianan. Migpangemusta keykew ka me alukuy nu kayi, wey ipangemuste e re degma te langun ne me alukuy ta due.

15 God will_give you(sg) peace. Your(sg) friends here greet you(sg), and I also send_regards to all of our(incl) friends there.
