galung
/galuŋ/
 
vt.
for someone (eggalung, miggalung, galung, agent, eg><ø-) to control something or someone
Ka galung ne lalag nekeg-iling te bantey wey ke tameng naa. Ka etew ne eggalung iyan ka etew ne egbatey eyew kenè egkeyimu te egbantayan wey ke egtamengan naa ka kenè ne meupiya ne eggimuwen. Nekeg-iling degma seini ne lalag te kural wey ke alad.
Mahirap ka eggalung te senge etew su due suman-suman.
It's difficult to guard someone because he/she can decide.
Kene ne meupiya ka eggalung su kene egpakabulig.
It not good to+control someone because it can't help.
Eng. watch; protect; guard; control
Tag. bantay
Ceb. bantay
