lasey
/lasɔy/
v_rt.
“make_life_difficult”
 
v.
for someone (eglasey, miglasey/inlasey, agent, eg><ø-) to make life difficult (eglasayen, inlasey/miglasey/piglasey, patient, eg><en-) for someone else by soomething specially in working (iglasey, inlasey, instrument, ig><ø-)
Eng. make life difficult; difficulties; work; punish
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for a person or animal (egkalasey, nalasey, agent, egkA><ø-) to be difficult by doing something (igkalasey, ingkalasey, reason, igkA><ø-)
Eng. difficult; tired
DDP: Tired (2.4.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egkalasayan, nalasayan, agent, egkA><an-) to suffer difficulties
Nalasayan e amana ka inayen ku te migpatey ka anggam ku.
My aunt suffered great difficulties due to my uncle dying.
Eng. suffer difficulties; difficulties; struggles; anguish; distress
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Upset (3.4.2.1.6)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
adj.
(malasey, mA1-) difficult
Wrd.Gloss: difficult
Syn. malehen
Syn. mahirap
Eng. difficult; hard (about difficulties); harsh times
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for someone (kalasey, agent, kA-) to be difficult
Kalasey ad e amana seini.
I am now in difficult.
Eng. difficult; hard (about difficulties)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for someone (eglasey-lasey, miglasey-lasey/inlasey-lasey, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to exert effort in doing something (eglaslasayan, inlaslasayan/miglaslasayan/piglaslasayan, eg><an-rdpCVC-)
Eng. exert effort; make effort; effort, make; exert; strive; strove; diligent
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(nalasey, nA-) haggard
Eng. exerted; struggled; haggard
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
