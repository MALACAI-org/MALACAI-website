hukum
/hukum/
Fr. Ceb. hukom
 
vt.
for a leader (eggukum, miggukum/ingukum, gukum, agent, eg><ø-) to judge or to pass sentence on someone (eggukuman, inggukuman/miggukuman/piggukuman, gukumi, location, eg><an-)
Cult. e.g. God (Manama) at the end of time
Egginguma ka timpu ne eggukum ka Manama te langun ne keet-etawan.
The time will come when God will judge all the people.
Syn. kukum
Eng. judge; sentence; condemn
Ceb. hukom
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
n.
(hukumanan, -an-an) council
Diyè eg-gukumi te baley te huwis ka nakasalè te balaud.
The one who sinned against the law will be judged in the courthouse.
Eng. council; court room; council chambers; courthouse
Tag. kórte; hukúman
Ceb. husgadu; kurti
Ilo.: kórte; pangukumán
OCM: Public Structures (344), Litigation (691)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
