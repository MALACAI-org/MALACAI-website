taweles
/tawɔlɔs/
 
vi.
for someone (egkataweles, nataweles, actor, egkA><ø-) to seem angry
Ka taweles ne lalag nekeg-iling dè seini te belù, wey te langet. Ka igkarangeb ne igpasabut kayi iling degma te talesew.
Amana a nataweles ganna te maselem su warè e pad napukew, mahulub e sikandan.
I was very angry this morning because before I woke up, they were already noisy.
Taweles ku naan dè iya perem ki Bura piru naaldek a kema ke egpangabang ka iney rin.
I got so mad Bura but I am afraid with his mother.
Mania te egpanaweles ka egkahi ne si Nante ka migpurut te seleppì nu?
Why do you accuse Nante by saying that he pick up your money?
Eng. guess; accuse; anger; angry; fume with anger; hypothesis
