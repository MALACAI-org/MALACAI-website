lantang
/lantaŋ/
 
v.
(eglantang, miglantang, eg><ø-) to attend a funeral
Ka seini ne lalag egpekeg-iling te damag. Igngaran seini te etew ne egbantey te minatey.
Egpakalipereng a te maaldew su miglantang a gabii te marusilem.
I'm sleepy during day because I attended a funeral last night.
Lantang ka kangkuwa diyè? kahi ni Undon.
Are you going to funeral? Undon said.
Eng. funeral
Ceb. lamay
