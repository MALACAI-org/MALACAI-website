samuk
/samuk/
v_rt.
“cause_trouble”
 
n.
commotion
Wrd.Gloss: commotion
Eng. commotion; disturbance; disorder; conflict; disagreement; confusion
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vt.
for someone (egsamuk, migsamuk/insamuk, agent, eg><ø-) to fight; to quarrel; to argue; to bother someone else (egsamuken, theme, eg><en-)
Wrd.Gloss: fight; quarrel
Egsamuk man-e ka me etew ne nenawubug.
Drunk people often cause disturbances.
Eng. fight; quarrel; argue; scrap; bother; disturb; interrupt; cause trouble; trouble, cause; riot
Ceb. gubut
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
(egkasamuk, nasamuk, egkA><ø-) to be disorderly
Egkasamuk man-e ka sumbaley ney.
Nasamuk e ka me batè ne egpangekelag.
Amana kasamuk ney dengan te warè pad nabalbalawi ka mareet ne batasan te amey ku.
Our lives before was full of trouble when my father's bad attitude not yet changed.
Eng. disorderly; riotous; disturbed; disturbance
Tag. magkaguló
Ceb. kagubut
Ilo.: guló
OCM: Ingroup Antagonisms (578), Inter-community Relations (628)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(masamuk, mA1-) bothersome; rebellious; troublesome
Eng. bothersome; rebellious; troublesome; disorderly; riotous
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for someone (egsamuk-samuk, migsamuk-samuk, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to cause trouble repeatedly
Egsamuk-samuk man-e ka me mangayew diyè te Dinukdukan.
Eng. cause trouble
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
adj.
(masesamuk, mA1-rdpCV-) troublesome
Eng. troublesome
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
