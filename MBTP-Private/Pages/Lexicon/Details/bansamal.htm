bansamal
/bansamal/
v_rt.
“embalm”
Fr. Ceb. balsamar
 
v.
for someone (egbansamal, migbansamal, imbansamal, agent, eg><ø-) to embalm (egbansamalen, imbansamal/migbansamal/pigbansamal, patient, eg><en-)
Egbansamal ka duktur te minatey.
The doctor embalm a corpse.
Eng. embalm; preserve
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
v.
for someone (egpabansamal, migpabansamal/impabansamal, eg><ø-pA-) to give an authority to embalm someone (igpabansamal, impabansamal, theme)
Impabansamal kud e ka hari ku ne impatey te egpamansamal gabii.
I let an embalmer to enbalmed my younger brother's dead body yesterday.
Eng. authority, to give
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
