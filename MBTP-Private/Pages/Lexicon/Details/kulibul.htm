kulibul
/kulibul/
v_rt.
“stir”
 
vt.
for someone (egkulibul, agent, eg><ø-) to stir something (egkulibulen, theme, eg><en-) round and round in a circular motion with something (igkulibul, instrument, ig><ø-)
Wrd.Gloss: stir
Egkulibul a pad te impegsewug ku ne arina wey asukal.
I will stir the flour and sugar that I mixed.
Syn. alu
O. Syn. buliyew
Eng. stir; mix
Tag. maghálò?(to mix)
Ceb. mag-ukay
Ilo.: kiwáren
OCM: Food Preparation (252), Ethnophysics (822)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
