Tulalang1
/tulalaŋ/
prop_n.
“Tulalang”
 
prop_n.
a man who lived in ancient times
Wrd.Gloss: Tulalang
Si Tulalang su pagpahisen sika ka warè egkeupian kandin ne mè malitan.
Cult. Tulalang was covered in small white sores (pahisen) which pitted his skin. Because of this, girls did not like his rough touch and refused to maary him. Tulalang became angry and began to war until the blood ran as deep as his calves (bakesalan). He later ascended into heaven.
Eng. deity
