lehadda
/lɔhadda/
n.
“chives”
 
n.
chives; basil
Ka lehadda iyan ka pinamula ne kenè amana ne malayat wey malapung. Meilem seini wey due bulak ne meilem ded piru maangkag e ke egmanlesut e. Bayew meemut ka ngareg din wey egkaayun ne igbawi te buwe. Mallilintek ubag ka dewun din wey bayew malapung.
Amuti nu te lehadda ka innilutù nu ne serè su eyew egmeemut wey eyew egmananam.
Mix with chives your viand so that it will be smelling nice and be delicious.
Wrd.Gloss: chives
Eng. chives; basil
