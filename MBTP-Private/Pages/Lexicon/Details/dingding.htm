dingding
/diŋdiŋ/
v_rt.
“block”
 
vt.
for someone (egdingding, migdingding/indingding, agent, eg><ø-) to intentionally block a person or thing (egdingdingan, indingdingan/migdingdingan/pigdingdingan, dingdingan, goal, eg><an-) with something (e.g., part of their body, a piece of paper) (igdingding, indingding, instrument, ig><ø-) e.g., so that they cannot see or are protected from something
Kenè ka egdingding keddì.
Don't block the light for me.
Eng. block intentionally; obstruct intentionally; protect; shield, to
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone or something (egpakaringding, nakaringding, actor, egpAkA><ø-) to unintentionally block a person or thing, or to intentionally use something (e.g., part of their body, a piece of paper) (igpakaringding, ingkaringding, instrument, igpAkA-) to block a person or thing
Nekey-a sika se nakaringding te baley enni Nunuy.
What is that obstructs view of the house of Nonoy.
Kenè nu pamulei te ahuu ka lama niyu su egpakaringding sika te baley niyu ke egdakel e.
Don't plant a wild pine tree in your front yard for it will obstruct the view from your house when it grows up.
Eng. obstruct unintentionally; block unintentionally; cut off view; block view; obstruct view; protect
DDP: Wind (1.1.3.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
