balew1
/balɔw/
v_rt.
“renew”
 
vt.
for someone (egbalew-balew, migbalew-balew/imbalew-balew, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to renew something (egbalbalawan, imbalbalawan/migbalbalawan/pigbalbalawan, location, eg><an-rdpCVC-)
Nalerut e ka liyang ku, sikan naa egbalew-balew ad man-e eggimu te iyam.
My basket is already worn out, that is why I need to remake a new one.
O. Syn. halin
Eng. renew; change; replace; remake; redo; exchange; alter; adjust; repair; rework; renovate; revoke; reorganize; reorganise; rebuild
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for something (egkabalbalawan, nabalbalawan, actor, egkA><an-rdpCVC-) to be renewed
Egkabalbalawan iya ka me etew ke egtulin e.
People really change when they get older.
Eng. renewed; changed; remade; redone; altered; adjusted; replaced; exchanged; repaired; revoked; reworked; reorganized; reorganised; rebuilt

Usage level: M
