wingì
/wiŋʔ/
 
v.
(egwingien, nawingì, eg><en-) to twist something
Ka seini ne lalag nekeg-iling te gulibed, wey keleng. Piru egkahamit ka wingì ke duè minsan nekey ne matul-id wey ke eleg naa ne nahulibed wey ke nakeleng, wey ke minsan nekey ne warè nekeikul te eleg wey meupiya.
Keilangan ne egwingien nu Noel ka tukud nu te deisek eyew egmatul-id.
Noel you should wrench your post a little bit so that it will straigth.
Eng. twist; wrench; wring
Tag. umikot
Ceb. baliko
