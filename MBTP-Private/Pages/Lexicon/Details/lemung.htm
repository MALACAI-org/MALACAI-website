lemung
/lɔmuŋ/
v_rt.
“dwell_together”
 
vi.
for someone (eglemung, miglemung/inlemung, lemung, agent, eg><ø-) to dwell with someone else (eglemungan, location, eg><an-) on a temporary, yet extended, basis
Iyan ka sabeka ne pamilya ne due mig-amut mig-ugpè ne lein ne pamilya. Seini sabeka ne batasan te me Matigsalug wey seini egkeyitabu ke due sabeka ne pamilya ne iyam pad migginguma puun te lein ne inged ne warè pad egke-ugpaan.
Eglemung a eg-ugpè diyè te baley te anggam ku.
Eglemung key pad kayi te kaniyu anggam su warè mam pad baley ney.
We'rè going to stay here with you uncle for we have no house yet.
Syn. eglapù
O. Syn. eggirehè
Eng. dwell together; live with; stay with; move in with; accommodate; live amongst; camp with
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for one of more families (egpalemlemungey, impalemlemungey, eg-pA><ey-rdpCVC-) to sleep together
Palemlemungey ki rè ka eg-ugpè su eyew egmekegbulig ki te me himu ta.
We will dwell together so that we can helped each other.
Impalemlemungey e sikandan ne eggibat te sabeka ne sinabeng.
They sleep together in one room.
Eng. sleep together
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
vi.
for two families (egpalemungey, eg><ø-pA><ey-) to dwell together
Egpalemungey e man-e ka talaasawa ne iyam nekeg-engked.
The newly divorced couple again dwell together.
Eng. dwell together
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
