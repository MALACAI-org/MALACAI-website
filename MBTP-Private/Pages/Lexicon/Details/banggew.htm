banggew
/baŋgɔw/
v_rt.
“hinder”
 
vt.
for someone or something (egbanggew, migbanggew/imbanggew, banggew, agent, eg><ø-) to delay; to interrupt; to disturb someone (egbanggawen, imbanggew/migbanggew/pigbanggew, banggawen, eg><en-)
Mania te egbanggawen a nikeykew?
Why are you delaying me?
Eng. delay, to; hinder; interrupt; disturb; tempt
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egkabanggew, nabanggew, kabanggew, agent, egkA><ø-) to be disturbed by someone or something
Kenè e rè egduruma te batè ka eggilamun su egkabanggew a.
I am not going to accompany the child because I might be interrupted.
Kabanggew kad e ka eggendiyè te bubungan ka egpangayu.
You will be hindered going to a mountain to pick a wood.
Wrd.Gloss: hindrance
Eng. disturb; put aside; interrupt; hinder; obstruct; delay
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
