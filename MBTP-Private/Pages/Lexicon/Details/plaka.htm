plaka
/plaka/
Var.1: palaka
 
n.
a record (such as for a gramophone) or a tape for a cassette recorder
Iulì nud ka plaka ku ne insambayan nu.
Return the cassette tape which you borrowed.
Eng. record; cassette tape
