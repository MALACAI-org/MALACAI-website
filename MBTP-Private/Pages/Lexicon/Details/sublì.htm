sublì
/subliʔ/
 
v.
for someone (egsublì, migsublì/insublì, sublì, agent, eg><ø-) to review something
Ka sublì ne lalag iyan igpasabut ka eg-abayan egpitawa, wey ke eggimuwa ka minsan nekey. Ka tuyù mania te keilangan ne egsublien ka inggimu te sabeka wey ke imbayè naa eyew egkasiguru ne eleg wey ke meupiya naa ka imbayè wey ke inggimu.
Nakasublì key mig-erek su ingkeen te iteng ka me palay.
We have to repeat planting because ants ate the rice plants.
Eng. review; go over; again; repeat; return; turn back
