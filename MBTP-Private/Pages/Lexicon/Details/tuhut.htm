tuhut
/tugut/
v_rt.
“allow”
 
vt.
for someone (egtuhut, migtuhut/intuhut, agent, eg><ø-) to allow someone else (egtuhutan, intuhutan/migtuhutan/pigtuhutan, location, eg><an-) to do something (igtuhut, intuhut, instrument, ig><ø-)
Ka tuhut ne lalag iyan sika peg-uyun te minsan nekey ne himu, ka kenè pegbalibad, wey ke pegdawat naa te eghangyuen te sabeka ne etew diyè te lein. Egkaayun degma ne ka pegparuma-ruma te igkeupii te duma.
Kenè egtuhut ka iney ku ne eg-asawa ad.
My mother won't allow me to get married.
Kenè egtuhutan te Manama ne egkeuripen ki te salè su dakel ka geyinawa rin kanta.
God does not allow us to be enslaved by sin because his love for us is great.
Syn. egkuwa
Eng. allow; permit; permission, give; give permission; let; accept
Tag. permíso; kapahíntulután
Ceb. pirmisu; panugot; sugot
Ilo.: palúbos
OCM: Government Regulation (656)
