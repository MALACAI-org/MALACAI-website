daruwa
/dadua/
Var. duwa
num.
“two”
 
num.
two (counting)
Gramm. The root duwa has been prefixed with rdpC-a. See entry duwa for other derived forms.
Gramm. Sometimes used as the counting form, although duwa is more common.
Eng. 02; two (counting)
OCM: Numeration (802)
DDP: Cardinal numbers (8.1.1.1), Two (8.1.1.1.2)
Online dict status: r
Usage level: B
 
adj.
two
Gramm. The root duwa has been prefixed with rdpC-a. See entry duwa for other derived forms.
Daruwa ne libu ka imbeli ku te kalabew ku.
Two thousand (pesos) is the purchase price of my of my carabao.
Eng. two (adjective)
