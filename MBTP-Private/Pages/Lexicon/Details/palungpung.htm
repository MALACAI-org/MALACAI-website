palungpung
/paluŋpuŋ/
n.
“decorative_headgear”
 
n.
a decorative headpiece such as a pompom or the decorative horse hair on a kuglung
Eng. decorative hair; hair, decorative; pompom; headgear
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
vi.
for a person (egpamalungpung, agent, eg><ø-paN-) to put a decorative headpiece on a horse (egpamalungpungan, location, eg><an-paN-)
Meupiya ne egpitawen ka kuddè ne egpamalungpungan su egpekeiling te malitan ne egsesangkad.
The horse that put on headpiece was attractive alike with the woman put on head bond.
Eng. decorate; headpiece, put on; put on headpiece
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
