pegnel
/pɔgnɔl/
v_rt.
“control”
 
vt.
for someone (egpegnel, agent, eg><ø-) to stop or control someone else (egpegnelan, pegneli, theme, eg><an-) from doing something
Ka pegpegnel iyan ka pegbalabag wey pegsapad te minsan hentew ne egbalew-balew wey eggimu te kenè ne eleg. Eggimuwen seini eyew egkabalahan wey kenè amana egpakabulus te eggimuwen iling te pegdereet te duma, pegleug-leug ne warè karuan, peg-iran-iran wey langun ne mareet ne hinimuwan. Minsan degma ka pegsapad te minsan hentew te kalanganan ne eggimuwen din, egkaayunan degma te seini ne lalag.
Pegneli niyu kun si Atù ka eggelebek egleug-leug.
You(pl) must control Atu from wandering around everywhere.
Mania te kenè niyu egpegnelan ka duma ne eggimu te mareet te egkareetan kew ma degma elin?
Why don’t you(pl) stop others doing bad things when you all will also be ruined (by their acts)?
Napegnelan nu naan ded ka asawa nu ne egduduruwey perem te hari nu?
Were you able to prevent your husband from marrying your younger sister?
Eng. stop; control; prevent; restrain; hold back; keep back; discipline; thwart; prohibit; forbid; rebuke; reprimand; scold; tell off; warn against; restrict
Ceb. disiplina
