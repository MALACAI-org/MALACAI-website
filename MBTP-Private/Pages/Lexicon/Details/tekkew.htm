tekkew
/tɔkkɔw/
v_rt.
“shocked”
 
vi.
for someone (egkatekkew, natekkew, katekkew, actor, egkA><ø-) to be shocked by something (igkatekkew, ingkatekkew, reason, igkA><ø-)
Egkatekkew kew ke eghinguma ka Magbebayè su iling seini te takawen.
You will be surprised by the return of God because it's just like a thief.
Iyan kud igkatekkew su miglepew e ka anak ku ne duen e induma ne malitan.
I shocked that when my son arrived he accompanied a woman.
Eng. surprised; shocked; stunned
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Surprise (3.4.1.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for something (egpakatekkew, nakatekkew, pakatekkew, agent, egpAkA-) that can surprise someone (igpakatekkew, impakatekkew, reason, igpAkA-)
Eng. surprise, can; surprise, able
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
