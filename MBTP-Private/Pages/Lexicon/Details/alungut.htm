alungut
/ʔaluŋut/
v_rt.
“approach”
 
vi.
for someone (eg-alungut, mig-alungut/in-alungut/pig-alungut, agent, eg><ø-) to approach someone (eg-alungutan, mig-alungutan/in-alungutan/pig-alungutan, location, eg><an-)
O. Syn. dugpù
Eng. approach
 
vi.
for two or more people (egpeg-alungutey, impeg-alungutey/migpeg-alungutey/pigpeg-alungutey, agent, egpeg><ey-) to approach each other
Migpeg-alungutey ka langun ne me istudyanti su migbebasbas te egkanteen dan.
Students gathered around because they practice singing their song.
Egpeg-alungutey ki rè ke egkeeng ki su deisek dè ka platu ta.
We will just asemblage when we eat because our plate is not enough.
Data Qual: example doesn't match gloss
Eng. approach; interfere; meddle; annoy; disturb; gather around; asemblage
