keddangan
/kɔddŋan/
 
n.
Mulleripicus_funebris
sooty woodpecker
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se amana ne mahulub ke egpamitew te egkakeen din diyè te tungenu ne kayu su egtetektek eyew egpanlihawang ka kated ne egkakeen din.
O. Syn. balansahan
Cult. A small black bird with a red comb on its head. It is usually found in dead trees, and produces noise by pounding wood using its beak, looking for food.
Eng. bird variety; sooty woodpecker
Tag. ibon
Ceb. langgam
OCM: Fauna (136)
DDP: Bird (1.6.1.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
