pammula1
/pammula/
Var. pamula
v_rt.
“plant”
 
vt.
for someone (egpammula, migpammula, pammula, agent, eg><ø-) to plant something
(igpammula, impammula, theme, ig><ø-) somewhere (egpammulaan, migpammulaan, pammulaan, location, eg><an-)
Wrd.Gloss: plant
Ka pammula ne lalag iyan igpasabut ka peglebeng te benì diyè te tanè. Sabeka seini ne paahi eyew kenè egkaamin ka minsan nekey ne egtubù te tanè. Duè degma me benì wey ke minsan nekey ne egtubù ne kenè igpamula.
Egpammula a te bulak diyè te lama ney su eyew meupiya ne egtengtengan.
I will plant flowers in our yard so that it will be good to see.
Pammula a te aheley kaaselem eyew duè egtubù.
I will plant+corn tomorrow so that there will be grown up.
Syn. ad-ad

n.
(pammulaan_te_gulay, -an) garden
Migmareet e ka pammulaan ku te gulay su insudsud te babuy te sumbaley ney.
My garden was destroyed by the pig of our neighbor that's why its now ugly.
Pammulaan ku te gulay ka indaru ni apu su meupiya.
I will plant avegetable the garden that father plowed.
