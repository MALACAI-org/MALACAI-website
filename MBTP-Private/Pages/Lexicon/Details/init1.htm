init1
/ʔinit/
adj_rt.
“hot”
 
1.
adj.
(meinit, mA1-) hot in temperature
Meinit ka aldew kuntee su warè gapun ne nakaringding te langit.
The weather today is hot because there are no clouds covering the sky.
Ant. maagsil
Eng. hot (of temperature)
Tag. maínit; mabanás
Ceb. mainit
Ilo.: napúdot
Tigwanon: ʔinit
Sarangani: ʔinit
WBM: ʔinit
Ilianen: ʔinit
Dibabawon: ʔinit
Cagayano: ʔinit 'to warm up'
Tagbawa: mɛnit
Obo: manit
Proto: ʔinit
OCM: Sensation and Perception (151), Ethnometeorology (821)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
v.
for someone (egkeinitan, neinitan, experiencer, egkA><an-) to feel hot
Keinitan a.
I feel hot.
Ant. kelepen
Eng. hot; warm
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vt.
for someone (eg-init, mig-init/in-init, eg-iinit, in-iinit/mig-iinit/pig-iinit, agent, eg><ø-, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to heat up something (eg-initen, in-init/mig-init/pig-init, patient, eg><en-) in something (eg-initan, in-initan/mig-initan/pig-initan, location, eg><an-)
Eg-iinit a te weyig su egke-upian a ne eg-inum te kepi.
I will heat some water for I want to drink coffee.
Subla e ne iiniti ka inlala ne weyig.
The warmed-up water was so hot now.
Eng. heat something up; warm something up
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
n.
(keinit, kA-) temperature
Eng. temperature; heat
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(iiniti, rdpCV–i) very hot
Eng. hot, very; hot, extremely
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
adj.
(meiniten, mA1–en) lukewarm (e.g., of water)
Eng. warm; lukewarm; tepid
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
2.
adj.
(meinit, mA1-) feverish
Meinit ka lawa ku su neuranan a gabii.
I am feverish because I was caught by rain yesterday.
Syn. dalu
Eng. feverish
