paa
/paʔa/
n.
“leg/foot”
 
1.
n.
the leg or foot (of a human or animal)
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se ighipanew te etew. Egkahamit ka paa te peggipanew, pegtakerang, pegsipè wey pegdiek. Te etew, egkahamit ka paa eyew iggipanew, igtakerang, igsipè, wey igdiek. Te me ayam, egkahamit ka paa, te peggipanew, pegkalkal wey senge kakuwa ne igsipè wey duma pad.
Due pali te ikakawanan ne paa ku.
There's a wound of my right foot.
Nabalingu ka paa ku su nakasipè te batu.
My foot is distorted because (it's) cause to hit stone.
Wrd.Gloss: leg; foot
Eng. leg; foot; hoof; paw; body part
Tag. paá
Ceb. batíis; bitíis
Ilo.: sáka
