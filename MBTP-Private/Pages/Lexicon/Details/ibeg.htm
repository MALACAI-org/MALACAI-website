ibeg
/ʔibɔg/
v_rt.
“feel_like_eating”
 
n.
appetite
Eng. appetite; desire; ambition; lust
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for a person or animal (egkeibeg, neibeg, agent, egkA><ø-) to feel like eating something
Egkeibeg ne egkeen te malamismis ka me egpangalaling.
Pregnant women love to eat sour food.
Eng. feel like eating; appetite; desire (a feeling of wanting to eat something); crave; long for
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Want (3.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
