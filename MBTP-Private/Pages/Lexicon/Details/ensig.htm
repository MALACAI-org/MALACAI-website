ensig
/ʔɔnsig/
v_rt.
“move”
 
vi.
for someone (eg-ensig, mig-ensig/in-ensig, agent, eg><ø-) to move slightly (for example to let someone past)
Wrd.Gloss: move
Eg-ensig e pad kayi te gumawan su egbayè pad ka apù ku.
Kenè kew e eg-ensig kayi te ledled su egkatipil ad.
O. Syn. egwanggil
Eng. move aside
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vt.
for someone (eg-ensig, agent, eg><ø-) to move something (eg-ensihen, theme, eg><en-)
Wrd.Gloss: move
Eg-ensig kew iya mulè te bubuyahen ne batang te kenè egkeelehan.
Eg-ensihen te pad ka batu diyè te dalan ta su eglised ki ka egbayi.
We will move the stone aside because its hard for us to pass.
O. Syn. wanggil
Eng. move aside; move sideways; nudge; push aside
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
