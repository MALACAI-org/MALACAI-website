umil
/ʔumil/
“backward”
 
adj.
for a child to still be physically slow at progressing; weak
Umil ne batè ka anak nu.
Your child is weak.
Eng. slow; backward; handicapped
 
vi.
for a child (egke-umil, neumil, actor, egkA><ø-) to still be physically slow at progressing; weak
Eng. weak
