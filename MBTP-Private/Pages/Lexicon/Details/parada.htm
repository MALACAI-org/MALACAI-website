parada
/parada/
 
n.
parade
Ka parada iyan ka pegsabeka peggipanew te malepet dè ne egbayaan isip pegpakita te kandan ne insabekaan ne himu wey ke katuyuan iling te pegsuman-suman te pegbunsud dan wey te impurtanti ne neyitabu kandan.
Meupiya ne egpitawen ka me menubù ne migsaluub te kumbalè dan diyè te parada.
Manobo are beautiful on their cloth (traditional uniform) on the parade.
Manì buwa te warè mig-apil si Uked te parada te menubù din ma perem?
Why Uked didn’t participate on the parade where in fact he is a manobo?
Eng. parade

v.
to parade
Ka egparada iyan egsabeka eggipanew te malepet dè ne egbayaan isip pegpakita te kandan ne insabekaan ne himu wey ke katuyuan iling te pegsuman-suman te pegbunsud dan wey te impurtanti ne neyitabu kandan. Ka igkarangeb ne igpasabut kayi iyan ka egpakita te katuenan diyè te duma ne me etew.
Egparada ka me Matigsalug te Hulyu isip pegsuman-suman te kalihawangan dan puun te pegleig-leig kandan te me biseyè.
Matigsalug will parade on July to remember their freedom from taken advantage by visayan (people).
Egparada kun kuntee ka matig-MLCA diyè te baskitbulan.
Those-from-MLCA will present at the basketball court now.
Egparada degma ka me matig-Dabaw ke kadayawan.
Those-from-Davao will also parade when/during Kadayawan.
Eng. parade

n.
parade
Ka igpasabut man-e kayi iyan ka pegpapitew te katuenan dan diyè te me etew iling te pegsayew.
Due parada te matig-Basak dengan ne sulad ne igkeupii ku te egpitew.
There was a presentation sulad of ones-from-Basak which I liked to watch.
Kenà ad egkeupian ne egpitew te parada kuntee te marusilem su masulug ka egkengewubug diyè.
I don’t want to watch a presentation tonight because there are many drunk there.
