uhat
/ʔugat/
n.
“tendon/vein”
 
1.
n.
tendon
Eng. tendon; ligament; fibre
Tag. lítid; ligamento; tendon
OCM: Ethnoanatomy (826)
DDP: Bone, joint (2.1.6), Flesh (2.1.7)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(uhaten, -en) fibrous
Mareet ka binggala ne ingkeen ney su uhaten.
The cassava that we had eaten was stringy.
Eng. fibrous; stringy
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
