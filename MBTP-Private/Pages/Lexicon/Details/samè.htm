samè
/samɔʔ/
v_rt.
“left_over”
 
n.
left-over
Ka samè iyan ka egkaahat ne baad puun te senge timman te minsan nekey iling te keenen, kasangkapan wey te duma pad.
Hendeid e ka samè te keenen ta gabii?
Where is the left-over of our food yesterday.
Iyan ku naan dè nakeen ka samè dan te egkeen su nakakabus ad.
Only left-over of their food I had eaten because I was late.
Warad samè te kumbalè ne impamehey ni Dutirti?
Is there no more remainder of clothes given by Dutirti?
Eng. left-over; remainder; rest
Tag. tíra; báhaw
Ceb. subra; bahaw
Ilo.: súbra; kilábban
OCM: Preservation and Storage of Food (251)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
vt.
for someone (egsamè, migsamè/insamè, eg><ø-) to set aside
Wrd.Gloss: set_aside some
Ke sapulù ka pitsay, egsameen ka sabeka eyew egbenien.
If you have ten pitsay plants, leave one aside to go to seed.
Eng. set aside
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for something (egkasamè, nasamè, egkA><ø-) to be left over
Eng. left-over; left over; remainder
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
