sambì
/sambiʔ/
v_rt.
“go_missing”
 
vi.
for someone, especially a child or a soldier, or something, especially an animal such as a chicken (egkasambì, actor, egkA><ø-) to go missing from somewhere (egkasambian, location, egkA><an-)
Ka egkasambì iyan ka egkalaag, egkatarin wey egkapatey ne baad te sabeka ne punduk etew wey me mananap.
Nasambian te sabeka diyè te Tul-an ka alamara su migpatayan sikandan ki Nidù.
The alamara (force) lost one at Tul-an because Nidu died.
Nasambian ka me piyak ku su narawiyan te banug.
Some of my chicks are missing because egle pounced them.
Masulug ka NPA ne nasambì diyè te Dilapa su masulug nahud ka sundalu.
Many NPA members died in Dilapa because the soldiers are many.
Wrd.Gloss: go_missing
O. Syn. tarin
O. Syn. laag
O. Syn. awè
Eng. go missing; missing, go; lost; stolen
