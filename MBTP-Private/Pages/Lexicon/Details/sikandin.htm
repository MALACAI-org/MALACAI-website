sikandin
/sikandin/
 
pron.
he; she; it; him; her
Egluyud a iya te asawa ku ke eggipanew sikandin.
I will certainly walk side-by-side with my husband if he walks.
Nakapanimet naan dè si Nanti te sanig su naaminan e sikandin te keenen.
Nanti was eating up the remaining burnt rice because he had already eaten the rice.
Gramm. 3rd person singular focus pronoun (Set I, full form)
Eng. he; she; it; him; her
Tag. silá
Ceb. sila
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Pronouns (9.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
 
pron.
as for him/her/it
Sikandin ka suluhuen ney.
As for her, she is our helper.
Gramm. 3rd person singular topic pronoun
Eng. as for him/her/it; he (topic); she (topic); it (topic); him (topic); her (topic)
Tag. silá
Ceb. sila
