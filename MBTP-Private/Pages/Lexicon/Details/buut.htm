buut
/buʔut/
v_rt.
“command”
 
v.
for someone (egbuut, migbuut, agent, eg><ø-) to command someone else
Ka igpasabut te buut iyan ka peg-awè te katenged te sabeka ne etew. Egpekeiling seini te uripen ne minsan hendei rè egsuhu-a. Meupiya ne egbatasanen ka iling kayi su migpeila te pegpariralem. Biniseyè seini ne lalag ne layun e eggamita te me Matigsalug.
Duè katenged din ne egbuut su sikandin ka pangulu.
He has the authority to rule because he is the president.
Si Bura ka migbuut ke nekey ka eggimuwen te me Hari rin.
Bura make decision for his younger brothers/sisters.
Kenè ne meupiya ne egbuutan te me ginikanan ka anak te kursu ne igkeupii su kenè egpekeimpus.
It's not good to parents to+decide of the course for their children because they will not succed.
Meupiya ne egbuutan ka etew ne buutan su mahaan egtuman.
It's good to+control the person who is kind because he/she easy to follow.
Syn. suhù
Eng. command; order; will; determine; decide; decision; control; rule
Tag. mag-útos; utosán
Ceb. sugù
Ilo.: bilínen
