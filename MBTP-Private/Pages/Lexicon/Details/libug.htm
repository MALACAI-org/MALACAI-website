libug
/libug/
v_rt.
“commit_adultery”
 
vt.
for a man (egpakalibug, agent, egpAkA-) to commit adultery with a married woman (egkalibuhan, egkA><an-)
Ka egpakalibug iyan ka egpekeyilabet te kenè ne asawa rin. Migpasabut seini te minsan te himu ne mareet pinaahi te peglablabetey te daruwa ne etew ne manggiasawè e.
Nakalibug ka anggam ku te asawa te sumbaley rin.
My uncle commit an adultery to his neighbor's wife.
cf. diek
Eng. commit adultery; adultery, commit
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for a man and woman (egpekeglibuhey, agent, egpekeg><ey-) to commit adultery together
Egpekeglibuhey en iya mulè kangkuwa ka tahasumbaley ne egparumdumeey te tagsè egbelegyè te lanut.
Eng. commit adultery; adultery, commit

Usage level: R
