dulug
/dulug/
v_rt.
“accompany”
 
1.
v.
for a person or animal (egdulug, migdulug, indulug/pigdulug, agent, eg><ø-) to go near; to accompany; to be together with (egduluhan, migduluhan, induluhan/pigduluhan, patient, eg><an-) someone or something (igdulug, indulug, reason, ig><ø-) for a purpose
Egkeupian a ne egdulug ki Inè ne eglipereng su layun dè egguhungutan.
I want to lay together with my grandma during night for she is going to tell us old stories.
Meupiya ne egduluhan ka kepitan ney su amana ne matinabangen.
It is good to approach to our captain because he is helpful.
Iyan ku igdulug te kepitan ney su due purublima ku.
The reason that I am going to approach of our captain because I have a problem.
Eng. to be near; near; approach; accompany; together
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
v.
for someone or something (egmegdulug, migmegdulug/immegdulug, agent, egmeg-) to put something close to one another or putting something together (egmegduluhen, immegdulug/migmegdulug, patient, egmeg-en)
Eng. put something close to one another
Ceb. pagduulon
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
