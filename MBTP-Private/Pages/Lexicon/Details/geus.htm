geus
/gɔʔus/
 
adv.
to do something accidentally when you shouldn't have
Mania te gues nu igbelegyè ka aheley te warè pad nahangu?
Why are you selling the corn when it's not dried yet?
Mania te geus ka eggipanew te egtahad ki pad te sinubba?
Why don't you want get going - we'll wait for what's cooking?
Eng. mistakenly; accidentally; impatient
