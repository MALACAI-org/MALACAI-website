silib
/silib/
v_rt.
“alert”
 
v.
for someone (egsisilib, migsilib/insilib, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to be spy or watchful (e.g. if things are going missing from your house) with out others being aware; to notice or to overhear (egsiliben, insilib/migsilib/pigsilid, theme, eg><en-)
Egsiliben e ni Tuntun ka ayè din ne egpatangtangkeey te mahaliyug din.
Tuntun spy her aunt intertaining her visitor.
O. Syn. ipat
Eng. spy; alert; aware; watchful; notice; overhear
DDP: Alert (3.1.2.1), Notice (3.1.2.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpakasilib, nakasilib, egpAkA-) to be aware of something
Nakasilib ad e te alunggun ne nekeg-ehet.
I am aware that the couple was troubled.
Eng. aware
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
