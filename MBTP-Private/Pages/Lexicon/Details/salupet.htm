salupet
/salupɔt/
v_rt.
“lie”
 
v.
(egsalupeten, eg><en-) to not tell the truth but for everyone to realise. e.g. to exaggerate or to alter a story
Egsalupeten ad e te me duma ku su egkeimma ma sikandan keddì.
My companions exaggerated me for they are envious of me.
Eng. not tell the truth; exaggerate; alter; lie; falsely accuse; accuse falsely
Tag. magsábi ng totoó
Ilo.: agpúdno
OCM: Norms (183), Non-electronic Transmission of Messages (202), Wrongs (673)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
(egsesalupet, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to gossip; to pass on rumors which may be true or may be false
Mesi eglibulung e ka me malitan ne egmei-hingutuwey, egsesalupet naan e man-e sikandan.
When the women gathered together and delousing with each other undoubtly they are gossiping.
Eng. slander; exaggerate; gossip
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
