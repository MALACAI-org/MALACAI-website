dagket
/dagkɔt/
v_rt.
“touch”
 
vt.
for someone (egdagket, migdagket/indagket, agent, eg><ø-) to move so as to touch something (egdagketan, indagketan/migdagketan/pigdagketan, location, eg><an-)
Kenè kew dagket te meinit ne weyig su egkaluwasan kew.
Don't touch hot water because you'll be scalded.
Eng. touch; contiguous
Tag. idaiti; idiít
Ceb. daít; dap-ig; dapat; dutdot
Ilo.: agkamang; ampir
OCM: Sensation and Perception (151)
DDP: Sense of touch (2.3.5)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something or part of the body (egpakaragket, nakaragket, iparagket, agent, egpAkA-) to be touched (igparagket, imparagket, igpA-) on something or at someone else (egparagketan, imparagketan/migparagketan/pigparagketan, location, egpA><an-)
Iparagket nu te weyig ka paa nu su eyew egkaheram nu ke mahagsil naan e.
Get your feet to touch hot water to see if it is cold already.
Eng. touch
Online dict status: c
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egkaragketan, naragketan, agent, egkA><ø-) touching e.g. a man touching a woman
Wrd.Gloss: touching
Kenad iya egkaragketan te lukes si Nina.
Nina never be touched by men.
Eng. touching
Online dict status: c
Usage level: M
