panulud
/panulud/
v_rt.
“escort”
 
1.
v.
for someone (egpanulud, migpanulud/impanulud, agent, eg><ø-) to escort someone (igpanulud, impanulud, theme, ig><ø-) somewhere, especially a bus or jeepney terminal (egpanuluran, impanuluran/migpanuluran/pigpanuluran, location, eg><an-) (often for them to catch a ride somewhere) but returning immediately
Wrd.Gloss: escort
Egpanulud a te me alukuy ku.
I will escort my friends.
Si Petel migpanulud kanami.
Peter showed us around.
Keyid-u rè iya te suled ku ne egpanulud keddì te eggendiyè te baley ney.
I feel compassion with my brother who walking me home.
Gramm. It could be that this word historically came from the root sulud or tulud, but modern speakers can't agree on which one.
Eng. escort, to; accompany; walking with; guide, to; show around; introduce; orientate
DDP: Show, let someone see (2.3.1.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpapanulud, migpapanulud/impapanulud, agent, eg><ø-pA-rdpCV-) to request to someone to scort or to accompany going home (egpapanuluran, impapanuluran/migpapanuluran/pigpapanuluran, location, eg><an-pA-rdpCV-)
Eng. request, to
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
v.
for someone (egpanulud, migpanulud/impanulud, agent, eg><ø-) to get rid of the head lies (egpanuluran, impanuluran/migpanuluran/pigpanuluran, location, eg><an-) using fine-tooth comb which is made of bamboo (igpanulud, impanulud, theme, ig><ø-)
Eng. get rid of
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
