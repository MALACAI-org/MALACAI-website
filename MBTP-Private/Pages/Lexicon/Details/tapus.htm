tapus
/tapus/
“end”
 
n.
death; end
Ka seini ne lalag iling ke due impatey ne emun ke iglebeng e ka minatey egseel te siyew ne aldew ka tagtenged eyew eggastu alang te pegpaliling te gimukud te impatey.
Seiini te eghinguma ne Mayu 09, 2009 iyan e tapus te impatey ne keilangan ne due pegpanubad ta te seini ne aldew.
This coming May 09, 2009 will be the end of her death and we need to have a ritual this day.
Eng. death; end
Ceb. tapus
