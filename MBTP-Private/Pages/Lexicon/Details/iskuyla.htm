iskuyla
/iskuyla/
 
vi.
to attend school
Ka igpasabut te iskuyla ka eg-angey te katuenan diyè te mindan nekey wey ke diyè naa te minsan hentew. Kene egkahamit ka seini ne lalag diyè te me ayam su kene ma eg-iskuyla sika.
Eg-iskuyla a eyew due katuenan ku.
I will study so that I gain knowledge.
Neiskuylaan kud ka langun kayi te ampew te tane.
I learned everything on earth.
for someone (eg-iskuyla, mig-iskuyla, actor, eg><ø-)
Eng. student; learn; study
Tag. mag-aaral
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
1.
n.
a school (iskuylaan, iskuyla:n, -an)
Eng. school; study place; education place
