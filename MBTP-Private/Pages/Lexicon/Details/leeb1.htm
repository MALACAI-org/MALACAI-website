leeb1
/lɔʔɔb/
n.
“small_shelter”
 
n.
a small shelter
Ka leeb iyan ka deisek ne baley ne innimu eyew egtagtaheeran te eg-ugpè. Egkaayun ne diyè seini igpes-ek te minsan hendei iling te kamet, te bariyu, te eglenaan wey minsan hendei pad. Innimu seini ligkat te kayu, damuluwan, luwey, minsan nekey pad ne eg-atepan te kalan, dewun te abaka wey minsan nekey pad ne egkaayunan.
Due leeb te migpangeg-ut diyè te bangalug.
Eng. temporary shelter; shelter; hut; booth
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for someone (egleleeb, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to build a small shelter, e.g., for a shelter for pigs (egleeban, location, eg><an-)
Egleleeb ka me bakuwitir eyew due egtaheeran te eg-ugpè.
The evacuated will build a small house to have a temporary shelter.
O. Syn. kayab
Eng. build small shelter
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
