ignuranti
/ʔignɔranti/
Fr. Eng. ignorant
 
adj.
ignorant
Ignuranti ne etew sikandin, sikan naa, egkaaldek sikandin ne eggendiyè te siyudad su kema kun ke egkalaag.
She is ignorant that's the reason that she will not go to city because she afraid to be lost.
Mahirap ka ignuranti ne etew su ware kalintahan te me nekey-nekey.
The ignorant person is difficult because he/she knows nothing.
Eng. ignorant; foolish; uneducated; stupid
