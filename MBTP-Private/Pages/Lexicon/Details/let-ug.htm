let-ug
/lɔtʔug/
v_rt.
“erect”
 
n.
pervert
Ka let-ug iyan sika ka egbehey te ibeg te minsan hentew ne egkeupian eg-abid. Due kayi ka langun ne due lebut ne iyan egpuunan ne egpakaanak emun ke egpekeg-uma ka benì te lukes wey te atelug te malitan. Ka let-ug iyan egpakeyinalig te minsan hentew ne eg-abid wey egpaabid. Diyè seini egpuun te suman-suman wey diralem te etew wey mananap naa. Seini ka igngaran te eggeramen ne egdumaan te baliyut, eyam, ibeg wey imma wey egkataman egkalet-uhan e. Egpakabehey seini te mareet ne batasan te etew emun ke kenè egpakatigkel wey egpeimma-imma rè due wey egpalihè-lihè.
Pegnela nu ka let-ug nu su egkareetan ki.
Control your pervert because we will be ruined.
Mareet ne egdumaan si Ulnu su iyan dè egsumsumanen se let-ug din.
Ulnu is not good to accompany with because he thinks only of his pervert
Eng. pervert
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
vi.
for a man (egkalet-uhan, location, egkA><an-) to have an erection
Ka egkalet-uhan iyan ka egkehalan te lasù para te me lukes su egkeupian ne eggilabet te igkeupii ran ne eggilabetan. Ne ka me malitan degma egpallebag buwa degma ke egkeemisan/egkalet-uhan. Ka me etew wey ka me mananap egkaayunan te seini ne lalag wey ka minsan nekey ne due betì, lasù, susu wey geyinawa wey ibeg.
Batè pad si Segana ne egkalet-uhan e.
Segana erected his penis even he's still young.
Egkalet-uhan e ka daruwa ne kuddè ne egpabutengey su egpekeg-ahew te beyan.
The two horses are erecting their penis because they are fighting for female horse.
Kahi te iney ku ne emun ke egpakegdani ka malitan wey lukes su egkalet-uhan kun e ka lukes.
My mother said that when man and woman will close to each other the penis of man will erect.
Ant. kiles
Ant. bulung
Eng. erect penis; penis erect

for someone (egpalelet-ug, location) to cause an erection
