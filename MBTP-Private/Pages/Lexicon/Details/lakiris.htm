lakiris
/lakiris/
 
vi.
for something (eglakiris, miglakiris/inlakiris, agent, eg><ø-) to go very swiftly
Iyan igpasabut te seini ne lalag se minsan nekey ne meiyal ne subla ne egweil wey ke egpallahuy naa.
Lakiris dè ne egpallahuy ka kuddè te apù ku.
My grandfather's horse running very fast.
Iiyali iya ne egpallahuy si anggam Buelan su eglakiris dè iling te kuddè.
Uncle Buelan is running so fast because he runs swiftly as horse.
Eng. swiftly; very quick; fast; fleet
Tag. tulin
Ceb. ab'tik; alis'to; kalit
