tuhun
/tugun/
v_rt.
“reach_destination”
 
1.
v.
for someone (egtuhun, migtuhun/intuhun, agent, eg><ø-) to reach one's destination; to go as far as the end of the field when planting (egtuhunan, intuhunan/migtuhunan/pigtuhunan, location, eg><an-)
Ka igpasabut te seini ne lalag iyan ka pegginguma diyè te pulipusan: iling te eggilamun egbunsud diyè te diralem ne egtuhun diyè te ampew wey ke nekeyingume e diyè te katamanan te miggilamunan. Egkaayun degma te minsan nekey ne himu basta egpekeyingume e diyè te katamanan.
Mahaan ad e egtuhun ka egpamula te tebesal.
I am now reaching the end the field planting squash.
O. Syn. lanting
Eng. reach end; end trip; land, to; finish trip
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpakatuhun, nakatuhun, agent, egpAkA-) to reach a goal on a journey or in an activity
Diyè key e nakatuhun te Temmuhan ka miglawang.
We had reached Tamugan upon riding a logs tied together.
Eng. reach; achieve
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
n.
(tuhunanan) to dock (a boat)
Syn. tamananan
Syn. dunggu
Eng. dock
Tag. piyér; pantalán; daúngan; dumáong
Ceb. pantalan; dungguan
Ilo.: piyér
OCM: Navigation (502)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
