ley-as
/lɔyʔas/
v_rt.
“lie_in_state”
 
v.
for someone (egpaley-as, egpaleley-as, agent, egpA><ø-, egpA><ø-rdpCV-) to lie a body down facing upwards (igpaley-as, instrument, igpA-)
Ka igpaley-as iyan ka igpeyibat ne nakaley-ang wey naayun ka paa. Minsan ka eggibat ne eggulep dè due ne egkaayunan te seini ne lalag wey ka igpaketang ne minatey eyew egdamahen wey wey egbehayan te katamanan ne limlimuan wey eyew degma ig-andam eyew iglebeng. Eggimuwen seini eyew igpaayad-ayad igpeyibat eyew egpakabunsud.
Kenè ka ka paleley-as ka eggibat su egdupaken ki te busew!
Do not lie down facing up because the evil spirits will come to us!
Hendei ipaley-as ka sumabaley niyu ne napatey?
Where is the dead body of your neighbor lie?
Impaley-as e ka batè ne nalened gabii diyè te Simud.
The child was laid down??? which was drowned yesterday in Simud river.
Eng. lie in state
Tag. nakabúrol
Ceb. gihayà
Ilo.: nakamansáyag
