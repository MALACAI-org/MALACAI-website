bubun
/bubun/
n.
“thigh”
 
n.
thigh
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se dibabew te buel wey diralem dè te lebut.
Iyan ku igkatelesi te egkeen ka bubun te bakbak su malambeg.
The thigh of frog is my favorite because it is fat.
Wrd.Gloss: thigh
Eng. thigh; upper leg; body part
