gakut
/gakut/
Data Qual: If the imperative/command form is hakut (see below) then I suspect that hakut should be the root form?
 
n.
tie/bundle
Ka gakut iyan igpasabut ka igbagkes ka minsan nekey wey ke ka minsan hentew diyè te minsan nekey wey ke minsan hentew. Ka duma ne lalag te seini iyan ka tagkes, bagkes, hiket wey gakus.
Inggakut ni Neru ka lubid diyè te lamisa.
Neru tie round up the rope on the table.
Hakut nu pa seini diyè te kayu.
Please tie it up on the tree.
Eng. tie up; bundle
Tag. bigkís; talaksáns; tungkós
Ceb. bugkos; bantal

Usage level: M
