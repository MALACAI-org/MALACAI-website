bennal
/bɔnnal/
v_rt.
“desire”
 
1.
v.
for someone (egkabennalan, nabennalan, egkA><an-) to accept a proposal or idea; to consent to a price (igkabennali, ingkabennali, ikabennali/ingkabennali, reason, igkA–i)
Kenà a egkabennalan te inlalag din keddì.
I will not agree of what he spoke to me.
Kenè buwa igkabennali ni Midi ka inlalag ni Ayun su migmareet e se ulaula ni Midi.
Midi might dis+like the story of Ayun because her face was disfigured.
Warè buwa ikabennali ni Bada ka imbeli te asawa rin ne salual su mania te kenè din ma egsaluuben te pegsimba.
Bada might dis+liked the pants that were bought by his wife because he did not wear it to worship.
Eng. accept; consent; agree; convinced; content; persuaded; believe

Usage level: M
