pamanihus
/pamanigus/
v_rt.
“wash”
 
v.
for someone (egpamanihus, migpamanihus/impamanihus, pamanihus, agent, eg><ø-) to bathe
Egpamanihus key te dahat kaaselem.
We will bathe on the beach tomorrow.
Eng. bathe; wash
Tigwanon: digus
Sarangani: digos
WBM: peziǥuʔ
Dibabawon: padigus
Cotabato Manobo: pedigus
Tasaday: pedigus
Tagbawa: pedigus
Proto: (pa+)digus
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for a group of people (egpampamanihus, migpampamanihus/impampamanihus, pampamanihus, agent, eg><ø-paN-) to keep on bathing
Egpampamanihus ka batè te Simud.
The child keep on bathing at Simud river.
Eng. bathe
Tag. malígò
Ceb. maligò
Ilo.: agdígus
OCM: Personal Hygiene (515)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for a group of people (egmamanihus, immamanihus/migmamanihus, mamanihus, agent, egmA1-) to bathe
Egpampamanihus ka me etew te dahat.
People bathing to the ocean.
Eng. bathe
Tag. malígò
Ceb. maligò
Ilo.: agdígus
OCM: Personal Hygiene (515)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
