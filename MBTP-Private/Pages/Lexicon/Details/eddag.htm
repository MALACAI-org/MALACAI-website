eddag
/ʔɔddag/
 
conj.
as if
Ka eddag iyan ded ka meddag ne lalag ne sabeka ne palinneu te pegdali-di wey pegdew-ey.
Eddag kun matagseb si Unyut kunaleg te hambug din bes dè iya.
Unyut pretend as wise but rather he just boast.
Mania te eddag bes dè iya ne mabulut ka te migsenehew ke naan ded iya?
Why you pretend as you are brave then you cried?
Eddag lukes kunaleg te egsayudsud ded se buel te liyas.
He pretend as true man (lit. male) but the knee shakes of fear.
Eng. pretend
