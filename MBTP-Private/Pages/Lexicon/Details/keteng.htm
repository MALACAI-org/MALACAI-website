keteng
/kɔtɔŋ/
v_rt.
“straighten_out”
 
1.
vt.
for someone (egketeng, migketeng/ingketeng, agent, eg><ø-) to straighten something out (egketengen, ingketeng/migketeng/pigketeng, patient, eg><en-)
Eggendiyè key te Luwey su egketeng key te sungkaleg ne impes-ek ney gabii su nahulibid.
We will going to Luway to straigthen the post that was layed out yesterday for it was disaligned.
Egketengen ku pad ka peka ku su masakit.
I'll just straighten out my back (by standing up straight) because it's sore.
Eng. straighten out; stretch out; extend
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
vt.
for someone (egketeng, migketeng/ingketeng, agent, eg><ø-) to put many trap straight as a line (egketengen, ingketeng/migketeng/pigketeng, patient, eg><en-)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
