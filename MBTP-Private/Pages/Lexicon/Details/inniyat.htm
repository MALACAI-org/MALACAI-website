inniyat
/ʔinniat/
v_rt.
“want_disappointed”
 
v.
for someone (egkeinniyat, neinniyat, meinniyat, egkA><ø-) to strongly desire to do something (igkeinniyat, ingkeinniyat, ikeinniyat, instrument, igkA><ø-) but to be unable to do it, and so be disappointed
Ka pekeinniyat iyan ka minsan nekey ne egpakabagget wey ke egpakeyinalig te etew wey me mananap.
Egkeinniyat a perem ne egduma te me alukuy ku piru kenè ma egbehey ka iney ku.
I really want to go with my friend but my mother isn't allowing me.
cf. ima
Eng. attracted to; be attracted; want to; wish; desire to (a feeling of wanting to do something e.g., going with friends or to accompany trips); covet; catch attention; attention catching
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Disappointed (3.4.2.1.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vt.
for something (egpekeinniyat, nekeinniyat, actor, egpAkA-) to be desirable to someone, or to be the thing (igpekeinniyat/impekeinniyat, reason, igpAkA-) which someone desires (even though they can't do or have it)
Eng. desirable, to be
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
