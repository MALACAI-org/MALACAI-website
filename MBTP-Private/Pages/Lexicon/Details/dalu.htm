dalu
/dalu/
n.
“sickness”
 
1.
n.
sickness
Wrd.Gloss: sickness; sicknesses
Masalig kuntee ka dalu kayi te inged ta.
There are many illnesses here in our place.
Eng. sickness; illness; disease
Tag. sakít
WBM: daru
OCM: Dying (763)
DDP: Disease (2.5.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for a person or animal (egderalu, migderalu/inderalu, experiencer, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to be sick
Kenè egpakaruma si Tata kanta su egderalu.
Tata cannot go with us for he is sick.
Eng. sick; ill; feverish
Tag. maysakít
Ceb. nagsakit
Ilo.: agsakít
OCM: Dying (763), Theory of Disease (753)
DDP: Fever (2.5.6.2), Body condition (2.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
adj.
(taharaluwen, taga–en) ill
Mahuyè si Undu su taharaluwen sikandin.
Undu is weak because he is ill.
Eng. ill
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
adj.
(daluwen, -en) unhealthy
Eng. unhealthy
DDP: Body condition (2.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
adj.
(taggaraluwen, tagga–en) sickly
Taharaluen ka anak ni Limpung.
Limpung's child is sickly.
Eng. sickly
DDP: Body condition (2.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
for a family (egdaluwan, migdaluwan/indaluwan, agent, eg><an-) to have a member got sick
Eng. get sick; got sick
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something (egpakaralu, nakaralu, theme, egpAkA-) to cause sick
Eng. sick, to cause
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F

n.
woman sick
Eng. woman sick; menstruation
