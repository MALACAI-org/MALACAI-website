pantew
/pantɔw/
“look_afar”
 
v.
for somone (egpantew, migpantew/impantew, agent, eg><ø-) to look at from a distance (egpantawen, impantew/migpantew/pigpantew, patient, eg><en-)
Eng. look, to; watch out, to
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
for a person or animal (egpamantew, migpamantew/impamantew/pigpamantew, agent, eg><ø-paN-) to look out constantly to a distance (egpamantawen, impamantew/migpamantew/pigpamantew, patient, egpA><en-)
Egpamantew a ke hentew seini se migginguma ne etew.
I will look out to+see who this person is that's coming.
Eng. look out from a distance; view; survey; observe
Tag. mátanawán
Ceb. miyahat
Ilo.: kumíta ti adayo
OCM: Sensation and Perception (151)
DDP: Look (2.3.1.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
v.      
for an area (egkapantew, napantew, theme, egkA><ø-) to be overlooking
Eng. overlooking, to be
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
