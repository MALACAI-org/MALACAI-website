uney2
/ʔunɔy/
v_rt.
“private”
 
vi.
for someone (eg-uney-uney, mig-uney-uney/in-uney-uney/pig-uney-uney, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to feed and look after oneself
Kenè pad egkateu ne eg-uney-uney ka malalab pad ne batè.
A young baby doesn't yet know how to look after itself.
Sikeddì mismu ka mig-uney miggendiyè te baley ni Ayit ka egpangguhud te neyitabù.
I myself will go alone to Ayit's house to tell what happened.
Eng. by yourself; personally; yourself, take upon; take upon yourself; upon yourself
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for a group (egpeun-unayey, impeun-unayey/migpeun-unayey, eg><ø-pA><ey-rdpCVC-) to fight within the group
Due me taltalahari ne egpeun-unayey egpeg-ehetey su warè ma Manana diyè te pusung dan.
Some brothers fighting with each other for they don't have God in their hearts.
Eng. civil war; fight; infighting; personal; private
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
adj.
(peununayey, pA><ey-rdpCVC-) personal; between family
Eng. personal; between family; connected; attached
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
