sugud
/sugud/
 
v.
(egsugud, migsugud/imbunsud, eg><ø-) to begin something
Ka sugud ne lalag nekeg-iling ka igpasabut te bunsud ne lalag. Iyan dè nakalein te seini su biniseyè ne egkahamit e te me matigsalug te egpanlelalag.
Emun ke egkamit kew e kayi kew sugud te diralem su eyew egmeupiya.
When you clearing the land, start below so that it will be nice.
Migkelkel dengan ka apu ku su nakarineg ne egbunsud e egpangimatey ka me Epun.
My grandfather/mother trembling when he/she heared that Epun begins killing.
Eng. begin, to; start, to; from, start; foundation
Tag. simulâ
Ceb. sugod
