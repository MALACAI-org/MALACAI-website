kulibew
/kulibew/
 
vt.
for someone (egkulibew, migkulibew, kulibew, agent, eg><ø-) to stir something (egkulibawen, kulibawa, theme, eg><en-)
Ka kulibew ne lalag iyan igpasabut ka pegweil te weyig, sabew wey kepi eyew egpekeg-amut ka in-amut te weyig, sabew wey kepi. Eyew egpekeg-amut ka minsan nekey ne in-amut te weyig, sabew wey kepi, eggamit te minsan nekey. Egtilengen ka minsan nekey ne igkulibew diyè te weyig, sabew wey kepi. Egkahamit man-è seini ne lalag eyew igpanunggiling te samuk.
Kulibawa nu ka kepi nu wey gerami nu ke eleg e.
Stir up your coffee and taste if it is enough.
Egkulibawen kid e seini, samuk diyè, samuk kayi.
It is stirs us–chaos there, chaos here.(Meaning: we are in chaos)
Eng. stir; chaos; tempest
