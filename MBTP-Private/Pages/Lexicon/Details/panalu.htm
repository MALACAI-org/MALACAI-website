panalu
/panalu/
 
v.
(egpanalu, migpanalu, panalu, eg><ø) victory
Ka panalu ne lalag iyan igpasabut ka pegdeeg diyè te sabeka ne etew.
Migpanalu si Pakyaw te pegbuksing su duè trupi rin.
Pakyaw had won from his boxing because he has a trophy.
Egpanalu ki te langun ne kunterè ta basta paniguru ki.
We will win to all of our enemy if we do our best.
Eng. win; won
Ceb. daog
