Matigsalug
/matigsalug/
prop_n.
“those_by_the_Salug_river”
 
prop_n.
the name of this tribe, literally "those who live by the Salug river", Salug being their name for the upper reaches of the Davao river
Wrd.Gloss: Matigsalug
Masalig ka me Matisalug ne nangebpè te Simud.
Many Matigsalug tribe living in Sinuda.
Eng. Matigsalug; tribe
