pakeid
/pakɔʔid/
v_rt.
“possess”
 
vi.
for someone (egpakeiran, migpakeiran/impakeiran, location, eg><an-) to be possessed by an evil spirit (busew) that causes them to do violent acts such as killing an innocent party (egpakeid, migpakeid/impakeid, agent, eg><ø-)
Egpekeiran buwad seini se duma ta ne egpanibbas dè due te minsan nekey.
It seems that this fellow is demon-possesed for he will chop at anything.
O. Syn. pamunu
Eng. possessed; murderous; violent; uncontrollable
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for something (egpakapakeid, nakapakeid, agent, egpAkA-) to cause someone to be possessed by an evil spirit (busew) that causes them to do violent acts such as killing an innocent party
Eng. cause, to
