tigri
/tigri/
 
n.
cat family
Ka tigri sabeka seini ne klasiyan te ayam. Meiyal seini ne egpallahuy, malidus ka me sulu, wey mabatek. Mabulut seini ne ayam wey manekal.
Kenè kew egdani te tigri su mabulut.
Don't draw near to tiger because it's cruel.
Mabatik bes ka tigri?
Is tiger colorful?
Eng. tiger
Ceb. tigri
