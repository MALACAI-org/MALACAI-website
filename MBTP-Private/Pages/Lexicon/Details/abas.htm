abas
/ʔabas/
Var. habas
n.
“measles”
 
n.
measles
Wrd.Gloss: measles
Ka abas dalu egginguma te me batè. Egpanlepew diyè te laplap ne mallintek piru malalab. Ne meyi kenè egkabawian ka batè egpatey.
Neyid-u a te hari ku su due abas ne migpanlepew diyè te kandin.
I felt sorry for my younger brother because he broke out in a measles rash.
Hapit migpatey si Tetuk te abas su neempet e ka lawa rin.
Tetuk almost die of the measeles because it's scattered on his body.
Eng. measles; rash
Tag. butlíg-butlíg
Ceb. típdas (k.o. measles)
OCM: Theory of Disease (753)
