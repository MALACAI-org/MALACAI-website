libed2
/libɔd/
n.
“waistband”
 
n.
a material waistband tied in a knot (especially that which is part of the traditional Matigsalug dress for a woman)
Ka libed iyan ka ngaran te igbagkes kayi te liwaraan te salual. Innimu ligkat te manggad ka para te malitan wey innimu ligkat te hinabel wey in-ampawan teyii te me balieg eyew igdayan-dayan kayi. Malalab ka batek te ka libed te me malitan piru nabatek-batek ka libed te me lukes. Piru kuntee ne timpu, kenad amana eggamit ka iyam pad nanulin ne me Matigsalug ne me batè su iyan dan e eggamiten ka iyam ne libed ne innimu ligkat te hinabel ne makepal wey ka duma innimu ligkat te me kayu wey putew wey me plastik. Ne iyan naan dè eggamit kayi ka me buyag ne tapey e ne miggamit en iya wey ka egpakabeli kayi su sabeka ne kuddè ka bali te sabeka ne libed.
Hendei nu beliya ka libed nu?
Where did you buy your waistband?
Eng. waistband; band, waist; girdle; sashs; belt
Ceb. bakos
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (eglilibed, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to wear a material waistband
Eglilibed a su maluag ka salual ku.
I will wear a belt because my pants are too big.
Eng. wear waistband; wear belt; belt, wear
