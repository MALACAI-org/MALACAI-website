iya
/ʔia/
part.
“indeed”
 
part.
indeed, really
Wrd.Gloss: indeed; really
Iling ded iya.
They'rè just the same.
Sein dè iya.
Just this.
Amana iya ne mateles ne eg-ulahing si Rosalyn.
Rosalyn is indeed very good at singing.
Gramm. emphatic aspectual adjunct
Eng. indeed; really; truly; surely; certainly; that's for sure; for sure; just; must
DDP: Particles (9.2.6), Yes (9.4.6)
Online dict status: r
Usage level: G
