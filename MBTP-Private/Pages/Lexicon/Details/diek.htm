diek
/diʔɔk/
v_rt.
“tread”
 
1.
v.
for someone (egdiriek, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to thresh (by treading on the rice stalks); ???To tread (something such as rice stalks)
Ka egdiriek iyan ka pegsayew te paley eyew egkapupu puun te lawa wey ke tagdey naa. Sabeka degma seini ne sayew te me menubu ne Matigsalug pinaahi te pegbales te me paa ran sasangan te egsaseyew diyè te inlimud ne paley. Seini ka himu te me menubu eyew te peglein te paley ligkat te lawa/tagdey iling te peglupu te aheley ligkat te bahal kayi.
Egdiriek a te paley.
I'm going to thresh a rice stalks.
Eng. trample; thresh; tread; trod
Tag. gumiík
Ilo.: bunután
OCM: Annual Cycle (221)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
(egkariekan, egkA><an-) to step on something
Ka egkariekan ka iyan ka pegsikal diyè te minsan nekey ne neunturan perem te paa. Migpasabut man-e seini meyitenged te minsan nekey ne needisan dè iya egkasuhat/egkeunturan te paa te pegsikal.
Eyra, egkariekan kud e perem ka pinamula niyu.
Eng. tread on; step on; trod on
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
v.
(egdi-diekan, eg><an-rdpCVC-) to abuse
Mani naa te egdi-diekan niyud ka keddì ne dengeg?
Why did you abuse my responsibility?
Eng. abuse
Ceb. abusaran
OCM: Sex and Marital Offenses (684)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
