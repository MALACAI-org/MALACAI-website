“head_louse”

v.
for someone (egkutuwen, ingkutu/migkutu/pigkutu, kutuwen, patient, eg><en-) to have head lice
Layun ka pamanihus su eyew kenè ka egkutuwen.
Take bathe regularly to avoid head lice.
Eng. lice infested; infested with head lice
