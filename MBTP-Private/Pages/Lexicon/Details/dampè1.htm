dampè1
/dampɔʔ/
v_rt.
 
v.
(egdampeen, dampeen, eg><en-) to catch a bird on the ground (igdampè, instrument, ig><ø-)
Wrd.Gloss: catch
Egdampeen ku perem ka bakbak piru mig-umbetut e diyè te weyig.
I am going to catch the frog but it jumped into the water already.
Eng. catch
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpakarampè, pakarampè, nakarampè, agent, egpAkA-) to be able to catch something
Ay-ayari niyu te egpitew ka egpandampeen niyu su kema ke egpakarampe kew te kenè ne eleg.
You should watch carefully of what you are going to catch because you might fall forwarded the wrong one.
Eng. catch, able
