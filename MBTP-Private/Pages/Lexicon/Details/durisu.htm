durisu
/dudisu/
 
adj.
Check GU dictionary??
Syn. tul-id
Eng. durisu
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
(egdurisuwan, eg><an-) to be prompt
Ka egdurisuwan iyan ka due-rue ne peggimu/pegpakita te minsan nekey diyè te minsan hentew. Ka seini ne lalag migpasabut te degma te warè peglamu-lamu te minsan nekey ne egkasuman-suman ne eggimuwen te warad pegsuman-suman ke nekey ka eggingumaan te eggimuwen din.
Mareet ke egdurisuwan nu te eg-inse ka etew ne nabeley pad.
It's not good to ask somebody promptly who is tired.
Eng. prompt; on time
