habel
/habɔl/
Var.1: abel
Data Qual: rani says it should be habul
Data Qual: maybe not
v_rt.
“weave”
 
n.
(hinabel/inabel, <in>-) woven material
Migpami-pì sikandin te me hinabel diyè te weyig.
Syn. manggad —cloth
Sabeka ne saku ka manggad ne naangey ku te migpamasku.
Eng. material; cloth, woven; woven cloth; fabric; blanket
Tag. téla
Ceb. panapton
Ilo.: téla
OCM: Woven Fabrics (286)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpangabel/egpang-abel, agent, eg><ø-paN-) to weave (cloth)
Data Qual: tans says he hasn't heard this form - he's only heard egpangimu te hinabel
Ka igpasabut te seini ne lalag iyan sika se pegpanampey te minsan nekey diyè te sampayanan iling te: kumbalè, lanut. Egkaayun degma ne ka pegpandampil te minsan nekey.
Egpanggabel a te me kumbalè ku.
I have been weaving my clothes.
Eng. weave
Tag. maghábi; laláhin
Ceb. panghabol
Ilo.: agabél
