puun
/puʔun/
v_rt.
“come_from”
 
prep.
from; since
Eng. from; since; ever since; out of
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
 
vi.
for someone or something (egpuun, migpuun/impuun, agent, eg><ø-) to come from somewhere (egpuunan, location, eg><an-)
Wrd.Gloss: come_from
Hendei ka egpuun?
Where did you just come from?
O. Syn. egligkat
Eng. come from; came from; been; originate; source
WBM: puun
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
n.
(egpuunan, eg><an-) reason
Eng. reason; cause; origin
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
