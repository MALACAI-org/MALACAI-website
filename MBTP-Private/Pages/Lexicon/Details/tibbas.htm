tibbas
/tibbas/
Var. tibas
v_rt.
“slash”
 
vt.
for someone
Ka igpasabut te tibbas iyan ka pegpali te minsan nekey. Egkaayun ne egkaperi, egkatamped, egkasapsap ka sika se egkatibbas.
(egtibbas, agent, eg><ø-) to cut with a knife (with one stroke) (egtibbasen, egpanibbasen, patient, eg><en-, eg><en-paN-) e.g., a sapling or small tree
Syn. habè
Syn. pisang
Syn. tempeg
Kenè ka eg-iran-iran ki Pidru su mahaan sika egtibbas.
Don't mock Pidru because he quickly slashes out (with a machete).
Egtibbasen e perem ni Pidru ka etew ne mig-iran-iran kandin.
Pidru tried to cut open the person who mocked him.
Eng. cut; slash; chop; gash
Tag. gulukan; pagtagâ

Ilo.: tagá
