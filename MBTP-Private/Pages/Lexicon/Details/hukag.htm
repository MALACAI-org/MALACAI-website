hukag
/hukag/
 
n.
stir or to be disturbed by noise or some other problem
Eng. trouble; bother; uproar
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Upset (3.4.2.1.6)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
vi.
for a group of people (egkewukag, newukag, actor, egkA><ø-) to become stirred up
Ka newukag iyan ka egkahubut wey nasamuk tenged te due igkeinu-inu wey igkasasew. Amana egwakigwang wey egkabantalan ka me etew ne egkewukag iling te me ayam ne igpalihawang diyè te kulungan ne amana ne mahulub.
Egkewukag ka taburuk te baley ran ne nasinggul.
The ants were disturbed in their house that got brushed (by somebody passing by).
Newukag ka matig-Tulay ganna su due nakabangga ne terak.
The people at Tulay village were at panic because there was truck accident.
Newukag ka me etew te pegginguma ni Hisus diyè te Hirusalim su amana sikandan nalibeg ke hentew sikandin.
The people were stirred up when Jesus came to Jerusalem because they were very confused about who he was.
Eng. in uproar; panic; tumult; stirred up; bothered; troubled; disturbed; disorder; roused; panicked; panicking
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Upset (3.4.2.1.6)
