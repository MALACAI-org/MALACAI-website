deldel
/dɔldɔl/
Var. delrel
v_rt.
“expel”
 
v.
for someone (egdeldel, migdeldel, agent, eg><ø-) to drive away someone else (egdeldelen, theme, eg><en-)
Wrd.Gloss: drive_away
Ka igpasabut te seini ne lalag iyan ka pegpaawè te minsan nekey; Egkaayun degma ne ka peg-abug te mananap puun te sabeka ne inged peendiyè te lein ne inged.
Malasi ku egdeldela ka babuy te sumbaley ney su egpanuwalen din ka me pinamula ku.
I frequently drive away the our neighor's pig because it roots up our plants.
Indeldel kud e diyè te ugpaan ney ka anaken ku su amana en iya ne pehulen.
I drove off my nephew from our house because he is really very lazy.
Eng. drive off; drove off; chase away; shoo away; scare away; excommunicate; expel; banish; exile; cast out; send away; sent away; herd; round up
