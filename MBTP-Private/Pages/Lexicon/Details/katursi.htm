katursi
/katursi/
num.
“fourteen”
 
num.
fourteen
Wrd.Gloss: fourteen
Katursi ka me baketin ku piru sapulù dè ka neuyag su migmanderalu ka hep-at ne namatey e seini.
I had fourteen piglets, but now only ten are alive, because four got sick and died.
Cult. Among the younger generation, Matigsalug numbers are rarely used for numbers over ten.
Eng. 14 (Spanish); fourteen (Spanish)
OCM: Numeration (802)
DDP: Cardinal numbers (8.1.1.1)
