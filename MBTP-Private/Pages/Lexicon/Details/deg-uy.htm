deg-uy
/dɔgʔuy/
v_rt.
 
vi.
for someone (egdeg-uy, migdeg-uy/indeg-uy, agent, eg><ø-) to wince with pain at something (igpakareg-uy, impakareg-uy, instrument, igpAkA-)
Ka igpasabut te lalag ne egdeg-uy ka pegdehengdeheng tenged te kasakit ne eggeramen te etew. Migpalinneu seini te masumpit e ka dalu wey ke eggeramen te sabeka ne etew. Egpakapanlagkuy naan dè sikandin tenged te masakit wey masumpit ne eggeramen din. Ka egdeg-uy iyan ka laheng te etew ne egdehengdeheng wey egpanlagkuy tenged te masakit ne miggeram din.
Malasi egdeg-uy te marusilem ka anaken ku su egkasakitan sikandin te ulu rin.
My niece always groaned at night for he suffers headche.
O. Syn. deheng-deheng
O. Syn. dalew
Eng. wince; cringe; whinge; grimace; groan; sigh
OCM: Sensation and Perception (151)
DDP: Pain (2.5.6.1)
