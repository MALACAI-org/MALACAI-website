deldeg2
/dɔldɔg/
 
n.
a part of a kuglung instrument.
Ka seini iyan sika se baad te lawa te kuglung ne lenged e te kelengan.
Mabatek ka deldeg te kuglung din.
The deldeg part of his kuglung guitar is colorful.
Ew meupiya ne egpitawen ka deldeg te kuglung nu ra!
Oh the deldeg of your kuglung guitar is good to see!
Eng. part of kuglung
