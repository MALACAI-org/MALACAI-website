binayu
/binayu/
v_rt.
“pound”
 
vt.
for someone (egbinayu, migbinayu/imbinayu, binayu, egbibinayu, migbibinayu/imbibinayu, bibinayu, agent, eg><ø-, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to pound something, especially rice paley, uliben, dawa, lenga, linaplap ne aheley, kepi ne eggimuwen ne pulbus (egbinayuwen, migbinayu/imbinayu, binayu, patient, eg><ø-) with a pestle (andu) (igbinayu, imbinayu, instrument, ig><ø-) in a mortar (lesung) (egbinayuwan, imbinayuwan/migbinayuwan/pigbinayuwan, binayuwi, location, eg><an-)
Miggendiyè ka iney ku te senge baley su egbinayu te paley.
My mother went to the neighbor to pound rice.
Nekey a ka egbinayuwen nu due?
What are you pounding with?
