lahetlet
/lahɔtlɔt/
 
v.
for something (eglahetlet, eg><ø-) to get worse
Ka lahetlet iyan ka nasì wey ke egtamtamanan dè iya te eggeramen. Egpekegduma seini te dalu wey kenè degma egkaayunan ka duma pad ne me lalag kayi.
Nasì dè iya lahetleti si Luminaya te buwè minsan migpanginum e sikandin te me delemetan.
Luminaya's cough indeed has gotten worse even though she took medicines.
Miglahetletan dè iya mulè si Amè te dalu rin su kenè ma egpalintutuu te talagbawì.
Father's illness got worse because he doesn't believe the doctor.
Su mahirap man-e ke eglahetlet e.
Because it's also difficult if it gets worse.
Eng. worst; worse, get; badly ill; remission; get sicker; sicker, get
