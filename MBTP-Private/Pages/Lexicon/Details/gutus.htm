gutus
/gutus/
v_rt.
“cause_wind”
 
1.
n.
stomach
Eng. stomach
OCM: Ethnoanatomy (826)
DDP: Stomach (2.1.8.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(gutusen, -en) have a big stomach
Eng. big stomach
DDP: Stomach (2.1.8.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
2.
vi.
for a food (eggutus, miggutus/inggutus, agent, eg><ø-) to cause the eater (eggutusan, inggutusan/miggutusan/piggutusan, location, eg><an-) to get stomach wind
Ka igpasabut te seini ne lalag iyan sika se egdakel se getek wey due kalamag diyè te getek, egkahung ka getek wey egmasakit seini. Egkaayun degma ne sika se egbuturan wey iyan egpuunan kayi ka pegkeen te meyilew ne egkeenen.
Emun ke egtutuug kew te aheley, ay-ayari niyu te eggilutù su eggutus.
If you will going to roast corn, cook it very well for it will cause wind.
Eggutusan a ke egkeen a te giniling.
My stomach will get wind if I will eat ground corn.
Eng. cause wind; wind, cause
