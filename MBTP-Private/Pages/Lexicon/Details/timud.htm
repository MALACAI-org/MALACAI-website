timud
/timud/
v_rt.
“accumulate_wealth”
 
1.
v.
for someone (egtitimud, migtitimud, timud, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to cheat by telling someone you have no food in order to get more or to steal when you are already wealthy
Ka timud ne lalag iyan igpasabut ka peglilimud te minsan nekey eyew egdakel. Meupiya ne subla ka egtitimud su emun ke due prublima mahaan due egkaangey. Piru te Matigsalug, ware nabatasani ka pegtitimud.
Egtitimud a te seleppì su egpalit a te kalabew.
I'm going to save money for I'm going to buy a carabao.
Migtitimud si Randy te hemey eyew eggamiten ne igkamet.
Randy gathered rice inorder to use during planting season.
Eng. save; gather
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
2.
v.
(egpakatimud, egpakatitimud, egpAkA-) to increase one wealth
Hapit ad e egpakatimud te seleppì ne egpalit ku te kuddè.
I'm now merely earned money for a horse purpose.
Eng. increase wealth; accumulate wealth
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
