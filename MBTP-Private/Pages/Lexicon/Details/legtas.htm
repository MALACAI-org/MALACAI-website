legtas
/lɔgtas/
v_rt.
“sever”
 
v.
for someone (eglegtas, miglegtas, agent, eg><ø-) to sever something (such as a piece of string or cane or rope) (eglegtasen, inlegtas, legtasa, patient, eg><en-)
Ka eglegtas iyan ka pegtamped te minsan nekey ne nakabanggut wey ke nakabalabag naa. Iyan man-e seini ka pegtempug te minsan nekey ne kenè amana ne dakel iling te gawed.
Eglegtas e pad te abaka su eggiket ku te manuk.
I'll chop off a piece of hemp fibre because I'll use it to tie up the chicken.
Eng. chop off; cut off; snip off; sever
Tag. magpalakól
Ceb. atsa; wasay
Ilo.: wásay
OCM: General Tools (412)

Usage level: M
