lablab1
/lablab/
n.
“male_pig”
 
n.
a male wild pig, used especially when referring to a wild boar
Ka lablab iyan ka ngaran te mahintalunan ne babuy ne lukesan. Piru huwey rè egngarani kayi emun ke bubuyahen e wey kenè egkaayun ne egngaranan kayi ka bakitin pad minsan lukesan.
Nekeutel gabii te lablab ka anggam ku ne migteteen te pimpung.
My uncle slaughtered a wild boar yesterday by means of a trap which explodes when a pig bites it.
Nakapamaneyik kun dengan ka iney ku te sehè su indula te lablab ne malayat e se ngipen.
My mother cause to climbed up with a poisonous shrub because she was attacked by a wild boar with long teeth.
Ant. baleug
O. Syn. baketin
Genr. babuy —sow
Eng. male pig; boar; pig, male
Tag. bulugan; barako
Ceb. butakál
Ilo.: bolá
OCM: Fauna (136)
DDP: Hoofed animals (1.6.1.1.3), Male and female animals (1.6.7)
