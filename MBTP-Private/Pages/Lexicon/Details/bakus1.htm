bakus1
/bakus/
 
v.
for someone (egpakabakus, nakabakus, agent, egpAkA-) to delay or hinder
Ka egpakabakus iyan ka egpakatalehen wey egpakabanggew te eggimuwen iyan naan pa iya ke egkaana-ana. Iyan man-e seini ka minsan nekey ne egpakatalanga wey egpakabalabag te eggimuwen iling te batè, me inuyanan, me ayam wey me kalaglahan ne amana ne susuluhi.
Kenè perem igparani te me hibateen ni Hisus diyè te kandin ka me batè su egpakabakus te pegpanulu din te me etew piru impeparani sikandan ni Hisus.
Hisus' disciples didn't allow the children to come to him because they will hinder him from teaching the people but Hisus let them come to him.
Eng. interrupt; hinder; obstruct; delay; block
