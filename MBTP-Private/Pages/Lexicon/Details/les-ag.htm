les-ag
/lɔsʔag/
v_rt.
“be_at_ground_level”
 
vi.
for someone (egles-ag, agent, eg><ø-) to sit or lie on the floor (egles-ahen, location, eg><an-) without a mat
Eng. sit on floor; lie on floor; sleep on floor; floor level
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something (igles-ag, ???, ig><ø-) to be at ground level; to not be put away
Ka igles-ag iyan ka pegtahù te minsan nekey ne kenè eg-ay-ayaran wey ke kenè egtalipunen su egbalahren dè due ne nakapahawa-hawa. Iyan man-e lein ne igpasabut kayi ka pegtahu te minsan nekey ne kenè egbelatan wey egtayeen iling te kasilè ne intahù dè due diyè te tanè ne warad iya ubag hapii.
Mania te igles-ag nud e sika se me pini-pian due te seeg te egkabiluwan?
Why you put the washed clothes in the floor for it will get dirty?
Ant. tambad —table level
Eng. ground level; floor level; put on floor; not put away; leave on floor; hide at floor level
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
