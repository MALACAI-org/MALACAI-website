sina
/sina/
v_rt.
“envious”
 
v.
for someone (egkasina, nasina, agent, egkA><ø-) to be envious or jealous of one's material possessions. This may eventually lead to his killing you. (igkasina, ingkasina, reason, igkA><ø-)
Ka egkasina iyan sika se pegkasekel wey pegkeimma te karatuan te duma ne etew. Ka egkasina due suman-suman ne mareet diyè te duma, egkaayun ne eggimatey wey egpanakew. Egkaayun degma ne ka etew egpaningkamut su eyew egkalabawan din ka karatuan te duma.
Masalig ne me etew ne mahaan egkasina ke egkita ne meupiya e se keugpè te sumbaley ran.
Many people envy of their neighbors who's fotunate one.
Igkasina ran ka meitem ne lap-lap ku.
They envy with my black skin.
O. Syn. sekel
Eng. jealous; envious
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Jealous (3.4.2.1.8)

Usage level: L
