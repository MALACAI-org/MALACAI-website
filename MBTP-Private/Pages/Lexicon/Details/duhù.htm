duhù
/duguʔ/
v.
“feel_cheated”
 
vt.
for someone or something (egduruhù, migduruhù, induruhù, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to disappoint somebody who already has a problem
Egduruhù kew keddì te egkalaggew.
You disappointed me, who was worried already.
DDP: Disappointed (3.4.2.1.4)
 
vi.
for someone (egkaruruhù/naruruhù, patient, egkA><ø-rdpCV-) who already has a problem to be disappointed by something (igkaduruhù, reason, igkA><ø-rdpCV-)
Egkaduruhù e si ayè ka egdaralu pad ne warè pad egkakeen.
Aunt is disappointed - she is still sick and still has no food.
Iyan din igkaduruhù se peggawas din puun te purisuwan impatey e ka amey wey iney rin.
He is disappointed that when he came out of prison, his parents were already dead.
I, naruruhu ad e seini su nalimbungan a te tindira ne desek pad ka seleppì ku.
Oh, I feel cheated because I was deceived by the shop assistant (and I already had little money).
Eng. feel cheated; cheated; disappointed
DDP: Disappointed (3.4.2.1.4)
