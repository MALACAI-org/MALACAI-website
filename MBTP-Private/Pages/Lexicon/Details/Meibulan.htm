Meibulan
/mɔʔibulan/
Data Qual: according to my research from some elders Meibulan is a female servant of souls and difinitely he is not a devil spirit just an incharge of the soul according to them [QDL]
Data Qual: Meibulan is a god who is incharge of souls. They are diff. from Satanas according to elders, therefore i decided to put a note. So totally we call Satan as Satanas. The clear evil spirits in a high level who is belong to Satan are, Pakeid, Manalingan, pamanu, tahalisew, tabangkak, mandalangan, iyakan, alimugkat, and gamew-gamew.. . and there are in the low level, the busew, meuy, mentiyanak, mantayu etc.
n.
“servant”
See ?ibul
 
n.
a servant of all souls
Wrd.Gloss: caretaker

Si Meibulan ka malitan ne talagbantey te me gimukud.
Meibulan is the female servant of all souls.
Eng. servant; servant; caretaker
