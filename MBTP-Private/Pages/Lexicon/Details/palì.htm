palì
/paliʔ/
v_rt.
“wound”
 
n.
wound
Kenè kew ubal te ugpit su masumpit pè ma ka due palì.
Wrd.Gloss: wound
Eng. wound, a; cut, a; graze, a; injury; scratch, a
Tag. súgat
Ceb. sámad
Ilo.: súgat
WBM: palì
OCM: Bodily Injuries (752)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egkapalian, napalian, agent, egkA><an-) to get wound with something (igkapalii, ingkaplii, reason, igkA–i)
Ka egkapalian iyan ka egkareetan ne laplap te minsan hentew wey te me mananap puun te minsan nekey ne egpuunan iling te egkapilak, egkatibas, egkahabè wey masalig pad ka duma.
Kenè kew gen-gen te ugpit su egkapalian kew.
Don’t touch a bolo because you will be wounded.
Mania te ubal ka te gepes te egkapalian kad man-e?
Why are you monkeying with a knife and you will be wounded again?
Mania te egkapalian ka ne warè ke ma migseluk-seluk?
You didn’t go off to the bush and why are you wounded?
Eng. wounded; injured
Tag. masugatan
Ceb. masamad
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
