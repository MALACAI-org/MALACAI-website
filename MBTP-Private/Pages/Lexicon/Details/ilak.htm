ilak
/ʔilak/
 
adj.
(pangilak-ilak, paN-rdpRT-) sparkling
Nekey buwa ka egpangilak-ilak seeyè te kewun-an ne egkakita ta?
What of those sparkling ahead that we can see?
Eng. sparkling; flashing
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
adj.
(meilak, mA1-) spectacular
Meilak amana ka bulawan ke egkasilaan te aldew.
The gold is very spectacular when it will expose of the sun.
Eng. spectacular
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
(eg-ilak, eg><ø-) to be resplendent
Egsilak ka alungan ke egkapanè te aldew.
The mirror will resplendent when hit by the sunrays.
Eng. resplendent
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
