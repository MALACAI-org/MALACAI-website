sanggel
/saŋgɔl/
v_rt.
“stop”
 
v.
for someone (egsanggel, migsanggel/insanggel, agent, eg><ø-) to stop doing something
Mania te egsanggel ka te himu nu?
Why are you stopping your work?
O. Syn. engked
Eng. stop; halt; cease; discontinue
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egsanggelan, location, eg><an-) to be stopped from continuing something
Eng. stopped; interrupted
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
Data Qual: should this be egkasaNGsanggelan?
 
v.
(egkasansanggelan, egkA><an-rdpCVC-) to take a long time to finish an activity because of continually stopping
Wrd.Gloss: stop
Eng. stop; cease; discontinue
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
