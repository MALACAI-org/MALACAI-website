amin1
/ʔamin/
v_rt.
“consume”
 
vt.
for someone (eg-amin, mig-amin/in-amin, agent, eg><ø-) to use up something (eg-aminen, in-amin/mig-amin/pig-amin, aminen, theme, eg><en-)
Eng. use up; consume; entirely; completely; exhaust, to; run out of
Tag. ubós
Ceb. huruts
OCM: Gratification and Control of Hunger (261)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for something, especially food (egkaamin, naamin, actor, egkA><ø-) to become used up (igkaamin, ingkaamin, ikaamin, reason, igkA><ø-)
Mahaan ku rè egkaamin ka seleppì ku su masalig ka bayaranan.
I could easily use up my money because I have so many things to be paid.
Eng. used up; depleted
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (eg-amin-amin, mig-amin-amin/in-amin-amin, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to go together somewhere (eg-amin-aminan, in-amin-aminan/mig-amin-aminan/pig-amin-aminan, patient, eg><an-rdpRT-) for a reason (ig-amin-amin, reason, ig-rdpRT-)
Eg-amin-amin ki eggendiyè te Davao te kasal ku.
We will go together to Davao for my wedding.
Eng. together; all together; everyone
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
