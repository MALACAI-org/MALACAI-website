buyag2
/buyag/
adj.
“enormous”
 
adj.
for something, e.g. a finger full of pus, a river after heavy rain (bubuyahen, theme, rdpCV–en) to be enlarged or swollen
Bubuyahen ne kayu ka mahatungal.
Mahatungal trees are huge.
Eng. enormous; huge; large; big; great; enlarged; swollen
DDP: River (1.3.1.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
