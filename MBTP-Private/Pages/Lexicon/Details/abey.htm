abey
/ʔabɔy/
v_rt.
“repeat”
 
v.
for a person or animal (eg-abey, mig-abey/in-abey, agent, eg><ø-) to do again something
Ka eg-abey iyan ka peggimu te minsan nekey ne innimu nud e te an-anayan naa wey ke naluhey e, meupiya naa wey se kenè, dakel na wey se kenè, maheteng naa wey se kein-inuwan naa ne ne egliberan te eggimu.
(eg-abayan, in-abayan/mig-abayan/pig-abayan, location, eg><an-) for a reason (ig-abayi, in-abayi, reason, ig><i-)
Engkeri niyu ka salè niyu su pekeetew kew eg-abey.
Leave behind your sins to become born again.
Eg-abayan ku pad te eggilamun ka ahelayan ku su eyew kenè egkeenen te ambew.
I will still weed again our corn field so that it cannot be eaten by rat.
Mig-abayan ku pa te eg-ebunu ka tebesalan ku su eyew egmasalig amana ka behas.
I applied again a fertilizer to my squash field so that it will bear many fruits.
Iyan ku ig-abayi te egpamula te minsan nekey su eyew egmatubung key.
The reason I planted again of any kind of root crops so that we will be abundant of food.
Eng. again
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for someone's action (eg-abey-abey, mig-abey-abey/in-abey-abey, abey-abey, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to be repeated (eg-abey-abeyan, in-abey-abeyan/mig-abey-abeyan/pig-abey-abeyan, abey-abeyan, location, eg><an-rdpRT-)
Eng. repeat; do again
Tag. ulítin
Ceb. usbon
Ilo.: ulíten
OCM: Numeration (802)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something action or event (egkaabey, naabey, theme, egkA><ø-) to happen again
Eng. happen again, to
