eteb
/ʔɔtɔb/
v_rt.
“do_a_miracle”
 
v.
for God, or a character in a tale (eg-eteb-eteb, egpangeteb-eteb, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-, eg><ø-paN-rdpRT-) to do a miracle, e.g., by speaking something into being (egpanget-eteben, theme, eg><en-paN-rdpCVC-)
Eggipanew ki rè due minsan egpaddusilem e su egpangeteb-eteb e rè eyew kenè eg-uran.
We will go even if the weather is not good - I will just pray so that it will not rain.
Eng. create; miracle; magic
