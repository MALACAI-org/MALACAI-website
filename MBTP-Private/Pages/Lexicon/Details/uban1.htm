uban1
/ʔuban/
 
1.
n.
gray hair
Batè pad sikandin ne duen e uban.
She is still young but she has already gray hair.
Eng. gray hair; grey hair; hair, gray
DDP: Hair (2.1.5)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
adj.
(ubanen, -en) gray haired
Due sabeka ne klasmit ney ne ubanen minsan batè pad sikandin.
One of our classmate with a gray hair even if she's still young.
Eng. gray hair
DDP: Hair (2.1.5)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (eg-ubanen, in-uban/mig-uban/pig-uban, agent, eg><en-) to have a gray hair
Eng. gray hair, to have
DDP: Hair (2.1.5)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(Ubanen, -en) a proper name of a person
Eng. name of a person
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
2.
n.
(Ub-ubanen, rdpCV–nen) a sitio village of Tawas, Kitaotao, Bukidnon
Eng. sitio; village; place name
