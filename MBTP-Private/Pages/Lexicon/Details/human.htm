human
/human/
conj.
“and then; before”
 
conj.
Ka human migpegsumpul te daruwa ne guhuren/himu iling te paahi te sabeka ne himu ke nekey ka eggimuwen te an-anayan wey ka egsinundul.
Impusi nu pa ka himu kayi te baley human ka leug-leug eyew kenè egkabelù ka iney nu te peg-ulì din.
Finish first the homeworks before you wander around so that your mother will not be upset when she comes.
