kalungat
/kaluŋat/
n.
“firm_nasal_mucus”
 
n.
firm nasal mucus
Kalungaten e kun dengan te batè e pad kahi te iney ku.
According to my mother I have a firm of nasal mucous during my childhood.
Eng. mucus; snot; booger
Tag. úhog
Ceb. síp-on
Ilo.: búteg
OCM: Theory of Disease (753), Ethnophysiology (827)
DDP: Mucus (2.2.4), Nose (2.1.1.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpangalungat, impangalungat/migpangalungat, actor) to remove nasal mucus
Ka egpangalungat iyan ka egkulitek te migkehal ne nguweg diyè te irung eyew eg-aween. Minsan nekey ka eggamiten te peg-awe te kalungat, piru ka me duma ne biseye wey ka me meyaman ne me etew, eggamit te me meweumel ne me kasangkapan eyew kenè egkareetan ka me irung dan. Piru ka me menubu, belad dan dè ka igkulitek.
Egpangalungat dè due ka duma minsan egkeen su kenè nahud egkaamin ka kalungat din.
My companion always take out his nasal mucous even eating because it never run out.
Eng. take out nasal mucus; remove mucus; pick one's nose
OCM: Elimination (514), Personal Hygiene (515)
DDP: Mucus (2.2.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
