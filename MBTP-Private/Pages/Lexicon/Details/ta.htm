ta
/ta/
Var. te
pron.
“1pi_NF(II)”
 
pron.
we (incl); by us (incl.)
Egtalagtahen ta ka dinampil ta ne aheley su eyew mahaan egkahangu.
We will spread out our (dried) corn so that it will dry quickly.
Egtahiken ta ka nalepuan su eyew kenè amana eglibahan.
Let us set the fracture so that it will not swell exceedingly.
Hanew te la.
I don't know!
Gramm. 1st person plural inclusive non-focus pronoun (Set II, brief form)
Eng. we(incl); us (incl), by
Tag. nátin
Ceb. natu
Binukid: taw; ta (dual)
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Pronouns (9.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
poss_pron.
our (incl); of ours (incl)
Wrd.Gloss: our(incl)
Seini ka egkameten ta su meupiya ka tanè.
This is the area for our kaingin because it has a good type of soil.
Kenè ki egpabulus te hipanew ta su tabangew ka eggingumaan ta.
We'll not continue our journey because it is uncertain what will happen at our destination.
Gramm. 1st person plural inclusive possessive pronominal specifier (postpositional)
Eng. our (incl); ours, of (incl)
Tag. nátin
Ceb. natu
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Pronouns (9.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
