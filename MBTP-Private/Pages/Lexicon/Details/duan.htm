duan
/duʔan/
v_rt.
“affect”
 
1.
n.
(karuan, kA-) the purpose or reason for something
Warè karuan te etew ne meupiya piru dereeti ka pusung din.
Eng. purpose; value; use; reason; point; in vain; wasted
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
v.
for someone (egduanan, location, eg><an-) to be affected by something they do
Data Qual: gloss - they do? Doesn't match example sentence
Wrd.Gloss: affected
Egduanan a degma ke egderalu ka anaken ku.
I will also be affected if my nephew is sick.
Eng. affected
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
