ureyik
/ʔudɔik/
v_rt.
“go_upstream”
 
vi.
for a person or animal (eg-ureyik, mig-ureyik/in-ureyik, egpeureyik, migpeureyik/impeureyik, agent, eg><ø-, eg><ø-pA-) to go upstream (eg-ureyikan, in-ureyikan/mig-ureyikan/pig-ureyikan, eg-ureyiken, in-ureyik/mig-ureyik, egpeureyikan, impeureyikan/migpeureyikan, location, eg><an-, eg><en-, eg><an-pA-)
Ka ureyik ne lalag iyan ka peggendiyè te diraya ne diyè egbayè te weyig. Ka pegsubal te weyig, pegsuluk.
Egkeupian a ne eg-ureyik te Simud ne weyig ka egpanaklag su masalig se me peit.
I'd like to go upstream in the Simud catching fish, because there are many peit fish there.
Ant. laras
Eng. upstream, go; go upstream; upriver, go
OCM: Routes (487)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(ureyikan, -an) upstream
Ka ureyikan pekeg-iling dè te diraya ne ke hendei ka ulu te weyig diyè ka ureyikan. Iling ded degma ka kelesada igbasi te red degma te weyig ke hendei ka diraya.
Diyè ta iggusgus ka gilu te ureyikan eyew egpakapalaras.
We will poured the poison there upstream so that it will flow downstream.
Eng. upstream
OCM: Ethnogeography (823)
DDP: River (1.3.1.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
