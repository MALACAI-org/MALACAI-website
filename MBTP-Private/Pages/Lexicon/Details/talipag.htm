talipag
/talipag/
 
n.
the opposite side of a river or valley
Hentew buwa etawa ka seeye te talipag?
I wonder who that person is over there on the other bank?
Syn. deyipag
Eng. bank of river; river bank
OCM: Ethnogeography (823)
DDP: Island, shore (1.3.1.5)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for two parties (egpekegtalipag, nekegtalipag, egpekeg-) to be on opposite sides from each other
Nekegtalipag ka kamet ney te suled ku.
My field and my cousins are on opposite sides.
Nekegtalipag ka baley ni Undun wey ni Neddi.
The houses of Undun and Neddi face each other.
Eng. opposite side; other side; facing
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
