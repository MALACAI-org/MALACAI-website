dagpù
/dagpuʔ/
 
adj.
to not be a local
Kenè niyu eg-eheti si Dadu su dagpù dè sikandin.
Do not scold Dadu because he is only a foreigner.
Syn. lapù
Eng. foreigner; settler; migrant; out of town
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egdagpù, migdagpù/indagpù, agent, eg><ø-) to approach or migrate in other nation (egdagpuan, indagpuan/migdagpuan/pigdagpuan, location, eg><an-)
Eng. approach, to; migrate, to
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
