utsinta
/ʔutsinta/
 
num.
eighty
Due seleppi ku ne walu ne pulù su igbeli ku te tag utsinta ne salual.
I have eight ten because I'm going to buy pant priced eighty pesos.
Utsinte e ka idad ni apu su kenad egpekeeleg.
Grandfather is aged eighty because is she/he is weak.
Eng. 80 (Spanish); eighty (Spanish)
Tag. walong sampo
Ceb. otsenta
OCM: Numeration (802)
DDP: Cardinal numbers (8.1.1.1)
