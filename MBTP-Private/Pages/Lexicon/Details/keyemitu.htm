keyemitu
/kayunitu/
 
n.
Chrysophyllum_cainito
star apple
Kenè kew layun pamaneyik te kayumitu su kema ke egkeulug kew.
Don't always climbing up the star apple tree for in the hope that you will be dropped.
Eng. star apple; sugar apple
Tag. kaymitu
OCM: Flora (137)
