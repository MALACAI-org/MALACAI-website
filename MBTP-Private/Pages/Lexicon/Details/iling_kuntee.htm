iling kuntee
/ʔiliŋ_kuntɔʔɔ/
See iling
 
adv.
like now
Keilangan ne eglilimud ki te seleppì su eyew due egkahamit ta te timpu ne egkeyirapan ki, iling kuntee te nabitil ki.
It is necessary for us to collect and save money that we can used in times of difficulties, just like now we are in hunger.
Eng. like now; like this
Tag. ganitó
Ceb. ingon niini
Ilo.: kástoy
OCM: Weights and Measures (804)
