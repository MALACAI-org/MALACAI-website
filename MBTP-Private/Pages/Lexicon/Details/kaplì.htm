kaplì
/kapliʔ/
 
v.
for someone (egpakaplì, migpakaplì/impakaplì, agent, egpA-) to attach something (igpakaplì, impakapli, theme, ig-pA-) on someone's body or something else (egpakaplian, impakaplian/migpakaplian/pigpakaplian, patient, egpA><an-)
Egpakaplì sikandin te bawì te palì din.
He is attaching medicine for his wound.
Tabakù ka igpakaplì ku te palì ku eyew mahaan egkeulii.
I will attach tabacco to my wound to be cure faster.
Egpakaplian ku pad ka te me dewun te kayu ka peka ku su amana ne masakit.
I'll attach my back with some leaves of trees because it's painful.
Eng. attach; put up; stick
Tag. idikit
Ceb. ipapilit
