salliya
/sallia/
v_rt.
“keep_away”
 
v.
for someone (egsalsalliya, migsalsalliya, salliya, agent, eg><ø-rdpCVC-) to keep away from others, e.g., a young girl who doesn't want to be talked into getting married
Ka egsalsalliya iyan ka egpaney-paney egpadpariyù wey egsabsabeka eyew egpakaalihu wey egpanalliya te duma ne etew wey te minsan nekey.
Egsalsalliye e kanta si An-an su egkeyilew sikandin su eg-inum ma sikandin.
An-an keeps away from us because he's ashamed because he had been drinking.
Migsalsalliya pad si Apa te me duma rin su kene sikandin egkeupian ne egduma kandan.
Apa avoided his companions because he doesn't want to go with them.
Eng. keep away; avoid; isolate
Ceb. likay
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
(egpanalliya, migpanalliya, panalliya, eg><ø-paN-) to avoid (meeting others)
Egpanalliyaan e te uyang ku ka alukuy rin su layun dè sikandan egpekeg-ehet.
My brother-in-law keeps away from his friend for they always quarrel with each other.
Eng. avoid; escape
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
adj.
(mapanalliya, mapaN-) timid
Eng. timid; shy
DDP: Shy, timid (3.4.2.4.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
