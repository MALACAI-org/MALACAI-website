kalasag
/kalasag/
n.
“shield”
 
n.
a shield
Ka seini iyan sika se igbunbun wey ig-atang te pilak te duma eyew kenè egkasuhat ke egpilaken, seini kayu ne imbatekan te malalab wey meitem.
Emun ke warè kalasag ku, nasuhat e buwad te piet te kunterè ku.
If I had no shield, I might struck by the bow of my enemy.
Wrd.Gloss: shield
Cult. not round
Syn. teming
Eng. shield; protector
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
