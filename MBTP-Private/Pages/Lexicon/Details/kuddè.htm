kuddè
/kuddɔʔ/
n.
“horse”
 
n.
Equus_caballus
a horse
Wrd.Gloss: horse
Eng. horse; stallion; mare; mule
Tag. kabáyo
Ceb. kabayò
Ilo.: kabályo
OCM: Fauna (136)
DDP: Hoofed animals (1.6.1.1.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
vi.
for someone (egkukuddè, migkukuddè/ingkukuddè, kukude, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to keep domestic horses for riding (egkukuddaan, ingkukuddaan/migkukuddaan/pigkukuddaan, location, eg><an-rdpCV-) for a purpose (igkukuddè, ingkukuddè, reason, ig-rdpCV-)
Egkukuddè ka me matig-Dalurung ne eggendiyè te Puntian.
People of Dalurung ride a horse going to Puntian.
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for someone (egpanguddè, migpanguddè/impanguddè, pangudde, agent, eg><ø-pA-) to ride a horse (egpanguddaan, impanguddaan/migpanguddaan/pigpanguddaan, location, eg><an-pA-) for a reason (ig-pangudde-i/in-pangudde-i, reason, ig-pA-)
Eng. ride; drive
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
