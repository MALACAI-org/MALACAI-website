luwak2
/luak/
Data Qual: QDL dosn't knw this word
Data Qual: Naomi check example sentences
n.
“wave”
 
n.
a wave of water – usually a breaker
Data Qual: check the definition of this - because lumpiyakan is synonym
Ka luwak iyan ka diyè te ka dakel ne bunlud. Emun ke egbaggiyu ka weyig ne derakel e ka ka me bunlud ne hendue te egmangensudkensud, sikan naan e iya. Piru senge kakuwa diyè dè egkakita seini te me tuwalen su warè amana ne luwak diyè te dahat su warè ma me batu ne egkasalampawan te weyig ne iyan egpuunan ne due luwak. Ka diyè te dahat neeling dè mulè ne bunlud su mapatag ma.
Pangen-gen kew su egtuwal kid te Ngipen te Panayangan ne amana ne derakel se luwak.
Hold (tightly) because we will now rough through great waves of the Teeth of Panayangan.
Nakasakebut a te weyig te migtuwal key te Dumalegdeg ne amana se me luwak.
I drank water when we rough trough terrible waves in Dumalegdeg.
