din
/din/
Var. rin
(used when the preceding word ends with a vowel)
 
pron.
he; she; it; him; her
Egpangelit din te kuglung.
He is strumming the kuglung with a pick.
Gramm. 3rd person singular focus pronoun (Set I, brief form)
Eng. he (focus); she (focus); it (focus); him (focus); her (focus)
Binukid: din
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Pronouns (9.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
 
pron.
he; she; it; him; her
Warè din ingkeupii ka batasan te atebey rin.
He didn't like the attitude of his sister.
Warè din ingkeupii ka imbehey nu kandin ne kumbalè.
He didn't like the item of clothing you gave to him.
Gramm. 3rd person singular non-focus pronoun (Set II, brief form)
Eng. he (non-focus); she (non-focus); it (non-focus); him (non-focus); her (non-focus)
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Pronouns (9.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
 
poss_pron.
his; her; its; of his; of hers
Ka dilè din egdiwalwal ke egkabeley sikandin.
Her tongue will come out when she gets tired.
Gramm. 3rd person singular possessive pronominal specifier (postpositional)
Eng. his; her; its; his, of; hers, of
Binukid: din
Ref.Gloss: his; her; its
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Pronouns (9.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
