kulugmut
/kulugmut/
 
n.
thick bush
Ka kulugmut iyan ka me lawed wey hilamunen ne egkampet te senge pinamule e degma wey minsan te me tued wey kayu. Egmukut-mukut seini wey eglapung ne em-empet te ingkapetan, ne egkataman, egkabunbunan e ka ingkampetan.
Diyè e rè eggeles te kulugmut eyew kenà a egkakita te duma ku ne iyan busew.
I will hide in the bush so that my companion who is the tag will not find me.
Kahiyen ku ne busew e ka migsasindeg diyè te dalan, kunaleg te kulugmut bes dè.
I thought something standing along the way was an evil spirit, but rather a thick bush.
Eng. bushy; bush; vine

v.
for the grass (egkulugmut, migkulugmut/ingkulugmut, theme, eg><ø-) to cover something (egkulugmutan, ingkulugmutan/migkulugmutan/pigkulugmutan, location, eg><an-)
Ka belun ta ingkulugmutan e te balunsari.
Our well is covered by a thorny grass.
Eng. cover, to
