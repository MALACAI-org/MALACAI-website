pusakè
/pusakɔʔ/
n.
“keepsake”
 
n.
something or someone too precious to give up
Wrd.Gloss: keepsake
Ka seini se Ancestral Domain ne egkahiyen, seini iya ka pusakè ne inged ta.
Ancestral domain is said to be as a precious land of the climants.
Eng. precious; keepsake; precious thing
