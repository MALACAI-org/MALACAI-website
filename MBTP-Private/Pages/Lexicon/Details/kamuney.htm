kamuney
/kamunɔy/
n.
“owner”
 
1.
n.
owner
Wrd.Gloss: owner
kamuney te tinda
owner of the store
Eng. owner; occupant
Tag. may-árì
Ceb. tag-iya
Ilo.: akin?
OCM: Retail Marketing (443)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
2.
v.
for someone (egkamkamuney, migkamkamuney/ingkamkamuney, agent, eg><ø-rdpCVC-) to accept to own something (egkamkamunayen, ingkamkamuney/migkamkamuney/pigkamkamuney, patient, eg><en-rdpCVC-)
Keddì ka egkamkamuney te kalabew ni amè su keddì ka panganey.
I will become the owner of my father's carabao because I am the oldest child.
Eng. own, to (something)
OCM: Retail Marketing (443)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for someone (egkamkamuney, migkamkamuney/ingkamkamuney, agent, eg><ø-rdpCVC-) to be the temporary owner (egkamkamunayen, ingkamkamuney/migkamkamuney/pigkamkamuney, patient, eg><en-rdpCVC-)
Eng. temporary owner
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpangamuney, migpangamuney/impangamuney, agent, eg><ø-paN-) to become the owner of something (egpangamunayen, impangamuney/migpangamuney/pigpangamuney, patient, eg><en-paN-)
Eng. owner, to become
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
