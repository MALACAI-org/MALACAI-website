anggam
/ʔaŋgam/
n.
“uncle”
 
n.
uncle
Wrd.Gloss: uncle
Migpanumbaley diyè te kanami gabii ka anggam ku, hari te iney ku.
My uncle visited to us yesterday, he is my mother's young brother.
