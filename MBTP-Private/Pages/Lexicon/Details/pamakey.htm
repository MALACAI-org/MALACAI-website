pamakey
/pamakɔy/
Data Qual: should the root be smaller here? - is there a paN- prefix?
v_rt.
“serve”
 
v.
for someone (egpamakey, migpamakey, agent, eg><ø-) to believe (and obey?) all (of God's commands) (egpamakayen, theme, eg><en-)
Wrd.Gloss: believe
Keupian a ne egpamakey te Manama su sikandin ma ka migbehey te keddì ne umul ku.
I want to obey God for He is the one who give me life.
Egpamakey te Manama.
Eng. believe; trust; obey; serve; follow a religion; worship
Ceb. alagad
OCM: Congregations (794)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(pegpamakey, peg><ø-) belief
Kumusta ka pegpamakey nu?
How is your belief?
Wrd.Gloss: belief
