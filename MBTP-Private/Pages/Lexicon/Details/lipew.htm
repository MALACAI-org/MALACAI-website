lipew
/lipɔw/
 
vt.
for the water (eglipew, miglipew/inlipew, lipew, agent, eg><ø-) to flow over something else (eglipewan, inlipewan/miglipewan/piglipewan, lipewan, location, eg><an-)
Ka eglepew iyan ka egsalampew te minsan nekey ne nekeelet wey nakabantal te egbayaan din wey egpabulusan din. Minsan nekey ne egpakaweil, egpekeyimu kayi iling te etew, mananap, weyig wey duma pad.
Miglipew ka weyig te tulay su migpanamantaman migduu.
The water flowed over the bridge because the flood is great.
Miglipew a te alad su migtalukunan a te asu ne egkahat.
I jumped over the fence because a ferocious dog was running after me.
Miglipew rè te tinggew ka baled ku su lelepeti nahud.
The swamp-hen just ran over my trap because it is too low.
Eng. overflow; run over; jump over; go over top; flow over top; run over top
DDP: Flow (1.3.2.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
