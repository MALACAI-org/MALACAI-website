mula
/mula/
 
n.
offspring of a male donkey and a female horse
Ka seini ne ayam anak te lukes ne asnu wey te beyan ne kuddè. Egpekeiling ded seini te kuddè wey asnu. Kaayun ne mabatek seini wey kaayun degma ne neelin ne meitem, meebul, wey malahem.
Manì buwa te dakel ka mula te deisek ma perem ka asnu?
How come that mula is big when asnu is small?
Eng. mule
Tag. mole
Ceb. mula

Usage level: L
