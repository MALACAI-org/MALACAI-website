sumpit
/sumpit/
adv_rt.
“extremely”
 
adj.
Masumpit ka peg-ugpe ta kuntee, ware egkakeen ta.
Our living now is extremely bad, we have no food to eat.
very (usually with a negative connotation)
Eng. very bad; extremely bad; severe
