leddak
/lɔddak/
“frayed”
 
n.
frayed cloth or woven mat
Ka leddak iyan ka igngaran te minsan nekey ne egkareetan e wey mahaan e egkamewù iling te hinabel, kumbalè, ikam, wey duma pad ne kasangkapan ne egkamewù.
Iyan ku red iglibed igsaluub ka salual ku ne leddak su warè pad nahangu ka iyam ne salual ku.
The reason why I'll wear my old frayed pants again is because my new pants aren't dry yet.
Eng. frayed fabric; torn fabric; ripped fabric; fallen apart; old clothes; rotten
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for fabric (clothing or woven mats) (egkaleddak, naleddak, egkA><ø-) to become frayed
Ka egkaleddak iyan ka egkareetan e wey egkamewù e su amane e ne naluhey wey ke buyag e. Piru iyan dè egkaayunan kayi ka me hinabel, kumbalè, salual wey duma pad ne me kasangkapan ne kenè ne putew wey plastik.
Egkaleddak e ka kumbalè ne iglibed-libed ku igsaluub su warad iya duma ne igsaluub ku.
My clothes which I wear over and over again are getting frayed because I don’t have any others to wear.
Kenè niyud isaluub ka sepatus ku rue su egkaleddak e ne warè mem pa igbeli ku te iyam.
Don't wear my shoes because they're falling apart and I don’t have anything to buy new ones.
Eng. frayed; fall apart; decay; perish; wear out; become rotten; rot

Usage level: M
