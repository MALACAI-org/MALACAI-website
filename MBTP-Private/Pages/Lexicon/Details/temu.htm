temu
/tɔmu/
v_rt.
“meet”
 
1.
vt.
for someone (egtemu, migtemu/intemu, agent, eg><ø-) to meet someone else (egtemuwen, theme, eg><en-)
Mighipanew si Inè su egtemuwen din si Amè dutu te kabalansihan.
Syn. talabu
Eng. meet; intercept
Tag. magkíta
Ceb. magtagbò; magkità; tagbuon
Ilo.: agkíta
OCM: Social Relationships and Groups (571)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for two or more people (egpekegtemu, egpekeg-) to unintentionally meet togther somewhere (usually in a house)
Kenè ki egbayè diyè te pinanguglungan su egpekegtemu ki te me mangngayew.
Eng. meet together
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for two or more people (pegtemuwey, peg><ey-) to meet togther somewhere (usually in a house)
Kaaselem ki egpatemuwey due te krusing.
Eng. meet together
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
v.
for a piece of wood or somethingto join on to another piece (egtemuwan, migtemuwan, temuwan, eg><an-)
Egtemuwan ku pad ka hari ku ne iyam pad nekehinguma.
Eng. join
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
n.
(tinemuwan, <in>–an) the crack between two boards in the wall of a house
Wrd.Gloss: crack
Kenad e egkalekeban ka gumawan su nekeg-isù ka tinumuwan.
Cannot be locked the door because the join is crack.
Eng. crack; gap
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
