durabli
/dudabli/
 
n.
a common term used for fruit trees
Ka durabli iyan ka nalein-lein ne me prutas, iling te mangga, durian, tebuwe wey te langun dè iya ne me prutas. Pinamula seini wey keilangan ne egtangguwen wey layun dè egkametan eyew kenè egbenesan wey eyew egpamehas degma te masalig. Minsan degma ka me kayu ne kenè egpamehas ne igpamula egkaayunan te seini ne lalag.
Egdakelen ku te egpamula te durabli ne me kayu su eyew due egkasalsalihan ku ke egkabuyag ad e.
I will plant more fruit trees so that I have something to rely on if I'm getting old.
Eng. fruit tree; tree, fruit
DDP: Tree (1.5.1)
Cult. e.g. coffee, cacao, coconut, durian
