ngisi
/ŋisi/
v_rt.
“laugh”
 
1.
vi.
for someone (egngisi, migngisi/ingngisi, agent, eg><ø-) laugh at something (egngisiyan, migngisiyan/ingngisiyan/pigngisiyan, location, eg><an-)
Egngisi a ke egkasuman-suman ku ka guhuren ni Dyuwil ne kangisiyan.
I laugh when I remember the funny story of Dionel.
Eng. laugh (loud or weak laugh)
Tag. táwa
Ceb. katawa
Ilo.: katáwa
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Mouth (2.1.1.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
2.
v.
for two or more persons (egpangngingisi, migpangngingisi/impangngingisi, agent, eg><ø-paN-) to laugh and laugh with a joke or something (egpangngingisiyan, migpangngingisiyan/impangngingisiyan/pigpangngingisiyan, location, eg><an-paN-)
Egpangngingisi ka me etew.
The people laugh and laugh.
Eng. laugh and laugh
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Mouth (2.1.1.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(kangisiyan, kA><an-) a comic
Kangisiyan ka imbasa ku ne diblu.
The book that you've read was comic.
Eng. comic
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(kangisiyan, kA><an-) funny
Kangisiyan si Kunsing ne eg-uuranda.
It's funny when Kunsing chanting.
Eng. funny
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(kangisiyan, kA><an-) a comedy; a joke
Kangisiyan amana ka guhuren ni Rubin.
The story of Rubin is comedy.
Eng. comedy; joke; drama; funny story
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(kangisiyan, kA><an-) humorous; ludicrous; ridiculous
Eng. humorous; ludicrous; ridiculous; hilarious
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(pakapangngisi, pAkA-paN-) can make laugh
Pakapangisi sikandin kanta ne egwali-wali.
She can make us laugh when giving a joke.
Eng. can make lough
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
