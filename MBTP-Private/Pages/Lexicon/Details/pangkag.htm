pangkag
/paŋkag/
 
vt.
(egkapangkag, nakapangkag, egkA><ø-) heated (e.g., exposed in the sunlight.)
Ka napangkag iyan ka narampil te meinit. Minsan warè eggimuwen ne neinitan wey ke duen naa iya eggimuwen, egkaayunan te seini ne lalag. Ka maralem ne kaluwasan kayi iyan ka egbasuk te intiru ne aldew.
Migsagsahukul ka duma ne trabahanti ni Imung su manì kun te impeg-iling ded ka unag dan te namewuri man e ka duma wey warè amana napangkag te meinit.
Few workers of Imung are complaining why other workers are paid fair with them instead they came late and not really heated by the sun.
Eng. heated; exposed to the sun; work entire day
DDP: Sun (1.1.1)
