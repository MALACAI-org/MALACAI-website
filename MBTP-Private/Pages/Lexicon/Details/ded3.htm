ded3
/dɔd/
 
part.
for security
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se me tapik naan dè te me guhuren.
Iyan ded meupiya ka eg-addat ki te kultura eyew kenè ki egkalueran.
It is better that we respect the culture so that we wont be cursed.
Sikan ded ka impammitew nu ne simat.
There it is the needle you are searching for.
Data Qual: example doesn't seem to fit gloss. What is the meaning of 'ded' here?
Te seeye ded ne aldew ...
On that very day
Ne mig-insaan dan si Abraham[gl], “Hendei ka asawa nu ne si Sara?” Ne migtabak sikandin, “Duen ded te seled te balungbalung.”
And they asked Abraham,[gl] “Where is your(sg) spouse Sara?” He answered, “Just there inside the tent.”
