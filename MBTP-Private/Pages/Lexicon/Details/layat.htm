layat
/layat/
adj_rt.
“long/tall”
 
1.
adj.
(malayat, mA1-) to be long or tall (of a person, a tree, a nose, a piece of string, etc.)
Malised ka malibutut ke ne talagbubula su egkalugsek ki rè te me matangkawal.
It is hard to be a short player for we will be only rejected by the tall ones.
O. Syn. matangkawal
O. Syn. matawilek
Ant. malibutut
Ant. malepet
Eng. long; lanky; tall
