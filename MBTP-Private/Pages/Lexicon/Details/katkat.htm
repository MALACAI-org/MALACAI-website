katkat
/katkat/
 
vt.
for someoneto pull down or root up something, esp. a vine (egkatkaten, patient, eg><en-)
Ka katkat iyan ka eggunhun wey egganuy te minsan nekey ne migpangahapkap wey migkampet iling te kasilè, sayuti, tebesal, wey masalig ka duma. Egkeyimu seini te minsan hentew ne egpakaweil wey egpekeyimu iling te etew wey me mananap.
Inkatkat ney e ka kasilaan ney su warad e behas wey warad egkakeen ney.
We uprooted our sweet potato plants because it is not having fruits anymore and we have no longer food to eat.
Inkatkat e te amey ku ka sayuti ne migkapet te baley ney su nekeiling e te lulubung.
My father removed away the sayote vine that crippered over our house for it looks like a hunting house now.
Eng. remove; uproot; pull out; pull down
