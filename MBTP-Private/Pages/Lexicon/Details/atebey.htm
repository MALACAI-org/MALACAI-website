atebey
/ʔatɔbɔy/
n.
“female_sibling/cousin”
 
n.
female sibling or cousin of a male
Wrd.Gloss: sister
Gramm. term of reference term of address: mengaatabey
Si Memie wey si Aquino, tahasuled, piru atebey red ni Aquino si Memie.
Memie and Aquino are cousins, but Memie is still Aquino's female sibling.
Eng. female sibling; sister; cousin
Tag. kapatíd na babae; pinsan na babae
Ceb. igsuon; igagaw
Ilo.: kabsat; kasinsin
OCM: Kin Relationships (602)
DDP: Male, female (2.6.5), Woman (2.6.5.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
v.
for someone (eg-at-atebey, mig-at-atebey, in-at-atebey, eg><ø-rdpCVC-) to call sister or cousin at someone else (eg-at-atebayen, mig-at-atebey/in-at-atebey/pig-at-atebey, patient, eg><en-rdpCV-)
Si Margaret Hunt, eg-at-atebayen ni Datu Martin Dumacon.
Margaret Hunt is being called a sister by Datu Martin Dumacon.
Eng. sister
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
term_of_address.
(menge-atebey, menge-) term of address for someone in the relationship of atebey
Eng. sister; cousin
DDP: Male, female (2.6.5), Woman (2.6.5.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
term_of_address.
(tala-atebey, tala-) term of address for someone in the relationship of atebey if that person is also a first cousin
Si Aquino wey si Rose, talaatebey.
Aquino and Rose are siblings.
Eng. sisters; brothers; cousin; siblings
DDP: Male, female (2.6.5), Woman (2.6.5.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
