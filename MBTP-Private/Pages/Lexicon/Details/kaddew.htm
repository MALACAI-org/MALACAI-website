kaddew
/kaddɔw/
 
n.
undress in protest
Ka kaddew iyan ka paahi te malitan eyew kenad egpekeg-ulì te asawa rin ne igkeupii rin ne eg-engkeran e puun te masulug ne egpuunan. Seini ka kinamewuriyan ne eggimuwen din eyew kenad egkabalbalawan te me talewusey ka suman-suman din ne kenad iya egpakiglibed te asawa rin. Mahirap seini ne himu para te minsan hentew.
Eng. protest
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for a girl (egkaddew, migkaddew/ingkaddew, agent, eg><ø-) to undress in protest
Ka egkaddew iyan ka eggimuwen te sabeka ne malitan eyew kenad egpakalibed te asawa rin ne eg-engkeran din. Egluluung sikandin diyè te tangkaan te me etew wey ke diyè naa te tangkaan te me talewusey. Kenad egkaapul sika te me datù su sika ka patamanan ne eggimuwen te malitan emun ke kenad iya sikandin eglibed pad te asawa rin wey kenè degma ne malemu seini ne eggimuwen su amana egpekeyilew.
Nenekeunduk naan dè ka me datu te migkaddew e ka malitan ne egghusayen dan perem.
Tribal leaders just bowed down their heads when the woman undress which they wanted to solve.
Nakapallahuy si Ruki te migkaddew e ka malitan diyè te tangkaan te me datu.
Ruki ran when the the woman undressed in front of the tribal leaders.
Eng. undress in protest; put oneself to shame; final decision to divorce
DDP: Divorce (2.6.1.4)
