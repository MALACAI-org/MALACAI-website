tembil
/tɔmbil/
v_rt.
“place_beside”
 
vt.
for someone (egtembil, migtembil, agent, eg><ø-) to place something beside something
Wrd.Gloss: place_beside
Ka tembil ne lalag nekeg-iling te egtimulan, wey egpeyiluahan. Amana egkahamit seini diyè te kamet wey kè te talihebe naa.
Naapunan a ka egtembil te talihebè ku.
I spent the whole afternoon to wide up my little farm.
Kenè nud e tembili ka kamet ku su egpekeg-ahew ki.
Do not place beside my little farm because we might snatch each other.
Eng. place beside; beside
