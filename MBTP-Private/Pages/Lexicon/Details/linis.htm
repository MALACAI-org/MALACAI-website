linis
/linis/
“clean”
 
adj.
(malinis, mA1-) clean; clear; organised
Cult. of floor, people, clothes, front-yard
Ant. malindit
Eng. clean
OCM: Water and Thirst (271)
DDP: Be in water (1.3.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
adj.
(malinis, mA1-) peaceful
Eng. peaceful
Data Qual: Queram, Alex and Tano say this doesn't seem to be a right meaning.
Tag. kapayapaán
Ceb. kalinaw
Ilo.: tálna
OCM: Warfare (726)
DDP: Calm (3.4.1.2.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
vt.
for someone (eglinis, miglinis/inlinis, linis, actor, eg><ø-) to clean something (eglinisan, inlinisan/miglinisan/piglinisan, linisi, location, eg><an-) with something (iglinis, inlinis, instrument, ig><ø-)
Eng. clean; clearing; cleanse
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
v.
for something (egkalinis, nalinis, agent, egkA><ø-) to be cleaned (igkalinis/ingkalinis, reason, igkA><ø-)
Iyan igkalinis te seeg su intahuan te hendue te taru.
The reason that the floor is clean is that floor wax was put on it.
Eng. cleaned
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
v.
for something (egpakalinis, nakalinis, agent, egpAkA-) to be able to clean something, or to be a thing (igpakalinis/impakalinis, reason, igpAkA-) which is able to clean something
Eng. clean, can; clean, able; cleaning agent
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
