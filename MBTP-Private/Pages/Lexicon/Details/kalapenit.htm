kalapenit
/kalapɔnit/
 
1.
n.
Macroglossus_lagochilus_lagochilus
small long-tongued fruit bat
Ka seini ne ngalap iyan sika se hep-at se paa ne masalig ke egpanlayang wey ka derakel kayi pekeiling dè te kinemu.
Mania te warà a nikaniyu lalahi te kalapenit bes ka insere ta ganna, te kenè e ma mulè egkeen!
Why are you can't told me that our viand earlier was bat for I can't eat it.
Cult. 3 in. long long snout edible
O. Syn. talabagsè
O. Syn. katagbungan
O. Syn. kabeg
