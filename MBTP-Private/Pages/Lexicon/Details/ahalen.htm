ahalen
/ʔagalɔn/
 
n.
master
Ka ahalen iyan ka datu wey dakel se karatuen wey duè me suluhuanen wey uripen. Iyan man-e sika ka pangulu te sabeka ne punduk. Agalen degma ka lein ne ngaran kayi.
Migkahi ka Bibliya te keilangan ne kenè eg-apul te ahalen din minsan mareet se batasan.
The Bible says that the servant should not refuse his master even if he (the master) has a bad attitude.
Ka ahalen ku su si Hisus.
My sustainer is Jesus.
Eng. Master; sustainer; creator; Lord; boss; commander
