enni
/ʔɔnni/
det.
“NF(II)_PERS(pl)”
 
det.
[Non-Focus(II) person plural determiner]
Mariyù bes ka ugpaan enni Bilting su pila ne bubungan ka eglampayen nu ke eggendiyè ka te kandan.
The home place of Belting is very far because going there needs to cross few peaks of the mountain.

Eng. by; of
