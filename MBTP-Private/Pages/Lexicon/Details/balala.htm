balala
/balala/
 
1.
n.
Calamus_usitatus_var.
a medium-sized variety of rattan (balahen)
Amana pepeiti ka ubud te balala ne balahen.
A soft flesh of a rattan variety is very bitter.
Cult. Its fruit is called akeb-akeb.
Genr. balahen
Eng. rattan variety; cane variety
OCM: Flora (137)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
2.
n.
a kind of evil spirit busew that calls "balala' balala' during nighttime
Eng. evil spirit; spirit, evil; bad spirit
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
