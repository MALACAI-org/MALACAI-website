liwarè
/liwadɔʔ/
n.
“half”
 
1.
n.
half
Eng. half
Tag. kalahátì
Ceb. katungà; tungà
Ilo.: kagudua
OCM: Mathematics (803)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
2.
v.
for the night (egliwarè, migliwarè/inliwarè, theme, eg><ø-) to be in the middle or midst in the evening
Eng. midst in the evening; middle at night
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
3.
n.
(taliwarè, ta-) middle; center
Kenè ka hendiyè te taliwarè te saliluan su egkaabileng ka.
Don't go in the middle of the twisting water for you can be swallow in.
Syn. elat
Data Qual: Which one is pt. equidistant from all other points, i.e.,center and which one is pt.equidistant from both extremes, i.e., middle?
Eng. middle; center
Tag. gitnâ
Ceb. tungà
Ilo.: baét; tíngnga
OCM: Miscellaneous Structures (349), Weights and Measures (804)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for something (egkawarè, naliwarè, theme, egkA><ø-) to split in half; to divide into two equal portions
Eng. split; divide; halve
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something (egpekegliwarè, nekegkegliwarè, theme, egpekeg-) to split in half; to divide into two equal portions
Ke egpekegliwarè kad e ka eggilamun, ulì kad e su egsubal ad e degma.
If you half already for your weeding, come home and I'll take over.
Eng. split; divide; halve
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
