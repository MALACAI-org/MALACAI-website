utas
/ʔutas/
v_rt.
“complete”
 
1.
vt.
for someone (eg-utas, mig-utas/in-utas, agent, eg><ø-) to say something completely (eg-utasen, in-utas/mig-utas/pig-utas, theme, eg><en-)
Eng. say properly; say completely; say clearly; enunciate
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vt.
for someone (eg-utas, mig-utas/in-utas, agent, eg><ø-) to clarify something completely (eg-utasen, in-utas/mig-utas/pig-utas, theme, eg><en-)
Eng. clarify, to (completely)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
vt.
for someone (eg-utas, mig-utas/in-utas, agent, eg><ø-) to finish doing a task (eg-utasen, in-utas/mig-utas/pig-utas, theme, eg><en-) e.g., cutting something through with a knife, or to finish telling a story
Wrd.Gloss: finish
Miggendiyè si amè te kamet su eg-utas te kayu ne migbunsuran din te egtempeg.
Father went to the farm to finish cutting the tree that he started
Eng. finish; complete
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(meutas-utas) no doubts for saying something
Amana ka iya ne meutas-utas ne eglalag.
You dont have fear for saying your words.
Eng. no doubts; no hesitation; no fear
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
