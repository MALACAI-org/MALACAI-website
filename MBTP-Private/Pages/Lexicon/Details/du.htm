du
/du/
 
conj.
than
Ka du iyan ka lalag ne eggamiten eyew egpeg-ilingen ka daruwa. Igpataliwarè seini eyew egpahunsilingan ka sabeka.
Meupiya pad ka egkaayu-ayu ne due pegkaaldek te Manama du te meyaman ne makasesalè.
A poor (person) who has the fear of God is better than a rich (person) who is a sinner.
Eng. than; compared; rather; cf; over; more than; instead of
