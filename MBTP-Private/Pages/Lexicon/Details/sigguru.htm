sigguru
/siggudu/
adv.

adv.
sure
Wrd.Gloss: certain
Syn. malehet
Eng. sure; certain; exact
Online dict status: -

v.
for someone (egpanigguru, migpaniguru/impaniguru/pigpaniguru, agent, eg><ø-paN-) to make sure that something gets done (egpaniguruwen, paniguruwen, patient, eg><en-pA-)
Egpanigguru ne egsukut si Nina keddì.
Nina make sure that I can pay her.
Egpanigguru en iya si Butanleg ne egpekeyipanew kaaselem.
Butanleg did his best to make sure that he can leave tomorrow.
Eng. make sure; be certain; take care; work diligently
