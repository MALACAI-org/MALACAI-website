aplidu
/ʔaplidu/
Var.1: apilidu
n.
“surname”
 
n.
surname; family name
Wrd.Gloss: surname
Ka apilidu iyan ka ngaran te kaap-apuan wey seini ka ilaan te langun ne me etew te me karumaan dan. Ka langun due me apilidu minsan nekey ne kalasi te etew. Iyan dè dengan eggimuwen ne apilidu te me etew ka ngaran te amey ran piru kenad kuntee.
Ka apilidu ni Mimi su Man-umbuk.
Man-omboc is the family name of Memie.
Man-umbuk pad dengan ka apilidu ni Mimi piru Adyung e kuntee su nakaasawa rin e ki Birni Adyung.
Man-imbuk was Mimi's surname before but now it is Adyung for she got married with Birni Adyung.
Eng. surname; family name; name, surname
