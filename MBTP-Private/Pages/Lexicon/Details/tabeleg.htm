tabeleg
/tabɔlɔg/
 
n.
poison
Eng. poison
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for someone (egkatabeleg, natabeleg, actor, egkA><ø-) to become faint or dizzy
Ka egkatabeleg iyan ka egkatileng se ulu ne iling te maagkap wey mabehat tenged te eggeramen ne masakit te ulu. Kenè dè ne dalu ka egpuunan ne egmemabbehat se ulu te etew/mananap ke kenè minsan degma ka minsan nekey ne egkaarek iling te egngareg, wey egkeinum/egkakeen iling te egpekewubug ne inumen, wey egkaheram iling te meinit ne aldew wey maagsil ne kalamag.
Kenà a amana egpakaayad-ayad eggipanew su egkatabeleg a.
I can't walk well because I'm faint.
Eng. faint; dizzy; light-headed; weak (to feel dizzy)
Tag. hílo; lasíng
Ceb. malipong; labad; hubog
Ilo.: maúlaw; nabártek
OCM: Theory of Disease (753), Drinking Establishments (275), Alcoholic Beverage's (273)
DDP: Weak (2.4.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
