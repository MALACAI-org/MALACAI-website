untud2
/ʔuntud/
v_rt.
“ride”
 
v.
for someone (eg-untud, mig-untud, in-untud, agent, eg><ø-) to mount or ride (a vehicle or animal) (eg-unturan, mig-unturan/in-unturan/pig-unturan, location, eg><an-)
Egkeupian a ne eg-untud te kuddè ke eggendiyè te bubungan.
I wanted to ride a horse when I go to mountain.
Syn. pentug
Eng. ride; mount; board
Tag. sumakáy
Ceb. sakay
Ilo.: agsakáy
OCM: Domesticated Animals (231), Vehicles (493)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
n.
(unturanan, -an-an) a vehicle
Batsilur ka unturanan peendiyè te Dabaw wey ke peendiyè te Kagayan.
The vehicle to ride going to Davao or Cagayan is a Bachelor Bus.
Eng. vehicle; transport
Tag. sasakyán; behíkuló
Ceb. sakyanan
Ilo.: paglugánan
OCM: Vehicles (493)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for a person or animal (egpanguntud, migpanguntud/impanguntud/pigpanguntud, agent, eg><ø-paN-) to ride something (egpangunturan, migpangunturan/impangunturan/pigpangunturan, location, eg><an-paN-)
Eng. ride; mount; board
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
