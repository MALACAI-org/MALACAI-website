tukù
/tukuʔ/
v_rt.
“push_slowly”
 
vt.
for someone (egtukù, migtukù/intukù, agent, eg><ø-) to push slowly and firmly (egtukuen, intukù/migtukù/pigtukù, theme, eg><en-)
Ka egtukù iyan ka himu ne egturul te minsan nekey wey minsan hentew ne diyè te katangkaan, kakiliran ne egkeumaan te belad. Egkaayun ne eggamitan te minsan nekey ka pegtukù iling te eggen-gen te kayu ne egtuù te minsan nekey.
Kenà a egtukua su egkeulug a.
Don't push me because I will fall.
In-akalan ni Agusting si Dunyur wey intukù din diyè te tikeba.
Agusting betrayed Dunyur and pushed him over the cliff.
O. Syn. tuù
Eng. push slowly; jostle
Tag. túlak
Ilo.: túlak
OCM: Weight Moving (483)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone or something (egkatukù, natukù, agent & theme, egkA><ø-) to be pushed by someone or something else
Eng. pushed, to be
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
(egpatuktukuey, migpatuk-tukuey/impatuktukuey, egpA><ey-rdpCVC-) to wrestle each other (arms only)
Egpatuktukuey pad sikandan taman migpasuntukey e.
They wrestled first until they punched each other.
Eng. wrestle; fight
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
