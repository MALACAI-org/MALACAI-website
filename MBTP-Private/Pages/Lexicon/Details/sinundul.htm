sinundul
/sinundul/
v_rt.
“follow”
 
1.
v.
for someone (egsinundul, migsinundul/insinundul, agent, eg><ø-) to follow someone else
Wrd.Gloss: follow
Minsan egkamenu ne egsinundul a iya te Magbebayè.
Whatever happened I will follow the Lord.
Eng. follow
Tag. sunód
Ilo.: surut
OCM: Locomotion (481), Ethnopsychology (828), Ordering of Time (805), Techniques of Inculcation (861)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
v.
for someone or something (egpekegsinundul, nekegsinundul, actor & theme, egpekeg-) to have straight alignment or to be followed after one another
Eng. straight alignment, to have; followed after one another, to be
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egmegsinundul, migmegsinundul/immegsinundul, egpegsinundul, migpegsinundul/impegsinundul, agent, egmeg-, egpeg-) to arrange something in a straight alignment
Eng. arrange, to (in a straight alignment)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
