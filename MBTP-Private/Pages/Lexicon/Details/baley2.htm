baley2
/balɔy/
 
1.
v.
for children (egbaley-baley, migbaley-baley/imbaley-baley, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to play somewhere (egbalbalayen, imbaleybaley/migbaleybaley/pigbaleybaley, patient, eg><en-rdpCV-) with toys
Ka egbaley-baley iyan ka ngaran te kelag te me batè ne eggimu te me leeb wey deisek ne me baley. Me kayu wey kalan wey derewunen ka eggamiten dan wey minsan degma ka me hinabel wey me saku. Ka igpasabut man-e kayi iyan ka pegyangyang wey peglemet, pegdeeg-deeg, pegdereet wey peglalag te mareet diyè te duma wey diyè te Manama.
Cult. Stronger than sumpalit
Egbaleybaley ka me batè te limatek te weyig.
The children play a leech of water.
Eng. disobeying; blaspheme; insult; profane; make fun of; fun of, make; make light of; speak badly of; pretend
Online dict status: c
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egbaley-baley, migbaley-baley/imbaley-baley, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to disobey someone else (egbalbalayen, imbaleybaley/migbaleybaley/pigbaleybaley, patient, eg><en-rdpCV-) e.g., to make them funny
Egbaleybaley kew te etew.
You are disobeying people.
Eng. disobeying; blaspheme; insult; profane; make fun of; fun of, make
Online dict status: c
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egbaley-baley, migbaley-baley/imbaley-baley, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to blaspheme someone else (egbalbalayen, imbaleybaley/migbaleybaley/pigbaleybaley, patient, eg><en-rdpCV-)
Kahi te me etew, "Egbaley-baley buwa si Hisus te Manama te pegkahi rin ne anak sikandin te Manama?"

Eng. blaspheme; disobeying; insult; profane; make fun of; fun of, make
Online dict status: c
Usage level: M
