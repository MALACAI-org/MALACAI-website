labung
/labuŋ/
v_rt.
“supper”
 
vi.
for someone (eglabung, agent, eg><ø-) to eat the evening meal (iglabung, patient, ig-)
Wrd.Gloss: evening_meal
Eglabung ki, anggam.
Uncle, we will take supper.
Eng. evening meal; supper; dinner; meal; tea; eat supper
