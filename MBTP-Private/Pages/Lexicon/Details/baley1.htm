baley1
/balɔy/
n.
“house”
 
n.   
house
Wrd.Gloss: house
Meupiya ne egpitawen ka baley niyu.
Your(pl) house looks good.
Baley ka ngaran te eg-ugpaan te minsan hentew.
Called house the place where any body could live.
Eng. house
