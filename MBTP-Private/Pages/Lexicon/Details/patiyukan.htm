patiyukan
/patiukan/
n.
“bee”
 
n.
Apis_spp.
a type of honeybee
Wrd.Gloss: bee
Cult. This is a large species that makes a paper nest that hangs from tree branches.
cf. tabubuleg
cf. tameing
cf. sabungan
cf. mangguwawa —Thread-waisted wasp
cf. tabuuwan —Vespa wasp
Eng. honeybee; bee variety; insect variety
Tag. pukyutan; pukyót
Ceb. putyukan
Ilo.: oyukan
OCM: Fauna (136)
DDP: Insect (1.6.1.7)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
adj.
(pepatiyukan, rdpCV-) speckled/spotted (similar to the body of a honey bee)
Cult. Used of a white horse which was covered in many brownish spots.
Eng. speckled; spotted
Online dict status: -
Usage level: MT
