lukbù
/lukbʔ/
 
v.
(eglukbù, miglukbù, lukbù, eg><ø-) fell because of something (e.g on the way)
Ka lukbù ne lalag iyan igpasabut ka nakarugsù wey nakalangkeb. Kaayun ne iyan egpuunan ne nakalukbù su nasaligkat, nakariek te batu wey nakaluwak.
Nakalukbù man em ka kuddè su nakaluwak.
Cause to fell indeed the house because it's step on a hole.
Eng. fell; lie on stomach; trap; hit; stab; stable; impale
Ceb. nadagmà
