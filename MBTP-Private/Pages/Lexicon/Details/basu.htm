basu
/basu/
n.
“drinking_glass”
 
n.
drinking glass
Ka basu iyan sika se ubab wey ke eg-inuman.
Nahepang ka basu.
The glass was broken.
Mareet ka basu ne bildu su egkapesè.
Is not good a cup glass because it's broke.
Eng. drinking glass; glass; tumbler
Tag. báso
Ceb. báso
Ilo.: báso
OCM: Utensils (415)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
