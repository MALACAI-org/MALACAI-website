dahang
/dagaŋ/
Data Qual: QDL doesn't know this word
v_rt.
“trade”
 
v.
for someone (egderahang, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to barter or trade something (igdahang, ???, ig><ø-) with someone else (egdahangan, location, eg><an-) (e.g., to trade bananas for salt, tinned fish, or clothing)
Igdulung ku pad seini se igdahang ku diyè te anuhang ku ne malitan.
I will bring this things which I'm going to trade to my mother-in-law.
Syn. beligyè
Eng. barter; trade; swap; sell
Tag. magbilí
Ceb. baligyà
OCM: Buying and Selling (432)
Online dict status: c
Usage level: R
 
v.
for someone (egderahang, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to sell food (e.g. lahas nuts, banana, or meat or fish) (igderahang, ???, ig><ø-) to someone else wealthier (egderahangan, location, eg><an-) for much more than the actual value
Wrd.Gloss: sell
O. Syn. pangiyew
