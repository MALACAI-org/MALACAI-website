palanu
/palanu/
Fr. Eng. plan
 
1.
n.
plan
Nekey-a ka palanu kuntee ne eggimuwen?
What is your plan now to do?
Eng. plan
OCM: Ethnopsychology (828)
DDP: Vision, hallucination (2.5.6.6)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
2.
adj.
(mapalpalanu, mA1-rdpCVC-) wise
Penpenengan ne datù sikandin su mapalpalanu.
He is the greatest chieftain because he is wise.
Eng. wise; knowledgeable
Tag. pantás; pahám
Ceb. makinaadmánun; maalamun; manggialamun
Ilo.: masírib
OCM: Ethnopsychology (828)
DDP: Vision, hallucination (2.5.6.6)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
v.
for someone (egpalanu, migpalanu/impalanu, agent, eg><ø-) to scheme something; to think about something (egpalanuwen, impalanu/migpalanu/pigpalanu, patient, eg><en-)
Ke kenè ka egpalanu te egmangimuwen nu, kenè ka iya eglampus.
If you will not plan your decisions, you will not succeed.
Usi, nekey-a ka egpalanuwen nu due te egpeeneng-eneng?
Joe, what you are thingking about there - who is silent.
Eng. scheme; plan; consider; think about; meditate
DDP: Vision, hallucination (2.5.6.6), Think (3.2), Think about (3.2.1.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
adj.
(mapalanuwen, mA1–en) visionary
Mapalanuwen ne etew si Bubung.
Bubung is an visionary man.
Eng. visionary
DDP: Vision, hallucination (2.5.6.6)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
