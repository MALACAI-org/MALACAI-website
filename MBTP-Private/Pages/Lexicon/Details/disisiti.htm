disisiti
/disisiti/
num.
“seventeen”
 
num.
seventeen
Wrd.Gloss: seventeen
Disisiti e ne leg-un ka anak ku.
My son is already seventeen years old.
Disisiti dè iya ka seleppì ku guntee.
I have only seventeen pesos today.
Disisiti e ka idad ku sika ka keilangan ad e ne egpangasawa.
I am already seventeen years old that's why I need to marry.
Cult. Among the younger generation, Matigsalug numbers are rarely used for numbers over ten.
Syn. sapulù_wey_pitu
Eng. 17 (Spanish); seventeen (Spanish)
OCM: Numeration (802)
DDP: Cardinal numbers (8.1.1.1)

Usage level: L,B
