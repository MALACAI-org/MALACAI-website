belegyè
/bɔlɔgyɔʔ/
Var. beligyè
Var. beligyà
v_rt.
“sell”
 
vt.
for someone (egbelegyè, migbelegyè/imbelegyè, belegyè, agent, eg><ø-) to sell something (igbelegyè, ingbelegyè, ibelegyè, theme, ig><ø-) (for money)
Wrd.Gloss: sell
Nekey ka igbelegyè nu?
What are you going to sell?
Egbelegyà a te aheley diyè te tindaan.
I'm going to sell a corn in the market.
Belegyè ka te manuk su warè egkeenen ta.
Sold it because we don't have food to eat.
Syn. egderahang
Eng. sell
Tag. magbilí
Ceb. baligyà
Ilo.: agláko
OCM: Buying and Selling (432)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpamelegyè, impamelegyè/migpamelegyè, agent, eg><ø-paN-) to be a vendor of any products (igpamelegyè, impamelegyè, theme, ig-paN-)
Eng. vendor, to be a; sell as vendor
 
n.
vendor (talagpamelegyè, talag-paN-)
Eng. vendor; seller
