dayi
/dayi/
 
conj.
if
Ka dayi iyan ka lalag ne igsalambag wey ke igpewun-a eyew te pegbalibad wey pegtambag naa tenged te pegduwa-ruwa. Layun egkakita seini diyè te kewun-aan te nabaley ne lalag.
Dayi perem ke keddì sika su egbehayan ku iya sikeykew.
If it is mine then I will really give you some.
Dayi ke sikeykew ka mig-ulahing su egkabennalan ad iya.
I will be entertained if it is you who did the singing.
Kenà ad egduma kaniyu su marusilem e, dayi perem ke maaldew.
I will no longer come with you because it is dark already, if it is daytime it's alright.
Eng. when; if
Ceb. kung
Tag. kung
Ceb. gawas
