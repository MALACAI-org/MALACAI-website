hinges
/hiŋɔs/
“breath”
 
v.
for someone (egginges, migginges/ingginges, agent, eg><ø-) a breath (eggingesen, ingginges/migginges/pigginges, patient, eg><en-)
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se geyinawa te sabeka ne egkarineg ta wey minsan degma me mananap. Seini ka geyinawa ne egkarineg ta ne eglehes, wey hendue te neepuk.
Nakarineg a te hinges ne hendue te nabeley diyè te sinabeng ku gabii.
I heard like a tired breath in my room yesterday.
