rue
/duʔɔ/
“there”
 
adv.
that
Ka seini ne lalag iling te sika wey due ne layun egkagamit te egpalallalahey.
Awè ka rue.
Get out there.
Due ne lugal.
In that place.
Syn. due
Eng. there
Tag. diyan
Ceb. diha
