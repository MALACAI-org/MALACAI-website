suman2
/suman/
 
n.
rice cake
Ka suman iyan ka egbunguhan ne hemey ne egsewuhan te sukal, wey iyan igweyig ka ingkeles ne weyig te niyug ne ingkudkud. Egbunguhan naan e seini te dewun wey egsubbeen e taman te egkalutu. Malambu seini ne egkeenen su insewuhan te weyig te niyug ne nakeles e wey mananam amana. Emun ke egkalutu e, egluwiten ped ka dewun ne imbungug human egkakeen. Iyan e mateu ka eggilutù ke mamenu karakel ke suman ne eggimuwen din. Piru ka senge kakuwa, senge dangew rè iya ka kalayat wey hendue karakel te timbebakal.
Egkeupian ad iya ne egkeen te suman su egpakabulung wey mananam amana.
I really like to eat rice cake because it is filling and very delicious.
Eng. rice bake
Ceb. suman
