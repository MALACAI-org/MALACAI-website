batikù
/batikuʔ/
Fr. Ceb. liko
 
n.
curve
Ka batikù ne lalag egpuun te Biseyè ne ka igpasabut nekeg-iling te begkung. Ka seini ne lalag egkahamit dè te dalan. Kenè seini egkahamit ne igpeila te me purma te kasangkapan.
Warè nakabatikù ka terak ne migkarga te guma su kahiyen te driber ne kenè ne batikuen, sikan naa ka napeled.
Truck load with rubber was not able+ to turn because driver thought that it is not curvy so it's a cause of overturning.
Amana ne batikuen ka Overview ne inged sikan naa ka masulug ka egpakaleus ne terak.
Overview place is so curvy and so there are many trucks go through.
Eng. curve; deflect; turn
Ceb. liko
