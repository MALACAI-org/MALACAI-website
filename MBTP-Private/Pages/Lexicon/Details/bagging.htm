bagging
/bagiŋ/
 
1.
v.
for a person or animal (egkabaggingan, nabaggingan, experiencer, egkA><ø-) to be attracted to someone or something (igkabaggingi, ingkabaggingi, ikabaggingi, reason, igkA–i)
Kenè a egkabaggingan kandin.
I'm not attracted to him.
Warè ku ikabaggingi sikandin.
I'm not attracted to him.
Warè a nabaggingan kandin.
I was not attracted to him.
Eng. attracted; wanted
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for a person or animal (egkabagbaggingan, nabagbaggingan, agent, egkA><ø-rdpCVC-) to be strongly attracted to someone or something (igkabagbaggingi, ingkabagbaggingi, ikabagbaggingi, reason, igkA-rdpCVC–i)
Ka egkabagbaggingan iyan ka amana egkateltelesan te minsan hentew ne meupiya ne egtengtengan wey egkeupian ne egkaangey rin seeyè.
Amana ad iya egkabagbaggingan te malitan ne tetelesi ne egpitawen.
I am really attracted to a woman who is very nice to look at.
Eng. attracted, more; wanted, more; desired, more; lusted, more
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Like, love (3.4.1.1), Attract sexually (2.6.2.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for someone or something (egpakabagging, nakabagging, actor, egpAkA-) to attract a person or animal, or be the thing (igpakabagging/impakabagging, instrument, igpAkA-) which attracts a person or animal
Egpakabagging kandin ka egtuuhen ne kasilè.
Roasted sweet potato can attract him.
Igpakabagging kandin ka egtuuhen ne kasilè.
A thing which can attract him is roasted sweet potato.
Eng. attract; draw; attraction, be an
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
adj.
(mabagging, mA1-) desirous
Eng. desirous; attractive
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
2.
v.
for someone (egbagging-bagging, migbagging-bagging/imbagging-bagging, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to pretend to someone else (egbagging-baggingan, imbagging-baggingan/migbagging-baggingan/pigbagging-baggingan, location, eg><an-rdpRT-) to be excited about something
Eng. pretend; pretend excitement; feign excitement
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
