binteng
/bintɔŋ/
v_rt.
“pull_apart”
 
vt.
for someone (egbinteng, migbingteng/imbingteng, bingteng, agent, eg><ø-) to grab something to pull it apart (egbintengen, imbinteng/migbinteng/pigbinteng, bingtenga, theme, eg><en-)
Inbinteng dè ka asu ney te me asu te sumbaley su sabsabeka rin dè nahud ka egbuteng kandan.
Our dog was grabbed and stretched by the dogs of our neighbor because it was alone fighting with them.
Eng. grab; pull apart; stretch; elongate; tear apart
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for something (egkabinteng, nabingteng, theme, egkA><ø-) to become stretched
Wrd.Gloss: stretched
Eng. stretched; elongated; distended; torn apart
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
