prublima
/prublima/
Var.1: purublima
n.
“problem”
 
n.
problem
Nakangilit e rè te migginguma ka prublima ku.
I was just smiling when my problem came.
Wrd.Gloss: problem
Eng. problem; difficulty; situation; dilemma
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
v.
for someone or something (egkaprublimaan, egkaprublima:n/egkapurublimaan, actor, egkA><an-) to have a problem
Amana a egkaprublimaan ke eggingume e ka me mahaliyug ney su egkapulihid ki iya ka eg-iininglis.
I was very troubled when our visitors arrived because I was forced to speak English.
Eng. problem, have; troubled; concerned; anxious; worried
Data Qual: range of meaning
DDP: Worried (3.4.2.4.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
