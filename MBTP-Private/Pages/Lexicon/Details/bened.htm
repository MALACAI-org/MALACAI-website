bened
/bɔnɔd/
v_rt.
“go_numb”
 
v.
for part of the body (egkabebened, nabebened, agent, egkA><ø-rdpCV-) to go numb
Egkabebened ka paa ku ke mananey a egsikuwey.
My legs will go numb when I streched out my legs slowly.
Eng. numb; pins and needles
Tag. manhíd; waláng pakiramdám
Ceb. binhud
Ilo.: napipikel
OCM: Sensation and Perception (151)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something reason (egpakabebened, nakabebened, agent, egpAkA-) to cause some parts of the body dead
Eng. dead leg, to cause; numb, to cause
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
