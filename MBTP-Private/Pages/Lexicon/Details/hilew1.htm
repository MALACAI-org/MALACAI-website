hilew1
/hilɔw/
adj_rt.
“immature”
 
1.
adj.
(meyilew, mA1-) (for a plant product such as rattan) to be immature
Egkeibeg sikandin te meyilew ne mangga su egpangalalin sikandin.
She wanted to eat an unripe mango because she was pregnant
Eng. immature; green; unripe; raw?
Tag. hiláw
Ceb. hilaw
Ilo.: naáta
OCM: Ethnobotany (824)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
v.
for someone (egmemeyilew, migmemeyilew/immemeyilew, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-mA1-) to vent their anger by killing someone else who was not even involved
Eng. kill to vent anger; vent anger by killing
DDP: Kill (2.6.6.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
