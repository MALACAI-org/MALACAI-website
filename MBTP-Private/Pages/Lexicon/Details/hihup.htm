hihup
/higup/
Var.1: ihup
v_rt.
“suck”
 
vt.
for someone (eggihup, agent, eg><ø-) to sip a beverage or suck air out of something (eggihupen, theme, eg><en-) e.g., to drink soup out of a plate
Ka eggihup iyan ka egsupsup te minsan nekey ne egkeinum wey te kalamag eyew egkeem-em wey egkaabeleng naa.
Amana a egkeupian ne eggihup te sabew te manuk su mananam.
I want to sip a soup of a chicken because it's tasty.
Eggihup a perem te sabew ne neeletan a su impatamtaman benesili.
I wanted to sip a soup but I was blocked by because it was spicy hot.
Syn. supsup
Eng. sip; suck
Tag. sumipsíp; humígop
Ceb. súyup; maghigup
Ilo.: ígup
OCM: Water and Thirst (271)
