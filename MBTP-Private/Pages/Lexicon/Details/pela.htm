pela
/pɔla/
v_rt.
 
v.
for a person or animal (egpela, migpela/impela, agent, eg><ø-) to make someone or something tired (egpeleen, egpele:n, impela/migpela/pigpela, patient, eg><en-)
Eng. tired
DDP: Tired (2.4.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
vi.
for a person or animal (egkapelaan, egkapela:n, egkapeleyi, napelaan, napela:an, napeleyi, agent, egkA><an-) to become tired of doing or eating something
Ka egkapelaan iyan ka egkapere ne egtetahad te minsan nekey wey minsan hentew. Egkaayunan ka himu, etew, wey minsan nekey ne egtetahad wey egtetaharan.
Egkapelaan e kuntee si Bibing ne egtetahad keddì diyè te iskuylaan su mananey ka hunda ne mig-unturan ku.
Bibing got tired of waiting for me at school because the motorcycle I rode was very slow.
Eng. tired of; bored
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Uninterested, bored (3.4.1.4.6), Annoyed (3.4.2.3.1), Tired (2.4.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for a person or animal (egpakapela, nakapela, agent, egpAkA-) to cause someone to become fed up for a reason (igpakapela, impakapela, reason, igpAkA-)
Eng. make tired of; irritate; annoy
DDP: Uninterested, bored (3.4.1.4.6), Annoyed (3.4.2.3.1), Tired (2.4.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
