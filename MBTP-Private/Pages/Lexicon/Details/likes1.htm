likes1
/likɔs/
v_rt.
“wrap_around”
 
v.
for someone or something (like a snake) (eglikes, miglikes/inlikes, agent, eg><ø-) to wrap itself around something else (eglikesan, inlikesan, miglikesan, location, eg><an-)
Wrd.Gloss: wrap_around
Ka eglikes iyan ka egbelebed, egsalsalambid diyè te minsan nekey.
Eglikes ka dulepu ke egdawi.
The cobra snake will wrap around its prey to catch.
Eng. wrap around; encircle; enclose; entwine

DDP: Animal actions (1.6.4)
