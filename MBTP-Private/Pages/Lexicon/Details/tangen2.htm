tangen2
/taŋɔn/
 
v.
for someone (egtangen, agent, eg><ø-) to attach something
Ka seini ne lalag egpekeg-iling te taud ne lalag te Biseyè. Iyan igpasabut kayi ne egpegdumeen ka minsan nekey.
Tangen nu pad ubag ka yuggu su eggipanew ad.
Please set up the yoke because I'm about to leave.
Egtangen ka amey ku te lahitan kuntee.
My father is going to set up steel bar now.
Eng. set up; attach; affix; inset; insert; span; spanning
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(tangenan, -an) the cane (balahen) used to form the mouth of a fishing net
Warà a nakapanaklag su nahepù ka tangenan te saklag.
I was not able to go fishing using fishing net because the handle was broken.
Eng. handle
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
