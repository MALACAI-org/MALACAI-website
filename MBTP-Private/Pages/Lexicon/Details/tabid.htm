tabid
/tabid/
adv.
“always”
 
adv.
always
Ka tabid iyan ka layun dè eglibed-libed te eggimuwen din ne minsan nekey. Egkaayunan te seini ne lalag ka minsan nekey ne egpakaweil wey due geyinawa iling te etew wey mananap.
Tabid dè egbuyù si Bab te asin diyè te sumbaley rin.
Bob is always asking for salt from his neighbor(s).
Tabid dè egpamanihus ka hari ku ne kinaharian.
My youngest sibling is always taking a bath (or swim).
Eng. always; frequently; continually
