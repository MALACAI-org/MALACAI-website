ulibuwan
/ʔulibuan/
“place”
 
n.
for somethinga place which mostly olive trees grown
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se lugal ne masalig ka migtubù ne kayu ne ulibu, ka behas te ulibu meupiya ne egkeenen.
Kaaselem eghendiyè ki te Ulibuwan su egkuwa ki te behas te ulibu.
Tomorrow we will go to Ulibuwan to get some fruits of Olive trees.
Eng. place of olives; place
Ceb. olibo
