sewug
/sɔug/
v_rt.
“mix”
 
n.
ingredient; flavor
Eng. ingredient; flavor
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for someone (egsewug, migsewug/insewug, agent, eg><ø-) to flavor something (egsewuhan, insewuhan/migsewuhan/pigsewuhan, location, eg><an-) with onions etc. (igsewug, insewug, instrument, ig><ø-)
Egsewuhan ku te kuluring seini se humba ne inggilutù ku.
I will mixed coloring to my recipe.
Eng. mixed, to; blend, to; flavor, to; flavour, to; spice, to; infiltrate
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpegsewug, migpegsewug/impegsewug, agent, egpeg-) to mix together different objects; to scramble up (egpegsewuhen, impegsewug/migpegsewug/pigpegsewug, patient, egpeg-)
Egpegsewuhen kud e seini se sukà wey tuyu.
I will mix these venegar and soy sauce.
Eng. mix together; blended; scramble
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpegsewug, migpegsewug/impegsewug, agent, egpeg-) to mix up (of cards); to shuffle (egpegsewuhen, impegsewug/migpegsewug/pigpegsewug, patient, egpeg-)
Egpegsewuhen ku rè seini se me litaratu.
I will put together this pictures.
Eng. put together; shuffle
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone or something (egpekegsewug, nekegsewug, agent, egpekeg-) to become mixed together
Eng. mixed, to become
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for the group (egkasewug, nasewug, agent, egkA><ø-) to be mixed with another group (egpakasewug, nakasewug, theme, egpAkA-)
Eng. mixed, to be
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egsewug-sewug, migsewug-sewug/insewug-sewug, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to join with other group (egsewug-sewuhan, insewug-sewuhan/migsewug-sewuhan/pigsewug-sewuhan, patient, eg><an-rdpRT-)
