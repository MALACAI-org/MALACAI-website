Bibliya
/biblia/
n.
“Bible”
 
n.
The Bible
Wrd.Gloss: Bible
Data Qual: Diblu?
Tapey wey iyam ne kasabutan ka Bibliya.
The Bible is composed of the Old and New Testament.
Keilangan ne egbasa ki te Bibliya te tagsè aldew su eyew egpakaangey ki te meupiya ne pamatasan.
We must read a bible everyday so that we took some good attitude.
Eng. Bible; Scriptures
Tag. Bíbliyá; Banál na Kasulátan
Ilo.: Bibliya; Nasántuán nga kasurátan
OCM: Sacred Objects and Places (778)

Usage level: L
