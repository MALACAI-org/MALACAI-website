301

Sikeddi miggimu rin

He still working on me

LTA

Sikeddi miggimu rin
Ne nekeiling kandin
Ka langun bituen, aldew miggimu rin
Minsan pad ka bulan wey kalangitan
Dakel ka geyinawa rin
Te peggimu keddi.

1. Ka geyinawa ku ware pad neiyam
Egtahad pad te pegparani rin
Natahaan kud neg keyid-us kandin keddi
Layun e re egleuyan kandin.

2. Iling te alungan ka kandin ne lalag
Kakita kud kag keumaan ku
Natahaan kud neg keyid-us kandin keddi
Sumale te kandin ne pegbuut.
