15

Panugtuli a ki Hisus

Tell me the story of Jesus

Hymn

Eb (D)

4/4

1. Panugtuli a ki Hisus, ipasabut nu keddi
Ka mateles ne guhuren ne wareg pekeiling
Ka panalihan migkanta te peg-anak e kandin
Kahalaan diyet langit, keupianant tane.

Panugtuli a ki Hisus
Ipasabut nu keddi
Ka mateles ne guhuren
Ne wareg pekeiling.

2. Migpuasa sikandin, nakabaye pad man-e
Te me peg-eleg-eleg, ne himun Meibulan
Piru ware din nahaha, su maresen sikandin
Ka langun ne himu rin, para kanta langun.

3. Panugtuli a ki Hisus, ka namatey diyet krus
Sikandin iyak neuyag ligkat te kamatayen
Ka geyinawan Hisu Kristu, amana ne maresen
Panugtul tat langun inged, ne inluwas kin kandin.
