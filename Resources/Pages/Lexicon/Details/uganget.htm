uganget
/ʔugaŋɔt/
 
v.
for a person or animal (egpeuganget, migpeuganget/impeuganget, agent, egpA-) to wait someone or something patiently whatever happen (egpeugangetan, impeugangetan/migpeugangetan/pigpeugangetan, location, eg><an-pA-)
Impeuganget ad migtatahad kandin minsan marusilem e su warad iya iglabung ney.
I waited him patiently even the night came because we don't have food for dinner.
Eng. wait, to (patiently whatever happen)
