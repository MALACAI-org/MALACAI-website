bantug
/bantug/
n.
“fame”
 
adj.
(mabantug, mA1-) famous; well-known; prominent; great
Wrd.Gloss: famous
Mabantug si Datu Gawilan kayi te Simud tenged su meupiya sikandin ne pangulu.
Datu Gawilan is very well-known here in Simod because he was a good leader.
Mabantug ne etew si Lapu-lapu su migpakamatey sikandin tenged te inged din.
Lapu-lapu is a famous person because he died for his country.
cf. dengeg
Eng. famous; well-known; great; honored; honorable; prestigious; prominent; patriotic; infamous
Tag. bantóg
Ceb. ilado; bantugan
Ilo.: dayaw
OCM: Status, Role, and Prestige (554)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M

fame; prestige; honor; renown
Eng. fame; prestige; honor; greatness; renown

v.
for someone (egbantug, migbantug/imbantug, agent, eg><ø-) to speak well about someone or something (egbantuhen, imbantug/migbantug/pigbantug, patient, eg><en-)
Egbantuhen ne kudde ka kudde ney su mabulut.
They speak well about our horse because it's brave.
Eng. speak well about; honor; praise; boast (about saying something (e.g., proud of theirselves) and other thing which are not their own)
Online dict status: -

v.
for a person, animal or place (egkabantug, nabantug, mabantug, agent, egkA><ø-) to be famous or great for a reason (igkabantug, ikabantug, reason, igkA><ø-)
Egkabantug sikandan ne me etew su me bahani ma te inged.
They are great people because they are heroes of the world.
Iyan igkabantug dan su mateles ka me batasan dan.
The reason that they are famous it is because they have a good values.
Eng. great; famous; honorable; patriotic; honorable; prestigious; infamous
