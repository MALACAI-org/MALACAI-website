kehil
/kɔgil/
v_rt.
“turn_over”
 
v.
for someone or something (egkehil, migkehil/ingkehil, agent, eg><ø-) to feel something (e.g., thorn by touching) (egkehilen, ingkehil/migkehil/pigkehil, theme, eg><en-)
Eng. feel, to (touching)
DDP: Sense of touch (2.3.5)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something (egkakehil, nakehil, theme, egkA><ø-) to be detected by touching
Keyey! palanggè ku ne anak kenè ku egkakita ka duhi kayi te palad ku ne nakapakpak piru egkakehil ku, kaayun ne pitawan pa ubag dagdahawi?
Oh! my dear son I can't see the thorn burried in my palm but I feel it in touching, can you see it for a minute?
Eng. detected
