lenged
/lɔŋɔd/
v_rt.
“line_up”
 
n.
direction
Ka lenged migpasabut te neugpaan/natahuan te minsan nekey. Migpanengneng seini te nasasindehan te sabeka ne baley, inged, me limlimuan, me tuus/palinneu, wey te langun ne egkakita kayi te ampew te tanè.
Hendei lenged ka Dabaw?
Which (lit. Where) direction is Davao?
Eng. direction; beside; in line with; lined up with; aligned with; nearby; by
Tag. dako
OCM: Routes (487)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
(igpalenged, igpA-) to place alongside; to be a guarantee of a debt
Igpalenged ku rè ka seini ne kuddè te nasambayan ku keykew ne babuy.
I'll guarantee my horse for the pig that I borrowed from you.
Eng. place alongside; guarantee; collatoral
Ceb. tapal
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
