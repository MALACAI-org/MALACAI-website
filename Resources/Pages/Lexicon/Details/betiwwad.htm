betiwwad
/bɔtiwwad/
 
n.
leeches which are products of mosquitoes
Ka seini ne kalasiyan te limatek iyan sika se maag-sik-agsik ne egweil-weil diyè te diralem te weyig ne dereisek ne tantu.
Masalig ne betiwwad due te belun.
There's a lot of leeches in the well.
Eng. leech
Ceb. utsu-utsu
OCM: Fauna (136)
DDP: Spider (1.6.1.8)
