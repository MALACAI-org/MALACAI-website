gussen
/gussɔn/
 
adj.
(guhussen, rdpCV-) unkempt
Ka guhussen iya sika se pegbalew-balew te baley, wey ka peg-ayad-ayad naa te baley. Egkaayun degma ne iyan sika se pegdereet te baley.
Guhussen te pad ka baley ta su mareet ne egpitawen.
We will unkempt our house for it is not good.
Eng. unkempt
