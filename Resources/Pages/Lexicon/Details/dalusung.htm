dalusung
/dalusuŋ/
 
vt.
for someone (egdalusung, migdalusung/indalusung, dalusunga, agent, eg><ø-) to urge on someone else (egdalusungen, indalusung/migdalusung/pigdalusung, dalusungen, theme, eg><en-) to work or move faster
Wrd.Gloss: urge
Mareet ne egdumeen si Atu te egpanikep su egdalusung eg-uli.
It's not good to accompany Ato to catch fish because he will urge to go home.
Mareet ke egdalusungen ka eggimu su kenad egkaay-ayaran.
It's bad to pressure someone working because they can't do well.
Eng. encourage; urge; pressure; hurry along; drive; push; force; stress; scold
