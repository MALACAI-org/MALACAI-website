kuntee
/kuntɔʔɔ/
adv.
“now/today”
 
1.
adv.
now
Wrd.Gloss: now
Na, guntee kenè ad egbulig kaniyu minsan ke-nu.
And then, now I will not help you anymore.
Eng. now
Tag. ngayón
Ceb. karun
Ilo.: itá
OCM: Ordering of Time (805)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
2.
adv.
today
Wrd.Gloss: today
kuntee te maselem
today in the morning (this morning)
daruwa ne simana kuntee
two weeks from today (now)
Eng. today
Tag. ngayón
Ceb. karun
Ilo.: itá
OCM: Ordering of Time (805)
Idiom: kunteen pad —just now
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
3.
adv.
nowadays; at the present time; in this age
Wrd.Gloss: nowadays
Eng. nowadays; at the present time
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
