ma
/ma/
Var. man
(before dè, ded, pà, and pad)
Var. mem
part.
“DIM”
 
part.
[diminutive]
Wrd.Gloss: DIM
Lalag seini ne igtimul te me pangasak eyew egkeeleg ka igpasabut te eglalahen.
Eleg man dè iya degma.
That's also right.
Ne kahi ni Huwan, "Egderalu kun ma anggam gabii te marusilem".
And John said, "They say and it's true, uncle, that he got sick last night".
Katuliku man dè iya ka dangeb ne simbaan.
The other church is really just Catholic.
Warè mem pad sikandan kayi.
They still not here.
Eng. diminutive
