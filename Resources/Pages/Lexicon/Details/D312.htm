antayap
/ʔantayap/
 
v.
for someone (eg-antayap, mig-antayap/in-antayap, antayap, eg><ø-) to watch or to witness a place (eg-antayapen, in-antayap/mig-antayap/pig-antayap, antayapa, patient, eg><en-)
Ka seini ne lalag iling te migpaneyik ka te bubungan ne egpanlangulangu ka te inged.
Egpamaneyik a te Sumalili su eg-antayapen ku ka keupiya te dalepè ta ne inaranan.
I will climbing up to the top of mount Sumalili te witness the beauty of our mother land.
Eng. watch witness
DDP: Watch (2.3.1.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something (egkaantayap, naantayap, theme, egkA><ø-) to be seen clearly
Eng. seen clearly
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for a venue or a place (egpakaantayap, nakaantayap, theme, egpAkA-) to be able someone to see something around
Eng. able to see; see, able to
