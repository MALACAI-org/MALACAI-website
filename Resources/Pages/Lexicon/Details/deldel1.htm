deldel1
/dɔldɔl/
Var. delrel
v_rt.
“herd”
 
v.
for someone (egdeldel, agent, eg><ø-) to round up animals
Wrd.Gloss: round_up
Ka igpasabut te seini ne lalag iyan ka pegpaawè te minsan nekey; Egkaayun degma ne ka peg-abug te mananap puun te sabeka ne inged peendiyè te lein ne inged.
Malasi ku egdeldela ka babuy te sumbaley ney su egpanuwalen din ka me pinamula ku.
I always round up the pig of our neighbor for it used to root up our plants.
Syn. eg-abug
Eng. round up; drive out; herd; cast out; sent away

Usage level: R
