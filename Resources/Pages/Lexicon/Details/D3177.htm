krus
/krus/
Var.1: kurus
“cross”
Fr. Eng. cross
 
n.
cross
Amana baybayari si Hisus te me Hudiyu su impatiang pad man-e sikandin te krus minsan kenè din e egpekeeleg.
The Jews tormented Jesus cruelly because they caused him to carry the crus even he can't moved anymore.
Migpalansang si Hisus te krus eyew te pegbayad te me salè ta.
Eng. cross
