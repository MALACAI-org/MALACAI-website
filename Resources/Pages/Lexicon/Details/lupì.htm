lupì
/lupiʔ/
v_rt.
“fold”
 
1.
vt.
for someone (eglulupì, miglulupi/inlulupi, lulupi, egpanlupì, migpanlupì/impanlupi, panlupì, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-, eg><ø-paN-) to fold something (eglupien, inlupi/miglupi/piglupi, lupiac, patient, eg><en-)
Ant. bukayat —spread out
Eng. fold; double over
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for something, e.g., paper (egkalupì, nakalupi, egkA><ø-) to become folded
Egkalupì e ka me pepil ku.
My paper is to become folded.
Eng. folded; bend over
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
v.
(egluplupien, inluplupien/migluplupien/pigluplupien, luplupia, eg><en-rdpCVC-) multiply
Eng. multiply; increase
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
