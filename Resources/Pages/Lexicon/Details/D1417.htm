deeg2
/dɔʔɔg/
v_rt.
“show_contempt”
 
v.
for someone (egdeeg-deeg, migdeeg-deeg, indeeg-deeg/pigdeeg-deeg, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to show contempt for or oppress someone (egde-deehen, indeeg-deeg/migdeeg-deeg/pigdeeg-deeg, theme, eg><en-rdpCVC-)
Iyan seini ka pegtayu-tayu/pegleigleig te duma rin Egkaayun seini te minsan nekey ne due geyinawa iling te me ayam, me manuk-manuk, me etew, wey duma pad.
Masalig ka me dumahat ne egdeeg-deeg te me Matigsalug su deisek ma katuenan dan.
Many dumagat oppressed the Matigsalug people for they have only little knowledge.
cf. lemet
Cult. e.g. a mother hen who sits on her eggs but none hatch.
Eng. insult; ridicule; oppress; show contempt; contempt, show; unfortunate
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpandeeg-deeg, migpandeeg-deeg/impandeeg-deeg, agent, eg><ø-paN-rdpRT-) to keep on contempting or oppressing someone else
Eng. keep on contempting
