taheed
/tagɔʔɔd/
v_rt.
“delay”
 
adv.
while
Taheed te warè pad sikandin eg-inut-inut ki pad mulè ne eghilhilamun kayi te lama ta.
We will weeds the yard while she is not yet around (coming).
Eng. while; as long as; when; whenever
Ceb. samtang
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adv.
(tagtaheed, rdpCVC-) to relieve someone temporarily in a job; to stay temporarily in someone else's home
Kayi e pad egtagtaheed eg-ugpè te baley te amey ku su warè e pad baley ku.
I will stay temporarily at my father's house because I don't have yet house.
Eng. temporarily; relieve; stay
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egtaheed, migtaheed/inataheed/pigtaheed, agent, eg><ø-) to relieve someone temporarily in a job; to stay temporarily in someone else's home (egtaheeran, migtaheeran/intaheeran/pigtaheeran, location, eg><an-)
Egtaheed le pad due egbasa te warè ka meisturu niyu ne egtis kaniyu.
You just read while your teaher is not yet around to give an examination.
Eng. while; relieve; substitute temporarily; temporarily substitute; stay
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(taheeran, -an) a borrowed house
Wrd.Gloss: borrowed
Taheeran ku pad te eg-ugpè ka baley nu taheed te eg-eatep e pad te baley ku degma.
Syn. sambayan
Eng. rented; borrowed
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
n.
(egkatagtaheeran, katagtaheeran, natagtaheeran, kA<>an-rdpCVC-) spending leisure time by an enjoyable activity
Mareet seini se mig-ugpaan ta su warè egkatagtagheeran ta te tagse maapun.
Cult. e.g. playing basketball or reading a book
Syn. katagtaheeran
O. Syn. kabelbelangen
O. Syn. kapulunganan
Eng. filling in time; time, filling in; relaxation; leisure
DDP: Enjoy doing something (3.4.1.1.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for something (egpakataheed, nakataheed, pakataheed, agent, egpAkA-) which replace temporarily (igpakataheed, inpakataheed/ingpakataheed, reason, igpAkA-)
Eng. temporarily; shortly
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
(egkatagtaheed, katagtaheed, natagtaheed, egkA><ø-rdpCVC-) in a moment
Iyal kew due su egkatagtaheed kuntee ne egkaapun e.
Go faster! because in a moment will be afternoon.
Eng. later; in a moment; momentarily; soon; shortly
Tag. sa isáng sandalì; sa isáng iglap
Ceb. sa kalit
Ilo.: apagkanitú
OCM: Ordering of Time (805)
DDP: Soon (8.4.6.4.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(igtaheed, ig><ø-) a (type of) traditional contraceptive (to delay childbirth) taken in conjunction with a spiritual invocation
Wrd.Gloss: contraceptive
Igtaheed ku pad ig-atep te liyuan ka trapal ne imbeli ku para egdampilan ku te aheley.
Cult. taken as a drink lasts up to five years
Eng. contraceptive
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
