ulì2
/ʔuliʔ/
v_rt.
“heal”
 
v.
for a person or animal (egkeulian, neulian, agent, egkA><an-) to recover (from sickness) for the reason (igkeulii, ingkeulii, reason, egkA><i-)
Egkeulian e ka amey ku te bewù din.
My father is getting well from his cough.
Iyan din igkeulii su nekeinum sikandin te delemetan.
The reason that she healed because she took a herbal medicine.
Eng. recover; healed; improve; get better; better, get; get well; well, get; restored
Tag. magalíng
Ceb. naayo; naulian
Ilo.: naímbagán
OCM: Theory of Disease (753)
DDP: Recover from sickness (2.5.1.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
v.
for someone or something (egpekeulì, nekeulì, agent & theme, egpAkA-) to cure diseases effectively
Eng. effective
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for someone (egkeul-ulian, neul-ulian, agent, egkA><an-rdpCVC-) to almost recover from sick
Eng. recover
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
