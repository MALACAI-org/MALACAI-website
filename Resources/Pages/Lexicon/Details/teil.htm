teil
/tɔʔil/
v_rt.
“arrange”
 
1.
v.
for someone (egteil, migteil/inteil/pigteil, agent, eg><ø-) to stop moving around or to cease being angry (egteilan, migteilan/inteilan/pigteilan, location, eg><an-)
Eng. settle down; cease; stop; remain still; relax; rest; arrange; organize; organise; set up; assigned; negotiate; bargain; agree; decide on price; set price; price, set; amount, set; appoint; reserve, to; set aside
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (pekegteil, nekegteil, agent) to stop moving around or to be still
Ka pekegteil iyan ka egpatelen e wey warè igkabantali ne minsan nekey. Ka igpasabut man-e kayi ne naay-ayaran naan e ka keugpè kayi wey kenad egkaleugleug.
Egpekegteil e si Amè su warad igkangilam ne mangayew.
Father is now relaxed because no more enemy (murderer) to worry.
Mania te kenè egpakegteil si Anggam gabii?
Why Uncle was not still yesterday?
Eng. still; relax
DDP: Relaxed (3.4.1.2.1), Worried (3.4.2.4.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
v.
for a person or animal (egpanteil, migpanteil/impanteil, agent, eg><ø-paN-) to arrange a set of something (egpanteilen, patient, eg><en-paN-)
Eng. arrange, to ( a set of something)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something (egkateil, nateil, kateil, agent, egkA><ø-) to be in order or arrange (igkateil, ingkateil, reason, igkA><ø-)
Eng. in order; arrange; order, put in; tidy
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone or something (egpakateil, nakateil, pakateil, agent, egpAkA-) to be able to arrange something in order (igpakateil, impakateil, reason, igpAkA-)
Eng. arrange, able; in order, able
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
3.
adj.
(nateil, nA-) constant
Eng. constant; unchangeable
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
