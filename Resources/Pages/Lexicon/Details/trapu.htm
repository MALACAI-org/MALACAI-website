/trapu/
Var. trapu
Var.1: tarapu
Var.1: trapu
Data Qual: ph would be the same
Data Qual: If you know what word this came from (Ceb trapo), it can be added here before the ps field. Use dc field with trapo. (I forgot to mention that on the other three words, but it probably also applies.)
Data Qual: Please check va, vd1&vd2. [RAA 09Jan14]
Fr. Span. trapo
 
n.
cleaning rag
Ka trapu manggad ne kenad egkaayun ne igsaluub iling te kumbale, salual wey nekey pad. Igpunas seini te minsan nekey iling te basak, lapek, mummu wey duma ne egpakalindit te baley.
"Inay kenè nu pa himuwa ne trapu ka salual ku su meupiya pad,'' kahi ni Noel.
"Inay don't make my long/short pants a rug for it is still good," Noel said.
Migpanulu ka meistra te ALS ke egmenuwen te eggimu ka trapu.
Alternative Learning System (ALS) teacher taught how to make a rag.
Data Qual: Most readers won't know what ALS means (I don't). Write it in full in the translation line. (It's ok in the MS line.)
Eng. rag; cloth; discloth
Data Qual: It's good to add English synonyms in extra ie fields, so maybe add ie cloth, ie dishcloth
