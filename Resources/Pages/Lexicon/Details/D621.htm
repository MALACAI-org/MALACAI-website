baliyut
/baliut/
“lust”
 
n.
lust
Ka baliyut iyan ka egkas te minsan nekey ne egpakaanak wey eg-anak iling te etew wey te me mananap. Sabeka seini ne dalu tenged te subla ne pegpeyilabet wey pegpangilabet naa. Seini ka egtamanan te egkasublaan ne ibeg wey egkas.
Eng. lust; pervert
DDP: Lust (3.3.1.8)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
for someone (baliyuten, agent) sexually immoral
Ka baliyuten iyan ka minsan hentew ne amana egkeupian te duma rin minsan manggiasawè e. Eggelebek sikandin egpanlibug wey egpalibug tenged te batasan din ne egkeupian eggilabet wey egpeyilabet te minsan hentew. Ka me etew ne egtakuseng dè due egpangilabet wey eg-ealig minsan manggiasawè e, sika iya ka etew ne baliyuten. Minsan degma ka me mananap egkaayunan kayi ne lalag.
Amana ne baliyuten ka sumbaley ney su minsan hentew rè ka egpewuliren din kandin.
Our (woman) neighbor is very lustful because she lets anyone sleep with her.
Baliyuten buwad iya ne etew si Kaluy su tagsè eglalag ne lebut ded te malitan ka egngaranan.
Maybe Kaluy is really pervert because he will mention the bottom (idiom of sex organ) of the woman every time he utters.
Eng. pervert; lusty; immoral; sexually immoral; womaniser/maniser; philander
Ceb. bigaon
DDP: Lust (3.3.1.8), Sexual immorality (2.6.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
