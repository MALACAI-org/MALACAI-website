timpad
/timpad/
 
v.
to complete the number, size or amount
Ka egtimpad iyan ka egsampung te minsan nekey ne warè pad nasampung wey iling te seleppì, kamet wey kasuluhan te etew wey te minsan nekey.
Kenè nu rè amina su eyew egkatimpad ded ka keddì.
Don’t take it all so that mine (my money) will be complete.
Deisek naan dè iya perem ne egkatimpad e ka igbeli ku te kuddè.
Only small amount is needed and the money that I will buy for a horse will be complete.
Natimpad e ka kasulug te eg-untud su eyew egpekeyipanew kid e?
Is the number of people that will aboard is exact so that we can go now?
Eng. exact number; complete amount
