siyatung
/siatuŋ/
v_rt.
“run_humming”
 
v.
for children who have lost a competition (egsiyatung, agent, eg><ø-) to run away while humming. The humming is to prevent them from breathing in while they are running.
Egsiyatung ka me batè diyè te kelsada.
