lingkus
/ʔaliŋkus/
v_rt.
“circle”
 
v.
for a person or animal (eg-alingkus, mig-alingkus/in-alingkus, agent, eg><ø-) to go around at someone else or something (eg-alingkusen, in-alingkus/mig-alingkus/pig-alingkus, alingkusen, patient, eg><en-) somewhere (eg-alingkusan, in-alingkusan/mig-alingkusan/pig-alingkusan, alingkusan, location, eg><an-)
Eng. circle; around; go around
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
1.
v.
for someone (egpaalingkus, migpaalingkus/impaalingkus, paalingkus, agent, eg><ø-pA-) to cause something to circle around (egpaalingkusen, impaalingkus/migpaalingkus/pigpaalingkus, paalingkusi, patient, eg><an-)
Egpakaalingkus e te baley ka batè ne egkahaldek te kuddè ne egpabutengey.
The child go around the house for he afraid fighting horse.
Eng. circle; around; go around
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for something (egkaalingkus, naalingkus, agent, egkA><ø-) to be not reach or gone (igkaalingkus, ingkaalingkus, reason, egkA><ø-)
Eng. unreach; gone, not; missed
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for a person or animal (egpakaalingkus, nakaalingkus, agent, egpAkA-) to be able to go around something for a reason (igpakaalingkus, impakaalingkus, reason, igpAkA-)
Eng. indirectly
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
v.
for something, e.g., a star (eglingkus, miglingkus/inlingkus/piglingkus, agent, eg><ø-) to circle around someone or something (eglingkusan, miglingkusan/inlingkusan/piglingkusan, location, eg><an-) for a purpose (iglingkus, reason, ig><ø-)
Eglingkus ka dulepu.
Eng. circle; move in circle
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
3.
v.
for a number of people (eglingkus, agent, eg><ø-) to surround something, e.g. when trying to catch a wild pig
Eglingkus ka me etew ne egpanganup te mahintalunan.
Syn. libut
Eng. encircle; surround
Cult. This old word is now regarded as deep Matigsalug.
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
