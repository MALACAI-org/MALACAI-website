amut
/ʔamut/
v_rt.
“mix-in”
 
n.
for someone (eg-amut, mig-amut/in-amut, amut, agent, eg><ø-) to add something (eg-amutan, in-amutan/mig-amutan/pig-amutan, amutan, location, eg><an-) at something (ig-amut, in-amut, theme, ig><ø-)
Eg-amut ki te tag-singku para te kuk.
We add five pesos for coke.
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
n.
(amutan, -an) a semi-mandatory collection (eg. rice given for a community celebration)
Dakel ka amutan ney kuntee te egginguma ne pista.
We have a big obligation on this coming fiesta.
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for someone (eg-am-amutan, mig-am-amutan/in-am-amutan, agent, eg><an-rdpCVC-) to add or contribute something
Eg-am-amutan ta te egbeli ka ukaba ne igpakeen te babuy ta.
Let us contribute money to buy food to feed our pig.
Eng. contribute; add
Tag. magdagdág; pagsamáhin
Ceb. dugang
Ilo.: agnáyon
OCM: Mathematics (803)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for something (peg-amuten, theme, peg><en-) to put together (something) in a pile
Syn. tambù
Eng. put in a pile; pile up; mix together
Tag. magsalansán; magtalaksán (firewood)
Ceb. han-ay; tapok; liundok; kamada
OCM: Labor and Leisure (461)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
