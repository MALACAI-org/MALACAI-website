beuu
/bɔʔuʔu/
n.
“turtle”
 
n.
Testudinata_spp.
land turtle
Ke due eg-inse keykew, kenè ka lenep iling te beuu su mareet sika ne batasan.
When someone asked you, don't hide your face like the turtle for it is not good behavior.
Eng. land turtle; turtle
Tag. pagóngs; pahás
Ceb. baó
