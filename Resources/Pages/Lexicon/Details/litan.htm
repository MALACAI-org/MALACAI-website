litan
/litan/
v_rt.
“care_for_baby”
 
vt.
for someone (eglitan, miglitan/inlitan, litan, agent, eg><ø-) to care for a young baby (eglitanen, inlitan/miglitan/piglitan, litana, theme, eg><en-)
Eglitan a te batè su warè anak ney.
I will adopt a baby for we do not have children.
Syn. egtanggu
cf. eggaat
cf. eg-amuk
Eng. care for baby; look after baby; adopt
Ceb. atiman
DDP: Care for a baby (2.6.4.1.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(igkalitan, igkA-) the youngest in a family
Malitan ka igkalitan ney.
Our youngest was a girl.
Syn. tinangkeran
Eng. youngest
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpanlitan, migpanlitan/impanlitan, panlitan, agent, eg><ø-paN-) to give care at someone else
Eng. care, to give

Usage level: M
