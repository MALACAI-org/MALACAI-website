betad
/bɔtad/
 
n.
given away from man to woman's family (dote)
Seini se betad seeini ka igbehey te lukes ne egpangasawa diyè te ginikanan te malitan, me kumbalè naa wey ke keddè wey duma pad. Minsan ke egpakasale ka diyè te duma ne malitan ke eghusayen ka ka egkeulug nu ne me kasangkapan kaayun sika ne egkahiyen ne betad.
Te peg-asawa ku kandin ka betad ni Amè dutu su kuddè dè iya wey kalawit.
When I marry her my dad gave only horse and spear.
Eng. given away
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for the groom's relatives (egbetad, migbetad/imbetad, agent, eg><ø-) to give something (igbetad, imbetad, theme, ig><ø-) to the bride's parent (egbetaran, imbetaran/migbetaran/pigbetaran, location, eg><an-)
Eng. give, to (something)
