pahunwarè
/pahunwadɔʔ/
 
n.
deny
Eng. deny
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpahunwarè, agent, eg><ø-) to deny someone or something
Ka egpahunwarè iyan ka kenè pegpangguhud te malehet wey kenè peg-angken te neila rin wey natahaan din naa. Iggeles din seini emun ke eg-insaan sikandin wey egpehet-pehet ne egkene-kenè.
Mig-insaan a te amey ku ke nekeila a te malitan ne miggendiyè te baley ney piru migpahunwara a su kema ke eg-ehetan a nikandin.
My father asked me if I recognized the woman came to our house but I deny because I might be scolded by him.
Minsan se alukuy ku iya ka malitan piru impahunwarè ku su kema ke egpeengkeren a te amey ku te eg-iskuyla.
Even if the girl is my girlfriend but I denied her because my father will no longer send me to school.
Mania te impahunwara a nikeykew, warè bes geyinawa nu keddì?
Why did you deny me, don't you love me?
DDP: No, not (9.4.6.1)
Eng. deny; refuse to tell; lying
Ceb. molimod
Tag. magmaangan
