palinneu
/palinnɔʔu/
n.
“mark/sign”
 
1.
n.
a mark or sign which is evidence of something else
Behey ka te palinneu ke eg-ayun kid e.
Just give a sign when we are going to go.
Wrd.Gloss: mark; sign
cf. tuus
Eng. sign; signal; mark; evidence; proof; symbol
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
2.
n.
level (of a liquid)
Eng. level
OCM: Weights and Measures (804)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
3.
n.
score (of a game)
Palinneu te kelag
Score of a game
Eng. score
OCM: Games (524)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
4.
n.
marker (on a path)
Palinneu te igtaleu
marker that (I) place
Eng. marker
OCM: Weights and Measures (804)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
5.
n.
position (of a celestial body)
Eng. position
OCM: Ethnogeography (823)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
6.
v.
(egpalinneuwan, eg><an-) (for a celestial body) to reach a certain position
Egpalinneuan ka aldew siini.
The sun has reached here.
Eng. reach
OCM: Ethnogeography (823)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
n.
a full-stop
Eng. full-stop; period
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(palinneuwan, -an) a period; a full-stop
Eng. period; full-stop
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
