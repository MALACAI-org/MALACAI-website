iyas2
/ʔias/
???.
“repeatedly”
 
???.
for someone to have repeated asked for something (egbuyù).
Ka iyas iyan ka egpanamantaman egpehes/egbuyù/egpakannekal/wey masalig pad ka duma. Seini ka paahi te peggimu ne egdumaan te bagget/inniyat/kanekal eyew egkeyimu/egkaangken dè iya ka minsan nekey ne igkeupii piru kenè din iya egkakuwa.
Iyas e rè iya se egpanamantaman eglupug te salareng piru mariyù e dè iya engkeri.
I really ran as fast as I could after the deer but I was left far behind.
