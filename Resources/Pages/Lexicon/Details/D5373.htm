sabuk
/sabuk/
v_rt.
 
1.
v.
for someone (egsabuk, migsabuk/insabuk, agent, eg><ø-) to place something e.g. put aside a heavy back basket so have a rest (igsabuk, insabuk, theme, ig><ø-) somewhere (egsabukan, insabukan/migsabukan/pigsabukan, location, ig><an-)
Igsabuk te pad ka me liyang ta su eggimeley ki pad.
We will place our baskets for we need to take a rest.
Eng. place; put
Tag. maglagáy; ilagáy
Ceb. ibutang
Ilo.: ikábil
OCM: Weight Moving (483)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
v.
for someone (egsabuk, migsabuk/insabuk, agent, eg><ø-) to drop something (igsabuk, insabuk, theme, ig><ø-) somewhere (egsabukan, insabukan/migsabukan/pigsabukan, location, ig><an-)
Eng. drop, to
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
