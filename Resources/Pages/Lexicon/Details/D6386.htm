tamiluk
/tamiluk/
n.
“glue”
 
n.
it is a product of honey of a bees
Wrd.Gloss: glue
Ka seini iyan sika se diyè egpuun te baley te tameing, patiyukan, wey tamusan ne malamiket ke egkehilutu e, ne iyan eggamiten te me buyag dengan ne igtahù te bulabula ke tanud eyew kenè egkalubid ke igpaneyi.
Tamiluk bes ka migdeket te paa ku kahiyen ku ne limatek e.
I expect that leech was attached to my feet but its honey product.
Eng. honey product; glue; sticky
