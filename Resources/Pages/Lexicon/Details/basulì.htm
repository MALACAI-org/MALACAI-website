basulì
/basuliʔ/
 
vi.
for someone (egbasulì, migbasulì/imbasulì, eg><ø-) to return
Ka egbasulì iyan ka eglibed peendiyè te migpuunan wey ke egliberan ka minsan hentew ne inggimu rin.
Ka peggimu te mareet egbasulì te miggimu.
To do an evil doing will turn back to whose did it.
Migsumbung si Atu diyè te Femmatrics meyitenged te tanè din piru nabasulì dè iya sikandin su kenè bes iya ne kandin ne tanè.
Atu complained to the Femmatrics (Tribal Council) about his land but he felt sorry because he found out that he is not the true owner of the land.
Eng. strike back; disappoint; return to source; return where came from; turn back; blame; repent; regret; sorry; feel guilty
