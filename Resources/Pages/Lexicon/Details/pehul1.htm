pehul1
/pɔgul/
v_rt.
“be_lazy”
 
adj.
(pehulen, -en) lazy
Ka pehulen ne etew kenè eghimu, kenè eghilamun, kenè egkamet su iyan din dè eghimuwen se egpanakew te due kamuney ne manuk wey me kuddè.
Lazy people don't work, don't weed, don't clear fields because the only thing they do is to steal chooks and horses that have an owner.
Eng. lazy; idle
Tag. tamád
Ceb. tapulan
Ilo.: sadút
OCM: Norms (183)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
vi.
for something (egpehul, migpehul/impehul, egpakapehul, nakapehul, agent, eg><ø-, egpAkA-) that cause laziness to someone (egpehulen, impehul/migpehul, pehulen, patient, eg><en-)
Eng. laziness, cause
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something (igpakapehul/impakapehul, reason, igpAkA-)
Egpakapehul ma ka layun eglipereng.
Always sleeping can cause laziness.
Eng. laziness, cause
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for someone (egpapepehul, migpapepehul/impapepehul, egpA><ø-rdpCV-) to be avoid work
Kenè kew egpapepehul.
Don't avoid in working.
Masakit e ka lawa ku - Egpapepehul e pad mulè.
My body is painful - I'll avoid from work, I should.
Eng. avoid work; work, avoid; relax; rest
DDP: Relaxed (3.4.1.2.1), Rest (2.4.5)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(pegpehulen, rdpCVC–en) very lazy
Eng. very lazy
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
