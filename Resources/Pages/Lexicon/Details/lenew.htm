lenew
/lɔnɔw/
v_rt.
“be_afraid”
 
vi.
for someone (egkalenew, nalenew, actor, egkA><ø-) to be afraid of something dangerous (such as falling out of a tree), often the stomach will tighten in fear (igkalenew, ingkalenew, reason, igkA><ø-)
Ka egkalenew iyan ka egkaaldek tenged te matikang ne neugpaan iling te migpamaneyik te kayu wey mig-untud te kuddè wey duma pad, wey egbayè te due lunew wey egpantew te amana deralemi ne lungag wey bitu, wey masalig pad ka duma.
Egkalenew a ne eg-untud te kalabew su kema ke egkeulug a.
I'm scared to ride a carabao for I might be fallen.
O. Syn. kehè
Eng. afraid; scared; fearful; stomach tighten; heights, scared of
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Afraid (3.4.2.4)

Usage level: M
