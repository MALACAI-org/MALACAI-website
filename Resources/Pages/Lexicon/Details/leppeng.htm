v.
for two people (egpekegleppeng, nekegleppeng, agent, egpekeg-) to be the same height, age or position
Iyan seini ka daruwa ne etew ne malmalepet pad ka sabeka taman nekegsuwey sikandan wey te pegkakita ran e egpekegseked e sikandan. Egpekegseked e ka kalayaten wey ke egpekeg-iling e ka kalayaten. Kenè dè man-e ne meyitenged te etew su minsan degma nekey ne egkaayunan te seini ne lalag iling te pinamula, mananap, himu, wey ka suman-suman.
Naluhey kid iya mulè ka warè nekegkita su deisek ke pad dengan, ne kuntee egpekegleppeng kid e.
It's been a long time we didn't meet
