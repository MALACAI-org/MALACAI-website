saman
/saman/
v_rt.
“use_up”
 
v.
for someoneto not be able to use up their food (egkasamanan, egkA><an-) before it spoils
Kenè ney egkasamanan ka egkeenen ney. Egkaayun ne igbehey ney diyè te sumbaley ney ka egkeenen.
We would be able to use up the food (before it spoils). We could give the food to our neighbors.
