aldew te peggimeley
/ʔaldɔw_tɔ_pɔggimɔlɔy/
 
n.
the day to worship and to rest
Seini iyan sika se aldew te pegsimba te Manama wey kenè egkaayun ka pegtrabahu te seini ne aldew.
Eggimeley ki kaaselem su aldew ma aselem te peggimeley.
Tomorrow will be the day of rest therefore we should take a rest.
Eng. saturday
Ceb. sabadu
