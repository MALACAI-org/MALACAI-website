lehuy1
/lɔguy/
 
n.
the husband of a woman who is the first cousin of a male
Ka lehuy iyan ka duma ne lalag te uyang. Eggamiten seini te taleuyang te minsan hendei ne tapid. Piru iyan dè egkaayunan ne eggamitan te seini ne lalag ka me lukes wey kenè egkaayun ka me malitan su ipag ka eggamiten ke lukes wey malitan ka egpegngaraney, wey andey ke neelin ne malitan.
Amana ne maaliali si lehuy su minsan marusilem pad ne egpatinugpu e ka eggendiyè te kamet.
My cousin-in-law is very industrious because he woke up early at dawn and went to the farm.
Eng. cousin-in-law
