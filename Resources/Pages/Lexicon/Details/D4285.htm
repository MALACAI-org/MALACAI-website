mangguwawa
/maŋguawa/
 
n.
Sphecinae_spp,
thread-waisted wasp
Cult. When the tabuuwan fallen from the tree it will become mangguwawa which live in the land and form their hole as their shelter.
cf. tabuuwan
Eng. thread-waisted wasp; wasp variety
OCM: Fauna (136)
DDP: Insect (1.6.1.7)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: RT
