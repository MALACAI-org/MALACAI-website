behat
/bɔgat/
adj_rt.
“heavy”
 
1.
adj.
(mabehat, mA1-) heavy
Wrd.Gloss: heavy
Mabehat ka mata rin.
His eyes are close to shutting.
Ant. maagkap
Eng. heavy; weighty
Tag. mabigát
Ceb. bug-at
Tigwanon: begat
Binukid: begat
Sarangani: begat
Dibabawon: begat
Cotabato Manobo: begat
Tasaday: begat
WBM: beǥat
Ilianen: bahat
Cagayano: begʔat
Kinamigin: bugat
Tagbawa: beggɛt
Obo: beggat
Proto: begat
OCM: Weights and Measures (804)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for someone (egpalumbehat, migpalumbehat/impalumbehat, agent, egpaluN><ø-) to make themself heavy, e.g., when sitting on the knee of someone else or to put something to another thing to make it heavy (egpalumbehatan, impalumbehatan/migpalumbehatan/pigpalumbehatan, location, egpaluN><an-)
Egpalumbehatan ku te batu ka karas su eyew egpakataneb diyè te tanè.
Ant. egpaluagkap
Eng. heavy, be
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
2.
n.
(talimbehat, talim-) an anchor (for a boat) or fishing sinker (made of any heavy material)
Eng. anchor, a; sinker, a; weight, a
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
