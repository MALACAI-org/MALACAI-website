dakep
/dakɔp/
v_rt.
“catch”
 
vt.
for someone (egdakep, migdakep/indakep, agent, eg><ø-) to capture or arrest somebody or something (egdakepen, indakep/migdakep/pigdakep, theme, eg><en-)
Egdakep ka pulis te etew ne due salè.
The police will arrest the man who has sinned.
Eng. capture; arrest; restrain; catch; kidnap; abduct; handcuff
Tag. dakíp
Ilo.: tilíw
OCM: Hunting and Trapping (224), Social Control (626), Crime (674)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
vi.
for a guilty or criminal (egkarakep, narakep, actor, egkA><ø-) to be caught or arrested
Mani buwa te kenè egkarakep si Binuy ne nakapatey ma perem te etew?
How come Binoy is not arrested when he killed someone?
Eng. caught; arrest; capture
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
