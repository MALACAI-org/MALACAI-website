tebengenen
/tɔbɔŋɔnɔn/
 
n.
people
Ka tebengenen iyan ka lalag ne bayew maralem ka kaluwasan wey kenè amana eggamiten te me etew su iyan dè eggamit kayi ka me etew ne mangngulahing. Ka igpasabut kayi iyan ka masulug ne me etew wey ke keet-etawan kayi te inged.
Mig-ulahing ka ayè ku ne migkahi, ’Pammineg kew ka langun; nengneng kew ka tebengenen su due pangguhuren wey due panugtulen ku’.
My aunt sang saying, "Listen all of you; hear you people for I have a story and I have news."
Iyan meupiya ne eggimuwen te me upisyalis ka egbulig te keupianan te tebengenen.
The best thing that the politicians is to help for betterment of the people.
Eng. mankind; crowd; audience
Ceb. katawhan
Tag. sangkatauhan
