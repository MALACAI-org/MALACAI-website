sahikalè
/sagikalɔʔ/
n.
“chain”
 
n.
a chain
Hiketi nu te sahikalè ka asu nu eyew kenè egpakalekè.
You just tie up your dog with a chain so that it can't be let go.
Gramm. This is also regarded as deep Matigsalug.
Cult. Matigsalug poet using this term for chain, they are usually used this term during telling story about war of powerful spirit that no other thing can use for binding some spirit instead of using golden chain bulawan ne sahikale.
Syn. keddina
Eng. chain; wire link; link
Ceb. kodina
