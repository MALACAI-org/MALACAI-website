libusen
/libusɔn/
adj_rt.
“round”
 
v.
for someone (eglibusen, agent, eg><ø-) to make something (eglibusenen, patient, eg><en-) round using a tool (iglibusen, instrument, ig><ø-)
Eglibusen e pad te kasing ku.
I will make my top rounded.
Eng. round, make; round, to
OCM: Garment Care (296), Ethnophysics (822)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(malibusen, mA1-) round
Malibusen ka kalibutan iling te bula.
The earth is round like a ball.
Eng. round (adj); circular; spherical; ball-shaped
Tag. bilóg
Ceb. lingin
Ilo.: nagbukél
OCM: Garment Care (296), Representative Art (deprecated) (532), Ethnophysics (822)
DDP: Liquid (1.2.3.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
adj.
(maliblibusen, mA1-rdpCVC-) very round
Eng. round, very; circular, very
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something (egmalibusenen, patient, eg><en-mA1-) to be shaped like a circle or ball
Egmalibusenen ka peggimu te belun.
The method of making a well is for it to be shaped like a circle.
Eng. circle; encircle
OCM: Garment Care (296), Ethnophysics (822)
DDP: Liquid (1.2.3.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
