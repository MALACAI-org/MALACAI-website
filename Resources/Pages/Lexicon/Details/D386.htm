asungul
/ʔasuŋul/
v_rt.
“face_down”
 
vi.
for someone (eg-asungul, mig-asungul/in-asungul, agent, eg><ø-) to lie their head down on something (eg-asungulan, in-asungulan/mig-asungulan/pig-asungulan, location, eg><an-)
Eg-asungul rè iya si Diyandiyan ke egsuhuen te iney rin.
Diyandiyan will just lying down his head when he is commanded by his mother.
Syn. usengul
Syn. useng
Eng. face down; head down; lie head down
