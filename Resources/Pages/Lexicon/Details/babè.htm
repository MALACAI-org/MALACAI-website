babè
/babɔʔ/
prep_rt.
“downriver”
 
n.
(dibabè, di-) downriver
Wrd.Gloss: downriver
Ka dibabè iyan ka baliyung ne lalag te diraya. Ka diyè dapit te diralem wey ka egpabulusan te weyig.
Diya a egpamunuwit te dibabè su masulug ka egsagmak ne peit diyè.
I will go fishing downriver because there are a lot of fish there that bite (the hook).
Ant. diraya —upstream
Eng. downriver; downstream
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
