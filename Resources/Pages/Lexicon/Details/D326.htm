anuhang
/ʔanugaŋ/
n.
“parent-in-law”
 
n.
the term of reference for a parent-in-law
Wrd.Gloss: parent_in_law
Cult. The commonly used term of address is enug.
Ka anuhang iyan ka ngaran te iney wey amey te egpangasawa. Ne minsan degma ka me anggam wey aye te minsan hendei ne kalapihan, te lukes naa wey te malitan, seini red degma ka igngaran te alunggun.
Piding ka ngaran te anuhang ni Andi.
Piding is the name of Andi's father-in-law.
Eng. parent-in-law; father-in-law; mother-in-law
Tag. biyenán; biyanán
Ceb. ugangan
Ilo.: katugángan
OCM: Kinship Terminology (601)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
n.
(anuhangen, -en) a prospective parent-in-law
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for a son-in-law (egpanganuhang, migpanganuhang/impanganuhang, eg><ø-paN-) to work for his father-in-law after the wedding as a part of the bride price
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
