balisu-sù
/balisuʔsuʔ/
Morpho. from su-su
 
n.
whirlwind
Nahaldek a ka nakakita te balisu-su su amana ne makeseg.
I was afraid when I saw the whirlwind because it was so strong.
Eng. whirlwind; tornado
Tag. ipu-ípo
Ceb. alimpúlus
Ilo.: alipúg-pug
OCM: Ethnometeorology (821)
DDP: Wind (1.1.3.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for a person or animal (egbebalisusù, migbebalisusù/imbebalisusù, bebelisusu, agent & theme, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to imitate or move like a whirlwind (e.g. when dancing)
Ka amey ni Randy egbebalisusu ke egsayew.
Randy's father moves like a whirlwind when he is dancing.
Eng. whirlwind, move like (of people or animals); like whirlwind (of people of people or animals); spin, to (of people or animals); turn, to (of people of animals); move in a circle (of people or animals)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
