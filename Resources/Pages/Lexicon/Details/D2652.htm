kabakaba
/kabakaba/
 
n.
a large reddish type of cockroach
Ka seini ne kalasiyan ne ipes iyan sika se dakel wey amana managareg seini wey usig ne egdubpù te sulù.
Egkahaldek a te kabakaba su malimerang ne egpitawen
I'm scared a cockroach for it is creepy in sight.
cf. kelulung
Eng. cockroach
OCM: Fauna (136)
DDP: Insect (1.6.1.7)
