panseb
/pansɔb/
v_rt.
“worry”
 
v.
for someone (egpanseb, migpanseb/impanseb, agent, eg><ø-) to be apprehensive or doubt about things in the future (egpanseben, impanseb/migpanseb, patient, eg><en-)
Egpanseb a ke egdaralu ka hari kun malitan su warè din nekeulì te miglahad ne aldew.
I doubt that my sister is sick because she was not able to came last day.
Syn. agseb
Eng. doubt; apprehensive; worry; concern; fear; dread
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Afraid (3.4.2.4), Worried (3.4.2.4.1), Nervous (3.4.2.4.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpanseb, impanseb/migpanseb, agent, eg><ø-) to think or speculate
Wrd.Gloss: speculate
Egpanseb a ke nekeulì e ka hari kun malitan ligkat te Manila.
I think that my sister is already here from Manila.
Eng. think; speculate
DDP: Think (3.2), Think about (3.2.1.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for someone (egkapanseb, napanseb, actor, egkA><ø-) to be apprehensive about something (e.g. if cannot overhear a conversation and wonder what is being said)
Egkapanseb key e mania te warè dè due nekeulì ka hari kun malitan ne diyè te Manila.
We apprehensive already that why is our sister from Manila still not here.
Eng. apprehensive; concerned
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Worried (3.4.2.4.1), Nervous (3.4.2.4.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone's movement (egpakapanseb, nakapanseb, theme, egpAkA-) to be strange that someone expect something negative
Egpakapanseb ka me weil din.
His move is strange.
Eng. strange, to be (of movement)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
