tambag
/sambag/
v_rt.
 
v.
for someone (egtambag, agent, eg><ø-) to advise or encourage someone else (egtambahan, patient, eg><an-) to change their mind
Ka tambag iyan ka bulig ne pinaahi te pegbehey te meupiya ne lalag. Pinaahi seini te lalag dè ke nekey ka meupiya ne eggimuwen te etew ne due prublima wey igkalaggew.
Meupiya perem ke egtambag kew keddì.
It is better if you give me advise.
Egtambahan ku pad ka amey ku te kenad amana egpeinumen su ingkene pe ma kandin sika te talagbawi.
I will advise my father to minimize drinking wine for its not permitted for him by the doctor.
Egtambahan ku ka hari ku te kenè egpeung-ungelen te peg-iskuyla rin su eyew egpakapenga rin.
I will advise my brother no to do foolishness to his studies so that he can graduate.
Eng. advise; encourage; convince; persuade
Ceb. tambag
