sinelibulan
/sinɔlibulan/
 
n.
brass beetlenut
Ka sinelibulan iyan ka kepulan ne innimu ligkat te burunsi ne egpekeiling te nabaad ne bulan wey igbanggut diyè te liwaraan ne egtahuan ne mameen, manika, tabaku, apug, ibbas, wey duma pad.
Egnangenan ku si Ame te nalaag ka sinelibulan din eyew kenè sikandin egpakapammitew.
I will te Dad that his nut holder is lost so that he cannot keep on looking.
Eng. beetle nut; nut holder
