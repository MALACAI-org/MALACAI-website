kumbut
/kumbut/
 
n.
mosquito net
Ka seini iyan sika se kasangkapan eyew kenè ka egkakahat te higtek, mahalat seini ne ne igpamitin ka hep-at ne me sidsid kayi.
Masalig ka higtek diyè te baley ney sika ka egkeilangan key te kumbut.
Many mosquitoes there in our house that's why we need a mosquito net.
Syn. kulambu
Eng. mosquito net
Ceb. kulambo
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egkukumbut, migkukumbut, ingkukumbut, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to put mosquito net (egkukumbutan, migkukumbutan, ingkumbutan/pigkumbutan, patient, eg><an-) to someone (igkukumbut, instrument, ig><ø-) from avoiding mosquito
Egkukumbut sikandin su masalig ka higtek.
She is putting mosquito net because of many mosquitoes.
Egkukumbutan ku pad ka se anak ku su masalig ka higtek.
I should prepare a mosquito net for my child from mosquitoes.
Kenè nu ikukumbut ka daan su ka iyam gamita nu.
Use the new mosquito net instead of old one.
Eng. putting mosquito net; mosquito

Usage level: R
