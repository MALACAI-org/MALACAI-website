lehet1
/lɔgɔt/
adj_rt.
“true”
 
1.
adj.
(malehet, mA1-) true (to fact, logic or known truth); real
Ka malehet iyan ka lalag ne kenè ne ubat. Egkaayun ne seini ne egkakita wey se kenè, dakel wey ke deisek, egkahen-genan wey se kenè.
Malehet ne iglangeti kud iya ka etew ne hambuhen.
I really hated the boastful man.
Syn. eleg
Eng. true; real; genuine; truly; really
Tag. totoó; túnay; talagá
Ceb. tinuod; gayud
Ilo.: agpáyso
OCM: Norms (183), Logic (811)
DDP: Yes (9.4.6)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for something (egkamalehet, namalehet, theme, egkA><ø-) to become true or real
Eng. true; real; verified; confirm; proven
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(leleheti, rdpCV–i) truly; really
Eng. truly; really
DDP: Yes (9.4.6)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
adj.
(malehet, mA1-) right
Iyan sika ka lalag ne karumaan te eleg wey ke maheteng. Egkaayun sika te himu wey te lalag wey te suman-suman.
Malehet bu red ka intabak nu?
Is your answer right?
Eng. right; correct; proper; rightful
Tag. támà; tumpák
Ceb. husto
Ilo.: umno; hústo
OCM: Norms (183), Numeration (802)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
adj.
(leleheti, rdpCV–i) rightful;
Eng. rightful
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something (egkamalehet, namalehet, theme, egkA><ø-) to become right
Eng. right; correct; proper; rightful; true; real
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
3.
v.
for someone (egmemalehet, migmemalehet/immemalehet, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-mA1-) to prove
Ka egmemalehet iyan ka pegpehetpehet egpangguhud te guhuren ne warè nanengnengi wey ke eggubabeen wey ke guhuren ne warè amana naay-ayari te egpammineg. Egkaayun degma ne kenè egpehetpehet eg-insè ke eleg naa ka narineg din ne warè amana namulmulii.
Keilangan ne egmemalehet ka ke egpangguhud su eyew egpalintutuuwen ka iya te duma.
You need to prove if you are going to tell history so that somebody will believe.
Keilangan ne egmemalehet ki ne Manama si Hisus.
We should prove that Jesus is Lord.
Eng. testify; prove
Tag. maniwálà; manálig; manámpalatáya
Ceb. tuu
Ilo.: patién
OCM: Religious Experience (781), Ethnopsychology (828)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
adj.
(legleheti, rdpCVC–i) to make sure
Legleheti nu te egkahi si Mimi te kenà a egpakaruma kandin te eggendiyè te Panganan su kema ke egpaketetahad sikandin.
Make sure to tell Mimie that I can't go with her to Panganan, for her to didn't wait for me anymore.
Syn. pegpeheti
Eng. important
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for someone (eglehet-lehet, miglehet-lehet/inlehet-lehet, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to be sure to do something (egleglehetan, migleglehetan/inleglehetan/pigleglehetan, location, egkA><an-rdpCVC-)
Eglehet-lehet ki ne egpamula te aheley kuntee ne bulan su egpakasalemak kun te mahal.
We will make sure that we can plant a corn because it will be harvested with a high price.
Eng. sure; certain
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(kamalehetan, kA><an-mA1-) truth
Keilangan ne egdawaten ta ka kamalehetan ne me makasesalè ki.
We shoul accept the truth that we are sinners.
Eng. truth; reality; genuineness
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
adj.
(kinamalehetan, kina–an) absolute truth
Eng. absolute truth
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egmemalehet, migmemalehet/immemalehet, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-mA1-) to bear evidence
Nekey-a ka nakita nu ki Milin ne egmamalehet ne sikandin iya ka migtakew te kumbalè ku.
What have you observe to Melin that bears an evidence that she really stolen my clothe.
Wrd.Gloss: testify
Eng. bear evidence; testify
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for someone (egpamalehet, migpamalehet/impamalehet, agent, egpA><ø-mA1-) to confirm something
Sikeddì ka egpamalehet ne kenè iya ne si Pipu ka migtuleng te miyew te kepitan.
I am going to testify that Pipo isn't the one who striked the captain's cat.
Eng. confirm; prove; witness; testify
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adv.
(malehet_bes, mA1-) is that so
Malehet bes iya ne dakel se geyinawa ni Uked ki Lidilin su kenad e sikandin egkeibeg ne egkeen ka egsuman-suman kandin.
It's really true that Uked loves Ladylyn because he loses his appetite just thinking about her.
Eng. is that so; is that true; really?
Tag. talagá!
Ceb. tinuod?; diay ba?
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: MI
 
4.
n.
(iglehet, ig><ø-) something to tell someone
Eng. something
Online dict status: -

5.

Egkalelehet a ne egkebeng te batè.
I am forcefully to pinch of a child.

Eng. intensely; strongly (about eagerness); forcefully; forcibly; self-fulfilling; come true; true, become
