tabù
/tabuʔ/
 
n.
(tabuan, -an) a market
Ka tabuan iyan ka inged ne intagelan te aldew eyew eggimuwen ka sabeka peglibulung te me etew eyew eggale-gale, egpakaseleppi wey egpakabeli te minsan nekey ne egkabeli.
Eggendiyè ki te tabu te Basak su kema ke due keupianan diyè.
We will go to the market-gathering at Basak for there might be good things there.
Eng. market
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
v.
(egpanabù, eg><ø-paN-) to sell at a market
Egpanabu ki diyè te palingki su sebaddu kuntee.
Eng. sell
