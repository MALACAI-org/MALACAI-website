tabulug
/tabulug/
 
1.
v.
(tabulug) bald
Eng. bald
Tag. gumupít
DDP: Hair (2.1.5)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
v.
(tabuluhan, -an) a haircut
Eng. haircut
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
3.
v.
(egpatabulug, migpatabulug/inpatabulug, patabulug, eg><ø-pA-) to have one's hair cut

Eng. hair cut

v.
