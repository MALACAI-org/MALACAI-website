ayatanan
/ʔayatanan/
n.
“reason”
Morpho. ayat+-an+-an???
 
n.
reason
Wrd.Gloss: reason
Ka ayatanan iyan ka puunan mania te due neyitabu. Migpasabut seini te ligkatanan te samuk wey keupianan naa.
Ka pegpanumbaley ni Ayit kayi te kanami ka ayatanan te nekeg-ehet sikandan ki Aha.
Ayit's visitation (to us) is the reason why Aha and she had fought.
Eng. reason
