utel
/ʔutɔl/
v_rt.
“obtain”
 
1.
vt.
for someone (eggingutel, miggingutel/inggingutel, agent, eggiN><ø-) to catch something (e.g., animal) (eggingutelen, inggingutel/miggingutel/piggingutel, patient, eggiN><en-)
Ka kaluwasan te seini ne lalag iyan ka pegdakup te minsan nekey ne mananap. Kenè ne keilangan ke neuyag naa wey ke namakey seini.
Eggingutelen ku ka usa
I will catch the deer.
Eng. catch, to
OCM: Hunting and Trapping (224)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for something (egkeutel, neutel, egkA><ø-) to be caught
Usa rè iya ka neutel ku gabii su meiyal ne egpallahuy ka me mahintaluanan ne babuy.
Only deer I'd caught yesterday because the wild pigs runs fast.
Eng. caught, to be
OCM: Hunting and Trapping (224)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpekeutel, nekeutel, agent, egpAkA-) to be able to catch
Egdulungan ku rè sikaniyu te ngalap ke egpekeutel a te Usa kaaselem.
I will bring a meat for you tomorrow if I can catch a deer.
Eng. catch
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
v.
for someone (egpekeutel, nekeutel, agent, egpAkA-) to be able to afford something
Te kanakan pad ka amey ku, layun kun dè egpekeutel sikandin te usa.
When my father was still single, he can always slaughtered a deer.
Eng. obtain; able to afford
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
