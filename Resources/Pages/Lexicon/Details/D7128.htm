umbeg
/ʔumbɔg/
v_rt.
“be_crazy”
 
v.
for someone (egkeumbeg, neumbeg, actor, egkA><ø-) to be crazy; to be insane
Ka egkeumbeg iyan ke kenè amana egtulik wey amana man-e ne wahal. Bayew rè seini egpakasahid te egbusawen su kenè ne meupiya wey eleg para te kasuluhan ka eggimuwen din. Ka himu rin kenè egpekeiling te himu te kasuluhan. Egkaayunan kayi ka langun ne due suman-suman, geyinawa, wey egpakaweil iling te etew wey mananap.
Egkeumbeg ka sika ne etew su kenè egpammineg ke nekey ka igpeyimu te ahalen din su eg-uney-uney rè due te eggimuwen din.
That man is foolish because he won't listen to his master's instruction and do what he wanted to do.
Eng. crazy; insane; immature; foolish
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(umbeg-umbeg, rdpRT-) used to describe a person who is always changing their mind
cf. welu
cf. tilanduk
Eng. always changing
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (eg-umbeg-umbeg, mig-umbeg-umbeg/in-umbeg-umbeg/pig-umbeg-umbeg, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to be inconsistent in obeying or believing
Ka eg-umbeg-umbeg iyan ka egwahal-wahal wey eggimu te kenè ne meupiya piru egpakabehey te kahale wey kasakitan, keupianan wey karereetan.
Senge kakuwa eg-umbeg-umbeg si Babi sikan naa ka kenè amana egkapalintutuuwan ka egkahiyen din.
Babi sometimes does foolishness that is why his words are not really reliable.
Eng. inconsistent; unreliable
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
