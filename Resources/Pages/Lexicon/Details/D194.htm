alu1
/ʔalu/
 
v.
for someone (eg-alu, mig-alu/in-alu/pig-alu, agent, eg><ø-) to ladle something (eg-aluwen, aluwen, theme, eg><en-) at the plate (eg-aluwan, mig-aluwan/in-aluwan/pig-aluwan, location, eg><an-) with something (ig-alu, instrument, ig><ø-)
Eg-aluwen nud e ka binulalu due su eggutasan ad.
Go ahead and ladle the porridge because I'm already hungry.
Delia, hendei nu itahù ka ig-alu ku te keenen?
Delia, where did you put the scoop of the food?
Eng. ladle food; ladle out food; ladle; out
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpangalu, migpangalu/inpangalu/pigpangalu, eg><ø-paN-) to serve or ladle out the food or distributed (egpangaluwen, pangaluwen, patient, eg><en-paN-) at someone else or something (egpangaluwan, migpangaluwan/inpangaluwan/pigpangaluwan, location, eg><an-paN-)
Egpangalu e si ine te iglabung ney.
Morpho. ?g initial C
Syn. pangeun
Eng. divide; distribute; serve
Tag. maglingkód
OCM: Serfdom and Peonage (566), Priesthood (793)
