kisami
/kisami/
 
n.
ceiling
Ka kisami ne lalag iyan igpasabut ka ig-akep te atep. Biseyè seini ne linalahan su warè kisami te Matigsalug.
Meinit ka ugpaan ney, warè nahud kisamè.
Our home is hot, there is no ceiling roof.
Meupiya ka baley ne duè kisami su kenè ne meinit.
House which have ceiling roof is better because it is not heat.
Eng. ceiling
Ceb. kisamè
