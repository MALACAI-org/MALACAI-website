belad
/bɔlad/
n.
“arm”
 
1.
n.
arm
Wrd.Gloss: arm
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se taman te kemel pehendiyè te bakelawan.
Masakit ka belad ku su imbuu.
My arm is painful because it has boil on it.
Eng. arm; upper limb
Tag. bisig; braso
Ceb. bukton
Ilo.: takiág

Eng. use, to (hands)

the sleeve of a garment
