layam
/layam/
v_rt.
“familiar_with”
 
vt.
for someone (eglayam, miglayam/inlayam/piglayam, agent, eg><ø-) to tame something, especially a bird or animal (eglayamen, layamen, theme, eg><en-) somewhere (eglayaman, miglayaman/inlayaman/piglayaman, location, eg><an-)
Eng. tame
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for something (egkalayam, kalayam, nalayam, actor, egkA><ø-) to become used to something (igkalayam, inkalayam/ingkalyam, reason, igkA><ø-)
Cult. e.g. a horse to its owner
Nalayam e ka kuddè te anaken ku kandin.
Nalayam e ka amey ku ne eg-inum te kepi minsan meudtu.
My father used to drink coffee even noontime.
Eng. used to; accustomed; familiar; fond of; regard; tame
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpalayam, migpalayam/impalayam, agent, eg><ø-pA-) to adjust theirselves to be accustomed (e.g., climate) (egpalayaman, impalayaman/migpalayaman/pigpalayaman, location, eg><an-pA-)
Eng. adjust, to theirselves to be accustomed
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
(igkalayami, igkA–i) to be familiar with; to be accustomed to
Igkalayami e te me anaken ku ne eg-ugpè diyè te kanami.
Eng. familiar; accustomed
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
adj.
(nalayami, nA–i) traditional
Nalayami ku ne eg-untud-untud te kuddè tagsè aldew.
Eng. traditional
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
