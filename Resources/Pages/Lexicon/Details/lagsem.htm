lagsem
/lagsɔm/
adj_rt.
“bad_tasting”
 
adj.
(malagsem, mA1-) tasteless; distasteful
O. Syn. mananam
Nekey buwa mulè ne tirè a seini se malagsem.
What kind of sugarcane is this so tasteless.
Eng. tasteless; distasteful; bad tasting; awful taste; awful
Tag. matabáng; walang lása
Ceb. tab-ang; walay lamì
Ilo.: awán ti ramánna; natámnay; naláb-ay
OCM: Sensation and Perception (151)
DDP: Taste (2.3.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for the taste (egmalagsem, migmalagsem/immalagsem, theme, eg-mA1-) to become tasteless
Eng. tasteless, to become
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
