leg-un
/lɔgʔun/
n.
“year”
 
n.
a year
Iyan kaluwasan te seini ne lalag ka tuig wey ke sapulù wey daruwa ne bulan ka eglihad, wey ka tatelu ne gatus hen-em ne pulu wey lalimma (3650 ne aldew.
Tagse leg-un ne egsahekeen key te pegkeetawi ku.
Every year we celebrate my birthday.
Wrd.Gloss: year
Cult. The oldest Matigsalug term for measuring years appears to be kamet, lit. clearing a field which was done once annually. Only some of the older people use kamet. Many people use leg-un, although many nearer the highway use the Cebuano tuig or the variant tuid.
Syn. tuig
Eng. year
Ceb. tuig
