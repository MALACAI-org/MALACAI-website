tigkan
/tigkan/
v_rt.
“suddenly”
 
v.
for something (egtigkan-tigkan, egtigtigkanan, eg><ø-rdpRT-, eg><an-rdpCVC-) to be sudden or unexpected
Wrd.Gloss: sudden
O. Syn. halì
Bekara nu ka batu tigtigkani su kema ke due kumang diyè te diralem.
Warè key amana newulep gabii su natigtigkanan key te masulug ne me terak ne eglihad.
Eng. sudden; unexpected
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
(patigkanan, pA–an) to put off paying something until a later time
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for someone (egpatigkan, agent, eg><ø-pA-) to resume or carry on with something
Kenè ki pad egpamanihus kuntee su egpatigkan kid egpamanihus diyè te Luwey.
Eng. resume; carry on; continue
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
