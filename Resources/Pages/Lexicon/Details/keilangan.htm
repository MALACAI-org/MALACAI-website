keilangan
/kɔʔilaŋan/
v_rt.
“need”
 
n.
need
Wrd.Gloss: need
Egkeilangan a te pepil su egsusulat a.
I need a paper because I'm going to write.
Eng. need; necessity; requirement
Tag. kailángan
Ceb. kinahánglan
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vt.
for someone (egkeilangan, agent, eg><ø-) to need something (egkeilanganen, theme, eg><en-)
Egkeilanganen ku amana ka sepatus su egpalihawangen key te meisturu ke warè sepatus ney.
I really need the shoes because we will be sent out by our teacher if we don't have a shoes.
Eng. need; necessary; require; must; should
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
