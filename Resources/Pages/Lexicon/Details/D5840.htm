sikeykew
/sikɔykɔw/
Var. sikuna
 
pron.
you(sg)
Pangguhuran ku sikeykew mengeanak.
I will tell you a story, nephew.
Gramm. 2nd person singular focus pronoun (Set I, full form)
Eng. you sg (focus)
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Pronouns (9.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
 
pron.
as for you
Saatupa, sikeykew kag angey regma te kandin ne gimuku keureme.
Therefore, you will also be the one to bring his soul in the future...
Sikeykew e ka egsuman-suman.
You are the one who should decide (the price to pay for something).
Gramm. 2nd person singular topic pronoun
Eng. as for you (sg)(topic); you (sg)(topic)
Tag. ikaw
Ceb. ikaw
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Pronouns (9.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
