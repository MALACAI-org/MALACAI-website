lakulas
/lakulas/
v_rt.
“rub_on”
 
vt.
for someone (eglakulas, miglakulas/inlakulas/piglakulas, agent, eg><ø-) to rub something (eglakulasan, inlakulasan/miglakulasan/piglakulasan, location, eg><an-) onto something else (iglakulas, inlakulas, ilakulas, theme, ig><ø-)
Ka eglakulas iyan ka egpamunas te minsan nekey ne iglibed-libed iglukalis diyè te egpamunasan.
Diyè mulè eglelakulas si Tetuk te sinilas din dè diddiraya te egkalebeg e ka weyig.
Tetuk is rubbing his slipper upriver and it becomes cloudy.
Iglakulas ku pad ka bawì kayi te lawa ku su egdehes.
I will rub on the medicine into my body for it is scratchy.
Eglakulasan ku buwa te delemetan ka palì ku su eyew egkeulian e.
I will rub the medicine in my wound so that it will be healed.
Cult. e.g., to rub medicine onto someone's skin, or to rub oil into the outside of a pot so that the soot won't stick to it, or to wipe one's feet on a mat.
Eng. rub on; smear on; spread on; grease; apply
Tag. ipáhid; pahíran
Ceb. ihaplas; haplasan
Ilo.: isápsapu
OCM: Medical Therapy (757)
DDP: Oil (1.2.3.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
