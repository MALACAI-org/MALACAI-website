mandù
/manduʔ/
n.
“rule”
Fr. Ceb. mando
 
v.
for someone (egmandù, agent, eg><ø-) to instruct or teach someone (such as his children) (egmanduen, theme, eg><en-)
Ka igpasabut te seini ne lalag iyan sika se pegpanulù te me etew ke nekey meupiya ne eggimuwen, ka pegpaney-paney, ka pegsuhù, wey egkaayun degma ne balaud.
Meupiya ne egmandù te keet-etawan si Datu Lorenzo Gawilan.
Datu Lorenzo Gawilan rules the people well.
Eng. rule; govern; lead, to; instruct; teach; command
Ceb. mando

Usage level: M
