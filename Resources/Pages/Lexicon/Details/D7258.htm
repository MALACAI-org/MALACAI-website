wahal
/wagal/
 
adj.
mischievious
Wahal ke bes iya mulè su mania te immusengan nu ka ulaula te miyew?
Oh! you're so mischievious realy why did you blacken the the face of the cat with a charcoal?
Eng. mischievious; naughty

Usage level: M
