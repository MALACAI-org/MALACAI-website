sumbung
/sumbuŋ/
v_rt.
“report_crime”
 
vt.
for someone (egsumbung, migsumbung/insumbung, agent, eg><ø-) to report something bad (igsumbung, insumbung, instrument) to someone else (egsumbungan, insumbungan/migsumbungan/pigsumbungan, location, eg><an-) (expecting some consequent action)
Ka pulis ka egsumbungan te me etew ke egkatakawan sikandan.
The police are the ones the people report to if they are burgled.
Eng. report crime; notify of crime; inform of crime; tell on; pimp; telltale; tattletale; charge with crime; crime, charge with; bring case; case, bring; accuse
