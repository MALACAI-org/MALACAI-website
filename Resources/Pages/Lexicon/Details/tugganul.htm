tugganul
/tuganul/
 
v.
for a person or animal (egtugganul, migtugganul/intugganul, tugganul, agent, eg><ø-) to care or treat someone or something (egtuganulen, intuganul/migtuganul/pigtuganul, tuganula, patient, eg><en-)
Tuganula nu seini se libru te due peg-amping, su amana seini ne mahal.
Treat this book with care, because it was very expensive.
Eng. treat carefully; care, to
