Lihawasan
/ligawasan/
 
n.
a place beyond the edge of the sky and the earth
Egkeupian a ne eggendiyè te inged te Lihawasan su egtengteng a te me etew diyè.
I desired to go in Lihawasan for I want to see people lived there.
Cult. maybe people live here
OCM: Cosmology (772)
DDP: Sky (1.1)
