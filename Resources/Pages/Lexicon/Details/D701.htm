bantey
/bantɔy/
v_rt.
“watch”
Fr. Ceb. bantáy
 
1.
vt.
for someone (egbantey, migbantey/imbantey, agent, eg><ø-) to guard something (egbantayan, imbantayan/migbantayan/pigbantayan, location, eg><an-)
Kenè e pad egpakaruma kaniyu ka eggendiyè te bubungan su egbantey e pad te hari ku.
I can't go with you to the mountain for I watch over my youngest brother.
O. Syn. gaat
Eng. guard; watch over
Tag. bantáy
Ceb. bantáy
Ilo.: bantáy
OCM: Social Control (626), Child Care (855)
DDP: Look (2.3.1.1), Care for a baby (2.6.4.1.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
2.
vt.
for someone (egbantey, migbantey/imbantey, agent, eg><ø-) to take care of someone or something (egbantayan, imbantayan/migbantayan/pigbantayan, location, eg><an-)
Ka iney ku egbantey te me apù din.
My mother takes care of her grandchildren.
O. Syn. gaat
Eng. look after; take care of; care for; watch out for; control
Tag. bantáy
Ceb. bantáy
Ilo.: bantáy
OCM: Social Control (626), Child Care (855)
DDP: Look (2.3.1.1), Care for a baby (2.6.4.1.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
adj.
(mabantey, mA1-) careful
Mabantey ne etew si Daril.
Daril was a careful person.
Eng. careful
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
adj.
(talagbantey, talag-) watchman
Eng. watchman; security; caretaker
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
