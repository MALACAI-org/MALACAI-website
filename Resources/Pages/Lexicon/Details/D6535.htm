tedted
/tɔdtɔd/
v_rt.
“effective”
 
adj.
(manedted, maN-) effective (as in good medicine or a pastor in praying for the sick)
Wrd.Gloss: effective
Manedted ka bawì ne imbehey te misyunari keddì.
The medicine that the missionary gave me was effective.
Manedted ka paahi ni Dadut te mig-ealig te malitan su nakasuhut ka malitan.
The way of Dadut to approach the girl was effective because the girl accepted.
Manedted ka inlalag ku diyè te kepitan su nakapalintutuu keddì.
What I said to the captain was effective because he did what I asked.
O. Syn. mabegbeg
Eng. effective; potent; active; helpful; useful

Usage level: R
