hagpi
/hagpi/
n.
“protector”
Data Qual: Should the root be gagpi? What other forms of the root are used (e.g., imperative)? [RJH] XXX
 
n.
something placed under something else that protects what is underneath. For example, a thick blanket used to cover a horse's back so that heavy loads don't scrape off the skin; or a coaster placed under a cup.
Wrd.Gloss: protector
Eng. coaster; protector; horse blanket; blanket for horse; cushion; saddle
Ceb. hapin
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vt.
for someone (eggehagpi, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to cover something (eggagpiyan/eghagpiyan, inhagpiyan, mighagpiyan/pihagpiyan, location, eg><an-) with something (especially a cloth or blanket) (iggagpi, instrument, ig><ø-) for protection or for the comfort of the person who will sit on top
Ka hagpi iyan sika se igbelat diyè te minsan hendei ne keilangan ne eggagpiyan. Ka igpaanpew te minsan nekey wey egkaayun degma ne bunbun.
Eggehagpi a te egtahuan ku te aheley.
Eggagpiyan ku te saku ka egpabulukuan ku te miyew.
Beheyi e kun te pepil su iggagpi ku te egpinnuuwan ku ne nasubid.
Give me a piece of paper so that I could cover my wet chair.
cf. sagpeng —cover
Eng. cover; protect
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
