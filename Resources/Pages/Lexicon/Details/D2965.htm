kelekes
/kɔlɔkɔs/
v_rt.
“wrap_around”
 
v.
for something (e.g. a snake, or a vine) (egkelekes, migkelekes/inkelekes, kelekes, agent, eg><ø-) to wrap itself around something else (e.g. the snake's prey, a tree) (egkelekesan, inkelekesan/migkelekesan/pigkelekesan, kelekesi, location, eg><an-)
Wrd.Gloss: wrap_around
Ka egkelekes iyan ka peglambed te minsan nekey ne egpananap diyè te impamaneyikan din iling te uled ne eglambed te kayu ne impamaneyikan din.
Egkelekes e degma ka baliti ka lawaan.
The baliti tree also wrapped around the lawaan tree.
