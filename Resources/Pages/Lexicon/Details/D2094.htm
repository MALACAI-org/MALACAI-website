gutas
/gutas/
v_rt.
“hungry”
 
n.
hunger
Wrd.Gloss: hunger
Ka gutas iyan sika se banlus, bitil wey egkaayun degma ne sika se timpu ne warè egkakeen.
Amana man-e mulè ne gutas seini.
This was a great hunger.
Eng. hunger
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for a person or animal (eggutasan, miggutasan/inggutasan/piggutasan, agent, eg><an-) to be hungry
Eggutasan ad e.
I'm already hungry.
Piggutasan ad e ka egpinpinuu kayi te baley minsan warè himu ku.
I was hungry sitting in our house eventhough I am workless.
Syn. banlus
Eng. hunger; hungry; starve
Tag. gutóm
Ceb. gutom
Ilo.: mabisiín
OCM: Sensation and Perception (151), Gratification and Control of Hunger (261)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
