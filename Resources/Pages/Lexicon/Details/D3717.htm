lengguk
/lɔŋguk/
 
v.
for a person or animal (eglengguken, eg><en-) to snore
Eglengguken a iya ne eglipereng ke egkabeley te maaldew.
If I tired of a daytime I will be snored when I sleep.
Eng. snore
Tag. hilík
Ceb. hágok
Ilo.: urók
OCM: Sleeping (513), Ethnophysiology (827)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for a person or animal (egpalelengguk, egpA-rdpCV-) to snore pretending
Egpalelengguk ubag ka hari ku eyew kahiyen ne nakalipereng.
My younger brother pretend to snored so that somebody will say he is asleep.
Eng. snore pretending; pretend; purr
OCM: Ethnozoology (825)
DDP: Animal sounds (1.6.4.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
