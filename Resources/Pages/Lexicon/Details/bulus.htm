bulus
/bulus/
v_rt.
“continue”
 
1.
v.
for someone (egbulus, migbulus/imbulus, egpabulus, migbulus/imbulus, agent, eg><ø-, eg><ø-pA-) to continue (doing a task) (egbulusan, imbulusan/migbulusan/pigbulusan, egpabulusan, impabulusan/migpabulusan/pigpabulusan, location, eg><an-, eg><an-pA-)
Minsan nenabeley kid e egpabulus ki pad te himuwen ta.
Eventhough we were tired we will continue our work.
Gramm. It's not frequent to hear this root used without a pA- but it can occur. However, I am still tempted to list pabulus as a lexeme.
Eng. continue; go on; carry on; keep on
OCM: Labor and Leisure (461), Travel (484)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
v.
for someone (egpakabulus, nakabulus, actor, egpAkA-) to continue
Warà a nakabulus ne egdaru diyè te ki Evelyn su migderalu a.
I wasn't able to continue plowing for Evelyn because I was sick.
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
v.
for someone or something (egpabulus-bulus, migpabulus-bulus/impabulus-bulus, agent & theme, eg><ø-pA-rdpRT-) to keep on doing something
Eng. keep on doing
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(mapabulus, mA1-pA-) sustainable
Eng. sustainable
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
v.
for someone (egpakapabulus-bulus, nakapabulus-bulus, agent, egpAkA-rdpRT-) to waste their time for doing unpredictable useless thing
Eng. waste, to (about time or chance)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
3.
adj.
(kapabulusan, kA><an-) result
Syn. kapangkusan
Eng. result
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
4.
v.
(ipabulus, ipA-) to continue (doing a task)
Ipabulus nu.
Continue with it.
Igpabulus ta.
We'll carry on with it.
cf. tumpis
Eng. carry on; keep doing; continue; go on
Tag. sigé; magtulóy
Ceb. hala sigi
OCM: Labor and Leisure (461)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
adj.
continuous
Ka igkeupii te kepitan ne isip din, kenè buwa ayu egkabulusan.
The (barrio) captains desire that he's been thinking, won't perhaps continue.
Eng. continuous
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
5.
n.
(igpabulus, igpA-) the fast forward control (on a tape recorder)
Eng. fast forward
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
6.
n.
(igpabulus, igpA-) destination
Hendei ke igpabulus nu?
Where's your destination?
Eng. destination; going
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
