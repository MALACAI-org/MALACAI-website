kaniyu
/kaniu/
pron.
“2p_NF(III)”
 
pron.
you (pl); to you(pl)
Kaniyu se me batè palintutuu kew ke nekey ka iglalag te me amey niyu su egpekemeupiya seini kaniyu.
All you children, follow what your father tells you, because to your father's advice because it will be good for you.
Ayad-ayad kew rè te peggipanew niyu su eyew warè mareet ne egginguma kaniyu.
Be careful on your journey so that nothing bad will happen to you.
Wrd.Gloss: you(pl); to_you(pl)
Gramm. 2nd person plural non-focus pronoun (Set III)
Eng. you (pl) (non-focus III)
Tag. kayó; inyó
Ceb. kamo; inyo
Binukid: inyu
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Pronouns (9.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
 
pron_COMPL.
(kaniyud, -d) you (pl); to you(pl) (with completive aspect)
Gramm. 2nd person plural non-focus pronoun (Set III) with completive aspect
Eng. you(plural)(non-focus III), with completive aspect
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
poss_pron.
your (pl)
Wrd.Gloss: your(pl)
Egpanumbaley a su eyew egpammineg a te kaniyu ne lalag.
I'm visiting so that I can listen to your speech.
Gramm. 2nd person plural possessive pronominal specifier (prepositional)
Eng. your (pl)
Tag. inyó
Ceb. inyo
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Pronouns (9.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
 
poss_pron.
yours (pl)
Kenè kew ubal te radyu ne kenè ne kaniyu su kema ke egpakarereet kew.
Don't play with the radio which is not yours for you might damage it.
Gramm. 2nd person plural possessive pronoun
Eng. yours(pl)
