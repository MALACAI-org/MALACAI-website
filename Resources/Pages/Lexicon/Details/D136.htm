alambri
/ʔalambri/
Var.1: alambi
n.
“wire”
 
n.
non-electrical? wire; string of the salurey
Eg-alaren ku ka baley ku te alambi su eyew warè egpakaseled ne me ayam.
I will fencing my house with a wire so that there will be no animal can enter.
Eng. wire; string
Tag. alambre
Ceb. kawad
Ilo.: barot
OCM: Miscellaneous Hardware (414)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
