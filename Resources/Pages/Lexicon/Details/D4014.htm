lugsing
/lugsiŋ/
 
vi.
for something, e.g., a spear (deldeg) or a nail (lansang) (egkalugsing, actor, egkA><ø-) to be bluntened, e.g., by hitting a rock or a piece of steel (egpakalugsing, agent, egpAkA-)
Eng. bluntened
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
(panlugsing, paN-) to clean teeth using pigs' hair tied around a cloth
Tahari a pa nikaniyu sug panlugsing a pad.
Eng. clean teeth
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
