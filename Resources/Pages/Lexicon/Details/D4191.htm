mahaliyug
/magaliug/
n.
“forecloser”
 
n.
a visitor from a long distance; an outsider
Cult. living here in order to foreclose a debt
Eng. visitor; outsider; forecloser; foreigner; guest
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for outsiders (magmahaliyug, rdpCVC-) to come to forclose
Ka mahaliyug iyan sika se me etew ne migpuun te diyè te lein ne inged wey ke lapu. Egkaayun degma ne ka migpanumbaley ne me etew diyè te karumaan dan.
Egmagmahaliyug e pad diyè te suled ku su naluhey key e ne warè nekegkita.
Eng. forclose
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
