bagget
/baggɔt/
Var. bahet
v_rt.
“look_forward_to”
 
n.
excitement; encouragement
Warè bagget ku te minsan nekey.
I don't have excitement anything.
Eng. excitement; encouragement
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
1.
adj.
(mabagget, mA1-) full of excitement; very willing; industrious
Eng. excitement, full of; willing; industrious
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for a person or animal (egkabagget, nabagget, mabagget, patient, egkA><ø-) to be excited about or looking forward to something (igkabagget, ingkabagget, ikabagget, reason, igkA><ø-)
Sabeka seini ne batasan te etew ne amana ne egkeupian te minsan nekey ne himu su eyew egkateu/egkahalè/egpakaheram/egpakaneem-neem sikandin. Kenè egkapehulan wey maagsik ne egweil-weil te minsan nekey ne eggimuwen din tenged su egkeupian sikandin te seeyè ne eggimuwen din.
Egkabagget a ne eg-iskuyila su eyew egkateu a ne egsulat wey egbasa.
I am excited about studying so that I will know how to write and read.
Kenà a egkabagget ne egpamunuwit su kenà a egpekeutel.
I'm not excited about going fishing because I can't really catch fish.
Egkabagget a ke due muepiya ne egkakeen ku te egtrabahu.
I am interested in working if I have good food.
Ikabagget nu sika ne himuwen.
Be excited about that work!
Warè ku ikabagget ne egpamunuwit, su mig-uran
I was not excited about going fishing, because it was raining.
Warè a nabagget ne egpamunuwit, su mig-uran.
I was not excited about going fishing, because it was raining.
Warè a mabagget ne egpamunuwit, su mig-uran.
I was not excited about going fishing, because it was raining.
Gramm. The last two example sentences have the same meaning, but the former (the 'non-past' form) is more common than the latter (the 'unactualised form')
Eng. excited, be; enthusiastic, be; look forward to; anticipate; keen, be; expectant, be; interested, be; want; desire (a feeling of willingness); longing
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Like, love (3.4.1.1), Enjoy doing something (3.4.1.1.1), Interested (3.4.1.4), Excited (3.4.1.4.1), Enthusiastic (3.4.1.4.2), Want (3.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vt.
for something (egpakabagget, nakabagget, actor, egpAkA-) to cause someone to be excited or expectant, or to be the thing (igpakabagget/impakabagget, reason, igpAkA-) which causes someone to be excited or expectant
Eng. exciting, be; interesting, be; excite; interest
Ka egdurulis egpakabagget keddì.
Going fishing excites me.
Igpakabagget keddì ka egdurulis su mateles ne igkeen te ngalap te weyig.
Something that excites me is fishing, because fish are really good food.
Mateles ne igkeen te ngalap te weyig, sika ka igpakabagget keddì te egdurulis.
Fish are really good food, that's why I'm excited about going fishing.
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Like, love (3.4.1.1), Excited (3.4.1.4.1), Enthusiastic (3.4.1.4.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(mabagget, mA1-) enthusiastic and energetic about a certain activity
Mabagget amana ka amey ku ke egwali.
My father is very enthusiastic and energetic when he is clearing land.
Eng. enthusiastic; interested; excited; energetic (about willingness)
Gramm. Note the difference between egkabagget and mabagget. Egkabagget refers to enthusiasm about something at a particular time, while the adjective mabagget refers to a general attitude to an activity.
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Like, love (3.4.1.1), Excited (3.4.1.4.1), Enthusiastic (3.4.1.4.2), Energetic (2.4.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for someone (egmabagget, migmabagget/immabagget, mabagget, eg-mA1-) to be excited about or interested in something
Egmabagget a ke due meupiya ne egkakeen ka egtrabahu.
I am interested in working if I have good food.
Egmakabagget te me etew ke due meupiya ne egkakeen ka egtrabahu.
People will be interested in working if they have good food.
Gramm. Note that the verb becomes plural here with the affix ma-.
Eng. enthusiastic; interested; excited; energetic (about eager)
DDP: Energetic (2.4.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
vt.
for someone (eggibagget, miggibagget/inggibagget, hibagget, agent, eg><ø-hi-) to encourage someone else (eggibaggeten, inggibagget/miggibagget/piggibagget, hibaggeta, patient, eg><en-hi-) (eggibaggetan, inggibaggetan/miggibaggetan/piggibaggetan, hibaggeti, location, eg><an-hi-) with their words (iggibagget, inggibagget, instrument, ig><ø-)
Eggendiyaan ku pad si Atu su eggibaheten ku red ne egpabulus te hipanawan din.
I will go to Atu to encourage him to continue his walk.
Hibaggeta nu pa sikandin su malungkug.
Encourage him because he is sorrowful.
Eggendiyaan ku pad si Atu su eggibaggetan ku red ne egpabulus te hipanawan din.
I will go to Atu to encourage him to continue his walk.
Hibaggeti nu pa sikandin su malungkug.
Encourage him because he is sorrowful.
Gramm. Note that the person who is encouraged can either be the patient or the location.
Eng. encourage; urge
DDP: Excited (3.4.1.4.1), Enthusiastic (3.4.1.4.2), Confident (3.4.1.5)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for someone (egpamagget, migpamagget/impamagget, agent, egpaN-) to encourage another person (egpamageten, impamagget/migpamagget/pigpamagget, patient, eg><en-paN-) using something (igpamagget, impamagget, theme, ig-paN-)
Eng. encourager; encouraging person
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for many things (egmakabagget, makabagget, actor, egmAkA-) that can cause excitement at someone else, or to be the things (igmakabagget, immakabagget, instrument, igmAkA-) which cause excitement
Eng. excitement; encouragement
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
3.
v.
for someone (egbagget-bagget, migbagget-bagget/imbagget-bagget, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to show interest in something
Egbagget-bagget a eyew egkaruma a te hipenawen ni Ame kaaselem.
I will show my interest so that Father will take me with him on his walk tomorrow.
Eng. show interest
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
