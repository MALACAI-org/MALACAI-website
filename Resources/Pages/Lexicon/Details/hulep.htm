hulep
/hulɔp/
v_rt.
“fall_asleep”
 
vi.
for someone (egkewulep, newulep, agent, egkA><ø-) to fall asleep, or to be sleepy
Seini se inlihad ne Sebadu, warà ad e migsahinda su newulep ad.
This last Saturday, I didn't know what happened because I was asleep.
O. Syn. lipereng —sleep
Eng. fall asleep; asleep, fall; sleepy; tired
Tag. tulóg
Ceb. túlog
WBM: hurep
OCM: Sleeping (513)
DDP: Tired (2.4.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for someone (egkewulep-hulep, newulep-hulep, agent, egkA><ø-rdpRT-) to be sound asleep
Egkewulep-hulep e pad ganna ne napukew ad te sumbaley ney ne mahulub su egkasasew te babuy ran ne miglesutan.
I am still sound to asleep a while ago but then I am awoke with the noise of our neighbor assisting their pig giving birth.
Eng. sound asleep; asleep
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(huhulepen, rdpCV–en) easily fall asleep
Eng. easily fall asleep
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
