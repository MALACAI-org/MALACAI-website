kalabew
/kalabɔw/
n.
“water_buffalo”
 
n.   
Bubalus_bubalis
carabao or water buffalo
Kenè kew egparani te sika ne kalabew su egpanungey sika ne kalabew.
Wrd.Gloss: water_buffalo
cf. nati —calf

Cult. The támaráw is a wild species found especially in the highlands of Mindoro.
