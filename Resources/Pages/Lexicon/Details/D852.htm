belig
/bɔlig/
v_rt.
“blessing”
 
v.
(imbelig, iN-) blessing
Ay-ayari nu te eg-uyan ka karatuan nu su seini imbelig te Manama keykew.
Take good care of your wealth because this is only a blessing that God has given to you.
Wrd.Gloss: blessing
Eng. blessing; gift; grace; fortunate; lucky

Usage level: R
