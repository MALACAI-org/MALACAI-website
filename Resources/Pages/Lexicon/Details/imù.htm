imù
/ʔimuʔ/
Data Qual: I think it is double m? QDL - check with others
v_rt.
“remove”
 
v.
for someone (eg-imuan, agent, eg><an-) to completely remove something (eg. crumbs from a plate)
Wrd.Gloss: remove
Ka eg-imu iyan ka eg-amin te minsan nekey ne eg-aminen iling te hilamunen, keenen wey duma.
Eg-imuan ni Diyarid ka hemey diyè te pinggan.
Jared will finish up the rice on the plate.
Imui nu te eggilamun ka duhi su mahaan dè egtubu.
Remove the thorns totally because it will grow quickly.
Eng. remove; empty; finish; totally
