pammula1
/pammula/
Var. pamula
v_rt.
“plant”
 
1.
vt.
for someone (egpammula, agent, eg><ø-) to plant something (igpammula, theme, ig-) somewhere (egpammulaan, location, eg><an-)
Wrd.Gloss: plant
Egpammula a te bulak diyè te lama ney su eyew meupiya ne egtengtengan.
I will plant flowers in our yard so that it will be good to see.
Syn. ad-ad
Syn. erek
Syn. bubul
Syn. budbud
Syn. tuwil
Eng. plant; sow
Tag. magtaním
Ceb. magtanum; magpugas
Ilo.: agmúla
OCM: Ethnobotany (824), Agricultural Science (242)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
n.
(pammulaan_te_gulay, -an) garden
Migmareet e ka pammulaan ku te gulay su insudsud te babuy te sumbaley ney.
My garden was destroyed by the pig of our neighbor that's why its now ugly.
Eng. garden
Tag. hardín; hálamanán
Ceb. hardin
Ilo.: minuyúngan
OCM: Vegetable Production (244), Grounds (351)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: MI
