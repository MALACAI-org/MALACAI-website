“cease”
 
v.
for someone (egteil, migteil/inteil/pigteil, agent, eg><ø-) to stop moving around or to cease being angry (egteilan, migteilan/inteilan/pigteilan, location, eg><an-)
Eng. cease; stop
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (pekegteil, nekegteil, agent) to stop moving around or to be still
Ka pekegteil iyan ka egpatelen e wey warè igkabantali ne minsan nekey. Ka igpasabut man-e kayi ne naay-ayaran naan e ka keugpè kayi wey kenad egkaleugleug.
Egpekegteil e si Ame su warad igkangilam ne mangayew.
Father is now relax because no more enemy (murderer) to worry.
Mania te kenè egpakegteil si anggam gabii?
Why uncle was not still yesterday?
Eng. still; relax
DDP: Relaxed (3.4.1.2.1), Worried (3.4.2.4.1)

Usage level: M
