ulu
/ʔulu/
n.
“head”
 
1.
n.
head
Wrd.Gloss: head
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se natahuan te utek ta wey ka natahuan te mata, talinga, irung wey duma pad ne nasakup kayi.
Ka ulu ku neupew su warè bulbul.
My head is bald because it doesn't have hair.
