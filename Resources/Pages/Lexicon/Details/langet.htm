langet
/laŋɔt/
v_rt.
 
1.
n.
anger; wrath
Eng. anger; wrath
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for a person or animal (eglangetan, miglangetan/inlangetan, location, eg><an-) to be angry for a reason (iglangeti, inlangeti, reason, ig–i)
Ka seini iyan sika se due igkeepes nu ne himu te duma iling te intampelè ka wey ke insumbag ka.
Kenè ka eggimu te mareet su eglangetan ka amey wey iney nu.
Dont behave badly so that your parents never get angry.
Syn. epes
Eng. angry; rage; hate
Tag. poót; kasuklamán
Ceb. kasukù; kasilag
Ilo.: kagúra
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Angry (3.4.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
v.
for a person or nimal (egkalangetan, nalangetan, agent, egkA><an-) to become angry (igkalangeti, ingkalangeti, reason, igkA><i-) for a reason
Nekey ka igkalangeti nu keddì?
Why are you angry with me?
Cult. Egkalangetan is much stronger than egkabelù. The cause could be the same, but the reaction is stronger.
Eng. angry, to become; rage
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for something (egpakalanget, nakalanget, agent, egpAkA-) to cause someone to be angry (igpakalanget, impakalanget, reason, igpAkA-)
Eng. angry, cause to
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for a group (egpanlangetan, migpanlangetan/impanalangetan, agent, eg><an-paN-) to become angry (igpanlangeti, impanlangeti, reason, ig-paN-i) for a reason
Eng. anger
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
2.
adj.
(lelangeten, rdpCV–en) ill-natured
Eng. ill-natured; bad-natured
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(kalanglangetan, kA><an-rdpCVC-) very angry
Eng. very angry
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Dislike (3.4.2.1.1), Want (3.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
