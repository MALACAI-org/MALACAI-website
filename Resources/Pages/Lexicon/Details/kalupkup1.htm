kalupkup1
/kalupkup/
 
v.
for a mother hen (egpangalupkup, migpangalupkup/impanglupkup, pangalupkup, agent, eg><ø-) to hide her babies under her wings
Ka egpangalupkup iyan ka pegpeeles te me piyak te inayan diyè te diralem te me pakpak din. Egkaayunan te seini ne lalag ka langun ne klasi te me manuk-manuk ne eglayang wey egpananap, ayam wey se kenè, derakel wey ke dereisek. Masulug ka egpuunan mania te eggimuwen seini te inayan, egkaayun ne tenged te iggeles din seini te egpandawi ne me manuk-manuk wey duma pad, wey ke egpeyimeleyen din naa, wey ke egpaliperengen din naa.
Mareet ka upa ku su kenè egkalupkup te me piyak din.
My hen is bad because it doesn't hide her chicks under her wings.
