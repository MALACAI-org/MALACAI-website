gamit
/gamit/
v_rt.
“use”
 
n.
gear or equipment
Eng. gear; equipment; tools
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
v.
for someone or something (eggamit, miggamit, inggamit, agent, eg><ø-) to use something (eggamiten, theme, eg><en-)
Eggamit e pad te lapis nu su igsulat ku.
I'll just use a pencil because I'll write it down.
Eng. use
Tag. gamit
Ceb. gamit; suna
Ilo.: gamit; panagipaay
OCM: Utensils (415)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
v.
for something (egkahamit, nahamit, actor, egkA><ø-) to be used
Uya, egkahamit ded degma sika.
Yes, that can also be used.
Eng. used
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
