hilabet
/hilabɔt/
Var.1: ilabet
v_rt.
“meddle”
 
1.
vt.
for someone (eggilabet/eg-ilabet, miggilabet/mig-ilabet/innilabet/in-ilabet, agent, eg><ø-) to meddle with something, e.g., another person's possessions (eggilabetan/eg-ilabetan, innilabetan/in-ilabetan/miggilabetan/mig-ilabetan/piggilabetan/pig-ilabetan, location, eg><an-)
Wrd.Gloss: meddle
Ka eggilabet iyan ka eggulid wey eg-abid te asawa wey duma pad ne malitan wey lukes naa. Egkaayunan degma kayi ka me mananap ne eg-ahad te duma ne mananap te kandan ne kalasi.
Eggilabet perem ka anggam ku te asawa rin piru eglangeseen ma.
My uncle wants to meddle with her wife but she's bleeding.
Kenè ka eggilabet te kenè ne asawa nu su egpakapanlibug ka.
Do not sleep with not your wife because you can commit adultery.
Mesì egkeupian kad ne eggilabet te malitan ne asawa kad en iya.
If you want to interfere with a woman then get married indeed.
Eng. meddle; interfere; touch; harm; sleep
Tag. makiapid
Ceb. makighilawas
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
2.
v.
for a man (eggilabet/eg-ilabet, miggilabet/mig-ilabet/innilabet/in-ilabet, agent, eg><ø-) to commit adultery with a woman (eggilabetan/eg-ilabetan, innilabetan/in-ilabetan/miggilabetan/mig-ilabetan/piggilabetan/pig-ilabetan, location, eg><an-)
Mareet ne himu ka eggilabet te asawa te duma su supak sika te balaud te Manama wey kayi te tanè.
Committing adultery with the wife of others is bad act because that's against the law of God and here on earth (lit. ground).
Eng. commit adultery; adultery, commit; rape
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
