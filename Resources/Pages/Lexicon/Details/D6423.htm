taneng2
/tanɔŋ/
v_rt.
“speechless”
 
v.
for someone (egkatanengan, natanengan, katanengan, actor, egkA><an-) unable to speak or move with fear or amazement (igkatanengi, ingkatanengi/inkatanengi, reason, igkA–i)
Emun ke egkatanengan ka te lalag kenè kad egpakalalag su naapulan kad e.
If you are unable to speak means you don't have idea.
Mania te egkatanengan ka due, nekey bes ka nasuman-suman nu?
Why are you speechless there, what you are thingking about.
Iyan din igkatanengi te pangasak te talagpangasak su umbeg nahud sikandin.
The reason that he was speechless by the message of their guest speaker just because he was a naugthy.
Eng. speechless; incredulous; astounded; amazed; stunned; shocked; silent
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Surprise (3.4.1.3), Shock (3.4.2.1.7), Afraid (3.4.2.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something (egpakataneng, nakataneng, pakataneng, agent, egpAkA-) that make us unable to speak (igpakataneng, inpakataneng/ingpakataneng, reason, igpAkA-)
Eng. unable
