pindit
/pindit/
 
v.
for someone (egpindit, migpindit/inpindit/pigpindit, agent, eg><ø-) to pick using two fingers (egpinditen, pinditen, patient, eg><en-)
Ka seini iyan sika se egpurut ne daruwa rè ka kemel ne egkagamit, ka tinurù dè wey ka timbebakal.
Sikandin su egpindit te keenen.
She is picking a food using two fingers.
Eng. picking with two fingers; pick
