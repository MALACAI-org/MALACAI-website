ambul
/ʔambul/
v_rt.
“enter_water”
 
vi.
for someone or something (eg-ambul, mig-ambul/in-ambul, theme, eg><ø-) to go into the water (eg-ambulan, in-ambulan/mig-ambulan/pig-ambulan, location, eg><an-) to place into water
Eng. enter water; go into water; walk into water; get wet; water, enter; river, enter
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone or something (imma-ambul, patient, imMA-)
Eg-ambul ki rè te weyig su eyew egkaluluan ka me paa ta.
We just go into water so that our feet will be washed.
Eg-ambulan te me batè ka weyig ne egpamanihusan.
The swimming pool is walk by the children.
Imma-ambul te me buyag ka me batè te lanew.
Old men placed the children into the water.
Ant. gakap
Eng. enter water; go into water; walk into water; get wet; water, enter; river, enter
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something (ig-ambul, in-ambul, reason, ig><ø-) to wear or throw something into the water
Wrd.Gloss: throw_into
Eng. throw into; wear into
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
