sapad
/sapad/
v_rt.
“scold”
 
v.
for someone (egsapad, migsapad/insapad, sapad, agent, eg><ø-) to reprimand someone (egsaparan, insaparan/migsaparan/pigsaparan, sapari, location, eg><an-) for something they are about to do
Egsaparan ku pad ka anaken ku ne kenè egsinehew.
I will tell off my niece to stop crying.
O. Syn. dew-ey
O. Syn. ehet
Eng. reprimand; prohibit; warn; forbid; rebuke; tell off; scold
Ceb. pugung
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(masaparen, mA1–en) repressive
Eng. repressive
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
