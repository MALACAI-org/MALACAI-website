huwas
/huwas/
Fr. Ceb. huwas
 
v.
for someoneto recover from something
Iyan igpasabut te seini ne lalag, ne neulian e ka sabeka ne etew puun te pegderalu rin wey ke pegkewubug din naa.
Warè ula-ula ne igpakapapitew ni Uding ki Kaykay te newuwasan sikandin su nasiniku rin ma ka bake din.
Uding is no face to show with Kaykay after he was relieved from drunkenness because he hit her chin.
Warè pad newuwasi si Apa su newubug-hubug gabii.
Apa is not recover yet because he was very drunk last night.
Eng. recover; relieve
Ceb. huwas
