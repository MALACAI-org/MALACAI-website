naa
/naʔa/
Var. naan
part.
“so/then”
 
part.
so; then
Wrd.Gloss: so; then
Ken-u naan dè ne eglanding ke eriplanu?
How much longer before the airplane will be landing?
Sikan naa ka kenà ad egbehey su eg-uubat ka me etew.
So that then is why I won't give because the people tell lies.
Gramm. confirmative aspectual adjunct
Eng. so; then; leftover
DDP: Particles (9.2.6)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
