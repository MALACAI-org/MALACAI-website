gulingan
/guliŋan/
 
n.
work; action
Ka gulingan ne lalag egpekeg-iling dè te himu. Piru duen dè lalag ne egkaayunan te seini ne lalag kenè ne iling te himu.
Ke pa, duen pad egkahulinganan ku kayi te lamisa, kahi ni Randy.
Wait a while I have something to do here on the table, Randy said.
Iyan ney eggulinganan diyè te simbaan ke eg-uran ka eg-ulahing.
Our activities in the church when there's a rain is singing.
Eng. do; work; activity
Tag. gawa
Ceb. buhat
