busagsag
/busagsag/
 
vi.
for something (egkabusagsag, nabusagsag, actor, egkA><ø-) to become scattered
Ka nabusagsag iyan ka minsan nekey ne nabusaye wey natalagtag e iling te egkeulaan ne me dinampil wey duma pad. Warè seini migpasabut ne layun egkeyitabu ne due tahuan su minsan degma warè tahuan piru napayatpat e te minsan hendei.
Te pegginguma ney te baley, nabusagsag e ka me kasangkapan ney su inseip te me takawen.
When we came home, our things were scattered (on the floor) because robbers got in (while we were away).
Eng. spill; scatter; fling out; fly out; take out
