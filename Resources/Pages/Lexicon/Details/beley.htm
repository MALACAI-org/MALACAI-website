beley
/bɔlɔy/
 
v.
for someone (egkabeley, nabeley, patient, egkA><ø-) to become tired out from working hard (e.g. clearing a field egkamet or walking eggipanew)
Ka beley ne lalag iyan igpasabut ka warad keseg ne eggimu te minsan nekey tenged te minsan nekey ne inggimu. Kaayun iyan egpuunan te beley ka migkamet. Kaayun degma ne iyan egpuunan ka miggipanew wey kaayun degma ne iyan egpuunan ka migpallahuy.
Eggimeley ki pad su hendue te egkabeley ad e.
Let us rest first because it seems that I get tired.
Egpakabeley ka layun eggipanew.
Always walking is tiring.
O. Syn. pu-pù
O. Syn. luya
Eng. tired; worn out; weary; sleepy; haggard
Tag. pagód
Ceb. kápoy
Ilo.: nabánnug
OCM: Sensation and Perception (151), Labor and Leisure (461)
DDP: Body condition (2.4), Tired (2.4.4)
