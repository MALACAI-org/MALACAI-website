peggasal
/pɔggasal/
v_rt.
“skite”
 
1.
vi.
for someone (egpeggasal, migpeggasal/impeggasal, agent, eg><ø-) to skite or show-off to someone (egpeggasalan, impeggasalan/migpeggasalan/pigpeggasalan, goal, eg><an-)
Cult. This is a bad attitude, especially if one boasts about their own work at someone else's expense.
Amana en iya ne egpeggasal si Ibit te kasilaan din ne malig-et bes dè.
Eng. show-off; boast (about talking his own ability or wealth); skite; brag
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Show off (4.3.2.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
2.
vi.
for someone (egpeggasal, migpeggasal/impeggasal, agent, eg><ø-) to boast about or be proud of something they have done (igpeggasal, impeggasal, ipeggasal, reason, ig><ø-) to someone (egpeggasalan, migpeggasalan/impeggasalan/pigpeggasalan, location, eg><an-)
Cult. This is a good attitude, e.g., being pleased with one's own work.
Amana nahalè si Ibit ka migpeggasal kanami te maluag e ka imbayè din teg gilamun minsan mig-uran-uran.
Eng. boast (about talking his own ability or wealth); proud; contented; satisfied; accomplished; job well done; arrogant
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Proud (4.3.2.3), Contentment (3.4.1.1.6)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
