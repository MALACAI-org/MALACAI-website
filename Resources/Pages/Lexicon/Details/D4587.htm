nengneng
/nɔŋnɔŋ/
v_rt.
“know/understand”
 
v.
for someone or something (egnengneng, agent, eg><ø-) to find out something (egnengnengan, theme, eg><an-)
Kenè egnengneng ne batè si dindin.
Eggendiyè e pad te Simud su egnengneng a ke malehet ka ingguhud din keddì ne egderalu ka iney ku.
I will go to Simud to check out if it is true that my mother was sick.
Eng. find out; investigate; check out
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpakanengneng, egpAkA-) to understand
Syn. sabut
Eng. understand; comprehend
Tag. unáwà; hulò; intindí; tulos; isip
Ceb. sabot
Ilo.: gaw-at; awat
OCM: Ethnopsychology (828)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
n.
(nanengnengan, nA><an-) understanding
Sika ka keddì ne nanengnengan.
That's what I understand.
Eng. understand; led to believe
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
