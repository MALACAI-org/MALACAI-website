pangkus
/paŋkus/
 
v.
(egpangkus, eg><ø-) to result in
Ka pangkus ne lalag nekeg-iling te egkatamanan. Iling ke due eggimuwen ne minsan nekey due iya degma egpangkusan due. Su langun ne eggimuwen due egpangkusan meupiya ma seini wey ke mareet.
Meupiya ka impangkusan te pegribildi ni Datu Gawilan Sr su neila ka Matigsalug ne tribu.
The outcome of Datu Gawilan Sr revolt was good because Matigsalug tribe became known.
Iyan egpangkusan te mig-iskuyla ka meupiya ne peg-ugpe.
The result of someone who is attending school is good living.
Eng. result in
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
n.
(kapangkusan, kA><an-) to be certain; to finish a task; to follow something through to the end
Eng. outcome; certain; finish; ending; complete; happen; carry out; follow through

Usage level: R
