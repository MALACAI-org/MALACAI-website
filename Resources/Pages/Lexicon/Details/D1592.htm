dulung
/duluŋ/
v_rt.
“transport”
 
vt.
for a person or animal (egdulung, migdulung/indulung/pigdulung, dulung, actor, eg><ø-) to carry something (egdulungan, migdulungan/indulungan/pigdulungan, location, eg><an-) somewhere (igdulung, indulungi, instrument, ig><ø-)
Egdulung a kaniyu te kasilè kaaselem.
I will bring you(pl) sweet-potato(s) tomorrow.
Diyè te puruk dus ka egdulungan ku te kasilè kangkuwa.
I will bring sweet potato later at purok two.
Iyan din igdulungi te kasilè sikandan su warè egkakeen dan.
He brought sweet potato for them because they don't have food.
Eng. carry; take; bring; transport
