sumbung
/sumbuŋ/
v_rt.
“report”
 
vt.
for someone (egsumbung, migsumbung/insumbung, agent, eg><ø-) to report bad news (igsumbung, insumbung, instrument) to someone else (egsumbungan, insumbungan/migsumbungan/pigsumbungan, location, eg><an-) (expecting some consequent action)
Ka pulis ka egsumbungan te me etew ke egkatakawan sikandan.
The people report to the police if/when they are robbed.
Eng. report; notify; pimp; telltale; tattletale; charge with crime; crime, charge with; bring case; case, bring; accuse
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(sumbunganan, -an-an) a meeting to solve a problem
Eng. meeting
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
