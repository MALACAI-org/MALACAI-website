delag1
/dɔlag/
adv_rt.
“rarely”
 
1.
adv.
(marelag, mA1-) rarely
Gramm. far apart in time
Marelag a rè egkakita nikeykew su diya ad e mig-ugpè te mariyù ne inged.
You can rarely see me for I'm now living in a far away place.
Eng. rarely; seldom; infrequently; not often; occasionally
Tag. bihírà
Ceb. panagsa; panalagsa; talagsa
Ilo.: ságpamínsan
OCM: Weights and Measures (804)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
adj.
(marelag, mA1-) far apart (in distance)
Marelag ka impamula ku ne suli piru mallambeg.
The taro that I planted were far from each other but they are healthy.
Syn. diyù
Eng. far apart
Tag. madálang; hiwá-hiwaláy
Ceb. lagyo; bulag-bulag
OCM: Weights and Measures (804)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(maddelahen, mA1-rdpC-en) far apart; infrequent
Maddelahen te rè te egpamula ka aheley su eyew kenè egpekegselungey.
We will plant the corn infrequently in order not to stunt it.
Eng. far apart; infrequent
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
