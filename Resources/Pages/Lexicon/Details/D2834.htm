karniru
/karniru/
n.
“sheep”
 
n.
a sheep
Ka karniru iyan ka ayam ne maangkag wey meitem se batek wey bayew egpekeiling te kambing. Mananam ka ngalap kayi wey egkaayun ne eggimuwen ne hinabel ka bulbul.
Neyid-u a te karniru ne miggimatayan ganna diyè te baley te alukuy ku.
I felt compassion for the sheep being slaughtered earlier at the house of my friend.
Wrd.Gloss: sheep
Syn. bilibili
Eng. sheep; lamb
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
