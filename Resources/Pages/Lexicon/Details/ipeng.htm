ipeng
/ʔipɔŋ/
 
v.
for someone (egkeipeng, neipeng, experiencer, patient, egkA><ø-) to worry about someone else or something (igkeipeng, ingkeipeng, reason, igkA><ø-)
Eg-ulì ad su egkeipeng e ka Iney ku diyè te baley.
I will go home now for my mother is worried about me
Eng. worry; concern
Tag. nababahálà; kabalisahán
Ceb. kabalaka; naguol
Ilo.: dánag
OCM: Adjustment Processes (154)
DDP: Worried (3.4.2.4.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something (egpekeipeng, nekeipeng, agent, egpAkA-) to make someone to be worry about (igpekeipeng, impekeipeng, reason, igpAkA-)
Eng. worry
DDP: Worried (3.4.2.4.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
adj.
(iipengen, rdpCV–en) cynical
Amana ne iipengen si Elina ke egman-leug-leug ka me anak din.
Elena is cynical when her children roaming around.
Eng. cynical
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
