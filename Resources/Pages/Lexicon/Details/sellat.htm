sellat
/sɔllat/
Data Qual: These senses/meanings seem unrelated??? [RJH 8Jul14]
 
1.
n.
medicine
Ngaran seini te delemetan ne egpakabalag te maddeet ne egkeyitabu te pegkeetew ta. Ne iling degma te eg-anak ka malitan ne egkeelinan te malitan wey ke lukes naa, kaayun ne eggamit ta seini ne delemetan ne iyan igngaran se sellat.
Eng. medicine; herbal
Tag. gamot
Ceb. tamabl
Data Qual: check spelling of tamabl
OCM: Preventive Medicine (751)
DDP: Medicine (2.5.7.2), Medicine (2.5.7.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
2.
v.
for someone (egkasellat, nasellat, agent, egkA><ø-) to be alternated ( esp. giving birth with male and female ) (igpakasellat, impakasellat, theme, igpAkA-) by the use of medicine
Inuma nu seini se delemetan eyew egkasellat ka anak ne (malitan wey lukes ).
Take this medicine so that your children will alternate ( boy and girl )
Eng. alternate
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
3.
v.
for someone (egpakasellat, nakasellat, agent, egpAkA-) to have time to do something (igpakasellat, impakasellat, reason)
Ka egpakasellat iyan ka egpakakita te timpu ne egpekeyimu te minsan nekey ne eggimuwen.
Nakasellat a ubag nakakamet ganna te sapiling ku su mahal ne aldew kuntee.
I had time to cut grasses in my yard a while ago because today is holiday.
Iyan ku impakasellat ne egbibinayu te paley ganna su warè migsinehew ka deisek ne anak ku.
The reason that I had a vacant time for pounding rice a while ago because my little child didn't cry.
Eng. time; vacant; space
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
