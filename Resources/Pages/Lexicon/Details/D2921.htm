keddì
/kɔddiʔ/
Var. keddiey
pron.
“1s_NF(III)”
 
pron.
I; me
Wrd.Gloss: me; to_me; from_me
Mania te eglelalis ka keddì?
Why did you threaten me?
Luhat-luhat nu pa ibehey keddì.
Please give it to me.
Mani buwa te egpamusitsit si Nina keddì?
Why is it that Nina is hissing at me.
Gramm. 1st person singular non-focus pronoun (Set III)
Eng. I (non-focus III); me (non-focus III)
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Pronouns (9.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
 
poss_pron.
my
Wrd.Gloss: my
Amana ne meupiya ka keddì ne salual ne imbeli ku te pista te Simud.
My trousers that I bought at fiesta are very nice.
Sika ka keddì ne nanengnengan.
That's my understanding
Gramm. 1st person singular possessive pronominal specifier (prepositional)
Eng. my; mine, of
Binukid: kanak
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Pronouns (9.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
 
poss_pron.
mine
Keddì sika ne kamet.
That field is mine.
Gramm. 1st person singular possessive pronoun
Eng. mine
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Pronouns (9.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
