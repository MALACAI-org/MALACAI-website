anak te dalan
/ʔanak_tɔ_dalan/
See anak
See dalan
 
n.
a child born out of wedlock
Ka anak te dalan iyan ka batè ne in-anak ne warè amey su naberesan dè ka iney rin wey warè asawaa te nakaberes kandin.Due degma duma ne egpeiyut-iyut dè kataman ke eg-anak e igdegpak dan dè seini.
Anak te dalan si Idwin su warè ma amey rin ne malehet.
Edwin is an illegitimate child for he has no real father.
Cult. If a single woman gets pregnant their child will be called as anak te dalan.
Eng. child, illegitimate; bastard; illegitimate child
Ceb. bastardu
DDP: Child (2.6.4.2)
