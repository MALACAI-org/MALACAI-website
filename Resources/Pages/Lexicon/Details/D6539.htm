tees
/tɔʔɔs/
v_rt.
“not_tell”
 
v.
for someone (egtees, migtees, agent, eg><ø-) to request someone (egteesan, location, eg><an-) to not tell something
Wrd.Gloss: not_tell
Ka egtees iyan ka egnangen te senge etew te heles dè ne kenè egpepangguhuren te minsan nekey ne nanengnengan dan te daruwa diyè te minsan hentew su eyew kenè egkawangal ka natahaan dan. Igkeupii rin seini ne egkeeles dè wey sikandan ka nakataha su kema ke egpakabehey te prublima diyè te kandin wey diyè te duma ne etew.
Egteesan ku pad iya ka suled ku te kenè egpepangguhuren engki Ine te migpanakew key te atelug ganna te iyug dan.
I will charge my brother secretly not to tell Mom that we stole an egg while they're gone.
Inteesan bes ni Ulmu ka duma rin sikan naa ka warè iya degma migpangguhud te neyitabu.
Ulmu secretly charged his companion to silent that is why he didn't tell what happened.
Eng. confidential; private; secret; not tell; silenced; sworn to secrecy; charge to silence
