tingkemed
/tiŋkɔmɔd/
v_rt.
“startle”
 
vi.
for a person or animal (egkatingkemed, natingkemed, katingkemed, actor, egkA><ø-) to be startled by an unexpected sound or event (igkatingkemed, ingkatingkemed, ikatingkemed, katingkemeri, instrument, igkA><ø-)
Amana ku ne igkatingkemed ka betu te sinapang.
I am really startled by a gun shot.
O. Syn. bayat
O. Syn. alimukawan
Eng. startled; surprised
Ceb. nakurat
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Surprise (3.4.1.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpatingkemed, migpatingkemed/inpatingkemed/pigpatingkemed, agent, egpA><ø-) to deliberately startle someone else (egpatingkemeren, patingkemeren, theme, egpA><en-) for a reason (igpatingkemera, inpatingkemera, reason, igpA><a-)
Egpatingkemeren ku sikuna te me Ammahal ne egpahal-halawey diyè te luyung te dakel ne bunuwang.
cf. egginaldek —frighten
Eng. startle; surprise; disturb; scare; torment
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something (egpakatingkemed, pakatingkemed, actor, egpAkA-) that can make surprised or disturbed by someone or something (igpakatingkemed, pakatingkemed, theme, igpAkA-)
Eng. surprise; disturb; torment
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
