agsik
/ʔagsik/
adj_rt.
“vigorous”
Data Qual: check range of meaning - possible two senses - enthusiastic and physically energetic
 
1.
v.
for a person or animal (eg-agsik, mig-agsik/in-agsik, agsik, actor, eg><ø-) to be energetic for doing something (eg-agsikan, in-agsikan/mig-agsikan/pig-agsikan, agsiki, range, eg><an-) for a reason (ig-agsik, ig-agsiki, reason, ig–i)
Eng. energetic, be; vigorous, be; enthusiastic, be; active, be
DDP: Enthusiastic (3.4.1.4.2), Energetic (2.4.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for a person or animal (egkaagsik, naagsik, actor & patient, egkA><ø-) to become energetic or vigorous for a reason (igkaagsik, ingkaagsik, ikaagsik, reason, igkA><ø-)
Eng. energetic, become; vigorous, become
DDP: Energetic (2.4.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something (egpakaagsik, nakaagsik, actor, egpAkA-) to cause someone to become energetic, or to be the thing (igpakaagsik, impakaagsik, instrument, igpAkA-) that causes someone to become energetic
Eng. energetic, make someone; vigorous, make someone
DDP: Energetic (2.4.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for a person or animal (egpaagsik, migpaagsik/impaagsik, patient, eg><ø-pA-) to need something (e.g., food or comfort) (egpaagsiken, impaagsik/migpaagsik/pigpaagsik, range, eg><en-pA-) in order to become energetic
Eng. energetic, need something; vigorous, need something
DDP: Energetic (2.4.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for someone (egmaagsik, migmaagsik/immaagsik, actor & patient, eg-mA1-) to become energetic
Keilangan ne egkeen te gulay ka me batè eyew egmaagsik layun.
The children must eat vegetables in order to become always energetic.
Eng. energetic, become; vigorous, become
DDP: Energetic (2.4.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
adj.
(maagsik, maagsik-agsik, mA1-, mA1-rdpRT-) vigorous; active
Ka maagsik iyan ka malaheylahey wey maagkap-agkap ne egweil-weil. Migpasabut seini te kenè ne malambened ne pegweil-weil, ke kenè, meiyal wey masiyapat iling te uled.
Maagsik ne egweil-weil si Bubuy su dulepu ka impangalalin kandin.
Bubuy moves vigorously because his mother ate a rattlesnake when she was pregnant (with Bubuy)
Wrd.Gloss: vigorous
Eng. vigorous; energetic (about activeness in doing something); active
DDP: Energetic (2.4.3)

Usage level: R
