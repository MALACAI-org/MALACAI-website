subal
/subal/
v_rt.
“replace”
 
1.
v.
for someone or something (egsubal, migsubal/insubal, agent, eg><ø-) to subtitute someone or something else (egsubalan, insubalan/migsubalan/pigsubalan, location, eg><an-) (in a game, a job, or at work)
Si Inday ka igsubal ki pastur Hinri ne eg-uyan te simbaan diyè te Dalurung.
Inday will be the substitute with Pastor Hinri to handle the church in Dalurung.
O. Syn. egsambey
Eng. subtitute; replace; change; succeed after
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for two people (egpekegsubal, nekegsubal, agent, egpekeg-) to change places with each other, e.g., by passing each other going in different directions along the road
Inday, egpekegsubal kew bes te amey nu kaaselem?
Inday, are you changing places with your dad tomorrow?
Eng. replace; substitute; exchange; change places; swap
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
2.
v.
for someone (egpasubaley, migpasubaley/impasubaley, agent, eg><ø-pA><ey-) to move different directions
Eng. different directions, to move
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
