pamaneug
/pamanɔʔug/
v_rt.
“climb_down”
 
v.
for someone (egpamaneug, migpamaneug/migpamaneug, agent, eg><ø-) to climb down (something such as a ladder, stairs or a tree)
Te egpamaneug ad e te kayu ne inpamaneyikan ku, nasagpi pa iya mulè ka sugpang ne indiekan ku, miglapenak e rè iya mulè te basak.
It was the time when I'm going to climd down in a tree I'd climbed on, the branch where I step on was splitted that's why I fall with a big sound in a mud.
Morpho. ?paN-
Eng. climb down; go down; descend
Tag. bumabâ
Ceb. miubos
Ilo.: bumabá
OCM: Locomotion (481)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
