kembang
/kɔmbaŋ/
n.
“head_sores”
 
n.
a head sore
Patabulihin pa si lang-lang su eyew egkeulian ka kembang din.
Let someone who will bald lang-lang so that her head sore will be healed.
Wrd.Gloss: head_sore
Eng. head sore; sores on head
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
adj.
(kembangen, -en) to have head sores
Egpanambal key te me batè ne kembangen se ulu.
Eng. head sores; sores on head
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
