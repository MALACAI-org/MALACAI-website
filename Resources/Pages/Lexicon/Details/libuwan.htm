libuwan
/libuan/
 
v.
for someone (eglibuwanan, miglibuwan/inlibuwan, libuwan, location, eg><an-) to sweat on the way to recovery from an illness
Meupiya su eglibuwanan ad eyew egkeulian ad ka egdatengen.
It is better that I am sweating for I can recover my headache.
Eng. sweat; recover
DDP: Sweat (2.2.6), Recover from sickness (2.5.1.1)

Usage level: R
