tilalam
/tilalam/
 
v.
for someone (egtilalam, agent, eg><ø-) to always be asking for things from other people because they have a desirable trait (egtilalamen, patient, eg><en-)
Eng. always ask; ask; request; bludge; scounge
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
(egkatilalam, egkA><ø-) to expect; to have a precedent; to want more and more
Wrd.Gloss: expect
Kenè kew behey ke due egbuyù te tirè su egkatilalam.
Eng. want more and more; expect; precedent
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
