warad
/warad/
 
1.
adv.
no more; none remaining
Warad e ra
There's no more [discouragement]
Warad e etew diyè te lasud.
No more people inside.
Gramm. This is , with completive aspect
Eng. no more; none left; none remaining; no longer
DDP: No, not (9.4.6.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
adv.
already lost
Warad ka manuk ku natarin e.
My chicken is already lost.
Data Qual: just checking... does this mean 'I have no more chickens because they are already lost'?
Eng. already lost
Online dict status: c
Usage level: M
