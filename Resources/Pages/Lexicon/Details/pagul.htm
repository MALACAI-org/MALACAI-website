pagul
/paggul/
Var. paggul
v_rt.
“lock_up”
 
n.
a lock usually made by putting a wooden bar across the doorWood or metal that use to prevent/stop door to open. Purpose of paggul is to secure the house from thief.
Eng. locking bar; lock
Ceb. trangka
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for someone (egpepagul, migpepagul/impepagul, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to lock up something (egpagulan, impagulan/migpagulan/pigpagulan, location, eg><an-)
Wrd.Gloss: lock up
Egpepagul a su kema ke egseleran a te me takawen.
I will lock up because otherwise a thief might get in.
O. Syn. sekeg
Eng. lock up; bar a door, to; secure
