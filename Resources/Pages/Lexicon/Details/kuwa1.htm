kuwa1
/kua/
v_rt.
“get”
 
v.
for someone (egkuwa, migkuwa/ingkuwa, agent, eg><ø-) to get something (egkuen, ingkuwa/migkuwa/pigkuwa, patient, eg><en-)
Wrd.Gloss: get
Egkuwa a te pinipian ku diyè te weyig.
I will get my clean laundry in the river.
Eng. take; get; obtain; avail; achieve; acquire
Tag. kúnin; kumúha
Ceb. kuhaon
Ilo.: aláen
OCM: Acquisition and Relinquishment of Property (425)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for something (egkakuwa, nakuwa, theme, egkA><ø-) to be acquired
Eng. acquired, to be; achieve, to be
