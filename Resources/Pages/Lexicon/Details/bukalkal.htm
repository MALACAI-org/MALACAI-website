bukalkal
/bukalkal/
v_rt.
“pull_unrolled”
 
1.
v.
for someone (egbukalkal, migbukalkal/imbukalkal, agent, eg><ø-) to pull something unrolled (egbukalkalan, imbukalkalan/migbukalkalan/pigbukalkalan, location, eg><an-)
Ka kaluwasan te seini ne lalag iyan ka pegbukelas te minsan nekey ne nabukusan, pegbelat, peg-awè te bukus.
Egbukalkalan lad sika se tinenges nu su eyew egkeenen tad.
Just unroll your rice wrap with the leaf of banana and we will eat it.
Eng. pull unrolled; unroll
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something esp. flowers (egbukalkal, migbukalkal/imbukalkal, agent, eg><ø-) to open up
Eng. open up, to
Ceb. bukadkad
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
v.
for something (egkabukalkalan, nabukalkalan, theme, egkA><an-) to be noticed or published
Eng. noticed, to be; published, to be; appeared, to be
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
