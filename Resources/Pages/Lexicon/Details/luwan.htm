luwan
/luan/
adj_rt.
“roomy”
 
1.
adj.
(maluwan, mA1-) for a house to be spacious or roomy
Maluwan amana ka baley enni Dunilyu piru pila-pila ran dè te mig-ugpè.
The house of Donilio was very spacious but they are few living in it.
Eng. spacious; roomy
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
2.
adj.
(maluwan, mA1-) for a rafting trip to be good because it wasn't difficult to get through the rapids
Eng. good
