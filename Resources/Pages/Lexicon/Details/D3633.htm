leeb2
/lɔʔɔb/
v_rt.
“cook_in_bamboo”
 
vt.
for someone (egleleeb, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to cook something (egleeben, patient, eg><en-) by roasting it inside a tube of bamboo (egleeban, location, eg><an-)
Ka egleleeb iyan ka peglablab te damuluwan wey ke bagtek naa ne intahuan te egkakeen wey ngalap eyew eggilutuen. Kenè dè amana egtutungen ka damuluwan su eyew kenè egkatutungan ka inlablab.
Egleeben ka peit ne neutel ku te migpanikep.
Cult. Thin-walled maahul bamboo damuluwan is cut into one section baney. It is then filled with the food and the top is blocked with leaves. It is placed into the blazing fire but turned so that it does not burn through in any one place. When almost burnt through, it is removed from the fire with tongs and the contents are ready to eat. Dragonflies are usually cooked this way.
Eng. cook
