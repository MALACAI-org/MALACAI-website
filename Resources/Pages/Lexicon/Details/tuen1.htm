tuen1
/tuʔɔn/
v_rt.
“know”
 
n.
(katuenan, kA><an-) knowledge
Meupiya ka masalig se katuenan su langun ne himu ne egkatuenan ta.
It is better to be skillful because many work you can do.
Eng. knowledge; intelligence; understanding; wisdom; skill; ability
Tag. álam
Ceb. hibalu (fact; skill); kaalam (special area; skill); kinaadman (gen/spec knowledge); katukato (common sense; reasoning; knowledge)
OCM: Ethnopsychology (828)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
vi.
for someone (egkatuenan, natuenan, natueni, location, egkA><an-) to know something or how to do something
Wrd.Gloss: able
Behey nu keddì ka se kuglung su egkatuenan ku sika te egpangelit.
Kindly give the kuglung to me, because I know how to play it.
Warè ku natueni ke nekey sika.
I don't know what that is.
Warè ku natueni ke nekey ka isip te etew.
I don't know what the people think.
Eng. know; know how
OCM: Sensation and Perception (151), Students (877)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for something (egkatuenan, natuenan, theme, egkA><ø-) to be easy by someone to do
Eng. easy, to be (to do)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something (egkatu-tuenan, natu-tuenan, theme, egkA><ø-rdpCV-) to be done accurately
Eng. accurate, to be; accurately
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
