utek
/ʔutɔk/
n.
“brain”
 
1.
n.
brain
Egpakarereet te utek ka mariwana.
Marijuana can damage our brain.
Wrd.Gloss: brain
Eng. brain
Tag. útak; seso
Ceb. utok
Ilo.: utek

OCM: Ethnozoology (825), Ethnoanatomy (826)
DDP: Head (2.1.1), Internal organs (2.1.8)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
adj.
(utekan, -an) intelligent; crafty; creative; ingenous
Utekan ne etew si Husi Risal su masalig ka kursu ne neimpusan din wey masalig degma ne linalahan ka natuenan din.
Dr. Jose Palanas Rizal was intelligent because many course he finished and many dialect that he able to speak.
Syn. matagseb
Eng. intelligent; bright; brainy; crafty; deceitful; wise; clever; creative; ingenous
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
n.
(utek_te_tul-an) bone marrow
Ew! duen bes utek te tulan?
Oh! there is bone marrow of bones?
Eng. bone marrow; marrow
OCM: Ethnozoology (825)
Data Qual: check- about animals too?
Data Qual: included animals
OCM: Ethnoanatomy (826)
DDP: Bone, joint (2.1.6)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
