ang-ang
/ʔaŋʔaŋ/
 
n.
notch
Mareet ne egtengtengan ka kayu ke due ang-ang.
The tree looks bad if it has notches.
Pila ne ang-ang ka haheran niyu?
How many notches does your ladder have?
Eng. notch
OCM: Visual Arts (5311)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for someone (eg-eang-ang, mig-eang-ang/in-eang-ang, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to make a notch on something (eg-eang-angan, in-in eang-angan/mig-eang-angan/pig-eang-anngan, location, eg><an-rdpCV-) with something (e.g.,ax or sharp bolo) (ig-eang-ang, in-eang-ang, theme, ig-rdpCV-)
Eng. make a notch, to; notch, to
