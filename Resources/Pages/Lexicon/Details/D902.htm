besik
/bɔsik/
v_rt.
“splatter”
 
v.
for something, e.g., burning resin, hot oil (egbesik, migbesik/imbesik, besik, egbebesik, migbebesik/imbebesik, bebesik, actor & theme, eg><ø-, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to splatter someone or something (egbesikan, imbesikan/migbesikan/pigbesikan, besiki, goal, eg><an-)
Wrd.Gloss: splatter
Ka gitè te lawaan ne eggimuwen ne sulu egbebesik.
The sap of the lawaan tree when used as a lamp splatters.
Eng. splatter, to; splash, to
DDP: Soil, dirt (1.2.2.1), Drip (1.3.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for someone or something (egkabesikan, nabesikan, patient, egkA><an-) to be splattered, e.g., when a truck passes on a muddy road
Eng. splattered, be; splashed, be
DDP: Soil, dirt (1.2.2.1), Pour (1.3.2.2), Wet (1.3.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(mabesik, mA1-) splattery
Eng. splattery; prone to splatter
DDP: Soil, dirt (1.2.2.1), Drip (1.3.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
