mahal
/magal/
adj.
“expensive”
 
adj.
expensive
Wrd.Gloss: expensive
Ka mahal iyan sika se minsan nekey ne kenè egkabayaran te deisek dè ne kentidad, dakel se kentidad.
Mahal e amana ka me belegyè kuntee.
All products today are so expensive.
Eng. expensive; dear; extravagant; valuable; luxurious
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
v.
for someone (egkamahalan) to not proceed with something because it is too expensive
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
