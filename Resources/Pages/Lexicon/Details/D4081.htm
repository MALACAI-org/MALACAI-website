lunangnang
/lunaŋnaŋ/
adj_rt.
“medium”
 
adj.
(malunangnang, mA1-) for something to be medium
Wrd.Gloss: medium
Cult. e.g. for the sun to shine but not be really hot, or for a river to have a only a slight current on one side.
Eng. medium; slight
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
