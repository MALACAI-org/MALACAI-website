lundis
/lundis/
 
n.
skin
Eng. skin
OCM: Ethnoanatomy (826)
DDP: Skin (2.1.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vt.
for someone (eglundis, miglundis/inlundisx, agent, eg><ø-) to remove skin (laplap or luwit) (eglundisen, inlundis/miglundis/piglundis, patient, eg><en-) off something, e.g., to pick off loose skin, or bark off a tree (eglundisan, inlundisan/miglundisan/piglundisan, location, eg><an-)
Eng. remove skin; skin, remove
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (eglulundis, miglulundis/inlulundis, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to remove the skin off something continuously (eglulundisan, inlulundisan/miglulundisan/piglulundisan, location, eg><an-rdpCV-)
Eng. skin removing
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for somone (egkalundisan, nalundisan, actor, egkA><an-) to suffer a (rope or pressure) burn
Nalundisan e ka kumel ku ne nalipit te lekeb te gumawan.
My finger skin is open for it's flattened by the door of doorway.
O. Syn. egkaludluran
Eng. open (clot or wound); rope burn; friction burn; burn, rope; graze skin; skin graze
