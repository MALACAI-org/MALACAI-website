saklag
/saklag/
n.
 
n.
a fishing net
Nahisì ka saklag ku su migsaharan te dakel ne kasili.
Eng. net; fish net
OCM: Fishing Gear (227)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egsaklag, egpanaklag, agent, eg><ø-, eg><ø-paN-) to fish with a small very fine net
Egkeupian a ne egpanaklag su masalig ka peit ne egkeutel ku.
I like to fish with a net for I can catch many.
Eng. fish with net; net
OCM: Fishing (226)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
