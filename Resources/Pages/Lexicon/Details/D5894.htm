Simud
/simud/
prop_n.
“Simud_river”
 
prop_n.
the river which runs through the Sinuda barangay. As a result, many Matigsalugs call the barangay by this name.
Wrd.Gloss: Sinuda_barangay
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
