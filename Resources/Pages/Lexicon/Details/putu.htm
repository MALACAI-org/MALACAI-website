putu
/putu/
Fr. Ceb. puto
 
n.
a grate food like a cake
Ka putu egkakeen, wey meemis. Senge ka kuwa ne malibusen wey senge kakuwa ne nalein-lein se purma wey ke batek. Eggilutuen seini puun te binggala, ginaling ne hemey, harina, pilit ne hemey, sahing wey duma pad.
Rato, bubanggud ka te binggala su egpuputu ki.
Rato, grate cassava we will cook a putu.
Mananam ka pilit ne eggimuwen ne putu su meemis wey mewumellumel.
Sticky rice cake is very delecious because its sweet and tender.
Eng. cake; bake
