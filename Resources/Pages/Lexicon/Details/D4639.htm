niyu
/niu/
pron.
“2p_NF(II)”
 
pron.
you (plural); by you (plural)
Mania te indadaatan niyu ka hemey?
Why did you waste the rice?
Gramm. 2nd person plural non-focus (Set II, brief form)
Eng. you (pl) (non-focus II); by you (pl) (non-focus II)
Binukid: nuy
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Pronouns (9.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
 
pron_COMPL.
(niyud, -d) you(pl); by you(pl) (with completive aspect)
Gramm. 2nd person plural non-focus (Set II, brief form) with completive aspect
Eng. you(pl) (non-focus II), with completive aspect; by you(pl) (non-focus II), with completive aspect
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
poss_pron.
your (plural); of yours
Wrd.Gloss: your(pl)
Kenè nu uyana ka bula diyè te baley niyu.
Don't (you) bring the ball to your house.
Gramm. 2nd person plural possessive pronominal specifier (postpositional)
Eng. your (pl); of yours (pl)
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Pronouns (9.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
