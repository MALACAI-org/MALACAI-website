gungut1
/guŋut/
v_rt.
“tell_story”
 
n.
(gungutan, -an) male
Ka gungutan iyan ka igngaran dengan te lukes ne etew ne mabantug tenged te keingesan wey katuenan din.
Due gungutan ne migpanumbaley keddì.
There was a man visited me.
Eng. male; man
DDP: Male, female (2.6.5), Man (2.6.5.1)
 
v.
for someone (egguhungutan, migguhungutan/ingguhungutan, agent, eg><an-rdpCV-) to tell stories about something (egguhungutanen, ingguhungutan/migguhungutan/pigguhungutan, patient, eg><en-rdpCV-)
Guhungutan ka Amè su egkewulep key e.
Father tell us a story because we are sleepy.
Eng. tell stories
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
n.
(gungutanen, -an-en) a folk-tale; an epic story; a drama
Due gungutanen ku.
I have story.
Cult. usually told at night
Eng. folk tale; fairy tale; epic story; testimony; drama
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
