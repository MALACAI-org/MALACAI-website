libeng
/libɔŋ/
v_rt.
“circle_around”
 
v.
for something (e.g. untethered horse or carabbao, aeroplane) (eglibeng-libeng, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to circle around a fixed area
Ka eglibeng-libeng iyan ka eglingut-lingut eggipanew te sabeka ne inged wey ke minsan hendei ne nalingeran te eggipanew wey egweil-weil naa ne diyè egbayè te aw-awangan, weyig wey tanè. Egkaayunan te seini ne lalag ka minsan nekey ne egweil-weil wey egpekeyipanew ligkat te sabeka ne inged peendiyè te lein ne inged, mariyù ma wey se marani, matikang ma wey se mababew, maralem ma wey se mababew, maluag ma wey se malig-et naa.
Iyan ku naan dè pulunganan diyè te baley ka eglibeng-libeng ka egpamitew simat ne natarin su igteyi ku perem te kumbalè ku ne nahisì.
I'm just roaming around the house looking for the lost needle which I will sew of my torn clothe.
Syn. libut
Syn. lingkus
Eng. encircle; circle; loop; roam around
