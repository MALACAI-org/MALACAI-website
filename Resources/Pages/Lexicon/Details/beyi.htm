beyi
/bɔi/
n.
“unmarried_girl”
 
n.
a term of direct address meaning woman or girl
Si beyi naan dè ka warè mig-asawa te kandan ne tal- talahari.
She is the only one left among her brothers and sisters who has not yet married.
Eng. girl; youth (female)

n.
(beyi-beyi, rdpRT-) a girl around the age of puberty (e.g. 13 years old); a young girl (not yet marriageable)
Amana ne mateles ne beyi-beyi si Ana su malayat ka bulbul rin.
Ana is a beautiful girl because of her long hair.
