buut
/buʔut/
v_rt.
“command”
 
v.
for someone (egbuut, migbuut, agent, eg><ø-) to command someone else (egbuutan, location, eg><an-)
Due katenged din ne egbuut su sikandin ka pangulu.
He has the authority to rule because he is the president.
Syn. suhù
Eng. command; order; will; determine; decide; decision; control; rule
Tag. mag-útos; utosán
Ceb. sugù
Ilo.: bilínen
OCM: Sanctions (681), Judicial Authority (692), Avoidance and Taboo (784)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
