liyu2
/liu/
 
v.
for the (ocean) tide (migliyu, mig><ø-) to come in
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se miglapewan e te baggiyu ka baley, bubungan wey duma pad.
Migliyu man-e ka dahat te dakel ne batu.
The ocean is come up over into the large stone.
Eng. tide come in; high tide
Tag. lakí ng túbig; táog
Ceb. taub sa dagat
Ilo.: panagadálem ti báybay
OCM: Ethnogeography (823)
DDP: Tide (1.3.2.6)

Usage level: M
