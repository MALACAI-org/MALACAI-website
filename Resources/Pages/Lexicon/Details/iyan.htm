iyan
/ʔian/
dem.
“that”
 
1.
dem.
that
Wrd.Gloss: that
Ka iyan migpasabut/migtinuru te minsan nekey ne igpataha te duma. Egkaayun ne egtinuruen seini wey igpasabut dè pinaahi te lalag.
Migkahi ka Magbebayè te keilangan ne kenè ki egpasimbeen te duma ne me diyus-diyus, iyan sika ka igkeupii rin su talikuren sikandin ne Manama.
The Lord said that we should not worship other gods, that is what He want for He is a jealous God.
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
 
2.
adv.
(iyan_naan_pad) how much more
Ka seini iyan sika se egpegkutereen wey egpeg-ilingen ka kanekal wey katuenan te minsan nekey wey minsan hentew eyew igpakita ke nekey ka kaleinan.
Warè nakareeg ki Pakyaw ne me Melikanu ne iyan naan pad ke me duma rin ded ne Pilipinu.
No American defeated Pakyaw and how much more his fellow Filipinos.
Eng. how much more
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
