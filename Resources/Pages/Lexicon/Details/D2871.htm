katted
/kattɔd/
n.
“grub”
 
n.
an edible grub that is found in fallen trees
Ka katted iyan sika se uled ne diyè eg-ugpè te lepu, migkeredkered ka lawa wey malepet dè su warè dè nekeuma te sabeka ne dangew, egkakeen wey malambu seini ne maangkag ka batek kayi.
Nabulung-bulung a te paniudtu ney su katted ka insere ney.
I'm so full of lunched because our viand was grub.
Eng. grub
DDP: Insect (1.6.1.7), Animal life cycle (1.6.3)
