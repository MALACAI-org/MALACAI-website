lasi
/lasi/
adv_rt.
“often”
 
adv.
(malasi, mA1-) often; constant; continuous
Malasi ka buyag egkalangeti keddiey su lewuk a nahud.
The old (adressing father) is always get agry with me because I can't stay at home.
Eng. often; constant; continuous; frequently
Tag. malímit
Ceb. kanunay
Ilo.: masánsan
OCM: Weights and Measures (804)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
v.
for someone (eglasi-lasi, miglasi-lasi/inlasi-lasi, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to be often doing something
Eng. often doing; often; always
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
