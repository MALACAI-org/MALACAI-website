ayun1
/ʔayun/
v_rt.
“possible/allowable”
 
1.
vi.
for something (egkaayun, naayun, egkA><ø-) to be possible or allowable
Migpasabut seini ne lalag te minsan nekey ne egkeyimu ne warè egsanggatan/egpakabalabag te himu in-uyunan te langun.
Egkaayun ne kenà ad egpaniudtu su nabulung e pad te ingkeen ku ganna.
It's okay for me not to have lunch because I'm still full of what I had eat awhile ago.
Wrd.Gloss: possible
Eng. possible; allowable; may; permissible; acceptable; can; could; okay
Tag. puwéde
Ceb. mahimù
OCM: Norms (183)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
vi.
for something (egkaayunan, naayunan, egkA><an-) to be a possibility
Ka egkaayunan migpasabut te egkapabulusan, egkeyimu wey egkapabayaan.
Ka egkaayunan te seini ne himu iyan dè seeyè se due katuenan.
The posibility of this work goes to those knowledgeable
Wrd.Gloss: possibility
Eng. possibility; choice
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(kaayunan, kA><an-) opportunity
Eng. opportunity
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
n.
(kaayunan, kA><an-) consumption
Eng. consumption

(egpaayun-ayun, migpaayun-ayun/impaayun-ayun, egpA><ø-rdpRT-) to be possible

v.
for a person or animal (egpaayun-ayun, migpaayun-ayun/impaayun-ayun, agent, egpA><ø-rdpRT-) to follow straight ahead a certain passage (egpaay-ayunan, impaay-ayunan/migpaay-ayunan/pigpaay-ayunan, location, eg><an-pA-rdpCVC-)
Eng. follow, to
