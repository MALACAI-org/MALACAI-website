teen1
/tɔʔɔn/
v_rt.
“trap_fish”
 
v.      
for someone (egteteen, migteteen, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to trap (fish)
Egteteen te bubu sikandan su kenad e eg-uran angkuwa.
He is trapping fish.
