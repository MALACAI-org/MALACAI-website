labit
/labit/
 
v.
for the skin (eglabit, miglabit/inlabit, theme, eg><ø-) to be wrinkled
Eng. wrinkle, to be
OCM: Ethnoanatomy (826)
DDP: Skin (2.1.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(pangulabit, paN-) the flesh between first rib down and hip bone
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se sapù wey laplap ne nataliwarè te sehang wey mangnguwud ne gusuk.
Masakit ka me pangngulabit ku te neulug a te mangga.
The flesh between my lower ribs and hip bones are painful due to accident when i fall a mango tree.
Eng. flesh
OCM: Ethnoanatomy (826)
DDP: Torso (2.1.2), Flesh (2.1.7)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
