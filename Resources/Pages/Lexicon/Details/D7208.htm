usembep
/ʔusɔmbɔp/
 
adj.
Data Qual: check affixation here
sad
Eng. sad; troubled
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Sad (3.4.2.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for someone (eg-usembep, mig-usembep/in-usembep, agent) to be troubled for thinking or worried (eg-usembepan, mig-usembepan/in-usembepan/pig-usembepan, usembepi, location, eg><an-) about something ( esp. problem ) (ig-usembep, in-usembep, i-usembep, reason, ig><ø-)
Ka eg-usembep iyan ka due igkasasew wey igkalaggew. Egkakita seini diyè te ulaula te etew ne dakel se prublima wey egkalangè ne egkahuhus dè due layun se ulaula tenged te pegkalanu.
Eg-usembep dè due layun si Ading su neenenduenan te inwali-wali ni Eteng.
Ading looks always sad because she is disappointed by Eteng's joke.

v.
for someone or something (egpekeusembep, nakapekeusembep, agent, egpAkA-) to make someone sad; or to be the thing (igpekeusembep, impekepakausembep/pigpekeusembep, reason, igpAkA-) that makes someone sad
Egpekeusembep a ke eg-engkeran a ni kuna beyi.
It makes me sad when you go away from me girl.
It would make me sad if you went away from me girl.
Iyan din igpakausembep su namatey kun ka me anak din diyè te abrud.
The reason that he's sad because his children in abroad were dead.
The thing that makes him sad is that his chidlren abroad are dead.
The cause of his sadness is that ....
