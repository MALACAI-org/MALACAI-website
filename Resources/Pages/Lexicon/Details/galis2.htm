galis2
/galis/
Data Qual: Tano does not know this meaning.
 
n.
a narrow strip of land
Sabeka seini ne kalasiyan te kamet ne egpamulaan te aheley. Malig-et dè ubag seini ne kamet.
Keilangan ne egpamulaan kud kuntee te aheley ka galis ku su mahaan e eg-uran.
My field must be planted with corn for the rain will come nowadays.
Cult. not usually ploughed; used to plant corn
Eng. land; field; farmland

Usage level: R
