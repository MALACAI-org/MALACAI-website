lamu
/lamu/
v_rt.
 
1.
vi.
for someone (eglamu-lamu, miglamu-lamu/inlamu-lamu, lamu-lamu, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to work slowly something (eglamu-lamuwen, inlamu-lamu/miglamu-lamu/piglamu-lamu, eglamu-lamuwan, inlamu-lamuwan/miglamu-lamuwan/piglamu-lamuwan, lamu-lamuwi, location, eg><en-rdpRT-, eg><an-rdpRT-)
Eng. slowly; time, take your; take your time; dawdle; delay; lazily; get around to it
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
v.
for someone (eglamu-lamu, miglamu-lamu/inlamu-lamu, lamu-lamu, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to waste time for working
Mania te eglamu-lamu ke rè due?
Why you are wasting time?
Eng. waste time; pass time
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
3.
v.
for someone (eglamu-lamu, miglamu-lamu/inlamu-lamu/piglamu-lamu, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to be lazy in working
Eng. lazy
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
4.
adj.
(egkalamu-lamu, egkA-rdpRT-) unconscious
Eng. aware, not; unconscious
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for someone (egpakalamu-lamu, nakalamu-lamu, actor, egpAkA-rdpRT-) that didn't know what to do because of something (igpakalamu-lamu, impakalamu-lamu, reason, igpAkA-)
Eng. unaware
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
