hakam
/hakam/
Var.1: akam
v_rt.
“selfish”
 
vi.
for someone (eggakam-hakam, actor, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to be selfish about something (eggakhakamen, theme, eg><en-rdpCVC-)
Wrd.Gloss: selfish
Amana iya eggakam-hakam si Eniyew te serè ne kenè din man ded iya man-e egkaamin.
Eggakhakamen ku ka pan ne imbeli te amey ku wey kenà a egpamehey.
Eng. selfish
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
adj.
(maakhakamen, maakam-hakamen, mA1-rdpCVC–en, mA1-rdpRT–en) selfish
Ka igpasabut te seini ne lalag iyan sika se leheren, ka kenè egpanalad te minsan nekey ne diyè te kandin.
Maakam-hakamen ne etew si Welu te minsan nekey ne kandin.
Welu is a selfish person, he will not share other what he had.
Eng. selfish
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
