ilud
/ʔilud/
v_rt.
“move”
 
vt.
for someone (eg-ilud, agent, eg><ø-) to move something (such as a piece of furniture) (eg-iluren, patient, eg><en-)
Wrd.Gloss: move
Eng. move; shift
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for someone (eg-ilud-ilud, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to walk on hands and feet with bottom down (i.e., one's hands behinds one's back)
Mekeyid-u-hid-u naan dè iya si Amè su eg-ilud ilud naan dè ke eggipanew su buyag e sikandin.
I feel sorry for my father walking on hands and feet for he was old now.
Eng. walk on hands and feet
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
