ayu2
/ʔayu/
v_rt.
 
v.
(eg-ay-ayuwen, eg><en-rdpCVC-) to be ridiculed
Ka eg-ay-ayuwen iyan ka eglegleihen tenged su egkalupihan wey egkaayu-ayu rè sikandin. Minsan nekey rè ka egkatamanan te etew ne eglegleihen wey layun egkasakitan, egkeyirapan tenged te igpeulaula kandin te egleig-leig kandin.
Amana a eg-ay-ayuwen te anggam ku puun su kenà a igkeupii nikandin.
My uncle ridiculed me so much because he doesn't like me.
Syn. leig
Eng. ridicule; mock; insult; scorn
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(kaayu-ayu, kA-rdpRT-) destitute
Ka egkaayu-ayu iyan ka keugpè te etew ne amana egkeyirapan su warè egkeyimu wey ke warè me ayam wey kasangkapan.
Puun te peglesut ku, egkaayu-ayu key en iya su warè egkeyimu ney.
We are really poor since I was borned because we have nothing to do.
Eng. destitute; poor; broke; bankrupt
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
