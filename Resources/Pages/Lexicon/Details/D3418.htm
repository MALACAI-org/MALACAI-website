lahet
/lagɔt/
 
v.
for someone (eglelahet, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to chew tobacco (iglahet, patient, ig-)
Wrd.Gloss: chew_tobacco
Naluhey key nekeyinguma te ugpaan ney su malasi nahud eglelahet ka apù ku due te igbayè ney.
We reached our place lately for my grandma always chew tobacco along the way.
Eng. chew tobacco; tobacco, chew; chewing tobacco
