dalem
/dalɔm/
 
1.
adj.
(mapariralemen, mA1-pA-di–en) patient
Meupiya ne etew si Huwan su mapariralemen sikandin.
Jonh is good man for he is a patient.
Mapariralemen ne etew si Husi su kenè sikandin ne mahaan egkabelu.
Jose is a patient man for he is not quick to be angry.
Eng. patient
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
adj.
(mapariralemen, mA1-pA-di–en) modest
Eng. humble; modest
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
