lebbubuk
/lɔbbubuk/
n.
“greenish_water”
 
n.
a pool of greenish colored water
Ka due lebubuk ne weyig iyan seeyè se weyig ne nasubung wey ke natalibubu ne weyig. Ka lebubuk iyan ka egpakamareet te weyig ne egkataman egkalebeg e ka weyig ne egmangareg e.
Mareet ka weyig ne due lebubuk su mangareg.
Water that has greenish particles has not good odor.
Wrd.Gloss: greenish_water
Eng. greenish water; water, greenish; water, stagnant; stagnant water; smelly water; algae in water
Data Qual: is the lebubuk the water or the algae? - seems like algae
Data Qual: the algae QDL
OCM: Water and Thirst (271)
DDP: Ocean, lake (1.3.1.1), Smell (2.3.4)
