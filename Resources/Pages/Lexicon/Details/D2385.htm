ihì
/ʔigiʔ/
“urine”
 
1.
n.
urine
Eng. urine
Tag. íhì
Ceb. ihì
Ilo.: ísbu
OCM: Ethnophysiology (827)
DDP: Urinate, urine (2.2.7)

n.
(ihian, -an) urinary bladder

Ceb. pantog
OCM: Ethnozoology (825), Ethnoanatomy (826)

(ihianan, -an-an) comfort room
Eng. comfort room
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
3.
vi.
for someone (eg-ihì, mig-ihì/in-ihì, agent, eg><ø-) to urinate somewhere (eg-ihian, in-ihian/mig-ihian/pig-ihian, location, eg><an-)
