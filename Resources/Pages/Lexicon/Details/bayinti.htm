bayinti
/bainti/
Var.1: beyinti
Var. binti
 
num.
twenty
Duen ma bayinti nu, piru mania te warè ka migbeli te asukal?
You had twenty pesos, but why didn't you buy sugar?
Cult. The Matigsalug forms of numbers over ten are only used by older people. Younger generations use the Spanish forms.
Syn. daruwampulù
Syn. daruwa_ne_pulù
Eng. 20 (Spanish); twenty (Spanish)
Ceb. bayenyte; baynte
OCM: Numeration (802)
DDP: Cardinal numbers (8.1.1.1)

Usage level: M
