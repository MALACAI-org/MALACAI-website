1.
v.
for someone (egpanaha, migpanaha/impanaha, agent, eg><ø-paN-) to ask someone else (egpanahaan, egpanaha:n, impanahaan, impanaha:n/migpanahaan, migpanaha:n/pigpanahaan, pigpanaha:n, eg><an-paN-) to give a message to someone else (igpanaha, impanaha, theme, ig><ø-)
Egpanaha a te keykew ne egpeulien ku ka hari ku diyè te Panganan.
I will ask you to inform my brother there in Panganan to go home.
Impanahaan a te anak ku ne egpabeliyen a te paan.
My niece asked me to buy bread (to brought).
Eng. inform; send message; message, send

Usage level: M
