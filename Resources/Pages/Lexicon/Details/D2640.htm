ka
/ka/
Var. ke
pron.
“2s_F”
 
pron.
you(sg)
Wrd.Gloss: you(sg)
Ka kaluwasan te seini ne lalag iyan ka igngaran te sabeka ne etew ne egguhuran te sabeka ne etew ne egpangguhud meyitenged kandin. Seini ka igngaran mesì egpangguhud ka sabeka ne etew meyitenged te etew ne egguhuran din ded wey kenè egngaranan te egpangguhud ka etew ne egguhuran din ne sikan ded iya man-e ne etew.
Sikeddiey e ka eg-uyan te seleppì ne igbehey nu te amey ke kenè ka egpekeulì diyè te kaniyu.
I am the one who will bring the money that you will give to your father if you can't go back to your place.
Kenè ka egpamakey ki Meibulan su kenè ne sikandin ka Manama nu.
Don't (you) worship Meibulan because he is not your God.
Gramm. 2nd person singular focus pronoun (Set 1, brief)
Gramm. In imperative sentences, the pronoun is always given (see example sentence 2)
Eng. you sg (focus)
Tag. ka; ikaw
Ceb. ka; ikaw

DDP: Pronouns (9.2.3)
