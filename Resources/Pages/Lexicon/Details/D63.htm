agkap
/ʔagkap/
adj_rt.
“lightweight”
 
1.
adj.
(maagkap, mA1-) lightweight
Maagkap iyan sika se minsan nekey ne ware amana kabehat.Iling te kayu ne nahangu e,manggad ne nahangu e wey gapas. Kenè ne mabehat.
Maagkap ka atsal minsan sabeka ne saku su migkahung ma ka elat.
The green pepper(s) are lightweight even though there's one sack, because they make a hollow sound in the middle.
Ant. mabehat —heavy
Eng. light in weight; lightweight; light
Tag. magaán
Ceb. gaan
OCM: Weights and Measures (804)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(maagkap_se_ulaula) happy (lit. light-faced)
Maagkap se ulaula ku kuntee su nasulbad e ka prublima ku.
I am happy (lit. light-faced) today because my problem is already solved.
Eng. happy; cheery; cheerful; light-hearted
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Happy (3.4.1.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: MI
