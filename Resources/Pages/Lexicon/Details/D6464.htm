tapil
/tapil/
 
1.
n.
a birthmark
Ka seini egkeiling te ila ne ligkat dè te peg-anak keykew minsan hendei egkakita wey bayew rè eglahem seini.
Eng. birthmark; mark, birth
OCM: Ethnoanatomy (826)
DDP: Birth (2.6.3), Skin (2.1.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
v.
for someone (egtapil, migtapil/intapil, tapil, agent, eg><ø-) to put viand over a share food (egtampilan, intampilan/migtampilan/pigtampilan, tampili, location, eg><an-)
Eng. put, to (viand)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
3.
v.
for a woman (egpakatapil, nakatapil, actor, egpAkA-) to die in childbirth
Ka seini iyan sika se eglesutan ka malitan ne egpatey ka batè wey iling te due egkalenew ne egkabunbunan ka wey ke egpakatapik ka ne egpatey ka iyan ded iya seini ne lalag.
cf. takin
Eng. die in childbirth; childbirth, die in
DDP: Die (2.6.6)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
