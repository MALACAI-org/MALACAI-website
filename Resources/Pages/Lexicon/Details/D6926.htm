tulin
/tulin/
v_rt.
“grow”
 
1.
adj.
(matulin, mA1–en) prosperous
Matulin ne iyam ne leg-un.
Prosperous new year.
Eng. prosperous
DDP: Grow, get bigger (2.6.4.6)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone or something (egtulin, migtulin/intulin, tulin, agent, eg><ø-) to grow
Ka egtulin iyan sika se pegdakel, pegkahalin te karakel, pegmalayat.
Igbelegyè kud e ka babuy ku su kenè amana egtulin.
I will sell my pig for it doesn't grow well.
Eng. grow
DDP: Grow, get bigger (2.6.4.6)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpatulin, migpatulin/impatulin, patulin, agent, eg><ø-pA-) raised, as a parent raised a child (egpatulinen, impatulin/migpatulin/pigpatulin, patulina, patient, eg><en-pA-)
Migpatulin key dengan te babuy taman te impameligyè ney e.
We raise swine before until such time we sold it.
Eng. raised
DDP: Grow, get bigger (2.6.4.6)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone or something (egpantulin, migpantulin/impantulin, pantulin, agent, eg><ø-paN-) to be individually growing
Eng. growing individually
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(igpatulin, ig><ø-) a rising agent
Eng. yeast; fertilizer
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
v.
for something (egtulin, migtulin/intulin, tulin, theme, eg><ø-) to increase (e.g., weight etc.)
Eng. increase, to
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
