patey
/patɔy/
v_rt.
“die”
 
v.
for someone (egpatey, migpatey/impatey, agent, eg><ø-) to die
Migpatey e sikandin gabii.
She was died yesterday.
Morpho. ma-?egka-?
Syn. peled
Syn. engked
Cult. When one dies then the real person goes. What is left is just the empty shell (umpak, lit. shirt) of the person.
Eng. die
Tag. mamatáy
Ilo.: natáy
OCM: Dying (763)
DDP: Die (2.6.6)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for someone (egpakapatey, nakapatey, agent, egpAkA-) to kill someone else
Si keddi egpakapatey te eleggasi.
I am able to kill a giant.
Eng. kill, to ( someone)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egkapatey, napatey, agent, egkA><ø-) to be died
Hendue te egkapatey ad.
It seems I am dying.
Eng. dies, to be
DDP: Die (2.6.6)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
