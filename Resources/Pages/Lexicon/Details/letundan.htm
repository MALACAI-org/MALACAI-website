letundan
/lɔtundan/
n.
“lotundan_banana”
 
n.
Musa_spp.
a (light yellow) type of banana
Wrd.Gloss: lotundan_banana
Cult. 6 in. long important commercially eaten uncooked
Ka letundan iyan iyan ka sahing ne bayew mallepet se lawa wey bayew mallapung se ulid. Kenè amana ne mallayat wey derakel se behas kayi wey kenè egkaayun ne egsubbeen ke meyilew pad, piru amana seini ne eemisi ka egkeyin-hinuhan e. Sabeka degma seini te me sahing ne egbeliyen wey igpamelegyè diyè te palingkiyan.
Amana ne dakel ka pinamula ney ne letundan su eyew egpakaseleppì key.
We plant many lotundan banana so that we can invest money.
Eng. banana variety
Tag. latundán
Ceb. alitundan; tundan
OCM: Flora (137)
