baley-baley
/baley-baley/
 
v.
for someone (egbaley-baley, migbaley-baley/imbaley-baley, baley-baley, agent, eg><ø-rdpCVC-) to pretend as in playing with a toy
Ka baley-baley iyan igpasabut ka peg-ubal te minsan nekey wey ke minsan hentew naa. Mareet te Matigsalug ka egbaley-baley su amana ne egpekeugseb ka kasakit. Seini degma ka egpuunan ne masulug ka egkasalè te dengan ne timpu su warè nakanengneng sikandan ne subla ne kenè ne meupiya ne eggimuwen seini.
Egbaley-baley si Jayham te terak din diyè te kelesadda eyew egkahale sikandin.
Jayham playing his toy car in the road so that he can enjoy.
Imbaley-baley ni Jeson ka buyag sikan naa ka nabelù.
Cause to angry the old person because Jeson making him fun.
Eng. play; fun
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
n.
(baley-baley, rdpCVC–en) a toy house
Meupiya ka baley-baley ney dengan su egpekeiling te malehet ne baley.
Our toy house before was nice because it's look like real house.
Kenè kew baley-baley te liwati su eg-anit.
Don't make+fun of earthworm because (it) will offend ani deity.
Eng. toy house; doll's house; house, doll's

Usage level: R
