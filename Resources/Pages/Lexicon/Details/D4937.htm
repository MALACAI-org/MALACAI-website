paras2
/paras/
 
n.
grapevine
Ka paras iyan ka ngaran te pinamula ne egpekeiling te ubì ka lawa ne egpangahapkap degma. Piru meilem ka dewun din. Mallibusen ka behas kayi wey egkatalibubu ne hendue karakel te behas te tebbuwè. Ne meilem pad ka behas kayi emun ke meyilew pad ne emun ke egkeyinuhan e, egmalugtem e. Meemis seini ne egkeinen wey egkaayun man-e ne eg-inumen ka weyig ne egkakeles puun kayi.
Kasabeke e pad iya nakakeen te behas te paras diyè te melikanu.
I have only eaten a fruit of grapevine once in the American.
Warè e rè due migkita te pinamula ne paras kayi te Pilipinas.
I have never seen a grapevine plant here in the Philippines.
Kuneen ku dengan ne egpekeiling te kayu ka paras, kunaleg te egpekeiling bes te manika ne egpangahapkap.
I thought before that the grapevine is like a tree, but rather it is alike to a betel which is to climb.
Suled migkita kad e te egngaranan te paras?
Brother have you seen of what is called grapevine?
