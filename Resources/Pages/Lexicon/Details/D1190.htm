bunsud
/bunsud/
v_rt.
“begin”
 
1.
v.
for someone (egbunsud, migbunsud/imbunsud, agent, eg><ø-) to begin something (egbunsuran, im bunsuran/migbunsuran/pigbunsuran, location, eg><an-)
Egbunsud kid e.
Let's begin.
Eng. begin, to; start, to
Tag. simulâ
Ilo.: rubbuat
OCM: Labor and Leisure (461)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(ipabunsuran, i-pA–an) the foundation stones of a house
Si Hisus ka pabunsuran te kanta ne pegpalintutuu su sikandin ka migluwas kanta.
Jesus is the foundation of our faith because he saves us.
Eng. foundation; stone
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(bunsuranan, -an-an) topic; title
Syn. ulu
Eng. topic; title
Ceb. tima
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(pegbunsud, peg><ø-) elementary
Eng. elementary
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(bunsuranan, -an-an) a capital letter
Eng. capital; upper case
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egbunsud-bunsud, migbunsud-bunsud/imbunsud-bunsud, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to begin a process
Si Runy egbunsud-bunsud egkankanakan.
Runy is becoming a man (i.e., in puberty).
Eng. puberty
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
n.
(pabunsuran, pA-rdpCV-) the foot of the stair
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se natahuan te paa te haheran.
Ka pabunsuran te haheran nu su basaken.
The foot of your stair is muddy.
Eng. foot stair
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
