gimun
/gimun/
v_rt.
“grin”
 
v.
for someone (eggimun-gimun, miggimun-gimun/inggimun-gimun, gimun-gimun, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to grin (with the lips tightly closed) (eggimun-gimunan, miggimun-gimunan/inggimun-gimunan/piggimun-gimunan, location, eg><an-rdpRT-)
Eggimun-gimun si Bibing keddì.
Bibing is grinning at me.
Eng. grin; smile; smirk
DDP: Mouth (2.1.1.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(mahimun-gimun, mA1-rdpRT-) smirky
Eng. smirky
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
