tugbung
/tugbuŋ/
n.
“bud/shoot”
 
n.
bud or shoot (of a plant)
Kuwa kew pa te dewun te sahing su igtenges ta te bewu ta piru kenè niyu rè kua ka tugbung su egkabehang.
Kindly get leaves of banana to wrap our food but then don't get the center one which is not yet open to be not stunted in growth.
Syn. sugpang
Syn. tubù
Eng. bud; shoot
Tag. súpang
OCM: Ethnobotany (824)
DDP: Growth of plants (1.5.6), Parts of a plant (1.5.5)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for a plant or tree (egpanugbung, migpanugbung/impanugbung, panugbung, agent, eg><ø-paN-) to shoot (leaves)
Egpanugbung e man-e ka sahing ne natamtamanan nakalamahan.
The banana which destroyed by the wind is shoot again.
Eng. shoot

Usage level: M
