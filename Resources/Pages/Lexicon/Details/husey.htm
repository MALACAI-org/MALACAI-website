husey
/husɔy/
v_rt.
“judge”
 
v.
for a leader (such as a datù) (eggusey, miggusey/inggusey, agent, eg><ø-) to resolve a case usually by specifying the payment of a peace offering (eggusayen, inggusey/miggusey/piggusey, eg><en-)
Eggusey ka huwis te etew ne nakasalè.
The judge settle case of the accused.
Te Matigsalug ka datu ka eggusey.
The Datu of Matigsalug tribe is the responsible to resolve the cases.
Eng. peace offering; settle case; resolve case; go-between; mediate; reconcile
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpekewusey, nekewusey, agent, egpAkA-) to be able settle some cases
Eng. settle, able to be
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for t he case (egkewusey, newusey, egkA><ø-) to be settled or resolved
Newusey e ka nakasalè.
The suspected was already settled.
Eng. settled, to be; resolved, to be
