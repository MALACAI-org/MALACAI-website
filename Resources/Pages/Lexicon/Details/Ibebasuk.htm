Ibebasuk
/ʔibɔbasuk/
 
n.
a good spirit; a benevolent deity responsible for rewarding hard workers (mabasuk)
Ka ibebasuk sabeka ne paninisingan ne eg-umawen te me kabuybuyahan ke egpanubad sikandan eyew kun egmabasuk sikandan su iyan ma egbikal te egbasuk seeni.
Emun ke egpangamet key e malasi eg-umawa ni Amè ka Ibebasuk eyew kun kenè egkangapehulan ka egmangamet.
When we were slashing and burning father is often calling the spirit of working Ibebasuk for we were not be tired and lazy.
Cult. A spirit that leads as to be a hard working.
Eng. Spirit working
