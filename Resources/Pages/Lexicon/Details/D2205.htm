hey
/hɔy/
 
ij.
hey
Ka lalag ne hey iyan ka igsaliyu te ngaran te etew emun ke eg-umawen eyew kenè egkalueran. Ig-umew degma seini te sabeka ne etew ne warè natueni ke nekey ka ngaran din.
Hey, mania te mig-indes ka rue te dalan?
Hey, why you have defecated at the road?
Hey! Hentew kaa?
Hey! Who are you?
Hey, henduen ma mulè te sikeykew ka migsumbag dengan keddì?
Hey, it seems that it’s you who struck me long ago?
Eng. hey; hi; hello
Ceb. hoy
Tag. hoy
