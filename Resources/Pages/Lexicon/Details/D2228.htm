hilew1
/hilɔw/
adj_rt.
“immature”
 
adj.
(meyilew, mA1-) (for a plant product such as rattan) to be immature
Egkeibeg sikandin te meyilew ne mangga su egpangalalin sikandin.
She wanted to eat an unripe mango because she was pregnant
Eng. immature; green; unripe; raw?
Tag. hiláw
Ceb. hilaw
Ilo.: naáta
OCM: Ethnobotany (824)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
