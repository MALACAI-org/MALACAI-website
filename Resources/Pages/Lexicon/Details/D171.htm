alimetew
/ʔalimɔtɔw/
v_rt.
“miss_someone”
 
v.
for someone (egkaalimetew, naalimetew, agent, egkA><ø-) to miss someone who is dead or whose whereabouts are unknown (igkaalimetew, ingkaalimetew, reason, igkA><ø-)
Egkaalimetew a te apù ku ne migpatey dengan, sikan naa ne egpakasinsinehew e rè due.
I miss my grandfather who died along ago, that's the reason I can just cry.
Wrd.Gloss: miss_someone
Eng. miss; lonely
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Lonely (3.4.2.1.5)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something (egpakaalimetew, nakaalimetew, agent, egpAkA-) that appear which can make someone to miss or able by someone to recall (igpakaalimetew, impakaalimetew, reason, igpAkA-)
Eng. recall, able; miss, can
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
