yaman
/yaman/
adj_rt.
“rich”
 
adj.
(meyaman, mA1-) rich
Iyan seini ka klasi te etew ne dakel se karatuan, tanè, ayam, nigusyu wey keuyahan.
Titimud kew te karatuan diyè te langit su kenè kew ne meyaman ke kayi kew egtitimud te tanè.
Save your wealth in heaven for you cannot be rich if you accumulate wealth here on earth.
Ka meyaman dè ka egkasunged te matubung wey meupiya ne peg-ugpè su egkaangey ran ka langun.
Only the rich people have the chance to live an abundance and better living for they can afford everything.
O. Syn. datù
Datù amana ka sumbaley ney su due me terak din.
Our neighbor is very rich for he have a trucks.
Eng. rich; wealthy; prosperous
