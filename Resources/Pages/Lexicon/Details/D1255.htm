buyù
/buyuʔ/
v_rt.
“ask_for”
 
v.
for someone (egbuyù, migbuyù/imbuyù, agent, eg><ø-) to ask to be given something (egbuyuen, imbuyù/migbuyù/pigbuyù, theme, eg><en-)
Egkaayun ne egbuyù a te lawa te binggala su eyew due igpamula ku?
Can I ask for the cassava trunk so that I have something to plant?
Eng. ask for; request; beg
Tag. makiúsap
Ceb. mohangyù
Ilo.: dawáten
WBM: buyù
OCM: Non-electronic Transmission of Messages (202), Acquisition and Relinquishment of Property (425), Gift Giving (431)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for something (egkabuyù, nabuyù, theme, egkA><ø-) to be asked by someone
Eng. asked, to be
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
adj.
(buyuen, -en) always asking something to someone
Eng. always asking
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
