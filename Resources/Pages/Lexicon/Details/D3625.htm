lebud
/lɔbud/
adj_rt.
“fragile”
 
1.
adj.
(malebud, mA1-) easy to break (e.g. a rope)
Iyan kaluwasan te malebud ka mewumelwumel ne minsan nekey iling te kayu, lubid wey duma pad ne mahaan egkatepù, egkahepù wey egkasagpì.
Malebud ne kayu ka alinguwug.
Alinguweg tree is easy to break.
Eng. easy to break; weak (soft things or easy to break); fragile; brittle; immature
DDP: Weak (2.4.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(malebud-lebud, mA1-rdpRT-) flabby as in flabby muscles
Eng. flabby
OCM: Ethnoanatomy (826)
DDP: Flesh (2.1.7)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
adj.
(malebud, mA1-) the people living in this world
Eng. people; living
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
