hentew
/hɔntɔw/
Var.1: entew
pron.
“who”
 
pron.
who
Wrd.Gloss: who
Gramm. identificational interrogative pronoun
Hentew-a me ballas kayi?
Whose are these (sacks of) rice?
Hentew ka egkeupian ne egpatey meyitenged ki Hisus?
Who wants to die because of Jesus?
Eng. whose
Tag. sino
Ceb. kinsa; hain kanila
Ilo.: sinó; asín; asino
WBM: henteʔi
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Question words (9.2.3.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
