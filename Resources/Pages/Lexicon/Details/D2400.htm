ila
/ʔila/
v_rt.
“recognize”
 
1.
n.
a freckle or black mark (but not a lump) on the body
Neupian a ki Dunya Mahal Mariya su due ila rin lenged te bibig din.
I like Princess Maria because she has a freckle near to her lips.
Eng. freckle; mole; mark on skin; recognizable mark
Tag. táling
Ceb. alom
Ilo.: síding
OCM: Ethnoanatomy (826)
DDP: Skin (2.1.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
2.
vi.
for a person or animal (egkeila, neila, actor, egkA><ø-) to be recognized of someone else
Neila ku sikandin.
I know him.
Eng. know someone; familiar; recognized, to be
Tag. mákilála
Ceb. makaila
Ilo.: malásin
OCM: Sensation and Perception (151), Social Relationships and Groups (571), Ethnopsychology (828)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
vt.
for someone (eg-ila, mig-ila/in-ila, agent, eg><ø-) to recognize somebody or something in somebody (eg-ileen, in-ila/mig-ila/pig-ila, theme, eg><en-) e.g., to recognize that someone is pregnant, or angry, etc.
Pron.: eg-ile:n
Eg-ila ubag ka batè te iney rin minsan deisek pad.
The baby can recognize her mother even she's still
Eng. recognize; discern; determine; acknowledge
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
3.
adj.
(meila, mA1-) afraid of humans (of an animal)
Meila ka manuk ne mahintalunan wey malantus ne eglayang.
The wild chicken is untamed and can fly high.
Ant. maamuk
Eng. untamed; unfamiliar; elusive; unfriendly; scared; unused to people
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egmeila, migmeila/immeila, agent, eg-mA1-) to become afraid of human
Eng. afraid, to become
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
adj.
(inila, <in>-) notable
Eng. notable
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
