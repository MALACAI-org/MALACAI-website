begkul
/bɔgkul/
v_rt.
crooked; bend
Ka begkung ne lalag iyan igpasabut ka kenè ne matul-id. Eggamiten seini ne lalag eyew igngaran te kayu wey ke minsan nekey naa ne kenè ne matul-id. Kaayun degma seini ne eggamiten eyew egpeiling te etew ne mareet se eggimuwen.
Kene ne meupiya ne egtukuren seini ne kayu su nabegkung.
Not good this tree to make as post because it is curve.
Egpekeiling ka etew ne mareet se eggimuwen te kayu ne nabegkung.
Man who did evil is liking a tree which is bending.
Eng. curve; bend over; hunch back; turn back; crooked; bend
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for something, e.g., a tree or a piece of wood (egkabegkul, nabegkul, patient, egkA><ø-) to be crooked
Kenè niyu pamaneyiki ka beyabas su egkabegkul.
Don't climb the guava because it 's going to be crooked.
O. Syn. pekù
Eng. crooked; bent
Tag. kilô; likô; balantík; kurbado; balukt't; balikukô; likú-likô
Ceb. balikô; balikóg
Ilo.: likko; bakkúg
OCM: Sensation and Perception (151)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egbegkul, migbegkul/imbegkul, agent, eg><ø-) to crook or bend (egbegkulen, imbegbul/migbegkul/pigbegkul, patient, eg><en-)
Eng. crook, to; bend, to
