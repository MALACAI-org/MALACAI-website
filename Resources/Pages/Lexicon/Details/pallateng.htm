pallateng
/pallatɔŋ/
v_rt.
“hope/expect”
 
v.
for someone (egpallateng, agent, eg><ø-) to look forward hopefully to getting something (egpallatengen, theme, eg><en-)
Taleinum ka amey ta, purisu kenè kew e egpallateng ne duen pad egkeuyan din te eg-ulì puun te pegbelegyè din te aheley.
Our father is drunkard, therefore don't expect that he will bring something for home from selling corn.
Egpallateng e kuntee ka amey ku ne egginguma a.
Eng. expect; anticipate; look forward; hope
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Want (3.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
