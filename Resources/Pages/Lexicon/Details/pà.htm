pà
/paʔ/
part.
“INCOMPL”
 
part.
still; yet
Wrd.Gloss: still
Behayi key pà te sabeka ne putus ne paan eyew egkaatag key kayi.
Kindly give us one pack of bread so that we could have even share here.
Ka palespes dè pelera niyu. Ka derakel, kenè niyu pà pelera su egkatamì ka kinamut.
Just chop down the slender trees. Don't chop down the big ones because the cleared area will get flattened.
Gramm. incompletive aspectual adjunct. Usually occurs in imperative sentences
Eng. still; yet; else; in addition to; future
Tag. pa
Ceb. pa
WBM: pa
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Particles (9.2.6)

part.
(pad, -d) still; yet [incompletive particle]
Kayi e pad taman te alas singku.
I'll still be here until five o'clock.
Duen pad senge gatus ne seleppì ku.
I have still one hundred [pesos].
Egmalig-eten din pad te egkamet.
He will clear a narrower still strip of the field.
Seini e pad nakakita te aheley ne egngaranan te Bigtawan!
This is my first time to see the corn named Bigtawan!
Gramm. incompletive aspectual adjunct. Occurs in non-imperative sentences
Eng. still; yet; else; in addition to; future; will
DDP: Particles (9.2.6)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
