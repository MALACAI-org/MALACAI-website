luhey
/lugɔy/
v_rt.
“be_a_long_time”
 
adj.
(egkaluhey, naluhey, egkA><ø-) a long time
Naluhey man e mulè seeyè.
Those took long time enough.
Eng. long time
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
adj.
(kaluheyan, kA><an-) how long takes something
Eng. how long
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
adj.
(paluhey-luhey, pA-rdpRT-) stay long
Syn. naney
Syn. langan
Eng. stay long
OCM: Weights and Measures (804)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for two people (egpekegluhayey, nekegluhayey, egpekeg><ey-) to be a long time at doing something
Eng. long time
