tipu
/tipu/
v_rt.
“dissuade”
Data Qual: Do we need to add a variant with two p's? [RJH 1Jul16]
 
v.
for someone (egtipu, agent, eg><ø-) to dissuade someone else (egtipuwen, theme, eg><en-)
Ka egtipu iyan ka ngaran te egbahet wey egpanulu te duma ke nekey ka eggimuwen eyew egkapangabangan sikandan. Migpasabut seini te pegpulihid te duma wey pegtambag kandan te eggimuwen.
Kenè ka panengneng ki Maning ne egpabulig ka te egkamet su kema ke egtipuwen din ka etew.
Don't inform Maning that you will cause men to help you cleaning an area for he might dissuade them.
Eng. dissuade; tell tales; convince
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
(intipu) persuade someone else
Intipu te me talagpanubad ka me etew ne iyan igpalihawang te purisuwan si Barabas su egkeupian sikandan ne egkeyimatayan si Hisus.
Tne priests persuaded the people that Barabas should be sent out from prison for they wanted Jesus to be killed.
Eng. persuade; tell tales; convince
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
