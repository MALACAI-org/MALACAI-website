litratu
/litratu/
 
n.
picture
Ka litratu iyan ka ulaula ne diyè egkakita diyè te pepil ne egpareketan te ulaula pegkapenga kayi te eglitratu. Egkaayunan kayi ka minsan nekey ne ulaula ne eg-ilingan wey eglitratuwan wey iggalin e diyè te me pepil, kayu, putew, plastik, silupin wey minsan nekey pad ne egkaayun ne egpareketan.
Mareet e naan dè iya ne egtengtengan diyè te litratu ku dengan su dakel ka getek ku.
I am not good looking in my recent picture because my belly is big.
Seini bes ded iya ka litratu te apù ku ne dengan ku rè egpamitawa!
Oh here is the photo of my grandpa/grandma which I have been looking for long time!
Eng. picture; photo; image; image copy
Tag. larawan
