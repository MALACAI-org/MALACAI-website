duampulu
/duawampulu/
 
n.
(daruwampulu, rdpCV-) twenty
Ka daruwa ne pulù iyan seini ka lalag meyitenged te numiru ne migsinundulan te sapulu wey siyew, wey nakasinundul man-e kayi ka numiru ne daruwa ne pulù wey sabeka.
Te daruwampulù pad ka idad ku amana a ne maagsik-agsik.
I'm very smart when I was twenty years old.
Eng. twenty
Tag. dalawampú
Ceb. kawhaan
Ilo.: duapúlo
OCM: Numeration (802)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
