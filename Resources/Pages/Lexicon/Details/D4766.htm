palù
/paluʔ/
 
v.
for someone (egpepalù, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to beat something (egpaluen, theme, eg><en-) with a stick (andu) (igpalù, instrument, ig-) on a hard surface (egpaluan, location, eg><an-)
Awè kew duè te dulug din su egpaluan ku pad ka egbales.
Eng. beat; pound
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpepalù, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to play the bankakawan drum by pounding on it with a pestle
Iyan ku egkateltelesi te egpitew te egbebangkakew ka etew ne egpepalu te kayu su egkangidsiyan.
I am pleased watching the bangkakew dance with the man beating the log for he was funny.
Wrd.Gloss: beat
Eng. beat; drum
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
