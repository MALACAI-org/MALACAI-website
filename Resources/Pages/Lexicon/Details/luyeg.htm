luyeg
/luyɔg/
 
n.
weak
Ka luyeg iyan igpasabut se kenè egpekeeleg. Kenè egpekeeleg ne egtiang, kenè egpekeeleg ne egbaba wey ke eg-utuk naa. Etew rè wey ayam ka egkaayun ne eggamitan seini ne lalag. Ne egluyeg ka etew ke egderalu sikandin, due prublima, warè migkeen wey duma.
Amana ne luyeg si Rani su kenè egpekeeleg te tengè dè te saku ne aheley.
Rani is very feeble for he is not able to carry even a half sack of corn.
Luyeg ka kalabew enni Jony su kenè egpekeeleg ne egdaru.
Jony's carabao is weak because it's not able to plow.
Eng. puny; feeble; weedy; frail; fragile; weak
Tag. mahina
Ceb. huyang
