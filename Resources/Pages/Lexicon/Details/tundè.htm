tundè
/tundɔʔ/
“point”
 
v.
for someone (egtundè, agent, eg><ø-)
Ka egtundè iyan ka eg-alam te minsan hentew eyew due igpeyimu wey igpeula-ula iling te sabeka ne etew ne eggimuwen ne pangulu wey ke talewusey naa te sabeka ne punduk te etew.
Intundè si Gawilan ne egkeyimu ne mabantug ne igbuyag te me Matigsalug.
Gawilan was appointed to be a great leader of Matigsalug people.
Intundè te me amey wey iney ne eggimuwen ne pangulu si Tutu diyè te iskuylaan te ilimintarya.
Tutu was chosen by the parents to be the president there in the elementary school.
Eng. choose; chose; appoint; select; elect
Ceb. pili
Tag. hirang
