sebu
/sɔbu/
n.
“tempered_edge”
 
n.
a tempered edge on a steel instrument
Wrd.Gloss: tempered_edge
Ka ugpit ne warè sebu, mareet su mahaan egkabingawi wey kenè ne mahalang, piru ke due sebu, mahalang amana.
The bolo that doesn't have a tempered edge is bad because it is easily bluntened and isn't sharp, but if it has a tempered edge, it is every sharp.
Eng. hard edge; tempered edge
