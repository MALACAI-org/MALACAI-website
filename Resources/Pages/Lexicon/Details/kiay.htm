kiay
/kiay/
 
v.
for someone (egkiay-kiay, migkiay-kiay, kiay, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to sway
Ka kiay iyan igpasabut ka pegweil te piyus wey ke pegtileng te piyus.
Mateles ne egkiay-kiay si Anggam su mewumel ka piyus din.
Uncle is good at swaying along because his buttocks is soft.
Makehal ka piyus, sikan naa ka kena a egkateu ne egkiay-kiay.
My buttock is stiff, that is why I cannot able to swaying+along.
Eng. sway; shimmy
Ceb. kiay
