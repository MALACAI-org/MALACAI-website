timelew
/timɔlɔw/
 
adj.
a whole part of something
Iyan seini ka pegtimman dè due te minsan nekey wey kenad egbaaren.
Syn. timman
Eng. whole
Tag. buu
Ceb. tibuuk
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egtimelew, migtimelew/intimelew, timelew, agent, eg><ø-) to prepare something without chopping ( esp. edible animal ) (egtimelawen, intimelew/migtimelew/pigtimelew, timulewa, patient, eg><en-)
Ka egtimelew iyan ka pegkeen te egkatimman dè due iling te gulay wey me ngalap ne kenad eggilutuen wey egpanggetaren.
Egtimelawen dè due te amey ku te egkeen ka benisil.
My father is eating raw hot pepper.
Syn. egtimmanen
Eng. whole; raw
Tag. buu
Ceb. tibuuk
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
1.
v.
for an evil spirit ( e.g witch ) (egtimelew, migtimelew/intimelew, timelew, agent, eg><ø-) to eat uncook animals without chopping ( e.g swallowing ) (egtimelawen, intimelew/migtimelew/pigtimelew, timelew, patient, eg><en-)
Kahiyen te amey ku, ka busew egkeen kanta ne egtimelawen pinaahi te peg-ameluk.
According to my father, witch wiil eat us as a whole by swallowing.
Syn. egtimmanen
Eng. whole
Tag. buu
Ceb. tibuuk

Usage level: M
