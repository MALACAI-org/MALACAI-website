ula
/ʔula/
n.
“way”
 
n.
(ula-ula, rdpRT-) custom
Wrd.Gloss: custom
Melehen se kanami ne ula-ula te Matigsalug.
Our Matigsalug customs are difficult.
O. Syn. batasan
Eng. custom; habit; way; system; history
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for someone or something (egpekeula-ula, nekeula-ula, actor, egpAkA-rdpRT-) to experience their destiny (most often negative)
Eng. experience; outcome; events; destiny; fate; history; future; happening; situation; circumstances; result
Ceb. kaagi
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(egkeula-ula, egkA><ø-rdpRT-) experience
Eng. experience
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for someone or something (eg-ul-uleen, in-ula-ula/mig-ula-ula/pig-ula-ula, actor, eg><ø-) lifestyle
Ka eg-ul-uleen iyan ka himu ne layun eggimuwa wey nabatasan e wey igkeupii e ne eggimuwen. Kaayunan kayi ka meupiya wey ke mareet naa ne eggimuwen te etew wey ke mananap naa.
Mareet dengan ka eg-ul-uleen te amey ku iya dè eggimuwen ka eg-iinum.
The bad habit of my father before is drinking liquor.
Kenè nu ipabulus ka eg-ul-uleen te amey nu ne maligsem su pakarereet sika te kabuhalan ta.
Don't continue the dirty lifestyle of your father because it will ruin our family (lit. descendant).
Eng. experience; habit; use to; accustom; behavior
Ceb. binuhatan
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
