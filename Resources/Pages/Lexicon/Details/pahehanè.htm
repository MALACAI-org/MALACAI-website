pahehanè
/pahehaneʔ/
 
v.
(egpahehane, migpahehane, pahehane, eg><ø-) the one who make magic and to command someone to make something
Ka pahehane ne lalag iyan igpasabut ka etew ne egpamulingan, egbanasal te pegkeen, egbasanal te pegkamet, egbanasal te kenè egkatanud te duma. Ka egpahehane due tatelu ne klasi: 1. kene egkeen 2. egpanlekat te naluhey e ne lebeng 3. panalepeng. Iyan igpasabut te panalepeng ka etew ne egbanasal te egsabeka ka langun ne etew. 4. egpatey ne eg-uli ded. Sabeka ne etew ne amana ne neilaan n
Due sabeka ne etew ne egpahehane ne egngaranan ki Duyan, mig-ugpè diyè te Dulag-Ayu ne sakup te Arakan Cotabato. Iyan din eggimuwen ka egtetamuk, egpamehey te kebel wey egnigusyu wey kene egkeutengan te lambus.
There is someone that make magic(see MS definition) whose name is Duyan, live in Dulag-Ayu belong to Arakan Cotabato. His work is to trade for animals, cause to give invulnerability.
Eng. magic(see MS definition)
