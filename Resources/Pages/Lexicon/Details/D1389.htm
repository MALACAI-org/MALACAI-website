dasig
/dasig/
v_rt.
“encourage”
 
1.
v.
for someone (e.g. a pastor) (egdasig, migdasig/indasig, agent, eg><ø-) to encourage others (e.g. church members) (egdasihen, indasig/migdasig/pigdasig, patient, eg><en-)
Wrd.Gloss: encourage
Egdasig a te me duma ku su kenad e egpanimba.
I'll encourage my companions because they don't go to church anymore.
Egdasihen ta ka tagsè sabeka kanta su eyew kenè ki egkapeled te kanta ne pegpamakey.
We encourage to everyone of us so that we can't fall over to serve the lord.
Eng. encourage
DDP: Confident (3.4.1.5)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
v.
for someone (egpadasig, migpadasig/impadasig, agent, eg><ø-pA-) to ak someone (igpadasig, impadasig, recipient, ig-pA-) for encouragement
Eng. request; encouragement
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L,B
 
v.
for someone (egkadasig, nadasig, agent, egkA><ø-) to be encouraged by someone or something (e.g. a good situation) (igkadasig, ingkadasig, instrument, igkA><ø-)
Ingkadasig din ka iyam ne himu rin su meupiya.
He is encouraged by his new work because it's good.
Eng. encouraged
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
v.
for someone or something (egpakadasig, nakadasig, agent, egpAkA-) to encourage someone, or to be the thing (igpakadasig/impakadasig, reason, igpAkA-) which encourages them.
Eng. encouraged
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
n.
(talagdasig, talag-) encourager
Eng. encourager
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
adj.
(madasig-dasig, mA1-rdpRT-) having skills for encouraging people
Eng. encourager
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
2.
v.
for someone (egdasig-dasig, migdasig-dasig/indasig-dasig, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to cheer themselves up (igdasig-dasig, indasig-dasig, idasig-dasig, reason, ig-rdpRT-)
Data Qual: rani says this means the same as egpadasig - not just for self
Eng. enjoy
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
