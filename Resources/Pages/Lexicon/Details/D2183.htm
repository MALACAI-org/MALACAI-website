hendini
/hɔndini/
Var.1: endini
v_rt.
“come_here”
See dini
 
vi.
for someone or something (eggendini/eg-endini, miggendini/mig-endini/inggendini/ing-endini, agent, eg><ø-) to come here
Eggendini kun si Pipitu su due tuyù keykew.
Phllip will come here for he had some business with you.
Eng. come here
Tag. halí; pumaríto; parini
Ceb. pag-anhi
Ilo.: umay
OCM: Locomotion (481)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for a place (egkeendiniyan, egke:ndiniyan/egke-endiniyan, theme, egkA><an-) to be able to be come to by someone
Eng. come, to be able
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
