tuliyuk
/tuliuk/
 
1.
n.
Megalurus_timoriensis
rufous-capped canegrass warblerstriated grassbird
Kasè tuliyuk ne bayaku su egpangeen te kutu te kalabew.
The striated grassbird is eating carabao lice.
Eng. warbler; grass bird; bird variety
OCM: Fauna (136)
DDP: Bird (1.6.1.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: RT
 
2.
v.
for a tuliyuk bird or a person (egtutuliyuk, migtutuliyuk/intutuliyuk, tutuliyuk, actor, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to dip their head (not their body) in water
Eng. birdbath; dip; wash
OCM: Personal Hygiene (515)
DDP: Be in water (1.3.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
