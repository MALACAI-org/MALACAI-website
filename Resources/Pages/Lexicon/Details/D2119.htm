halap
/halap/
v_rt.
“moist”
 
v.
(eggalap-halap, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to use just a little water in the bottom of a pot
Ka egmangalap-halap iyan ka deisek naan dè iya ka samè ne warad/egkaamin e iling te weyig diyè te sakeru, ka layag te sulu ne mahaan e egkeebukan, ka mata te buyag ne kenad amana egkita wey ke egkabigtawan wey nalepu naa.
Egpanakeru e pad su eggalap-halap naan dè ka weyig ney diyè te gelun.
I will fetch water for only moist was left in the water container..
Eng. moist; little water
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for a lamp (egmangalap-halap, egmaN-rdpRT-) light going out (down to a flicker)
Eng. dying light; light fading; lamp dying; fading light; flickering light; smoldering
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
