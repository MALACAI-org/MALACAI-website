panalliya
/panalliya/
 
v.
for someone (egpanalliya, agent, eg><ø-) to depart at someone else (egpanalliyaan, egpanalliya:n, patient, eg><an-)
Ka egpanalliya iyan ka egpariyù wey egpaney-paney ne kenè egkareetan te minsan nekey ne egpadereet kandin. Emun ke due igkaaldek din sikan naa ka kenad sikandin egparani wey egpakaddiyù e.
Egpanalliya ad ne eggendiyè te Dabaw ne eg-untud te mutur su egpandakep ka pulis ke warè pepilis ta.
I will never go to Davao riding motorcycle because the police inforcer will sieze us if we don’t have papers.
Eng. stay away
Ceb. molikay
Tag. iiwas
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpakapanalliya, agent, egpAkA-) to be able to escape
Ka egpakanalliya iyan ka egpakapariyù te minsan nekey ne igkaaldek egpadereet kandin.
Kenè ki iya egpakapanalliya te weyig ne egsipuk kun keuremè su warad kayu ne egpakabulig egsupsup te weyig.
We cannot escape when the waters will overflow because there are no longer trees that can absorb the water.
Egpakapanalliya ke naa te te egginguma ne gubut keuremè?
Can you escape from war that will happen soon?
Eng. stay away; refrain
Ceb. makalikay
Tag. makaiwas
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
