salubè
/salubɔʔ/
v_rt.
“catch”
 
v.
(insalubè, iN-) unstop throwing something to someone
Ka igpasabut te salubè ne lalag iyan ka pegdegpak te minsan hentew te minsan nekey ne kene egpe-eneng-eneng taman te egpakalihawang ka etew diye te seled wey ke egpakatinugpu naa diye te kayu.
Insalubè ney ka ubal.
We throw stone to the monkey.
Saluba a niyu ka baley enni Mimi eyew egpakatinugpu te baley.
Throw something to Mimi's house so that they can jump off from their house.
Eng. throwing something; stoned
OCM: Hunting and Trapping (224)
