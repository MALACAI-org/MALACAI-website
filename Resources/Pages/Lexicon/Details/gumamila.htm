gumamila
/gumamila/
 
n.
a large red flower
Ew, seini bes ka bulak ne gumamila. Masalig ma seini diyè te baley ney piru warà a nekeila te ngaran.
Oh, this is a gumamila flower. There's many at our house but I didn't know the name.
Eng. hibiscus
