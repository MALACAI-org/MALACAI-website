ahet
/ʔahɔt/
 
adj.
excited or eager (maahet, mA1-)
Ka maahet iyan ka maali, masahelsel wey maagsik-agsik ne te minsan nekey ne eggimuwen iling te pegpangguhud wey pegsayew.
Maahet naan dè iya egpangguhud si Peun te me kawalian.
Peun is really enthusiastic in telling jokes.
Maahet naan dè iya ne egpasalinggahew egbasa si Luwin te sulat minsan kenè egkateu.
Luwin is really eager to volunteer to read even though he doesnt know how.
Nakarapig a su maahet ne egsayew ka egsayew ka duma ku ne malitan.
I was pushed aside from dancing because my female partner danced eagerly.
Eng. eager; enthusiastic; excited; energetic (about eagerness in doing something)
DDP: Energetic (2.4.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
