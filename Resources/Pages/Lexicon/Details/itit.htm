itit
/ʔitit/
n.
“orphaned_chick”
 
n.
orphaned chick
Wrd.Gloss: orphaned_chick
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se piyak ne amana egkalayam te etew wey kenè egkahaldek minsan nekey su ligkat pad te deisek pad eglayamen e te kamuney egdumdumaha sika ka kenè egkahaldek te etew.
Ka amey ku due itit ne uhis ne piyak.
My father has an orphaned white chick.
Eng. orphaned chick; chick, orphaned
OCM: Ethnozoology (825)
DDP: Animal life cycle (1.6.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for someone (eg-iitit, mig-iitit/in-iitit, iitit, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to tame a chick (eg-ititen, in-ititen/mig-ititen/pig-ititen, ititta, patient, eg><en-)
Eng. tame, to; orphan, to
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
vi.
for a chick (egkeitit, ingkeitit, migkeitit, actor, egkA><ø-) to lose its mother
Maamuk ka piyak ne egkeitik su nalayam ma seini te etew.
The orphaned chick is tame for it was accustomed with people always.
Eng. chick, become orphaned
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
