dutun
/dutun/
dem.
“there”
 
dem.
that over there
Wrd.Gloss: there
Ka seini ne lalag iyan karumaan te lalag ne dutu ne migtinuru te sabeka ne inged wey ke eglengeren naa ne inged. (Pitawa niyu ka lalag ne dutu)
Dutun buwa te dibabew ka masalig se abaka ne le-ew.
Maybe there is lot of wild abaca over there.
Gramm. d3 non-focus common pronoun
Data Qual: ?locative prn yonder
Eng. there; yonder; that over there
Tag. noón
Ceb. niadtu
