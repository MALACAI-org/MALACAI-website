iyam
/ʔiam/
adj.
“new”
 
adj.
new; fresh; modern
Ka iyam iyan ka bag-u/sikan pad iya, wey kenè seini ne tapey. Egkaayunan te seini ne lalag ka langun ne sikan pad iya neyimu/neyitabu/neetew/wey masalig pad ka duma.
Keilangan ne egbebeli a te iyam ne salual su nalembis e ka tapey ne salual ku.
I need to buy a new pants for my old one is getting wear-out.
Iyam pad iya migligkat si Tutu ka miggendiyè te Balinsiya su egbeli te balieg ne eggimuwen ne banda.
Toto leaves recently to Valencia to buy some beads for tribal necklace. (lit. Toto leaves recently to Valencia because he will buy a beads for necklace making.)
Wrd.Gloss: new
Syn. bag-u
Eng. new; recent; fresh; modern; verdant?

I was burdened before when I stayed in Davao because I am unaccustomed to their living.
