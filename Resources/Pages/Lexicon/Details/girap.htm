girap
/gidap/
adj_rt.
“difficult”
 
1.
adj.
(mahirap, mA1-) difficult
Mahirap ka egkeen ne warè serè su deisek ka egkakeen ta.
It is difficult to eat without viand for we can only took less.
Syn. sumpit
Eng. difficult
Tag. mahírap
Ceb. lisud; budlay (tiring); hágò (requiring effort)
Ilo.: rigat
OCM: Sensation and Perception (151)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
2.
adj.
(mahirap, mA1-) miserable
Eng. miserable
Tag. kahabág-habág
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
(egkeyirapan, neyirapan, egkA><an-) to have no provisions
Egkahirapan key ne eggimu te baley su warè me gamit ney.
Its hard for us to build a house for we have no materials.
Eng. struggle; have difficulty; difficulty, have; suffer
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
