kita
/kita/
v_rt.
“see”
 
1.
n.
(egkita, migkita/ingkita, eg><ø-) sense of sight
Egkita sikandin te busew.
She has a sense of sight for she can see an evil spirit.
Eng. sense of sight; sight
Tag. paningín
Ceb. panan-aw
Ilo.: panagkíta
OCM: Sensation and Perception (151)
DDP: Sense, perceive (2.3), See (2.3.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
2.
v.
for someone (egkita, migkita/ingkita, agent, eg><ø-) to see something (egkiteen, ingkita/migkita/pigkita, theme, eg><en-)
Kenè amana egkita ka mata ku ke mariyù e.
My eyes cannot see clearly if (the object) is farther.
Eng. see
Tag. makakíta; mámulát
Ilo.: makakíta
OCM: Sensation and Perception (151)
DDP: Eye (2.1.1.1), See (2.3.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
v.
for something (egkakita, nakita, actor, egkA><ø-) to be found or noticed
Egkakita tad e kuntee ka hari ku ne egginguma.
We can notice sooner my younger brother who is coming.
Eng. found; find; notice; discover
DDP: See (2.3.1), Notice (3.1.2.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for someone (eggingita, miggingita/inggingita, agent, eg><ø-giN-) to go to see someone (eggingiteen, eggingite:n, inggingita/miggingita/piggingita, eg><en-giN-)
Eggingita a te amey ku ne warè ku pad nakita.
I go to see my father whom I didn't met before.
Eng. go see
DDP: See (2.3.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone or something (egpakita, migpakita/impakita, eg><ø-pA-) to show or reveal something (igpakita, impakita, instrument, igpA-)
Eng. show; reveal; appear
DDP: Show, let someone see (2.3.1.4), Appear (2.3.1.5.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
