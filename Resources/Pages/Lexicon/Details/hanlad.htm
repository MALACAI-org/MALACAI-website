hanlad
/hanlad/
 
v.
(egpahanlad, migpahanlad, hanlad, egpA><ø-) move slowly
Ka igpasabut te lalag ne hanlad iya ka kenè ne meiyal ne eggipanew wey ke egpallahuy naa. Masulug ka egpuunan mania te egpahalad eggipanew ka minsan nekey wey ke minsan hentew naa. Sabeka kayi su nabeley e ka eggipanew wey ke mareet naa ka dalan.
Egpahalad ka etew ne egdakep te manuk su eyew kene eglayang.
A man traps chicken will move+slowly so that it will not fly.
Eng. move slowly; slowly; light; soft; mild
