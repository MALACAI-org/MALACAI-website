kahat
/kagat/
v_rt.
“bite”
 
1.
v.
for a person or animal (egkahat, migkahat/ingkahat, eg><ø-) to bite something (egkahaten, ingkahat/migkahat/pigkahat, patient, eg><en-)
Ka seini iyan sika se egpalian ka pinaahi te ngipen te etew wey ke duma ne mananap ne due ngipen.
Egkahat ka higtek ke kenè ki egpupules ke eglipereng te marusilem.
The mosquito will bite us if we don't pull our blanket during night.
Ingkahat te asu ka manuk.
The chicken was bitten by the dog.
Eng. bite
