leput
/lɔput/
v_rt.
“protrude”
 
vi.
for something (egleput, agent, eg><ø-) to protrude, as muscle from a deep wound.
Wrd.Gloss: protrude
Ka egleput iyan ka egleddut ne minsan nekey puun te nalebengan kayi iling te kasilè wey te mata, wey masalig pad ka duma.
Egleput e ka tul-an te paa rin ne mangune-kunè se palì.
The bone protrudes out of his uncured wounded foot.
Eng. protrude; stick out; poke out; poking out
