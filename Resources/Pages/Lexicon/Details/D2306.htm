hulid
/hulid/
v_rt.
“lie_beside”
 
vt.
for someone (eggulid, miggulid/inggulid, agent, eg><ø-) to lie down with someone who is lying down (egguliren, miggulid/inggulid/piggulid, theme, eg><en-) (usually in bed)
Cult. This is often used as a euphemism for a sexual relationship, similar to the English "sleeping together." However, like the English, a sexual relationship is not necessarily implied.
Eggulid ka me anaken ku keddì.
My nieces/nephews sleep with me.
Eng. sleep with; lie beside; lie with; cuddle; snuggle; seduce
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for two people (peggulirey, agent, peg><ey-) to sleep in the same bed
Peggulirey key te me anaken ku.
My nephew/niece and I slept in the same bed.
Eng. sleep together; lie together
OCM: Sleeping (513)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
