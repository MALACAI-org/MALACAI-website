taggal
/taggal/
n.
“agreed_time/date”
 
n.
a pre-arranged time or date
Kaaselem ka taggal ney te eggendiyè te bakayan ka egpanaheuli te binggal, kasilè wey me suli.
Eng. prearranged time/date; agreed time/date
 
vi.
for two or more people (egtaggal, eg><ø-) to agree on a certain date or time for a meeting or other event
Egtaggalan ku sikaniyu te sengebulan ka egpammitew te kalabew ne igbetad niyu te anak ku.
Syn. taheen
Eng. agree on date; pre-arrange
