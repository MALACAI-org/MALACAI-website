tubù1
/tubuʔ/
v_rt.
“sprout”
 
n.
bud or shoot (of a plant)
Ka kayu ne impamula ku egmemate e ka tubù te salingsing.
The tree that I planted was sprouted.
Syn. sugpang
Syn. tugbung
Eng. bud; shoot
Tag. súpang
OCM: Ethnobotany (824)
DDP: Growth of plants (1.5.6), Parts of a plant (1.5.5)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for a seed (egtubù, migtubù/intubù, tubù, agent, eg><ø-) to sprout (egtubuan, intubuan/migtubuan/pigtubuan, tubui, theme, eg><an-)
Egtubù e ka kalabasi ne impamula ney gabii.
Our planted squash yesterday is sprouted.
Syn. egleteb
Syn. egsimat
Eng. sprout, to; shoot, to; bud, to; grow
Tag. tumúbò; siból
Ceb. tubò; nagtubò
Ilo.: saríngit; rúsing
WBM: tuvù
OCM: Ethnobotany (824)
DDP: Growth of plants (1.5.6)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for the plants (egtubù, migtubù/intubù, tubu, agent, eg><ø-) to grow
Eng. grow, to
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for the plants or disease (egpanubù, nanubù, eg><ø-paN-) to be self-sown
Nanubù e ka aheley ne neulaan diyè te dalan.
The corn which scattered in the path way are growing.
Cult. e.g. seluyut
Eng. self-sown
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
