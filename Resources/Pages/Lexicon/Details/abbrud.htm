abbrud
/ʔabbrud/
Var.1: abburud
/ʔabburud/
v_rt.
 
vt.
for someone (eg-abbrud/eg-abburud, mig-abbrud/mig-abburud/in-abburud/in-abburud, abburud/abburud, agent, eg><ø-) to go abroad (eg-abbruran/eg-abbururan, in-abbruran/in-abbururan/mig-abbruran/mig-abbururan/pig-abbururan/pig-abbururan, abbruri/abbururi, location, eg><an-) for some reason (ig-abbrud/ig-abburud, in-abbrud/in-abburud, iabbrud/iabburud, reason, ig><ø-)
Eg-abbrud a diyè te Amirika ke egpekeimpus ad te kursu ku su amana egdawaten seini ne kateunan diyè.
After I graduated my course I will abroad to United States of America because this course is demmand their.
Mateles ne eg-abbruran ka Amirika ne inged te keddì ne puhawang.
It is good to abroad in America according to my opinion.
Sika ka ig-abbrud ku su warè seleppì ku para te pegbebaley ku.
I dont have money to finance for my house that is why i'm going abroad.
Iyan nu abruri ka inged ne Hawaii.
Just abroad at Hawaii country.
Eng. abroad, go; overseas, go; go abroad
