aney2
/ʔanɔy/
adj.
“beginning”
 
adj.
(an-anayan, rdpCVC–an) first (in time)
an-anayan ne bulan
the first month (January)
Morpho. ?-an
Eng. first; formerly; previously; beginning
Tag. dáti; noóng úna; dáti-ráti
Ceb. kaniadtu
Ilo.: dáti
OCM: Ordering of Time (805)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
 
adj.
(aney-aney, rdpRT-) formerly; previously
Eng. formerly; previously
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(panganey, paN-) eldest child
Sikeddì ka panganey te pamilya ney.
I am the eldest in our family.
Eng. eldest; oldest
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
