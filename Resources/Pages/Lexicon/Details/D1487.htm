diddid
/diddid/
v_rt.
“skin_sugarcane”
 
vt.
for someone (egdiriddid, migdiriddid/indiriddid, diddid, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to strip the outer skin off sugarcane (egdiddiran, migdiddiran/indiddiran/pigdiddiran, diddiri, location, eg><an-) with an instrument such as a bolo or puruk weeding tool (igdiddid, indiddid, instrument, ig><ø-)
Wrd.Gloss: skin_sugarcane
Ka egdiriddid iyan ka pegluwit wey pegsapsap te luwit te tire pinaahi te ugpit wey minsan nekey pad ne mahalang. Iyan dè egkaayunan te seini ne lalag ka tirè wey warad lein ne egkahamitan te seini ne lalag wey egluwitan e ka duma.
Egdiriddid e pad te tirè.
Egdiriddid e pad te tirè taheed te egtetahad a ki ame.
I will skin the sugarcane while waiting for Father.
Egdiddiran ku te ugpit ka tirè.
I will skin the sugarcane with a bolo.
Eng. skin sugarcane; strip skin off sugarcane; sugarcane, skin
