kanta2
/kanta/
v_rt.
“sing”
Fr. Ceb. kanta
 
v.
for someone (egkanta, agent, eg><ø-) to sing a song (egkanteen, egkante:n, patient, eg><en-)
Wrd.Gloss: sing
Iyam e seini ne lalag su iyan nekeyimu kayi ne lalag ka me iyam e nanubu ne me batè, su me biseye ka kamuney te seini ne lalag ne iyan perem sukub te menubu ne lalag ka eg-ulahing. Ka igpasabut te seini ne lalag ka peg-ulahing, peg-uuranda, peg-eggung te minsan nekey ne ulahingen. Egkaayun ne egbalesan ka peg-ulahing te minsan nekey ne me egdahing iling te kuglung, salurey, kubing, pulendag wey duma pad wey egkaayun degma ne kenè.
Egkanteen ta ka me ulahingen te pegdayan wey pegsaye te Manama su egpakapahale kandin sika.
Let's sing the songs for praising and worshiping God because it will please him.
Eng. sing
Ceb. kanta
