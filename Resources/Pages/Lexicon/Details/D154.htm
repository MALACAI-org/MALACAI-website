alew2
/ʔalɔw/
v_rt.
 
v.
for someone (egpaalew, migpaalew/impaalew, agent, eg><ø-pA-) to be concerned about someone (egpaalawan, impaalawan/migpaalawan/pigpaalawan, range, eg><an-pA-)
Tani, kenè nu peuntura ka batè te kuddè su egpaalew a ke egkeulug.
Tani, don't let the boy ride the horse because I'm worried if he falls.
Eng. concerned, be; worried, be
Data Qual: queram doesn't know this word, maybe it is deep or not used in our family word, but i think it is reak MS
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Worried (3.4.2.4.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for someone (egpaalew-alew, migpaalew-alew/impaalew-alew, agent, eg><ø-pA-rdpRT-) to have a try at something (egpaalew-alewan, impaalew-alewan/migpaalew-alewan/pigpaalew-alewan, location, eg><an-pA-rdpRT-)
Egpaalew-alew e rè due ne eghangyu ki Ilmu te seleppì su kema ke duen ded iya seleppì din.
I'll just have to try to beg money to Elmo if he has still money.
O. Syn. eg-eleg-eleg
Eng. try; attempt; strive
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
