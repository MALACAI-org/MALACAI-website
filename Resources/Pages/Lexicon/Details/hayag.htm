hayag
/hayag/
v_rt.
“moon_rise”
 
v.
for the moon (eggayag, agent, eg><ø-) to be about to rise in the west
Ka seini ne lalag diyè egpuun te Biseyè ne iyan igpasabut se layag te minsan nekey ne egsiha. Egkaayun degma seini ne eggamiten te igpanengneng ka minsan nekey diyè te keet-etawan.
eggayag e ka bulan
new moon
Tetelesi ne egpitawen ka timman ne bulan su meupiya ka layag din.
How nice the light of the full moon because its light is good.
Igpahayag ta ne sikanta Matigsalug te langun ne tribu.
We pronounce that we are Matigsalug to all tribes.
Eng. rise, for moon; new moon; pronounce; articulate; proclaim
