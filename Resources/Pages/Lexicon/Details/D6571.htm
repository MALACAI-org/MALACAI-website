telek
/tɔlɔk/
 
n.
purely native
Ka telek iyan ka etew ne warè nasewuhi ka langesa te lein ne langesa puun pad te kabuybuyahan din. Neetew sikandin ne nekeg-iling dè iya ka tribu te iney wey amey te iney rin wey ka me kaap-apuan dan nekeg-iling dè iya degma wey warè nasewuhi te lein ne tribu wey linalahan naa. Ka seini ne senge tribuwan, migmeel-eliney rè migmaas-asaweey wey nangasawa te duma ne tribu wey linalahan. Sikan naa ka amana sikandan egtengteng te batasanen dan wey eg-ikul dutu. Piru kuntee, due me telek ne kenad eg-ikul te batasanen dan su eg-ikul e te duma.
Telek pad iya ne me Menubu ka me matig-Sita su warad iya Biseyè ne nakasewug kandan diyè.
Inhabitants in Sita are purely native because no Visayan mixed with them.
Menubu bes iya ne telek si Eteng su egmena mem pad iya ka me kalayunan din.
Eteng is indeed a pure native because his relatives are still primitive (lit. holding their customs and traditions that are no longer applicable to this generation).
Eng. pure native; purebreed; native; genuine; undiluted
