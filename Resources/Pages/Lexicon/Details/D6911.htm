tulen
/tulɔn/
 
1.
v.
for a person or animal (egtulen, migtulen/intulen, agent, eg><ø-) to grab occassionally (e.g, time or opportunity) (egtulenen, intulen/migtulen/pigtulen, theme, eg><ø-)
Egtulen a ne egpangayu diyè te engki Mirna.
I occassionally getting firewood in Mirna's residence.
OCM: Ethnozoology (825)
DDP: Animal movement (1.6.4.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
v.
for a person or animal (egpatulen, migpatulen/impatulen, agent, eg><ø-pA-) to move animals into pasture (egpatulenan, impatulenan/migpatulenan/pigpatulanan, location, eg><an-pA-)
Egpatulen e pad te asu ku egpakeen diyè te migkasalan su masalig ne tul-an dutu.
I will go to wedding venue to feed my dog occassionaly there because many bones are there.
Eng. graze
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
