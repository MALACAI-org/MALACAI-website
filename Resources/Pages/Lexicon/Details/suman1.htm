suman1
/suman/
v_rt.
 
1.
v.
for someone (egsuman-suman, migsuman-suman/insuman-suman, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to think something (egsumsumanen, insuman-suman/migsuman-suman/insuman-suman, theme, eg><en-rdpCVC-)
Ka egsuman-suman iyan ka eg-isip-isip, egpalemlem, egpamuhawang te minsan nekey ne egpakatahù diyè te suman-suman din. Ka minsan ne nekey ne igkeupii rin ne eggimuwen ne diyè te isip te etew wey ke mananap naa.
Egsuman-suman a pad ke nekey ke eggimuwen ku kaaselem.
I will think what I am going to do tomorrow.
Egsuman-suman a pad ke egpakapabulus a ne egduma keykew.
I will think if I can go with you.
Eng. think; ponder; decide; consider; evaluate
Tag. mag-isíp
Ceb. maghunàhunà
Ilo.: agpanúnot
OCM: Ethnopsychology (828), Logic (811)
DDP: Think (3.2), Think about (3.2.1.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
vi.
for someone (egpakasuman-suman, nakasuman-suman, agent, egpAkA-rdpRT-) to remember something (egkasuman-suman, nasuman-suman, theme, egkA><ø-rdpRT-)
Nasuman-suman ku ne hendei nakatagu.
I remembered where I kept it.
Eng. remember
OCM: Ethnopsychology (828)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M

n.
(suman-suman, rdpRT-) mind
Ka suman-suman iyan ka isip te langun ne eggeheyinawa iling te etew, mananap wey duma pad ne due utek. Seini ka eggamiten eyew egsuman-suman, egpalemlem, egpamuhawang te minsan nekey.
Mamenu buwa ka suman-suman te egbusawen? Iling bu red te suman-suman te malenggew?
How does it look like the+thinking of crazy person? Does it look like with the mind of the normal person?
Nekeiling bu red ka suman-suman te etew te suman-suman te me mananap?
Is the mind of human being the same with the animals?
