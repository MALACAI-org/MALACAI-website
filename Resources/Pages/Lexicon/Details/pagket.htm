pagket
/pagkɔt/
 
v.
for someone (egpagket, migpagket, pagket, agent, eg><ø-) to attach someone to something
Ka igpasabut te pagket iyan ka pegpareket te minsan nekey diyè minsan nekey. Ka seini ne lalag talagduma te pakpak, wey deket.
Egpagket e pad te me balieg diyè te tangkulu ku eyew meupiya ne egpitawen.
Im going to attach necklace on my native hat so that it is good to see.
cause to attach to something (igpagket, impagket, instrument, ig><ø-)
Eng. attach; drive in; push in; poke in; nail in; spike
Ceb. pilit; bugkos
