ney
/nɔy/
pron.
“1pe_NF(II)”
 
pron.
we (exclusive); by us (exclusive)
Wrd.Gloss: we(excl);
Warè ney natahari sikuna.
We're not able to wait for you.
Gramm. 1st person plural exclusive non-focus pronoun (Set II, brief form)
Eng. we (excl) (non-focus II); by us(excl) (non-focus II)
Tag. námin
Ceb. namù
Binukid: day
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Pronouns (9.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
 
poss_pron.
our (exclusive); of ours (exclusive)
Due masalig ne nipa diyè te inged ney.
There are a lot of nipa palms in our(excl) place.
Gramm. 1st person plural exclusive possessive pronominal specifier (postpositional)
Eng. our (excl); ours, of (excl)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
