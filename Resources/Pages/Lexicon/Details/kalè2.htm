kalè2
/kalɔʔ/
“frying_pan”
 
1.
n.
a frying pan or wok
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se hilutuanan te me gulay su meupiya su masikeung wey maluag se be-bè.
Keilangan ne egbeli ki te kalè su egkagamiten ta seini te pegguhulay.
We need to buy a wok because we can use it for cooking vegetables.
Wrd.Gloss: frying_pan
Eng. frying pan; wok; pan
Tag. kawálì
Ceb. kaláhà
Ilo.: páryok
OCM: Utensils (415)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
vi.
for something, e.g., a pan of water (egkale-kalè, migkale-kalè/ingkale-kalè, kale-kalè, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to boil (egkale-kalaan, ingkale-kalaan/migkale-kalaan/pigkale-kalaan, location, eg><an-rdpRT-)
Cult. Stage 2 in cooking rice
Egkale-kalè e ka weyig.
The water is already boiling.
Eng. boil
Tag. maglága
Ceb. maglàgà; magbukal
Ilo.: aglíngta
OCM: Food Preparation (252)
DDP: Water quality (1.3.6)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something, e.g., a pan of water (egpakakale-kalè, nakakale-kalè, agent, egpAkA-rdpRT-) to cause something boiling, or to be a thing (igpakakale-kalè, impakakale-kalè, reason, igpAkA-rdpRT-) which causes something boiling
Eng. boil
Tag. maglága
Ceb. maglàgà; magbukal
Ilo.: aglíngta
OCM: Food Preparation (252)
