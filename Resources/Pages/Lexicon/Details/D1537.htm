diyè
/diɔʔ/
Var. diya
(before a)
Data Qual: should this have a glottal?
dem.
“there”
 
loc_pron.
there (far away, not specific or out of sight)
Wrd.Gloss: there
Nekey-a ka egkuen nu riyè te puwalas?
What you are going to get there in the forest?
Diya a te Dihungan gabii ka migsinug-ung te asawa ku.
I was there in Dihungan yesterday meeting my wife.
Eng. there (far away, not specific or out of sight)
