hinguma
/hiŋuma/
Var. himuma
Var.1: inguma
Morpho. uma was probably the original root
v_rt.
“arrive”
 
1.
vi.
for someone (egginguma/eg-inguma, migginguma/mig-inguma/ingginguma/in-inguma, agent, eg><ø-) to arrive at a place (eggingumaan/eg-ingumaan, inggingumaan/in-ingumaan/miggingumaan/mig-ingumaan/piggingumaan/pig-ingumaan, location, eg><an-)
Wrd.Gloss: arrive
Egginguma ka Prisidinti kayi te inged ta kuntee.
The president will arrive here in our place today.
cf. lepew —appear
Eng. arrive; come; appear; reach
DDP: Appear (2.3.1.5.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something (egkeyingumaan, neyingumaan, theme, egkA><an-) result
Eng. result
Online dict status: c
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egkeyingumaan, neyingumaan, theme, egkA><an-) to be reached
Eng. reached, to be
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
vi.
for someone or some event (egginguma/eg-inguma, migginguma/mig-inguma/ingginguma/in-inguma, agent, eg><ø-) to be imminent; impending
Wrd.Gloss: arrive
Eng. imminent; impending; happen; occur
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for the day (egginguma/eg-inguma, migginguma/mig-inguma/ingginguma/in-inguma, actor) next; the following
Egginguma ne Lunis ka peggendiye ku te Malaybalay.
Next Monday will be going to Malaybalay.
Eng. next; following
Online dict status: c
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(peyingumeen/peingumeen, pA–en) to be impending
Eng. impending
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
