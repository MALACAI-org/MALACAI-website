paney
/panɔy/
v_rt.
“plan_ahead”
 
vi.
for someone (egpaney-paney, migpaney-paney/impaney-paney, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to think, do, plan, prepare and to instruct ahead of time at someone else or something (egpanpanayan, impanpanayan/migpanpanayan/pigpanpanayan, location, eg><an-rdpCVC-)
Egpaney-paney ka amey ku te me himuwen din.
My father prepare his work ahead of time.
Egpaney-paney ka Manama te keet-etawan din ne kenè eggimu te mareet.
God give instruction to his people to never commit sin.
cf. upus
Eng. think ahead; instruct; plan; prepare; do in advance; in advance; advance; warn
DDP: Think (3.2), Think about (3.2.1.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egkapanpanayan, napanpanayan, agent, egkA><an-rdpCVC-) to have been prepared
Wrd.Gloss: prepared
Napanpanayan ney e ka eggimuwen ney kaaselem.
We've been prepared the work that we will work tomorrow.
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for someone (egpakapaney, nakapaney, actor, egpAkA-) to be born with a physical disability
Nakapaney sikandin nabutud.
He was born without an eye.
Eng. born like; birth, from; deformed at birth; disabled from birth
DDP: Disabled (2.5.4), Birth (2.6.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for someone (egpakapaney, nakapaney, actor, egpAkA-) to have had a habit since they were small
Nakapaney sikandin mabulut.
Since he was young he is brave.
Eng. like it since young
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
