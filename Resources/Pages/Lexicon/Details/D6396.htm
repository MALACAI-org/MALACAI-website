tampuk
/tampuk/
 
v.
smash
Ka tampuk iyan ka pegbaras wey pegkawè te belad ne iyan eggamiten te pegtagpi te duma wey te minsan nekey ne egkeyisen. Belad dè iya ka eggamiten te pegtanggis su duen e lein ne ngaran emun ke duen e egkahen-genan.
Intampuk ku ka bula ne hendei ke pa iya su buked te buyag ne dutu nakareeg!
I smashed the ball and the old man's head was hit!
Eng. smash; spank; beat; smack; strike
