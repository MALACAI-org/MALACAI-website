dew-ey
/dɔwʔɔy/
v_rt.
“scold”
 
v.
for someone (egdew-ey, migduw-ey/indew-ey, dew-ey, agent, eg><ø-) to scold someone else (egdew-ayen, indew-ey/migdew-ey/pigdew-ey, dew-aya, goal, eg><en-) (egdew-ayan, indew-ayan/migdew-ayan/pigdew-ayan, dew-ayi, location, eg><an-) for something that they have already done
Ka egdew-ey iyan ka eglalag te mareet diyè te minsan nekey wey minsan hentew.
Egdew-ey ka me etew te malitan ne mareet se eggimuwen.
The people will disparage a woman whose action is bad.
Egdew-eyen e te me etew ka malitan ne mareet se himu.
People are scolding the woman who is evildoer.
Indew-ey ni Hisus ka me etew ne warè migpalintutuu minsan nakakite e sikandan te me kein-inuwan.
Jesus scolded the people did not believe eventhough they had seen miracles.
O. Syn. sapad
Syn. sawey
Eng. disparage; scold; tell off; reprimand; scorn; scoff; berate; criticize; rebuke; despise
Tag. magmurá; muráhin
Ceb. kasab-an
Ilo.: pagúngtan
OCM: Gestures and Signs (201), Social Control (626), Techniques of Inculcation (861), Non-electronic Transmission of Messages (202), Transmission of Cultural Norms (867)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vt.
for someone (egdew-ey, migdew-ey/indew-ey, agent, eg><ø-) to criticize someone or something else (egdew-ayen, indew-ey/migdew-ey/pigdew-ey, patient, eg><en-) about something (igdew-ey, indew-ey, reason, ig><ø-)
Eng. criticize, to
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone or something (egkarew-ey, narew-ey, agent, egkA><ø-) to be criticized by someone else (igkarew-ey, ingkarew-ey, theme, igkA><ø-) about anything
Eng. criticized, to be
