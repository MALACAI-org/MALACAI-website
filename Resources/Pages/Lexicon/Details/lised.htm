lised
/lisɔd/
Data Qual: Tig.
adj_rt.
“difficult”
 
adj.
(malised, mA1-) difficult; severe
Eng. difficult; severe
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
n.
(kalised, kA-) difficulty or hardship
Eng. difficulty; hardship
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone or a family (egkalised-lised, egkA><ø-rdpRT-) to have difficulties
Kenè a pad egpakapangabang te prublima nu suled su egkalisedlised key pad kuntee.
Eng. have difficulties; struggling
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
(eg><ø-) for someone (eglised, miglised, lised, agent) to experience diffuculty
Eglised a te Inglis su kene ne linalahan ku.
I have difficulties with English because this is not my language.
Eng. difficult
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
