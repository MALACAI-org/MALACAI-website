bukbuk1
/bukbuk/
n.
“fine_powder”
 
n.
a fine powder such as insecticide or flour
Wrd.Gloss: fine_powder
Eng. powder
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vt.
for someone (egbukbuk, migbukbuk/imbukbuk, agent, eg><ø-) to sprinkle a fine powder such as flour or insecticide (igbukbuk, imbukbuk, instrument, ig><ø-) onto something (egbukbukan, imbukbukan/migbukbukan/pigbukbukan, location, eg><an-)
Ka kaluwasan te seini ne lalag iyan ka pegtahù diyè te minsan nekey eyew egkabunbunan seini. Egkaayun degma ne egkahamit te pegpangilutù, iling te pegbukbuk te asin diyè te gulay, pegbukbuk te sukal diyè te sahing ne egbinayuwen, pegbukbuk te sukal diyè te binekbek.
Atu, angey ke pa te abu ne igbukbuk te tei te manuk.
Boy, get some of ashes to sprinkle the feces of a chicken.
Eng. sprinkle; spray
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
