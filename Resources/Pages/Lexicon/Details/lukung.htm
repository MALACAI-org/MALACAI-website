lukung
/lukuŋ/
n.
“vat”
 
n.
a barrel (for storing grain)
Wrd.Gloss: barrel
Cult. made from linas - a tree bark
Ka lukung iyan ka luwit te kayu ne eglulunen eyew egmalibusen ne egtahuen te paley, sahing, kasilè, wey duma pad. Linas te indaluhung ka eggimuwen ne lukung wey minsan nekey pad ne kayu egkaayun degma ke kenè mahaan egkahitu wey egkahisi ka luwit.
Pitawa niyu ka sahing due te lukung su kema ke neyinuhan e.
See if the banana inside the drum is ripe already.
Napenù te beni ne hemey ka lukung ni Amè.
Dad's barrel is full of rice seed.
Syn. langkap
Eng. barrel; drum; vat; bark container; container for grain; grain container
OCM: Preservation and Storage of Food (251), Utensils (415)

Usage level: M
