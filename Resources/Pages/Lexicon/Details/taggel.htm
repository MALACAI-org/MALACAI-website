taggel

v.
for someone (egtaggel, migtaggel/intaggel, agent, eg><ø-) to prepare something (egtaggelen, intaggel/migtaggel/pigtaggel, location, eg><en-)

I'll prepare my bolo for cutting grasses because I'm going to leave.
