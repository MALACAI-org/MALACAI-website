salengan
/salɔŋan/
v_rt.
“arrive_during”
 
vi.
(egpakasalengan, nakasalengan, egpAkA-) to arrive when something else is happening
Wrd.Gloss: arrive_during
Nakasalengan kew, suled, te eglabung key e.
Brother you arrived right when we going to have dinner.
Nakasalengan ka pegkeetew ku te ting-init wey mantungè naan dè ka egkakeen.
I was born during the hot season when we only had mantunge to eat.
Nakasalengan key nekeutel te babuy te tinggaani.
We got a (wild) pig while it was harvest time.
Eng. arrive when something else is happening; simultaneous; coincide; while; during; interrupt
