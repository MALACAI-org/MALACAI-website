lipetuk
/lipɔtuk/
 
n.
a short big type of snake; venomous
Ka lipetuk iyan ka malibusen wey malepet ne uled ne egpesik wey egpakaletu ne mariyù ka egkareehan. Amana ne malantus ne egletu wey amana ne maagsik ne egdawi.
Mani buwa te malantus ne egletu ka lipetuk te warè ma pakpak?
Why is it that the short snake could jump high even no wings?
Cult. When this kind of snake chase a person, it will rolled in order to catched prey in an easiest way.
Eng. giant grub; short snake; round giant grub
OCM: Fauna (136)
DDP: Snake (1.6.1.3.1)
