eled
/ʔɔlɔd/
 
vt.
for someone (eg-eled, mig-eled/in-eled, eled, agent, eg><ø-) to soak something (ig-eled, in-eled, instrument, ig><ø-) in a liquid esp. in water (eg-eleran, in-eleran/mig-eleran/pig-eleran, eleri, location, eg><an-)
Ka igpasabut te ig-eled iyan ka pegtahù te minsan nekey diyè te weyig. Ka pegtahù kayi kenè ne dagdahew rè, ke kenè eggingumaan seini te me sabeka ne aldew wey ke daruwa naa. Ka tuyu te peg-eled eyew egkewumelan seini wey egkaawè ka bilu.
Eg-eled e pad te beni ku ne paley diyè te belun.
I will soak my rice seeds there in the well.
Ig-eled ku pad ka kumbalè ku ne me maangkag su eyew mahaan dè egkaawè ka bilu.
I will soak my white clothes in water so that the dirt can be removed easily.
Eng. dip; soak; immerse
Tag. magbábad
Ceb. humolan; ?bas-on
Ilo.: iyúper
OCM: Food Preparation (252), Garment Care (296)
DDP: Wet (1.3.3), Be in water (1.3.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for someone (eg-eeled, mig-eeled/in-eeled, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to keep on soaking something
Eng. keep on soaking
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something (egpekeeled, nekeeled, egpAkA-) soaked into the water
Nalibeng ad e ka egpamitew te salual ku, kunaleg te nekeeled ku bes te weyig.
Searching my pants drived me tired but on the contrary I had soaked it in the water.
Eng. dipped; soaked; immersed
DDP: Wet (1.3.3), Be in water (1.3.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
