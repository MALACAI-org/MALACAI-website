tikeba
/tikɔba/
n.
“vertical_bank”
 
n.
a vertical bank, usually formed where the river has cut into its banks, or by a landslide in the hills
Hapit a neulug te tikeba ganna te marusilem su kahiyen ku ne mariyù pad ka tikeba.
I almost fell down a vertical bank earlier in the night because I thought that it was still far away.
Wrd.Gloss: vertical_bank
cf. lukiyab
Eng. vertical bank; steep bank; cliff; bank, vertical
