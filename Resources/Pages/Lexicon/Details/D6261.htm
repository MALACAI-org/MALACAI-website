tahù
/taguʔ/
v_rt.
“put_away”
 
1.
n.
contents
Eng. contents
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egtahù, migtahù/intahù, agent, eg><ø-) to hide (something); to store (something in a container or house); to put (something somewhere); to keep (egtahuan, intahuan/migtahuan/pigtahuan, location, eg><an-)
Egtahù ki te serè para kaaselem.
We will put aside viand for tomorrow.
Wrd.Gloss: put_away
Eng. store; put; hide; keep
Tag. tágò
Ilo.: lemméng
OCM: Preservation and Storage of Food (251), Property in Movables (422), Weight Moving (483)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
n.
(intahù, iN-) to deposit; to place inside
Pitawa nu ka seleppì ne intahù ku dutu te puntil ku.
See the money I put there in my bag.
Syn. taleu
Eng. deposit
Tag. depósito; páunang-báyad
Ceb. diposito
Ilo.: depósito
OCM: Banking (453)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(tahuanan, -an-an) container
Migbange te asu ka tahuanan ku te seleppì su egsulenen din.
The box that I've placed money was carried by the dog for its toy.
Eng. container; holder; box; place
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
2.
n.
(pegpanahu-tahù, peg-paN-rdpRT-) decoration; adornment
Eng. decoration; adornment
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
