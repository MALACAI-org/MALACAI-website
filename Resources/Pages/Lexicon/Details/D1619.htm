dus
/dus/
num.
“two”
 
num.
two
Ka igpasabut te dus, daruwa. Seini ne lalag numiru ne egpakasinundul te sabeka wey ke an-anayan.
Daruwa ne libu ka kentidad te kuddè ne nabeli ku.
The horse that I bought cost two thousand pesos.
Gramm. Borrowed from Spanish via Cebuano, but in very common use, e.g., in domains such as money and telling the time.
Wrd.Gloss: two
Syn. duwa
Syn. daruwa
Eng. 02 (Spanish); two (Spanish)
Ceb. duha
OCM: Numeration (802)
DDP: Cardinal numbers (8.1.1.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
