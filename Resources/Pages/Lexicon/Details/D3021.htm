kenè ne malehet
/kɔnɔʔ_nɔ_malɔgɔt/
See leget
 
adj.
incorrect
Mania te migpalintutuu kew te innangen ku sikaniyu te kenè ne malahet su migwali-wali e rè.
Why you are believing me what I've told you, that those were not true I'm just joking.
Eng. incorrect; not right; wrong
Tag. hindi támà; hindi tumpák; malî
Ceb. sayup; dilì hustu
Ilo.: saan nga úmno
OCM: Norms (183)

Usage level: MI
