ubat
/ʔubat/
n ???.
“lie”
 
n.
a falsehood
Kahiyen ku ne malehet ka guhuren ni Anggam ne egbetu kun ka kalibutan ke egpamalawag, kunaleg te ubat bes.
I think that the story of uncle true when the sky turn to orange it will exploded, but it is false.
Eng. falsehood; lie; untruth; deceit
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
adj.
false; untrue
Eng. false; untrue
Tag. malì; sála; hindi totoó; hindi tamà
Ceb. sayup; dilì hustu
Ilo.: saán nga agpáyso
OCM: Norms (183)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for someone (eg-uubat, mig-uubat/in-uubat, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to lie to someone (eg-ubatan, in-ubat/mig-ubat/pig-ubat, location, eg><an-)
Wrd.Gloss: lie
Kenè ka eg-uubat su egpakasalè ka diyè te Manama.
Don't lie for you to be not commited sin to the Lord.
Eg-ubatan ku ka amey ku te due egbayaran ku diyè te iskwilaan su eyew egbehayan a te seleppì.
I'll make a lie to my father that I have some obligation in my school so that he provides me money.
Syn. talù
Syn. enggeb
Syn. tuwek
Eng. lie; falsify
Tag. magsinungalíng; magyabáng
Ceb. mamakak
Ilo.: úlbod
OCM: Norms (183), Non-electronic Transmission of Messages (202), Wrongs (673)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
adj.
(ubaten, -en) prone to lying
Henduen ka te meupiye en iya ne eglalag-lalag piru ubaten ke bes ne dakel.
When you talked you're so good but behind that you are liar.
Eng. liar
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
