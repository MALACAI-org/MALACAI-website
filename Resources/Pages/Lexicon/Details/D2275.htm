hirbabuwina
/hirbabuwina/
 
n.
Mentha_cordifolia
marsh mint
Ka hirbabuwina iyan ka pinamula ne egpekeiling te benisil. Meemut ka dewun kayi wey delemetan para te buwè wey nguweg wey masakit se ulu. Egkaayun man-e seini ne igsewug ne egkakeen isip leggù su meemut.
Egpupu e pad te dewun te hirbabuwina su igbawi ku te buwè ku.
I will pick some leaves of mint to cure my cough.
Meupiya eg-inumen ka linala ne hirbabuwina su egpakaawè te maddeet ne eggeramen ta.
Boiled of mint is good to drink because it removes bad things we feel.
Masuluhen ku te egpamula ka hirbabuwina kayi te sapiling ney su masalig ka egkahamitan kayi.
I will plant many mint in our backyard because it has many purposes.
Cult. It is a medicine for sinusis, cough, and heache.
Eng. marsh mint; mint; herb; spice; flavoring
Tag. herba buena
OCM: Flora (137)
DDP: Grass, herb, vine (1.5.3)
