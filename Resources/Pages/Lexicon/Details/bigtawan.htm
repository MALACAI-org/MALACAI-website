bigtawan
/bigtawan/
Data Qual: I suspect this needs to be deleted? [RJH]
 
n.
a variety of corn
Seini e pad nakakita te aheley ne egngaranan te bigtawan!
This is my first time to see the corn named bigtawan!
Wrd.Gloss: corn
Cult. The kernel is stout and difficult to remove from the cob.
Eng. corn
