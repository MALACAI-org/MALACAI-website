laglag
/laglag/
Data Qual: are there two meanings here ? baggage/move
n.
“baggage”
 
n.
(kalaglahan, kA><an-) baggage, possessions e.g. a hammer, a chair
In-ay-ayaran itines ni Idan ka me kalaglahan din.
Eng. baggage; luggage; possessions
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(kalaglahan, kA><an-) a storage container for clothes
Cult. e.g. this might be a liyang, a keyyeheng, or a tinangeb.
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
1.
v.
(eglaglagen, eg><en-) to carry (more than one item from one place to another)
Eglaglahen ku pad seini se me kasangkapan ney diyè te ugpaan ney ne iyam.
I will carry this things first to our new home.
Eng. carry
OCM: Burden Carrying (482)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
n.
(kalaglahan, kA><an-) a (large) storage basket (with cover and strap for clothes)
Igpamehey kud e ka duma te me kalaglahan ku su dakel e amana ne warad egkatahuan diyè te baley ney.
I will give to others the rest of my belongings for there is no more place to keep them.
Eng. storage basket; basket
OCM: Mats and Basketry (285)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
(pangalaglag, paN- ???) to transfer to another village
Pangalaglag kew pa diyè te lain ne inged su layun dè egmanderalu ka me anak niyu kayi.
You have to transfer to another place for your children always be sick here.
Eng. transfer; move away; shift
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
(pangalaglahan?, paN-rdpCVC–an???) to do househould chores such as to carry water or firewood
Cult. no longer in common useage
Syn. kayu
Eng. carry firewood; collect firewood; chores; work
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
