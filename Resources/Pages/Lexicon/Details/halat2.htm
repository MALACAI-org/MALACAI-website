halat2
/halat/
 
adj.
(mahalat, mA1-) spaced apart
Kenà a egpakalipereng te marusilem su mahalat ka alabat ney wey maagsil amana.
I can't sleep at night because our wall(boards) are spaced apart and it's very cold.
Mahalat ka atep ney ne kalan, sikan naa eg-uranan key.
Our cogon grass roof is widely spaced, that's why we get rained on.
Eng. spaced apart; separated; gaps; far apart; widely spaced
