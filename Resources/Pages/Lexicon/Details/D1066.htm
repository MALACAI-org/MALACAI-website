bugsù
/bugsuʔ/
v_rt.
“transplant”
 
v.
for someone (egbugsù, migbugsù/imbugsù, bugsu, agent, eg><ø-) to transplant a banana seedling (igbugsù, inbugsù, patient, ig><ø-) somewhere (egbugsuan, imbugsuan/migbugsuan/pigbugsuan, bugsui, location, eg><an-) by digging a hole and then standing it in the hole
Hendei niyu igbugsu sika se sepa te sahing?
Where you going to plant that young (dig out) banana?
Impamugsù bes e ni Amadu ka me sahing?
Amado has been transplanted the bananas.
Eng. transplant banana, taro; plant banana, taro; banana, plant; taro, plant
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for someone (egpamugsù, migpamugsù/impamugsù, agent, eg><ø-paN-) to keep on transplanting bananas (egpamugsuan, impamugsuan/migpamugsuan/pigpamugsuan, location, eg><an-paN-)
Eng. keep on transplanting
