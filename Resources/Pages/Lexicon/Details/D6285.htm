talad
/talad/
v_rt.
“divide_out”
 
v.
(egtalaran, eg><an-) to divide (among many)
Egtalaran ku ka suled ku te inuyanan ku ne paley.
I will divide this rice with my brother.
Syn. baad
Eng. divide; apportion
Tag. pagbahá-bahaginin
Ceb. bahinon
Ilo.: bingáyen
OCM: Acquisition and Relinquishment of Property (425)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
(egpanalaren, eg><en-paN-) to have a share
Kenè kew pa hipanew su egpanalaren ku pad seini se ngalap ne samè te imbelegyè ku.
Don't leave for we'll share with the remaining meat which I sold.
Eng. have a share; share
Tag. tumúlong; magpabaláto
Ceb. bahini; hatag
Ilo.: ibíngay
OCM: Property System (421), Social Relationships and Groups (571)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
(egtalad-talad, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to deal out (e.g. playing cards); to distribute
Egtaharan te pad si Pilipi ka egtalad-talad te hemey ne imbehey ni Mayur.
We'll wait for Felipe who will distribute the rice given by Mayor.
Eng. deal; distribute
Ceb. bahin
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpanalad, agent, eg><ø-paN-) to distribute or share out portions
Kahiyen dan ne si Pilipi kun ka egpanalad te hemey ne imbehey ni Mayur?
They said that Felipe will be the one to distribute the rice which is given by Mayor.
Cult. E.g. when a large snake was killed
O. Syn. egpamehey
Eng. distribute; share
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
(egpataltalaarey, egpataltala:rey, egpA><ey-rdpCVC-) to divide into portions
Eng. divide; apportion
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
