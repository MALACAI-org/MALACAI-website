heles
/hɔlɔs/
Var. eles
v_rt.
“hide”
 
v.
for someone (eggeles/eg-eles, miggeles/mig-eles/inggeles/ing-eles, agent, eg><ø-) to physically hide (iggeles/ig-eles, inggeles/ing-eles, theme, ig><ø-) oneself somewhere (eggelesan/eg-elesan, inggelesan/ing-elesan/miggelesan/mig-elesan/piggelesan/pig-elesan, location, eg><an-)
Ka eggeles iling te egsuksuk ka te diralem te lantawan ne eyew kenè ka egkakita te duma nu.
Geles kew su egpamitawen ku sikaniyu.
