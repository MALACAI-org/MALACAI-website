tareng
/tadɔŋ/
 
adj.
(matareng, mA1-) perfect; holy; upright; righteous
Syn. maheteng
Eng. perfect; sensible; right-living; straight-living; well-behaved; behaves well; sane; holy; upright; righteous; excellent
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(katareng, kA-) righteousness
Eng. righteousness; holiness
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(katarengan, kA><an-) an argument, case, evidence, or reason as in a court
Ka igpasabut te seini ne lalag iyan ka pegkene-kenè wey ke pegpamitew te pamaahi ne egpakaligwe wey kenè egkaapil te minsan nekey ne himu wey ke me hitabu naa. Egkaayun degma ne ka pegpamalehet te minsan nekey ne hitabu.
Kenè egkasuhù si Liya su layun ne due katarengan.
Wrd.Gloss: evidence
Eng. defence; defense; evidence; testimony; argument; case; reason; justification
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
