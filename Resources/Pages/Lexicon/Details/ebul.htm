ebul
/ʔɔbul/
adj_rt.
“brownish”
 
1.
adj.
(meebul, mA1-) brownish- white
Matesan e ka tebesal su meebul e.
The squash is already matured because it's dirty white already.
Meebul se laplap din.
His skin is dark.
Eng. dirty white; dark (about color of something)
DDP: Skin (2.1.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something (egpangebul-ebul, migpangebul-ebul/impangebul-ebul, pangebul-ebul, eg><ø-paN-rdpRT-) to be brownish-white in color
Emun ke egpangebul-ebul e ka tebesal, eleg naan e ne egpupuwen su matesan e.
When the squash become dirty white it's time to pick them because they are already mature.
Eng. color, brownish-white; color variety; colour variety
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
vi.
for someone (eg-ebul, mig-ebul/in-ebul, ebul, patient, eg><ø-) to be lazy
Eng. lazy, be
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
