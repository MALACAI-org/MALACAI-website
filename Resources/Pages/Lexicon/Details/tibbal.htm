tibbal
/tibbal/
adj.
“naked”
 
adj.
naked to the sight; visible
Tibbal dè due sika se inggeles nu ne sahing.
The banana that you hid is still visible.
Eng. naked; visible
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
part.
presumptuous
Tibbal egpanaad si Bilyu te warè pad kamalehetan.
Bilyu is just making a promised without truthness.
Eng. presumptuous
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for someone (egtibbal-tibbal, migtibbal-tibbal/intibbal-tibbal, tibbal-tibbal, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to hide something that hopefully out of sight but still visible for someone else
Miggeles si Sara ki Binhamin su naaldek piru tibbal rè due sikandin ka miggeles.
Benjamin hid from Sarah and he hoped that Sarah couldn't see him but in the sight of Sarah he is visible.
Eng. visible
DDP: Visible (2.3.1.5)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
