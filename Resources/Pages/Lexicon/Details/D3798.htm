lieg
/liʔɔg/
n.
“neck”
 
n.
neck
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se ampew te pamanayen ne diralem dè maa te bake ta.
Malayat ka lieg te lapay ne tinggew.
The neck of a kingfisher bird is so long.
Eng. neck
