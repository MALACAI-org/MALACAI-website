teyul
/tɔyul/
 
v.
(egteyul, eg><ø-) to set properly
Ka egteyul iyan ka eg-ayad-ayad te minsan nekey ne warè nateil wey nakaayad-ayad nakapabunsud. Ka igpasabut pad man-e ne igpasabut kayi iyan ka egteil wey eg-ayad-ayad te eggimuwen din.
Teyula nu kun ka kendiru sika su nakeipi te hapuy.
Kindly set the pot properly for it is missing the fire.
Kema teyul ke kun hipanew su eyew kenè ki egkasaligkat ka egsinundul kandin.
Please walk properly so that I will not be tripped over coming behind you.
Eng. behave; still; fix; set up
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(mateyul, mA-) still
Ka mateyul iyan ka meupiya wey mateles ne pegkeyimu te minsan nekey ne innimu re. Iyan man-e igpasabut kayi ka maleman wey mateles ne etew wey ke mananap naa. Kenè eggimu te minsan nekey ne egpakarereet kandin wey amana egkakita ka keupiya rin.
Mateyul en iya ka innimu nu ne baley su bayew nakasulad.
The house that you built was good because it slants a little. (Irony)
Eng. good; nice; perfect; straight; proper; well-made; well made; well-done; well done
Ceb. maayo
