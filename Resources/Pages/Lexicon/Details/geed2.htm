geed2
/gɔɔd/
Pron.: ge:d
 
v.
to be steadfastly continuing
Ka egpaheed-geed iyan ka egparahas-dahas te minsan nekey ne eggimuwen?
Nekey buwa ka ig-ayat ta te batè ne egpaheed-geed ne egsinehew.
What makes the child which keep on crying in order to stop?
Mania te egpaheed-geed ka eglipereng te matikang e ka aldew?
Why are you keep on sleeping and the sun is already high?
Egpaheed-geed e pad perem ka egkeen, kunaleg te naamin bes e ka keenen.
I was keeping on eating, but the food ran out.
Eng. keep on; keep doing; go forward
Ceb. padayon
Tag. patuloy
