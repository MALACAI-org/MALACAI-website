alumama
/ʔalumama/
v_rt.
“be_friendly”
 
adj.
friendly
Alumamaa nu ma sika se mahaliyug nu su egkeenduduenan.
You should be friendly to your visitors because they might be upset.
Eng. friendly
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for someone (eg-alumama, mig-alumama/in-alumama, agent, eg><ø-) to welcome another (eg-alumameen, eg-alumame:n, in-alumama/mig-alumama/pig-alumama, theme, eg><en-)
Mareet ka su kenè nu eg-alumama te me alukuy nu ke eglambag nu diyè te lein.
You are bad because you won't greet your friends when you meet them in other place.
O. Syn. eg-elit-elit
Eng. friendly; welcome; greet
Ceb. maabiabì
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for someone (eg-al-alumama, mig-al-alumama, in-al-alumama, agent, eg><rdpCV-) to intertain
Eng. welcome; intertain; care
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
adj.
(maal-alumama, mA1-rdpCVC-) hospitable; friendly
Meupiya ne malitan si Nita su maal-alumama.
Nita is good girl because she is hospitable.
O. Syn. meelit-elit
Eng. hospitable; friendly
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
