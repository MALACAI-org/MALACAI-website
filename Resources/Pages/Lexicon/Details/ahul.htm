ahul
/ʔagul/
adj_rt.
“thin-walled”
 
adj.
thin-walled (of bamboo)(so that the hole is large) (maahul, mA1-)
Wrd.Gloss: thin-walled
Iyan ded meupiya ka diyè te ampew te liwaraan te damuluwan diyè te uud su maahul ka lungag.
The damuluwan bamboo over half-way up (amongst the foliage) is good because it is thin-walled.
Ant. buheng
Eng. thin-walled
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
adj.
(ahulan, -an) hollow
Eng. hollow
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for a person, animal or thing (eg-ahul, mig-ahul/in-ahul/pig-ahul, agent, eg><ø-) to follow a stream or river (eg-ahulen, ahulen, patient, eg><en-) somewhere (eg-ahulan, mig-ahulan/in-ahulan/pig-ahulan, location, eg><an-)
Data Qual: including 'thing'?
Eg-ahul ka uled kayi te lungag su maluag wey maawang amana.
The snake will follow this hole because it's wide and smooth.
