item
/ʔitɔm/
adj_rt.
“black”
 
adj.   
(meitem, mA1-) black or brown as in brown skin
Cult. The Matigsalugs see themselves as black and Caucasians and Chinese as white.
Ant. maangkag —white
Eng. black; brown-skinned; ebony; color variety
