talagtag
/talagtag/
v_rt.
“spread_out”
 
vt.
for someone (egtalagtag, migtalagtag/intalagtag, talatag, agent, eg><ø-) to spread out something (such as corn or rice) (egtalagtahen, intalagtag/migtalagtag/pigtalagtag, talagtaha, theme, eg><en-) to dry in the sun on something (such as a mat) (egtalagtahan, intalagtagtahan/migtalagtahan/pigtalagtahan, talagtahi, location, eg><an-)
Wrd.Gloss: spread_out
Kenè ku rè egdumeen ka anak ku ne si Tuni su warè egtalagtag te dinampil.
I do not let my child Tony come with me because nobody can spread out the drying crops.
Egtalagtahen ta ka dinampil ta ne aheley su eyew mahaan egkahangu.
We will spread out our drying corn so that it will dry quickly.
Eng. spread out; lay out; laid out; set out
DDP: Sun (1.1.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for something (egkatalagtag, natalagtag, actor, egkA><ø-) to become spread out
Kenè niyu ubala ka me manggad ne impanlupì ku su egkatalagtag.
Don't play around with clothes that I folded because they will get spread out.
Eng. spread out, become; unfolded; fall apart; disintegrate
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
