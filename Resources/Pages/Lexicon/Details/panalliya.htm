panalliya
/panalliya/
v_rt.
“avoid”
 
v.
for someone (egpanalliya, agent, eg><ø-) to avoid something or someone (egpanalliyaan, egpanalliya:n, patient, eg><an-)
Ka egpanalliya iyan ka egpariyù wey egpaney-paney ne kenè egkareetan te minsan nekey ne egpadereet kandin. Emun ke due igkaaldek din sikan naa ka kenad sikandin egparani wey egpakaddiyù e.
Egpanalliya ad ne eggendiyè te Dabaw ne eg-untud te mutur su egpandakep ka pulis ke warè pepilis ta.
I never go to Davao on a motorcycle because the police will arrest us if we don’t have our registration papers.
Eng. avoid; refrain from; keep away; stay away
Ceb. molikay
Tag. iiwas
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpakapanalliya, agent, egpAkA-) to be able to escape
Ka egpakanalliya iyan ka egpakapariyù te minsan nekey ne igkaaldek egpadereet kandin.
Kenè ki iya egpakapanalliya te weyig ne egsipuk kun keuremè su warad kayu ne egpakabulig egsupsup te weyig.
We cannot escape when the waters will overflow because there are no longer trees that can absorb the water.
Egpakapanalliya ke naa te te egginguma ne gubut keuremè?
Can you escape from war that will happen soon?
Eng. stay away; refrain
Ceb. makalikay
Tag. makaiwas
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpanpanalliya, agent, eg><ø-paN-) to alert someone else (egpanpanalliyaan, egpanpanalliya:n, patient, eg><an-paN-)
Eng. allert
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
1.
v.
for someone (egsalsalliya, migsalsalliya, salliya, agent, eg><ø-rdpCVC-) to keep away from others, e.g., a young girl who doesn't want to be talked into getting married
Ka egsalsalliya iyan ka egpaney-paney egpadpariyù wey egsabsabeka eyew egpakaalihu wey egpanalliya te duma ne etew wey te minsan nekey.
Egsalsalliye e kanta si An-an su egkeyilew sikandin su eg-inum ma sikandin.
An-an keeps away from us because he's ashamed because he had been drinking.
Migsalsalliya pad si Apa te me duma rin su kene sikandin egkeupian ne egduma kandan.
Apa avoided his companions because he doesn't want to go with them.
Eng. keep away; avoid; isolate
Ceb. likay
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
(egpanalliya, migpanalliya, panalliya, eg><ø-paN-) to avoid (meeting others)
Egpanalliyaan e te uyang ku ka alukuy rin su layun dè sikandan egpekeg-ehet.
My brother-in-law keeps away from his friend for they always quarrel with each other.
Eng. avoid; escape
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
