for someone (egseluk, migseluk/inseluk, agent, eg><ø-) to go off to the bush; to stay out of sight selung
/sɔluŋ/
 
v.
for the plants (egkaselung, naselung, theme, egkA><ø-) to be stunted in growth or less development because it is shaded of some plants and there's a lack of sunlight
Ka naselung iyan ka nabehang ne pinamula su naampawan te derakel wey matikang ne me pinamula. Iyan egpuunan ne egkabehang su kenè egkasilaan te aldew wey layun egkasubid.
Naselung e ka pinamula su due kayu.
The plants are stunted-and-wither because there are trees (covering/shading them)
Eng. stunted and wither
