alemud
/ʔalɔmud/
v.
“not agreeing_sound”
 
v.
for someone or something (esp. a ghost)
Data Qual: gli and gl fields not correct for this entry
(eg-alemud, mig-alemud, in-alemud, agent, eg><ø-) making sound for not convinceable (eg-alemuran, in-alemuran/mig-alemuran/pig-alemuran, location, eg><an-) for something with a facial expression (ig-alemuri, in-alemuri, reason, ig–i)
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se egpagawas ka te laheng nu te kenè peg-uyun te me lalag wey puhawang te me duma wey egparumaan te pammiyed.
Mig-alemud dè iya sikandin te puhawang te igdatu ney.
