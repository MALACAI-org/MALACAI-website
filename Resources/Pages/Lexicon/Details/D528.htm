bakus2
/bakus/
Fr. Ceb. bakos
 
n.
a belt
Ka bakus iyan ka biseye ne lalag ne iyan igpasabut ka libed wey sinturun.
Derengana seini se bakus ku piru warè ku rè due engkeri su egkahamit pad.
This belt is with me long ago but I never abandoned it because it is still useful.
Syn. sinturun
Eng. belt
Ceb. bakos
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
v.
for someone (egbebakus, migbebakus/imbebakus, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to wear a belt
