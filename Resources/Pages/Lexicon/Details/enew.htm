enew
/ʔɔnɔw/
v_rt.
“get_up”
 
n.
get up
Eng. get up
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
vi.
for someone (eg-enew, mig-enew, in-enew, agent, eg><ø-) to get up (from a lying position)
Eg-enew a egselem-selem su masalig ka eggimuwen ku kaaselem.
I will get up early tomorrow because I have a lot of work to do.
Eng. get up; getting up; arise; sit up
Tag. bángon
OCM: Sleeping (513), Postures (516)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for someone (egpeenew, migpeenew, impeenew, agent, eg><ø-pA-) to make someone or something (eg-enawen, in-enew/mig-enew/pig-enew, theme, eg><en-) stand upright
Egpeenew a te kayu.
I am making the log stand upright.
Eng. make upright; stand upright; place upright; stand up
Tag. tumaás; itaás
Ceb. pataas
Ilo.: agpangátu; ipangátu
OCM: Weight Moving (483)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpeenew, migpeenew, impeenew, agent, eg><ø-pA-) to ask or request someone's help (eg-enawen, impeenew/migpeenew/pigpeenew, theme, eg><en-) to stand upright
Egpeenew ka amey ku.
My father asking help to+stand him upright.
Eng. make upright; stand upright; stand up
Tag. tumaás; itaás
Ceb. pataas
Ilo.: agpangátu; ipangátu
OCM: Weight Moving (483)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(nekeenew-enew, nAkA-rdpRT-) able to be up and about (of a sick person)
Hapit e egkeulian si Ilin ka egderalu su nekeenew-enew e ubag gabii.
Ilin is getting well now for she was able to be up yesterday.
Eng. able to be up and about; recover; up and about; better
Tag. mabúti ang pakiramdám
Ceb. maayo ang paminaw
Ilo.: sumáysayáat ti rikna
OCM: Theory of Disease (753)
DDP: Recover from sickness (2.5.1.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for the hair (egpangenew, migpangenew, impangenew, theme, eg><ø-paN-) to rise up
Egpangenew ka bulbul ku, ke maagsil ka timpu.
My body hairs will rise up when the weather is cold.
Cult. used of body hairs standing up when a person is cold
Eng. rise up
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
