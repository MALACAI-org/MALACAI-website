bunbun
/bunbun/
v_rt.
“cover”
 
n.
a cover (placed directly on top of something)
Eng. cover
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egbubunbun, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to cover something
Egbubunbun e pad te lungag sika te dalan ta.
I will cover the hole in our pathway.
Eng. cover
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
(inbunbunan, in–an) to be covered
Eng. covered
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(pigbunbunan, pig><an-) veiled
Eng. veiled
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
(kabunbunan, kA><an-) to be convered or buried
Kenè ne meupiya ne diyè egbebaley te kalandihan su kema ke egkalunew ne egkabunbunan e ka baley.
It's not advisable to build a house at the side of the mountain for in case of landslide the house will be buried.
Cult. e.g. by a landslide
Eng. covered; buried
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
