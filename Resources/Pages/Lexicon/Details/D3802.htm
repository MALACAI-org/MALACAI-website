liggid
/liggid/
 
n.
a wheel
Ka ligid iyan ka ngaran te malibusen ne eggimuwen ne paa te terak, mutur, bangkalew wey keritun. Innimu ligkat te putew wey egpakaplian te guma eyew kenè egkareetan ka putew. Due lungag kayi te elat-elat ne egtiyukan te igbantal eyew kenè egpakalekè diyè te lawa.
Migbetu ka ligid te terak su natiyuk te lansang.
The wheel of a truck burst because it was pierced with a nail.
Cult. Long ago this might have been called paa te terak "feet of the truck"
Eng. wheel
Ceb. ligíd
