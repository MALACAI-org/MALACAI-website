kendiru
/kɔndidu/
n.
“kettle/pot”
 
n.
a pot or kettle
Wrd.Gloss: kettle/pot
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se egsubbaan te egkakeen wey minsan nekey ne egkaayun ne egkasubba seini iya ka eggamiten ne egsusubbaan.
Imbeli e pad te iyam ne kendiru su mareet ka tapey.
I bought a new one cause my old pot is ugly already.
Eng. kettle; pot; pan
Tag. kaldéro
Ceb. kaldiro
Ilo.: kaldéro
OCM: Utensils (415)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
v.
for someone (egpangendiru, migpangendiru/impangendiru, agent, eg><ø-paN-) to eat out of the pot
Eng. eat out of pot; pot, eat out of; scrape out pot
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
