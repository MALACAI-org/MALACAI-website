iras
/ʔidas/
n.
“spouses_of_two_siblings”
 
n.
a sibling-in-law (of either sex) by virtue of two marriages, i.e., the spouses of two siblings (or first cousins)
Wrd.Gloss: brother-in-law; sister-in-law
Dakel ka neutel te iras ku ne bakbak te migpaneng-ew.
My brother-in-law obtained many frogs when he was hunting frog during night with light and two-pronged spear.
Eng. spouses of siblings; brother-in-law; sister-in-law
