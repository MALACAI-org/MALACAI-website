salareng1
/saladɔŋ/
n.
“male_deer”
 
n.
a male deer
Masalig pad ka me salareng dengan piru kuntee pila pila naan dè.
Before there were still many male deer but now it's very few.
Genr. usa —deer
Eng. deer, male; buck
