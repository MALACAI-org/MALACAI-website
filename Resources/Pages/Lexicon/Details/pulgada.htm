pulgada
/pulgada/
 
n.
a unit of length equal to 1/36 yard
Ka pulgada iyan ka diyè te gintang te me kemel. Kaayun seini ne eggamiten eyew igseked te minsan nekey.
Due sapulu wey daruwa ne pulgada ka sabeka ne paa.
There are 12 inches in a foot.
Eng. inch
