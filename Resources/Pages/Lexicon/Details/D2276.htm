hirehè
/hidɔgɔʔ/
Var.1: irehè
v_rt.
“stay_overnight”
 
1.
vi.
for someone (eggirehè/eg-irehè, miggirehè/mig-irehè/inggirehè/mig-irehè, agent, eg><ø-) to stay (overnight) somewhere (eggirehaan/eg-irehaan, inggirehaan/in-irehaan/miggirehaan/mig-irehaan/piggirehaan/pig-irehaan, location, eg><an-)
Eggendiyè key te Panganan kuntee piru eggirehè key pad diyè te Sita.
We will go to Panganan today but we will stay overnight at Sita for the moment.
Suled kayi kad iya eggirehè kanami.
Brother just stay here with us overnight.
O. Syn. eglemung
Eng. stay; overnight; board; lodge; accommodate
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
n.
(hireheanan, -an-an) lodge; space for accomodation
Eng. lodge; space for accomadation (always for sleeping)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
v.
for someone (egpangirehè, migpangirehè/impangirehè, agent, eg><ø-paN-) to hunting wild animal in the forest land (egpangirehaan, impangirehaan/migpangirehaan/pigpangirehaan, location, eg><an-paN-)
Eng. hunt, to
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
