bungkag
/buŋkag/
v_rt.
“grind_corn_(first_pass)”
 
v.
for someone (egbungkag, agent, eg><ø-) to put corn through the first pass in the grinder
Wrd.Gloss: grind
Sika se turu ne kalabew ka eggamiten ta te egdadu su egbungkag ki men pad.
Eng. grind
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
1.
v.
(egkabungkag, egkA><ø-) to shatter
Kenè kew am-amana lehud-lehud su egkasikeung niyu ka impanlimud kud e ne aheley, ne egkabungkag pad man-e.
Don't play so much because you might stumble on the corn that I had gathered and it will shatter again.
Wrd.Gloss: shatter
Syn. wahey
Eng. shatter
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
