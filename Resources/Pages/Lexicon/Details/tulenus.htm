tulenus
/tulɔnus/
v_rt.
“unplanned”
 
v.
for someone (egtulenus, agent, eg><ø-) to do something unplanned
Ka tulenus ne lalag iyan sika se peggipanew, peg-ayun, ka pegpangalaglag ne warè pegpanengneng te minsan hentew. Sahuhunè ne naawè, nalaag, nawarè ka sabeka ne weyig.
Migtulenus miggipanew si Elisa.
Elisa went without telling anyone.
Eng. unplanned; spontaneous; impetuously; without telling; unannounced
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for someone (egkatulenusan, actor, egkA><an-) to do something, e.g., accidentally fall asleep
Natulenusan e sikandin migpatey su warè ma migtanggu kandin.
He (happened to) die because no-one looked after him.
Eng. accidentally
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
