ku
/ku/
pron.
“1s_NF(II)”
 
pron.
I; by me
Wrd.Gloss: I; by_me
Kahi, "Neg leuyeng ku pad".
He said, "I'll go and check (the field)".
Eglepung e pad te hilamunan ku human a egpamipi su eyew warad igkeubil ku.
I will first finish weeding my field before I wash clothes, so I will have no more things to bother me.
Gramm. 1st person singular non-focus pronoun (Set II, brief form)
Eng. I (non-focus II); me (non-focus II)
Tag. ko
Ceb. ko
Binukid: ku
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Pronouns (9.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
 
pron_COMPL.
(kud, -d) I; by me (with completive aspect)
Gramm. 1st person singular non-focus pronoun (Set II, brief form) with completive aspect
Eng. I (non-focus II), with completive aspect; me (non-focus II), , with completive aspect
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
poss_pron.
my
Wrd.Gloss: my; of_mine

My father knows how to sing.
Gramm. 1st person singular possessive pronominal specifier (postpositional)
