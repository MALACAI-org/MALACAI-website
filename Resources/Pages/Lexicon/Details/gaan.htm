gaan
/gaʔan/
adj_rt.
“fast”
 
adj.
(mahaan, mA1-) quickly; fast; imminent
Ka mahaan iyan ka himu ne meiyal, iling te pegpallahuy wey minsan nekey ne himu ne kenè ne mananey.
Mahaan kad!
Hurry up!
Mahaan key rè nekeuma diyè te Dabaw su meiyal ma ka mig-unturan ney ne terak.
We arrived in Davao quickly because the vehicle which we took was fast.
Syn. iyal
Eng. fast; imminent; imminently; soon; quick; quickly; brief; briefly; short time; shortly; swift; swiftly; instant; hurry; hurried; hurriedly; almost; close
Tag. mabilís; madalî
Ceb. paspas; dalì
Ilo.: napaspás
OCM: Weights and Measures (804)
DDP: Quick (8.4.8.1), Soon (8.4.6.4.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for someone (eggaan-gaan, miggaan-gaan/inggaan-gaan, gaan-gaan, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to hurry at doing something (eggaan-gaanan, inggaan-gaanan/miggaan-gaanan, gaan-gaanan, theme, eg><an-rdpRT-)
Eng. hurry; rush
Tag. mágmadalì; mág-apurá
Ceb. magdalì
Ilo.: agápurá
OCM: Locomotion (481)
DDP: Quick (8.4.8.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
