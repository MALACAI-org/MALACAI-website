palanan
/palan/
 
vi.
for someone (egpalpalanan, migpalpalanan/impalpalanan, agent, eg><ø-rdpCVC-) to startle someone out of sleep by doing something (igpalpalani, impalpalani, instrument, ig><i-) startle
Ka egpalpalanan iyan ka egkalamu wey kenè egpakataha te eggimuwen minsan malenggew e wey nekeyimate e. Ka duma egkaalimekawan wey human pad egkeinu-inu mania te neyimu rin ka iling dutu wey mania te warè din nanengnengi ka innimu rin.
Eng. unconscious; startle
DDP: Surprise (3.4.1.3), Lose consciousness (2.5.6.4), Lose consciousness (2.5.6.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egkapalpalanan, napalpalanan, patient, egkA><ø-rdpCVC-) to be surprised out of sleep by something (igkapalpalanani, ingkapalpalanani, instrument, igkA><i-rdpCVC-) and so not be fully awake
Emun egkapalpalanan ka hari ku egkaalatan a su mahaan egkalamet te ugpit.
I am very frightened when my younger brother is startled out of sleep, because he will swing a sword.
Eng. startled; dopey
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
