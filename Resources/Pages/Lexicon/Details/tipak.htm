tipak
/tipak/
v_rt.
“cut”
 
n.
a cut piece
Eng. cut piece
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
vt.
for someone (egtipak, migtipak/intipak, agent, eg><ø-) to cut some thing (egtipakan, intipakan/migtipakan/pigtipakan, location, eg><an-) with a bolo (igtipak, intipak, instrument, ig><ø-)
Wrd.Gloss: cut
Eng. cut; slash; release
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for something (e.g., a stone on a hillside) (egkatipak, actor, egkA><ø-) to break loose
Eng. come loose; break loose; loosen
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
n.
(tipak_te_buel) an obligation of male sides to be given at female sides especially at grandmother and granfather either it is money, things, or animal. This kind of dowry is for the payment of being restlesssness of caring during the babyhood stage of the female.
O. Syn. lebut_te_panamung
O. Syn. kapulanan
O. Syn. punas_te_tei
Eng. dowry
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
