emis
/ʔɔmis/
adj_rt.
“sweet”
 
1.
n.
sweetness
Seini ka igngaran te eggeramen te minsan nekey wey minsan hentew egkaalihan wey due ibeg. Ka seini ne eggeramen egdumaan te nanam, eyam, ibeg wey let-ug taheed te egpewulirey ka malitan wey lukes. Iyan man-e igpasabut kayi ka minsan nekey ne egkakeen ne mananam wey meemis.
Mamenu buwa ne emis ka eggeramen te egpewulirey?
What kind of sweetness will be felt in sexual intercourse?
Henduen bes ka emis te duriyan, subla ne mananam!
The sweetness of durian is like this, very tasty!
Warè nekeg-iling ka emis te mangga wey marang.
The sweetness of mango and marang differs.
Eng. sweetness
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
for something (eemisi, theme, e<>i-)
Eng. very sweet; sweety
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(meemis, mA1-) sweet
Ka igpasabut te meemis iyan sika kenè ne mapeit, ke kenè, meupiya wey mananam. Piru due masalig ne kalasi te meemis iling te sukal, tirè, sahing, kasilè. Egkaayun degma gulay sika se eleg e ka asin ne in-amut kayi.
Kenaa amana egpakeenen te duktur te meemis ne egkakeen su egkeulat a te dalu ku.
The doctor did not allow me to eat much sweet foods for it makes me get sick again.
Meemis ne egkeenen ka balangas emun ke egkeyinuhan amana.
Rambotan is sweet if it is very ripe.
Wrd.Gloss: sweet
