pamalay
/pamalay/
 
v.
for a man's family (egpamalay, migpamalay, pamalay, agents, eg><ø-) to find out the bride-price ig-asawa required for marrying a girl (egpamalayan, impamalayan, location, eg><an-)
Egpamalay e si Rubin ki Marisul su naluhey e ne uyab sikandan.
Rubin find out the bride-+price to Marisul because they have long relationship.
Impamalayan e ni Rani si Genelyn su eg-asaween din.
Rani propose to Genelyn because he will marry her.
Eng. find bride-price
