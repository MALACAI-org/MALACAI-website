nayè
/nayɔʔ/
n.
“trail”
 
n.
a trail or impression left in the foliage by a person or animal moving past
Wrd.Gloss: trail
Ka igpasabut te naye iyan ka minsan nekey ne naggulemek ne hilamunen wey ke natamisak naa ne basak ne nabayaan te etew wey ke ayam naa .
Kayi ta igteen ka litag te nayè te tinggew eyew egsahad sikandin.
We will put our trap here in the swamp-hen impression so he/she can trapped.
Kenè kew hendiyè te puwalas su inteen ni Damilag ne pusil diyè te dalan te babuy.
Dont go in the forest for Damilag put a firearm in the trail of the wild big.
Eng. trail; impression
