suluhuanen
/suluhuanɔn/
 
n.
servant
Ka suluhuanen iyan ka igngaran te etew ne due ahalen wey egsuulan. Bayew egpekeiling seini te uripen piru ka uripen hendue mulè te pinirisu ne egkalasayan ne kenè egsuulan te ahalen din, piru ka suluhuanen egsuulan te minsan nekey ne eggimuwen din. Ka etew ne egkaayu-ayu wey ka due bayew egkeyimu piru due ahalen dan ne meyaman ne egpatrabahu kandan, iyan iya sika ka egngaranan te suluhuanen. Ka suluhuanen, kaayun ne eglemung diyè te ahalen din wey egsuwey naa eg-ugpè.
Masumpit amana ka egkeula-ula te suluhuanen su minsan nekey ne igsuhu te ahalen ne keilangan ne egtumanen iya.
It is very difficult to be a servant because whatever the master intended to command it must be obeyed.
Meupiya red ka suluhuanen su egpakaneem-neem ded keupianan, piru ka uripen layun naan dè iya egkalasey.
A servant is better than a slave because he can taste goodness, but the slave is always burdened.
Eng. servant; worker; employee
