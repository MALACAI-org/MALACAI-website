batun
/batun/
v_rt.
“pull_up”
 
vt.
for someone (egbatun, migbatun/imbatun, agent, eg><ø-) to pull someone or something up (egbatunen, imbatun/migbatun/pigbatun, theme, eg><en-) e.g., a basket on a rope up a tree, or for God to raise someone to heaven
Egginguma ka aldew ne egbatunen diyè te langit ka langun ne migpalintutuu te Manama.
The time is come that the all believers will be lifted by God.
Imbatun ni Noel ka aheran din su egngilam te mangngayew.
Noel lift up his ladder because he alerting of the killer.
Cult. e.g. at the return of Christ
Eng. pull up; lift up; raise up; move up; ascend; ascenscion; rapture
Ceb. kayab; sakgaw
