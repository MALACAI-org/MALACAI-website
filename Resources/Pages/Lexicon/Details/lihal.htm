lihal
/ligal/
v_rt.
“mate”
 
v.
for a male dog (eglihal, miglihal/inlihal, lihal, agent, eg><ø-) to mount a female dog that is on heat
Wrd.Gloss: mate
Eglihal ka beyan ne asu te lukes ne asu.
The male and female dogs are in heat each other.
Eng. in heat; wanted copulation, to become
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for two dogs (egpaliglihaley, migpaliglihaley/impaliglihaley, paliglihaley, eg><ø-pA><ey-rdpCVC-) to be in heat together
Egpaliglihaley e ka me asu.
The dogs are in heat together.
Eng. in heat; wanted copulation, to become

Usage level: R
