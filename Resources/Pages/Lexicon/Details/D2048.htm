gul-an
/gulʔan/
 
adj.
(magul-anen, mA1–en) cheerless
Magul-anen a su mig-engkeran a te uyab ku.
I'm cheerless because my sweetheart is leaving me.
Eng. cheerless; sad
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Sad (3.4.2.1)

Usage level: L
