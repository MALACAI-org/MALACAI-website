baras1
/badas/
v_rt.
 
vt.
for someone (egbaras, migbaras/imbaras, agent, eg><ø-) to strike something (egbarasan, imbarasan/migbaras/pigbaras, location, eg><an-)
Kenè ka ulì ke kenè nu egkakita ka ilab ne inlaag nu su egbaras si Nanay.
Don't go home if you can't find the knife you misplaced because Mommy will beat you.
cf. lampes
cf. bahung
Eng. strike; hit; beat; bash
