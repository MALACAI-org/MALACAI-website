pahetpet
/pahɔtpɔt/
 
adj.
(mapahetpet, mA1-) active; energetic
Ka mapahetpet iyan ka masahelsel wey mangunè-kune ne eggimu wey eglalag. Migpasabut seini te amana egkateu ne egpangasak wey amana egkateu ne eggimu. Ne minsan degma ka masumpit wey mangunè-kunè pegpatuman te balaud, pegpanulù wey planu, egkaayunan te seini ne lalag.
Mapahetpet naan dè iya ne egpanlalag ka igdatù ney kayi te Simud su neilaan e iya degma ka tribu ney te minsan hendei.
Our leader here in Simud is really good speaker for he made known our place anywhere.
Kenè ne eleg ne egbatbateen nu rè si Dindu su mapahetpet sika ne egguusey.
Don't treat Dindu as a child because he is an expert mediator.
Iyan buwa meupiya ne eggimuwen ne pangulu si Tarung su mapehetpet ne egguhud.
Maybe it's better to make Tarung as our leader because he is a smart talker.
Amana ne mapahetpet ka panulu te talagpanulu ney su egmakadingel key rè te katarengan.
The teaching of our teaching is relevance because we are speechless (idiom).
Eng. dedicated; rigorous; serious; smart; expert; good speaker; energetic (about consistency of work); vehement; relevance; strongly (about consistency); firmly; active; persuasive; strict; plausible
Tag. magaling; matalas
DDP: Strong (2.4.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egmapahetpet, migmapahetpet/immapahetpet, mapahetpeta, agent, eg-mA1-) to make decision firmly; active ; energetic (egmapahetpeten, immapahetpat/migmapahetpet/pigmapahetpet, mapahetpeti, patient, eg><en-mA1-)
Mapahetpeta nu ma te egpangasak eyew egkabagget ki ka egpammineg.
Be smart to talk so that we will be encourage to listen.
Mapahetpeta nu te egpangatarengan su egkaprisu ke ma ke kenè egkaay-ayaran.
Be enthusiastic on reasoning out because we will be imprisoned if not done good.
Eng. firmly decision; firmly; strict, be; active, be; energetic, be; smart, be
