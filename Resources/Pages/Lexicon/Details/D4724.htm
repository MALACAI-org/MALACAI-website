palansa
/palansa/
 
n.
flat iron
Migsambayan te sumbaley ney ka palansa ne iyam ku pad nabeli.
Our neighbor borrowed the iron that I just newly bought.
Eng. flat iron; iron
Tag. plántsa
Ceb. plansa
Ilo.: plánsa
OCM: Appliances (416)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
(egpalansa, eg><ø-) to iron a garment
Egpalansa a te kumbalè ku.
I will iron my garment.
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
(egpamalansa, eg><ø-paN-) to iron several garments
Egpamalansa a te me kumbalè ney su eyew meupiya ne egtengtengan ke igsaluub.
I will iron our garments so that they will look nicely when worn.
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
