tuen2
/tuʔɔn/
v_rt.
“arrive”
 
vi.
for someone (egtuen, migtuen/intuen, tuen, agent, eg><ø-) to arrive (egtuenan, intuenan/migtuenan/pigtuenan, tueni, location, eg><an-) in a specific place and stay for a short time
Wrd.Gloss: arrive
Diya a egtuen te anggam ku diyè te Temmuhan.
I will arrived at my uncle's house their in Tamugan.
Mahirap ke warè egtuenan ta su egkalaag-laag ki.
It's difficult to us when we don't have a dwelling place to stay for we can reach anywhere.
Eng. arrive; appear; place to stay; dwelling place
DDP: Appear (2.3.1.5.1)
