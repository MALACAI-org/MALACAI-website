keles
/kɔlɔs/
v_rt.
“squeeze_water_out”
 
v.
for someone (egkeles, migkeles/ingkeles, keles, agent, eg><ø-) to wring out the water of something (egkelesen, ingkeles/migkeles/pigkeles, kelesi, patient, eg><en-) (egkelesan, ingkelesan/migkelesan/pigkelesan, kelesi, location, eg><an-)
Wrd.Gloss: squeeze_water_out
Iyan kaluwasan kayi ka peggulipis te minsan nekey ne due weyig wey ke gite naa eyew egkaawè seeyè.
Amana mignekal-nekal ka suled ku ne egkeles te niyug su amana ne maweyig.
My brother uses his might to wring out the water of coconut (grated) because it's watery.
Syn. eggulipis
Eng. squeeze water out; press out; wring out; extract juice; juice, to; twist
OCM: Food Preparation (252)
DDP: Dry (1.3.3.1)

Usage level: R
