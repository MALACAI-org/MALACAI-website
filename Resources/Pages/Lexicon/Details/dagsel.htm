dagsel
/dagsɔl/
v_rt.
“snack”
 
vt.
for someone (egdagsel, migdagsel/indagsel, agent, eg><ø-) to have a snack (egdagselen, indagsel/migdagsel/pigdagsel, patient, eg><en-) at someone's residence (egdagselan, indagselan/migdagselan/pigdagselan, location, eg><an-)
Wrd.Gloss: snack
Eggendini kun kangkuwa si Payi su egdagsel te egkakeen.
Payi will come later to eat snacks.
Susubba kew te kasilè su egdagselen ta kangkuwa ke eggutasan kid.
You should cook sweet potato so that we have something to eat for snack when we come back.
Eng. snack; merienda
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for someone (egkaragsel, naragsel, actor, egkA><ø-) to request a snack or to be eaten
Duen ded egkaragsel ku ke egpanumbaley a engki Amè?
Do I have something to eat when I go to my father?
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
