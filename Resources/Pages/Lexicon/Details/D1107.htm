bula-bula
/bulabula/
Data Qual: If the reduplication is really in the root (and there's no glottal), the hyphens need to be removed [RJH]
 
n.
thread
Ka seini iyan sika se igtiyuk te simat eyew te pegpaneyi te me manggad wey te me nanaggisi.
Iyan ku igkeupii ne bula-bula ka malalab.
I like the red thread.
Syn. lanut
Eng. thread
Tag. panahî; sinúlid
Ceb. hilo; tanod
Ilo.: sinúlid
OCM: Clothing Manufacture (294)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
