menu1
/mɔnu/
v_rt.
“how”
 
ques.
how
Menua si Anlakeb kuntee?
How is Anlakob today?
Mamenu kew red diyè?
How are you all there?
Mamenu kun-a karalem ka weyig.
How deep do they say the river is?
Mamenua ka tahasalug?
What is the 'tahasalug' type of rice like?
Kenè ne iyan ku innendini ne menu egbuyu a keykew te seleppì!
It is not my purpose of coming in order to ask you money!
Eng. what like
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Question words (9.2.3.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
ques.
(egmenuwen, eg><en-) how; why
Egmenuwen tad te eg-engked ka me salè ta?
How do we go about leaving behind our sins?
Eng. how; why
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Question words (9.2.3.4)
Online dict status: -

v.
(egkamenu, namenu, egkA><ø-) to have happened; what happened
Namenu kun a ka manuk? Ingkahat te asu.
What did they say happened to the chicken? It was bitten by a dog.
Eng. happened; what happened; affected; harmed; injured; damaged; broken
Tag. anó ang nangyari
WBM: menu
OCM: Grammar (193)

Usage level: G
