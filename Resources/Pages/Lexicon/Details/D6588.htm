temew2
/tɔmɔw/
 
vt.
(nakatemew) ambigious
Ka nakatemew iyan ka lalag ne warè ikaayad-ayad ikapangguhud wey napantek dè te eglalag. Sikan naa ka egpammineg masulug se egkasuman-suman din ke nekey ka igpasabut dutu.
Kahiyen ku ne iyan igpasabut te Datu ka eggimatayan e ka nakasale, kunaleg te kenè bes. Warè ku nasabut su nakatemew man dè nahud ka lalag din.
I thought that the Chieftain declared to kill the convicted, but not. I didn't understood what he said because it is unclear.
He anggam kuwa ka, ke ka egkuwa.
Hey uncle don't hesitate to (do something like eating, etc.)
Eng. misunderstand; unclear; ambigous; hanging
