sapihu
/sapihu/
 
v.
(egsapihu, migsapihu, sapihu, eg><ø-) side; edge of something(e.g house, cliff, person)
Ka lalag ne sapihu iyan sika se kakiliran te minsan nekey. Kaayun ne diyè te baley, dalayap wey ke diyè naa te dakel ne batu.
Sapihu ka te sika ne baley eyew egkakita nu ka dalan.
Just go beside that house for you to see the way.
Migsapihu e rè iya te sika se dakel ne baley duèn e migdani keddi.
I just arrived beside of the building when someone approach me.
Eng. beside; side; edge
Ceb. kilid; likod
