salibut
/salibut/
 
v.
cross behind
Ka salibut iyan ka egsapihu te sabeka ne etew wey ke eglingkus naa. Piru ka seini ne lalag egkahamit diyè te egmalup-lupuhey wey egmaan-antukey. Minsan degma ka maheteng ne eggiphipanew egkaayunan te seini ne lalag emun ke egsapihu ka duma wey emun ke kenè egkeumaan ka dangeb ne eggendiyaan su nekeyipanew e degma.
Egsalibuten ku perem ka manuk ne egdakepen ney piru nakabantey ma wey miglayang e.
I tried to crossed behind the chicken that we are seizing to but it was alarmed and flew away.
Nasalibut ney si Birtu diyè te kandan su miggendiyè kun degma te baley ney.
We missed Birtu in thier house because he went to our house also.
Nekegsalibut key ki Tetuk su miggediyè din te Dabaw ka egpangikul keddì te peg-ulì kud e degma kayi te Simud.
Tetuk and I crossed our paths because he went to Davao when I returned home here in Sinuda.
Eng. walk around; cross around; crossed path
