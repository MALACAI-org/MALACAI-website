gebè
/ʔgɔbɔʔ/
 
v.
for someone (eggebè, agent, eg><ø-) to destroy something (e.g house) (eggebeen, patient, eg><en-) using something (e.g digging bar or hammer) (iggebè, instrument, ig><ø-)
Eng. destroy, to; destruct, to
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something (egkagebè, theme, egkA><ø-) to be broken or destroy
Ka gebè iyan ka nahuhus wey nareetan e. Minsan nekey ne meupiya wey naayun pad perem piru nareetan e wey nabalbalawan e, egkaayunan te seini ne lalag iling te baley ne egkalundus wey duma pad ne matikang ne egkewunak.
Migkahi si Hisus te ka langun ne batu ne nekeg-untud-untud te timplu, egginguma ka timpu ne egkahebè ka langun wey warè iya egsamè diyè te neunturan din.
Hisus said that all stones piled up in the temple time will come that all will break down and nothing is left above each other.
Nahebè ka baley ne imbayaan te nenekali ne kalamag su mahebu e ka me sungkaleg su naluhey e.
The house collapsed when strong wind blows because the posts were rotted for they were old enough.
Kenè kew makeddel ka eggipanew su egkahebè ka seeg ta.
Don't walk hardly because the floor will collapse.
Eng. break down; collapse; destroy; destruct; fall down
Ceb. tumpag
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(nahebè, nA-) destroyed
Eng. destroyed
