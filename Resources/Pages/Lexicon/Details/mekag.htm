mekag
/mɔkag/
 
n.
a kind of tadpole which is a product of edible frog
Seini ka me ngaran te butiti ne anak te bakbak ne egkengakeen.
Masalig bes ka Mekag due te lubuggan te kalabew.
I noticed that there are many tadpoles in the wallowing hole of carabao.
Eng. tadpole variety
OCM: Fauna (136)
DDP: Amphibian (1.6.1.4)
