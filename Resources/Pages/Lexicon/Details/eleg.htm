eleg
/ʔɔlɔg/
adj.
“true”
 
1.
adj.
true (to fact, logic or known truth); exact or enough
Ka igpasabut te eleg iyan sika se kenè ne subla wey kenè ne kulang. Ka duma ne kaluwasan kayi iyan sika se malehet, kenè ne sayep. Egkaayun ne eggamiten seini diyè te kumabalè, ka kenè egsekel wey kenè egkaglung ne kumbalè diyè te egsaluub.
Eleg ka intabak din su migpuun te Bibliya.
His answer is correct because it is from the Bible.
Wrd.Gloss: true
Syn. lehet
Eng. true; exact; correct; fit; fitting; right; appropriate; sufficient; adequate; enough; proper; ok; suitable; opportune; alright; sensible; ready
Tag. totoó; túnay; talagá
Ceb. tinuod; gayud
Ilo.: agpáyso
OCM: Norms (183), Logic (811)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
adj.
(nekeg-eleg, nekeg-) compatible; proper
Nekeg-eleg dè iya sikandan ne alunggun su neelin ne due trabahu ran.
That couple are compatible because both of them have job(s).
Eng. compatible; proper
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(eleg-eleg, rdpRT-) absolute
Eng. absolute
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something (egkeelehan, neelehan, egkA><an-) to be fit or be correct
Hentew buwa ka egkeelehan te sising ne imbeli ku?
Who can fit this ring which I've bought?
Eng. fit; correct; match; capable; able
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
v.
for someone (egpekeeleg, nekeeleg, agent, egpAkA-) to be able to carry something heavy
Eng. able, to (carry or bring something)
Ceb. makadaug
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
3.
vt.
for someone (eg-eleg-eleg, mig-eleg-eleg/in-eleg-eleg, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to try out something (eg-el-elehan, in-el-elehan/mig-el-elehan/pig-el-elehan, ig-eleg-eleg, in-eleg-eleg, instrument, eg><an-rdpCVC-, ig-rdpRT-) e.g., try on a shirt for size in a shop
Ig-eleg-eleg ku la igsaluub ka kumbalè ne nabeli ku.
I will try on the shirt that I bought.
O. Syn. geram —try
Eng. try; test; check out; sound out; evaluate; experiment
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(eleg-eleg, rdpRT-) an example; a sample
Eng. example; sample
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
