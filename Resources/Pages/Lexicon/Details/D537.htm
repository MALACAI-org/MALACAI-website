balahen
/balagɔn/
n.
“rattan”
 
1.
n.
Calamus_maximus
any type of vine
Wrd.Gloss: vine
Kenè niyu da-daati ka balahen su eggimuwen ku ne aweg.
Don't waste rattan because I'm going to make it into a bird chaser.
Egkapiyut e si Nunuy ka egganuy te balahen su mabehaat amana seini.

n.
Calamus_spp._Daemonorops_spp.
rattan
Wrd.Gloss: cane
labiren ne balahen
woven rattan
Cult. A climbing palm used for furniture, walking sticks, lashing. The fruit of Calamus usitatus, (WBM) tuvu, is called (WBM) erimuran and lepenu.
Eng. rattan; cane variety
Tag. uwáy
Ceb. uway

Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpamalahen, migpamalahen/impamalahen, pamalahen, agent, eg><ø-paN-) to strip balahen vines in order to make flexible cane somewhere (egpamalahenan, impamalahenan/migpamalahenan/pigpamalahenan, pamalahenan, location, eg><an-pA-)
Iyan ney rè dengan egpulunganan ka egpamalahen su eggimuwen ney ne bukag wey uwaan.
Before we used to strip rattan in order to make a basket and craddle.
Eng. strip
