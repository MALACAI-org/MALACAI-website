balarew
/baladɔw/
 
n.
a type of knife (with a double-edged blade)
Ka balarew iyan ka dè-deisek dè te ugpit piru dakel-dakel te ilab. Mahalang seini ne wey malidus wey sengekakuwa daruwa se galang kayi. Seini ka eggamiten te peggilang te me ayam iling te babuy, kambing, karniru, wey duma pad.
Egduslaken ad e perem te kunterè ku te balarew din su migsuman-suman sikandin ne in-ahew ku ka tratu rin.
My enemy is about to stab me with his knife because he thought that I took his girlfriend from him.
Eng. knife variety
