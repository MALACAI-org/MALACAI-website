leew
/lɔʔɔw/
 
n.
a wild animal or plant, e.g., leew ne sinkamas
Ka leew iyan ka me ayam ne diyè te kabenesan. Egkaayun ne tapey iya ne leew ka ayam wey ke miglunus dè iya miggipanew ligkat te kamuney kandin wey ke ugpaan din iling te miyew, me manuk-manuk ne narakep ne migmaamuk e.
Migdawi te leew ne miyew ka piyak ne in-itit ku.
Eng. wild animal; feral animal; beast; wild plant; wild abaka
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for an animal (egleew, agent, eg><ø-) to return to the wild
Egleew e man-e ka ayam ney ne ekang ne nalekaan.
Our owl pet was return again to the wild.
Eng. return to the wild
OCM: Domesticated Animals (231)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
