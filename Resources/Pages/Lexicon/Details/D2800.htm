kandin
/kandin/
pron.
“3s_NF(III)”
 
pron.
him; her; he; she; it
Diyè ka te kandin buyù te seleppì su naamin e ka keddì.
Ask money from him/her because mine is used up.
Warè din ingkeupii ka imbehey nu kandin ne kumbalè.
He didn't like the item of clothing you gave to him.
Wrd.Gloss: him; to_him; from_him; about_her; with_it; by_him
Gramm. 3rd person plural non-focus pronoun (Set III)
Eng. him (non-focus III); her (non-focus III); he( non-focus III); she (non-focus III); it (non-focus III)
Tag. siyá; kaniyá
Ceb. siya; kaniya
Binukid: kandin
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Pronouns (9.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
 
poss_pron.
his; her; its
Wrd.Gloss: his; her; its
Pigbalahad sikandin meyitenged te kandin ne prublima.
He is helpless to solve his problem.
Gramm. 3rd person singular possessive pronominal specifier (prepositional)
Eng. his; her; its
DDP: Pronouns (9.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
 
poss_pron.
his; hers
Wrd.Gloss: his; her
Kandin bes ka keenen ne nakeen ku ganna.
The food I had eaten accidentally) earlier was hers/his.
Kandin kun ka seleppì ne ingkita nu diyè te dalan.
The money you found on the road was his/hers.
Gramm. 3rd person singular possessive pronoun
Eng. hers; his; its; belong; owned; possess
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Pronouns (9.2.3)
