bayung
/bayuŋ/
v_rt.
“accuse”
 
v.
for someone (egbayung, migbayung/imbayung, agent, eg><ø-) to accuse someone (egbayungan, imbayungan/migbayungan/pigbayungan, eg><an-) of something (igbayung, ig><ø-)
Mania te egbayungan a nikeykew?
Why do you accused me?
Eng. accuse
Tag. magparátang; mag-akusá
Ceb. moakusar pasangil
Ilo.: pabasólen
OCM: Non-electronic Transmission of Messages (202), Ingroup Antagonisms (578), Litigation (691)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egkabayungan, nabayungan, agent, egkA><ø-) to be accused about something
Eng. accused
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
n.
(nabayungan, nA><an-) the person being accused of a misdeed
Kenè ka seled te baley ne warè etew su kema ke egkabayungan ka ke due egkalaag.
Don't enter in the house in absence of the owner because you might be accused if there is something lost.
Eng. accused
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
