kalasag
/kalasag/
n.
“shield”
 
1.
n.
a shield
Ka seini iyan sika se igbunbun wey ig-atang te pilak te duma eyew kenè egkasuhat ke egpilaken, seini kayu ne imbatekan te malalab wey meitem.
Emun ke warè kalasag ku, nasuhat e buwad te piet te kuntere ku.
If I had no shield, I might strike by a bow of my enemy.
Wrd.Gloss: shield
Cult. not round
Syn. teming
Eng. shield
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
n.
kalasag mountain which is located at the west side of Sinuda
Cult. Long time ago there was a guy from cotabato went here in Sinuda to request repayment of a debt to Datu Gusasa the leader of the community. But there was an unexpected tradegy happened, there was a strong rain accompanied with wind. The place was covered of water and all the people turned into eel. Only those guy holding the lady remained who was the lovable daughter of Datu Gusasa. The guy decided that the lady would be the payment of Gusasa's debt. But suddently there was a voice came somewhere and said "leave the woman for you to be saved and not included the wrath". But the guy shouted "no! I can't, I love this woman! and then the water chasing to them and and the guy used his shield to block the water and it becomes mountain.
Eng. kalasag mountain; mountain
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
