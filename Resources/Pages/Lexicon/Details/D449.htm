babbat
/babbat/
 
n.
a metal ankle legbracelet
Ka seini ne kalasiyan te saliyew iyan sika se diyè igtahù te paa ne malibesen wey egkeiling te tikes ka ulaula piru putew seini wey amana seini ne marahing.
Warè e pad iya nakkita te babbat ne kasangkapan sukad.
I've never seen an ankle metal bell legbracelet eversince.
Eng. metal bell; ankle bracelet
