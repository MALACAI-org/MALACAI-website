ulaluy
/ʔulaluy/
 
v.
for someone (eg-ulaluy, mig-ulaluy/in-ulaluy, ulaluy, agent, eg><ø-) to scream in pain (eg-ulaluyen, in-ulaluy/mig-ulaluy/pig-ulaluy, ulaluya, patient, eg><en-)
Ka eg-ulaluy iyan ka egpanaman-taman egpanguleyi tenged te mareet ne eggeramen. Ka duma eg-ulaluy tenged su nasakitan sikandan mismu wey egpabulig te duma ne egdineg kandan, ne ka duma eg-ulaluy tenged te egbulig te duma ne nasakitan. Ne ka duma eg-ulaluy eyew te pegpalihawang te masakit ne eggeramen dan wey sengekakuwa ne egkarumaan seini te pegsinehew.
Mig-ulaluy si Kiram diyè te kamet din te peg-ulì te uyab din diyè te Amirika.
Kiram cried just like screaming in pain when his girlfriend went home to America.
Eng. cry; yell; scream; wail
Ceb. nagbakho
OCM: Sensation and Perception (151)
DDP: Pain (2.5.6.1)
