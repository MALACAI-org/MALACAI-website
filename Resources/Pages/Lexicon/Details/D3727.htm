lepeng
/lɔpɔŋ/
 
adj.
same height with the others
Nekeg-iling ka kalayat te duma, warè nekegtimpa su nekegseked dè iya.
Lepeng ku rè iya ka iyam ne klasmit ney.
Our new classmate and I are the same height.
Eng. same height
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for two persons or things (egpekeglepeng, nekeglepeng, agent & theme, egpekeg-) to have the same height
Eng. same height, to have
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone or something (eglepeng, miglepeng/inlepeng, agent & theme, eg><ø-) to level about something (e.g.,height, ability or thingking) to other person or thing (eglepengan, inlepengan/miglepengan/piglepengan, location, eg><an-)
Eng. level, to; compare, to
