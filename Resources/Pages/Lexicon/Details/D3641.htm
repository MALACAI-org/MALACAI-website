legpeng
/lɔgpɔŋ/
v_rt.
“close”
Data Qual: Tano, QDL,Toto, Rose, Mera doesn't know this word but alex does.
 
v.
(eglegpengan, eg><an-) to close a door
Ka eglegpengan iyan sika se egsengsengan, egsagpengan. Egkaayun ne minsan nekey ka eglegpengan iling te lungag, gumawan, wey butbut. Ka tuyu te eglegpengan su eyew kenè egpakalebas ka minsan nekey ne diyè te ligwang wey ke diyè naa te seled.
Eglegpengan ku ka gumawan su makeseg ka kalamag.
I will close the door for the wind is heavy.
O. Syn. lekeb
