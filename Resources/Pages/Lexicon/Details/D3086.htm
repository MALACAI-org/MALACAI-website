kewengetè
/kɔwɔŋɔtɔ/
 
n.
discussion or trouble
Eng. argument
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
(egkewengetè, eg><ø-) to discuss themselves
Ka egkewengetè iyan ka egkasamuk wey egmagud-guhurey te masumpit ne egpekeiling e te lakulak ne mahulub. Ka duma egmaap-apuley ne ka duma egmekegtuleddu se suman-suman sikan naa ka egmalayat ka guhuren dan wey egmahulub.
Amana migkewengetè ka me hibateen ni Hisus diyè te balangey te migpanpanayan din sikandan meyitenged te igpatulin te paan te me Parisiyu.
Jesus disciples were discussing themselves when he warned them about the yeast of the Pharisees.
Migkewengetè dè iya ka me etew ne egmahuhurey te kuddè ne migpabutengey ne inkita ran langun.
The people are very noisy talking together about the horsefight that they had have seen altogether.
Migkewengete ka me istudyanti ne mig-ap-apuley diyè te klasi ran meyitenged te ubal ne iyan kun impuunan te etew.
The students are talking altogether on thier debate about the monkey which the man came from.
Eng. discuss; argue; stirred; troubled
