sagkil
/sagkil/
v_rt.
“suspicious”
 
vi.
for someone (egkasagkilan, nasagkilan, actor, egkA><an-) to became suspicious or even angry, e.g., at someone laughing or whispering
Ka sagkil ne lalag iyan igpasabut ka minsan nekey (lalag ma, wey weil) ne egpakaweil te kabelu te senge etew. Sabeka seini ne mareet ne eggeramen te me Matigsalug su sumansuman dan ne ingkaminusan sikandan te duma ne etew. Puun te sumansuman ne ingkaminusan sikandin te senge etew egsesale e su eyew egkabangun ka bansa rin.
Kenè kew mananaasey ke eglihad si Pebuwalan su mahaan sikandin egkasagkilan.
Don't whisper with each other when Pabuwalan passes because he is very suspicious.
Mahaan egsinehew ka anggam ku su mahaan sikandin egkasagkili.
My uncle easily cry because he touchy.
Eng. suspicious; wary; touchy; indignant
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Angry (3.4.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
Egpasagkilan ku si Nante eyew egkabelu.
I will make unnecessary/bad to Nante so that he will get angry.

Usage level: R
