bilu
/bilu/
n.
“dirt”
 
n.
a layer of dirt on a person's face or skin
Wrd.Gloss: dirt
Eng. dirt
Ceb. buling
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
adj.
(biluwen, -en) dirty
Biluwen amana ka kumbalè ku.
My dress is very dirty.
Eng. dirty
Cult. this word has no negative emotive component
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
v.
(kabiluwan, kA><an-) to get dirty
Kenè nu ikukumbalè ka kumbalè nu ne maangkag te egkekali te liyuan su kabeluwan.
Do not wear your white t-shirt when you digging a toilet for it will be get dirty.
Eng. dirty
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
