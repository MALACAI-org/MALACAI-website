buu
/buʔu/
n.
“boil”
 
n.
a boil
Wrd.Gloss: boil
Eng. boil; swelling
Tag. pigsá
Ceb. hubag
Ilo.: lítteg
OCM: Theory of Disease (753)
DDP: Swell (2.5.6.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for someone (egbuuwen, imbuu/migbuu/pigbuu, experiencer, patient, eg><en-) to be inflicted with boils
Kahi te me buyag, emun ke eg-iilu ki kun te batu su egbuuwen ki kun.
The elders said that if we wipe our bottom with stone we will be inflicted with boils.
Eng. boils, get
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for the skin (egbubuu, migbubuu/imbubuu, theme, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to swell (egbubuuwan, imbubuuwan/migbubuuwan/pigbubuuwan, location, eg><an-rdpCV-)
Eng. swell, grown to be; forming swell

Usage level: R
