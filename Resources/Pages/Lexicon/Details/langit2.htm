langit2
/laŋit/
v_rt.
“become_angry”
 
vi.
for someone (eglangit-langit, miglangit-langit/inlangit-langit, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to become angry at someone else (eglanglangitan, inlanglangitan/miglanglangitan/piglanglangitan, location, eg><an-rdpCVC-) and insult them by speaking in a raised voice
Eglangit-langit en iya layun sikandin te minsan hentew.
She is always raising her voice to someone.
Mani buwa ne eglanglangitan a ni anggam?
Why is it that uncle raised voice at me.
Syn. belu
Syn. langet
O. Syn. ehet
Eng. speak angrily; angrily speak; speak harshly; harshly speak; harsh; become angry; angry; insult; raised voice; loud voice; voice raised; rude
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Angry (3.4.2.3), Loud (2.3.2.4), Language and thought (3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
adj.
for speech to be (malangit, mA1-) loud and/or angry
Eng. loud voice; raised voice; voice raised; angry speech; hurting language; agressive; cynical
DDP: Loud (2.3.2.4), Language and thought (3)
