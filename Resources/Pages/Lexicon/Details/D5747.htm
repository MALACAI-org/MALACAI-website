seneb
/sɔnɔb/
v_rt.
“enter_water”
 
1.
v.
for someone (egseneb, migseneb/inseneb, seneb, agent, eg><ø-) to dive in water (egseneban, inseneban/migseneban/pigseneban, senebi, location, eg><an-)
Egseneb a diyè te weyig te Simud.
I will dive there at Simud river.
Eng. bathe
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpaseneb, migpaseneb/impaseneb, paseneban, agent, eg><ø-pA-) to dip someone or something (igpaseneb, impaseneb, instrument, igpA-) into the water (egpaseneban, inpaseneban/migpaseneban/pigpaseneban, pasenebi, location, eg><an-pA-)
Igpaseneb ku pad seinis babuy ku diyè te weyig.
I will dip my pig into the water.
Eng. dip, to
OCM: Food Preparation (252)
DDP: Be in water (1.3.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
v.
for someone (egseseneb, migseseneb/inseseneb, seseneb, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to dive in the water for fishing (egseseneban, inseseneban/migseseneban/pigseseneban, sesenebi, location, eg><an-rdpCV-)
Cult. Usually wearing goggles or a face mask. Diving goggles are often hand-crafted out of wood with two small round pieces of glass fitted in.
Egseseneb key kuntee.
We will go diving into the water for fishing today.
Eng. dive
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
