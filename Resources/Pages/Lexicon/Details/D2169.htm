heg-ut
/hɔgʔut/
Data Qual: Melyn says that in Panganan, they only use egpangeg-ut, not eg-ut
v_rt.
“extract_hemp_fibres”
 
v.
for someone (eggeg-ut/eg-eg-ut, miggeg-ut/mig-eg-ut/inggeg-ut/ing-eg-ut, geg-ut, agent, eg><ø-) to extract the white fibres from the hemp plant (eggeg-utan/eg-eg-utan, ingeg-utan/ing-eg-utan/miggeg-utan/mig-eg-utan/piggeg-utan/pig-eg-utan, geg-uti/eg-uti, location, eg><an-) with something (iggeg-ut/ig-eg-ut, inggeg-ut/ing-eg-ut, instrument, ig><ø-)
Eggeg-ut ki.
We will extract hemp.
Eggeg-utan ta ka abaka ney su malapung e.
We will extract hemp from our abaca plantation for it was very thick now.
Eng. extract hemp; harvest hemp; hemp, extract; strip hemp fibres
