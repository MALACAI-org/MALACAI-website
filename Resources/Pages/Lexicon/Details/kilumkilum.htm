kilumkilum
/kilumkilum/
 
adj.
mostly dark
Ka kilumkilum iyan ka igngaran te egkarusilem e wey ke egkasagkup e. Ka dangeb man-e ne kaluwasan kayi iyan ka egkapawè e wey kenè pad egkaliwaswas.
Kilumkilum e ne migginguma key puun te Dabaw su nareetan diyè te deralanen ka unturanan ney.
It was already dark when we arrived from Davao because our vehicle broke down along the way.
Eng. darkish; dark (for a day); evening
