unipurmi
/ʔunipurmi/
Var.1: unipulmi
n.
“uniform”
Fr. Eng. uniform
 
n.
uniform - this may refer to a school uniform, or to the revised traditional Manobo dress
Wrd.Gloss: uniform
Eg-ay-ayaran ku rè te egpi-pì ka unipurmi ku su eyew warad iya egkasamè ne belu.
I will wash my uniform very well so that no more dirt will be left
Eng. uniform; costume
