seleppì
/sɔlɔppiʔ/
n.
“money”
 
1.
n.
money
Wrd.Gloss: money
Due seleppì ne nakita ku diyè te dalan.
There was money which I've found in the way.
Eng. money
Tag. péra
Ceb. kwarta
Ilo.: kuwárta
OCM: Medium of Exchange (436)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
adj.
(selepianen, -an-en) wealthy
Eng. wealthy
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
adj.
(pakiselepp, paki-) materialistic
Pakiseleppì e pad diyè te kakey ku ne lukes.
I will produce money there at my eldest brother.
Eng. produce, to; materialistic
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
(egpaneleppì, migpaneleppì/inpaneleppì, paneleppì, eg><ø-paN-) to pay for
Miggendiyè ka amey ku te Tibungul su egpaneleppì sikandin diyè.
My father went to Tibungol to find money.
Eng. find, to; look, to
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
(egkaseleppi, nakaseleppi, egkA><ø-) to be paid for
Mahaan e egkaseleppì ka impambugsu ku ne sahing.
My planted banana is coming to be harvested and sales.
Eng. money, become; paid
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
n.
silver
Eng. silver
Tag. pílak
Ceb. salapì
Ilo.: pirák
OCM: Medium of Exchange (436), Mining and Quarrying (316), Metallurgy (325)
DDP: Metal (1.2.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
