lanab2
/lanab/
 
v.
for fat (eglanab, miglanab/inlanab, theme, eg><ø-) to float on the surface of water (eglanaban, inlanaban/miglanaban/piglanaban, location, eg><an-)
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se egkakita nu ka tabe diyè te weyig.
Eglanab e ka kalambuan te babuy sika te weyig.
The fat of the pork is floated and visible on water surface.
Eng. float, to; visible; in water
DDP: Visible (2.3.1.5)

Usage level: M
