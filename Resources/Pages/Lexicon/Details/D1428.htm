deha
/dɔga/
v_rt.
 
v.
for someone (egkareha, nareha, actor, egkA><ø-) to refuse to do something again because of the bad experience at first time
Ka kaluwasan te sika ne lalag iyan ka kenad e eg-abey man-e eggimu ke mareet ka impangkusan te an-anayan ne innimu rin, wey kenad e sikandin egkeupiian ne egkeyitabu pad man-e ka iling due.
Egkareha ad e keykew su warè ka migbayad te insambayan nu ne seleppì.
I will not allow you to borrow my money again for you didn't repay me.
Kenè ad egpasambey keykew su nareha ad e.
I never allow you to barrow something for you failed to return it.
Eng. bankrupt
Ceb. nguta
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egdereha, migdereha/indereha, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to refuse; to turn down; to not want
Migderehe e ka batè su in-ahawan te amey te paan.
The child turn down his food because his father XXX
Cult. e.g. a child to turn down his food e.g., not contented of something given to him as their share
Eng. refuse; turn down; not want
DDP: Want (3.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
