suruk
/suduk/
 
v.
for someone's blood (egsuruk, migsuruk/insuruk) to become high or rise to be angry at someone else (egsurukan, insurukan/migsurukan/pigsurukan, suruki, location, eg><an-)
Egsuruk ka langesa ku ke egkiteen ku ka etew ne migsuntuk dengan keddì.
I got angry when I see the man who punched me before.
Egsuruk ka langesa ku ke egpakarineg a te due batè ne eggilabetan te amey rin.
I realy got angry when I hear that a child is raped by her father.
Mahaan egsuruk ka langesa ku ke egpakarineg a ne miggumaan te amey ku ka iney ku.
I will be easily get angry when I hear that my father beaten my mother.
Cult. It seems like an idiom that not totally the blood will become high or arise but just getting angry to someone.
Eng. angry
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
v.
for something (egpakasuruk, nakasuruk, agent, egpAkA-) to cause by someone to get angry (igpakasuruk/impakasuruk, reason, igpAkA-)
Eng. angry
