sukul
/sukul/
v_rt.
“fight_back”
 
vi.
for someone (egsukul, agent, eg><ø-) to fight back?; to speak back?; to rebel against someone else (egsukulan, location, eg><an-)
Masalig ne me batè kuntee ne egsukul te me amey wey iney ran.
Many children these days contend against their fathers and their mothers.
Egsukul a ke kenè ku egkeyimu ka igpeyimu keddì.
I will resist if I can't do what's been asked of me to do.
O. Syn. supak
O. Syn. atu
Eng. contend against; talk back; rebel; fight back; speak back; stand against; resist; have on; disagree; disallow; disobey

Usage level: L
