tanggap
/taŋgap/
v_rt.
“receive”
 
vt.
for someone (egtanggap, agent, eg><ø-) to receive something (egtanggapen, theme, eg><en-) (e.g. food that is set in front of him)
Wrd.Gloss: receive
Egtanggap kew red ke egbeheyan ku sikaniyu te aheley?
Do you accept when I offer you corn?
Egtanggapen e bured te meistara ney ke eglibed e red ka eg-iskuyla?
Shall I be accepted of our teacher when I go back to school again?
Eng. receive; accept
Ceb. dawat
DDP: Accept (3.3.5.1)
