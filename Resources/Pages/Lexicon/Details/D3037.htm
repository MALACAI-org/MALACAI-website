kepis
/kɔpis/
v_rt.
“contract”
 
v.
for something (egkepis, actor, eg><ø-) to shrink or contract
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se puun te dakel ne egdeisek uya su neinitan wey ke naleeb.
Wrd.Gloss: contract
Ant. ketul
Ka pandesal, egtengtengan nu, malibusen, eggen-genan nu, egkepis.
If you look at "pan de sal", it's round, but if you hold it, it shrinks.
Egkepis ka bula ke egkaawaan te kalamag.
If a ball loses it's air, it collapses.
Eng. contract; collapse; shrink
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for someone (egpakepis, agent, egpA><ø-) to pull their stomach in
Ant. egpaketul
Eng. pull stomach in
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
