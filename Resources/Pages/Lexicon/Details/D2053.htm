gulemek
/gulamɔk/
v_rt.
“screw-up”
 
vt.
for someone (eggulemek, agent, eg><ø-) to screw-up or crumple something, e.g, a rag or piece of paper (eggulemeken, patient, eg><en-)
Wrd.Gloss: crumple
Inggulemek te Iney ku ka pepil ku su kakuwa te kenà a eg-iskuwilya.
Kenè nu gulemeka ka pepil ku ne insulatan ku te guguheren.
Ant. bukalas
Eng. screw-up; crumple
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
