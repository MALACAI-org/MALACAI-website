halin
/halin/
v_rt.
“transfer”
 
n.
the money obtained by selling or trading something
Eng. proceeds; income; profit; money
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (eggalin, miggalin/inggalin, agent, eg><ø-) to transfer something (eggalinen, inggalin/miggalin/piggalin, theme, eg><en-) somewhere (eggalinan, miggalinan/inggalinan/piggalinan, location, eg><an-)
Eggalin key kaaselem diyè te iyam ne ugpaan ney.
We will transfer tomorrow to our new residence.
Keilangan ne eggalinen ka hemey diyè te kuren eyew egpakasusubba kid e man-e.
It is necessary to transfer the rice cooked at the pot so that we can cook again.
Eng. transfer; move; shift
Tag. lumípat
Ceb. mobalhin
Ilo.: umális
OCM: Weight Moving (483)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
adj.
(pangalin, paN-) contagious
Hendei niyu bes pangalina ka me bulak ku kayi?
Where did you transfered my flowers here?
Eng. contagious
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
adj.
(eggalin-halin, eg><ø-rdpRT-) flighty
Kenè kew layun eggalin-galin te eg-ugpaan niyu su eyew kenè kew egkeyirapan ka egpanlaglag te me kasangkapan niyu.
Don't always transfer anywhere so that you will not be difficultied to carry your things.
Eng. flighty
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
adj.
(halin-halin, rdpRT-) migratory
Layun dè eggalin-halin si Milin te egkeugpaan din.
Melin is always transfering residence.
Eng. migratory
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
adj.
(halinen, -en) movable
Eng. movable
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
adj.
(naalin, nA-) changed; transferred
Cult. used of the date of a meeting
Warà a nakapalintutuu te pegkakita ku ki Bin te impaley-as su naalin e ka ulaula rin.
I almost not believing that Ben was dead because his face was changed.
cf. bala
Eng. changed; transferred
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
adj.
(mahalin, mA1-) unsteady
Eng. unsteady
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
adj.
(kahalin, kA-) changeable
Kahalin layun ka puhawang din.
His mind is changeable.
Eng. changeable; unsteady
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
adj.
(mighalin, mig><ø-) variable
Nekey buwa ne uras ne mighalin ensi Inuk ganna te maselem?
What time Inuk move in the morning?
Eng. variable
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
(iggalin, ig><ø-) to transplant; to translate
Iggalin teg pamula ka me bulak diyè te me lata.
Transplant the flowers into thin can.
Eng. transplant; translate
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
(eggalinan, eg><an-) to jump across (e.g. a flea)
Nekey-a ka egkaayun ne eggalinan due te hemey?
What will be the good empty space where we can trasfer the rice.
Eng. jump across
