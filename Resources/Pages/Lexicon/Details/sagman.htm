sagman
/sagman/
v_rt.
“give_attention”
 
v.
for someone (egsagman, migsagman/insagman, agent, eg><ø-) to give attention to visitors
Ka sagman ne lalag egpekeg-iling seini te sahipe, elit-elit wey atiman.
Sagman kad ma due Noel su egkaaminan ka te keenen.
Noel take+action now because you might run out of food.
Keyey, mahirap e seini ne timpu su si Datu Gawilan migsagman.
Oh, it's dangerous time now because Datu Gawilan take an action.
(egsagmanen, insagman/migsagman/pigsagman, theme, eg><en-) by preparing them food
Wrd.Gloss: show_favor
Gramm. This is considered to be deep Matigsalug.
Kenad iya egsagman si Andung te asawa rin te iyam pad sikandan nekeg-engked.
Andung no longer gave any attention to his wife when they were newly separated.
Eng. give attention; show favor; prepare; hospitable; be hospitable; help
DDP: Show, let someone see (2.3.1.4), Attention (3.1.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for someone (egpasagman, migpasagman/impasagman, agent, eg><ø-pA-) to be shown favor
Egpasagman ki te Manama.
We will be shown favor by God.
DDP: Show, let someone see (2.3.1.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
