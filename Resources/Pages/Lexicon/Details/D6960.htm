tunga
/tuŋa/
 
n.
half
Ka tunga iyan ka lalag ne migpasabut te liware dè wey ke nabaad dè.
Tunga dè te kilu te babuy ka imbeli ku su kenè egpekeutel ka seleppì ku.
Only half kilo of pork I've bought for my money is not enough.
Eng. half
Ceb. tunga
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
adj.
(katunga, kA-) half
Eng. half

v.
for something (egkatunga, natunga, theme, egkA><ø-) to split in half; to divide into two equal portions
Hendini nu ka katunga te tirè ne in-uus nu.
Eng. split; divide; halve; separate
