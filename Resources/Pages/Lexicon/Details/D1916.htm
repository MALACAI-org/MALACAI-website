geleb
/gɔlɔb/
v_rt.
“feed”
 
v.
for bees and wasps e.g., patiyukan, tameing, tabuuwan, tabubuleg (eggeleb, migeggeleb/inggeleb, geleb, agent, eg><ø-) to feed from flowers kabukaran e.g., of the ulayan and lawaan trees (eggeleben, inggeleb/miggeleb/piggeleb, geleba, patient, eg><en-)
Wrd.Gloss: feed
Pitawa niyu kun ke hendei eg-ulì ka tameing ne eggeleb te kabukaran te aheley ku.
Eng. feed, for bees
