habeng
/habɔŋ/
Var.1: abeng
 
n.
fence
Ka habeng iyan ka alad ne kenè amana ne malig-en wey matikang iling te ig-alad te babuy wey te baley. Seini ne klasi te para rè te me baled wey ig-atang te kenè amana ne derakel ne me mananap.
Keilangan ne due habeng te baled su eyew kenè egpakaalihu ka ambew.
There should be fence of a trap so that mouse cannot by-pass.
Eng. stake; fence
