sabew
/sabɔw/
n.
“broth”
 
n.
soup
Wrd.Gloss: broth
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se meupiya ne eghihupen ne weyig te gulay ta wey ke weyig te ngalap ne duen e bitsin wey asin.
Dakel ka sabew te gulay ku gabii.
My (cooked) vegetables had a lot of water (with them) yesterday.
Eng. soup; broth; juice; water, vegetable; vegetable water
