bangkalew3
/baŋkalɔw/
 
n.
a thin rotating object, e.g., a frisbee
Eng. frisbee
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
1.
n.
bicycle
Iyan ka due ligid ne daruwa wey egkeunturan, due pinnuuwan wey gengenan, wey eggipanew ne innimu puun kayu wey putew naa. Ka eg-untud keilangan ne egtimbangan din ka lawa rin wey keilangan ne kenè sikandin egpanuked ke egpallahuy e seeyè.
Napiulan dengan si Hisi su nakasuwey te dalan ka bangkalew ne mig-unturan din.
Jessie was injured long time ago because the bicycle went off the road.
Eng. bicycle
