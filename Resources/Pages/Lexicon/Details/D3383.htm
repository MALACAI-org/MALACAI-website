lablab2
/lablab/
 
v.
to singe or barbecuing something on fire
Ka eglablab iya ka egtuug te minsan nekey ne due bulbul, luwit diyè te ampew te hapuy iling te egtutuug te aheley ne kenè egluwitan wey egsisinalab te manuk, babuy wey duma pad ne me mananap ne due bulbul.
Iglablab ku rè ka aheley eyew mahaan dè egkalutù.
I will just singe the corn in order to cook it quick.
Kahiyen ku ne kenè egkaayun ne iglablab ka binggala su kema ke egkatutung, te kenè bes ded.
I thought it's not possible to singe on fire the cassava because it might be burnt, in fact it will not.
Eng. singe; barbecue; bake; roast; cook
Ceb. sugba
Tag. kilaw
