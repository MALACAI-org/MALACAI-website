taneb1
/tanɔb/
adj_rt.
“middle_of_night”
 
adj.
(mataneb, mA1-) in the middle of the night
Eng. middle of night; night, middle of
Mananey nekeyimata ka asawa ku ganna te maselem su mataneb e nekeulì ka egpaneng-ew.
My husband woke up late because he came back here from catching frog was already middle in the night.
Eng. midnight
