leus2
/lɔʔus/
 
v.
(egkaleusan, eg><ø-) to pierce somebody with a spear by mistake when just playing around
Egkaayunan te seini ne lalag ka minsan nekey ne intiyuk ne mig-abas wey due lein ne neumaan wey ke nasundang naa.
Kenè ka parani te egpaneyi su kema ke egkaleusan ka te simat.
Don't draw near to the one sewing because might be pierced with a needle.
Syn. abbas
Syn. tued
Syn. tiyuk
Eng. pierce
Tag. maglagós; tumúsok
Ceb. duslak
Ilo.: tudúken
OCM: Bodily Injuries (752)
