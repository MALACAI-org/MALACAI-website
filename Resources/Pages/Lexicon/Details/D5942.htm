sinug-ung
/sinugʔuŋ/
v_rt.
“meet”
 
1.
vi.
for someone (egkasinug-ung, nasinug-ung, actor, egkA><ø-) to chance to meet somebody else or accidentally meeting someone elseon the trail
Egkasinug-ung kud e kuntee ka etew ne igkeepes ku.
Eng. meet; encounter
 
v.
for someone (egsinug-ung, migsinug-ung/insinug-ung, agent, eg><ø-) to meet something or somebody else (egsinug-ungen, insinug-ung/migsinug-ung/pigsinug-ung, theme, eg><en-) (accidentally) on the trail
Egsinug-ungen ku ka apù ku ne kunteem pad iya migleuy kanami.
I will meet my grandparents who are first time to visit us.
Eng. meet
Tag. salúbong
Ilo.: sabaten
OCM: Social Relationships and Groups (571)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
2.
v.
for someone (egsinug-ung, migsinug-ung/insinug-ung, agent, eg><ø-) to move against the force (egsinug-ungen, insinug-ung/migsinug-ung/pigsinug-ung, theme, eg><en-)
Eng. move, to (against)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
