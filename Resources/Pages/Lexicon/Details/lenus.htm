lenus
/lɔnus/
v_rt.
Wrd.Gloss: escape
 
v.
for someone (eglenus, miglenus/inlenus, agent, eg><ø-) to run away from doing something
Ka lenus ne lalag nekeg-iling dè te migpallahuy, ka nekalein dè te lenus su due egpuunan wey warè sabeka ne etew ne nakanengneng. Kaayun ne diyè sikandin migbayè te gumawan, diyè te bintana, wey ke minsan hendei, basta su warè nakakita.
Lenus kad Beyi su egbunuen ka te amey nu.
Beyi escape now because your father will kill you.
Miglenus e si Dayan su egpaasawaan te amey rin te lukes ne warè din ikeupii.
Dayan ran away because her father wanted her to marry a man that she didn't like.
Wrd.Gloss: escape
Eng. escape; run away; flee; fled; get out of something; avoid
