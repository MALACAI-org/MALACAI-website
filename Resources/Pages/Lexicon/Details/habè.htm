habè
/habɔʔ/
 
v.
(egkahabè, nahabè, egkA><ø-) to cut with a slicing action
Ka egkahabe iyan sika se egkapalian te minsan nekey ne paahi iling te: egkatibas, egkasundag, egkasiyal, egkasanggat te duhi, egkapilak, egkapusil.
Egkahabe ka te mahalang ne ugpit.
You will be cut with the sharp bolo.
Syn. tibas
Syn. pisang
Eng. cut (slice); slice; slash; tabas
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (eggabè, miggabè/inggabè, gabea, eg><ø-) to cut something or someone with a slicing action (eggabeen, inggabè/miggabè/piggabè, gabeen, patient, eg><en-)
Kenè nu gabea ka laplap te belad nu ne nalundis su igpareket te red.
Don't slice off the skin of your arm that was grazed because we will stick it back down.
Syn. tibas
Syn. pisang
Eng. cut (slice); slice; slash
Ceb. tabas
