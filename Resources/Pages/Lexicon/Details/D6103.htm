sumpangil
/sumpaŋil/
 
n.
“beard”
Tag. bigote
Ceb. balbas; bungut
DDP: Hair (2.1.5)
 
adj.
(sumpangilen, -en) adult; old; having beard
Wrd.Gloss: old enough
Ka sumpangilen iyan ka sumpeen e wey ke buyag e wey kenad ne batè pad. Ke eggamiten ka seini ne lalag, kenè amana iyan igpasabut ka sumpe te etew/mananap ke kenè ka kabuyahi rin.
Egkelag pad si Utu minsan sumpangilen e.
Utu still playing (along with chidren) even he is old enough.
Eng. bearded; whiskery; facial hair; adult
OCM: Age stratification (561)
DDP: Hair (2.1.5), Adult (2.6.4.4)
