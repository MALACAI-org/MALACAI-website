bulan
/bulan/
n.
“moon/month”
 
1.
n.
moon
Wrd.Gloss: moon
Eghayag e ka bulan.
It is a first quarter moon now.
Tag. palakí ng buwán bagóng buwán
Ceb. udtong bulan bag-ong bulan
Ilo. umuna nga kidém baru nga búlan
Egkapareng e ka bulan.
It is a last quarter moon now.
Tag. paliít ang buwán papatáy ang buwán
Ceb. himatayun
Ilo. maudí nga kidém
Eng. moon
Tag. búwan
Ceb. bulan
Ilo.: búlan
Tigwanon: bulan
Binukid: bulan
WBM: bulan
Ilianen: bulan
Tagbawa: bulan
Cotabato Manobo: bulan
Sarangani: bolan
Dibabawon: buan
Kinamigin: buan
Obo: buan
Cagayano: bułan
Proto: bulan
OCM: Ethnometeorology (821)
DDP: Moon (1.1.1.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
2.
n.
(bulan) month
Wrd.Gloss: month
Eng. month
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
vi.
for someone or something (egbulan, migbulan/imbulan, bulan, actor, eg><ø-) to be in a certain state (e.g. being owned, being lost) for exactly a month
Egbulan e si Amè ka nalaag.
Dad has already been lost for a month.
 
vi.
for sometone or something (egbulanan, imbulanan/migbulanan/pigbulanan, bulani, location, eg><an-) to be in a certain state or place for nearly a month
Egbulanan e si Amè ka nalaag
Dad has been lost for almost a month.
Eng. month, for a
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adv.
(sinundul ne bulan) next month
Eng. next month
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
3.
vi.
for a woman (egbulanen, imbulan/migbulan/pigbulan, bulana, theme, eg><en-) to menstruate; to have a monthly period
Eng. menstruate; period, monthly
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
4.
n.
(bulan-bulan, rdpRT-) a type of stinging plants that cause you one month to suffer for pain
Eng. stinging plants
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
5.
n.
(bulan-bulan, rdpRT-) a Matigsalug game perform during full moon evening
Eng. game
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
6.
n.
(bulanan, -an) shield middle part
Wrd.Gloss: center part
Cult. The middle part of a shield which is form like a moon
Eng. middle part
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
