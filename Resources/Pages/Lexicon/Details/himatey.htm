himatey
/himatɔy/
Var.1: imatey
v_rt.
“kill”
 
vt.
for a person or animal (eggimatey/eg-imatey, miggimatey/mig-imatey/inggimatey/in-imatey, egpangimatey, migpangimatey/impangimatey, agent, eg><ø-, eg><ø-paN-) to kill someone or something else (eggimatayan/eg-imatayan, inggimatayan/in-imatayan/miggimatayan/mig-imatayan/piggimatayan/pig-imatayan, theme, eg><an-) using deadly weapons or anything (iggimatey/ig-imatey, inggimatey/in-imatey, instrument, ig><ø-)
Kenè niyu lalahi te mareet si Agustin su eggimatey mulè sika ne etew.
Don't you try to tell Agustin a bad one because he kill you.
Miggimatayan dan ka kalabew su masalig ka me etew ne nalibulung te aldew te Matigsalug.
They killed a carabao because there were so many people gathered during Matigsalug Day.
Piggimatayan din ka sabeka ne etew su nasalaan sikandin.
He killed that one man because he commited sin against him.
Eng. kill; execute; murder; slaughter

The two people were killing each other because they disagreed with each other.
