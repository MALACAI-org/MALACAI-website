degpak
/dɔgpak/
v_rt.
“throw”
Var. debpak
 
vt.
for someone (egdegpak, migdegpak/migdebpak, agent, eg><ø-) to throw something overhand (igdegpak, indegpak/indegpak, pigdegpak/pigdebpak, patient, ig><ø-) at something or someone (egdegpakan, indegpakan/migdegpakan/pigdegpakan, location, eg><an-)
Iyan seini ka pegsabulak wey peg-antug te minsan nekey diyè te minsan hendei. Egkaayun ne diyè ig-antug ka minsan nekey ne inggen-genan te aw-awangan, tanè, etew, kayu wey weyig, wey minsan nekey pad. Egkaayun ne due eggisuhaten wey se warè su ka impurtante kayi ne egkalekaan ke miggen-genan ne ig-antug wey ke igsabulak naa.
Egdegpaken ku sikeykew te batu su miggun-aan e ma nikeykew te egdegpak.
I will stone you because you stoned me first.
Neinu-inu si Pastur Paundag te duèn e migdahing diyè te atep din kunaleg te indegpakan bes e sikandin te teì.
Pastur Paundag surprised that there is sound on his roof knowingly that he throwing a dung.
