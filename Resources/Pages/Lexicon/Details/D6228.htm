tagpì
/tagpiʔ/
v_rt.
“slap”
 
1.
vt.
for someone (egtagpì, agent, eg><ø-) to spank or hit (with palm of hand) someone else (egtagpien, theme, eg><en-)
Kenè kew ngisi ke eglihad si Engkil su egtagpì mulè sika ne etew.
O. Syn. tampaling
Eng. spank; slap; hit
Tag. palúin
Ilo.: baúten
OCM: Techniques of Inculcation (861), Social Control (626)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
v.
(egtagpien, eg><en-) to thresh grain (by hitting the stalks on the ground)
Eng. thresh
OCM: Annual Cycle (221)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
3.
v.
(egtagpien, eg><en-) to hit with (the palm or side of) the hand
Hentew a ubag sika se etew ne egpanampelek keddì su egtagpien ku.
Eng. hit with hand; slap
Tag. sampál
Ceb. sagpà
Ilo.: sipatén
OCM: Offenses Against the Person (683), Bodily Injuries (752)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
4.
v.
to hit vertically (with a stick or other instrument)
Eng. hit vertically
OCM: Offenses Against the Person (683), Bodily Injuries (752)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
