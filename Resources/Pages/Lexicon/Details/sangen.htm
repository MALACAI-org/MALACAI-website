sangen
/saŋɔn/
“attach”
 
vt.
for someone (egsangen, migsangen, agent, eg><ø-) to attach something (esp. a yoke) (isangen) to something (esp. a water buffalo) (insangen)
Ka egsangen iyan ka egtangen te yuggu te kalabew wey te lahitan. Deisek dè ka egkaayunan te seini ne lalag su due degma ne lalag te duma ne himu iling te eg-uyamu te baled, egpes-ek te baley, wey masalig pad ka duma.
Isangen nu pa ka lahitan su egpanleuy e pad te abakaan.
Kindly attach the the steel stripping bar because I'm going to survey the abaka plantation.
cf. lahitan —steel stripping bar
Eng. attach; tie on; set up; organize; install; assemble
Ceb. taud
