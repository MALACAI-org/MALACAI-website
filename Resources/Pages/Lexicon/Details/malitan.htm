n.
(igmalitan, ig><ø-) wife
Ka igpasabut kayi iyan sika se asawa ne malitan, ka dangeb te lukes, ka paris te lukes ne malitan.
Miggendiyè te Bariyu ka igmalitan ku su egpalit te hemey.
My wife went to village to buy a rice.
