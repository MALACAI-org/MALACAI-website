apil
/ʔapil/
v_rt.
“include”
Fr. Ceb. apil
 
adj.
including
Syn. lagkes
Ka kaluwasan te lalag ne apil iyan ka pegtipul diyè te duma ne due inggimu wey ke migkekelag naa. Minsan nekey ne egpakatimul wey egpakalagkes, sikan en iya ka igpasabut kayi.
Egkeupian a ne eg-apil kayi te egbubula su eyew egkateu a degma.
I want to join to the basketball game so that I can learn also.
Eng. including; plus
Tag. patí
Ceb. apil
OCM: Numeration (802)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
vi.
for someone (eg-apil, mig-apil/in-apil, agent, eg><ø-) to include something (eg-apilen, in-apil/mig-apil/pig-apil, theme, eg><en-)
Kenè ka eg-apilen te egbubula su amana kad e egkateu ne egbubula.
You won't be included in playing basketball because you'rè very skillful at playing basketball.
Eng. include; involve; participate; enter; enrol; join
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
v.
for someone (eg-apil-apil, mig-apil-apil, in-apil-apil, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to participate any activities (eg-apil-apilan, in-apil-apilan/mig-apil-apilan/pig-apil-apilan, location, eg><an-rdpRT-)
Eng. participate; associate
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
