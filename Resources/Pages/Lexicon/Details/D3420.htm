lahetlet
/lahɔtlɔt/
 
vt.
worse
Ka lahetlet iyan ka nasì wey ke egtamtamanan dè iya te eggeramen. Egpekegduma seini te dalu wey kenè degma egkaayunan ka duma pad ne me lalag kayi.
Nasì dè iya lahetleti si Luminaya te buwe minsan migpanginum e sikandin te me delemetan.
Luminaya's cough indeed gotten worse even she took medicines.
Miglahetletan dè iya mulè si Amè te dalu rin su kenè ma egpalintutuu te talagbawì.
Father's illness got worse for he doesn't believe to the doctor.
Eng. worst; worse; badly ill; remission; get sicker; sicker, get
