yayung
/yayuŋ/
v_rt.
“carry_stretcher”
 
n.
Eng. stretcher
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for two or more people (egyayung, migyayung/inyayung, agent, eg><ø-) to carry someone or something (egyayungan, migyayungan/inyayungan/pigyayungan, location, eg><an-) with a stretcher (igyayung, inyayung, theme, ig><ø-) a stretcher
Migpangumew si Aliks te etew ne egpabulihen din te egyayung te bukag ne napenù te keenen.
Alex call somebody to help him to carry the basket full of food.
Egtempug a te kayu ne mabasag su igyayung ney te babuy ne neutel ney.
I'll cut some small and a hard wood for stretcher of caught wild boar.
Eng. carry on stretcher; carry on poles
