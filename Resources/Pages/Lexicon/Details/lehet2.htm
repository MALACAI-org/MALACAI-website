lehet2
/lɔgɔt/
 
adj.
eager
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se egkarayan ne egkalangetan, wey egkabulù amana ka sabeka ne etew puun dutu egpanamtamanan din e te egpanumbag ka kalaban din wey ke immenu rin.
Eng. eager; encourage
Ceb. nahagit
OCM: Drivers and Emotions (152)
DDP: Excited (3.4.1.4.1), Enthusiastic (3.4.1.4.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egkalelehet, nalelehet, agent, egkA><ø-rdpCV-) to eager or to be encouraged to do something (igkalelehet, ingkalelehet, reason, igkA><ø-rdpCV)
Nalelehet a ka insumbag su intampelè e ma ni kandin.
I was eager to punch him cause he teased me.
Eng. eager; encouraged
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something (egpakalelehet, nakalelehet, actor, egpAkA-rdpCV-) to cause someone to be eager, or to be the thing (igpakalelehet/impakalelehet, reason, igpAkA-rdpCV) which causes someone to be eager
Eng. eager; encouraged
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
