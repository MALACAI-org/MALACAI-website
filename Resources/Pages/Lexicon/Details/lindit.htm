lindit
/lindit/
adj_rt.
“dirty”
 
adj.
(malindit, mA1-) dirty
Impangawe ney ka me lapek wey me basak diyè te lama ney su malindit e amana.
We removed junks and mud in our yard because it's so dirty.
Syn. maligsem
Ant. malinis
Eng. dirty; filthy; unclean; vile; unhealthy; impure
Tag. marumí
Ceb. hugaw
Ilo.: narugít
OCM: Water and Thirst (271), Housekeeping (356), Personal Hygiene (515), Public Health and Sanitation (744)
DDP: Water quality (1.3.6)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
v.
for someone (eglindit, miglindit, agent, eg><ø-) to dirty something (eglinditan, location, eg><an-)
Eng. make dirty; desecrate
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
