kelesuddè
/kɔlɔsuddɔʔ/
n.
 
n.
a platform such as a porch or a raised bench for sleeping
Ka kelesuddè iyan ka inlantawan ne ugpaan ne eggimelayan. Sabeka seini ne baad te baley ne diyè lenged te atep wey ke diyè naa lenged te seeg. Egkaayun degma ne kenè ne lenged te gumawan wey ke diyè naa te ampew.
Egkeupian a ne egpinpinnuu diyè te kelesuddè ke warè eggimuwen ku.
I want to be seated in the balcony if I have no work.
Eng. seat; bench
