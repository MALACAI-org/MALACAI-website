tumbuk
/tumbuk/
 
adj.
something equivalent to something
Iyan igpasabut te tumbuk ka igsaliyu te minsan nekey ne igbehey diyè te senge-etew. Egkahamit degma ka seini ne lalag te pegtinurù te minsan hentew ne iyan miggimu te minsan nekey ne meupiya ke mareet naa.
Si Undon ka intumbuk ni Nante ne iyan migtakew te seleppi din.
Nante accused Undon who stole his money.
Ke egkeupian kew ne egpakanengneng te urasyun hendiyè kew re te amey ku piru tumbuka niyu.
If you want to know magic spell just go to my father but pay it.
Eng. pay bride price; accuse; exchange
Ceb. bugay
