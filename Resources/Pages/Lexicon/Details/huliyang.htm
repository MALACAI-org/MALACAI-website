huliyang
/huliyaŋ/
 
vi.
(egkahuliyang, nahuliyang, egkA><ø-) in trouble
Ka guliyang ne lalag nekeg-iling te samuk, piru te seini ne lalag amana egkanengnengi ne suble e ne samul. Kaayun ne egkallingawan e ka minsan nekey(e.g,. anak, me karatuan, wey minsan nekey.)
Nahuliyang ka Pilipinas dengan te leg-un 1941-45 su eg-ahawen te Epun.
The Philippines is in trouble in the year 1941-45 because Epun want's to own it.
Nahuliyang ka Simud dengan su migribildi ka buyag.
The Simud is in trouble long ago because the leader rebelled.
Eng. trouble; commotion; disturbancee; disorder; conflict; disagreement; confusion
Tag. magkaguló
Ceb. kagubut
