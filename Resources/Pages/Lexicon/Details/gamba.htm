gamba
/hamba/
 
v.
(eghamba, mighamba/hamba, eg><ø-) overpower
Ka hamba ne lalag iyan igpasabut ka egkariekan ka katenged wey ke egkaribabawan naa. Mareet ka egkaambaan ka katenged te sabeka ne etew su kene egpakaweil sikandin te igkeupii rin.
Kene kew hamba te duma ne etew su mareet.
Do not overpower other because that is not good.
Nahambaan si Chairman te duma rin ne etew seeye naa ka nakabehey sikandin te seleppi.
Chairman was overpowered by his member that is why cause him to give money.
Eng. overpower; overmaster; overwhelm
