lingew
/liŋɔw/
v_rt.
“forget”
 
vt.
for someone (eglingew, miglingew/inlingew, agent, eg><ø-) to (intentionally) forget someone or something (eglingawan, inlingawan/miglingawan/piglingawan, theme, eg><an-)
Ka eglingawan iyan ka kenad pegsuman-suman te minsan nekey wey minsan hentew. Seini ne lalag migpanengneng te pegbalahad wey kenad pegsahipè te minsan nekey wey minsan hentew. Eg-aween e diyè te suman-suman wey pusung.
Eglingawan kud e ka mareet ne me himu ku su egpamakey ad te Manama.
I will forget my bad deeds for I will serve God.
Eng. forget; ignore
DDP: Ignore (3.1.2.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vt.
for someone (egkalingew, nalingew, actor, egkA><ø-) to (unintentionally) forget something (egkalingawan, nalingawan, theme, egkA><an-)
Ka egkalingawan iyan ka egkabalahad wey kenad e egkeuyan wey ke egkaruma. Migpasabut seini te minsan nekey ne naahat wey warè nasuman-suman.
Malasi a egkalingew te eg-uyanen ku ne kasangkapan ke egkaana-ana ad ne eggipanew.
I'm always forgot to brought things I needed during my journey to hurry.
Nalingawan ku ka litratu nu.
I forgot (to bring) your photo.
Ant. suman-suman —remember
Eng. forget
