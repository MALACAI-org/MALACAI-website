salut
/salut/
v_rt.
“soon_after”
 
vi.
for someone (egsalut, agent, eg><ø-) to do something soon after something else, e.g., a write an answer to a letter straight away, or to visit someone when they've only just arrived home
Ka egsalut iyan ka sahuhunè ne peggimu te minsan nekey pegkapenga te duma iling te peglalag, pegsulat, pegtabak wey duma pad.
Mesi egginguma key e ne egsalut dè iya egpanumbaley ka sumbaley ney.
Even if we've(excl.) just arrived, our(excl.) neighbors visit straight away.
Egsalut dè iya degma si Kiram ne egtabak te Melikanù ne egpanginsè kandin.
Kiram immediately answers to the American which is asking him.
Migsalutan e rè iya te amey ku te migbaras te nahepù ku ka ugpit din.
My father right away beat me when I broke his bolo.
Eng. soon after; straight away; immediately; directly
DDP: Soon (8.4.6.4.1), Immediately (8.4.6.4.4)
