red
/dɔd/
 
part.
it indicates again and again
Ka seini egkeiling te abey wey iling te sikandin ded man-e warad panahun te duma.
Iyan ke red man-e ampu su masakit ka abelengan ku.
You will be the one to pray again because my throat is painful.
Eg-uran bu red man-e su dakel ka kibel.
Maybe it will rain again because the rain cloud is big.
Eng. again; repeat; once again
Ceb. gihapun
DDP: Particles (9.2.6)

Usage level: B
