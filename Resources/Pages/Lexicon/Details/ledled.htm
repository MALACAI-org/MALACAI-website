ledled
/lɔdlɔd/
v_rt.
“move_to_corner”
 
vi.
for someone (egledled, migledled/inledled, agent, eg><ø-) to move out of the way, e.g., into a corner
Ka egledled iyan pegdapig diyè te sungguk wey ke pegpeilis naa eyew kenè egkariekan wey egkasikeung naa. Iyan man-e lein ne kaluwasan kayi ka pegtahù te minsan nekey diyè te ilis wey ke sungguk.
Ledled ka suled su egkaridiekan ka te me batè.
Move to the corner brother because you'll get trampled on by the children.
Eng. move to corner
OCM: Weight Moving (483)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vt.
for someoneto put something (igledled, inledled, patient, ig><ø-) into a corner
Eng. put in a corner
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
n.
edge (of room)
Ka ledled iyan ka ilis/sungguk te baley wey ke sinabeng naa. Egkaayun degma ne iyan igpasabut ka ilis diyè te innelesan wey ke natahuan naa te minsan nekey.
Diyè ku rè igpabuluku te ledled ka asu kema ke egkaagsilan diyè te selib.
I will let the dog lie at the edge of our house because it might be cold under the house.
O. Syn. ilis
Eng. edge; corner, internal; far end
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
