gakap
/gakap/
v_rt.
“get_ashore”
 
vi.
for someone (eggakap, agent, eg><ø-) to come out of the water, e.g. after crossing a river, onto the beach or bank (eggakapan, location, eg><an-)
Ka eggakap iyan ka peg-awè puun diyè te weyig, pegkeimpus te eglumbuk, puun te weyig peendiyè te tanè.
Eggakap ad e su maagsil amana ka weyig.
Gakap kad e su eggendiyè kid e.
Just leave water right now for we just go there.
Ant. ambul
Eng. leave water; exit water; get out of water; come ashore; go ashore; ashore, go
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
(igpahakap, igpA-) to remove an animal (esp. a water buffalo) from the water
Igpahakap ku pad ka kalabew ku.
I will remove my carabao from the water.
Eng. remove
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M

v.
(iggakap, ig-) to remove from the fire
Iggakap niyud ka sinuba su egpangeun kid.
Syn. ig-ilis
Eng. remove from the fire
OCM: Food Preparation (252)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
