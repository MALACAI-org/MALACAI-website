saharal
/sagaral/
“disaster”
 
1.
v.
for someone (egsaharal, migsaharal/insaharal, patient, eg><ø-) to be overtaken by a natural disaster (egsaharalan, insaharalan/migsaharalan/pigsaharalan, location, eg><an-)
Wrd.Gloss: was_overtaken_by_disaster
Eng. disaster; crisis; trauma; calamity; plague; experience
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
v.
for someone (egpasaharal, migpasaharal/impasaharal, agent, eg><ø-pA-) to expose someone else to something (egpasaharalen, impasaharal/migpasaharal/pigpasaharal, patient, eg><ø-pA-)
Ka egtanud egpasaharal kanta te kalised ke egsuwey ki te igkeupii rin.
The creator will expose us difficulties when we separate his will.
Eng. expose, to
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
