antug
/ʔantug/
v_rt.
 
vt.
for someone (eg-antug, mig-antug/in-antug, agent, eg><ø-) to gently throw something underhand (ig-antug, in-antug, theme, ig><ø-) to something or someone (eg-antuhan, in-antuhan/mig-antuhan/pig-antuhan, location, eg><an-)
Ka eg-antug iyan ka egdegpak/egsawilè te minsan nekey. Piru egpasaselib ka paahi te pegdegpak wey kenè ne igparibabew te pamanayen su buntug/degpak e ka ngaran due.
Eg-antug a perem te bula piru intampuk e ma ni Rikrik.
I am about to thow a ball but Rikrik beat it.
Bantey ka su ig-antug ku diyè te keykew seini se paan.
Watch out because I'm going to throw this bread to you.
Hentew-a David ka eg-antuhan nu te sahing?
Who will you throw this banana at David?
Syn. degpak —throw overarm
Ant. sakellew —catch
Eng. throw underarm; underarm throw
Tag. maghágis
Ceb. ilabay
Ilo.: ibató
OCM: Weight Moving (483)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone or something (egpakaantug, nakaantug, actor & theme, egpAkA-) to be thrown away accidentally
Eng. thrown away, to be (accidentally)
