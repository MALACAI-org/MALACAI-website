tulimpas
/tulimpas/
v_rt.
“go_off_course”
 
v.
for something (egkatulimpas, natulimpas, actor, egkA><ø-) to go off course (e.g. when trying to sew straight)
Ka egkatulimpas iyan sika se egpakasuwey te egbayaan, egsulindab, wey ke kenad egpakapabulus te imbayaan.
Kenè nu banggewa si Amè su kema ke egkatulimpas ka egperiyen din ne balahen.
Don't disturb father spliting rattan into pieces because it might go off course.
Wrd.Gloss: go_off_course
Kenè niyu sumbayata si Inè ka egpangubpit te balahen su egpakatulimpas ka himu rin.
Eng. go off course; crooked, go; veer; deviate
