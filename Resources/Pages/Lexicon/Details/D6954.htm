tumpis
/tumpis/
adj_rt.
“difficult”
 
1.
adj.
(matumpis, mA1-) generous
Ka seini ne lalag sabeka ne batasan te etew. Ka igpasabut kayi iyan ka hid-u wey mabehey-behey diyè te duma, kenè ne leheren.
Masalig ka alukuy ni Daril su matumpis sikandin.
Daril has many friends because he is generous.
Eng. generous
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
2.
v.
(egtutumpis, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to keep on (doing something)
Data Qual: Tano doesn't know this formation of tumpis...maybe it is "egtumpis-tumpis" which means doing something alone...
Egtutumpis epad egkeen keen su egkebuyag ad e mahaan.
cf. bulus
Eng. keep on; carry on; continue
Tag. sigé; magtulóy
Ceb. hala sigi
OCM: Labor and Leisure (461)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
3.
n.
(pegtumpis, peg><ø-) debt of gratitude
Due pegtumpis ku kandan su dakel ka ingkabulig dan dengan keddì.
I have a debt of gratitude to them because they had helped me a lot.
Eng. debt of gratitude
Tag. útang na loób
Ceb. utang kabubut-on
Ilo.: útang nga naímbag nga nákem
OCM: Borrowing and Lending (426), Social Relationships and Groups (571)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
4.
v.
(patumpisey, pA><ey-) to be generous with each other
Patumpisey kew ka talaari.
You siblings, be generous with each other.
Eng. generous with each other
OCM: Norms (183)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
(pegpatumpisey, peg><ø-pA><ey-) (for two or more people) to be generous to each other
Ka pegpatumpisey iyan ka egpakameupiya te me etew du te pegpeid-iraney.
Generosity contributes harmony to the people than backbiting.
Eng. generous
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
