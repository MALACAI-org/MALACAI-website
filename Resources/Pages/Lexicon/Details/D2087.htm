guseng
/gusɔŋ/
v_rt.
“disorganized”
 
vt.
for someone (egguseng, agent, eg><ø-) to make something (eggusengen, patient, eg><en-) disorganized
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for something (e.g., a person's thoughts) (egkahuseng, actor, egkA><ø-) to be disorganized
Ka igpasabut kayi iyan sika se egmekegsalsalambid ne minsan nekey iling te lanut, bulbul ne egkamukutmukut wey egkasalapid wey ke warè naa naay-ayarari te egtahu.
Kenè kew kekelag due te indampilan te lanut su egkahuseng.
Don't play where the abaca fiber dried up for it will be disorganized.
Wrd.Gloss: disorganized
Eng. disorganized; disjointed; random thoughts; unable to concentrate; untidy
DDP: Language and thought (3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for things (such as a pile of papers) (egkahuseng-guseng, actor, egkA><ø-rdpRT-) to be disorderly or untidy
Mareet ke warè sugkad ta su egkahuseng-guseng dè due ka bulbul ta.
It it not good if we do not have comb for our hair will always disorganized.
Eng. disorganized; untidy; disorderly; disarrayed
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
