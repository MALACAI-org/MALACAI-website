tingkalusan
/tinkalusan/
 
n.
a medium-sized rodent which live in the forest
Dakel ne Ambew ka tinkalusan wey amana ne malambu.
The tingkalusan type of rodent is big and and fatty.
O. Syn. kegsik
O. Syn. kawilì
O. Syn. paspas
O. Syn. tilad
O. Syn. takubung
O. Syn. timbabukag
cf. ambew
Eng. rodent (live in the forest); rat (live in the forest); mouse (live in the forest)
OCM: Fauna (136)
DDP: Rodent (1.6.1.1.4)
