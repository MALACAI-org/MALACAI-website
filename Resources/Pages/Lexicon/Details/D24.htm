abli
/ʔabli/
v_rt.
“open”
Fr. Ceb. abri
 
vt.
for someone (eg-abli, mig-abli/in-abli, agent, eg><ø-) to open something (such as a book (diblu) or a can of fish (tinapa)) (eg-abliyan, in-abliyan/mig-abliyan/pig-abliyan, abliyan, location, eg><an-)
Ka eg-abliyan iyan ka peglulukat te minsan nekey iling te baley, bintana, kehun, kaban, wey ke kabil naa. Egkaayun degma ne ka peg-awè te supè te minsan nekey.
Abliyan ku pad ka gumawan wey bintana te baley.
I will still open the door and window of the house.
O. Syn. lukat
O. Syn. puwas
Eng. open
Tag. buklát; bukás
Ceb. bukás
Ilo.: bisrat; bukás
