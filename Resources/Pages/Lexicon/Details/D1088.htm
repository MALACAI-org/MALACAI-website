bukale
/bukalɔ/
 
adj.
(mabukale, mA1-) strict
Ka seini ne lalag sabeka ne kineiya te etew ne malalag-lalag, matale, mapangasak, be-baan wey mahulub. Egkaayun degma ne mapangpangguhud te minsan nekey ne natahaan din ne me belita.
Amana ne mabukale ka kepatas ney te trabahu su minsan eggimeley ke rè te dagdahew kenè iya egkaayun.
Our lead man in work is very strict for even a minute to rest he did not allow it.
Eng. strict
