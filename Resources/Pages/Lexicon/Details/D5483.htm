sali
/sali/
v_rt.
 
v.
(egpasalsaliyey, eg><ø-pA><ey-rdpCVC-) to race (each other)
Egpasalsaliyey ka me batè ne egkeen su migsabut sikandan ne eg-ahawan ka egkamewuriyan.
The children races to eat for they agree with each other whoever finished last his food will be eaten by others.
Syn. lungkesu
Eng. race
Tag. karéra; pátulinán; takbúhan
Ceb. lumbà; karera
Ilo.: lúmba
OCM: Athletic Sports (526)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
