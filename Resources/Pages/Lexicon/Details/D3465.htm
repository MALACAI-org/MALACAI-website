lambag
/lambag/
v_rt.
“deviate”
 
1.
vi.
for someone (eglambag, miglambag/inlambag, agent, eg><ø-) to deviate from established customs
Eng. deviate
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
2.
vi.
for someone (eglambag, miglambag/inlambag, agent, eg><ø-) to cross along the biggest way (eglambahan, miglambahan/inlambahan/piglambahan, location, eg><ø-)
Eglambag ad te kelesadda.
I am crossing now along the road.
Eng. cross, to
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
n.
(iglambag, instrument, ig><ø-) a crossing or sideroad
Diya a tahari te iglambag te dalan.
Just wait me at the crossing.
Eng. crossing; sideroad
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
3.
vi.
for someone (eglambag, miglambag/inlambag, agent, eg><ø-) to meet someone else (eglambahen, inlambag/miglambag/piglambag, location, eg><en-)
Eng. meet, to
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for two or more people (egpekeglambag, nekeglambag, agent, egpekeg-) to unintentionally meet each other
Egpekeglambag ki red suled.
We can see each other, Brother.
Eng. see each other; meet
DDP: See (2.3.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
4.
v.
for something (egpakalambag, nakalambag, theme, egpAkA-) to be suddently occur in someone's minds and thingking
Eng. suddently occur
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
