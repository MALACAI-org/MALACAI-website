anupul
/ʔanupul/
n.
“tree variety”
 
n.
a small type a wild tree which is usually used as a roud timber for a house
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se kalasiyan te kayu diyè te katalunan ne egkaayun ne eggeeren para te baley.
Etuwey! Anupul bes ne kayu ka intempug nu!
Wow! Surprisingly that you cut off the anupul tree!
