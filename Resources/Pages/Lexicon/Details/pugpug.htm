pugpug
/pupug/
 
adj.
missing finger tip
Ka pugpug iyan ka due baad te lawa ne natempuhan iling te kemel, belad, paa, sulu, ikug, tuktuk, dilè wey minsan nekey pad. Minsan degma ka me kasangkapan ne mallayat ne natempuhan egkaayunan degma te seini ne lalag.
Pugpug ka sabeka ne kemel ni Eteng su napisel te dilè te mekina.
Eteng's finger tip is missing because it was chopped by the belt of a machine.
Eng. chopped off; cut off; fingertip, missing
