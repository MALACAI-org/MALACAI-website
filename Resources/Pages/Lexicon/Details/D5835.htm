sikeddì
/sikɔddiʔ/
pron.
“1s_TOPIC”
 
pron.
as for me
Wrd.Gloss: as_for_me
Sikeddì ka mabantug ne mannitingen.
I am the famous hunter.
Si keddì wey sikeykew me Matigsalug.
Me and you are both Matigsalug.
Eng. as for me; I; me
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Pronouns (9.2.3)
