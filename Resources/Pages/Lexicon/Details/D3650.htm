leheb
/lɔgɔb/
v_rt.
“distribute”
 
v.
for someone (egleheb, agent, eg><ø-) to distribute something or spread it around
Ka egleheb iyan ka peg-atul-atul pegbehey te minsan nekey diyè te duma ne me etew. Kenè ne iyan dè egkaayun ig-atul-atul igbehey ka me kasangkapan wey ka egkahen-genan wey egkakita, egkaayun degma ka guhuren ne eg-atul-atul diyè te minsan nekey.
Egleheben ku te kumbalè ka me hari su eyew warè egkaal-alangan diyè te kandan.
I will give clothe each my younger brothers and sisters so that all should be given.
O. Syn. sawed
Eng. distribute; spread
