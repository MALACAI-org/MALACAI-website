hawì
/hawiʔ/
v_rt.
“retrieve”
 
vt.
for someone (eggawì, miggawì, agent, eg><ø-) to retrieve a loaned item (eggawien, innawì, theme, eg><en-)
Eggawien kud e ka insambayan din ne kendiru su nabutbutan e ka kendiru ney.
I will take back the pot that he had borrowed for our pot was pierced.
Eng. retrieve; recover; get back; take back
