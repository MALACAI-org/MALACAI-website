lameg1
/lamɔg/
v_rt.
“not_properly _washed”
 
v.
for someone (eglelameg, miglelameg/inlelameg, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to not wash or bathe properly because of a shortage of water (eglamehen, inlameg/miglameg/piglameg, patient, eg><en-)
Minsan dakel ka weyig ne eglelameg dè ne egpamanihus si Husi.
Husi not properly take a bathe even theres a lot of water.
Eng. wash in very little water; bathe in very little water
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
vi.
for someone, or something like the laundry (egkalameg, nalameg, actor, egkA><ø-) to be not properly washed because there wasn't enough water available
Wrd.Gloss: not_properly_washed
Eng. washed, not properly; dirty, still; still dirty
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for the water (egpakalameg, nakalameg, theme, egkA><ø-) to become shortage
Eng. shortage
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
