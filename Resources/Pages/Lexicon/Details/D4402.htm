menu2
/mɔnu/
tag question
Ka menu ne lalag egkahamit kenè dè para te maheteng ne insè, ke kenè, egkahamit degma seini isip igpasundul ne insè te maheteng ne lalag eyew egkatuenan ke eg-uyunan ka inlalag din wey kenè naa. Diyè seini egkakita te pulipusan te maheteng ne lalag wey eg-epusen te insè ne palinneu.
Nakita nu ma si Eteng diyè te Tulay, menu ma iya?
You have seen Eteng at Tulay, haven't you?
Eng. tag question
Data Qual: does this usually expect a 'yes' answer or a 'no' answer (or both). Eg, 'You're happy, aren't you?' and 'You're not happy, are you' - can I use 'menu' in both these sentences?
DDP: Markers expecting an affirmative answer (9.4.6.2)
Online dict status: c
Usage level: M
