meemen
/mɔhɔmɔn/
Pron.: me:men
 
v.
for someone (egmeemen, egme:men, migmeemen, migme:men/immeemen, imme:men, agent, eg><en-) to speak out loudly (egmeemenen, egme:menen, immeemen, imme:men/migmeemen, migme:men/pigmeemen, pigme:men, patient, eg><en-rdpCV-)
Egmeemenen ta teg lalag su kenè egdineg si anggam.
We will speak loudly because uncle has hearing problem.
O. Syn. balukan
Eng. speak out; loud
DDP: Loud (2.3.2.4)
