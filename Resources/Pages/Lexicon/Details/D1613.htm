dupak
/dupak/
Data Qual: duppak???
v_rt.
“help”
 
v.
for someone (egdupak, agent, eg><ø-) to help someone (egdupaken, theme, eg><en-)
Dupaka niyu ka asu ne eggebu.
Go and see to the dog that's barking (i.e., at wild game)
Syn. bulig
Eng. help; assist; accompany
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
(egpakarupak, egpAkA-) to be able to help
Iyan seini ka peggendiyè te duma eyew te pegbulig ke nekey ka eggimuwen din. Seini warè bayad, ke kenè egpakenen dè ka etew isip bayad te pegbulig.
Kenè e bu pad egpakarupak kaaselem keykew te egkamet su duen pad impurtanti ne eggimuwen ku.
I think that I just can't able you to help clearing area tomorrow because I still have something important to do.
Eng. help
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for someone (egdupak, agent, eg><ø-) to assist someone in their work such as egkamet clearing a field, eg-eeti and eg-erek etc.
O. Syn. amung
Eng. assist; help; labor
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
