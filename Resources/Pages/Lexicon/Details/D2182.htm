hendei
/hɔndɔʔi/
Var.1: endei
pron.
“where”
 
pron.
where
Wrd.Gloss: where
Gramm. locative interrogative pronoun
Hendei ka awasa nu?
Where is your spouse?
Hendei ka igkeupii nu, egpanulu wey ke kenad e?
Where you prefer, you teach or not anymore?
Eng. where; which
Tag. saán; nasaan
Ceb. hain; diin (past); asa (fut)
Ilo.: adino; andino; ayanna
WBM: hende-i
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Question words (9.2.3.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
