barang
/badaŋ/
n.
“curse”
 
n.
something which eaten causes an enlarged stomach and eventual death
Bantey kew te me etew ne matigsikihur su due barang.
Be careful of the people from Sikihur because they have something which causes an enlarged stomach and eventual death.
Cult. may be put into food by an enemy
O. Syn. lambus
Eng. curse; poison
DDP: Poison (2.5.3.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
