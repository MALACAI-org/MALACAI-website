nigusiyanti
/nigusianti/
Var. nigusyanti
Fr. Ceb. negosyante
 
n.
businessman or woman
Ka nigusyanti etew, egpamelegyè wey egpamalit te minsan nekey ne belegyè iling te lamisa, terak, baley wey duma pad. Warè amana nigusyanti te Matigsalug su mabehey-behey nahud wey kenad e egkeilangan ka bayad.
Nigusiyanti iya mulè si Navarro su minsan sè butilya igbelegyè.
Navarro is a businessman indeed because he will sell even bottles.
Leheren ka nigusyanti ne etew su kema ke egkapurduy.
Business people are selfish because they are afraid to be broke.
Eng. businessman; businesswoman; shopkeeper; storekeeper; merchant; trade
Tag. mangangalakal
Ceb. negosyante
