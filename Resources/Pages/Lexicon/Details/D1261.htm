da
/da/
Var. daa
Pron.: da:
Var. ra
Var. raa
Pron.: ra:
part.
“EXCLAM”
 
part.
[discouragement]
Em!em! da! Kenè ka eg-iling due.
Oh! don't do like that.
Warad e ra!
There's no more.
Sika iya ra.
That is what.
Gramm. discouragement aspectual adjunct (this particle communicates a feeling of discouragement, weariness or grief)
DDP: Particles (9.2.6)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
