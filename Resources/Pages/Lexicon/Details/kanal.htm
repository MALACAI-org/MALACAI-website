kanal
/kanal/
 
n.
channel
Ka kanal tanè seini ne ingkali te me etew. Ka tuyù te seini ne kali iyan ka egpabayaan te minsan nekey. Kaayun ne me mummu, weyig ne egpuun te baley wey ke beuhan naa.
Lilinditi ka kanal kayi te Dabaw su masulug ka mummu ne namewù.
The canal here in Davao is so dirty because of the rice particle.
Meupiya iya ka due kanal su kenè egseled ka weyig diyè te baley.
Canal in the house is good because it is preventing water to enter in the house.
Eng. canal; channel
Tag. kanal
Ceb. kanal
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
v.
(egkekanal, migkekanal, kanal, egkA><ø-) someone make a canal
Ka seini ne lalag egpasabut ne due etew ne miglulungag diyè te tanè. Eglulungag eyew egpabayaan te minsan nekey.
Egkekanal si Noel eyew due egbayaan te weyig.
Noel make a canal so that there's a way of water.
Migkekanal a gabii diyè te baley ney.
I was made a canal in our house yesterday.
