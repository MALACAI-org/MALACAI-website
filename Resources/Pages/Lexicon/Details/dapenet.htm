dapenet
/dapɔnɔt/
adj_rt.
“pure/holy”
 
adj.
(marapenet, mA1-) pure or holy
Ka marapenet iyan ka kenè amana ne makehal-kehal wey maweil-weil su maleman wey malambened. Meupiya ka pegweil-weil din wey matelen. Iyan dè egkaayunan kayi ka due geyinawa wey egpakaweil.
Marapenet ne etew si Alags su kenè ne sensè iling ki Unat.
Iskila is still and not like Unat who is irking.
Meupiya ne egdumeen ka etew ne marapenet su hendue te warad iya igkasasew wey igkaana-ana.
It nice to accompany a formal person for it seems that he has no worries and not hurrying.
Wrd.Gloss: pure/holy???
Eng. formal; pure???; holy???
Data Qual: I don't think holy is the right word here... (Who is "I"?)
Ceb. maligdom
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
(egdapenet, eg><ø-) to remain fixed in one place
Migdapenet dè iya te sungguk ka itù ku ne imbasbas ku eyew egkenè eg-ahew te keenen wey egtahad ne egsungguran.
My puppy which I trained in order not greedy and will wait to be fed stay in a corner.
Eng. remain; fixed; permanent; stay
