anggè
/ʔaŋgɔʔ/
v_rt.
“restrain”
 
n.
something which holds one back
Eng. holds back; prevent; restrain; prohibit
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for someone (egkaanggè, kaanggè, naanggè, patient, egkA><ø-) to be held up or delayed by something or someone (egpakaanggè, pakaanggè, nakaanggè, actor, egpAkA-)
Ka egkaanggè iyan ka egkatalehen te minsan nekey ne eggimuwen wey egkatalangaan e te himu rin iling te eggipanew, eggilamun, wey masalig pad ka duma.
Kenè e kun talehena su egkaanggè a te himu.
Don't bother me because I will delayed with my work.
Kema, kenè kew layun sambey keddì te ilab su egkaanggè a te eggimuwen ku.
Look, don't always borrow my knife because I will be delayed of what I am doing.
Warè key nekeulì gabii su naanggè key te mahaliyug.
We didn't return yesterday because we were interrupted by the visitors.
Syn. talahen
Eng. held up; constrained; delayed; restrained; prevented; interrupted
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for a person (eg-angge-anggè, mig-anggè-anggè/in-anggè-anggè, anggè-anggè, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to be a rough eater and spill lots on the floor; to not look after their family or animals (eg-ang-anggaan, in-ang-anggaan/mig-ang-anggaan/pig-ang-anggaan, ang-anggaan, location, eg><an-rdpCVC-)
Ka eg-angge-anggè iyan ka egbalahad wey kenè peglayam, pegsahipè, wey pegsam-samili te minsan nekey wey minsan hentew. Nalein-lein ka egpuunan mania te egkeyimu ka iling kayi.
Mareet ne iney si Adar su amana eg-angge-anggè te me anak din.
Adar is unworthy mother for she disregard her children.
Eng. ignore responsibilities; disregard; irresponsible; uncaring; unreliable; despise; reject; maltreat; mistreat; selfish
DDP: Ignore (3.1.2.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
