utu-utu
/ʔutuʔutu/
 
v.
for something (eg-utu-utu, mig-utu-utu/in-utu-utu, utu-utu, agent, eg><ø-) to be very full ( esp. basket )
Ka eg-utu-utu iyan ka amana egkapenu-penù wey eg-um-umambu.
Eg-utu-utu e ka liyang ni Payi te binggala.
Payi’s basket is very full of cassava.
Egkabulung-bulung ad iya kuntee su mig-utu-utu ka pinggan ku te egkeenen.
I will be very full today because my plate is so+ full.
Eng. very full; full
Tag. puno
Ceb. puno

Usage level: R
