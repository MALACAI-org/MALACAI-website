libat
/libat/
v_rt.
 
vt.
for someone (eglibat, eglilibat, agent, eg><ø-, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to distract someone else (eglibaten, patient, eg><en-)
Ka eglilibat iyan ka eg-uubat ne kenè egkeila te innimu rin eyew kenè egkanengnengan te duma ke nekey ka innimu rin pinaahi te pegpangguhud te kenè ne malehet ne innimu rin wey peggimu te minsan nekey ne egpakabunbun te innimu rin ne eggeles din. Egkayitabu seini emun ke egkasapenan wey ke eggimu naan e minsan hentew ne eggelesan te malehet, mareet ma wey ke meupiya.
Malasi eglilibat ka me anak ku emun ke egginguma ad e.
My children often pretend (by doing something) when I arrive.
Data Qual: gloss needs updating to fit example sentence and ie fields [The gloss doesn't have to "fit" the ie fields, but it should indeed fit the example sentences. The ie fields can be more broad, even slightly inaccurate, as they are only an index. RJH]
O. Syn. ayat
Eng. distract; tempt; lure; entice; pretend; fool, to; trick, to
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for someone (egkalibat, nalibat, actor, egkA><ø-) to backtrack; make a mistake in speaking
Wrd.Gloss: backtrack
Egkalibat ad e mulè seini.
Eng. backtrack
