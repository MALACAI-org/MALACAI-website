tabla
/tabla/
n.
“lumber”
Data Qual: Add verb forms, e.g., intablaan [RJH]
 
n.
sawn lumber
Ka tabla iyan ka baad te kayu ne inggabas wey ke in-uney-uney rè due intabla te etew. Egngaranan seini te tablun ke inggabas ne bayew makepal. Warè nenekeg-iling ka kakepal, kalayat wey kaluag kayi su siggun te karakel te kayu ne intablaan.
Kahiyen ni Pidru ne kenè kun ne tabla ka inggamit te peggimu te balungbalung ne temuwanan su pididus kun.
According to Peter that it's log post has been used to built a tent of meeting and not lumber.
O. Syn. tablun
Eng. sawn lumber; board; timber; lumber
Tag. tablá
