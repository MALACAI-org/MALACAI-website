eneng
/ʔɔnɔŋ/
Data Qual: Is it really possible for this root to even stand alone without reduplication? (No examples below although Nahum 1:2 uses en-enengan.) [RJH]
v_rt.
“silent/still”
 
1.
vi.
for someone or something (egpeeneng-eneng, migpeeneng-eneng/impeeneng-eneng, agent, egpA><ø-rdpRT-) to quieten or stop (egpeeneng-enengen, impeeneng-eneng/migpeeneng-eneng/pigpeeneng-eneng, patient, egpA><ø-rdpRT-) saying or doing something (egpeeneng-enengan, impeeneng-enengan/migpeeneng-enengan/pigpeeneng-enengan, location, egpA><an-rdpRT-) for a reason (igpeeneng-eneng, reason, igpA-rdpRT-)
Mesì egpeeneng-eneng ka ka egpallahuy kenè ka egpinnuu geus.
When you stop running you should not sit directly.
Egpeeneng-enengen mulè ka egmangekelag due su due egderalu.
Let the ones playing stop because there is someone sick.
Eng. quiet; keep quiet; silent; be silent; silence; still; calm; stop; cease; restrain; do nothing; relax
Tag. manahímik; tahímik; payápà
Ceb. paghilum
Ilo.: agulímek
OCM: Non-electronic Transmission of Messages (202)
DDP: Quiet (2.3.2.5)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
v.
for something (egpakapeeneng-eneng, nakapeeneng-eneng, pakapeeneng-eneng, agent, egpAkA-rdpRT-) to able to stopped by someone (igpakapeeneng-eneng, impakapeeneng-eneng, reason, igpAkA-rdpRT-)
Eng. stopped, able; unable to speak; can't speak; silenced
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for someone (egpapeeneng-eneng, migpapeeneng-eneng/impapeeneng-eneng, agent, eg><ø-pA-rdpRT-) to stop or turn off (egpapeeneng-enengen, impapeeneng-eneng/migpapeeneng-eneng/pigpapeeneng-eneng, patient, eg><en-rdpRT-) something (igpapeeneng-eneng/impapeeneng-eneng, theme, igpA-rdpRT-)
Igpapeeneng-eneng pad ka radyu su egbunsud si Ruwil ka egpanubad para te iglabung ta.
(Press) the stop button of the radio because Ruel is now praying for our supper.
Eng. stop; turn off
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
