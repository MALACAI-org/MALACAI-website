ki1
/ki/
pron.
“1pi_F”
 
pron.
we(incl); us(incl)
Ka ki karumaan ded te kiw ne igsaliyu te me ngaran te etew. Egkahamit seini emun ke kenè egngaranan ka me etew ne egdawiten wey lagkes en iya degma kayi ka etew ne egdawit wey eg-umew te me duma rin.
Wrd.Gloss: we(incl); us(incl)
Warè ki nakataha te peglibed te Manama.
We don't know the return of God.
Gramm. 1st person plural inclusive focus pronoun (Set 1, brief form)
Eng. we incl (focus); us incl (focus)
Tag. táyo
Ceb. kita
Binukid: kuy; ki (dual)
OCM: Grammar (193)
DDP: Pronouns (9.2.3)
 
pron_COMPL.
(kid, -d) we(incl); us(incl) (with completive aspect)
Kenè kid egpabulus te hipanew ta su mareet ka taheinep ku gabii.
We will not continue on our journey because my dream last night was bad.
Egkeen kid e su nekeuntud e ka aldew wey egmamitu-pitu naan dè ka geyinawa ku te gutas.
We will eat now because the sun is at its zenith and I can't breathe anymore because of hunger.
Gramm. 1st person plural inclusive focus pronoun (Set 1, brief form) with completive aspect
Eng. we incl (focus) with completive aspect; us incl (focus) with completive aspect
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
