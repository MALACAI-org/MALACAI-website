gumawan
/gumawan/
n.
“doorway”
 
n.
doorway; opening; gateway
Lukati nu kun ka gumawan su egbaya a.
Please open the door (literally doorway) because I'm coming through.
Eng. doorway; opening; gateway; entrance; door
Tag. pintô; pintúan
Ceb. pultahan
OCM: Miscellaneous Structures (349)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
