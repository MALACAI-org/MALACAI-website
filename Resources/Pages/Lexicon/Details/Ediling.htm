Ediling
/ʔɔdiliŋ/
prop_n.
“Ediling”
 
prop_n.
Wrd.Gloss: Odiling
Matumpis wey eleg ne egkaruputan te me etew si Ediling dengan te kayi pad sikandin te inged.
Ediling was generous and worthy to appeal with people when she was still here on earth.
