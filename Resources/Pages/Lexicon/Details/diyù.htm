diyù
/diuʔ/
adj_rt.
“far”
 
1.
adj.
(mariyù, mA1-) far
Mariyù ka Panganan wey mahirap ka dalan su egtakereg wey egtupang.
Panganan was far and the road was difficult for it goes up and down.
Ant. marani
Eng. far; away; far away; distant; remote; afar
Tag. maláyò
Ceb. layù
Ilo.: adayú
Binukid: diyuʔ

adj.
(nekegdiyù, nekeg-) far apart
Nekegdiyù ka Dabaw wey Kagayan.
Syn. delag
Eng. far apart; separated
Tag. madálang; hiwá-hiwaláy
Ceb. lagyo; bulag-bulag
OCM: Weights and Measures (804)

2.
v.
for someone (egpariyù, migpariyù/impariyù, agent, eg><ø-pA-) to go a distance
Eng. go away; distance, go

Usage level: M

n.
(kariyuan, kA><an-) distance
