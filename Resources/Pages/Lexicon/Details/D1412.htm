ded2
/ded/
Var. red
 
n.
Kuwa ke red te kalamantis?
Will you take any tomatoes?
Nakasuman-suman ke red te karumaan ta ne si Buas ne naruma nu se me suluhuanen ne me malitan?
Do you(sg) remember our(incl) relative Buas whose female servants became your(sg) companion?
egdereetan nu naan ded ka intiru ne siyudad minsan nasalinan e te lalimma ne etew ne matareng?
will you(sg) destroy the entire city although five righteous people are lacking?”
Eng. question
