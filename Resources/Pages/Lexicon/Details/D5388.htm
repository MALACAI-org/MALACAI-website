sagman
/sagman/
v_rt.
“give_attention”
 
v.
for someone (egsagman, migsagman/insagman, agent, eg><ø-) to give attention to visitors (egsagmanen, insagman/migsagman/pigsagman, theme, eg><en-) by preparing them food
Wrd.Gloss: show_favor
Gramm. This is considered to be deep Matigsalug.
Kenad iya egsagman si Andung te asawa rin te iyam pad sikandan nekeg-engked.
Andung didn't gave attention of his wife whent they were newly separated.
Eng. give attention; show favor; prepare; help
DDP: Show, let someone see (2.3.1.4), Attention (3.1.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for someone (egpasagman, migpasagman/impasagman, agent, eg><ø-pA-) to be shown favor
Egpasagman ki te Manama.
We will be shown favor by God.
DDP: Show, let someone see (2.3.1.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
