batasan2
/batasan/
 
v.
for someone (egpamatasan, migpamatasan/impamatasan, agent, eg><ø-paN-) to take food or a small animal (igbatasan, imbatasan, instrument, ig><ø-) to a datu (egbatasanan, imbatasanan/migbatasanan/pigbatasanan, location, eg><an-) in order to request his assistance (igpamatasan, impamatasan, ig><ø-paN-)
Egpamitew e pad te babuy su igbatasan ku diyè te ki datu Pablu eyew egbulihan a nikandin te prublima ku.
I'm going to find a pig to take to datu Pablu in order to request his assistance so that he will help me concerning my problem.
