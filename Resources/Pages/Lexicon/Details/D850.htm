beley
/bɔlɔy/
 
v.
for someone (egkabeley, nabeley, patient, egkA><ø-) to become tired out from working hard (e.g. clearing a field egkamet or walking eggipanew)
Eggimeley ki pad su hendue te egkabeley ad e.
Let us rest first because it seems that I ge tired.
O. Syn. pu-pù
O. Syn. luya
Eng. tired; worn out; weary; sleepy; haggard
Tag. pagód
Ceb. kápoy
Ilo.: nabánnug
OCM: Sensation and Perception (151), Labor and Leisure (461)
DDP: Body condition (2.4), Tired (2.4.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for someone or something (egbeley, migbeley/imbeley, agent, eg><ø-) to cause someone to be tired (egbelayen, imbeley/migbeley/pigbeley, patient, eg><en-)
Eng. tired, make to
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something (egpakabeley, nakabeley, agent, egpAkA-) to cause someone to be tired
Eng. tired, cause
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
Data Qual: should this be a verb?
(egpakabeley, nakabeley, egpAkA-) tiresome
Eng. tiresome
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(bebelayen, rdpCV–en) always tired
Eng. always tired; lazy
Online dict status: -
