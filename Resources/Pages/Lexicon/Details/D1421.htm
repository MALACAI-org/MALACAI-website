dees
/dɔʔɔs/
v_rt.
“eat”
 
v.
for something (especially an animal such as a dog)
Data Qual: could this be a person too?
(egderees, migderees/inderees, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to eat raw meat from a carcass (egdeesan, indeesan/migdeesan/pigdeesan, location, eg><an-)
Ka egderees iyan ka pegpanamantaman pegkeen te minsan nekey. Egpekeiling seini te etew ne eggutasan ne amana egpameedpeed egkeen. Himu seini te etew ne bel-is.
Egderees ka me etew te babuy ne neutel dan.
Indeesan e ka impanued ne babuy.
The pig that was hunted was already eaten (by the dogs).
Cult. Usually this term ofently used to animals.
Eng. eat; greedy; glutton; over eat
DDP: Eat (5.2.2)
