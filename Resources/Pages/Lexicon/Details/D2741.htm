kalibutan
/kalibutan/
Var. kelibutan
n.
“world”
 
n.
the world
Masulug se me keyirapan kayi te kalibutan.
There are many difficulties here in the world.
Wrd.Gloss: world
Eng. world; earth; globe
OCM: Cosmology (772), Ethnogeography (823)
DDP: World (1.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L,B
 
adj.
(kalibutanen, -en) pertaining to this world, as distinct from the spiritual world
Eng. physical; worldly; earthly; natural
OCM: Cosmology (772)
DDP: World (1.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L,B
 
n.
(kalibutanen, -en) a person who lives in this world, as distinct from people who have died
Eng. living person; person, living
OCM: Life and Death (761)
DDP: Person (2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L,B
