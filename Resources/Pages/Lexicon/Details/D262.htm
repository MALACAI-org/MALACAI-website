andam
/ʔandam/
v_rt.
“prepare”
Fr. Ceb. andam
 
adj.
ready
Eng. ready
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
vt.
for someone (eg-andam, mig-andam/in-andam, agent, eg><ø-) to prepare something at someone else (eg-andamen, in-andam/mig-andam/pig-andam, andamen, theme, eg><en-) somewhere (eg-andaman, in-andaman/mig-andaman/pig-andaman, location, eg><an-)
Eg-andam ki te egkakeen ta su eyew kenè ki eggutasan diyè te deralanen.
We will prepare something to eat so that we will not be hungry along the way.
Eng. prepare; plan; advance preparation; be ready
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
v.
(egpangandam, migpangandam/impangandam, eg><ø-paN-) to secure things (before going away) (egpangandaman, impangandaman/migpangandaman/pigpangandaman, location, eg><an-paN-)
Ka pegpangandam iyan ka pegpanines te minsan nekey ne ingkeilangan ne eggamiten, peg-ayun-ayun wey pegtahahè te minsan nekey ne eg-uyanen emun ke eggipanew. Minsan degma ka pegkeetew egkaayunan te sika ne lalag.
Egpangandam ki te eg-uyanen ta kaaselem su eyew kenè ki egkalamu.
We will store something for tomorrow so that we will not run out.
Data Qual: gloss doesn't fit example sentence
Eng. secure; store; lock up; put away
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
