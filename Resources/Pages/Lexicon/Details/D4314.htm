manunubbuk
/manunubbuk/
 
n.
a tiny fly variety that named after its character mannubuk that is fond of entering the eyes
Iyan igngarani te seini su egserep nahud te mata, dereisek seini ne kalasiyan te langew wey diyè te kalabew meyilut egdupak seini.
Eng. fly
