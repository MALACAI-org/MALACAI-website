uranda
/ʔudanda/
v_rt.
“chant”
 
n.
a story chanted without accompaniment
Eng. chant
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for a person (male or female) (eg-uranda, mig-uranda/in-uranda, agent, eg><ø-) for a person (eg-uuranda, in-uuranda/mig-uuranda/pig-uuranda, actor, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to chant a story without musical accompaniment
Ka uranda sabeka seini ne pamaahi te pegkanta te me Matigsalug ne iling seini te eglalahen dè piru meupiya seini ne egpamminehen.
Mig-uranda rin ka ulahingen din gabii te miting wey amana ne meupiya ne egpamminehen.
He was singing uranda (a kind of chanting) yesterday in the meeting and it was very good for listening.
Eg-uuranda si Ana ke eg-ayat te batè ne egsinehew.
Ana will chant when distracting a crying baby.
cf. kumapey
Eng. chant
