selag
/selag/
 
v.
(egselag, migselag, selag, eg><ø-) pass through
Ka selag ne lalag taltalagduma te suluwag, leddut, lepew wey, suleddu. Egkahamit seini ne lalag ke egkeulug ka minsan nekey puun te ampew. Masulug ka egpuunan mania te egkeulug ka minsan nekey: an-anayan tenged su nareetan e ka atep. Tenged degma su nakalekè wey mareet e ka nareehan, egpuunan ne nakaleus diyè te diralem. Purisu eyew kenè ki egselag keilangan ne egpitawen ka egdiekan wey ke meupiya naan pad seini eyew kenè ki egselag.
Hapit migselag si Undon diyè te atep te upisina su kuneen din ne meupiya pad seini.
Undon almost through in the office seiling because he thougth that is still reliable.
Eng. pass through; through, sneak; go through; sneak through; appear; arrive; show up; attend
Ceb. lusot
