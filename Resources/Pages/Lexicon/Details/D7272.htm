wangì
/waŋiʔ/
 
n.
Wrd.Gloss: jaw_pivot
the pivot point of one's jaw; the end points of the lips
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se tamanan te bibig ta ke eghinganga ki.
Masakit ka wangwangian ku su nabayatbayat a.
The end points of my lips are aching from laughing.
Masakit se wangwangian ku sikan naa ka kenà a egpakakeen te me makehal ne egkeenen.
The end point of my mouth is aching that's why I can't eat solid food.
Eng. mouth, ends points of the; lips
