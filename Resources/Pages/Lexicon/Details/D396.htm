atehan
/ʔatɔgan/
Morpho. ateg -an ???
v_rt.
“be_constipated”
 
v.
for something, e.g., bananas, panuen, pangeyilen (eg-atehan, agent, eg><ø-) to cause constipation (eg-atehanan, experiencer, location, eg><an-)
Ka etew ne eg-atehanan, mananey egpakalihawang ka tei.
A person who is constipated, his feces is taking a long time to come out.
Eng. constipated
Tag. pagtitibí
Ceb. gitubol
Ilo.: agtábbel
OCM: Theory of Disease (753), Ethnophysiology (827)

Usage level: M
