kulitek
/kulitɔk/
 
v.
for someone (egkulitek, migkulitek/ingkulitek, agent, eg><ø-) to stir something e.g., coffee or milk (egkuliteken, ingkulitek/migkulitek/pigkulitek, patient, eg><en-)
Syn. kulibbul
Eng. stir, to
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egkulitek, migkulitek/ingkulitek, agent, eg><ø-) to screw the hole. e.g. using finger in ears (egkuliteken, ingkulitek/migkulitek/pigkulitek, patient, eg><en-)
Ka egkulitek iyan ka egtiyuk te minsan nekey ne due lungag eyew eg-aween wey egbanggawen ka diyè te seled iling te pegtiyuk te kemel te talinga wey irung eyew eg-aween ka me malindit dutu. Ne due degma egbelangbelang dè due egpaniyuk ne warè kamalehetan.
Egkuliteken ku ka talinga ku su eglinisan ku.
I will clean out my ears with my finger.
Eng. clean ears; twist
OCM: Elimination (514)
DDP: Ear (2.1.1.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
