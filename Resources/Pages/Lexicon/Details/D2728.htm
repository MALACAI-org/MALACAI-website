kalatkat
/kalatkat/
n.
 
n.
a piece of wood leaning against a high house with a special opening cut in the house so that a dog, or more especially, a puppy can enter and leave the house
Ka seini iyan sika se egpabayaan te asu ne egkeiling te haheran ne amana ne malayat wey malig-et dè ubag ne ka eleg dè iya ne egbayyaan te asu.
Tuwey! amana ne matikang ka kalatkat te asu.
Oh! the dog ramp is so high.
Eng. dog ramp; stair
