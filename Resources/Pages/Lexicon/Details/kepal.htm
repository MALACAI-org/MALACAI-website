kepal
/kɔpal/
adj_rt.
“thick”
 
adj.
(makepal, mA1-) thick (in depth)
Iyan ku egpaliten ka makepal ne ulesen su maagsil kayi te kanami.
I will buy the thick blanket because it's cold at our place.
Nakita ku diyè te TV ne nasilaban ka factory te sepatus– kekepali se mig-utu-utu ne ebel.
I saw on TV that the shoe factory was burned down–the smoke was piling up very thick.
Ant. manipis
Eng. thick; voluminous; dense
Tag. makapál
