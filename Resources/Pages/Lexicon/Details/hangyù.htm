hangyù
/haŋyuʔ/
Var.1: angyù
v_rt.
“request”
Fr. Ceb. hangyù
 
1.
vi.
for someone (eggangyù/eg-angyù, agent, eg><ø-) to bargain on a price
Kena a eggangyu su due etew egkeyilew a.
I will not bargain because there's a lot of people I ashame.
Eng. bargain; haggle
Ceb. hangyù
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
2.
vi.
for someone (eggangyù/eg-angyù, agent, eg><ø-) to ask someone else (eggangyuen/eg-angyuen, location, eg><en-) to do something
Hangyuen ku si Paster Empec ne egpanubad.
I am asking if Pastor Empoc could please pray.
Eng. request; ask; implore; insist; plead; coerce
Ceb. hangyù
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
