buhuk1
/buguk/
 
n.
an egg in the nest which is not fertile and is abandoned by the hen when the others hatch
Ka kaluwasan te seini ne lalag iyan ka atelug ne mareet wey kenè egpigsè. Warè nabaluy ne piyak tenged su neuranan wey ke layun dè egkakeil ka atelug.
Migpigsè e ka manuk ku piru tatelu ka buhuk.
The egg of my chicken was already burst but three did not fertile.
Eng. egg
DDP: Egg (1.6.3.1)
