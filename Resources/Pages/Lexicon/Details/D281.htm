anggad
/ʔaŋgad/
v_rt.
“wait”
 
v.
for someone (eg-anggad, mig-anggad/in-anggad/pig-anggad, agent, eg><ø-) to check with someone else before taking action (eg-anggaren, anggaren, patient, eg><en-)
Wrd.Gloss: wait
Kenà a egtaha te aldew te peg-ulì ku su iyan ku eg-angaren ka timpu ke meupiya naa.
I won't fix the day of my returning because I will wait if the climate is good.
Kenà a eggun-a eggipanew su eg-anggad a ke nekey ka isip ni Toto.
I won't go there ahead because I will wait what Toto think.
Iyan ku rè eg-anggaren ka timpu ke nekey ka meupiya kayi.
I'm just waiting for the exact time if what is good for it.
Eng. waiting for; letting; wait; check; confirm
