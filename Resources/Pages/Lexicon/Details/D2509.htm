indes
/ʔindɔs/
v_rt.
“defecate”
 
1.
v.
for someone (eg-indes, mig-indes/in-indes, agent, eg><ø-) to defecate somewhere (eg-indesan, mig-indesan/in-indesan/pig-indesan, location, eg><an-)
Wey rè eg-indes ka hari ku ke egkeen e.
My young brother only defecating during he eat.
Eng. defecate
Tag. tumáe
Ceb. libang
Ilo.: tumákki
OCM: Personal Hygiene (515), Ethnophysiology (827)
DDP: Defecate, feces (2.2.8)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
2.
v.
for someone (eg-iindes, mig-iindes/in-iindes, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to have diarrhea
Eg-iindes a su meyilew ka inkeen ku ne aheley gabii.
I have diarrhea today because I ate yesterday a uncooked corn.
Syn. hilis
Eng. diarrhea
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
