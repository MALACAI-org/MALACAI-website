uyan1
/ʔuyan/
v_rt.
“carry”
 
1.
vt.
for someone (eg-uyan, mig-uyan/in-uyan, agent, eg><ø-) to bring something (eg-uyanen, in-uyan/mig-uyan/pig-uyan, patient, eg><en-) somewhere (eg-uyanan, in-uyanan/mig-uyanan/pig-uyanan, location, eg><an-)
Hentew-a ka eg-uyan te hemey ne igbehey ku te amey ku?
Who will be the one to bring rice for my father?
Eg-uyanen kud e ka hemey para te amey nu.
I'm going to bring the rice for your father.
Eng. bring; carry; take
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for someone (eg-uyan-uyan, in-uyan-uyan, mig-uyan-uyan, agent, eg><ø-rdpRT-) to keep on bringing something (eg-uyan-uyanen, in-uyan-uyan/mig-uyan-uyan/pig-uyan-uyan, patient)
Eg-uyan-uyan sikandin te puruk minsan diyè te siyudad.
She is bringing bolo even in a city.
Eg-uyan-uyanen ku ka mameen ku minsan hendei.
I always bringing my bettle nut anywhere.
Syn. tuluy
Syn. alap
Eng. carry; bring; take
Tag. magdalá; dalhín
Ceb. gidala; dalhon; dalha
Ilo.: inala; ipán
OCM: Burden Carrying (482)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for someone (egpeuyan, migpeuyan/impeuyan, agent, eg><ø-pA-) to request or ask someone (egpeuyanen, impeuyan/migpeuyan/pigpeuyan, patient, eg><en-pA-) to load something (igpeuyan, impeuyan, theme, igpA-) somewhere (egpeuyanan, impeuyanan/migpeuyanan/pigpeuyanan, location, eg><an-pA-)
Impeuyan ka kepitan te sulat.
The (barrio) captain sent a letter.
Eng. request; ask favor
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
vt.
for someone or something (egkeuyan, neuyan, actor, egkA><ø-) to be carried along by someone or something else (igkeuyan, ingkeuyan, reason, igkA><ø-)
Egkeuyan nu red ka ugpit ku ne nalingawan ku gabii dini?
Could you bring my bolo which i've left their yesterday here?
Eng. carried along; carried off; included; enticed
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
3.
vt.
for someone or something (egkeuyan, neuyan, actor, egkA><ø-) to be convince or dominated someone's ideas or opinion or without self decision. (igkeuyan, ingkeuyan, reason, igkA><ø-)
Eng. dominated; convinced; led astray; astray, be led
DDP: Attract (3.4.1.4.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
