kulimel
/kulimɔl/
 
n.
remaining particle
Ka kulimel iyan ka samè naan dè te minsan nekey iling te keenen, inumen, wey minsan nekey pad ne egmemegsuwey wey bayew mewumel.
Kulimel naan dè ka insamè keddì te iney ku su masulug ka diyè migkeen te kanami.
Only leftover set aside for me of my mother because many people have eaten in our house.
Kenè nu keena ka rue su kulimel naan dè ne igsamè ta te uled.
Don’t eat the remaining food because that is set for your father.
Eng. left; particle
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
to use up remaining particle like burnt rice (egpangulimel)
Ka egpangulimel iyan ka egpanguwa te samè-samè naan dè.
Egpangulimel e naan dè te keenen su naaminan ad.
I will eat leftover because nothing left for me.
Mania naa te egpangulimel ke naan dè due te dakel mem pad ka keenen due te deisek ne kendiru?
Why are just taking leftover that we still have much food in the small pot?
Egpangulimel e naan dè te kuks su nakakabus ad.
I will just drink remaining coke drinks because I am late. (lit. out of budget)
Eng. drink remaining drinks; eat leftover; take particle
Ceb. magtilok
