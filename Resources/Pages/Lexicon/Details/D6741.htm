timul
/timul/
v_rt.
“add”
 
vt.
for someone (egtimul, migtimul/intimul, agent, eg><ø-) to add something (igtimul, intimul, instrument, ig><ø-) to something (egtimulan, intimulan/migtimulan/pigtimulan, location, eg><an-)
sabeka pegtimul te pitu equals? walu.
One plus seven equals eight.
Timulan te pad ka bewu nu eyew egkabulung ka kangkuwa te meudtu.
We will add more rice for your lunch so that you will be fulled for your lunch time.
Eng. add; increase; plus
WBM: uman
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for someone (egpegtimul, migpegtimul/impegtimul, agent, egpekeg-) to add two numbers or to mix something together (egpegtimulen, impegtimul/migpegtimul/pigpegtimul, patient, egpeg><en-)
Eng. add. to; mix, to
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for someone (egpatimul, migpatimul/impatimul, agent, eg><ø-pA-) to request additional for their share (egpatimulan, impatimulan/migpatimulan/pigpatimulan, location, eg><an-pA-)
Eng. request, to (additinal share)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
n.
(timulan, -an) profit
Eng. profit
Ceb. patung
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
