kakkak1
/kakkak/
Var. kakak
v_rt.
“cluck”
 
v.
for a hen (upa) (egkakkak/egkakak, agent, eg><ø-) to cluck
Wrd.Gloss: cluck
Ka seini iyan sika se lalag te manuk ke eglahalaha e su egpaahad e te lumansad wey pehendiyad e seini te eg-atelug e.
Egkakkak ka manuk diyè te tanè.
The chicken on the ground is clucking.
Egkakkak ka manuk te kenè pad egsilè ka aldew.
The chicken clucks before the sun rises.
cf. pammekak
Eng. cluck
