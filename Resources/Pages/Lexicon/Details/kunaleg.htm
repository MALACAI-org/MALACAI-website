kunaleg
/kunalɔg/
conj.
“whereas”
 
conj.
whereas
Wrd.Gloss: whereas
Kahiyen ni Nining ne nalaag e ka saklag kunaleg te inggeles din e su egkaaldek din ne egkalaag.
Nining thought that the fishing net was lost whereas she had hidden it because she was afraid that it would get lost.
Cult. This conjunction is used only when the the result is completely unexpected
Eng. whereas; but rather; however; on the other hand; on the contrary; truly
