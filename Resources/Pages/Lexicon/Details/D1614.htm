duput
/duput/
v_rt.
“appeal”
 
v.
for someone (egduput, agent, eg><ø-) to appeal a case (igduput, ig-) to another authority (egduputan, location, eg><an-)
Wrd.Gloss: appeal
Ka egduput iyan ka egparani diyè te duma ne etew eyew egpepangabang wey egpabulig. Egkaayun ne egpatabang meyitenged te keuyahan, prublima, wey dalu wey me kalasayan.
Egduput e pad diyè te karumaan ku su dakel ka prublima ku kuntee.
I will appeal to my relatives because I have a big problem today.
Eng. appeal; refer case
