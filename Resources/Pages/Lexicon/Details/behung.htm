behung
/bɔhuŋ/
 
v.
for someone (egbehung, migbehung, imbehung, actor, eg><ø-) to hunt wild animals (egbehungan, imbehungan, migbehungan/pigbehungan, location, eg><an-) with something (igbehung, instrument, ig><ø-)
Egbehung a ka egngangalap su marelag naan dè kuntec ka ngalap.
I will use this method for hunting because wild animals are rare.
Migbehung a ka migngangalap su marelag e nahud ka ngalap.
I used this kind of hunting because wild animals are rare.
Diyè te sita ka imbehungan ney te migngangalap.
Sita was the place for this method for hunting.
Tungtung wey lesung ka eggamiten ne igbehung.
Fireflies and mortar are the instruments used for this hunting technique.
Eng. method for hunting; techniques; hunting; strategy
OCM: Hunting and Trapping (224)
DDP: Hunting birds (6.4.3)
