gelebek
/gɔlɔbɔk/
v_rt.
“frequently”
 
vi.
for someone (eggelebek, agent, eg><ø-) to often be doing something
Ka eggelebek iyan ka eglasi-lasi wey kenè egkaaldek ne eggimu te minsan nekey ne igkeupii rin. Egkeyimu rin e seini ne batasan ke layun din eggimuwa. Ka maheteng te kaluwasan kayi iyan ka peg-uney-uney peggimu te minsan nekey ne igkeupii rin te warè pegkehè wey pegduwa-ruwa.
Kenè ka eggelebek egkelag due.
Don’t always play with it.
Egpekeiling e si Usi te busawen su eggelebek dè due egpangasak te warè karuan.
Usi is like a crazy person because he continuously talks nonsense.
cf. layun —always
Eng. often; always; repeatedly; habitually; continuously; freqently; permanently
