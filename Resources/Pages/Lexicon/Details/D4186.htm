magkukulam
/magkukulam/
 
n.
for someonesorcerer witch craft devil advocate (talaglambus)
Ka duma ne umew te iling kayi ne etew iyan sika se Talaglambus ne egpadereet te umul te me etew.
Talaglambus naande iya sika ne etew.
That man is a sorcerer.
Syn. talaglambus
Eng. devil advocate
Tag. mangkukulam
Ceb. mamarangay

Usage level: M
