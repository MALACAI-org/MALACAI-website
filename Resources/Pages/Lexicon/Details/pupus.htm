pupus
/pupus/
 
vi.
for someone (egkapupusan, napupusan, egkA><an-) loss of cool
Ka egkapupusan iyan ka egkeepusan e te pegpariralem wey kenad e egkaantus su igpakapalihawang e iya ka diyè te diralem din. Ka seini ne lalag, warè dè migpasabut te pegkabelu, ke kenè minsan nekey ne igkeupii ne eggimuwen ne igkaruwa-ruwa piru warad iya naantus egkaayunan degma te seini ne lalag. Su ka seini ne lalag migpasabut te pegkeepus te egpakabalag te minsan nekey ne igkaruwa-ruwa ne eggimuwen.
Napupusan e ganna si Peyi sikan naa ka warad sikandin nakatigkel te langet din diyè te etew ne igkeepes din.
Peyi lost her cool so didn't control her anger to the person she hated.
Emun ke egkapupusan e ka etew egpakasukul e sikandin.
If a person loss his patient he will fight back.
Eng. loss of cool; forced into a corner; loss patient
