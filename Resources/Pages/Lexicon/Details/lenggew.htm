lenggew
/lɔŋgɔw/
adj_rt.
“well”
 
1.
adj.
(malenggew, mA1-) to be well after being sick
Wrd.Gloss: well
Ka malenggew iyan ka manekal wey ke neuyag pad/ded. Migpasabut seini te langun ne due geyinawa wey kenè dè ne iyan igpasabut kayi ka migderalu.
Eng. well; better; recovered; healthy
DDP: Recover from sickness (2.5.1.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
adj.
(malenggew, mA1-) conscious; still alive
Eng. still alive
Kahiyen ku te egsamsam te uled ne migketeng diyè te dalan ke napatey e, kunaleg te malenggew bes ded.
I think that the snake lie down on my way is dead but I'm wrong, it was still alive.
Eng. alive still; conscious; aware
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
