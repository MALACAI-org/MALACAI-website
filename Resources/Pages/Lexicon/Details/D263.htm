andana
/ʔandana/
n.
“storey”
 
n.
storey
Ka andana klasi te baley wey minsan nekey ne masulug se baad ne pineg-untud. Piru iyan dè egkaayunan kayi ka derakel iling te baley, barku, bilding wey minsan nekey pad ne egkeupaan. Senge baad ka kaluwasan te andana.
Daruwa ne andana ka baley ney.
Our house has two storey.
Wrd.Gloss: storey
Eng. storey; floor, multistorey
