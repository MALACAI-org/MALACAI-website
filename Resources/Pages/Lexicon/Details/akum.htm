akum
/ʔakum/
n.
“a_vegetable”
 
n.
an erect smooth-branched plant, which is eaten as a leafy vegetable
Ka akum iyan ke hilamunen ne bayew malalab ka batek wey egkakeen ka dewun din. Kenè amana ne mallayat ka seini ne me hilamunen wey bayew due me duhi rin ne dereisek ne diyè egpanubù te lenged te dewun wey lawa rin. Egpanibulasan seini ka egkabuyag e.
Keupian a ne egkeen te akum ne gulay su dakel se kalambuan.
I want to eat an akum vegetable for it is rich in vitamins.
O. Syn. bansahen
Eng. vegetable variety
