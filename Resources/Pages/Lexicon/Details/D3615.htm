layun
/layun/
adv.
“always”
 
adv.
always
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se eg-abey-abey, tabid dè, eglibed-libed wey malasi.
Layun dè eg-uran kayi te kanami.
Here in our place it's always raining.
Wrd.Gloss: always
Syn. tabid
Eng. always; often; repeatedly; continually; frequently; constantly; frequently; permanently
Tag. lágì
Ceb. kanunay
Ilo.: kanáyon
OCM: Weights and Measures (804)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: G
