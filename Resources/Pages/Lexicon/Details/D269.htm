andu
/ʔandu/
n.
“pestle”
 
n.
pestle
Ka andu in-abat ne kayu ne egkakamkamelan dè ka karakel wey sengerepa wey ke subla pad ka kalayat. Eggamiten seini eyew igbinayu te paley ne diyè igtahù te lesung.
Deisek ka andu ney piru mabehat.
Our pestle was small but its too heavy.
