butal
/butal/
v_rt.
“elect”
 
v.
for someone (egbutal, agent, eg><ø-) to vote or elect an official (egbutalan, location, eg><an-)
Wrd.Gloss: elect
Egbutalan ku sikeykew. [Kaayun ne egbutal a.]
I will vote you.
Eng. elect; vote
