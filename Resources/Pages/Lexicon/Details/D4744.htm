palemlem
/palɔmlɔm/
v_rt.
“plan”
 
n.
for someone (egpalemlem, migpalemlem/impalemlem, agent, eg><ø-) to plan something (egpalemlemen, impalemlem/migpalemlem/pigpalemlem, patient, eg><en-)
Wrd.Gloss: plan
Palemlem ka ke nekey ka meupiya ne eggimuwen ke lengleng.
Plan what is good to do this rainy season.
Egpalemlemen ku pad ke nekey se eggimuwen ku kaaselem te maselem.
I'll think about what I'll do tomorrow morning.
Eng. plan; think about; ponder; consider; thought; scheme; expect
DDP: Language and thought (3), Think (3.2), Think about (3.2.1.1)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
