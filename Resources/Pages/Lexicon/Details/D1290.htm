dahew
/dagɔw/
v_rt.
“shortly”
 
v.
for someone (egdagdahew, migdagdahew/indagdahew, agent, eg><ø-rdpCVC-) to do something quickly (egdagdahawan, indagdahawan/migdagdahawan/pigdagdahawan, location, eg><an-rdpCVC-)
Eng. quickly; hurriedly; briefly; momentarily
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
 
v.
for someone (egparagdahew, migparagdahew, inparagdahew/pigparagdahew, agent, eg><ø-pA-rdpCVC-) to request someone to finish something immediately (egparagdahawan, imparagdahawan/migparagdahawan/pigparagdahawan, location, eg><an-pA-rdpCVC-) to go (somewhere) and return fairly shortly, e.g., go in the morning and return by lunch
Eggendiyè e pad te kamet egdagdahew.
I will go to my field briefly.
O. Syn. seulì
Eng. briefly; quickly (in time); intermittently; soon; shortly; immediately
OCM: Travel (484)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
