lameg2
/lamɔg/
 
v.
for someone (eglelameg, miglelameg/inlelameg, lameg, agent, eg><ø-rdpCV-) to mix the food of pet animals (eglamehan, inlamehan/miglamehan/piglamehan, lamehi, lamehan, patient, eg><an-) with viand (iglameg/inlameg, theme, ig><ø-)
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se igsewug nu ka serè diyè te keenen eyew egmalehed ne egkeen ka asu wey ke miyew ne ayam. Seini diyè dè iya egkahamit ne lalag te ayam kenè egkaayun te etew su due regma igngaran te duma.
Lamehi nu kun ka pa ka keenen te asu su kenè egkeen.
The food for dog must be mix with viand so that the dog will have an appetite to eat.
Eng. food which mix with viand; food; mix viand; viand
Ceb. sagul
