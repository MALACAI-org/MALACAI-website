duyè
/duyɔʔ/
v_rt.
“curse”
 
v.
for someone (egduyè, agent, eg><ø-) to curse another person (egduyaan, location, eg><an-)
Wrd.Gloss: a curse
Ka egduyè iyan ka pegdilus wey ke peglalag te amana ne mareet wey ke masakit ne lalag ne egdumaan te mabehat ne legpad wey ke dusa diyè te sabeka ne etew. Migpalintutuu ka duma ne duen kun geem te lalag ne iglalag te etew.
Kenè ka egduyè te kuntere nu, ke kenè, keilangan ne eggeyinawaan nu sikandin.
Do not curse your enemy but rather you must love him.
Eng. curse; condemn; hex
