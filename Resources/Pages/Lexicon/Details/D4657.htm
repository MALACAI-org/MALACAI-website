paahi
/paʔagi/
 
n.
way; means
Egpamitew ki te paahi ke egmenuwen te eggingutel ka palees ne eggelebek egpandawi te me manuk.
We will find a way on how to catch t he lizard that always attacking our poultry.
Eng. way; means; method; system
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
 
adj.
(pamaahi, paN-) way; method; strategy: system; idea
Eng. way; method; strategy; system; idea
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B,L
 
v.
for something e.g., problem (egpamaahiyan, migpamaahiyan/impamaahiyan, theme, eg><an-pA-) to be find by someone's solution
Eng. find by someone's solution, to be
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F,L
 
prep.
(pinaahi, <in>-) by means of
Eng. by means of
Egkaangken ta ka keupianan pinaahi te geyinawa ta te tagsè sabeka.
We will have prosperity through love of each other.
Eng. through; using
Online dict status: -
Usage level: L
