bunayuk
/bunayuk/
n.
“stomach_flab”
 
n.
stomach flab
Wrd.Gloss: stomach_flab
Ka bunayuk iyan ka kalambuan te etew diyè te getek ne egmambitin-bitin e wey egkalulun e ka laplap te gettek. Iyan egpuunan kayi ka amana ne pegkeen te mallambu ne egkakeen wey ka amana ne pegkabel-is te etew.
Due talagpanulu ney ne amana ka bunayuk din su egkapiyut e sikandin tenged te dakel ka liwaraan din.
We have a teacher who has extreme stomach flab because her large waist makes her looks no backside.
Eng. stomach flab; flabby stomach; rolls of flab; fat rolls
Ceb. bilbil
OCM: Ethnoanatomy (826)
DDP: Flesh (2.1.7)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
adj.
(bunayuken, -en) a person with a flabby stomach
Eng. flabby stomach; fat rolls
Ceb. bilbilon
Online dict status: -
