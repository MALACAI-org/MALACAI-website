ungud
/ʔuŋud/
 
adj.
(ungud-ungud, rdpRT-) slow to learn
Ungud-ungud naan dè iya ne kenè egkateu sieni ne etew ne derengana.
This person is very slow learner even he is too old.
Eng. slow to learn; backward; dull
