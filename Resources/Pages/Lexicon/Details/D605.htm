balikid
/balikid/
v_rt.
“turn_over”
 
v.
for someone or something (egbalikid, migbalikid/imbalikid, agent, eg><ø-) to rotate something (egbalikiren, imbalikid/migbalikid/pigbalikid, patient, eg><en-) left and right direction (egbalikiran, imbalikiran/migbalikiran/pigbalikiran, location, eg><an-)
Layun dè egbalikid si Diyun ke eggibat.
Diyun is always rolling over when he sleeps.
Egbalikiren ku pad ka ikam ne indampil ku te aldew.
I will invert the mat that I dried in the sun.
Syn. bagkeleng
O. Syn. baliyung
Eng. rotate; turn around; about turn; turn in place; spin; overturn; roll over; invert; turn over; inverted, to be
Tag. umíkot
Ceb. tuyuk
OCM: Locomotion (481), Ethnophysics (822)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something (egkabalikid, nabalikid, agent, egkA><ø-) to become inverted
Eng. turn over; inverted, to be
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
