asemud
/ʔasɔmud/
v_rt.
“angry_discontented”
 
1.
v.
for someone (eg-asemud, mig-asemud/in-asemud, asemura, agent, eg><ø-) to dislike someone's ideas and opinions (eg-asemuran, in-asemuran/mig-asemuran/pig-asemuran, asemuri, location, eg><an-) for a reason (ig-asemud, in-asemud, reason, ig><ø-)
Eng. disagree; disapprove; discontented, be; anger, express; anger, show; unconvinced, be
DDP: Show, let someone see (2.3.1.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
for something (egpakaasemud, nakaasemud, pakaasemud, agent, egpAkA-) to cause someone to show disapproval on their face; to be the cause (igpakaasemud/impakaasemud, reason, igpAkA-) of someone showing disapproval on their face
Igpakaasemud ku ka lalag din ne mareet.
The thing that made me show disapproval was his bad words.
Eng. disapproval, cause; offend
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
2.
vi.
for someone (egpaasemud, migpaasemud/impaasemud, agent, egpA><ø-) to show disapproval of someone or something (egpaasemuran, impaasemuran/migpaasemuran/pigpaasemuran, paasemuran, location, egpA><an-) with their facial expressions (igpaasemud, impaasemud, instrument, igpA-) or for a reason (igpaasemud, reason, igpA-)
Syn. egkabelù
Nakataha a ne nabelù si Dudung su migpaasemud e sikandin.
I know that Dudung was angry becuase he poked his bottom lip out.
Igpaasemud e te batè ka ula-ula rin
The face of the child shows disapproval.
Iyan din igpaasemud su warè nabennali te lalag nu.
He is showing disapproval because he dislikes your words.
Eng. bottom lip, poke out; look stern; stern; discontented; unhappy; angry; upset; sulk; frown; scowl
DDP: Contentment (3.4.1.1.6), Upset (3.4.2.1.6), Discontent (3.4.2.1.9), Angry (3.4.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
for a group (egmaasemud, migmaasemud/immaasemud, agent, egmA-) to show their disapproval of something or someone (egmaasemuran, immaasemuran/migmaasemuran/pigmaasemuran, location, egmA–an) by their facial expressions
Eng. bottom lip poked out; look stern; stern; discontented; unhappy; angry; upset; sulk; frown; scowl
DDP: Contentment (3.4.1.1.6), Upset (3.4.2.1.6), Discontent (3.4.2.1.9), Angry (3.4.2.3)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
