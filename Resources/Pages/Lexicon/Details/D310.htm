antag
/ʔantag/
v_rt.
“vibrate”
 
vi.
for someone (eg-antag, mig-antag/in-antag, agent, eg><ø-) to tap something to vibrate or wobble it (eg-antahen, in-antag/mig-antag/pig-antag, antahen, theme, eg><en-) somewhere (eg-antahan, in-antahan/mig-antahan/pig-antahan, location, eg><an-)
Wrd.Gloss: vibrate
Masakit ka peka ku su naantag a ka mig-untud te terak.
My back is aching because I was wobbled when I ride the track.
Eng. vibrate; shake; wobble; tap
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vt.
for something (egkaantag, naantag, actor, egkA><ø-) to be vibrated by movements (igkaantag, ingkaantag, reason, igkA><ø-)
Wrd.Gloss: vibrate
Amana ka tanè ne egkaantag ke egbibinayu ensi Elisa te hemey.
The ground vibrate when Ilisa pound a rice.
Eng. vibrate; shake; wobble
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
