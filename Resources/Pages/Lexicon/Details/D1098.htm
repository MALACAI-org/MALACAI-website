bukelas
/bukɔlas/
v_rt.
“unwrap”
 
v.
for someone (egbukelasan, agent, eg><ø-) to unroll something
Ka egbukelas iyan ka eglekes wey eglukat te minsan nekey ne nabukusan wey nabunbunan. Iyan seini ka pegpakita te minsan nekey ne nakabengel wey nekeeles iling te bewu ne imbunguhan te dewun.
Nekey-a ka egbukalelasan din?
What is he unwrapping?
Huwey nud e bukalasi ka indasag ku keykew ke sabsabeka nu naan dè.
You can open your gift when you are left alone.
Wrd.Gloss: unwrap
Eng. open; present; take off the cover; unroll
O. Syn. belat —unroll sleeping mat
Eng. unroll
