upa
/ʔupa/
Var. uppa
n.
“hen”
 
n.   
female chicken
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se malitan ne manuk ne egpangatelug wey kenè ne isasangkub su egpaateluhen dè.
Egkeupian a ne egmemanuk te upa su eg-atelug.
I'd like to raise a hen because it will bear eggs.
Ant. lumansad —rooster
Eng. female chicken; hen; fowl
Tag. inahín (mother animal)
Ceb. himungà (hen); inayan (mother animal)
Ilo.: upa; pamusián
OCM: Fauna (136)
DDP: Bird (1.6.1.2), Male and female animals (1.6.7)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: B
