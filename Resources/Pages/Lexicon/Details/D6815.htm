titib
/titib/
 
n.
the yellowish part of the teeth
Ka se makalawag ne migdeket te ngipen ne puun te kenè ka egpakapanupras wey ke layun ka egkebkeb te behas.
Duen bes mulè titib ku sika ka egngisiyan à te me Laha ke egngisi à.
I have tartar on my tooth that's the reason when i smile to a lady they laugh at me.
Syn. tama
Eng. yellowish part of the teeth; teeth yellowed; tartar
OCM: Personal Hygiene (515)
DDP: Tooth (2.1.1.5)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
