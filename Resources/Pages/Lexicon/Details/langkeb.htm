langkeb
/laŋkɔb/
v_rt.
“lie_on_stomach”
 
1.
vi.
for someone (eglangkeb, miglangkeb/inlangkeb, agent, eg><ø-) to lie on one's stomach
Eglangkeb ka anaken ku ne eggibat.
My nephew/niece lies on his/her stomach when he/she lies down.
Genr. gibat
Eng. lie on one's stomach
Tag. dumapâ
Ceb. hapà
Ilo.: agpakléb
OCM: Postures (516)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for a young baby (eglangkeb, miglangkeb/inlangkeb, eg><ø-) to roll over onto its stomach
Cult. the sixth stage of a young baby
Eng. roll over
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
2.
vi.
for someone (egpakalangkeb, nakalangkeb, actor, egpAkA-) to slip over and fall prostrate (egkalangkeban, nalangkeban, location, egkA><an-)
Egpakalangkeb e kangkuwa sika se batè ne egpalpallahuy te malerek.
That child that's running on that slippery place will fall over head first soon.
Eng. slip over; fall prostrate; head first, fall
Tag. mádapà
Ceb. hapa
Ilo.: napaídda
OCM: Bodily Injuries (752)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
3.
vi.
for someone (eglangkeb, miglangkeb/inlangkeb, agent, eg><ø-) to get down
Eng. get down
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
