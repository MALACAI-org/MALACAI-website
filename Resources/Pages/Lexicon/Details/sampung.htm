sampung
/sampuŋ/
 
v.
for money (kasampung, kA-) to reach the exact (extra??) amount
Eng. exact
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
(egkasampung, nasampung, egkA><ø-) to be exact (as in money)
Sabeka naan dè ka kulang ne egkasampung e ka sapulù ne patuk ney.
Only one lack then our ten ducks will be completed.
Eng. exact; correct; equal; complete; round off
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
(igsampung, ig><ø-) to make up for a previous loss; to add more
Wrd.Gloss: make_up
Igsampung kud e ka sabaka ne patuk nu te siyew ne patuk ku eyew egkeyimu ne sapulù langun.
I will make up your one duck to my ten to make them all ten.
Syn. igtapal
Eng. make up; catch up; fill up; add
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
