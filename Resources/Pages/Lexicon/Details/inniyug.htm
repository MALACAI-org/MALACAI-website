inniyug
/ʔinniug/
v_rt.
“turn_one's_back”
 
1.
v.
for someone (eg-inniyug, agent, eg><ø-) to turn one's back on something
Eg-inniyug ad e perem te himu ku piru kenè egkaayun su ispurtanti amana seini.
Wrd.Gloss: turn_one's_back
Eng. turn your back; turn from
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
prep.
(keinniyuhan, kA><an-) behind
Eng. behind
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
2.
v.
(egkeinniyuhan, egkA><an-) to be at the back
Egkeinniyuhan ku sikaniyu me suled su duen pad eggimuwen ku kayi.
Eng. back; behind; rear
Tag. máhulí; likód
Ceb. ulahi; luyu; likud
OCM: Miscellaneous Structures (349)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
v.
(pein-inniyuhey, pA><ey-rdpCVC-) (for two people) to have their backs facing each other
Mig-in-inniyuhey sikandan ka mig-untud te kalabew.
Eng. backs facing each other; back to back
OCM: Postures (516)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: R
 
3.
v.
for someone (eg-inniyug, mig-inniyug, agent, eg><ø-) to repent
Nakasuman-suman a ne eg-inniyug ad e te me salè ku.
Eng. repent
DDP: Sorry (3.4.2.2)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
