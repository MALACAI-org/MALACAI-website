abbet
/ʔabbɔt/
adj_rt.
“strong”
 
adj.
(maabbet, mA1-) strong (of something such as wind, or someone throwing) or loud (of a sound)
Ka abbet ne lalag iyan igpasabut ka malantus, meiyal wey manekal. Egkasabut seini ke eggamiten e diyè te peglalag.
Maabbet sikandin ne egdegpak su neumaan din pad te egdegpak ka bayaku seeyè te ulepung te kayu.
He is strong in throwing because his throw reached the bird at the top of the tree.
Maabbet ne egpallahuy si Buelan su layun egdaug.
Buelan run+fast because he is always won the race.
Eng. strong (of wind or throwing); forceful; loud
OCM: Sensation and Perception (151), Ethnometeorology (821)
DDP: Wind (1.1.3.1), Loud (2.3.2.4), Strong (2.4.1)
