keyis
/kɔis/
n.
“sweep_away”
 
v.
for someone (egkeyis, agent, eg><ø-) to push something or someone aside
Mania te egkeyisen nu ka me sagbet ne inlimud ku ne egsilaban ku ma sika?
Eng. push aside
Ceb. waklì
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
v.
(egpangeyis, eg><ø-paN-) to sweep away rubbish - not the normally sweeping action but much coarser as in removing a pile of rubbish with a stick broom
Te! Egpangeyis e ka miyew te alabat.
Syn. kuwit
Eng. gather
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
1.
v.
for someone (egpangeyis, agent, eg><ø-paN-) to struggle and resist something, e.g. a child who doesn't want to take medicine so kicks his feet and fights
Malehet en iya ne egpangeyis ka anaken ku ne egpeinumen te bawi.
Eng. struggle; resist
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
