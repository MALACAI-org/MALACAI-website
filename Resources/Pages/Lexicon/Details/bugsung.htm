bugsung
/bugsuŋ/
v_rt.
“wrap_in_bundle”
 
vt.
for someone (egbugsung, migbugsung/imbugsung, bugsung, agent, eg><ø-) to wrap something (e.g. rice or eggs) (egbugsungen, imbugsung/migbugsung/pigbugsung, bugsunga, theme, eg><en-) up in a cloth and tie it into a small bundle, or to put a software into
Wrd.Gloss: wrap_in_bundle
Egbugsungen din ka paley ne imbehey te amey rin .
He is going to wrap (by own clothe) the palay that was given to him by his father.
Eng. wrap in bundle; bundle up; put in bag
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
 
vi.
for something, e.g., water (in a plastic bag), or rice (in a cloth) (egkabugsung, nabugsung, actor, egkA><ø-) to become wrapped up
Warè igkabugsung ku te mangga ne imbeli ku su nalungahan e ka intahuan ku.
My mango burst up and I don't have to wrap for it.
Eng. wrap up; bundle up
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
