gulub
/gulub/
adj_rt.
 
adj.
(mahulub, mA1-) noisy (of sounds or voice or talkative)
Mahulub ka me batè ne egmangekelag diyè te lama ney.
The children is noisy playing in our front yard.
Mahulub ka mulè da.
Oh your so talkative.
Eng. noisy; loud (of sounds or voice or talkative); rowdy; talkative
Tag. íngay
Ceb. sabà
Ilo.: naríri; naariwáwa
OCM: Sensation and Perception (151)
DDP: Loud (2.3.2.4)
Online dict status: -
Usage level: F
 
v.
for group (eggulub, miggulub/inggulub, agent, eg><ø-) to make noise
Eng. noise, to make
Online dict status: -
Usage level: M
