bewu2
/bɔu2/
v_rt.
“cool_down”
 
v.
(nabewu, nA-) (for food) to become stale or cool
Ipeinum nud te batè seini se kepi su nabewu e.
Get the child drink this coffee because it is already cool down.
Eng. stale; lukewarm; cool food
Ceb. báhaw
OCM: Sensation and Perception (151), Preservation and Storage of Food (251)

Usage level: M
