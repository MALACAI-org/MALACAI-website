ulambak
/ʔulambak/
“to lean on”
 
v.
to lean on
Ka seini ne lalag iyan sika se iling te asu ke eg-ala-ala eg-ukembè wey hendue te egpamaneyik.
Ka asu meyilut ne eg-ulambak.
The dog is fond of lean on.
