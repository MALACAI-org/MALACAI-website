asikasu
/asikasu/
 
v.
(eg-asikasu, mig-asikasu/in-asikasu, asikasu, eg><ø-) to manage/supervise something/someone
Ka asikasu ne lalag iyan igpasabut ka peg-atiman wey ke kene pebalahad te minsan nekey. Ka duma ne igpasabut te seini ne lalag ka pegpeinum te kepi wey ke te weyig naa diyè te etew ne migpanumbaley, sabeka ne klasiyan te peg-asikasu. Meupiya ne subla ka etew ne eg-asikasu te sumbaley, mahaliyug, wey te subla ne egkaayu-ayu.
Asikasuwa niyu ka prublima ni Mammay eyew ware egpatey.
Take care to the problem of Mammay so that no one will die.
Mahirap ka ware eg-asikasu diyè te huspital.
Difficult when no will oversee in the hospital.
Eng. manage; oversee; superintend; supervise
Ceb. asikáso
