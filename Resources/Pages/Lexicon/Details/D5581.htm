sangen
/saŋɔn/
“install”
 
v.
for someone (egsangen, agent, eg><ø-) to install something
Ka egsangen iyan ka egtangen te yuggu te kalabew wey te lahitan. Deisek dè ka egkaayunan te seini ne lalag su due degma ne lalag te duma ne himu iling te eg-uyamu te baled, egpes-ek te baley wey masalig pad ka duma.
Isangen nu pa ka lahitan su egpanleuy e pad te abakaan.
Kindly set up the the steel bar because I'm going to survey the plantation of abaka.
Eng. set up; organize install; assemble
Ceb. taud
