local
/loh-kəl/
 
adj.
 
1.
Def.: minsan nekey ne diyè dè te nateil ne inged
something local is in and around a particular place.
When I went to the dentist, he gave me a local anaesthetic in my mouth.
The damage from the flood was only local, and the people in the next barangay didn't have any problems.
I bought some of the local coffee when I visited the village.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: etew ne nangugpè te in-ugpaan nu
MS: etew ne nangugpè te in-ugpaan nu
a person who lives in the area where you are
If you can't find the building, ask one of the locals.
Source: GSL0351-0375
