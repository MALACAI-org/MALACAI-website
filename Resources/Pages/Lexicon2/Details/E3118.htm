up
 
prep, adv.
 
1.
Def.: egpatakereg; egpamaneyik; egkatut
MS: egpatakereg; egpamaneyik; egkatut
If you move up something such as a ladder, you move to a higher position on that thing.
They climbed up the mountain.
Migpatakereg sikandan te bubungan.
 
prep.
 
2.
Def.: patakereg; paribabew
MS: patakereg; paribabew
If something is located up something such as a ladder, it is located at a higher position on that thing
Their house is about 2km up the mountain.
Ka baley ran su me daruwa ne kilumitru pad patakereg te bubungan.
 
prep.
 
3.
Def.: egpatiralan; egpeureyik; egpalaras
MS: egpatiralan; egpeureyik; egpalaras
If you move up something such as a road, you move along that thing.
We walked up the road.
Migpatiralan key te kelesadda.
 
prep.
 
4.
Def.: te kewun-aan [kewun-a͡an]
MS: kewun-aan, te
If something is located up something such as a road, it is located at a further position on that thing.
Their house is about 2km up the road.
Ka baley ran su me daruwa ne kilumitru pabulus diyè te kewun-aan [kewun-a͡an] te kelesadda.
 
adv.
 
5.
Def.: egparibabew; egmallayat; egderakel
MS: egparibabew; egmallayat; egderakel
If a number or level goes up, it becomes higher or larger.
My grades went up at college because I studied more.
Migderakel ka gradu ku te kulihiyu ad su migpakanekal a mig-istadi.
The temperature is going up.
The water level in the river is going up.
 
adj.
 
6.
Def.: neimpusan; napenga
MS: neimpusan; napenga
If a period of time is up, it is finished.
Your time for the test is up, so please stop writing.
Neimpusan e ka uras te tis nu, sika ka palihug sanggel kad ka egsulat.
Source: prepositions; adverbs; GSL0026-0050
