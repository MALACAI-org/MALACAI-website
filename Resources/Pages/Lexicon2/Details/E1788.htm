mass1
/mæss/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: makehal ne karakelan te minsan nekey ne warè nateil ne ula-ula
a solid quantity of something all together, with no special shape (e.g., a mass of rice).
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: kamenu ka karakelan wey ka kaluahan ne egkahamit
the mass of something is how big it is, and how much space it uses.
We measured the mass of the basketball.
Migseked ney ka kaluahan te baskitbulan.
 
adj.
 
3.
Def.: Eggamit ka seini ke eglalag ke te amana ne dakel wey ke susuluhi (ne minsan nekey).
You use mass to talk about a very large amount of something, or a very large group of people (e.g., mass media, mass-production).
Mass-produced products from factories are cheaper.
 
vi.
 
4.
Def.: eglibulung eyew egkeyimu ne dakel
MS: eglibulung
If people or things mass together, they move together to make one large body.
The fish massed together when the sharks came.
Miglibulung ka me peit te migginguma ka ihu.
Source: GSL0876-0900
