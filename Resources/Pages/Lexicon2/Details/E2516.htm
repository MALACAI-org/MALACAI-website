roll
/rohll/
 
vi.
 
1.
Def.: egkalilid; egkabalikid
MS: egkalilid; egkabalikid
If someone or something rolls, they move by turning over one or more times.
The ball rolled along the ground
Ka bula nalilid diyè te tanè.
The baby rolled over onto his back.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egbalikid; eglilid
MS: egbalikid; eglilid
If you roll something, you cause it to move by turning over one or more times.
He rolled the ball along the ground.
Miglilid din ka bula diyè te tanè.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: eglulun
MS: eglulun
If you roll or roll up something flat, you turn it over and over until it is in a round shape like a tube.
He rolled up his sleeping mat.
Miglulun din ka ikam din.
 
n-ct.
 
4.
Def.: linulun
MS: linulun
something which is made of a flat thing made into a round shape like a tube.
There is a sleeping roll in the room.
Due linulun ne ikam diyè te sinabeng.
Source: GSL0876-0900
