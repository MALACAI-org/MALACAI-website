bear
/bair/
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: egpekeeleg; egpekeuyan
MS: egpekeeleg; egpekeuyan
If something bears another thing, it carries the weight of that other thing.
The bridge needs to be strong so that it can bear the weight of all the trucks that pass.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: eg-uyan
MS: eg-uyan
If you bear something, you carry it.
She came into the room bearing a cake.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: eglesutan
MS: eglesutan
If a woman bears a child, she gives birth to it.
 
vt.
 
4.
Def.: egbehas
MS: egbehas
If a tree bears fruit, it produces fruit
 
vt.
 
5.
Def.: egkakaya; egkaantus
MS: egkakaya; egkaantus
If you bear something difficult or painful, you continue to live with that difficult thing.
I can't bear that terrible noise! Please stop it!
Kenè ku egkaantus ka kahulub! Sanggela niyud!
We can't do anything about the bad weather, so we'll just have to bear it.
Warè egkeyimu ta kayi te mareet ne timpu, eg-antusen te naan dè iya.
 
vi.
 
6.
Def.: egsungkiyè te deisek dè
MS: egsungkiyè te deisek dè
If you bear left or bear right, you change your direction a little to the left or right.
The road bears left at the bottom of the hill.
Ka kelesadda migsungkiyè te igkahibang te taliwarè te labuntud.
Irreg. v.  bore, borne [born].
