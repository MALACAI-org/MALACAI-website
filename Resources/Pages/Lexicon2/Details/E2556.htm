same
 
adj, adv.
 
1.
Def.: nekeg-iling
MS: nekeg-iling
If two things are the same as each other, they are exactly like each other.
You have the same T-shirt as me.
Nekeg-iling ka kumbalè ta.
 
adj, adv.
 
2.
Def.: warè naalin [na͡alin]; warè nabalbalawi
MS: warè naalin; warè nabalbalawi
If something is the same as it was before, it hasn't changed.
I haven't seen her for years, but she still looks the same.
Egtuihan e ne warè ku nakita sikandin, piru iling te warè naalin [na͡alin] sikandin.
 
adj.
 
3.
Def.: iyan ded; sikan ded
MS: iyan ded; sikan ded
just one thing
She always wears the same hat.
Layun egsaluub sikandin te iyan ded ne kalù.
 
adj, pron.
 
4.
Def.: iling ded
MS: iling ded
You use the same to talk about something which you talked about just before.
She sat down and he did the same.
Migpinnuu sikandin wey miggimu te iling ded.
Her bike had a broken wheel, and his bike had the same problem.
Source: GSL0126-0150
