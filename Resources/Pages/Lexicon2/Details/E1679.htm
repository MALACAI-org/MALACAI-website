lecture
/lek-cher/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: pegpanulù
MS: pegpanulù
a speech in which someone teaches a large group about something (give a lecture).
He gave an interesting lecture on Philippine history.
Migbehey sikandin te amana ne makabebagget ne pegpanulù puun te guhuren te Pilipinas.
 
vi, vt.
 
2.
Def.: egpanulù diyè te katangenan
MS: egpanulù diyè te katangenan
If someone lectures, they teach a large group about something.
He lectures in science at the University.
Migpanulù sikandin te Sayans diyè te siyudad ne iskuylaan [iskuyla͡an].
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: egpakiglalag te mapahetpet
MS: egpakiglalag te mapahetpet
If you lecture someone about something, you talk to them strongly for a long time because you think they are wrong, or did something wrong.
When I arrived home after dark, my mother lectured me about safety.
 
n-ct.
 
4.
Def.: pangasak wey ke panulù
MS: pangasak; panulù
a long talk by someone because they think you are wrong or did something wrong (give someone a lecture).
When I arrived home after dark, my mother gave me a lecture about safety.
