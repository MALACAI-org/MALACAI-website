age
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: leg-un; tuig; idad
MS: leg-un; tuig; idad
The age of a person or thing is the number of months or years that they have existed.
What's your age?
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: kaluhayan; kabuyahan (meyitenged te pegbunsud te timpu)
MS: kaluhayan; kabuyahan
a long period of time in history.
 
vi.
 
3.
Def.: egkabuyag
MS: egkabuyag
If someone or something ages, they become older.
My grandmother has aged a lot in the past two years, and she is much weaker now.
 
vt.
 
4.
Def.: egpakabuyag (meyitenged egpuunan)
MS: egpakabuyag
If something ages you, it causes you to feel or look old.
If you spend a lot of time in the sun, it will age your skin.
Ke layun ka egkarampil te aldew, mahaan dè egkabuyag ka lap-lap nu.
Source: GSL0351-0375
