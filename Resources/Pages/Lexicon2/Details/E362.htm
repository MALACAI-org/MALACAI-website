bond
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: egpegdekket; egpegsumpul
MS: egpegdekket; egpegsumpul
If something bonds two things together, it causes them to stick or join together.
The glue bonded the two pieces of paper together.
Ka parekket migpegdekket te daruwa ne buuk ne pepil.
 
v-recip.
 
2.
Def.: egkalayam
MS: egkalayam
If you bond with someone, you start to have a close relationship with them.
A baby bonds with its mother quickly.
Ka batè mahaan dè egkalayam te iney.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: mapahetpet ne eggeramen te geyinawa
MS: eggeramen te geyinawa; geyinawa, eggeramen te
a strong feeling of love, friendship or togetherness between people.
There is a strong bond between my brothers and me.
Due mapahetpet ne geyinawa ney te tahasuled.
Source: AWL6
 
 
bonds
 
n-pl.
Def.: hiket
MS: hiket
things which tie you up (like rope around your hands or feet).
