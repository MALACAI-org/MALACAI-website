reduction
/ri-dak-shən/
 
n-uc.
 
1.
Def.: pegpariisek; pegsalin; peg-angey; pegkuwa
MS: pegpariisek; pegsalin; peg-angey; pegkuwa
the action of causing something to be smaller or less.
The reduction of prices will be very welcome.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: nasalin
MS: nasalin
A reduction in something is an amount which is taken away from it to make it less.
This month there was a 10% reduction in the price of gas.
Te seini ne bulan nasalinan te sapulu ne pursintu ka bali te gasulina.
