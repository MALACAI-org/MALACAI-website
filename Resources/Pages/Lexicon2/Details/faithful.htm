faithful
/faith-fəl/
 
adj.
 
1.
Def.: mapalintutuu; matinumanen
MS: mapalintutuu; matinumanen
If someone is faithful, they continue to do the things which they have promised, or the things which are their responsibility.
 
adj.
 
2.
Def.: egpabulus-bulus te geyinawa diyè te senge etew, wey kenè egkeiyam wey ke egkabag-u.
If someone is faithful, they continue their relationship with a person, and do not change (e.g., a faithful friend).
 
adj.
 
3.
Def.: kenè egpanlibug diyè te asawa te senge etew
MS: kenè egpanlibug
If someone is faithful to their husband or wife, they don't have sexual relationships with other people.
Sem.Dom.: keyterms; GSL0801-0825 - related

MS: pegkamapalintutuu; pegkamatinumanen; pegpabulus-bulus te geyinawa diyè te senge etew; kenè pegpanlibug diyè te asawa te senge etew
the state of being faithful.
