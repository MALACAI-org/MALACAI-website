design
/də-zine/
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: egsumansuman te ula-ula
MS: egsumansuman te ula-ula
If you design something, you plan what it will look like, and what parts it will have.
He designed the house and then built it.
Migsusuman-suman din ka ula-ula te baley wey miggimu rin seini.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: nasulat wey ke neyimu ne ula-ula te minsan nekey
a drawing of what something will look like, and what parts it will have.
He showed me the design for the house that he wanted to build.
Impakita rin ka neyimu rin ne ula-ula te baley ne igkeupii rin ne eggimuwen.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: pegkeyimu
MS: pegkeyimu
the way that something looks and the different parts it has.
The design of that bridge is not very attractive, but it is strong.
Ka pegkeyimu te sika ne teytey kenè amana ne meupiya, piru malig-en.
 
n-ct.
 
4.
Def.: ilinganan; dayan-dayan
MS: ilinganan; dayan-dayan
a pattern on something such as clothes.
That is a very pretty design on her skirt.
Amana sika ne meupiya ne dayan-dayan te saya rin.
 
n-ct.
 
5.
Def.: igkeupii ne egkuen
MS: igkeupii ne egkuen
Your design when you do something is the thing that you want to achieve.
His design in walking to the river was to see the horse.
Ka igkeupii rin ne egkuen te pegbayè diyè te weyig iyan ka pegtengteng te kuddè.
