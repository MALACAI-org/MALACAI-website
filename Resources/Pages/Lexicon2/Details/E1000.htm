elder
/el-der/
 
n-ct.
Def.: buyag; igbuyag
MS: buyag; igbuyag
an older person in a group who has some authority and responsibility to lead.
 
adj.
 
1.
Def.: buyag-buyag; kakey; kakey-kakey
MS: buyag-buyag; kakey; kakey-kakey
You use elder before 'sister' and 'brother' if they are older than you.
I live with my elder sister.
Mig-ugpa a diyè te kakey ne atebey ku.
