show
/shoh/
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: egpakita
MS: egpakita
If you show something to someone, or show someone something, you cause them to look at it.
She showed me her new T-shirt.
 
vi.
 
2.
Def.: egkakita
MS: egkakita
If something shows (e.g., your feeling, or a part of your body), people can see it.
She was very sad, and it showed in her eyes.
Her toes show when she wears those shoes.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: egpakakita; egpakanengneng
MS: egpakakita; egpakanengneng
If someone or something shows something, people can see or know that thing.
She showed no emotion when I told her the news.
Her open shoes showed her toes.
Research shows that too much salt is bad for blood pressure.
 
n-ct.
 
4.
Def.: egkalanglanganan ne egkakita wey egkarineg
a time of entertainment which you watch or listen to (e.g. a variety concert, a TV or radio program).
Irreg. v.  showed [shohd], showed/fb:shown [shohn]).
