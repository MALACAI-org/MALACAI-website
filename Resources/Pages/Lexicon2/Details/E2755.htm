sound2
 
adj.
 
1.
Def.: warè nareeti; warè prublima
MS: warè nareeti; warè prublima
If something is sound, it is not broken or damaged, and it has no problems.
They checked the building after the landslide, and it was still sound.
My horse had a sore leg, but now the leg is sound.
She is very old, but her mind is still sound.
 
adj.
 
2.
Def.: maheteng wey matagseb
MS: maheteng wey matagseb
If thinking is sound, it is good and wise.
Her reasons for her decision were sound.
 
adj.
 
3.
Def.: eleg iya
MS: eleg iya; kumplitu
kumplitu
If something is sound, it is very complete (e.g., sound sleep)
Our team was much stronger than the other team, so we won a sound victory.
Ka grupu ney amana ne makeseg du te duma ne grupu, sika ka migdeeg key iya te eleg ne pegdeeg.
She was very tired, so she fell into a sound sleep.
Source: GSL0376-0400
