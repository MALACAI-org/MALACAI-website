dominate
/dom-ə-nate/
 
vi, vt.
 
1.
Def.: maresen wey egpandeeg te minsan nekey
MS: egpandeeg
If someone or something dominates, they are the most powerful or successful person or thing.
Our basketball team has won all our games, so we've dominated the competition.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egbeye-beyè
MS: egbeye-beyè
If you dominate someone, you control them in some way.
She is not popular, because she always dominates people.
Kenè ne mabantug sikandin, su layun din egbeybeyei ka me etew.
Source: AWL3
