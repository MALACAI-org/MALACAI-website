side
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: kilid
MS: kilid
an area between the top and bottom of something such as a house or a door. Or the part of the body under the arm, between the shoulder and legs.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: lenged
MS: lenged
a part of something which is not in the middle (e.g., the right side, the north side).
She lives on the south side of the village.
Mig-ugpè sikandin te lenged te balabahan diyè te bariyu.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: ilis
MS: ilis
the area which is next to something (e.g., the side of the road)
 
n-ct.
 
4.
Def.: sabeka ne punduk te daruwa ne manggalaway wey ke egpeehetey
one of the two groups playing a game or fighting.
My son was on the winning side in the basketball game today.
Ka anak ku ne lukes diyè kuntee sikandin te egdeeg ne punduk te egpambubula.
 
n-ct.
 
5.
Def.: lein ne baad
MS: lein ne baad
one of the parts of a person or situation that is different from other parts
It is good to live near water, but there is also a bad side: floods can damage your house and garden.
Meupiya ne eg-ugpè diyè te marani te weyig, piru due degma lein ne baad ne mareet: ke egbaggiyu egkareetan degma ka baley wey me pinamula ne gulay.
Source: GSL0201-0225
