stand
/stæhnd/
 
vi.
 
1.
Def.: egsasindeg
MS: egsasindeg
If a person or animal stands, or stands up, they move from a sitting or lying position to an upright position.
He stood up when I called him.
 
vi.
 
2.
Def.: nakasasindeg (meyitenged te etew wey ke mananap)
MS: nakasasindeg
If a person or animal is standing, they are upright, and their legs are straight.
There are no chairs, so he is standing.
 
vi.
 
3.
Def.: nakatahù; nakasasindeg (meyitenged te minsan nekey)
MS: nakatahù; nakasasindeg
If something stands somewhere, it is in that place.
The house stands near the river.
Ka baley nakasasindeg te ilis te pahamayan ne weyig.
 
vt.
 
4.
Def.: egpasasindeg; egtahù
MS: egpasasindeg; egtahù
If you stand something somewhere, you put it in that place.
He stood the chair near the window.
 
vi.
 
5.
Def.: egkasuman-suman
MS: egkasuman-suman; baruganan
baruganan
If you stand somewhere on a particular topic, that is what you think about it.
I don't agree with that idea. Where do you stand on it?
 
n-ct.
 
6.
Def.: mapahetpet ne peglalag te nasuman-suman wey warè pegbalewbalew.
an action of saying strongly what you think, and not changing your idea (take a stand, make a stand).
I took a stand and told him that I would not tell lies for him.
 
vt.
 
7.
Def.: egpakahaha; egpakaantus
MS: egpakahaha; egpakaantus
If you can stand something difficult or painful, you can continue to live with it.
I can't stand that noise! Please stop it!
Kena a egpakahaha te kahulub! Palihug sanggela niyu!
 
n-ct.
 
8.
Def.: deisek ne pinnuuwan
MS: pinnuuwan ne deisek
a small stall where you can buy something (e.g. food)
an ice-cream stand.
 
n-ct.
 
9.
Def.: papinnuuwan
MS: papinnuuwan
something which you can put things on (e.g., a TV stand).
 
n-ct.
 
10.
Def.: maluag ne egkeugpaan diyè te galawanan ne egpaminuuwan.
the place at a large sports ground where people can sit.
The stand can hold 20,000 people.
Irreg.  stood, stood.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0176-0200
