connect
/ko-nekt/
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: egpegsumpul
MS: egpegsumpul
If something connects two other things, it is between those two things, and joins them.
This pipe connects the spring with the tank.
This road connects my sitio and your sitio.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egpegsumpul
MS: egpegsumpul
If you connect one thing to another thing, you join them together.
He connected the spring to the tank with a pipe.
He connected the door to the wall.
 
vi.
 
3.
Def.: egpakasumpul
MS: egpakasumpul
If one thing connects to another thing, those two things meet and stay together.
This wire connects to that box.
 
vt.
 
4.
Def.: egsuman-suman ne due kalabetan te tagsè sabeka kandan
If you connect two things or people in your mind, you think that there is a relationship between them.
I never connected him with the crime, but now I know that he did it.
Source: GSL1451-1475
