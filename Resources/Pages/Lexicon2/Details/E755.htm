culture
/kol-cher/
 
n-uc, n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: menà; kultura
MS: menà; kultura
the ways of life of a tribe or nation of people, including things such as music, attitudes and food.
Note: is it ok to use 'tribe' - do MS people feel OK about this word?
In traditional Matigsalug culture, old people are respected.
 
n-uc.
 
2.
Def.: himuwen ne due kalabetan te ulahingen wey duma pad
activities related to things such as art, music and literature.
The art gallery in that city is free, so everyone can experience some culture.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: pamaahi wey pegbebatasan te etew diyè te grupu
The culture of an organization or group is the way that people in the group behave and feel.
Our family has a culture of respect for others.
Sikanami se taltalaanak due pegbebatasan ne egtahud te duma.
 
n-ct.
 
4.
Def.: kahew ne in-uyag
MS: kahew ne in-uyag
a group of bacteria or cells which somebody is growing (usually for science, or for food such as yoghurt).
This yoghurt has three different good bacteria cultures.
 
 
cultural
/kol-chə-rəl/
 
adj.
Def.: 1 due kalabetan te menà wey kultura; 2 due kalabetan te ulahingen wey duma pad; 3 due kalabetan te pegbebatasan te etew diyè te grupu
related to culture
She would love to go to cultural events such as concerts, but she doesn't have enough money.
