degree
/də-gree/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: kamenu karakel wey ke kamenu kakeseg
MS: kamenu karakel; kamenu kakeseg
The degree of something is how much or how strong it is.
This job requires a high degree of skill and commitment.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: baad te timpu
MS: baad te timpu
a unit of temperature. The symbol is °. We usually measure temperature either in degrees Celcius or degrees Fahrenheit.
30° (degrees) celcius
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: baad te kaluahan te egkasiku
MS: baad te kaluahan te egkasiku
a unit of measurement for angles. The symbol is °. There are 360° (degrees) in a circle.
 
n-ct.
 
4.
Def.: neimpusan ne kursu te pegkeimpus te pegiskuyla diyè te unibirsidad
a qualification you receive after finishing study at a university.
I have two degrees: a Bachelor of Science and a Master of Education.
 
n-ct.
 
5.
Def.: ka baad ne kaluahan te natahuan te minsan nekey
a unit of measurement of the position of a place in the world, compared with the equator (north-south) and a place in England called Greenwich (east-west).
The location of the Philippines is 13° 00 N, 122° 00 E [Source: www.mapsofworld.com/lat long/philippines-lat-long.html]
Source: GSL0701-0725
