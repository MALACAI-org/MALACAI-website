fit1
 
vi, vt.
 
1.
Def.: eleg ka kaluag (kenè ne dakel wey kenè ne deisek)
MS: eleg ka kaluag
If something fits, it is the right size, not too big and not too small.
These tsinillas don't fit me because they are too big.
Ka seini ne sinilas kenè ne eleg keddì su amana ne dakel.
 
vt, v-recip.
 
2.
Def.: egpekegtupù; egpekeg-eleg
MS: egpekegtupù; egpekeg-eleg
If something fits another thing, the two things are good together.
His story didn't fit the facts, so I didn't believe him.
Ka guhuren din warè nekegtupù te kamalehetan, sika ka kena a egpalintutuu kandin.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: 1 pegkeeleg; 2 pegpekegtupù; pegpekeg-eleg
MS: pegkeeleg; pegpekegtupù; pegpekeg-eleg
how well something fits
Note: (sense 1, 2).
This shirt is not a good fit, because it is too big.
That job is a good fit for you.
 
vt.
 
4.
Def.: egpakepkep; egparekket; egtahù (diyè te lein ne kasangkapan)
MS: egpakepkep; egparekket; egtahù
If you fit something somewhere, you attach it to that thing.
He fitted a new lock on the door.
Intahù sikandin te iyam ne paggul te lekeb.
 
adj.
 
5.
Def.: eleg pad ne egkahamit te nateil ne igkeupii wey ke meupiya pad te sika ne butang.
If something is fit for a particular purpose, it is good enough to use for that purpose. If someone is fit to do something, they are good enough to do that thing.
That food is old, and is not fit to eat.
Ka sika ne pegkeen naluhey e, wey kenad ne meupiya ne egkeenen.
He is sick, and is not fit to travel.
 
adj.
 
6.
Def.: manekal wey meupiya se peglelawa
MS: manekal wey meupiya se peglelawa
If you are fit, you are strong and healthy, because you exercise.
I go jogging to keep fit.
Irreg. v.  fit/fb:fitted, fit/fb:fitted.
