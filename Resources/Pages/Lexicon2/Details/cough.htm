cough
/koff/
 
vi.
Def.: egbuween
MS: egbuween
If you cough, you push air out of your mouth in a short, noisy action.
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: pegpabubuwè
MS: pegpabubuwè
a single action of coughing (see sense 1)
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: prublima te lawa ne egpakapabuwè te amana; buwè
MS: buwè
a problem with your body which causes you to cough a lot.
I have a bad cough, but I feel OK.
Due mareet amana ne buwè ku, piru hendue e te peggeram-geram ku ne meupiye e.
Sem.Dom.: GSL2051-2075
Status: ready
