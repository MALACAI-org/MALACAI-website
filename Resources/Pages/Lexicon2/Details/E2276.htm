progress
/prə-gress (v), prog-ress (n)/
 
vi.
 
1.
Def.: egmeupiya te peggimu wey ke egpekeyimu te subla pad
If you progress in doing something, you become better at doing it, or you do more of it.
My son started learning to read last year, and he is progressing fast.
 
vi.
 
2.
Def.: egkameupiya wey ke egpalundaniyan e te pegkapenga
If something progresses, it gets better or gets closer to being finished.
Work on the new road is progressing fast, and we hope it will be finished by December.
Ka pegtrabahu te iyam ne kelesadda amana ne meiyal, wey eglaum key ne egkeimpusan seini te me Disimbri.
 
n-uc.
 
3.
Def.: 1 pegmeupiya te peggimu wey ke pegkeyimu te subla pad; 2 pegkameupiya wey ke pegpalundaniyan te pegkapenga
the action of getting better or getting closer to finishing (make progress).
Note: sense 1,2
We are making good progress in our work, and we should finish by next week.
My teacher is happy with my progress. carefully.
 
vi.
 
4.
Def.: egpabulus-bulus ne egkeyitabù; egkasasangan
MS: egpabulus-bulus ne egkeyitabù; egkasasangan
If an event progresses it continues to happen.
Many people arrived as the meeting progressed.
Masalig ka me etew ne migpanginguma te ka egkasasangan ka miting.
Source: GSL0751-0775
