arrow
/æ-roh/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: tunud; busug
MS: tunud; busug
a long, thin weapon which is shot from a bow. One end is sharp, and the other end usually has feathers.
He shot the dangerous animal with an arrow.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: aru; egtinuru
MS: aru; egtinuru
a shape (→) that you draw to point at something or to organize information.
Source: GSL1901-1925
