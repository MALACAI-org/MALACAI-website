consist
/kon-sist/
 
vi.
 
1.
Def.: neyimu puun te minsan nekey
MS: neyimu puun te
If something consists of other things, it is made of those things.
This soup consists of water, fish, cabbage and salt.
Seini ne sabew neyimu puun te weyig, ngalap te weyig, gulay wey asin.
 
vi.
 
2.
Def.: iyan ka
MS: iyan ka
If something consists in another thing, the two things are the same.
Her problem consisted in eating too much sugar.
Ka prublima rin iyan ka eg-am-amana egkeen te asukal.
