impact
/im-pækt (n), im-pækt (v)/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: kakeseg te sabeka ne butang ne egpakasuhat diyè te lein ne butang.
the action or force of one thing hitting another thing.
In the accident, the impact of the car caused the tree to fall down.
Te aksidinti, ka kakeseg te terak ka nakapapeled te kayu.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: igkabehey wey egkalibed ne eggeramen ne egpakabalew-balew puun te minsan nekey.
The impact of something on a person or situation is the way that it changes or affects them.
That book had a big impact on me, and changed my thinking.
Ka sika ne libru dakel ka ingkabehey keddì, wey sika ka nakapabalew-balew te suman-suman ku.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: egpakabehey te meupiya wey ke mareet ne kapabulusan
MS: egpakaapiktu
egpakaapiktu
If something impacts a person or situation, it changes or affects them in some way.
Your decision will impact a lot of people, so be very careful about what you decide.
Ka puhawang nu egpakapabalew-balew te katangenan, sika ka ayad-ayad ka te pegbehey te puhawang.
