live
/liv (v); layiv (adj)/
 
vi.
 
1.
Def.: eg-ugpè
MS: eg-ugpè
If you live in a house, town, or country, that is your home.
 
vi.
 
2.
Def.: egkeuyag
MS: egkeuyag
If you live, you have life, and are not dead.
It was a very bad accident, but I think he will live.
Dereeti seeyè ne pangitabù, piru te suman-suman ku egkeuyag ded sikandin.
My grandmother lived until she was 90.
Neuyag ka inè ku ne buyag taman te siyew ne pulù ka idad din.
 
adj.
 
3.
Def.: neuyag pad
MS: neuyag pad
A live animal or plant is not dead.
They sell live pigs, so you need to kill the pigs yourself.
Egpamelegyè sikandan te neuyag pad ne babuy, sika ka keilangan ne sikeykew pad ka eggimatey.
 
adj, adv.
 
4.
Def.: egkakita wey egkarineg (iling te tibi wey radyu) te timpu te pegpakita wey pegparineg.
A live television or radio program is heard on the TV or radio at the same time that it is made. In a live performance, you watch a real person or group performing, not a recording.
The Mangulahing band is going to perform live in our village.
Ka Mangulahing ne grupu egpakita te katuenan ne egkakita ta diyè te bariyu ney.
 
adj.
 
5.
Def.: due kurinti
MS: due kurinti
A live wire has electric current in it.
 
adj.
 
6.
Def.: guhuren ne masalig ka me puhawang puun te me etew wey ke masalig ka egkeupian ne eglalahen seini.
A live topic is something that a lot of people have opinions about or want to talk about.
Source: GSL0201-0225
