such
 
adv.
 
1.
Def.: amana
MS: amana
You use such to describe something more strongly.
This room is such a mess.
Amana ne nahuseng ka seini ne sinabeng.
I had such a bad day.
Amana ne mareet ka aldew ku.
 
det.
 
2.
Def.: iling (te lalag ne newun-a)
MS: iling
something like that
I was surprised when I saw it, because I had never seen such a thing before.
Neinu-inu a te nakita ku seeyè, su warè e pad nakakita te iling dutu ne kasangkapan ligkat pad dengan.
Source: determiners; GSL0051-0075
 
 
such as
Def.: iling te
MS: iling te
for example.
I love yellow vegetables such as corn.
Egkeupian a te makalawag ne me gulay iling te aheley.
Status: ready
