transition
/træhn-zish-ən/
 
n-ct.
Def.: pegbalew-balew
MS: pegbalew-balew
the process of changing from one state or situation to another (make a transition).
When we moved to a different town, the transition was difficult for our children.
Te peggalin ney te lein ne lungsud, ka pegbalew-balew te me anak ney amana ne malised.
We made a transition from eating white rice to eating red rice.
Sem.Dom.: AWL5
Status: ready
