there
/thair/
 
pron.
 
1.
Def.: due
MS: due
You use there is or there are to say that something exists.
There is a mango tree near my house.
Due lawa te mangga diyè te dulug te baley ku.
 
adv, pron.
 
2.
Def.: seeyè te (meyitenged te lugar)
MS: seeyè te
You use there to talk about another place, not where you you now.
Is that your bike there near the house?
Bayik nu ka seeyè te dulug te baley?
 
adv, pron.
 
3.
Def.: duen te pad; kayi te pad (meyitenged te nateil ne uras te himuwen)
MS: duen te pad; kayi te pad
You use there to to talk about a certain time in an activity.
Let's stop the lesson there, and we'll continue tomorrow.
Duen te pad egtamana ka egpangasaken ta, wey kaaselem kid egpabulus.
Sem.Dom.: pronouns; adverbs; GSL0026-0050
Status: ready
