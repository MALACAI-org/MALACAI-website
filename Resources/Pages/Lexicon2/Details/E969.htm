dynamics
/dayi-næm-iks/
 
n-pl.
 
1.
Def.: me pamaahi ne ka tagsè sabeka diyè te grupu ne due kalabetan
The dynamics in a group are the way that the people in the group relate to one another.
The dynamics of our group are interesting, because there are some very different people in the group.
Ka kalabetan te tagsè sabeka te grupu ta su makabebagget, su due me duma ta ne iyam te grupu ta.
 
n-pl.
 
2.
Def.: ka pegpalin te kanekal wey pegkameyimulung te dahing
In music, the dynamics are the changes in loudness and softness of the sounds.
The song was boring because there were no dynamics; it was all loud and strong.
Ka ulahingen warè keupiya su warè pegpalin te kanekal wey ke pegpeyimulung; derahingi wey amanan kekesehi.
Source: plural nouns; AWL7
