study
/stad-ee/
 
vi, vt.
 
1.
Def.: eg-iskuyla
MS: eg-iskuyla
If you study, you are a student, and learn about a particular subject or subjects
He is studying at college this year.
 
vi, vt.
 
2.
Def.: egbasa eyew te pegbebasbas
MS: egbasa eyew te pegbebasbas
If you study, you read the things that you need to learn so that you will know or remember them.
I have to study, because I have exams next week.
 
n-uc.
 
3.
Def.: 1 peg-iskuyla; 2 pegbasa eyew te pegbebasbas
MS: peg-iskuyla; pegbasa eyew te pegbebasbas
the action of learning things as a student (do study).
Study is hard work.
I need to do some study, as I have exams next week.
 
vt.
 
4.
Def.: egnengnengen
MS: egnengnengen
If you study a particular topic, you research it to learn more.
He is studying herbal medicines of this area.
 
n-ct.
 
5.
Def.: himuwen ne egnengneng ka te minsan nekey
a project in which you research something (do a study).
He is doing a study on herbal medicines of this area.
 
vt.
 
6.
Def.: egpitawen eg-ay-ayari
MS: egpitawen eg-ay-ayari
If you study something, you look at it very carefully.
The doctor studied my foot carefully and said that it was broken.
Source: GSL0226-0250
