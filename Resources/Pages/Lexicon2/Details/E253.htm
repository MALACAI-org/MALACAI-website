available
/a-vay-lə-bəl/
 
adj.
 
1.
Def.: due layun
MS: due layun
If something is available (e.g., in shops), people can get it.
Bread is available in all the shops.
Ka paan [pa͡an] due layun te langun ne tindaan [tinda͡an].
 
adj.
 
2.
Def.: egkaayun su warè eggimuwen
MS: egkaayun
If a person is available, they can talk to you, because they are not busy.
I'm sorry, he's not available at the moment. Can he call you back later?
Source: AWL1
 
 
availability
/a-vay-lə-bill-ə-tee/
 
n-uc.
Def.: 1 due layun; 2 pegkaayun puun te warè eggimuwen
MS: due layun; pegkaayun puun te warè eggimuwen
the state of being available.
What's your availability: are you free every day?
We will buy some mangoes, depending on availability.
