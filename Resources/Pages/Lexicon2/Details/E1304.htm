generation
/djen-ə-ray-shən/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: langun ne me etew ne nekeg-iling-iling se kabuyahan
all of the people who are a similar age.
That kind of music is popular with the younger generation.
Sika ne klasiyan te ulahingen mabantug te kuntee ne me batè pad ne kabuhalan.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: klasiyan te kabuyahan
MS: klasiyan te kabuyahan
One step in the cycle of people growing up and having children. In humans, one step is about 30 years.
We have three generations living in our house: my grandmother, my parents and me.
Due tatelu ne klasiyan te kabuyahan ne nangugpè te baley ney: ka inè te apu ku, ka amey wey iney ku, wey sikeddì.
 
n-uc.
 
3.
Def.: pegpekeyimu
MS: pegpekeyimu
the action of producing something.
