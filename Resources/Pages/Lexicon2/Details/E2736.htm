somebody
/sam-bod-ee/
 
pron.
Def.: senge etew
MS: senge etew
You use somebody about a person if you don't know or don't say exactly who they are.
I met somebody yesterday, but I can't remember her name.
Nakalambag a te senge etew gabii, piru kenè kud egkasuman-suman ka ngaran din.
Source: pronouns; GSL1226-1251
