can1
/kæhn, kən/
 
v-aux.
Def.: egpaka-; egpeke-; egpekeyimu; egkaayun; egkateu
MS: egpeke-; egpekeyimu; egkaayun; egkateu
If someone or something can do something, it is possible for them to do it, because they have the ability or permission to do it.
My mother said that I can go with you.
Ka iney ku miglalag te egkaayun ne egpakaruma a keykew.
I can swim, because my father taught me.
Egpakalumbuk a, su impanulù a te amey ku.
Irreg. v.  could, been able.
Source: auxiliary verbs; GSL0026-0050
