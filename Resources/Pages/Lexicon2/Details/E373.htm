bow2
/bow/
 
n-ct.
Def.: hun-aan [hun-a͡an] ne baad te bangka
MS: hun-aan [hun-a͡an] ne baad te bangka
The bow of a boat is the front of it.
She sat in the bow of the boat so that she could see clearly.
Migpinnuu sikandin diyè te hun-aan [hun-a͡an] ne baad te bangka eyew egpakita sikandin.
Source: GSL1751-1775
Status: nc3
