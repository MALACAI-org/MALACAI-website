conscious
/kon-shəss/
 
adj.
 
1.
Def.: sinuman-suman (meyitenged te himu wey ke weil)
MS: sinuman-suman
A conscious action or thing is one that you know about and think about.
She made a conscious decision to do that; it was no accident.
Nasakat sikandin ka miggimu te sika ne suman-suman; kenè ne tinuyuan dè sika.
 
adj.
 
2.
Def.: egpakataha
MS: egpakataha
If you are conscious of something, or are conscious that something is the case, you know about it.
He was not conscious that there was a snake in his bed.
Warè nakataha sikandin te duen bes uled diyè te lantawan din.
 
adj.
 
3.
Def.: due kalintahaan [kalintaha͡an]
MS: due kalintahaan
If you are conscious, you are fully awake and your mind is working.
When we sleep we are not conscious.
Ke eglipereng ki warè kalintahaan [kalintaha͡an] ta.
Source: GSL0801-0825
