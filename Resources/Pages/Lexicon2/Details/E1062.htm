eternal
/i-ter-nəl/
 
adj.
 
1.
Def.: egpabulus-bulus te warè egtamanan
MS: egpabulus-bulus te warè egtamanan
If something is eternal, it continues forever.
 
adj.
 
2.
Def.: due layun te warè bunsuranan wey warè egtamanan
When we say that God is eternal, we mean that he has always existed, and will always exist forever.
Source: keyterms
