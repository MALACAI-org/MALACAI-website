agree
/a-gree/
 
vi, v-recip, vt(+prep).
 
1.
Def.: egkabennalan; nekegsinabut; nasabeka se suman-suman; nekeg-uyun; egpekegbennaley; egkasabeka ka suman-suman; egpekegsinabut; egpekeg-uyun
MS: egkabennalan; nekegsinabut; nasabeka se suman-suman; nekeg-uyun; egpekegbennaley; egkasabeka; egpekegsinabut; egpekeg-uyun
If you agree with someone, you both have the same thinking about something.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egparuma-ruma; egpalintutuu; eg-uyun
MS: egparuma-ruma; egpalintutuu; eg-uyun
If you agree to do something, you say that you will do it when someone asks you.
She asked me for help, and I agreed to help her.
Migpabulig sikandin keddì, wey migpalintutuu a ne egbulig kandin.
 
v-recip, vi.
 
3.
Def.: egpekeg-iling
MS: egpekeg-iling
If two things agree with each other, they fit together or are similar.
I believe them, because his story agrees with hers.
I believe them, because their stories agree.
You can't say 'He want', because the pronoun and the verb don't agree.
Source: GSL0401-0425
