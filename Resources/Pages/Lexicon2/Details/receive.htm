receive
/ri-seev/
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: egpakakuwa te igbehey; egpakarawat te igbehey
MS: egpakakuwa te igbehey; egpakarawat
If you receive something, someone gives or sends it to you.
I received a Christmas gift from my godfather.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: eggeram wey ke eglalag te nateil ne pamaahi tenged te minsan nekey; egpakarawat
MS: egpakarawat
If you receive something in a particular way, you feel or talk about it that way.
She didn't receive the news very well; in fact, she was very upset.
Warè din narawat ka balita; kamalehetan, neepes sikandin.
His new movie has been very well-received by the public.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: egdawat te etew
MS: egdawat te etew
If you receive a person, you welcome them to a place.
Her job as receptionist was to receive visitors and clients.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0351-0375
Status: ready
