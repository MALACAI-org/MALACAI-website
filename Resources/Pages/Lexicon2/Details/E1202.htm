fire1
 
n-uc, n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: hapuy
MS: hapuy
They lost all their things in the fire.
Natarinan sikandan te langun ne kasangkapan dan te hapuy.
The fire destroyed their corn crop.
Ka hapuy nakareet te ahelayan dan.
We use fire to clear their fields.
She put the pot of rice on the fire.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egpabetu te sinapang
MS: egpabetu te sinapang
If you fire a gun at something, you shoot.
He fired at the animal and it fell to the ground, dead.
 
n-uc.
 
3.
Def.: pegpabetu te sinapang
MS: pegpabetu te sinapang
Fire is the shooting of guns.
He heard the sound of gunfire close by.
Our commander told us to hold fire, so we stopped shooting.
 
vt.
 
4.
Def.: eg-initen ne puun te siramiks eyew egpakehalen
If you fire something made of ceramics, you bake it in a very hot fire to make it hard.
Source: GSL0401-0425
