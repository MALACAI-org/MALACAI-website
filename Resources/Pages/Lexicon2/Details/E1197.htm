fine1
 
adj, adv.
 
1.
Def.: malintek
MS: malintek
If something such as hair, sand or cloth is fine, the individual pieces are very thin or small
The baby's hair is very fine and soft.
She used very fine threads to weave the cloth, so the cloth was very light, and the pattern was very clear.
If you make pansit, it's good to chop the carrots into fine pieces.
 
adj.
 
2.
Def.: meupiya se klasiyan
MS: meupiya se klasiyan
Something fine is of very good quality.
That shop makes very fine chairs, but they are expensive.
 
adj, adv.
 
3.
Def.: warè prublima
MS: warè prublima
If someone or something is fine, there is not a problem. If it is fine to do something, there is no problem if you do it.
Your X-ray looks fine, so I think no bones are broken.
It's fine to wear your shoes in this part of the house.
I'm fine, thanks, and how are you?
Source: GSL0551-0575
