space
 
n-ct, n-uc.
 
1.
Def.: warè tahù; warè nekeugpè
MS: warè tahù; warè nekeugpè; bakanti
bakanti
a place which doesn't have anything there.
There was a space next to her at the table, so I sat there.
Due warè migpinnuu diyè te kasinundulan din ne lamisa, sika ka diya a migpinnuu.
 
n-uc.
 
2.
Def.: lihawangan te kalibutan
MS: lihawangan te kalibutan
the very big area outside the earth, which contains stars, other planets, etc.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0526-0550
