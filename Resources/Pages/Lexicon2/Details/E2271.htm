production
/pro-dak-shən/
 
n-uc.
 
1.
Def.: pegkeyimu
MS: pegkeyimu
the action of one thing creating another thing.
This carabao's milk production is very good.
Meupiya amana ne pegkeyimu ka seini ne gatas te kalabew.
 
n-uc.
 
2.
Def.: pegpangimu wey ke pegpatubù
MS: pegpangimu; pegpatubù
the action of making or growing something.
This city has a lot of car production.
Seini ne siyudad due masalig ne pegpangimu te terak.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: peglibulung eyew te pegpangimu iling te salida wey ke CD
the action of organizing everying to make something such as a movie or CD.
 
n-ct.
 
4.
Def.: igpakita ne guhuren wey ke galew
MS: igpakita ne guhuren wey ke galew; drama
drama
Something such as a play, which actors perform in a theater.
I saw a production of Shakespeare's 'Romeo and Juliet'.
Source: GSL0676-0700
