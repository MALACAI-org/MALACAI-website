warm
/wawm/
 
adj.
 
1.
Def.: bayew meinit
MS: bayew meinit
If something is warm, it is a bit hot, but not very hot.
 
vi.
 
2.
Def.: egmeinit
MS: egmeinit
If something warms up, it becomes warmer.
The weather was cold this morning, but the day is warming up now.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: eg-init; eg-iinit; egpameinit; eg-dalap; egderalap
MS: eg-init; eg-iinit; egpameinit; egdalap; egderalap
If you warm something, or warm something up, you cause it to become warmer.
She sat close to the fire and warmed her hands.
 
adj.
 
4.
Def.: maal-alukuy
MS: maal-alukuy
If someone is warm, they are friendly.
She said hello to me with a warm smile.
They gave me a warm welcome.
Source: GSL0951-0975
 
 
warmth
/wormth/
 
n-uc.
Def.: 1 pegkabayew menit; 4 pegkamaal-alukuy
MS: pegkabayew menit; pegkamaal-alukuy
the state of being warm.
