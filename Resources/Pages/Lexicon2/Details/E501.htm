church
/cherch/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: ka me etew ne migmalintutuu ki Hisus
MS: migmalintutuu ki Hisus, ka me etew ne
all the true Christians in the world
The church is waiting for Jesus to return.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: nateil ne punduk te migmalintutuu
MS: punduk te migmalintutuu
a particular group of Christians who meet together.
 
n-uc.
 
3.
Def.: egsimba
MS: egsimba
a Christian meeting (usually on Sunday) which has activities such as singing, prayer and preaching.
We visited my aunt after church.
 
n-ct.
 
4.
Def.: simbaan [simba͡an]
MS: simbaan
a building where Christians meet
There is a church on the hill.
Source: GSL0226-0250; keyterms
