now
 
adv.
Def.: kuntee
MS: kuntee
this moment or time
We are leaving now.
Egkuwa kid e kuntee.
He sold his horse, and now he had enough money to buy a carabao.
Our ancestors were hunters, but now we have farms.
We made a tank to collect water, so now we don't have to carry so much water.
Now, what were we talking about?
Note: possible meanings of 'now' - Don't include in student dictinary, but included for future reference:
Note: You use now to talk about this moment, or a time very soon (Kuntee te egkatagtaheed)
Note: When you are talking about the past, you use now to talk about a moment in the past. ne kuntee (meyitenged te nanlihad e)
Note: You use now to talk about the present era. piru kuntee (meyitenged te eglalag te dengan wey kuntee)
Note: You use now to say that something is different from before, because of some action. egamiten te eglalag te kaleinan te dengan peehendiyè te kuntee
Note: You use now or now that to mean 'because something is true that wasn't true before'. su kuntee (meyitenged egkamalehet ka warè namalehet dengan)
Note: You use now when you want to speak about a new topic, or to go back to a topic. na kuntee (meyitenged te eglalag ka te iyam ne guhuren)
