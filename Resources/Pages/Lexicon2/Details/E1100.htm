exclusive
/iks-kloo-siv/
 
adj.
 
1.
Def.: para rè te nateil ne etew
MS: para rè te nateil ne etew
Something exclusive is only for a certain person or people.
This fishpond is for my family's exclusive use, not for everyone in the sitio.
Seini ne uyahanan te ngalap te weyig para rè te pamilya ku, kenè ne para te langun ne me etew te sityu.
 
adj.
 
2.
Def.: egpakahamit ka meyaman wey ke mabantug ne me etew (meyitenged te lugar wey ke ugpaanan)
An exclusive place is used only by rich or famous people (e.g., an exclusive hotel).
 
adj.
 
3.
Def.: egkateil dè ka magasin wey ke prugrama te tibi ne egkatahuan wey ke egkakitaan
If a news story or interview in a magazine or on TV is exclusive, it is only in that particular magazine or TV program.
Tomorrow night, we will have an exclusive interview with the president.
Kaaselem te marusilem, due peg-inse-insè ney te prisidinti te sikanami rè.
