amend
/a-mend/
 
vt.
Def.: egbalbalawan
MS: egbalbalawan
If you amend something such as a document or law, you change it.
They amended the rules so that younger children could join.
Migbalbalawan dan ka balaud eyew ka me batè egpakaapil.
Sem.Dom.: AWL5
 
 
amendment
 
n-ct.
Def.: pegbalew-balew
MS: pegbalew-balew
a change to something such as a law (make an amendment to something).
They made some important amendments to the club rules.
