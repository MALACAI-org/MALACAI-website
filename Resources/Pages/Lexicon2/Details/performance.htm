performance
/per-form-ənss/
 
n-uc.
 
1.
Def.: peggimu te minsan nekey
MS: peggimu te minsan nekey
the action of doing something.
He is very weak, so the performance of daily jobs is difficult for him.
Amana sikandin mahuyè, su ka peggimu te inaldew-aldew ne trabahu rin malised para kandin.
 
n-uc.
 
2.
Def.: kamenu keupiya ka peggimu diyè te minsan nekey
The performance of a person or thing is how well they do something.
That motorbike has very good performance.
Amana ne meupiya sika ne mutur (meyitenged te himu).
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: pegpapitew te minsan nekey
MS: pegpapitew te minsan nekey
an action of doing something such as a dance in front of a group of people.
Everyone liked the children's dance performance.
Ka tagsè sabeka neupian te pegpapitew te sayew te me batè.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0676-0700
Status: ready
