resolve
/ri-zolv/
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: egpehet-pehet
MS: egpehetpehet
If you resolve to do something, you decide strongly that you are going to do it.
After her bad experience, she resolved never to travel alone again.
Impus te mareet ne nabaybayaan din, migpehet-pehet sikandin ne kenad eg-abey eggipanew ne egsabsabeka.
 
n-uc.
 
2.
Def.: mapahetpet ne suman-suman te peggimu te minsan nekey minsan malised.
a strong attitude that you will continue to do something even if it is difficult.
It took a lot of resolve for her to finish the race, because she was in pain.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: eg-ayad-ayad; egsulbad
MS: eg-ayad-ayad; egsulbad
If you resolve a problem, you fix it.
 
vt.
 
4.
Def.: egkapeeneng-eneng (meyitenged te apulanan wey ehetayey)
MS: egkapeeneng-eneng
If people resolve an argument, they stop arguing or fighting.
The couple have resolved their differences, and are not going to separate.
Napeeneng-eneng ka gubut te alunggun, wey kenad egpasuwayey sikandan.
Sem.Dom.: AWL4
Status: ready
