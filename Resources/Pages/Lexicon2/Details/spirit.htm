spirit2
/spi-rət/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: panisingan
MS: panisingan
a being without a physical body. For example, a busew is a bad spirit.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: panisingan; geyinawa
MS: panisingan; geyinawa; gimukud
the spirit of a person is the part of them that is not physical. It is the part of them which makes them alive.
Sem.Dom.: keyterms; GSL0351-0375
 
 
spiritual
/spi-ri-choo-wəl/
 
adj.
Def.: due kalabetan te Manama wey me panisingan
related to things such as God and spirits, not to the physical world.
Status: ready
