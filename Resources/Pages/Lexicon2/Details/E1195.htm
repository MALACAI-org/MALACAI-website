finance
/fie-nænss/
 
n-uc.
 
1.
Def.: trabahu ne pegdumalè te seleppì, peutang wey te pegpasambey
the work of managing money, credit and investment.
He studied finance and then got a job in a bank.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egpamayad
MS: egpamayad
If you finance something, you give the money for it.
My uncle is financing my college study.
Ka anggam ku iyan egpamayad te peg-iskuyla ku te kulihiyu.
Source: AWL1
 
 
finances
/fie-nænss-əz/
 
n-pl.
Def.: seleppì
MS: seleppì
the money that a person or company has.
Our family's finances are quite low, so it's not easy to pay for everything.
Ka seleppì te pamilya ney kuntee amana ne deisek, sika ka malised ne egpamayad te langun-langun.
Source: plural nouns
 
 
financial
/fi-næhn-shəl/
 
adj.
Def.: due kalabetan te seleppì
MS: seleppì, due kalabetan te
Something financial is related to money, credit and investment.
financial problems
prublima te seleppì
That company has a lot of financial problems at the moment.
