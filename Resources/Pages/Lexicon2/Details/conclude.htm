conclude
/kong-klood/
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: eggukum
MS: eggukum
If you conclude that something is the case, you decide it after you think about it.
He looked at all the places, and concluded that the hill was the best place for his garden.
Migtengtengan din ka langun ne lugar, wey miggukum sikandin te ka miglabuntud ka eleg ne egkatahuan te pinamula rin.
 
vi.
 
2.
Def.: egkeimpusan
MS: egkeimpusan
If something concludes, it finishes.
The fiesta concludes on Friday.
Ka pista egkeimpusan kayi te Birnis.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: eg-impusan
MS: eg-impusan
If you conclude something, you finish it.
They concluded the fiesta with a community lunch.
Mig-impusan dan ka pista te pegpaniudtu te keet-etawan.
 
vt.
 
4.
Def.: egpekeyimu te kasabutan
MS: egpekeyimu te kasabutan
If people or groups conclude something such as a business agreement, they make the agreement.
The two companies concluded a deal.
Ka daruwa ne kumpanya nekeyimu te kasabutan.
Sem.Dom.: AWL2
