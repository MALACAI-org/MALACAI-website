leave1
/leev/
 
vi, vt.
 
1.
Def.: eg-awè; eg-engked
MS: eg-awè; eg-engked
If you leave a place, you go from that place.
I usually leave home at about 6:30am.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: eg-engkeran
MS: eg-engkeran
If you leave a person or organization, you go away from them and do not plan to come back to them.
He left his wife and four children.
He left his job because his boss was unfair.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: egpakahaat [egpakaha͡at]; eggaat [egga͡at]; egpakalingew
MS: egpakahaat; eggaat; egpakalingew
If someone or something leaves a thing somewhere, they go away from that place but that thing stays there.
Oh! Someone left their umbrella on the bus.
Tuwey! Due nakahaat [nakaha͡at] te payung din kayi te terak.
She left a gift for you.
Miggaat [migga͡at] sikandin te dasag kuna.
Eating balingbing seeds can leave a strange taste in your mouth.
Ka pegkeen te behas te balingbing egpakahaat [egpakaha͡at] te lein ne nanam te be-bè nu.
They left mud on the floor when they walked through the house in their shoes.
 
vt.
 
4.
Def.: egbalahad
MS: egbalahad
If you leave people or a situation, you don't join them or do anything to them.
If the children are arguing, just leave them, and don't get involved in their argument.
 
vt.
 
5.
Def.: egsameen
MS: egsameen
If you leave something, you don't use it, so that it remains.
Note: I've changed it a little. does this \dv seem clear to you?
If you can't eat all the rice, just leave it, and we will eat it later.
We’ll eat the mangoes, but we’ll leave the apples for him
Irreg. v.  left, left.
