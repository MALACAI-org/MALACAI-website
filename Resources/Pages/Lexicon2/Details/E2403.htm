region
/ree-djən/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: lugar ne due nateil ne kaleinan
MS: lugar; rihiyun
rihiyun
an area which has a certain characteristic (e.g., a mountainous region).
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: nateil ne baad te minsan nekey
a particular area of something (e.g., the body).
She has a lot of pain in the lower back region.
Due amana masakit diyè te diralem ne peka rin.
 
 
regional
/ree-djən-el/
 
adj.
Def.: due kalabetan te nateil ne me lugar
MS: lugar, due kalabetan te nateil ne me
related to particular regions.
We joined the regional competition.
 
adj.
Def.: due kalabetan te me lugar ne mariyù te siyudad
MS: lugar ne mariyù te siyudad, due kalabetan te me
related to the non-city areas of a country.
She lives in regional Mindanao, far from any major cities.
