well1
 
adv.
 
1.
Def.: eg-ay-ayaran; egpaay-ayaran; tetelesi; meupiya amana
MS: eg-ay-ayaran; egpaay-ayaran; tetelesi; meupiya
If you do something well, you do it skilfully, and the quality of the action is good.
He speaks English very well.
Egpakalalag sikandin te meupiya amana.
She sings very well.
Mig-ulahing sikandin ne amana ne tetelesi.
 
adv.
 
2.
Def.: eg-ay-ayaran
MS: eg-ay-ayaran
If you do something well, you do it very completely.
You must wash it well so that there is absolutely no dirt remaining.
Eg-ay-ayaran nu seini te egpi-pì eyew warad iya bilu ne egkahaat [egkaha͡att].
 
adj.
 
3.
Def.: meupiya (meyitenged te peglelawa)
MS: meupiya
If you are well, your health is good.
Q: How are you? A: Well, thanks.
Kumusta kad e? Meupiya, salamat.
 
adv.
 
4.
Def.: duen pad malayat ne timpu wey ke dakel ne kaleinan te daruwa ne numiru.
You use well with words like before and under to say that there is a large difference between two numbers or times.
We will arrive well before dark, so don't worry.
Egpekeyinguma key te kenè pad egkarusilem, sika ka kenè ka kalaggew.
We paid well over 100 pesos for it.
Nakabayad key te subla pad te mahatus te para kayi.
Irreg. adv.  well, better, best.
Source: GSL0076-0100
