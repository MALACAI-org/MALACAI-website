get
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: egpakakuwa; egpakarawat
MS: egpakakuwa; egpakarawat
If you get something, you receive it.
I got a present for my birthday.
Nakakuwa a te dasag te aldew ne neetawan ku.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: eg-angey; egkuen
MS: eg-angey; egkuen
If you get something, you go to a place and take the thing from there.
Can you please get my cup? It's on the table.
 
vi.
 
3.
Def.: egkeyimu
MS: egkeyimu
become.
She stopped swimming because she got cold.
Migsanggel e sikandin su egkaagsil [egka͡agsil] e sikandin.
 
vt.
 
4.
Def.: egpeyimu; egpatrabahu
MS: egpeyimu; egpatrabahu
If you get someone to do something, or get something done, you ask or cause someone to do it.
I got my daughter to help me.
Migpabulig a te anak kun malitan.
I got my motorbike fixed yesterday.
Impeupianan ku ka mutur ku gabii.
 
vi.
 
5.
Def.: eggendiyè
MS: eggendiyè
If you get to a place, you travel there.
Q: How will you get there? A: By motorbike.
Q: Egmemenu kaa ka eggendiyè? A:Egmumutur a.
 
vt.
 
6.
Def.: eg-untud
MS: eg-untud
If you get public transport, you use that transport to go somewhere.
I got a bus to Davao.
Irreg. v.  got, got/fb:gotten [got-ən].
