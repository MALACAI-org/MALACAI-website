agent
/ay-djent/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: etew ne egpangimu te minsan nekey eyew te lein ne etew
a person who organizes something (e.g., business) for another person.
travel agent
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: minsan nekey ne egpakapeyitabù te minsan nekey
something (e.g., a chemical) which causes something to happen.
Salt is the preserving agent in this meat.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: talag-ipat; talagsilib
MS: talag-ipat; talagsilib
a spy.
He is a secret agent for his country
Talag-ipat sikandin diyè te nasud din.
Source: GSL1051-1075
