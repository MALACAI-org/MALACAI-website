evaluate
/i-væl-yoo-wate/
 
vt.
Def.: egpitew ke kamenu keupiya, kakeilangan wey ke karakel; egsusi
MS: egsusi
If you evaluate something, you decide how good, important or large it is.
We had a test to evaluate how much we had learned.
Due peggeram kanta eyew egpitawen ke hendeid taman ka natuenan ta.
Source: AWL2
 
 
evaluation
/i-væl-yoo-way-shən/
 
n-uc, n-ct.
Def.: pegpitew; pegsusi
MS: pegpitew; pegsusi
the action of evaluating something.
Evaluation of the students will take about a week.
 
n-ct.
Def.: ka puhawang nu pegkeimpus te pegpitew wey pegsusi nu
what you think about something after you have evaluated it.
According to his evaluation, it will take about 6 months to complete this project.
Status: ready
