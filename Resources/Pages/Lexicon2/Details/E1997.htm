of
 
prep.
 
1.
Def.: Egkahamit seini ke ka sabeka ne lalag egbehey te dakel ne kanengnengan te lein ne lalag.
Of connects words when one word gives more information about the other.
I have no idea of what to do.
Warè puhawang ku ke nekey ka eggimuwen.
 
prep.
 
2.
Def.: te (egpegsumpul te pegpangamuney)
MS: te
Of connects the owner and the thing that they own.
He is a friend of my father.
Alukuy sikandin te amuy ku.
 
prep.
 
3.
Def.: te (egpegsumpul te baad wey te langun)
MS: te
Of connects the part and the whole.
The cat is on the roof of the house.
Ka miyew diyè te atep te baley.
 
prep.
 
4.
Def.: te (egpegsumpul te karakelan wey ke tahuanan wey te minsan nekey)
MS: te
Of connects the quantity or container and the material
I bought a kilo of rice.
Migbebeli a te sabeka ne kilu te behas.
I drank a cup of coffee
 
prep.
 
5.
Def.: te (egpegsumpul te me weil wey ke sitwasyun te etew wey ke butang)
MS: te
Of connects the action or situation and the person or thing.
She was sad because of the death of her grandfather.
Miglungkù sikandin te pegpatey te apu din ne buyag.
 
prep.
 
6.
Def.: diyè te (eglalag meyitenged te weil)
MS: diyè te
You use of to describe an action.
That was kind of him to help.
Meupiya diyè te kandin ka pegbulig.
Source: prepositions; GSL0001-0025
