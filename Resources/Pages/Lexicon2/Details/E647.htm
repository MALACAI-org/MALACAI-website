consideration
/kon-sid-ə-ray-shən/
 
n-uc.
 
1.
Def.: pegsuman-suman
MS: pegsuman-suman
the action of thinking about something (give something consideration).
Please give this problem your careful consideration.
 
n-uc.
 
2.
Def.: pegsuman-suman wey pegtanud-tanud te nateil ne kamalehetan
MS: pegsuman-suman wey pegtanud-tanud te nateil ne kamalehetan
the action of remembering or using a particular fact when you think or decide something. (take something into consideration).
When you choose a worker, you should take into consideration their character.
Ke eg-alam ka te trabaanti [traba͡anti] keilangan ne due pegsuman-suman nu te me batasan dan.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: egtantanuran ne kamalehetan ke eggimu te lein
something that you need to remember or think about when you think or decide something.
When choosing a boarding house, the price is a very important consideration.
 
n-uc.
 
4.
Def.: peg-addat; pegbassa
MS: peg-addat; pegbassa
an attitude of caring about other people, and wanting them to be comfortable and happy.
My roommate always shows consideration for me, and tries to be very quiet if she arrives home late.
