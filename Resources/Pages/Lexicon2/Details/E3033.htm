too
 
adv.
 
1.
Def.: ded maa; red maa; red degma; red man-e
MS: ded maa; red maa; red degma; red man-e
You use too to add something.
I lost my keys, and I lost my cell phone, too.
Nalaag ku ka yabi ku, wey nalaag ku red maa ka silpun ku.
 
adv.
 
2.
Def.: degma
MS: degma
You use too to say that something is true of another person or thing.
She likes cooking and her brother likes it too.
Egkeupian sikandin ne egsusubba wey ka suled din degma.
 
adv.
 
3.
Def.: egkasublaan [egkasubla͡an] sika ka due prublima
You use too to say that something is more than it should be, so there is a problem.
This coffee is too hot, so I can't drink it yet.
Iiniti pad seini se keppi, sika ka ke ku pad egkeinum.
She put in too much coffee, so I don't like it.
Nasublaan [nasubla͡an] ka keppi ne intahù din, sika ka kena a egkeupian.
Source: adverbs; GSL0101-0125
