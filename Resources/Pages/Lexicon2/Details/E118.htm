altar
/awl-ter/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: altar (ne eg-unturan te egtutungen)
MS: altar
a structure where people do religious activities including sacrificing animals.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: altar
MS: altar
In some churches, the table which is used for communion (also called Mass, or the Eucharist) is called the altar.
