organize
/or-gə-nayiz/
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: egpangimu te himuwen eyew egkeyitabù
MS: egpangimu te himuwen eyew egkeyitabù
If you organize an event, you cause it to happen by doing all the things to make it happen.
My mother organized a party for my birthday.
Ka iney ku migpangimu te kalibulungan eyew te aldew ne neetawan ku.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egteilen; egtapiren
MS: egteilen; egtapiren
If you organize things, you put them in a particular order or place.
He organized the books into subject groups.
Source: GSL0401-0425
 
 
organized
/or-gə-nayizd/
 
adj.
Def.: nateil; natapid
MS: nateil; natapid
If things are organized, they are in a particular order or place because somebody put them like that.
Her study notes are very organized.
 
adj.
Def.: mateil; matapid
MS: mateil; matapid
An organized person plans their time and their things very well.
She is very organized. Everything in her room has a particular place
