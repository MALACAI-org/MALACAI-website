minister
/min-ə-ster/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: pangulu; uluwan; egdumaan [egduma͡an] (meyitenged te dipartamintu te gubirnu)
MS: pangulu; uluwan; egdumaan
the leader of a government department (e.g., the Minister for Education).
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: talagwali te guhuren te Manama; pastur
MS: talagwali te guhuren te Manama; pastur
a pastor.
 
vi, vt(+prep).
 
3.
Def.: egtanggu wey egbulig
MS: egtanggu wey egbulig
If you minister to someone who has a need or problem, you care for them and help them.
When I was sick, my sister really ministered to me.
Te pegderalu ku, ka atebey ku iyan amana migtanggu keddì.
Source: AWL6; GSL1101-1125
