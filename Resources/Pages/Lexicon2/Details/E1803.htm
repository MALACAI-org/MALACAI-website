mean1
/meen/
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: igpasabut
MS: igpasabut
If you mean something, that is the message that you want to communicate. If something means something, that is the message that it communicates.
You misunderstood me; I didn't mean that I wanted to leave now.
What does this word mean?
Bad weather here in Mindanao often means that there is a typhoon in Manila.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: eggimuwen ne due tuyù
MS: eggimuwen ne due tuyù
If you mean to do something, you do it because you want and choose to do it.
He meant to step on your foot! It was not an accident.
Intuyuan din iya te egdiek ka paa nu ne! Kenè mulè ne aksidinti rè.
Oh, I'm sorry! I didn't mean to step on your foot! It was an accident.
Tuwey, pasayluwa a, warè ku tuyua ne egdiekan ku ka paa nu! Aksidinti rè.
 
v-passive.
 
3.
Def.: para (meyitenged pegbehey wey ke peggamit)
MS: para
If something is meant for a particular person or purpose, you want to give it to that person or use it for that purpose.
Don't eat that bread! It's not meant for you.
 
vt.
 
4.
Def.: ne duen naa (egpuunan ke mania te due ka duma ne butang)
MS: ne duen naa
If something means another thing, it causes that other thing.
If it rains a lot, it means that we will have a flood.
Ke eg-uran seini te dakel, ne duen naa baggiyu.
More chickens mean more eggs.
Ke masulug ka manuk masulug naa degma ka atelug.
 
vt.
 
5.
Def.: due keimpurtanti; dakel ka igpasabut
MS: due keimpurtanti; dakel ka igpasabut
If something means something to you, it is important to you.
This bracelet means a lot to me because my best friend gave it to me.
Ka seini ne bulawan due keimpurtanti kayi te keddiey su imbehey seini te telek ne alukuy ku.
Irreg. v.  meant [ment], meant.
Source: GSL0126-0150
