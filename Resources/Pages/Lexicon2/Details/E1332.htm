gracious
/gray-shəss/
 
adj.
 
1.
Def.: amana ne buutan wey uupiyei
MS: buutan; uupiyei
If someone is gracious, they are very kind and good.
She was gracious to me, and shared her lunch.
Amana ne buutan sikandin keddì, wey intuhen a nikandin te paniudtu rin.
 
adj.
 
2.
Def.: maluwan, meupiya wey mahal (meyitenged te minsan nekey)
MS: maluwan; meupiya wey mahal
If something such as a house is gracious, it is very comfortable, beautiful and expensive.
Source: keyterms; GSL1376-1400 - related
