make
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: eggimu
MS: eggimu
If you make something, you cause it to exist (by using other things or putting them together).
My mother made some coffee.
My father made a seat for our house.
Note: please add definition to one of these - is it natural to use 'eggimu' in either of these sentences?
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egpehes
MS: egpehes
If you make someone do something, you cause them to do it by force.
His mother always makes him help in the garden.
Layun egpehes ka iney rin ne egpabulig kandin te gulayan.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: egpekeyimu
MS: egpekeyimu
If something makes you do something, it causes you to do that thing. If something makes you ADJECTIVE, it causes you to be that way.
That movie made me cry.
Nakapasinehew keddì sika ne salida.
The good news made me happy.
Nakapahalè keddì sika ne panugtulen.
Irreg. v.  made, made.
Source: GSL0026-0050
