trend
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: nabantug ne kapandayan
MS: nabantug ne kapandayan
something which is popular with a lot of people.
It's a trend at the moment to wear your hair like that.
Nabantug kuntee ne kapandayan ka eg-ilingen due ka bulbul.
She liked to keep up with the trends in fashion.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: kinatibuk-an ne pegkaalin [pegka͡alin]
MS: pegkaalin, kinatibuk-an ne
a general change in something.
There is a trend towards buying brown sugar rather than white sugar.
Due kinatibuk-an ne pegkaalin [pegka͡alin] te pegbebeli te makalawag ne sukal du te maangkag ne sukal.
There is a downward trend in the price of gas.
