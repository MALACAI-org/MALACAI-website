concern
/kon-sern/
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: egkasahukul; egkalangè
MS: egkasahukul; egkalangè
If something concerns you, you feel worried about it.
It concerns me that my brother hasn't texted me. I hope he is OK.
 
n-uc.
 
2.
Def.: pegkasahukul; pegkalangè
MS: pegkasahukal; pegkalangè
a feeling of being worried about something.
I could see the concern on her face as she looked at the sick child.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: igkasahukul
MS: igkasahukul
something that you feel worried about.
My uncle's bad health is a real concern for us.
 
n-uc.
 
4.
Def.: geyinawa ne keilangan (ka minsan nekey)
MS: geyinawa ne keilangan
a feeling that something is important.
My teacher shows great concern for correct spelling.
Ka talagpanulù ney amanan keilangan diyet kandin ka ispiling.
 
vt.
 
5.
Def.: meyitenged te (due kalabetan te minsan nekey)
MS: meyitenged te
If something concerns a particular topic, it is about that topic.
His speech concerned the plans for the new building.
Ka guhuren din meyitenged te suman-suman te iyam ne egbaleyen.
 
vt.
 
6.
Def.: egkalabet; egkaapil
MS: egkalabet; egkaapil
If something concerns you, then you are part of it, or it has an effect on you.
Please don't ask me about that, as it's personal and doesn't concern you.
Palihug kena a insei meyitenged due, amana rè ne para keddiey sika, wey kenè ka egkalabet due.
 
n-ct.
 
7.
Def.: labet
MS: labet
something that you are part of, or which has an effect on you
Please don't ask me about that, as it's personal , and is not your concern.
Palihug kena a insei meyitenged due, amana ren para keddiey sika, wey warè labet nu due.
Source: GSL0301-0325
 
 
concerned
/kon-sernd/
 
adj.
Def.: egkasahukul; egkalangè
MS: egkasahukul; egkalangè
If you are concerned about something, you are worried about it.
I'm very concerned about my sick baby.
