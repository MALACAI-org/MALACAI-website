lead1
/leed/
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: eggun-a eyew te pegpanulù te egbayaan
MS: eggun-a
If you lead people, you go before them to show them the way.
He led the group of people along the path.
Miggun-a sikandin te punduk te me etew diyè te dalan.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egkitkit; egtuyuk
MS: egkitkit; egtuyuk
If you lead a person, you go before them holding their hand or arm. If you lead an animal, you go before it holding it with something like a rope.
She led the horse along the path.
Migtuyuk din ka kuddè diyè te dalan.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: eg-uyan
MS: eg-uyan
If you lead a group or activity, you organize or control it.
He led some games for the children.
Mig-uyan sikandin te duma ne galew te me batè.
 
vi.
 
4.
Def.: egpeendiyè [egpe͡endiyè] (meyitenged te dalan)
MS: egpeendiyè
If a path leads to a place, it goes there.
This path leads to my house.
Ka seini ne dalan egpeendiyè te baley ku.
 
vi.
 
5.
Def.: egpakahated; egpakarulung
MS: egpakahated; egpakarulung
If a situation leads to another situation, it causes that situation.
His bad action led to many problems.
Ka mareet ne weil din nakahated te masalig ne prublima.
 
n-ct.
 
6.
Def.: hun-a
MS: hun-a
The lead is the first position in front of a group.
I know the way to that place, so I will take the lead.
Nakataha a te dalan te sika ne lugar, su sikeddì ka eggun-a.
 
n-ct.
 
7.
Def.: inggimu ne eg-ilingan te duma
MS: inggimu ne eg-ilingan te duma
A person's lead is the things they do that others copy.
He sat down and we followed his lead.
Migpinuu sikandin wey mig-iling key te inggimu rin.
Irreg. v.  led, led.
