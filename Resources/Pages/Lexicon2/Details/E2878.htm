sufficient
/sa-fish-ənt/
 
adj.
Def.: eleg
MS: eleg
If something is sufficient, it is enough.
100 pesos is not sufficient to buy food for all those people.
Ka mahatus kenè ne eleg ne igbeli te pegkeen te langun ne etew.
Source: AWL3

sufficiency
/sa-fish-ən-see/
 
n-uc.
Def.: pegkeeleg te karakel wey kasalig
MS: pegkeeleg te karakel wey kasalig
