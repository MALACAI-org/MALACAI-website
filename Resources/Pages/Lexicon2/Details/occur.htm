occur
/o-ker/
 
vi.
 
1.
Def.: egkeyitabù
MS: egkeyitabù
If something occurs, it happens.
A solar eclipse will occur next Friday afternoon.
Ka lekep egkeyitabù kayi te seup ne Birnis.
 
vi.
 
2.
Def.: egkakita
MS: egkakita
If something occurs somewhere, it is there in that place.
This plant only occurs in the Philippines.
Ka seini ne pinamula kayi rè egkakita te Pilipinas.
This kind of weather pattern only occurs near the equator.
 
vi.
 
3.
Def.: egpakalambag te suman-suman
MS: egpakalambag te suman-suman
If something occurs to you, you suddenly think of it.
I was taking a rest when it occurred to me that I had forgotten to collect water.
Miggimeley a te nakalambag te suman-suman ku ne nalingew e bes ne egpanakeru.
Sem.Dom.: AWL1
 
 
occurrence
/o-ka-rənss/
 
n-ct.
Def.: pegkeyitabù
MS: pegkeyitabù
the action of something happening.
The occurrence of an eclipse is always interesting.
 
n-ct.
Def.: hitabù
MS: hitabù
something which happens.
Eclipses are not a common occurence.
