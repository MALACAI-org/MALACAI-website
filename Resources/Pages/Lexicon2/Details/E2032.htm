order
/or-der/
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: egsuhù
MS: egsuhù
If you order someone to do something, you tell them that they must do it.
My father ordered me to help my mother.
Ka amey ku migsuhù keddì te pegbulig te iney ku.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: suhù
MS: suhù
words which tell someone what they must do.
I always follow my father's orders.
Layun a egpalintutuu te me suhù te amey ku.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: egpeuyan wey ke egpabehey te minsan nekey ne igkeupii (iling diyè te inumanan te keppi wey ke keenanan ne egbayaran).
MS: egpeuyan; egpabehey
If you order something from a shop, you ask them to get it for you or send it to you. If you order food in a restaurant, you ask them to bring it to you.
I ordered some chicken in the restaurant.
Migpabehey a te manuk diyè te keenanan.
I ordered some shoes from the catalogue.
 
n-ct.
 
4.
Def.: pegpabehey; pegparulung (meyitenged te minsan nekey diyè te keenanan wey ke tindaan [tinda͡an]).
a request to a shop to get something for you, or to a person in an eating place to bring you a particular food.
The lady in the calendaria took my order for soup and rice.
Ka malitan diyè te keenanan mig-uyan te igkeupii ku ne sabew wey keenen.
 
n-ct.
 
5.
Def.: pegkateil; pegpekegsinsinundul
MS: pegkateil; pegpekegsinsinundul
an arrangement of things one after the other (with one thing first, another thing second, and so on).
In a dictionary, the words are in alphabetical order.
Te diksyunari, ka me lalag wey litra nenekegsinsinundul.
 
vt.
 
6.
Def.: egteilen; egpegsinundul
MS: egteilen; egpegsinundul
If you order things, you arrange them so that one thing is first, another thing is second, and so on.
She ordered the children from oldest to youngest.
Migteil din ka me batè puun te buyag-buyag peendiyè [pe͡endiyè] te kinabataan.
 
n-uc.
 
7.
Def.: nenateil
MS: nenateil
a state of things being well-organized.
Everything in her house is in order, because she cleaned this morning.
Langun ne diyè te baley rin nenateil, su migpanlinis sikandin te maselem.
 
 
in order to
 
phrase.
Def.: eyew
MS: eyew
You use in order to to say why you do something.
She fed the baby in order to stop him crying.
Impakeen din ka batè eyew egsanggel ka egsinehew.
