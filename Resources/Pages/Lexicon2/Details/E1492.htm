immediate
/im-mee-dee-yət/
 
adj.
 
1.
Def.: sahuhunè
MS: sahuhunè
If an action is immediate, it happens just after another action.
He sent an immediate answer to my text message.
Sahuhunè ne migpeuyan sikandin te tabak te tiks ku kandin.
 
adj.
 
2.
Def.: taltalaanak (iling te amey wey iney nu, wey ka me kakey wey hari nu)
MS: taltalaanak
Your immediate family are your parents, brothers and sisters (not your cousins, uncles or other relatives).
I could only have 4 visitors for my graduation, so just my immediate family came: my parents and my two brothers.
Duen dè hep-at ubag ne bisita ku te graduwisyun ku, ka sikanami rè iya ubag se taltalaanak: ka me ginikanan ku wey ka daruwa ne suled ku.
Source: GSL0526-0550
 
 
immediately
/im-mee-dee-yət-lee/
 
adv.
Def.: sahuhunè ne egkeyitabù impus te newun-a
If something happens immediately after another thing, it happens just after that other thing.
They went home immediately after lunch.
Sahuhunè ne mig-uli sikandin pegke-impus te paniudtu.
