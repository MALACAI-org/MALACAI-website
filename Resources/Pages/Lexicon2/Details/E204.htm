arrest
/a-rest/
 
vt, n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: egdakep
MS: egdakep
If the police arrest a person, they catch them and take them to a place such as a police station.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: pegpandakep
MS: pegpandakep
an action of arresting someone (make an arrest).
There were many arrests when the police found the hide-out of the killers.
Due masalig ne pegpandakep te natulituan te me pulis ka helesanan te me talaggimatey.
 
vi.
 
3.
Def.: egpeeneng-eneng
MS: egpeeneng-eneng
If a process arrests, it stops.
His heart arrested during the operation.
Migpeeneng-eneng ka pusung din te timpu te mig-upirahan sikandin.
 
n-ct.
 
4.
Def.: pegpeeneng-eneng; pegsanggel
MS: pegpeeneng-eneng; pegsanggel
an action of something stopping.
He went into cardiac arrest during the operation.
 
vt.
 
5.
Def.: egpasanggel; egpapeeneng-eneng
MS: egpasanggel; egpapeeneng-eneng
If someone or something arrests a process, they stop it.
This pesticide arrests the growth of weeds.
Egpasanggel seini ne hilu te pegtubù te me hilamunen.
Source: GSL1451-1475
