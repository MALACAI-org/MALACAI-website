interest
/in-trəst/
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: egpakabagget
MS: egpakabagget
If something interests you, you want to do it or know more about it.
Farming interests him.
 
n-uc, n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: bagget; ahet; pegbagget (meyitenged te eggeramen nu ne igkeupii nu te eggimu ne minsan nekey, wey ke pegneneng pad te minsan nekey)
MS: bagget; ahet; pegbagget
a feeling that you want to do something, or know more about something (have an interest in something).
He has an interest in farming
Due bagget din te pegkekamet.
My mother encouraged an interest in learning.
Ka iney ku mig-ahet keddì te pegbagget te pegbebasbas.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: minsan nekey ne igkeupii nu te eggimu wey ke egnengneng
something that you like doing or learning about.
He has many interests, including music and history.
 
n-ct.
 
4.
Def.: due kalabetan; apiktadu
MS: due kalabetan; apiktadu
the fact that something will affect you (have an interest in something).
We should include farmers when making plans for the land, because they have an interest in the decision.
Keilangan ne eg-apilen ta ka mangametay ke eggimu ki te planu te tanè, su due me kalabetan dan te pegbehey te me puhawang.
 
n-ct.
 
5.
Def.: pegkamkamuney te baad te kumpanya
MS: pegkamkamuney te baad te kumpanya
that fact that you own part of a company (have an interest in a company).
 
n-uc.
 
6.
Def.: anak (meyitenged te tubù); tubù te nasambayan
MS: anak; tubù te nasambayan
the extra money which you pay when you borrow money. Usually a percentage of the amount which you borrow.
Source: GSL0151-0175
 
 
interested
/in-trəss-təd/
 
adj.
Def.: egkabagget
MS: egkabagget
If you are interested in something, you want to do it or know more about it.
I'm very interested in history.
 
 
interesting
/in-trəss-ting/
 
adj.
Def.: makabebagget
MS: makabebagget
If something is interesting, it causes you to want to know more.
I think that history is very interesting.
