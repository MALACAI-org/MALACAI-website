feel
 
vi.
 
1.
Def.: eggeram
MS: eggeram
If you feel a certain way, that is the state of your body, mind or heart.
feel sick, feel happy.
egderalu, egkahalè.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egtuu
MS: egtuu
If you feel that something is the case, you believe that it is the case.
I feel that we should help her.
Egtuu a ne keilangan ki ne egbulig kandin.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: egtaluheramen
MS: egtaluheramen
If you feel something, you use your hands to help you know what it is like.
She felt the bag to find out what was inside.
Migtaluheram din ka kabil ke nekey ka tahù.
Irreg.  felt, felt.
 
 
feel like
Def.: egkeupian
MS: egkeupian
If you feel like doing something, you want to do that thing.
I'm tired, so I feel like going to bed.
Nabeley a, sika ka egkeupian a ne eggendiyad te hibatanan.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0101-0125
Status: ready
