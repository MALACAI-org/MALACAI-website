memory
/mem-ree/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: kalantus te pegtanud-tanud wey pegsuman-suman
your ability to remember things.
She has a very bad memory, and is always forgetting to do things.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: minsan nekey ne neyitabù ne miglihad ne egkasuman-suman nu
something that happened in the past that you remember.
I have some nice memories from when I was a child.
Due memeupiya ne egkangasuman-suman ku puun te deisek e pad.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: pegsuman-suman te migpatey
MS: pegsuman-suman te migpatey
The memory of someone who has died is the fact that people remember them.
My grandmother's memory will continue for a long time.
 
n-uc.
 
4.
Def.: tahuanan te me kanengnengan diyè te kumpyutir
the space on a computer where it keeps information.
That computer has a lot of memory, so I can put photos on it.
Source: GSL0826-0850
