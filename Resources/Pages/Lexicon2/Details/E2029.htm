optional
/op-shən-əl/
 
adj.
Def.: egpakaalam ke egkeupian ka wey ke kenè
If something is optional, you can choose if you want it or not.
The basketball game tomorrow is optional, so you don't need to join if you don't want to.
Ka baskitbul kaaselem egpakaalam ka, sika ka kenè ne keilangan ne eg-apil kew ke kenè kew egkeupian.
