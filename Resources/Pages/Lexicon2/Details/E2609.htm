select
/sə-lekt/
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: eg-alam; egpilì
MS: eg-alam; egpilì
If you select something, you choose it from a group of things.
She selected the biggest mango from the bag.
 
adj.
 
2.
Def.: tineil ne in-alam ne grupu te etew wey ke minsan nekey
MS: ispisyal
ispisyal
A select group is a special group of people or things who have been chosen.
Only a select few were invited to the meeting.
