stage
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: senge-baad te pegkasinsinundul te weil wey pegbalewbalew
one of the steps of a series of actions or changes.
The frogs are only at the tadpole stage at the moment.
Ka me bakbak butiti pad te kuntee ne baad.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: hun-aan [hun-a͡an]; tangkaan; kewun-aan (ka egsasindehan te grupu wey ke sabeka rè ne egkanta)
MS: hun-aan; tangkaan; kewun-aan
the raised part at the front of an area, where people such as singers perform for a group.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: eggimu te kalibulungan te katangenan
MS: eggimu te kalibulungan te katangenan
If you stage a public event such as a fiesta, you cause it to happen.
MLCA staged a talent night at Fiesta.
Ka MLCA miggimu te kalibulungan ne pegpapitawey te katuenan te pista.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0451-0475
Status: ready
