monitor
/mon-nə-ter/
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: egtengteng wey ke egbantey ne egpabulus-bulus
If you monitor something or someone, you continue to watch them carefully.
The doctor said he should stay in the hospital so that they can monitor him.
Ka duktur migkahi te ugpè ka te uspital eyew egkatengtengan nu sikandin.
The police monitored the suspect from a car near the house.
You need to monitor your spending, so that you still have money at the end of the week.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: talagtengteng; talagbantey
MS: talagtengteng; talagbantey
a person who watches something carefully, to make sure that it is done properly.
The UN sent monitors to that country during the election, to make sure that the voting was fair.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: mekina eyew te pegnengneng te pangitabù
a machine which checks what is happening with something (e.g., a heart monitor checks what your heart is doing).
They attached a heart monitor to his chest to measure his heart rate.
 
n-ct.
 
4.
Def.: iskrin ne egpakita te impurmasyun
MS: iskrin
a screen which shows information (e.g., a computer screen).
