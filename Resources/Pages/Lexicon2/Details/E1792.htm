match1
/mætch/
 
vt, v-recip.
 
1.
Def.: egpekeg-eleg
MS: egpekeg-eleg
If one thing matches another thing, or two things match, they fit together well.
Your red shirt and green pants do not match.
The key matches the lock.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egpeg-eleg
MS: egpeg-eleg
If you match things that are similar or connected, you fit them together.
The police matched the footprints to the criminal's shoes.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: eleg
MS: eleg
two things that fit together very well.
This key is a match for this lock.
 
n-ct.
 
4.
Def.: keiling
MS: keiling
someone or something that is as good as another person or thing.
I can run faster than everyone else, but my brother is my match.
Egpakapallahuy a te iiyali te minsan hentew pad, piru ka suled ku ka keiling ku.
 
n-ct.
 
5.
Def.: galawen iling te baskitbul wey ke balibul
MS: galawen
a sports event such as a game of basketball or volleyball.
Source: GSL0926-0950
