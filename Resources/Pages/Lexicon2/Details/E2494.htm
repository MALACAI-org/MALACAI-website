right1
/rite/
 
adj, adv.
 
1.
Def.: meupiya (meyitenged te himu)
MS: meupiya
A right action is good.
Parents need to teach their children to do what is right.
Ka me ginikanan dapat egpanulù te me anak dan ke nekey ka meupiya ne himu.
 
adj, adv.
 
2.
Def.: eleg; meupiya
MS: eleg; meupiya
Something right is true or correct, or fits the particular place or situation (e.g., the right key for the door).
He got twenty-eight answers right on the exam.
No, you're not singing that right! Try again.
She thought he might have a boil, and she was right.
No, that's not the right key; try the other one.
Nursing is the right job for me.
The box is upside down; you need to turn it over so that it will be right-side up.
Your T-shirt is inside out; you need to change it so that it is right-side out.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: katenged
MS: katenged
If you have a right to do something, you should be able to do it (maybe because the law allows you to do it).
Parents have a right to advise their children.
 
vt.
 
4.
Def.: eggusey; egsulbad
MS: eggusey; egsulbad
If you right a problem, you fix it.
I went to the dentist to try to right the problems with my teeth.
Source: GSL0126-0150
