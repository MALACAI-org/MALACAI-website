frequent
/free-kwənt (adj), fri-kwent (v)/
 
adj.
 
1.
Def.: malasi
MS: malasi
If an action or event is frequent, it happens often.
There is frequent rain here.
Due malasi ne peg-uran kayi.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: layun eggendiyè
MS: layun eggendiyè; eggendiyè layun
Note: my re, is it ok?
Note: yup it's okay
If you frequent a place, you go there very often.
This is the shop that he frequents.
Seini ka tindaan [tinda͡an] ne layun din eggendinii [eggendini͡i].
Source: GSL0776-0800
 
 
frequently
 
adv.
Def.: malasi
MS: malasi
If something happens frequently, it happens often.
I frequently eat at my aunt's house.
Malasi a egkeen diyè te baley te ayè ku.
