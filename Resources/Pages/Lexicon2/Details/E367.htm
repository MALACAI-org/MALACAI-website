both
/bohth/
 
det, pron, conj.
Def.: warè tapid; langun; elin tapid
MS: warè tapid; langun; elin tapid
You use both to talk about the two members of a group of two.
Both twins are sick.
Langun te talaseping migderalu.
I can't choose one of them, because I like both.
Warè tapid egsamilian ku kandan, sika ka warè sabeka ne eg-alamen ku.
Both my wife and I like gardening.
Both of my parents are old now.
Source: determiners; pronouns; conjunctions; GSL0101-0125
