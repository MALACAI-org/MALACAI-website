care
/kair/
 
vi.
 
1.
Def.: eggeramen ne keilangan
MS: eggeramen ne keilangan
If you care about something, it is important to you.
 
vi.
 
2.
Def.: eggeyinawa
MS: eggeyinawa
If you care about someone, you love them and want good things for them.
I really care about my sister.
 
vi, vt(+prep).
 
3.
Def.: egtanggu; eg-atiman
MS: egtanggu; eg-atiman
If you care for someone or something, you do for them the things that they need.
My mother is caring for my sick grandmother at the moment.
They don't really care for their house, so it looks very untidy.
 
n-uc.
 
4.
Def.: pegtanggu; peg-atiman
MS: pegtanggu; peg-atiman
Care is the things that you do for someone or something (take care of somebody or something).
This garden needs some care, as it is very overgrown.
Thank you for your care of me while I was ill.
 
n-ct, n-uc.
 
5.
Def.: bantal; laggew
MS: bantal; laggew
something that you are worried about.
She looks so relaxed, as if she doesn't have a care in the world.
 
n-uc.
 
6.
Def.: pegngilam; peg-andam; pegbantey
MS: pegngilam; peg-andam; pegbantey
Care is the things that you do to make sure that something bad doesn't happen (take care).
Use care when you remove the lid from the boiling pot, as it will be very hot.
Bantey ka ke egpuwas te egkale-kalè ne kendiru, su iiniti.
This vase is breakable, so handle it with care.
Source: GSL0276-0300
 
 
careful
/kair-fəl/
 
adj.
Def.: mangilam-ngilam; mapanalliya
MS: mangilam-ngilam; mapanalliya
If you are careful, you take care so that something bad will not happen.
Be careful! That water is hot.
