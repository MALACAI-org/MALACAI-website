connection
/ko-nek-shən/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: pegkasumpul
MS: pegkasumpul
the place where two things join, or the thing that joins them together.
The water is coming out between the pipe and the tank, because the connection is bad.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: kalabetan
MS: kalabetan
the relationship between two things.
There is no connection between the food he ate yesterday, and his illness today.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: sumpul (meyitenged te tilipunu)
MS: sumpul
a telephone link.
I couldn't hear him well on the phone, because the connection was bad.
Source: GSL1051-1075
