hit
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: egsumbag; eglampes
MS: egsumbag; eglampes
If you hit someone or something, you touch them strongly and quickly with your hand or fist, or with something such as a stick.
He hit the dog with a stick.
Miglampesan din ka asu te tuked.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egpakasangkub; egpakabundul
MS: egpakasangkub; egpakabundul
If one thing hits another thing it touches it strongly and quickly.
The motorbike hit the dog.
Ka mutur nakabundul te asu.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: pegpakasangkub; pegpakabundul
MS: pegpakasangkub; pegpakabundul
an action of one thing hitting another thing.
The dog suffered a direct hit from the motorbike.
Ka asu nalasey te duritsu ne pegkabundul te mutur.
 
vt.
 
4.
Def.: egpasalerep; egpasaharal
MS: egpasalerep; egpasaharal
If something bad hits a person, place or thing, that person, place or thing experiences the bad thing.
A bad typhoon hit Manila last year.
Ka bagyu migpasalerep te Menila te miglihad ne tuig.
Irreg.  hit, hit.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0676-0700
