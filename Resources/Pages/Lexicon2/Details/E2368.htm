read
/reed/
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: egbasa
MS: egbasa
If you read words, you look at them and understand them.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egbasa ne marahing
MS: egbasa
If you read words, you look at them and say them aloud.
Could you please read that sentence to the class.
Palihug basaa [basa͡a] nu pa ubag te marahing ka pangasak.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: egpitawen wey egnengnengen ka igpasabut
If you read something that does not have words (e.g. an X-ray or a thermometer), you look at it and understand the information on it.
I could read from his face that he was upset
I can't read this map. It's too small.
 
vi.
 
4.
Def.: egpakita te numiru
MS: egpakita te numiru
If a piece of measuring equipment reads a certain number, it shows that number.
It's so hot! The thermometer reads 37.
Irreg. v.  read [red], read [red].
Source: GSL0251-0275
 
 
reading
 
n-ct.
Def.: natahaan [nataha͡an] nu puun te baseen [base͡en] wey ke sitwasyun
what you understand about something that you read, or about something that happened.
What's your reading of the situation? Do you think he's upset?
Nekey ka natahaan [nataha͡an] te sitwasyun te suman-suman nu neepes sikandin?

n-ct.

MS: seked te kanengnenganan

The reading on the thermometer is 37.
