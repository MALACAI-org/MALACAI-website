response
/ri-sponss/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: tabak
MS: tabak
the thing that you write or say because someone wrote or said something to you first.
I have sent three text messages to her, but I haven't received a response.
Katatelu ad migtiks kandin, piru warè tabak puun kandin.
 
n-ct, n-uc.
 
2.
Def.: bales
MS: bales
the thing that you do because of a particular action or sitation.
Her crying was a response to the bad news.
Ka pegsinehew rin ka bales te mareet ne guhuren ne narineg din.
Source: AWL1
