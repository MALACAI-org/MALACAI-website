effect
/i-fekt/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: egkaheram; egpangkusan; egkatamanan
MS: egkaheram
Def.: egpangkusan
MS: egkatamanan
the effect of something is the result or change which it causes (have an effect on something).
The medicine had no effect, and my headache did not stop.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: egpeyitabù
MS: egpeyitabù
If you effect something, you cause it to happen. (used in formal language).
He effected many good changes in the town.
Sikandin ka migpeyitabù te masalig ne meupiya ne pegbalew-balew diyè te lungsud.
Source: GSL0326-0350
Status: ready
