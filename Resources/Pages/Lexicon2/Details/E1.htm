abandon
/a-bæhn-dən/
 
vt.
Def.: eg-engkeran
MS: eg-engkeran
If you abandon someone or something, you leave them and do not plan to come back to them.
When his wife died, he abandoned his children, so now his children have nobody to care for them.
Te pegpatey te asawa rin ne malitan, mig-engkeran din e ka me anak din, su ka me anak din kuntee warad e egtanggu.
They abandoned the broken bike by the roadside, and continued on foot.
 
 
abandonment
 
n-ct, n-uc.
Def.: peg-engked
MS: peg-engked
the action of abandoning.
