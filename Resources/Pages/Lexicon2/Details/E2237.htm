present2
/prez-ənt (noun); prə-zent (verb)/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: dasag
MS: dasag
a gift
a Christmas present
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egbehey te minsan nekey diyè te minsan hentew (meyilut maheteng wey ke diyè te kalibulungan ne pamaahi).
If you present someone with something or present something to someone, you give it to them (usually in a formal or public way).
My co-workers presented me with a gift when I left my job.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: egpakita; egpapitew
MS: egpakita; egpapitew
If you present something such as ID, you show it.
You will need to present some ID to enter that place.
 
vt.
 
4.
Def.: egpanengneng wey ke egpeila te minsan hentew diyè te grupu te kenè pad eglalag wey ke eggimu diyè te tangkaan te grupu.
If you present someone to a group of people, you introduce them in a formal way, because they are going to speak or do something in front of the group.
Now may I present the next singer in our program.
 
vt.
 
5.
Def.: egpakita te minsan nekey ne katuenan diyè te kasuluhan (iling te salida wey ke duma pad).
If you present something such as a movie or performance, you show it in public.
The band presented a few of their songs.
Ka me mangulahing migpakita te duma ne me ulahingen dan.
 
vt.
 
6.
Def.: eglihawang
MS: eglihawang
If something presents itself, it appears.
Many problems presented themselves during the meeting.
Masalig ne prublima ne miglihawang te timpu te miting dan.
 
vt.
 
7.
Def.: egpakabehey
MS: egpakabehey
If something presents another thing, it causes or brings that thing.
This sports competition will present a challenge for all of the players.
Seini ne pegpapitawey te katuenan egpakabehey te pegkabagget te langun ne manggalaway.
Source: GSL0151-0175
