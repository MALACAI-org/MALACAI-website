cold
 
adj.
 
1.
Def.: maagsil [ma͡agsil]
MS: maagsil
 
n-uc.
 
2.
Def.: pegkamaagsil [pegkama͡agsil]
MS: pegkamaagsil
the fact that the temperature is low.
I like the cold here in the mountains.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: kahew ne egpekeyimu te masakit ne ulu, egdehes ne abelengan, atisè wey buwè
a virus which can cause you to have a headache, sore throat, sneezing and coughing.
 
adj.
 
4.
Def.: kenè ne maal-alukuy
MS: kenè ne maal-alukuy
If someone is cold towards another person, they are very unfriendly.
 
adj.
 
5.
Def.: kenè ne mabagget
MS: kenè ne mabagget
If you are cold about something, you are not excited or enthusiastic about it.
His music leaves me cold, and I don't find it exciting at all.
Source: GSL0551-0575
