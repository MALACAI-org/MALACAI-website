option
/op-shən/
 
n-ct.
Def.: alamanan; pilianan
MS: alamanan; pilianan
one of the things which you can choose.
You have two options while you are here: you can either stay with me, or with your aunt.
Due daruwa ne alamanan nu taheed te kayi ka: kaayun ka ne eg-ugpè kayi te keddiey, wey ke diyè naa te ayè nu.
