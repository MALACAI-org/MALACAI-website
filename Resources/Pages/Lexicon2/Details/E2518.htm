room
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: sinabeng
MS: sinabeng
a section of a house or building which usually has four walls and a door.
This house has two rooms.
 
n-uc.
 
2.
Def.: natahahè; bakanti
MS: natahahè; bakanti
space where there is nothing or nobody
There is no more room on this jeepney, so you'll need to wait for the next one.
Warad bakanti kayi te dyip, sika ka keilangan kad ne egtahad te seup.
Sit here. There is room for you on this seat.
Source: GSL0226-0250
