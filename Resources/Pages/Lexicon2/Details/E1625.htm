just1
/djast/
 
adv.
 
1.
Def.: iyam pad iya; sikan pad iya
MS: iyam pad iya; sikan pad iya
If something just happened, it happened a very short time ago.
She has just arrived and is taking off her shoes now.
Sikan pad iya nekeuma sikandin, wey sikan pad iya egluluung sikandin te sepatus din.
 
adv.
 
2.
Def.: en iya
MS: en iya
If something is just happening, it is happening now, or will happen very soon.
She is just leaving now.
Eggipanew en iya sikandin.
 
adv.
 
3.
Def.: dè iya
MS: dè iya
You use just to say that something happens only at the last moment, or is only true by a small margin.
He only just finished his work before it started to rain.
Nekeimpus dè iya sikandin te himu rin ne migbunsud e ka uran.
We arrived just in time for the start of the program.
My younger brother is just tall enough to pick the santol from the tree.
 
adv.
 
4.
Def.: dè; rè (egkahamit seini diyè te minsan nekey ne kenè dè amana ne dakel wey ke impurtanti, wey ke de-deisek dè te duma ne me butang).
You use just if something is not very big or important, or is less than you expected.
Don't be frightened, it's just a small dog.
Kenè ka egkaaldek, deisek dè sika ne asu.
He just told me his name, nothing more.
Migpanugtul dè sikandin te ngaran din, warad lein.
There's just a santol tree in front of our house, nothing else.
Don't be frightened, it's just a lizard.
I didn't bring much with me, just an extra set of clothes.
 
adv.
 
5.
Def.: eleg; malehet; iya
MS: eleg; malehet; iya
exactly
Thank you, it's just what I wanted.
Salamat, seini iya ka igkeupii ku.
She told me just what she heard.
