pay
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: egbayad te seleppì eyew te pegtrabahu
MS: egbayad
If you pay someone, you give them money because they did some work for you.
 
n-uc, n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: bayad; suwildu
MS: bayad; suwildu
the money that you receive for working.
Here is your pay for this week
 
vt, vi.
 
3.
Def.: egbayad te pegbebeli
MS: egbayad
If you pay someone, or pay for something, you give money because you are buying something.
I paid the shop assistant for the eggs.
 
vi.
 
4.
Def.: egkalasey (meyitenged te egkapangkusan te inggimu)
MS: egkalasey
If you pay for something that you did, you experience something bad because you did that thing.
If you don't care for your teeth now, you will pay for it when you are older.
Ke kenè nu eg-apingan ka ngipen nu kuntee, egkalasey ka kayi ke egkabuyag kad.
 
vi.
 
5.
Def.: egpakabehey te meupiya ne egkatamanan
MS: egpakabehey te meupiya ne egkatamanan
If an action pays, it brings a good result.
Your hard work now will pay in the harvest season.
Ka amana nu ne pegtrabahu ka egpakabehey te dakel ne egkahaani nu te tinggaani.
Irreg. v.  paid, paid.
