farther
/far-ther/
 
adv, adj.
Def.: diyè dapit (meyitenged te kariyuan te sabeka ne lugar puun te sabeka); madmariyù
MS: diyè dapit; madmariyù
You use farther (or further) to compare distances. If one thing is farther from a place than another thing, it is more far (the distance is bigger).
Irreg.  far, farther/fb:further, farthest /fb:furthest.
