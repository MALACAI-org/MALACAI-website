align
/a-line/
 
v-recip.
 
1.
Def.: egpekegsinsinundul; nenekeglenged
MS: egpekegsinsinundul; nenekeglenged
If two or more things align with each other, they are in a particular position with relation to each other (especially in a straight line).
If you stand just here and look at the mountains, all the mountains align with each other to make a straight line.
Ke egsasindeg ka kayi wey egtengteng te me bubungan, langun ne bubungan nenekegsinsinundul ne nalingya.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egpegsinundul; egpeglenged
MS: egpegsinundul; egpeglenged
If you align two things, you cause them to be in a particular position with relation to one another.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: egpanahù te me lalag te tineil ne pegkatahù
MS: egpanahù te me lalag te tineil ne pegkatahù
If you align words on a page, you put them in a particular position (left, right, centred).
Sem.Dom.: extra
Status: ready
