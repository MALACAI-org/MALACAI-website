even1
/ee-vən/
 
adj.
 
1.
Def.: iling (egkahamit seini ke kenè egkabalbalawan wey ke nekeg-iling ka langun ne baad)
MS: iling
Something even is the same at different times or in different parts of it.
She continued at an even speed when she was running, and didn't slow down or speed up.
Migpabulus sikandin te iling ne kakeseg te pegpallahuy, wey warè migmeyimulung wey ke migmakmakeseg.
Try to keep the fire at an even temperature.
 
adj.
 
2.
Def.: nekeg-iling
MS: nekeg-iling
If two things are even, they are equal or at the same level.
The scores of the two teams are even.
Ka nakuwa te daruwa ne grupu nekeg-iling.
 
adj.
 
3.
Def.: mapatag; egpekegseked
MS: mapatag; egpekegseked
An even surface is flat and smooth.
When you write, it is easier if you put the paper on an even surface.
 
adj.
 
4.
Def.: nekeg-atag
MS: nekeg-atag
An even number can be divided by 2 (e.g., 4, 6, 8)
Source: GSL0076-0100
