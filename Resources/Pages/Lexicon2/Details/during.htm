during
/doo-ring/
 
prep.
Def.: te (meyitenged te timpu)
MS: te
You use during to talk about the period of time when something happens.
She woke up three times during the night.
Eggimata sikandin te katatelu te marusilem.
She changed a lot during the three years that she lived with our family.
Dakel ne pegkabale-balew kandin te tatelu ne tuig ne peg-uugpè din diyè te kanami ne pamilya.
Sem.Dom.: prepositions; GSL0151-0175
Status: ready
