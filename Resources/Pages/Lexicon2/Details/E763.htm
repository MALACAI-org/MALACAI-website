current2
/ka-rənt/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: kabeheng te pegduù [pegdu͡ù]
MS: kabeheng te pegduù
a strong flow of water in a river or the sea.
Be careful of the current in the river, because it is very strong.
Bantey ka te kabeheng te weyig su amana ne kekesehi.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: peg-usug te kalamag
MS: peg-usug
a flow of air.
There was a current of air flowing through the house.
Due peg-usug te kalamag diyè te baley.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: peg-usug te kurinti te alambri
MS: peg-usug
a flow of electricity through wires.
It's dangerous to touch wires if they are carrying an electric current.
Dilikadu ka eggen-gen te me wayir ke due kurinti.
 
n-ct.
 
4.
Def.: geyinawa wey puhawang ne diyè te punduk te me etew
a feeling or idea that is there in a group of people.
There were currents of unhappiness within the group.
Due geyinawa te warè pegkahalè te grupu.
Source: GSL0701-0725
