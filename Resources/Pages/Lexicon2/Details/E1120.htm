export
/iks-port (verb); ek-sport/
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: egbelegyè diyè te lein ne nasud
MS: egbelegyè diyè te lein ne nasud
If a group or country exports something, they sell it to another country.
That country exports 30% of its rice to Europe.
Ka sika ne inged egbelegyè te tatelun pulù ne pursintu te behas diyè te Iyurupa.
 
n-uc.
 
2.
Def.: pegbelegyè diyè te lein ne nasud
MS: pegbelegyè
the action of selling to another country.
The export of fruit is important for our country.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: belegyè
MS: belegyè
a product or service that a person or country sells to another country
Beef is an important export for Australia.
 
vt.
 
4.
Def.: egpanahù; egpanggalin
MS: egpanahù; egpanggalin
In computing, if you export a file from one program, you save it so that another program can read it.
