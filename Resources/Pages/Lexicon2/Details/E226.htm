association
/a-soh-shee-yay-shən/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: punduk te me etew ne due pegkabagget te nateil ne minsan nekey
an official group of people who are all interested in a particular thing.
I am a member of an association which researches Philippine history.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: pegbehey te dakel ne timpu; pegduma-ruma
MS: pegbehey te dakel ne timpu; pegduma-ruma
the action of spending time with a person or people.
My parents were not happy about my association with that person.
Ka ginikanan ku kenè egkeupian te pegduma-ruma ku te seika ne etew.
 
n-uc, n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: egpegsumpul wey ke egpeglagpù te daruwa ne butang diyè te suman-suman
the action of connecting two things in your thinking (make an association).
The association of rain with sickness is common.
Ka suman-suman ne egpeglagpù ne ka uran egpakabehey te dalu su amana ne kumun.
Source: GSL0626-0650
