predict
/pri-dikt/
 
vt.
Def.: eg-agseb
MS: eg-agseb
If you predict that something will happen in the future, you say that it will happen.
He predicts that the Sinuda team will win the game.
Mig-agseb sikandin ne ka grupu te Simud egdeeg te kuntis.
Sem.Dom.: AWL4
 
 
prediction
/pri-dik-shən/
 
n-uc.
Def.: peg-agseb
MS: peg-agseb
the action of predicting (make a prediction).
 
n-ct.
Def.: agseb
MS: agseb
something that you say will happen
I do not agree with his prediction that the Sinuda team will win.
