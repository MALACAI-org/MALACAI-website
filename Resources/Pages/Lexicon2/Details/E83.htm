agency
/ay-djən-see/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: me klasiyan te kumpanya ne eggimu te minsan nekey para te etew wey ke lein ne kumpanya
a company which organizes something for another person or company (e.g., employment agency, travel agency, advertising agency).
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: kumpanya ne kuniktadu te gubirnu ne sugpang te dakel ne grupu puun te gubirnu wey ke baad te gubirnu
MS: ahinsiya
ahinsiya
an organization which is connected to the government, or is part of the government (e.g., the PNA—Philippines News Agency).
Note: have you heard of this?
 
n-uc.
 
3.
Def.: pegpeyitabù; pegpeyimu
MS: pegpeyitabù; pegpeyimu
the action of causing something to happen.
This happened independently, without her agency.
Neyitabù seini ne warè pegpangabang din.
Source: GSL0851-0875
