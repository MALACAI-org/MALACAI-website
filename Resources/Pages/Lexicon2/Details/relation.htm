relation
/ri-lay-shən/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: karumaan [karuma͡an] te taltalaanak
MS: karumaan te taltalaanak
a member of your family (e.g., a cousin).
Many of my relations came to my birthday party.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: kalabetan wey ke sumpulan te daruwa ne kasangkapan wey duma pad.
MS: kalabetan; sumpulan
The relation of one thing to another, or the relation between two things is the way that they are connected.
There is no relation between his headache and the pain in his legs.
 
 
relations
 
n-pl.
Def.: geyinawa wey himu ne due kalabetan te daruwa ne grupu
Relations between countries or groups are the communications, feelings and actions between the two groups.
Relations between the two countries are not good at the moment.
Ka pegpanengnengey te daruwa ne nasud kenè ne meupiya te seini kuntee.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0651-0675
