institution
/in-sti-too-shən/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
dakel, impurtanti ne urganisasyun iling te unibirsidad wey ke bangku
a large, important organization such as a university or bank.
The University of Central Mindano is a very large educational institution.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: malu-ag ne ugpaan, iling te prisuwan wey ke tahuanan te me batè ne baley ne due nateil ne aliddanan.
a large place such as a prison or children's home where people stay for a particular reason.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: menà wey ke kukuman (iling te peg-asawa)
MS: menà; kukuman
a custom or law (e.g. marriage).
Marriage is an institution in most societies.
 
n-ct.
 
4.
Def.: pegbunsud te peggamit te minsan nekey (iling te kukuman wey pamaahi)
the action of making and starting to use something such as a rule or way of doing something.
The institution of the new system will take time.
