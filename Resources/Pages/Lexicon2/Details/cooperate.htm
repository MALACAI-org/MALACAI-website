cooperate
/koh-wop-ə-rate/
Var. co-operate
 
v-recip.
 
1.
Def.: egpabulihey
MS: egpabulihey
If you cooperate with someone, you work together with them to do something.
The two brothers cooperated in planting their fields.
Ka daruwa ne tahasuled migpabulihey te pegpamula te kamet dan.
 
vi.
 
2.
Def.: eggimu ke nekey ka igpeyimu
MS: eggimu ke nekey ka igpeyimu
If someone cooperates with you, they do what you tell them to do.
Teaching is difficult if students don't cooperate.
Ka pegpanulù malised ke ka me istudyanti kenè egparuma-ruma.
Sem.Dom.: AWL6

cooperative
/koh-wop-ə-rə-tiv/
 
adj.
Def.: mabulig-bulig; maparuma-ruma
MS: mabulig-bulig; maparuma-ruma
If someone is cooperative, they do what you want them to do.
Teaching is difficult if students are not cooperative.
Ka pegpanulù malised ke ka me istudyanti kenen maparuma-ruma.
