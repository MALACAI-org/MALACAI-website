joint
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: sumpulan
MS: sumpulan
the place where two things connect (e.g., two bones in the body).
 
adj.
 
2.
Def.: impegsumpul
Note: ?
MS: inpegsumpul
You describe something as joint if two people or groups own it or use it.
My husband and I have joint ownership of the house.
