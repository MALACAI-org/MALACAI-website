rest1
 
vi.
 
1.
Def.: eggimeley
MS: eggimeley
If you rest, you stop work, and relax.
I feel tired, so I will rest now.
 
n-uc, n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: peggimmeley
MS: peggimmeley
a state of relaxing, not working (have a rest).
I feel tired, so I will have a rest now.
 
n-uc.
 
3.
Def.: kenè pegweil
MS: kenè pegweil
a state of not moving (be at rest, come to rest).
The ball rolled down the hill and came to rest at my feet.
 
vi.
 
4.
Def.: egpakaampew; egpakarantey; egpakasandig
MS: egpakaampew; egpakarantey; egpakasandig
If something rests on another thing, it is on that other thing.
The fallen tree is resting on the roof.
Her hand rested on the child's head for a moment.
 
vt.
 
5.
Def.: egdantey; eg-ampew; eggiba
MS: egdantey; eg-ampew; eggiba
If you rest something on another thing, you put the weight of it on that other thing.
She rested her head on the table.
She rested the child on her knees, because her arms were tired.
 
n-ct.
 
6.
Def.: tukeg; sandihanan
MS: tukeg; sandihanan
something which is made to support another thing (e.g., a footrest).
The seats on the bus are comfortable because they have headrests, but they usually don't have armrests.
She used the plate as a spoon rest for the dirty spoon.
Source: GSL0451-0475
