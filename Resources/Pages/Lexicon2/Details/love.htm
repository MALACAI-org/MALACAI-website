love
/lav/
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: eggeyinawaan
MS: eggeyinawaan
If you love a person or animal, you have a deep feeling about them, and you want good for them.
 
n-uc.
 
2.
Def.: geyinawa
MS: geyinawa
a deep feeling about a person or animal who is very important to you.
I really appreciate my best friend's love.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: igkeupii te eggimu
MS: igkeupii
If you love something, or love doing something, you like or enjoy it very much
I love reading.
 
n-uc, n-ct.
 
4.
Def.: pegkeupii te peggimu
MS: pegkeupii te peggimu
a feeling of liking or enjoying something very much.
My love of reading started when I was a young child.
Ka pegkeupii ku te pegbasa migbunsud te deisek e pad ne batè.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0301-0325; keyterms
