sentence2
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: eggukuman te nateil ne igbennal
MS: eggukuman te nateil ne igbennal
If a judge sentences you to a particular punishment, they say that you will have that punishment.
The judge sentenced them to 5 years in prison.
Ka talaggukum miggukum kandan te lalimma ne tuig ne pegkaprisu.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: hukum ne egkarawat
MS: hukum ne egkarawat
the punishment that a judge says you must receive.
He is serving a five-year sentence in prison.
Source: GSL1351-1375
