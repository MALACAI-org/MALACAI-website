close1
/klohss/
 
adj.
 
1.
Def.: marani
MS: marani
If one thing is close to another thing, it is near that thing.
My house is close to the school.
Ka baley ku marani rè te iskuylaan [iskuyla͡an].
 
adj.
 
2.
Def.: mahaan e
MS: mahaan e
If a future event is close, it will happen soon.
It's December already! Christmas is close.
Disimbri e, mahaan e ka Pasku.
 
adj.
 
3.
Def.: nekeg-iling
MS: nekeg-iling
If two things are close, they are very similar.
These two insects are very close, so it's hard to see the difference between them.
Ka seini se daruwa ne uled-uled amana ne nekeg-iling, sika ka malised ne egkakita ka kaleinan dan.
 
adj.
 
4.
Def.: marani (meyitenged te pegkaalukuy)
MS: marani
If two people are close, they know and like each other a lot.
She is a close friend of mine.
Marani ne alukuy ku sikandin.
 
adj.
 
5.
Def.: kenè pad iya egkahuhud (meyitenged te karumaan [karuma͡an], iling ke suled, atebey, anggam, wey duma pad)
MS: kenè pad iya egkahuhud
If two relatives are close, the relationship is quite direct, and there are not many relatives in between them.
He's only a third cousin, not a close relative.
Igkatelu rè ne egkasuled sikandin, kenè amana ne marani ne karumaan [karuma͡an].
 
adj.
 
6.
Def.: mapaayad-ayad (meyitenged te pegtengteng)
MS: mapaayad-ayad
A close look at something is careful and detailed.
If you have a close look at this insect, you can see the tiny hairs on its legs.
Source: GSL0201-0225
