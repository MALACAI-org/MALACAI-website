peace
/peess/
 
n-uc.
 
1.
Def.: meupiya ne sitwasyun, ne due meupiya ne rilasyun, peglelawa wey warè kalaggawan; keupianan; kalinew
MS: keupianan; kalinew
a good situation, with good relationships, health and no worries.
She has no peace because she is always worrying about her son.
 
n-uc.
 
2.
Def.: warad ehetanan; warè samuk
MS: warad ehetanan; warè samuk
If there is peace between groups or countries, there is no fighting.
After years of fighting, there was finally peace between the two countries.
 
n-uc.
 
3.
Def.: pegkamalinew te lugar
MS: pegkamalinew te lugar
a state of quietness in a place.
I love walking in the mountains, because I enjoy the peace and quiet there.
Egkeupian a ne egleug-leug te me bubungan, su amana a egkeupian te kalinew wey kenè ne mahulub diyè.
Source: GSL0476-0500; keyterms
 
 
peaceful
/peece-ful/
 
adj.
Def.: malinawen (meyitenged te himu)
MS: malinawen
A peaceful activity does not include fighting
We hope the two countries will find a peaceful solution to the problem.
 
adj.
Def.: malinawen (meyitenged te lugar wey ke timpu)
a peaceful place or time is quiet and not stressful.
The mountain is a peaceful place where I can hear birds and water.
