academic
/æk-a-dem-ik/
 
adj.
 
1.
Def.: due kalabetan te iskuylaan [iskuyla͡an] wey te peg-iskuyla
MS: iskuylaan wey te peg-iskuyla, due kalabetan te
Something academic is related to schools, colleges and study (e.g., the academic year).
The academic year starts in June.
 
adj.
 
2.
Def.: mapuhawang
MS: mapuhawang
Something academic needs study and thinking more than practical skills.
Her course was very academic, as there was a lot of theory.
Ka kursu rin amana ne mapuhawang su masalig ka me suman-suman.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: talagnengneng wey talagpanulù diyè te iskuylaan [iskuyla͡an] te kulihiyu wey unibirsidad
MS: talagnengneng
someone who does research and teaches at a college or university.
