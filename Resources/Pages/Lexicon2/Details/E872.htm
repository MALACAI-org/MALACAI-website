difficult
/diff-i-kəlt/
 
adj.
 
1.
Def.: malised
MS: malised
If something is difficult to do or understand, it is not easy.
It's difficult to see the path in the dark.
Liliseri ka pegtengteng te dalan te marukilem.
His name is difficult to remember.
Malised ka ngaran din ne egsumsumanen.
 
adj.
 
2.
Def.: malasey
MS: malasey
A difficult situation is painful and uncomfortable.
Life is very difficult for them at the moment, because they lost their field.
Malasey ka umul dan kuntee, su nawarè ka kamet dan.
 
adj.
 
3.
Def.: malehen (meyitenged te batasan te etew)
MS: malehen
A difficult person quickly becomes angry, and it is not easy to make them happy.
He is a difficult man, and argues with many people.
Malehen ne etew sikandin, wey egpakig-apul te masalig ne me etew.
Source: GSL0651-0675
