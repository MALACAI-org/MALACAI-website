lots, a lot
 
n-ct, pron.
Def.: masulug; masalig; dakel
MS: masulug; masalig; dakel
a large amount or number of something.
She is not busy, so she has a lot of free time.
Warè himu rin, su masalig ka bakanti ne timpu rin.
She works a lot, so is busy.
Source: pronouns
 
adv.
 
1.
Def.: layun egkeyitabù wey ke amana rè
MS: layun egkeyitabù; egkeyitabù layun; amana rè
If something happens a lot, it happens often, or very much.
My baby cries a lot.
Source: GSL0626-0650
