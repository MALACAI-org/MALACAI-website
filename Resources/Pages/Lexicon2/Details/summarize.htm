summarize
/sam-ə-rize/
 
vt, vi.
Def.: egmalepeten
MS: egmalepeten
If you summarize something, you say or write a summary of it.
She didn't add any new ideas, she just summarized what she had read.
Warè migtimul sikandin te iyam ne puhawang, immalepet din dè ka migbasa rin.
Sem.Dom.: AWL4
Status: ready
