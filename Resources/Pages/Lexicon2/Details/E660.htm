consumption
/kon-samp-shən/
 
n-uc.
 
1.
Def.: pegkeen; peg-inum
MS: pegkeen; peg-inum
the action of eating or drinking.
The consumption of alcohol is not allowed in this area.
Ka peg-inum te egpekewubug ne inumen kenè egkaayun kayi ne inged.
 
n-uc.
 
2.
Def.: pegpameli
MS: pegpameli
the action of many people buying things.
Consumption is less this year, because people have less money.
Ka pegpameli te seini ne leg-un deisek, puun te deisek ka seleppì te me etew.
 
n-uc.
 
3.
Def.: kakeen; kaamin
MS: kakeen; kaamin
the amount of a material (e.g. fuel) that something uses.
Motor bikes have lower fuel consumption than vans.
Ka mutur deisek dè ka egkaamin ne gasulina kunterè te terak.
