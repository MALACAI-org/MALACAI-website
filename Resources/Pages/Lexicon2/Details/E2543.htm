sacrifice
/sæk-rə-fayiss/
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: egsakripisyu te minsan nekey
MS: egsakripisyu
If you sacrifice something that is important to you, you give it, or live without it in order to help someone, or to get another thing.
He sacrificed his free time to help me with my work.
Insakripisyu rin ka bakanti rin ne uras te pegbulig te trabahu ku.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: peglasey-lasey; sakripisyu
MS: peglasey-lasey; sakripisyu
something that you give or live without in order to help someone or get another thing. (make a sacrifice).
My parents made a lot of sacrifices to pay for my schooling.
Ka ginikanan ku miggimu te dakel ne peglasey-lasey eyew te peg-iskuyla ku.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: egpanubad ne eggimatey te mananap
MS: egpanubad
If someone sacrifices an animal, they kill the animal for a god or spirit.
 
n-ct.
 
4.
Def.: pegpanubad ne eggimatey te mananap, wey ke egbehey te minsan nekey diyè te diyus-diyus wey panisingan.
a ceremony in which an animal is killed, or something is given to a god or spirit.
 
n-ct.
 
5.
Def.: impanubaran
MS: impanubaran
the animal which is killed, or the thing which is given for a god or spirit.
