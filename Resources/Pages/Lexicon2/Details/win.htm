win
 
vt, vi.
 
1.
Def.: egdeeg
MS: egdeeg
If you win a race, competition or argument, you get the best result.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egpakaangey te unag
MS: egpakaangey te unag
If you win a prize, you receive a prize in some kind of competition.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: pegdeeg
MS: pegdeeg
an action of getting the best result in a competition.
Our team had seven wins during the season.
 
vt.
 
4.
Def.: egpakaalig wey ke egpakapeupian te me etew
If you win people, you cause them to like you.
The politician won a lot of support when he visited the town.
She won a lot of sympathy when she told people her sad story.
Irreg.  won, won.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0601-0625
Status: ready
