reliant
/ri-layi-yənt/
 
adj.
Def.: keilangan su egpakabulig; egsalig
MS: keilangan su egpakabulig; egsalig
If you are reliant on someone or something, you need them because they help you in some way.
My family is reliant on our garden for food.
Sikanami se taltalaanak egsalig dè diyè te pamulaan ney para te pegkeen ney.
See rely
Source: AWL3
 
 
reliance
/ri-layi-yənss/
 
n-uc.
Def.: pegkakeilangan
MS: pegkakeilangan
the state of being reliant on someone or something.
I am worried about her reliance on me. She needs to become independent.
