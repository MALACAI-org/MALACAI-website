bottom
/bot-əm/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: lebut; igtubù
MS: lebut; igtubù
The bottom of something is the lowest part of it, or the part which it sits or stands on.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: diralem te weyig (ka egkasadsad tad ne tanè wey batu)
MS: diralem te weyig
The bottom of a body of water such as the sea or a river is the ground under the water.
I can see my ring there on the bottom of the river.
Egkakita ka sising ku diyè te diralem te weyig.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: piyus
MS: piyus
Your bottom is your buttocks, the part of the body which you sit on.
I've been sitting down for many hours, so my bottom is sore.
Dakel ne uras ka pegpinnuu ku, sika ka masakit e ka piyus ku.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0976-1000
Status: ready
