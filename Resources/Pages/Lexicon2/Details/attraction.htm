attraction
/a-træk-shən/
 
n-uc.
 
1.
Def.: 1 pegpakabagget; 2 pegpekewusud; pegpakasupsup; pegpakahanuy
MS: pegpakabagget; pegpekewusud; pegpakasupsup; pegpakahanuy
the action of attracting something.
Note: see 'attract' sense 1,2
 
n-uc, n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: pegkeupian; pegkeupii (te malitan wey lukes)
MS: pegkeupian; pegkeupii
a feeling of liking someone of the opposite sex.
I felt a strong attraction to him.
Miggeram a te amana ne pegkeupian kandin.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: minsan nekey ne egleuyen te me etew eyew te pegpitew wey ke peggimu
something which people visit a place to see or do (e.g., a zipline).
Sem.Dom.: GSL1826-1850
