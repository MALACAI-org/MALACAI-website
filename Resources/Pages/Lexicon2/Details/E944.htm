draft2
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: hiyup te maagsil ne kalamag diyè te sinabeng
MS: hiyup
a flow of cold air in a room.
I was cold because of the draft from the door, so I moved my chair.
Naagsil [na͡agsil] a te hiyup te kalamag puun te gumawan, sikak mig-ensig ku ka bangku ku.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: karakelan te minsan nekey ne eg-inumen iling te bawì
an amount of something that you drink, such as medicine.
She prepared a draft of cough medicine for the child.
Migtahahè sikandin te eleg ne karakel te bawì te buwè te anak din.
She tipped back her head and took a long draft of the fruit drink.
 
adj.
 
3.
Def.: ka mananap ne egpangguyud te mabehat ne alapen
A draft animal is one which pulls heavy loads (e.g., a carabao).
