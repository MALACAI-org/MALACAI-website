integrate
/in-tə-grate/
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: egpegtimul; egpegsewug
MS: egpegtimul; egpegsewug
If you integrate one thing with another, you join those things together so that they become one.
They integrated the preschool with the elementary school to make a larger school.
 
vi.
 
2.
Def.: egsewug, eg-apil wey eg-iling
MS: egsewug; eg-apil; eg-iling
If you integrate into a group of people, you become part of that group, and feel comfortable in that group.
When they went abroad, it was very difficult for them to integrate into the new culture.
Te peggendiyè dan te lein ne inged malised amana kandan ka egsewug wey ke eg-iling te iyam ne pegbebatasan.
Sem.Dom.: AWL4

/in-tə-gray-shən/
 
n-uc.
