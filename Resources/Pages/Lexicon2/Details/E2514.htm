rock2
 
vi.
 
1.
Def.: egkaanluwag; egkatali; egkaantuwal; egkabiyal
MS: egkaanluwag; egkaantuwal; egkabiyal
If a person or thing rocks, they move from side to side, or backwards and forwards.
The table rocks because one leg is too short.
Egkaantuwal ka lamisa su malepet ka sabeka ne paa.
The small boat rocked when I got into it.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: eg-antuwal; eg-anluwag; egbiyal
MS: eg-antuwal; eg-anluwag; egbiyal
If you rock something, you cause it to move from side to side, or backwards and forwards.
She rocked the baby in her arms to make him sleep.
In-anluwag din ka malalab pad ne batè diyè te belad eyew egpakalipereng.
We rocked the car to try to move it.
Source: GSL0776-0800
