table
/tay-bəl/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: lamisa
MS: lamisa
Def.: a flat board with one or more legs. People often sit at a table to do various activities such as eating.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: migsulat ne kanengnengan ne natapid ne migpunduk-punduk
written information which is arranged in rows
The pronouns are listed in a table.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: eglalahen eg-ay-ayari diyè te me etew te timpu te eglibulung
If you table an idea, you say formally that you want people to talk about it at a meeting.
They tabled a proposal to build a new road.
 
vt.
 
4.
Def.: egtahù pad seup e eglalaha
MS: egtahù pad seup e eglalaha
If you table an idea, you decide to talk about it another time, not now.
Let's table that for the next meeting.
Source: GSL0451-0475
