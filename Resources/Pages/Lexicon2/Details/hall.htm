hall
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: libulungan ne baley
MS: libulungan
a large room or building for public events.
We had the singing contest in the hall.
Due kuntis te ulahingen diyè te libulunganan ne baley.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: malayat ne bayaanan ne paseled diyè te baley
In a large house, a hall or hallway is a long room which you pass through to enter other rooms.
She walked along the hall and opened the door to her room.
Miggipanew sikandin diyè te bayaanan ne paseled te baley wey miglukatan din ka gemawan te sinabeng din.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0701-0725
Status: ready
