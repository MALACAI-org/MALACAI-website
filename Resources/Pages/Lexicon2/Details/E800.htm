decision
/də-sish-ən/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: puhawang ne mig-alam
MS: puhawang ne mig-alam
the thing that you choose to do (make a decision).
I don't agree with his decision to sell his carabao.
The Barangay is going to make a decision about the new water pipe soon.
 
n-uc.
 
2.
Def.: katuenan te pegbehey te puhawang ne meiyal wey siguradu meyitenged te suman-suman din
the ability to decide things quickly and feel sure about them.
She lacks decision, so it is difficult for her to decide things.
Kulang sikandin te katuenan te pegbehey te meiyal ne puhawang puun te suman-suman, sika ka malised kandin ka pegdisisyun.
Source: GSL0601-0625
