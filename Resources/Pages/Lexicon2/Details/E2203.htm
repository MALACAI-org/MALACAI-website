post1
/pohst/
 
n-uc.
 
1.
Def.: pamaahi te pegpeuyan te sulat
MS: pamaahi te pegpeuyan te sulat
a country's system for sending letters and parcels.
He sent me a letter by post.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egpeuyan te sulat
MS: egpeuyan
If you post a letter or parcel you put a stamp on it and send it.
He posted a letter to me.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: egtahù diyè te tahuanan ne masalig ne etew ka egpakabasa
If you post something such as an advertisement, you put it in a place where many people can read it.
The teacher posted the list of winners on the wall.
Source: GSL0801-0825
