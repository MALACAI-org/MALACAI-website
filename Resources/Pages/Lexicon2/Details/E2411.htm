reject
/ri-djekt (verb), ree-djekt (noun)/
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: eglalag wey ke egpakita te kenè pegkeupii
If you reject someone, you say or show that you don't want to have a relationship with them.
His friends rejected him because he told them many lies.
Ka me alukuy rin migpakita te kenè pegkeupii kandin su layun din eg-ub-ubati.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egsamilì
MS: egsamilì
If you reject something, you decide not to have it or use it.
The fish-cart seller rejected my fish, because they were too small.
Ka egpamelegyè te ngalap te weyig migsamilian ka ngalap ku, su dereisek.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: igdegpak e wey kenad egkahamit ne minsan nekey
something that you throw away or decide not to use.
These small fish are cheap because they are rejects.
Baratu e seini se dereisek ne isda su igdegpak e su kenad egkahamit.
Source: AWL5
