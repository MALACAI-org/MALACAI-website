job
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: himu; trabahu
MS: himu; trabahu
the work that you do to earn money.
She lives in Malaybalay because she has a job in a shop there.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: senge himuwan; sabeka ne himu
MS: senge himuwan; himu
one piece of work.
It was a big job to cut down the large tree.
