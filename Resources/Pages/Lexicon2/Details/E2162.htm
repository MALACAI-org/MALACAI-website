plant
/plæhnt/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: pinamula
MS: pinamula
a living thing which has parts such as leaves, which can't move alone, and which uses light to make its food.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egpamula
MS: egpamula
If you plant something such as a seed or a plant, you put it in the ground so that it will grow.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: egpanukaddel
MS: egpanukaddel
If you plant something in a place, you put it there strongly.
She planted her feet firmly and tried to push the carabao out of the way.
Migpataman-taman sikandin migpanukaddel wey miggeraman din te egtukù ka kalabew paawè te dalan.
 
n-uc.
 
4.
Def.: ka me derakel ne me baley wey me mekinaryas ne eggamiten eyew te himu.
the buildings and machines which are used for work (e.g., on farms or in factories).
 
n-ct.
 
5.
Def.: ka lugar ne iling te kumpanya ne himuwanan, ne egkeyimuwan wey ke egpuunan te minsan nekey.
a place such as a factory, where things are produced (e.g., a power plant).
Source: GSL0451-0475
