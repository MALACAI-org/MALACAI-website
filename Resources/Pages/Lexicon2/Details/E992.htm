effective
/i-fek-tiv/
 
adj.
 
1.
Def.: manedted; bagnel
MS: manedted; bagnel
If something is effective, it produces the result that you want it to.
A mosquito net is the most effective way of protecting yourself from mosquitoes at night.
 
adj.
 
2.
Def.: egbunsud te nateil ne pitsa (iling te balaud wey sistima)
MS: egbunsud te nateil ne pitsa
If a rule or system is effective from a particular date, that is the time when it starts.
The new bus prices will be effective from next month.
Ka iyam ne bali te terak ne bas egbunsud te seup ne simana.
Source: GSL0551-0575
 
 
ineffective
 
adj.
not effective (sense 1).
Status: ready
