especially
/i-spesh-ə-lee/
 
adv.
 
1.
Def.: labi en iya
MS: labi en iya
You use especially to say that something is very true or important for a particular group or situation.
You need to keep drinking water, especially if you are working outside.
Keilangan ne layun ka eg-inum te weyig, labi en iya ke egtrabahu ka diyè te lihawangan.
Amoeba is a dangerous illness, especially for children.
 
adv.
 
2.
Def.: Egkahamit seini ke eglalag ka te leleheti.
You use especially to say that something is very true.
Today is an especially hot day.
Kuntee ka iiniti ne aldew.
Source: adverbs; GSL0651-0675
