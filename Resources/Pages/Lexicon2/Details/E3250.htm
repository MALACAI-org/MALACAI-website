will2
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: katuenan te peg-alam te igkeupii wey pegbeye-beyè te me weil
Your will is your ability to choose what you want to do, and to control your actions.
He has a very strong will, and so I can't make him change his mind.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: igkeupii
MS: igkeupii
Your will is the thing that you want to happen.
Excellent sportsmen have the will to win.
In a democracy, there are elections to find out the will of the people.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: dukumintu te impangamuney ne kasangkapan
the document that you leave when you die, to say who will receive your money and things.
It's important to write a will, so that your family knows how to divide your property.
Source: GSL0026-0050
