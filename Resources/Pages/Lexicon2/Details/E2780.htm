spite
 
 
in spite of
 
conj.
Def.: minsan pad...piru
MS: minsan pad...piru
You use in spite of to say that one fact is not changed or affected by another fact.
In spite of loving sport, she doesn't play much now.
Minsan pad indakelan din te geyinawa ka peggehalew, piru kenad amana eggalew sikandin.
Source: conjunctions; GSL1351-1375
