possible
/poss-i-bəl/
 
adj.
 
1.
Def.: egkeyimu; egkaayun
MS: egkeyimu; egkaayun
If it is possible to do something, you can do it.
It's not possible to swim across the Pacific Ocean.
Kenè egkaayun ne egpamalasen dè te eglumbuk ka peglapas te dahat.
 
adj.
 
2.
Def.: hendue te; kema ke
MS: hendue te; kema ke
If it is possible that something is the case, maybe it is the case.
It's possible that she still lives there, but I really don't know.
Hendue te diyè pad mig-ugpè sikandin, piru warè ad amana nakataha.
 
adj.
 
3.
Def.: egkeyitabù
MS: egkeyitabù
If it is possible that something will happen, maybe it will happen.
It's possible that we will buy a horse next month.
Egkeyitabù e ne egpakabebeli kid e te kuddè te seup ne bulan.
Source: GSL0151-0175
