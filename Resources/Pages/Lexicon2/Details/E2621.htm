sequence
/see-kwənss/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: pegkasunud-sunud; pegkateil
MS: pegkasunud-sunud; pegkateil
an arrangement of things in a particular order (with one thing first, another thing second, and so on...).
What is the sequence of songs on the CD?
Nekey ka pegkasunud-sunud te me ulahingen due te plaka?
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: sunud-sunud ne egkeyitabù
a group of things which happen in a particular order.
She told me about the sequence of events which had happened.
Migkahiyan a nikandin te sunud-sunud ne neyitabù.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: egpegsunud-sunud
MS: egpegsunud-sunud
If you sequence things, you put them in a particular order.
She sequenced the words in alphabetical order.
Source: AWL3
