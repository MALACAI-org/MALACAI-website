ratio
/ray-shi-yoh/
 
n-ct.
Def.: kasuluhan wey ke karakelan ne igpeg-iling te tagsè sabeka
the size of two numbers or amounts compared with each other.
There are twenty girls and ten boys in our class, so the ratio of girls to boys is two to one.
Due daruwan pulu ne malitan wey sapulu ne me lukes diyè te klasi ney, su ka kasuluhan te malitan wey lukes daruwa ne malitan te sabeka ne lukes.
