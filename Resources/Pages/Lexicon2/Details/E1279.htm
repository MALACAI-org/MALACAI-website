fundamental
/fan-da-men-təl/
 
adj, n-ct.
Def.: malemu wey keilanganen ne egnengnengen
MS: malemu wey keilanganen
If something is fundamental to a particular subject, it is one of the simple and important things that you need to know about that subject.
The multiplication table is fundamental to mathematics, so you should learn it well.
Ka multiplikisyun tibul su ka malemu wey keilangan te matimatiks, sika ka keilangan ne egbebasbasan seini eg-upiani.
I know the fundamentals of that language, but I can't really understand much.
Kateu a te malemu wey keilangan te sika ne linalahan, piru kena a amana egpakasabut.
