another
/a-na-ther/
 
det, pron.
Def.: duma pad; esa pad; lein pad
MS: duma pad; esa pad; lein pad
Another thing is one more thing that is similar.
Would you like another cup of coffee?
Egkeupian ka te lein pad ne keppi?
You've finished your cup of coffee. Would you like another?
Neimpusan nud ka keppi nu. Keupian ke pad te lein? (wey ke egtimul ke pad?)
This cup is broken, so I'll bring you another one.
Seini ne basu nabuang, egkuan kad te duma ne basu.
 
 
one another
 
pron.
Def.: egpabalbalesey
MS: egpabalbalesey
If two or more people do something to one another, the first person does that thing to the second person, and the second person does it to the first person.
My neighbor and I help one another with our gardens.
Migpabulbulihey key te sumbaley ku te pegtrabahu te gulayan ney.
Sem.Dom.: pronouns; GSL0126-0150
Status: ready
