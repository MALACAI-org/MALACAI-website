come
/kam/
 
vi.
 
1.
Def.: eggendini; eggengkayi
MS: eggendini; eggengkayi
If someone or something comes, it moves closer to the place where you are.
Come here!
Hendini ka!
 
vi.
 
2.
Def.: eglepew; egginguma
MS: eglepew; egginguma
If you come, you arrive.
They came at about 7pm.
Migginguma sikandan te me alas siti te marusilem.
 
vi.
 
3.
Def.: egtaman; egkataman
MS: egtaman; egkataman
If something comes to a certain point, that is where it reaches or stops.
The river is not deep: it only comes up to my knees.
Ka dakel ne weyig kenè ne maralem, taman dè seini te buel ku.
 
vi.
 
4.
Def.: egpuun
MS: egpuun
If something comes from a place, or comes from another thing, that is the place where it is from, or the thing which it is made from.
This coffee comes from Luzon.
Coffee comes from coffee beans.
Ka keppi egpuun te balatung.
Irreg. v.  came [kaim], come [kam].
Source: GSL0051-0075
