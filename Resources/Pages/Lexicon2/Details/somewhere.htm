somewhere
/sam-wair/
 
adv.
 
1.
Def.: minsan hendei; hendei buwa
MS: minsan hendei; hendei buwa
You use somewhere about a place if you don't know or don't say exactly where it is.
Please put your bag somewhere and come and sit down.
Palihug itahù nu ka baag [ba͡ag] nu minsan hendei wey hendini ka wey pinnuu ka kayi.
Where is my coffee? I put it down somewhere.
Hendei ka kepi ku? Hendei ku buwa ikatahù gana sika.
Sem.Dom.: adverbs; GSL1276-1300
Note: include because basic
Status: ready
