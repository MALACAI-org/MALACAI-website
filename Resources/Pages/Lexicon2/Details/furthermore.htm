furthermore
/fer-ther-more/
 
adv.
Def.: labet pad maa; labet pad due
MS: labet pad maa; labet pad due
You use furthermore to add another idea or fact to what you have just said.
I don't like those flowers because they are ugly. Furthermore, they have sharp thorns.
Kena a egkeupiyan te seeyè ne me bulak su mareet. Labet pad man e, memaggalang se duhi.
Sem.Dom.: adverbs; AWL6
Status: ready
