well2
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: belun
MS: belun
a hole in the ground from which you get water
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: lungag ne maralem ne eg-angayan te gesulina wey ke gas
MS: tanil
tanil
a deep hole in the ground from which you get oil or gas.
 
vi.
 
3.
Def.: egsuwak; egpanihis
MS: egsuwak; egpanihis
If a liquid wells up, it flows up or out from a place (e.g., tears from your eyes, or water from a well).
If you dig a hole in the ground near a spring, water will well up.
Source: GSL0076-0100
