beat
/beet/
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: egbahubal; egbahung; egpamayupes; egtepung (meyitenged te minsan nekey)
MS: egbahubal; egbahung; egpamayupes; egtepung
If you beat something, you hit it many times.
She beat the blanket because it had a lot of dust in it.
Impamayupes din ka ulesen su due dakel ne alinepung.
He used a stick to beat the gong.
Miggamit sikandin te lampes te pegbahung te ahung.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: eglampes; eggumi; egbahung; egbahubal (meyitenged te etew wey ke mananap)
MS: eglampes; eggumi; egbahung; egbahubal
If you beat a person or animal, you hit them hard many times, maybe using a stick.
He is a kind man who never beats his children.
Amana ne meupiya sikandin ne etew su warad iya miglampes te me anak din.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: egkulibbul
MS: egkulibbul
If you beat a soft or liquid food such as eggs, you stir it very, very quickly.
 
vi.
 
4.
Def.: egketu-ketù
MS: egketu-ketù; egpitik
egpitik
If something such as your heart beats, it makes a regular sound or movement.
I was so scared that I could feel my heart beating loudly.
Amana a naaldek [na͡aldek] su narineg ku ka makeseg ne pegketu-ketu te pusung ku.
 
n-ct.
 
5.
Def.: makeseg, kenè dè ne manekal wey kenè dè man e ne mananey wey ke pegweil, labi en iya diyè te ulahingen
MS: biit
biit [bi͡it]
a strong, regular sound or movement, especially in music.
They clapped with the beat of the music.
 
vt.
 
6.
Def.: egtalu; eglupig
MS: egtalu; eglupig
If you or your team beats another person or team in a game or competition, you win the game or get a better result than them.
Our school beat all the other schools in the intramural basketball competition.
Irreg.  beat, beaten.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0976-1000
