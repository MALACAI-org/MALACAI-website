record
/ri-kord (v); rek-ord (n)/
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: egpanahù (meyitenged te pegsulat); eglista
MS: egpanahù; eglista
If you record something, you write it down to show that it has really happened, or to help people to remember.
She recorded their payments in the accounting book.
Inlista rin ka bayaranen dan diyè te libru ne listaanan [lista͡anan] te seleppì.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: nakatahù ne kanengnenganan (meyitenged te nasulat); listaan [lista͡an]
MS: nakatahù ne kanengnenganan; kanengnenganan, nakatahù ne; listaan
a piece of writing which shows what happened.
She keeps careful records of who comes to the Barangay Hall.
Amana sikandin eglista ke hentew seeyè se eggendiyè te Barangay Hall.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: egtahù te ulahingen wey ke minsan nekey (meyitenged te peggalin wey peg-iling)
MS: egtahù te ulahingen; egrikurd
egrikurd
If you record sounds, you put them onto something such as a computer or CD, so that you can play them again and again.
We recorded his song using an MP3 player.
Migrikurd key te kanta rin ne iyan ney inggamit ka MP3 ne pliyir.
 
n-ct, adj.
 
4.
Def.: kinameupiyaan wey marelag dè ne neyimu diyè te nateil ne weil
the best result ever for a particular action (e.g., a world record)
He broke the world record for the 100m sprint at the last Olympics, and set a new record.
Nahuhus din ka tetelesi ne neyimu te intiru ne kalibutan ne iyan sika se pegpallahuy te sabeka ne gatus ne mitrus diyè te katamanan ne Ulimpik, wey duen e man-e iyam ne lista.
Wow, we caught 43 fish! That's a record.
Huwi, nekeutel key te hep-at ne pulu wey tatelu ne ngalap! Seini ka kinameupiyaan wey marelag dè ne egkeyitabù.
He finished his dinner in record time, and ran outside to play with his friends.
Nakapenga sikandin ka miglabung sumalè te uras ne egpekeimpus sikandin, wey migpallahuy e ka egpakiggalew te me alukuy rin.
