volume
/voll-yoom/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: karakelan te minsan nekey, wey ke kamenu karakel ka kaluahan ne egkahamit
MS: karakelan; kamenu karakel ka kaluahan
the amount of something, or how much space it uses.
A large volume of traffic travels along this road.
Ka masulug ne karakelan te trapik ne nekeusug te kelesadda.
What is the volume of water in that bucket?
Kamenu karakel ka weyig sika te baldi?
 
n-uc.
 
2.
Def.: kamenu keemen [ke͡emmen] ka dahing
MS: kamenu keemen
how loud a sound is.
Could you please turn down the volume of the music? It's too loud.
Kaayun ubag ne peyimulungi nu rè ka dahing te musika nu. Amana ne meemen [me͡emen].
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: libru
MS: libru
a book.
