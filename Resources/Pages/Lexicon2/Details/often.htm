often
/off-ən/
 
adv.
Def.: dahayun; malasi
MS: dahayun; malasi
If something happens often, it happens many times, with not a long wait between these times.
I often forget my friend's birthday.
Malasi ku egkalingawi ka aldew ne neetawan te alukuy ku.
Sem.Dom.: adverbs; GSL0276-0300
Status: ready
