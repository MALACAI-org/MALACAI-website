applaud
/a-plawd/
 
vt, vi.
 
1.
Def.: egpalakpak wey egkuleyi puun te amana natelesi te egtengtengan wey egpamminehen
If a group of people applaud someone, each person claps their hands together to show that they like something that the person has done.
The audience applauded the performers at the end of the concert.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egdayan; egdeyrey
MS: egdayan; egdeyrey
If you applaud an action or attitude of a person, you say that their action or attitude was very good.
The police applauded the shop assistant for his courage during the robbery.
Migdayan te pulis ka suluhuanen te tindaan [tinda͡an] te kabulut din te migtakawan ka tindaan [tinda͡an].
Source: GSL2101-2125
