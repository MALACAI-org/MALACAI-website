term2
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: katammanan ne timpu (meyitenged te aldew; uras; simana; bulan wey ke tuig)
MS: katammanan ne timpu
a fixed period of time (e.g., a president's term of office, a prison term).
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: sabeka ne baad te leg-un diyè te iskuylaan
MS: baad te leg-un diyè te iskuylaan
one part of a year at a school or college, between vacations or breaks.
Our school year is divided into 2 terms.
Source: GSL0426-0450
