usual
/yoo-shoo-wəl/
 
adj, n-uc.
Def.: naanaran; nalayaman; meyilut
MS: naanaran; nalayaman; meyilut
If something is usual, it is the same as what often happens.
I just had my usual breakfast: rice with sardines.
Miglem-ag a te nalayaman ku: hemey wey tinapa.
Not smoking inside is a usual regulation in the church.
Meyilut ne kukuman te simbaan ka kenè egpanihupan diyè te seled.
Source: GSL0276-0300
