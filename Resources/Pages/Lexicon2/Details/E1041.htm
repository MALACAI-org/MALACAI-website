entry
/en-tree/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: peglasud; pegseled
MS: peglasud; pegseled
the action of entering a place.
Nobody noticed her entry into the room.
They refused him entry to the shop because he was very aggressive.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: minsan nekey ne igpewun-a ne igbehey diyè te eg-uyan te pegpapitawey te katuenan eyew te peg-apil (iling te litratu wey ke ulahingen naa)
something (e.g., a picture or song) which you put in a competition.
If you want to join the drawing competition, you need to give us your entry before the end of the month.
There were 23 entries in the singing competition.
Due daruwan ne pulù ne migpalista eyew te peg-apil te pegpapitawey te katuenan te ulahingen.
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: sinulat wey ke kanengnengan diyè te libru wey ke kumpyutir
a piece of writing or information which someone has written in a book or typed into a computer.
She writes an entry in her journal every night.
