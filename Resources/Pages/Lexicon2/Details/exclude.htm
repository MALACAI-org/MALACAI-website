exclude
/iks-klood/
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: kenè egpaseled wey egpaapil
MS: kenè egpaseled wey egpaapil
If you exclude someone from something, you do not allow them to enter or join that thing.
He was sad because the other children excluded him from their game.
Amana ne nalangè sikandin su ka duma ne me batè warè migpaseled kandin te galew ran.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egpagawas; kenè iglabet
MS: egpagawas; kenè iglabet
If you exclude something from another thing, you do not allow it to be part of that thing.
They are going to exclude disco parties from the fiesta program this year.
Kenè dan igpalabet ka sayawan te prugrama ran te pista te seini ne tuig.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: egpakasiguru ke malehet naa wey kenè ka minsan nekey
If you exclude something that you can choose, you become sure that it is not a correct or good choice.
We have excluded the possibility that the dog was poisoned. It must have just got sick.
Nakasihuru kid ne ka asu ware hiluwi, Migderalu bu rè.
Sem.Dom.: AWL3
 
 
exclusion
/iks-kloo-shən/
Def.: 1 kenè egpaseled wey egpaapil; 2 pegpagawas; 3 pegpakasiguru ke malehet naa wey kenè ka minsan nekey
MS: kenè egpaseled wey egpaapil; pegpagawas; pegpakasiguru ke malehet naa wey kenè ka minsan nekey
the action of excluding someone or something.
He was sad about his exclusion from the game.
