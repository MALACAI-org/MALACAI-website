draw
 
vt, vi.
 
1.
Def.: egsulat te nateil ne ulaula
MS: egsulat te nateil ne ulaula
If you draw a picture of something, you use a pencil and paper to show what it looks like.
Can you draw a map to show me where it is?
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egganuy; egtuyuk
MS: egganuy; egtuyuk
If something draws another thing, it pulls that thing.
The carabao drew the cart along.
She drew the child close to her side.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: eggusud; egbarut
MS: eggusud; egbarut
If you draw something out of another thing, you pull it out of that thing.
He drew the gun out of the holster.
He drew a pencil out of his pocket.
 
vt.
 
4.
Def.: egkuwa wey ke eg-angey te minsan nekey
MS: egkuwa; eg-angey
If you draw something from somewhere, you get that thing from that place (e.g., water from a well).
We draw our water from the spring.
The writer drew many of her ideas from her own life experiences.
 
vt.
 
5.
Def.: egpekeyinggat; egpakabagging; egpakabagget; egpekeimma
MS: egpekeyinggat; egpakabagging; egpakabagget; egpekeimma
If something draws you, it attracts you, so that you want to go to it.
The light is drawing a lot of moths.
The singing competition drew a large audience.
 
n-ct.
 
6.
Def.: galew ne eggusud ka te pepil diyè te tahuanan ne warè nu nakita
MS: bunut-bunut
bunut-bunut
a competition in which someone pulls a ticket out of a container without looking, to find a winner (a lottery).
 
vi.
 
7.
Def.: egpekeg-iling ka nakuwa te tagsè punduk te kelag; egkatabla
MS: egpekeg-iling ka nakuwa; egkatabla
If two sports teams draw, they finish with equal scores in the game.
 
n-ct.
 
8.
Def.: nekeg-iling ka nakuwa te tagsè punduk te kelag; tabla
MS: nekeg-iling ka nakuwa; tabla
the result in a game where the two teams finish with equal scores.
 
vi.
 
9.
Def.: egpakarulug wey ke egpakapalundani te nateil ne pegkatahù
If something draws to a particular position, it comes to that position.
He is drawing closer to the leader in the race, but I don't think he will overtake him.
Egpakarulug e sikandin te uluwan te lumba, piru kena a egpalintutuu ne egkaliyuwan din ka uluwan.
The motorbike drew to a stop.
Irreg.  drew, drawn.
