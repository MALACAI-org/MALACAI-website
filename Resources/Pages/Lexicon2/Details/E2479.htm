return
/ri-tern/
 
vi.
 
1.
Def.: eglibed
MS: eglibed
If you return to a place where you were before, you go back there.
We are going to Valencia, but we'll return later today.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: peglibed diyè te lugar
MS: peglibed diyè te lugar
the action of going back to a place where you were before.
Everyone was very happy about my brother's return to our village.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: eg-uli
MS: eg-uli
If you return something, you take it back to the owner or to the place where it was before.
Thank you for letting me borrow your bolo. I'll return it later today.
She returned the cup to the kitchen after she finished the coffee.
 
vt.
 
4.
Def.: egpakabehey wey egpakapalibed te seleppì
If work or business returns money, it produces money for you.
His new business is not returning much money, so maybe he will need to stop.
 
n-ct.
 
5.
Def.: nakuwa wey nakalibed ne seleppì
MS: seleppì ne nakuwa wey nakalibed
the money that you get from work or business
His vegetables are very good quality, so he gets high returns when he sells them.
Source: GSL0276-0300
