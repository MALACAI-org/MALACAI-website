send
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: egpeuyan
MS: egpeuyan
If you send someone something, or send something to someone, you cause it to go to them.
She sent me a letter.
Her brother was going to Davao, so she sent the gift with him.
She sent a text message to me.
The teacher sent the student to the principal's office.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egpekeyimu
MS: egpekeyimu
If something sends you ADJECTIVE, or sends you to a particular state, it causes you to be in that state.
That movie is so boring that it always sends me to sleep.
Ka sika ne salida su makapepela sika naa ka egpakapalipereng keddì.
She had a bad temper, and even small things could send her into a terrible mood.
Irreg. v.  sent, sent.
Source: GSL0426-0450
