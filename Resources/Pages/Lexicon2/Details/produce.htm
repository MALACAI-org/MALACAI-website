produce
/prə-dooss (v), prod-dooss (n)/
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: egpuunan te lein ne butang
MS: egpuunan
If something produces another thing, that other thing comes out of it.
The boiling water produced steam.
Ka egkale-kalè ne weyig egbubukè.
The tree is producing fruit now.
Ka kayu egpamehas e kuntee.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egpangimu
MS: egpangimu
If you produce something thing, you make it.
That writer produces one new book every year.
That factory produces swimwear.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: egpapitew (meyitenged te ID)
MS: egpapitew
If you produce something such as ID (identification), you show it to someone.
We needed to produce identification in order to enter the buildling.
 
vt.
 
4.
Def.: egpangimu wey eglibulung te salida
MS: egpangimu wey eglibulung te salida
If someone produces a movie, they are the one who organizes everything to make the movie.
 
n-uc.
 
5.
Def.: belegyè ne prutas wey gulay
MS: prutas; gulay
fruit and vegetables for sale.
There was wonderful fresh produce for sale at the market.
