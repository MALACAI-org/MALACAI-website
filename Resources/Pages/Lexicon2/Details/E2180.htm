police
/pə-leess/
 
n-pl.
 
1.
Def.: me talagbantey te kalinew; pulis
MS: me talagbantey te kalinew; pulis
the official group in a country who make sure that people obey the law. One member of this group is called a police officer.
He called the police.
Twenty police came to the town.
Source: plural nouns
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egpaaddat te kukuman
MS: egpaaddat te kukuman
If people police a rule or place, they make sure that other people obey the rule.
Teachers police the rule about no drugs.
It is difficult to police that area, because it is a dangerous place.
Source: GSL0676-0700
