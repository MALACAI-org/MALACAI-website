pass
 
vt.
 
1.
Def.: egbayè; eglihad
MS: egbayè; eglihad
If you pass something, or go past it, you move from one side of it to the other.
On your way to Valencia, you pass CMU.
The bullet passed right through his hand.
We passed through the mountains to get to the village.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egbehey; egtaruwey
MS: egbehey; egtaruwey
If you pass something to someone, or pass someone something, you use your hand to move it to them.
Could you please pass me the salt.
 
vt, vi.
 
3.
Def.: egpasar; egpakapasar
MS: egpasar; egpakapasar
If you pass a test or exam, or pass in a subject, your score is good enough.
I'm happy because I passed in all my subjects.
 
vi.
 
4.
Def.: eglihad (meyitenged te timpu)
MS: eglihad
If time passes, it goes by.
Many years passed before they saw one another again.
 
vt.
 
5.
Def.: egdawat te balaud
MS: egdawat te balaud
If a law-making group passes a law, they accept it so that it can become a law for everybody.
Source: GSL0326-0350
