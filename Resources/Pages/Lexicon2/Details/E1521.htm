individual
/in-di-vi-djoo-wəl/
 
adj.
 
1.
Def.: tagsè sabeka
MS: tagsè sabeka
An individual thing is one thing which is separate from other things in the group.
We did not have individual plates, and we all took food from one big plate.
Warè platu para te tagsè sabeka kanta, egpatuheney ki rè sika te dakel ne platu.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: sabeka ne etew
MS: sabeka ne etew
one person.
Source: AWL1
