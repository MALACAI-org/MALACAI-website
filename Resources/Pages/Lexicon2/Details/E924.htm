diverse
/dayi-verss/
 
adj.
Def.: masalig ne klasiyan
MS: masalig ne klasiyan
If a group of things or people is diverse, there are many different kinds of things or people.
There is a diverse range of birds in the forest.
Due masalig ne klasiyan te bayaku te katalunan.
Source: AWL6
 
 
diversity
/dayi-verss-ə-tee/
 
n-uc.
Def.: kalkalasiyan
MS: kalkalasiyan
the state of being diverse.
I enjoy the diversity of the birds in the forest.
