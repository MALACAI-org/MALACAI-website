happy
/hæp-ee/
 
adj.
 
1.
Def.: nahalè
MS: nahalè
If you are happy, you have good feelings.
 
adj.
 
2.
Def.: due kahalaan
MS: due kahalaan
If something such as a time or a relationship is happy, it causes you to have good feelings.
They have a happy marriage.
We had a very happy day at the river.
 
adj.
 
3.
Def.: warè prublima te peggimu te himu
MS: warè prublima te peggimu te himu
If you are happy to do something, you feel no problem about doing it.
I'm happy to help if you have a problem.
Source: GSL0551-0575
