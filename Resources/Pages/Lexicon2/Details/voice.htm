voice
/voyss/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: laheng
MS: laheng
the sound that you make when you speak or sing.
She has a beautiful voice, so I love to hear her sing.
I could hear voices in the room, but I wasn't sure who it was.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: due kanekal egpakalalag te igkeupii
MS: due kanekal egpakalalag te igkeupii
If people have a voice, they are able to say what they think and want.
The Femmatrics helps to give tribal people a voice in decisions about land.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: eglalag
MS: eglalag
If you voice a feeling or idea, you say it.
After the meeting she voiced to me her unhappiness with the decision.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0426-0450
