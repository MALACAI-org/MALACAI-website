derive
/də-rive/
 
vi.
 
1.
Def.: egligkat; egpuun
MS: egligkat; egpuun
If something derives from another thing, it comes from that other thing.
Her sickness derives from bad food.
Ka dalu rin migligkat te mareet ne pegkeen.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: ingkuwa; egpapuun
MS: ingkuwa; egpapuun
If you derive something from another thing or place, then you get it from that other thing or place.
This shampoo is derived from plants.
Ka siyampu ingkuwa ligkat te pinamula.
Sem.Dom.: AWL1
 
 
derivation
/de-rə-vay-shən/
 
n-uc, n-ct.
Def.: migligkat; peg-alawat, pegpapuun
MS: migligkat; peg-alawat; pegpapuun
the action of deriving.
The derivation of coconut oil from coconuts takes time.
 
n-ct.
Def.: ligkatanan; puunanan
MS: ligkatanan; puunanan
the source that something such as a custom or word came from.
I don't know the derivation of that custom.
wara a nakataha te ligkatanan te pegmemanè.
