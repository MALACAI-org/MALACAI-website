extend
/iks-tend/
 
vi.
 
1.
Def.: egliyu; diyè pad dapit
MS: egliyu; diyè pad dapit
If something extends to a certain point, that is how big or how long it is.
My land extends to the river.
Ka tanè ku migliyu pad te pahamayan ne weyig.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: egparakelen wey ke egpamalayaten
MS: egparakelen; egpamalayaten
If you extend something, you make it bigger or longer.
They extended their house by putting one more room on the side.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: egpamananey
MS: egpamananey
If you extend something, you make the period of time longer.
At first, I planned to stay for one week, but I extended my stay to two weeks.
Te hun-a, migsuman-suman a ne eg-uugpè te sabeka ne simana, piru ka peg-uugpè ku migpeyinguma ku te daruwa ne simana.
 
vt.
 
4.
Def.: egketeng te baad te lawa
MS: egketeng te baad te lawa
If you extend a part of your body, you stretch it out so that it is more straight.
She extended her arm in order to shake his hand.
Sem.Dom.: GSL0801-0825
