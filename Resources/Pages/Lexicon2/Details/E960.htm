dry
 
adj.
 
1.
Def.: nahangu
MS: nahangu
Something that is dry doesn't have much or any liquid in it.
The clothes are dry now, so we can wear them.
Ka me manggad nenaggangu e kuntee, sika ka egkaayun e ne igpansaluub.
This rice is very dry, because I didn't use much water when I cooked it.
 
vi.
 
2.
Def.: egkahangu
MS: egkahangu
If something dries, the liquid disappears from it.
The clothes are drying in the sun.
Ka me manggad egkaggangu te aldew.
 
vt.
 
3.
Def.: egpahangu; egpakahangu; egdalap; egpeinit
MS: egpahangu; egpakahangu; egdalap; egpeinit
If someone or something dries something, they cause the liquid to disappear from it.
The sun dried the rice.
Ka aldew nakahangu te paley.
She dried the baby with a towel.
Impahangu rin ka batè te tawil.
