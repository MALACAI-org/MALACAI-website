those
/thoze/
 
det, pron.
 
1.
Def.: sika; seeyè; due (meyitenged te daruwa wey ke masulug ne kasangkapan ne mariyù keykew)
MS: sika; seeyè; due
You use those to talk about two or more things which are not close to you
Those guavas on the table over there are for you.
Ka seeyè ne me beyabas te dibabew te lamisa su para kuna.
 
pron.
 
2.
Def.: seeyè (meyitenged te nateil ne grupu te me etew)
MS: seeyè
You use those to talk about a particular group of people.
Those who arrived late could not find a seat.
Ka seeyè se migginguma ne namewuri kenad egpakapinnuu.
Source: determiners; pronouns; GSL0101-0125
