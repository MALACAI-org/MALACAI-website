implement
/im-plə-mənt (n); im-plə-ment (v)/
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: kasangkapan wey ke malemu ne butang ne eggamiten nu te peggimu te minsan nekey.
MS: kasangkapan; butang
a tool or simple thing (e.g., a fork) that you use to do something.
Bolos, knives and scissors are all cutting implements.
Ugpit, ilab wey me gunting iyan sika me pantempug ne kasangkapan.
I don't know the name of it, but this is an implement for removing staples.
 
vt.
 
2.
Def.: eggimu; egpatuman
MS: eggimu; egpatuman
If you implement a plan or system, you do the action that you planned, or start to use the system.
We will start implementing the new rules next week.
Egbunsud ki eggimu te iyam ne balaud te seup ne simana.
Source: AWL4
