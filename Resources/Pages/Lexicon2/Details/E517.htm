class
 
n-ct.
 
1.
Def.: sabeka ne klasi ne due pegpekeg-iling
MS: sabeka ne klasi ne due pegpekeg-iling
a group of things which are similar in some way.
This class of mushrooms are highly poisonous.
 
n-ct.
 
2.
Def.: punduk te me etew ne due pegpekeg-iling (meyitenged te keugpè wey katenged)
MS: punduk te me etew ne due pegpekeg-iling
a group of people who have a similar social and economic position (e.g., upper class, working class).
 
n-ct.
 
3.
Def.: grupu te me istudyanti ne nekegduruma
MS: grupu te me istudyanti ne nekegduruma
a group of students who are studying together.
My best friend is in my class at school.
 
n-ct.
 
4.
Def.: igpanulù
MS: igpanulù
a lesson at school.
I have science class next.
Sayans ka seup ne klasi ku kuntee.
 
n-uc.
 
5.
Def.: pegkameupiya wey pegdakel se bali
MS: pegkameupiya wey pegdakel se bali
If something such as clothing has class, it is high quality and looks good.
She dresses with real class.
Source: GSL0376-0400
