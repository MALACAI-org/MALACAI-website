Those who worked at MALACAI on the two dictionaries in 2009-2010 include:

Naomi (team leader)

Naomi Keith is from Brisbane, Australia, where she is a teacher of English to international students. She started working with MALACAI in May 2009, when she came to Sinuda, and returned to Australia in April 2010. Her happy memories from the project include learning lots about dictionaries, the MALACAI song performance at fiesta, speaking Matigsalug (or trying to!), the office bible study, office merienda times and many jokes with her MALACAI colleagues.
